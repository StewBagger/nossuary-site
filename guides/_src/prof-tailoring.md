Cloth armour, bags, cloaks and shirts, from cloth that drops rather than from a gathering profession. **Its Bind-on-Pickup caster gear starts at level 10, and five of its bags got bigger on November 11.**
:::scope
The bag changes, the [[Faction Banner]] and the vendor-price cut are Blizzard's. Bag sizes and camp-object skill levels are checked against the 1.60.1.70938 client. Recipes, reagents and pattern sources are client data as read by Wowhead (November 1, before launch) and foreverchanges.pro, a third-party site. The 1 to 225 route and the trainers are Icy Veins' (November 4). The 225 to 300 route is foreverchanges.pro's, built from recipe data and not from a played run. Thin: nothing that needs raid materials has been crafted, because raids open on December 9, and 57 recipes make an item the client does not describe yet.
:::
## Is Tailoring worth taking?
It earns **Legacy Points** at skill 150, 225 and 300, and it is the only source of a run of Bind-on-Pickup cloth gear.
Icy Veins recommends Tailoring with Enchanting for Mages (Frost levelling page, November 10): *"Tailoring now lets you craft your own Bind-on-Pickup Rare gear, which is some of the best leveling gear available for how easy it is to access."* Its Tailoring page names Mages, Priests and Warlocks as the classes that *"get the most out of the armor they make."*
The catch is the binding. Icy Veins again: *"Both bind when picked up, so take Tailoring on the character that will wear them."* You cannot buy this gear and you cannot make it for a friend.
If you only want bag space, any class can use bags another tailor made.
## What it pairs with
**Enchanting.** Icy Veins (November 3): *"Enchanting doesn't rely on a particular gathering profession, so Enchanters occasionally pursue Tailoring to allow them to quickly create magic items to disenchant."* See [Enchanting](/guides/wow-forever/professions/enchanting/).
**It needs no gathering profession**: *"Tailoring uses cloth looted from humanoids, so it does not need a specific gathering profession."* It does pull in Pristine Leather for the healing line, and Rugged Leather, Ironweb Spider Silk and Mooncloth at the top.
**Vendors pay less for some crafted armour since November 10.** Blizzard cut the vendor price of Bind-on-Pickup pieces that *"a player was intended to craft only once for themselves"*. Disenchant spares; do not vendor them.

**Five bags changed on November 11.** Blizzard said they were *"unintentionally smaller than intended"*, and raised both the size and the cloth cost. Wowhead's page predates this and still prints the old values.

A bag you make goes into your inventory, and you drag it into an empty bag slot yourself.
## Camp objects and the Spinning Wheel
| Skill to place | Object | What it does | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Faction Banner]] | Spirit for members of **your faction** sitting nearby: 14 below level 40, 19 from 40, 27 from 50, 32 at 60. Exclusive with [[Divine Spirit]] | 1 Bolt of Linen Cloth, 1 Coarse Thread |
| 140 | `Spinning Wheel` | A crafting station, and keeps the banner's Spirit | 2 Simple Wood, 5 Bolt of Silk Cloth, 3 Fine Thread |
| 300 | `Loom` | A crafting station for recipes such as Venomspew Cord, and keeps the banner's Spirit | Bottled Screams, 4 Bolt of Runecloth, 4 Simple Wood |
Blizzard publishes the banner as *"14-32 increased Spirit (level dependent)"*. The steps between are the client's.
**74 recipes can only be made beside a Spinning Wheel**, on foreverchanges.pro's count: the Slippers, Handwraps, Cords, Waistcords and Sandals lines, the Nether shoulders and cuffs, and Classic pieces such as Robe of Power, the Dreamweave set and Cloak of Fire.
**The wheel still needs skill 140.** Blizzard lowered the Tanning Rack to 125 on November 6 and did not touch the wheel. foreverchanges.pro lists the six skill-125 Leggings as wheel recipes, so a tailor at 125 to 139 crafts those at someone else's wheel.
1. A wheel needs a campfire and is used up when placed. foreverchanges.pro says it lasts 15 minutes
2. Every camp object you place shares a one-hour cooldown, so carry the whole batch's reagents first
3. The Blueprint drops from the Unstable Sentinel in the City of Dalaran, and both it and the wheel can be traded
The Loom's Blueprint source is not published.

## Gear you make while levelling
- **Level 10:** `Novice Arcanist's Sash` and `Novice Ardent's Sash`, from the trainer at Tailoring 35. They cost bolts of linen and potions
- **Skill 60–125, 30 Favor each:** six lines named by school. Shining (Holy), Shadow, Pristine (healing), Pearly (Arcane), Flame (Fire) and Silky (Frost), each in boots, gloves, sash, circlet, chest and leggings. [[Pristine Circlet]] is the skill-100 healing helm. Filigreed versions of some pieces are world drops
- **Skill 150–235:** Gilded, Frothing, Fiery, Black, Golden and Radiant Slippers, Handwraps, Cords, Waistcords and Sandals, plus Nether shoulders and cuffs at 195 and 205. Wowhead has the Slippers on the trainer; the rest cost 60 Favor, and the Sandals 90. All need a Spinning Wheel
- **Stormsewn**, a Bind-on-Equip set at skill 50 to 60 from the Zephras Isle reputation vendor
There are 67 Favor patterns and the Certification. Mivin Shadowweave sells them at Three Corners in Redridge, and Jim'bek outside the Crossroads. See [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/).
## Level-60 crafted gear
- **Tier-set pieces at 300:** Conviction (8 patterns), Demonheart (4) and Manaflare (4), in boots, gloves, legs and shoulders. Manaflare is the Mage set. Conviction and Demonheart read as Priest and Warlock, which is our inference
- **What a piece costs**, on Wowhead's table: 3 [[Malleable Essence of Nature]], 2 Mooncloth, 16 Bolt of Runecloth and 8 Rune Thread. Legs take 5, 3 and 22. The patterns come from Aisarra Nightmeadow in Mount Hyjal
- **The essence comes from the raids.** The client's item text says it is *"acquired from disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps"*. Both open December 9, so nobody has crafted a piece yet
- **The 295 epics all take 10 Viridian Dye now.** [[Belt of the Archmage]] is 16 Bolt of Runecloth, 8 Mooncloth, 8 Ghost Dye, 10 Essence of Water, 10 Essence of Fire, 4 Large Brilliant Shard, 6 Rune Thread and the dye. [[Robe of the Archmage]], [[Robe of the Void]], [[Gloves of Spell Mastery]] and [[Truefaith Vestments]] sit at 295 too
- **[[Bottomless Bag]]** needs 12 Mooncloth, 5 Pristine Scale and 5 Pristine Hide, and no Core Leather
- **At 285–295:** Swarmtender's and Venomspew gloves, cords and footpads, with patterns from Mishta in Silithus
- **Raid-gated and unplayed:** Flarecore Wraps, whose pattern is a Molten Core drop, and the Core Felcloth Bag, which needs Core Leather
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor gives the title *"the Tailor"*
## Specialisations
**There is no Tailoring specialisation.** No source lists one. Do not go looking.
## Levelling it
**1 to 225, Icy Veins' route (November 4).** It uses trainer recipes only and no crafting station. None of the five changed bags is on it, so the November 11 hotfix does not alter it.
1. **1 → 30**: Bolt of Linen Cloth, about 30
2. **30 → 55**: Linen Belt, about 28. Train Journeyman at 50
3. **55 → 80**: Bolt of Woolen Cloth, about 32 in all
4. **80 → 100**: Woolen Cape, about 32
5. **100 → 120**: Bolt of Silk Cloth. The route uses 233 bolts in total
6. **120 → 135**: Azure Silk Hood, about 17
7. **135 → 140**: Formal White Shirt, 5. Train Expert at 125
8. **140 → 150**: Bolt of Mageweave
9. **150 → 170**: Crimson Silk Vest, about 20
10. **170 → 190**: Crimson Silk Pantaloons, about 26
11. **190 → 215**: Black Mageweave Gloves, about 38
12. **215 → 225**: Mageweave Bag, about 26, at 4 bolts each
**Icy Veins' shopping list to 225:** 932 Silk Cloth, 900 Mageweave Cloth, 96 Wool Cloth, 60 Linen Cloth, 104 Silken Thread, 94 Fine Thread, 76 Heavy Silken Thread, 28 Coarse Thread, 92 Red Dye, 34 Blue Dye, 10 Bleach.
With 150 Runecloth in hand, Icy Veins switches at 205: 30 Bolt of Runecloth from the Artisan trainer, then 10 Runecloth Belts from 210. That skips the bags and saves 520 Mageweave Cloth.
**225 to 300.** Icy Veins says it does not have enough information to give a route yet. foreverchanges.pro computes one from the client's recipes:
1. **225 → 234**: Runecloth Belt, about 11, from the trainer
2. **234 → 241**: Runecloth Cloak, about 7. Pattern from Darnall in Moonglade
3. **241 → 250**: Runecloth Gloves, about 10. Pattern from Qia in Winterspring
4. **250 → 295**: Runecloth Cuffs, about 135. Pattern from Pexmit in Mount Hyjal
5. **295 → 300**: Mooncloth Robe, about 7. Pattern from the Shen'dralar Provisioner in Dire Maul
Its whole 1 to 300 list carries 4,240 Runecloth, 142 Ironweb Spider Silk, 187 Rune Thread, 40 Rugged Leather, 28 Mooncloth and 14 Golden Pearl. The counts are averages, the site reprices reagents to pick the cheapest path, and it says the skill each trainer recipe is learned at is Classic's.
**Runecloth is the wall.** Silk and Mageweave carry you to 225 on vendor thread and dye; the last 75 points cost thousands of Runecloth and a stack of Ironweb Spider Silk. Tailoring is the cheapest crafting profession to start and one of the dearest to finish *(ours, not a source's)*.
