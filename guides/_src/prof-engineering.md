Bombs, trinkets, goggles and gadgets. **Forever kept everything that made Engineering a choice**: items only an engineer can use, and the Gnomish and Goblin schools. It added two brands of bombs that anyone can throw. It got no crafted set, and the fewest trade-camp recipes of any crafting profession.

## Is Engineering worth taking?
It earns one **Legacy Point** each at skill 150, 225 and 300. It has 29 entries at the trade camp, counting its Certification, against 62 for Blacksmithing.
What the guide sites say it is for:
1. **PvP.** Icy Veins (November 3) calls it *"a good pick for PvP if you want another way to stop a heal or buy time to get out of melee range."* Its Survival Hunter guide lists *"Engineering for valuable PvP items"*
2. **An escape while levelling.** Icy Veins again: drop a Target Dummy to pull enemies off you
3. **Guns and ammunition for Hunters.** Engineers are the only crafters who make guns (Wowhead)
4. **Selling to non-engineers.** Wowhead (November 7) sees *"a lot more emphasis on Engineering items that even non-engineers can use"* and says it *"may actually make Engineering turn out to be an amazing gold-maker, but only time will tell."*
**If you are a Gnome**, your racial was rewritten for it. See below.
Icy Veins' Frost Mage guide adds a cost warning about Engineering and Alchemy: *"without a Herbalism or Mining alt, they will be difficult or expensive to level."*
**There is no profession perk.** Wowhead says it *"has not found any source for claims that WoTLK Style Profession Perks are presently in WoW: Forever."*
## What it pairs with
Engineering eats bars and stone, and [Mining](/guides/wow-forever/professions/mining/) is the only profession that gathers them. Icy Veins and Wowhead both name Mining first. Wowhead offers [Blacksmithing](/guides/wow-forever/professions/blacksmithing/) as the alternative for plate and mail wearers, since the two share materials.
Its camp objects are vendors and a workbench. None of them is a buff, so nothing here scales with level.

## Specialisations
**Gnomish and Goblin are live.** Wowhead (November 7) and Icy Veins (November 3) agree on the entry: **character level 30 and Engineering 200.** Each is a three-step quest chain.
| | Goblin | Gnomish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starts | The Engineering trainer in Ratchet | Any Engineering trainer |
| Ends | The Goblin Engineering trainer in Gadgetzan | Ironforge for the Alliance, Booty Bay for the Horde |
| Hand in | 20 Big Iron Bomb, 20 Solid Dynamite, 5 Explosive Sheep | 6 Mithril Tube, 2 Advanced Target Dummy, 1 Accurate Scope |
| Makes | Goblin Sapper Charge, The Big One, Goblin Mining Helmet, Goblin Rocket Helmet, Goblin Jumper Cables XL, the Everlook teleporter | Gnomish Shrink Ray, Net-o-Matic Projector, Battle Chicken, Mind Control Cap, Death Ray, World Enlarger, the Gadgetzan teleporter |
foreverchanges.pro counts 11 recipes on each side at build 70938. They are the vanilla lists.
**The membership card expires.** Finishing the chain gives a Goblin or Gnome Engineer Membership Card, which is what lets you buy that school's trainer schematics. Wowhead gives its life as 14 days of in-game time, 336 hours. A renewal quest replaces it and mails you a package that can hold schematics found nowhere else, `Schematic: Pet Bombling` for Goblins and `Schematic: Lil' Smoky` for Gnomes.
**Switching schools is not covered by any source**, and neither is its cost.
## Levelling it
**To 225 this is Icy Veins' route**, dated November 4. Every recipe on it comes from a trainer, so it works for either school.
1. **1 → 30**: Rough Blasting Powder, 60. Keep it
2. **30 → 50**: Handful of Copper Bolts, 30. Keep them. Make an Arclight Spanner at 50
3. **50 → 75**: Rough Copper Bomb, about 30
4. **75 → 100**: Coarse Blasting Powder, 45, then Coarse Dynamite
5. **100 → 125**: Silver Contact, Bronze Tube, then Standard Scope. Skip the scopes and make more tubes if Moss Agate is dear
6. **125 → 150**: Heavy Blasting Powder, 42, all kept. Then Whirring Bronze Gizmo, 15, all kept
7. **150 → 175**: Bronze Framework, 15, then Explosive Sheep. Keep 5 sheep if you plan to go Goblin
8. **175 → 200**: Solid Blasting Powder, 26, then Iron Grenade, then Mithril Tube. Keep 6 tubes if you plan to go Gnomish
9. **200 → 225**: Unstable Trigger, 20, then alternate Mithril Casing and Mithril Frag Bomb
**Shopping list to 225** (Icy Veins): 84 Bronze Bar, 66 Copper Bar, 56 Mithril Bar, 12 Iron Bar, 5 Silver Bar, 60 Rough Stone, 45 Coarse Stone, 42 Heavy Stone, 52 Solid Stone, 60 Wool Cloth, 45 Linen Cloth, 20 Mageweave Cloth, 12 Silk Cloth, 15 Medium Leather, 12 Weak Flux and 10 Moss Agate. Buy a Blacksmith Hammer too.
**The old [[Ice Deflector]] step is gone.** Earlier routes sent you to buy its schematic for 160 to 175. Neither current route uses it.
**Do not vendor the grenades.** Blizzard's November 10 hotfix cut the Iron Grenade's vendor price from 5 silver to 1 silver 25 copper. A craft makes three, and Wowhead reported the same day that the vendor shuffle built on them is dead.
**Above 225 no guide site has a route.** Icy Veins says it does not yet have enough information. The only published path is foreverchanges.pro's, computed from the client's skill colours at build 70938 and not from play:
1. **225 → 245**: Hi-Impact Mithril Slugs, about 62
2. **245 → 273**: Mithril Gyro-Shot, about 48
3. **273 → 289**: Thorium Widget, about 19. Its schematic is vendor-sold
4. **289 → 300**: Thorium Tube, about 12. Its schematic is sold in Riverglades and Winterspring
That path's whole 1 to 300 list holds 378 Solid Stone, 189 Mithril Bar and 129 Thorium Bar. It is cheap in bars next to Blacksmithing.
**These counts ignore `Working Overtime`**, the Legacy perk that adds 4% skill-up chance per rank, 20% at five ranks.
## At level 60, against Blacksmithing
- **Engineering's lock is on using items, not buying them.** In the client these need Engineering to use: Goblin Sapper Charge at 205, Gnomish Battle Chicken at 230, Thorium Grenade at 260, Dark Iron Bomb at 285, and Arcane Bomb, Force Reactive Disk and Arcanite Dragonling at 300. The Sapper deals 450 to 750 Fire damage to all nearby enemies and 375 to 625 to you
- **The new grenades need no Engineering.** No EZ-Thro or SAF-T explosive in the client carries a skill requirement, and the tooltip on [[EZ-Thro Thorium Grenade]] reads *"Anyone can use grenades with EZ-Thro!"* It deals 300 to 500 Fire damage with a 3-second stun. An engineer can sell these to anyone. The Sapper has no EZ-Thro copy
- **Nineteen recipes need an Anarchist's Workbench**, the skill-300 camp object. They include all four new goggles and all four new belts, [[EZ-Thro Dark Bomb]], [[EZ-Thro Mana Bomb]], [[SAF-T Ultra Precision Scope]], the skill-300 rifle and both new ammunition types. Below 300 you need someone else's workbench
- **The top recipes run on new bars.** The scope takes a Legionite Bar and 12 [[Heavy Thorium Bar]]. `Hyper Deluxe Sniper Rifle Mk XVII` takes 4 [[Malleable Essence of Nature]], which comes only from disenchanting raid items, so nobody can make it before December 9
- **Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow can be traded.** Blizzard's November 10 hotfix removed their Bind on Pickup, with the note that they are *"not available until raids unlock"* and that the change *"may influence profession choice."* A craft makes 200 from 8 Heavy Thorium Bar
- **Blacksmithing locks the opposite way**: most of its level-60 plate is Bind on Equip and can be bought. The [Blacksmithing guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/blacksmithing/) lists the Bind on Pickup exceptions
## What Forever changed
- **The Gnome racial was rewritten.** Vanilla gave *"Engineering skill increased by 15."* Forever gives *"Your gnomish ingenuity reduces the rate of engineering devices failing or backfiring when you use them by 20%."*
- **Two new brands, "SAF-T" and "EZ-Thro"**, spanning the whole skill range from SAF-T Dynamite at 25. The top ones are real. [[EZ-Thro Dark Bomb]] at 300 deals 225 to 675 Fire damage with a 4-second stun. [[EZ-Thro Mana Bomb]] burns 675 to 1125 mana, silences for 5 seconds and deals 50% of the drained mana as damage
- **Those two brands are the Druid's bombs.** Blizzard's class deep dive says most engineering bombs still cannot be used while shapeshifted, *"but some specific new recipes can be used while shapeshifted. See the SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes."* A Druid taking Engineering should level those two lines
- **Goggles for every armour type.** New at skill 200 for 180 Favor each: Floppy (cloth), Bent (leather), Stuckbutton (mail) and Dented (plate). They bind on pickup, need Engineering 200 to wear and need the Workbench to make. New belts at 175 follow the same rules
- **[[SAF-T Ultra Precision Scope]]** at 300: a permanent 2% critical strike on a bow, gun or crossbow
- **Satchels of bombs.** Satchel of Copper, Bronze, Iron and Dark Iron Bombs are Bind on Pickup thrown weapons with Stamina whose throws *"explode on impact"*. They need Engineering to use, from 85 for Copper to 285 for Dark Iron
- **[[Compact Critter Carrier]]** at 225, for 270 Favor: a Hunter engineer places it for 3 minutes, and it *"allows Hunters in your group to access their stabled pets."*
- **Four skill-300 gadgets at the trade camp** for 360 Favor each: Gnomish Poultryizer, Stealthman 52, Ultralight Goblin Glider and Ultrasafe Rechargeable Battery. Each takes at least one of the new materials: Legionite Bar, Azerothium Bar, Torbernite or Pitchblende
- **Yields changed in both directions.** foreverchanges.pro's comparison at build 70938 finds 5 recipes with new reagents and 19 with a new yield. Rough Dynamite takes 1 Rough Blasting Powder where it took 2. Every rocket makes 5 where it made 3. But several explosives make fewer per craft: Iron Grenade, Large Copper Bomb and Big Bronze Bomb make 3 where they made 4, and Heavy Dynamite makes 3 where it made 5
- **The Tinkerbox cannot be made.** The client's Engineering list still holds seven Tinkerbox and Tinker recipes, but the Tinkerbox and its three modules are missing from the item table. Three finished items that take a module, SAF-T Nitro Boosts, SAF-T Teleport and EZ-Thro Magnetic Displacer, are stuck behind the same gap *(ours, read from the client)*. No guide site lists a Tinkerbox recipe
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor unlocks the title "the Engineer"
- **A teleporter to Mount Hyjal.** `Dimensional Transporter - Mt. Hyjal` at 300 takes 2 Legionite Bar and is not listed under either school. foreverchanges.pro has its schematic sold by Pexmit on Mount Hyjal
- **There is still no engineering mount.** foreverchanges.pro's pets-and-mounts group holds four recipes. None is new and none is a mount
## What is not known
How to switch schools and what it costs. Where the Anarchist's Workbench Blueprint and the Essence of Anarchy drop. Where the schematics for the skill-300 ammunition and rifle come from, beyond Blizzard's word that they wait on raids. What 33 further recipes make: foreverchanges.pro leaves that many out because the client does not describe their item. And how Engineering performs in raids, since none is open.
