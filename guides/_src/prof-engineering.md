Bombs, trinkets, goggles and gadgets. **Forever did not fold it into the new crafted-gear economy**: no tier set, and the fewest trade-camp recipes of any profession. It is still the most expensive thing in the game to level *(ours, not a source's)*; there are no prices before launch.

## Is Engineering worth taking?
It earns **Legacy Points** like the other crafting professions, but it got no tier set and only 29 trade-camp recipes, the fewest of any profession. Take it for utility and fun; it is also the most expensive to buy out *(ours, not a source's)*.
Three reasons to take it anyway, **two of them published**:
1. **You are a Gnome.** The racial was rewritten for Forever (see below)
2. **PvP.** The published Hunter advice is *"Engineering for valuable PvP items"*, and MyBigBlaze and mulltiy both name Engineering the profession for PvP
3. **As a second profession at 60**, once Blacksmithing has given you the gear: *"After Blacksmithing has provided the gear you need, you can replace it with Engineering."*
[[Minor Recombobulator]] at Engineering 140 appears in **32 of the level-20 best-in-slot lists**, effectively every class and spec in the game. That count comes from level-20 lists and has not been re-checked at higher levels.
The Mage guide adds a warning: *"without a Herbalism or Mining alt, these will be difficult or expensive to level."*
## What it pairs with
Engineering eats ore and stone, and **Mining is the only profession that gathers them.** That is unchanged.
Its camp object is [[Reagent Bot]] at skill 20, a reagent vendor at the fire. [[Repair Bot]] follows from a Blueprint at 140 and adds repairs, and the Anarchist's Workbench at 300. See the [Camping guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/camping/).
## At level 60, against Blacksmithing
- **Engineering's lock is on using items, not buying them.** In the launch client these need Engineering to use: Goblin Sapper Charge at 205, Gnomish Battle Chicken at 230, Thorium Grenade at 260, Dark Iron Bomb at 285, and Arcane Bomb, Force Reactive Disk and Arcanite Dragonling at 300. The Sapper deals 600 Fire damage to all nearby enemies and 500 to you. The Disk deals 30 damage to all nearby enemies each time your shield blocks.
- **The new grenades need no Engineering.** No EZ-Thro or SAF-T item in the client carries a skill requirement, and the client describes `EZ-Thro Thorium Grenade` as *"Anyone can use grenades with EZ-Thro!"* It and [[EZ-Thro Dark Bomb]] carry the same base Fire damage as Thorium Grenade and Dark Iron Bomb, 400 and 450. An engineer can sell grenades to anyone, and a non-engineer can throw them. The Sapper has no EZ-Thro copy.
- **Blacksmithing locks the opposite way**: almost all of its level-60 plate is Bind on Equip and can be bought. The [Blacksmithing guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/blacksmithing/) lists the Bind on Pickup exceptions.
## What Forever changed
- **The Gnome racial was rewritten.** Vanilla gave *"Engineering skill increased by 15."* Forever gives *"Your gnomish ingenuity reduces the rate of engineering devices failing or backfiring when you use them by 20%."*
- **Engineering-only gating is fully intact, and is being applied to brand-new items.** 98 items carry a *"Requires Engineering"* line, 20 of them new. The vanilla ones are untouched: Gnomish Rocket Boots, Parachute Cloak, Arcanite Dragonling, Force Reactive Disk, Goblin Jumper Cables XL, Master Engineer's Goggles, Bloodvine Goggles.
- **Goggles now exist for every armour type.** New at skill 200: Floppy (cloth), Bent (leather), Stuckbutton (mail), Dented (plate). Engineer headgear was effectively cloth-only in vanilla, so plate and mail wearers now have an engineer head slot. New engineer-only belts at 175 too.
- **Two new consumable brands, "SAF-T" and "EZ-Thro"**, spanning the whole skill range. The top ones are real. [[EZ-Thro Dark Bomb]] at 300 deals 225–675 fire damage and a 4-second stun. [[EZ-Thro Mana Bomb]] burns 675–1125 mana, silences for 5 seconds and deals 50% of the drained mana as damage.
- **Those two brands are the Druid's bombs.** Blizzard's class deep dive is the only official word on Engineering: most engineering bombs still cannot be used while shapeshifted, *"but some specific new recipes can be used while shapeshifted. See the SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes."* A Druid taking Engineering should level those two lines; the rest of the bomb list stays unusable in form.
- **[[SAF-T Ultra Precision Scope]]** at 300: a permanent +2% critical strike on a bow, gun or crossbow.
- **New thrown weapons**: Satchel of Copper, Bronze, Iron and Dark Iron Bombs, which *"explode on impact"* and carry Stamina.
- **[[Compact Critter Carrier]]** at 225 *"allows Hunters in your group to access their stabled pets."*
- **Bombs got cheaper and fireworks got much more efficient.** 23 recipes changed reagents. Rough Dynamite now takes 1 Rough Blasting Powder instead of 2. Every rocket now makes 5 instead of 3 for the same cost, and there are three new colours.
- **There is no Tinkerbox.** The Tinkerbox, its three modules and their seven schematics are not in the launch client, so a guide that lists it as an Engineering perk is out of date.
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor unlocks the title "the Engineer".
- **There is still no engineering mount.** The pets list holds four entries and all four are vanilla. If you remember an engineering mount, that is Burning Crusade.
## Specialisations
**Gnomish and Goblin are confirmed as a live system in Forever.** They are the only specialisation with a published sentence: *"After completing a unique quest, you can specialize in either Goblin or Gnomish schools of engineering. Each school specializes in crafting unique items, some of which aren't available to the opposing style of craftsmanship."*
**The skill level is not published** (vanilla's was 200), nor is the switch cost. Exclusivity is implied by that wording but never stated outright.
## Levelling it
1. **1 → 30**: Rough Blasting Powder, ~33
2. **30 → 59**: Handful of Copper Bolts, ~50. Keep them
3. **59 → 95**: Copper Tube, then Coarse Blasting Powder ~40
4. **95 → 160**: Copper Modulator → Silver Contact → Practice Lock → Large Copper Bomb → Heavy Blasting Powder → Whirring Bronze Gizmo → Bronze Framework → Aquadynamic Fish Attractor
5. **160 → 175**: [[Ice Deflector]]. Its schematic is gold-bought from Rizz Loosebolt in Alterac Mountains, and it is a genuine bottleneck
6. **175 → 195**: Solid Blasting Powder, ~72 crafts
7. **195 → 225**: Iron Grenade → Inlaid Mithril Cylinder → Mechanical Repair Kit → Hi-Impact Mithril Slugs
**Shopping list to 225:** 144 Solid Stone, 120 Copper Bar, 80 Bronze Bar, 46 Coarse Stone, 33 Rough Stone, 28 Linen, 26 Mithril Bar, 26 Weak Flux, 21 Wool, 15 Frost Oil, 12 Iron Bar, 12 Silk.
**225 → 300:** 162 Thorium Bar, 176 Solid Stone, 88 Mithril Bar, 30 Dense Stone, 28 Runecloth.
**Engineering is cheap in bars and expensive in schematics.** Several key steps are gold-bought rather than trainer-taught, and that is where the money goes.
## What is not known
The Gnomish and Goblin skill level and switch cost. **Whether Engineering's raid value changed at all** (no source states it either way). What 33 of its recipes make, and where about twenty of the new high-skill schematics come from.
