Healer with one big single-target heal, a chain heal across a group, and from 40 an instant heal over time on a 12-second cooldown. Totems and [[Mana Tide Totem]] support the group around it.

## What Restoration is for
**Group healing that is not limited by cooldowns.** [[Healing Wave]] is the single-target heal from level 1. [[Chain Heal]] at 40 heals three targets, each jump half as strong as the last. [[Riptide]] at 40 is an instant heal over 15 seconds, and a Chain Heal cast directly on the Riptide target is 25% stronger.
**It is a group spec, and both sites say so.** Icy Veins: *"As a Healer specialization, Restoration lacks most of the damage talent support of other classes and has no area damage, making it more of a group leveling specialization."* Wowhead: *"Overall, you should look to play Enhancement instead if you are solo leveling for the most part."* Icy Veins rates it 4.5 of 5 overall, 4.5 for dungeons, 5 for utility and 2 for levelling; a levelling score measures solo kill speed.
**Before 40 the group tools are totems and Mana.** [[Water Shield]] at exactly 20 returns 2% of your maximum Mana when you are hit or land any spell crit. [[Mana Tide Totem]] at 25 restores Mana to the group. `Nature's Swiftness` at 30 makes your next Nature spell instant, in practice a [[Healing Wave]] you cannot be interrupted out of.
**If you solo as Restoration, you play a weaker Elemental.** Wowhead's levelling list for the spec is a weapon imbue, a shield, [[Searing Totem]] and [[Lightning Bolt]], with [[Fire Nova]] on packs.

**What the order is buying.** `Tidal Focus` first gives 5% hit for the damage you do between heals, and 5% off healing costs. `Mindfulness` keeps 50% of your Mana regeneration running while you cast. `Improved Healing Wave` takes 0.5 seconds off [[Healing Wave]]. `Healing Way` adds 25% to Healing Wave and `Purification` 10% to every heal. The three milestones land on your 11th, 16th and 21st points.
**Wowhead's levelling build, 0/17/34**, spends differently after the core: only 1 point in `Improved Healing Wave`, then `Mental Dexterity` 3, `Shamanistic Focus` and `Improved Ghost Wolf` 2 in Enhancement for the solo stretches. It is also dated November 4.
**Level 60: two shapes, both published.**
- **0/10/41**, Icy Veins' standard and Wowhead's alternate: the list above. Everything in Restoration that raises healing, plus `Restorative Totems`.
- **0/17/34**, Wowhead's first raid and dungeon build: drops `Restorative Totems` and 2 points of `Tidal Focus` for `Mental Dexterity` 3, `Elemental Weapons` 2 and `Improved Ghost Wolf` 2. Wowhead's case is mobility: an instant [[Ghost Wolf]] usable indoors. Icy Veins skips it for *"the huge amount of dead talents that would be needed"*.
## Healing priority
Before the pull:
1. **[[Water Shield]]** up from 20; [[Lightning Shield]] before that
2. **Totems**, the Fire totem last
Levels 1 to 39:
1. **[[Healing Wave]]** on injured targets, at a lower rank when the damage is light
2. **[[Lesser Healing Wave]]** from 20, when the cast has to land in 1.5 seconds
3. **`Nature's Swiftness`** into [[Healing Wave]] from 30, as the emergency button
4. **[[Mana Tide Totem]]** from 25, at about 80% Mana so none is wasted
5. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** when nothing needs healing and Mana allows
From 40, the priority both sites publish:
1. **[[Riptide]]** on the most injured target, on cooldown
2. **[[Chain Heal]]** on a target that has Riptide, when several people are hurt
3. **[[Healing Wave]]** when one person is hurt
4. **[[Fire Nova]] and [[Chain Lightning]]** on packs, or [[Lightning Bolt]], only when nothing needs healing
**Why this order.**
- **Downranking is the Mana tool.** Wowhead: *"The healing difference between ranks in WoW: Forever is far lower than in Vanilla WoW, meaning you're more incentivized to downrank to save on Mana."* Icy Veins' lists say the same of [[Healing Wave]] and [[Chain Heal]]. One creator healing levelling dungeons [keeps the top rank and one two or three ranks lower on the bars](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88d4Etzi9t4&t=593), and treats [[Lesser Healing Wave]] as an emergency button only; that is one player's method.
- **Low ranks of [[Healing Wave]] are also fast.** Rank 1 casts in 1.5 seconds, rank 2 in 2.0, rank 3 in 2.5 and rank 4 up in 3.0, before `Improved Healing Wave`. Rank 2 costs 45 Mana.
- **[[Riptide]] first, because it sets up [[Chain Heal]].** The 25% bonus applies to a Chain Heal cast directly on the Riptide target. Rank 1 heals 445 over 15 seconds for 150 Mana on a 12-second cooldown, so it can sit on one target at all times.
- **[[Chain Heal]] costs more than it looks on one target.** Rank 1 heals 246 to 281 for 260 Mana on a 2.5-second cast; the value is in the two jumps.
- **Wait before you heal when you can.** Spirit regeneration stops for 5 seconds after any spell that costs Mana. Wowhead's advice is one full-rank [[Healing Wave]] on a deeply injured target rather than several small ones, to spend more time outside that window.
- **[[Water Shield]] has three globes.** Each returns 2% of maximum Mana, and the tooltip says only one fires every few seconds. Recast it when the globes are gone.

## What Forever changed for Restoration
- **New:** `Mindfulness`, [[Water Shield]] and [[Riptide]]. Blizzard placed `Mindfulness` low *"to make it more accessible for Elemental Shaman"*. Removed: `Nature's Guidance`, the only Restoration talent Blizzard lists as cut. `Totemic Mastery` is gone from the tree too, because every buff totem reaches 30 yards with no talent spent.
- **[[Riptide]] lost its direct heal before launch.** Blizzard's November 2 notes: *"Riptide no longer deals any initial healing, just healing over time"*, with the Mana cost cut by about 40% and the cooldown raised from 6 seconds to 12.
- **`Healing Way` is a flat bonus to [[Healing Wave]]**, instead of a stacking per-target buff you built up with low-rank casts. If you played a Restoration Shaman before, stop pre-stacking.
- **[[Mana Tide Totem]] is the 16-point milestone, not the 31-point capstone**, so it arrives at 25 instead of 40.
- **`Tidal Focus` also grants hit** with all spells and attacks. `Healing Focus` is 3 ranks reaching 70%. `Improved Reincarnation` also grants 2% maximum health a rank. `Natural Grace` (formerly `Healing Grace`) is row 2 and cuts threat from all your spells, not just heals.
- **`Totemic Focus` is row 2, with `Tidal Focus` at row 1, and it discounts more than totems:** `Totemic Call` and the totem bar's three Call spells too, which matters because [[Call of the Elements]] charges you for all four totems at once.
- **`Tidal Mastery` no longer touches Lightning spells**, so your filler [[Lightning Bolt]]s get no critical strike chance from it.
- **Buff totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards instead of 20.** Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Windfury, Flametongue and the resistance totems cover the whole raid rather than your party.
- **A capstone conflict:** `Nature's Swiftness` needs 21 Restoration points, and either other tree's capstone needs 31: 52 against a 51-point budget. `Nature's Swiftness` cannot coexist with `Rage of the Farseer` or `Lightning Mastery`.
## At level 60
**Sourced:** the two builds and the priority list above. Icy Veins calls the result *"an excellent dungeon healer"* and Wowhead expects [[Chain Heal]] to be *"stronger than ever with Riptide to boost its power"*. Both tell you to drop [[Mana Tide Totem]] at about 80% Mana and to keep `Nature's Swiftness` for a tank who drops suddenly.
**Not known:** throughput and Mana over a raid fight. No raid opens until December 9, so no raid log exists, and Icy Veins' stat page says the endgame gear picture was incomplete when it was written.
