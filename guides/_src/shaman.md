Forever's Shaman changes are mostly upkeep: weapon imbues last an hour instead of five minutes, buff totems last five minutes and all totems drop on a 1.0-second global cooldown, and a totem bar drops four at once.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Elemental](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/elemental/)** | Ranged DPS | [[Chain Lightning]] at 32 hits three targets, and [[Fire Nova]] bursts around your Fire totem from 12 | [[Lightning Bolt]] filler, area damage on cooldown, `Lightning Mastery` burst at 40 |
| **[Enhancement](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/enhancement/)** | Melee DPS | A slow two-hander with [[Windfury Weapon]] from 30: each hit has a 20% chance of two extra attacks, and it now stacks with [[Windfury Totem]] | One two-hander and no dual wield, with [[Stormstrike]] on an 8-second cooldown from 25 |
| **[Restoration](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/restoration/)** | Healer | [[Riptide]] at 40 makes a [[Chain Heal]] cast on that target 25% stronger | [[Healing Wave]] to 40, then Riptide and [[Chain Heal]] |
**Shamans cannot dual wield in Forever.** Wowhead: *"There won't be any official support for dual-wielding, and the skill has been removed in this version of the game."* If you have played a later expansion, unlearn that.
**Gear Enhancement for Strength, not Intellect.** Blizzard said so on November 6 and changed the talents on November 12. Guides written before then say the opposite. See [Enhancement](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/enhancement/).
Blizzard endorses a narrow version of Enhancement tanking. The redesigned `Spirit Weapons` keeps the Shaman's *"unique ability to generate high threat and serve as a temporary tank in small group gameplay"*. `Improved Stormstrike` is written for *"when serving as a temporary tank."* **Temporary, and small group, is the whole of the sanctioned claim.** Shaman has no taunt and no defensive cooldown. One community guide on Wowhead publishes dungeon tanking builds; see [Enhancement](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/enhancement/).
## Is Shaman for you?
Play Shaman if you want one character that can heal, cast at range or fight in melee, and you do not mind four class quests gating your totems on the way up.
Blizzard's own summary sets the trade. Shaman *"can be effective melee or spell damage dealers and healers of parties or raids"* and are *"among the sturdiest of classes, with the use of Mail armor and a Shield"*. But they also *"lack an effective crowd control"* and *"need those allies to make up for their own weaknesses in control and survivability."*
:::strengths
- Heals, ranged damage and melee, all from one character
- Buff totems last 5 minutes and reach 30 yards, and Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Windfury, Flametongue and the resistance totems cover the whole raid
- [[Ghost Wolf]] at 20 gives 40% run speed, twenty levels before a mount
:::
:::weaknesses
- Mana pays for everything, including every totem you drop
- Four class quests gate your totems, and the Water one sends you across the world
- No taunt, no defensive cooldown, and no dual wield in Forever
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** A weapon imbue, [[Lightning Shield]], totems, a shock, then swing or cast.
**Is it forgiving?** You heal yourself, and [[Reincarnation]] at 30 is a self-resurrect.
**What carries you while levelling?** A weapon imbue lasts an hour, so a pull costs you nothing but a totem drop. Wowhead sends solo levellers to the Enhancement tree.
**Does it need a group?** No, but Restoration levels best in one. The spec pages carry the published ratings and what they measure.
:::

| 22 | **`Totemic Call`**, new, brings every live totem to where you stand, on a 30-second cooldown |
| 25 | Your 16-point talent: [[Stormstrike]], `Earthbound` or [[Mana Tide Totem]]. `Improved Ghost Wolf` also opens here |

| 40 | [[Chain Heal]], your 31-point capstone, mount, a third totem set, and **Dual Specialization**, which Wowhead reports costs 50 gold at the class trainer |
| 42 | [[Grace of Air Totem]] — **the last new Shaman ability in the game.** 43 to 60 is ranks only |
**The second and third totem sets have swapped names.** Blizzard's deep dive called them Call of the Spirits at 30 and Call of the Ancestors at 40. The live client's spellbook has Call of the Ancestors at 30 and Call of the Spirits at 40. Either way you get one at each level.
**Tranquil Air Totem is gone.** Blizzard's deep dive says so flatly: *"Tranquil Air Totem: Removed."* [[Windfury Totem]] and Flametongue Totem are raid-wide buffs now, not weapon enchants, and they affect players only. They exclude each other; [[Grace of Air Totem]] stacks with either.
**Your own imbue no longer blocks the matching totem.** Until November 12 a Shaman with [[Windfury Weapon]] got nothing from [[Windfury Totem]]. Blizzard's hotfix: *"Weapon imbues and Weapon totems can now be used at the same time."* The [[Windfury Weapon]] and [[Flametongue Weapon]] tooltips may still show the old line.
## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Icy Veins says the same of Enhancement: the differences *"are so small that it comes down to preference and utility."*
**Alliance — Dwarf, and it is forced**, being the only Alliance Shaman. `Artisan's Precision` adds 1% hit with spells and attacks, and `Stoneform` removes Bleed, Poison and Disease effects and cuts physical damage taken by 10% for 8 seconds.
**Orc.** `Blood Fury` gives +10% attack power and spell power for 15 seconds on a 2-minute cooldown, so it serves all three specs. `Hardiness` shortens stuns on you by 20%.
**Troll.** `Berserking` gives 10% attack and casting speed for 15 seconds. It no longer grows as your health drops.
**Tauren.** `Endurance` is 5% Health and nothing else, `War Stomp` is a 2-second stun, and `Plainsrunning` builds 2% run speed a stack to 60% while unmounted, until damage or standing still wears it down.
**Skyborne.** `Wind Blessed` is a flat 2% haste to melee, ranged and spellcasting alike, and `Elemental Insight` adds 5% damage against Elementals. `Skysight` gives 15% run speed for 15 seconds, or 30% while Charged, an hour-long buff from using it at an elemental convergence. Wowhead leans Skyborne for Elemental and Orc for Restoration, and Icy Veins leans Skyborne for Enhancement *"by a slim margin"*.
## Quality of life
- **The four totem class quests gate the class.** Earth at 4, Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30, and each gives you a totem item. Icy Veins' advice on the Water chain is *"Complete this over time between quests"*: for Dwarves it runs from Loch Modan to the Wetlands, Redridge, Ashenvale and Westfall. At least you have [[Ghost Wolf]] by then.
- **The Air quest itself is short. The chain after it is not.** The Tempest's Weapons rewards a class weapon, and Icy Veins calls it *"Difficult at Level 30"*: a Razorfen Kraul run, drops from zones around 35 to 40, and a gauntlet of elites.
- **Right-click a totem on the tracking bar to unsummon that one totem.** The bar shows each active totem and its time left; [[Totemic Recall]] clears all four and returns 25% of their Mana.
- **[[Call of the Elements]] is a 3-second cast** that costs the sum of all four totems. Totems drop on a 1-second global cooldown, so Icy Veins' advice is to drop singles on the way in and keep the Call for when you need all four.
- **Carry a shield for survival, not damage.** Blizzard removed `Shield Specialization`, so no talent pays for one. Enhancement uses a two-hander.
- **Expect leather for a long while.** The guide sites' levelling gear lists are leather through 30. One guide gave level 40 for Mail during the beta. Nothing we hold now confirms the level, so check your trainer.
- **The Legacy perk `Talented` starts your talent points early**, at level 9 down to level 5 by rank, with the cap still 51. Every talent level on these pages assumes you do not have it.
- **The Legacy perk `Permanence` doubles long buffs**, and reading that onto totems — five minutes becomes ten — is ours, not a source's.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes, not 5** | One refresh an hour, and an imbue now runs **alongside** a sharpening stone, weight stone or oil, which Classic never allowed |
| **Buff totems last 5 minutes and reach 30 yards** | "Per pull" is no longer the unit. Every buff totem reaches 30 yards with no talent spent, which is what `Totemic Mastery` used to grant. That talent is gone from the Restoration tree |
| **The totem bar** | Three saveable four-totem loadouts, one button to drop a set, one to pick them all up for **25% of their Mana back**, and one to bring them to where you stand |
| **Windfury and Flametongue totems are auras** | Raid-wide, players only, and since November 12 they work alongside your own imbue. No more totem twisting |
| [[Disease Cleansing Totem]] now lasts its full 5 minutes | Like every other buff totem |
| [[Fire Nova]] is no longer a totem | It bursts around your active fire totem instead |
| `Improved Ghost Wolf` makes it instant and indoor-usable | Vanilla never allowed either. It is a row-4 Enhancement talent, 25 at the earliest |
| Alliance Shaman exist | Dwarf only |
## At level 60
**What is published.** Wowhead and Icy Veins both carry level-60 raid and dungeon builds for all three specs, dated November 4, and the spec pages give them. The 31-point capstones are `Lightning Mastery`, `Rage of the Farseer` and [[Riptide]], each at 40.
**What every group wants from you.** [[Windfury Totem]] for melee, Strength of Earth and Grace of Air beside it, and the cleansing, Tremor and Grounding totems when a fight calls for them. Wowhead's Enhancement overview: *"every melee group will require at least one Shaman"* for Windfury Totem.
**What is not known.** Raids open December 9 with Barrow Deeps for 10 players, Hyjal Summit for 20 and Onyxia's Lair for 40, so no raid log exists. Enhancement's builds and stat lists have not been republished since November 12. Blizzard has said it will tune classes on Tuesdays when needed.
