Alliance Shaman exist, Windfury was rebuilt from the ground up, and the widely-repeated claim that Blizzard ruled out Shaman tanking has no Blizzard source.
## Who can be a Shaman
**Alliance Shaman are Dwarf, and only Dwarf.** Horde Shaman are Orc, Tauren, Troll and Skyborne — not Undead.
The Skyborne split is the elegant part, and Blizzard justified it with lore: *"Horde-aligned Skyborne embrace elemental traditions and gain access to the Shaman class, while Alliance-aligned Skyborne pursue the arcane legacy of their ancestors and gain access to the Mage class."* One people, shared ancestry, divided by how they answered a crisis — the wind spirits vanished from Zephras Isle, and the Windshapers want them restored while the High Order concluded that dependence on elemental patrons had left them vulnerable. Faction is a character-creation choice, not a race choice.
For **Dwarf** Shaman, by contrast, no lore justification appears anywhere. It is simply listed.
## Shaman tanking: the honest answer
**There is no primary Blizzard statement that Shaman cannot tank, and probably none exists.**
This is worth stating carefully, because the claim circulates as though it were official. A staff-flag audit across sixteen threads and roughly six hundred posts — every Shaman-tank and tank-roster thread in the Forever categories — found **zero posts from any Blizzard account**. The community manager's complete Forever posting history contains nine topics, none about tanking or Shaman. Blizzard's Deep Dive recap never enumerates tank classes at all.
The "only Warrior, Paladin and Bear Druid" line traces to a *player's* post titled "Only 3 Tank Classes feels like a mistake" — his own inference from the announced trees. The strongest evidence it is not official is the community's own behaviour: a thread from 23 September asks Blizzard to "clarify what classes are designed to be tanks" and calls the situation "rumors or observations". It got no reply.
**What the client data says, and it does lean against a supported tank spec:**
- **Shield Specialization is removed from the Enhancement tree** — the block-chance and block-value talent, gone. Also removed: Weapon Mastery, Enhancing Totems, Two-Handed Axes and Maces.
- No taunt, no defensive cooldown, no Defense-stat support anywhere in the trees. Surviving mitigation is Anticipation, Toughness — now **+2% Stamina per rank, no longer armour** — and Elemental Warding.
- Uncrittable is 440 Defense, and nothing in the Shaman kit helps reach it.
**But the threat talents are real, which is why players keep asking.** Spirit Weapons: *"Gives a chance to parry enemy melee attacks, reduces all threat generated by your attacks by 30% while Rockbiter Weapon is **not** active, and increases all threat generated by 30% while Rockbiter Weapon **is** active."* A ±30% threat swing keyed to your weapon imbue is a tanking-shaped design. There is a second in **Restoration** — Natural Grace now reduces the threat of your *spells*, widened from healing-only — and a third indirectly, since Lightning Overload's free copies generate no threat.
The practical trap: **check your imbue before group damage, because Rockbiter reverses the talent's benefit.** And Earth Shock still functions as a soft taunt through sheer threat.
Careful reads conclude "not a raid tank" without ruling out dungeons. The warmest: Spirit Weapons *"alone allows Enhancement Shamans to perform off-tanking duties, or serve as tanks for dungeons in a pinch"*. In practice at the cap: *"Playing a Shaman up to 20, I primarily find myself tanking in dungeons simply because that's the role most are unwilling to perform."*
## Totems were genuinely reworked
And notably, without adding a totem bar to the action bars.
- **Four new management spells.** Call of the Elements drops up to four totems from a configurable bar, with **Call of the Ancestors** and **Call of the Spirits** as further independent loadouts. **Totemic Recall** refunds 25% of the mana. **Totemic Projection** relocates your active totems at 30 yards.
- **Totem GCD is 1 second, not 1.5.**
- **Ranges went 20 to 30 yards and durations 2 to 5 minutes** across the set.
- Totems remain **party-only, not raid-wide**.
- **Twisting survives**, and the anti-stacking rules are the design position: Tranquil Air, Windfury and Grace of Air do not stack even across different Shamans. Note the asymmetry — Paladin seal twisting was deliberately removed while Shaman totem twisting was left in.
- **Fire Nova is no longer a totem.** It is a 30-yard cast that detonates around your *active* Fire Totem.
## Windfury was rebuilt
**Windfury Totem stopped being a weapon enchant and became an area aura.** The old Classic Era enchant chain no longer exists in the client. The consequences are real: it now procs off **any** melee auto-attack or damaging special from **either hand**, where it was main-hand only; its internal cooldown is **100 milliseconds** rather than 1.5 seconds; it **cannot proc from other procs**; and swapping your main-hand weapon no longer drops it.
**Windfury Weapon grants two genuine extra swings**, not Burning Crusade-style instant special hits. Because they are ordinary white swings, glancing blows, weapon procs and swing-timer resets all apply. **Completely untested** — it is trained at 30 and the cap is 20.
All weapon imbues went from **5 minutes to 60**, which is among the most-praised changes in the class.
## Talents
Trees grew from 15/16/15 to **16/18/16** talents. **Removed entirely:** Elemental Mastery, Totemic Mastery, Nature's Guidance, Enhancing Totems, Shield Specialization, Two-Handed Axes and Maces, Weapon Mastery, Improved Weapon Totems.
The new 16-point row is the cleanest illustration of what the extra milestone is for, because **two former 31-point capstones were pulled down into it**:
| Spec | 11 | **16** | 21 | 31 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elemental | Elemental Focus | **Call of Thunder** | Earthbound | **Lava Burst** |
| Enhancement | Shamanistic Focus | **Stormstrike**, was tier 7 | Spirit Weapons | **Rage of the Farseer** |
| Restoration | Water Shield | **Mana Tide Totem**, was tier 7 | Nature's Swiftness | **Riptide** |
**Enhancement** gains **Maelstrom Weapon** — five stacks, each cutting the cast time and mana of your next **Lightning Bolt only**, so five stacks is instant and free. Mental Dexterity and Mental Quickness convert Intellect into attack power and spell damage. **Thundering Strikes widened from melee-only to all spells and attacks.**
**Restoration** gains Mindfulness, Water Shield and Riptide. **Healing Way was rebuilt** from a stacking chance-based buff into a flat **+8% per rank with no ramp**. **Tidal Mastery was narrowed to healing crit only**, a real loss for a Resto shaman dipping into damage.
## Abilities
**Stormstrike is rebuilt:** an instant strike that increases the damage of your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock by 20% for 12 seconds. Vanilla buffed the next *two* sources of Nature damage — a shared, raid-contended resource. It is now personal and timed, and the **cooldown dropped from 20 seconds to 8**.
**Lava Burst** is the Elemental capstone: +20% if your Flame Shock is on the target, 2.5-second cast, 10-second cooldown. It is a **damage bonus, not a guaranteed crit** — players are asking for the later-expansion guaranteed crit and have not got it.
**Direct damage values fell steeply, and this is not a nerf in isolation.** Lightning Bolt rank 10 went from 419–467 to **185–207**, with its cast cut to 2.5 seconds. Chain Lightning rank 4 went from 493–551 to **116–130**. Forever deliberately moved caster power off spell ranks and onto gear spell power, so casters must keep upgrading rather than coasting on rank.
**Riptide** is instant on a 6-second cooldown and boosts Chain Heal cast *directly on that target* by 25% — note it does **not** consume Riptide the way later expansions do.
**Improved Ghost Wolf now makes it instant *and* usable indoors.** **There is no Dual Wield for Shaman in Forever**, and the removal of the two-handed talent establishes neither lost access nor gained dual wield.
## Rotation
**At 20, Enhancement:** Rockbiter solo but **Flametongue in groups** for the threat reduction, Lightning Shield up before the pull, rank-1 Lightning Bolt to pull, Earth Shock solo but **alternate Flame Shock and Frost Shock in groups to avoid threat problems**, Fire Nova with an active Fire Totem. Fire Nova is the AoE payoff and is brutally mana-hungry.
**At 30**, Enhancement has **neither Windfury nor Maelstrom**, so it is autos and shocks.
**At 60**, projected from full trees rather than tested: **Elemental** maintains Flame Shock, Lava Burst on cooldown, Lightning Bolt filler — noting that Fire and Nature are **distinct investments, not interchangeable**. **Enhancement** is bolt-weaving: build Maelstrom on autos, spend five stacks on an instant free Lightning Bolt, Stormstrike every 8 seconds to buff the next bolt. **Restoration** primes with Riptide then Chain Heals that target.
## Stats
The unified hit and crit change names Enhancement explicitly. **But the precision every guide glosses:** only the stat **printed on the item** is merged. **Agility → melee crit and Intellect → spell crit remain separate per-class conversions** — merging those would put Enhancement near 80% crit. So Enhancement still cannot buy spell crit with Agility.
**Strength gives Shamans 1 block value per 20 points**, same as Warriors and Paladins, plus 2 attack power.
**Intellect is the Enhancement story:** mana, attack power via Mental Dexterity, spell crit, and spell damage via Mental Quickness — four functions from one stat. Agility is now the weakest melee stat for Shaman because it carries no attack-power component.
Published priorities: **Elemental** Spell Power, Hit, Haste, Intellect, Crit. **Enhancement** Hit and Expertise, Strength, Intellect, Agility, Crit — though neither Hit nor Expertise appears on any beta gear yet. **Restoration** Healing Power, Intellect, MP5, Spirit, Haste, Crit.
## Race
**Alliance: Dwarf, because it is the only option.** Mace Specialization's +1% crit is now live for all three specs since crit is unified. **Stoneform now reduces physical damage taken** rather than granting armour, making it useful to a caster for the first time.
**Horde splits cleanly by spec.** **Orc** for Enhancement — Blood Fury is now +10% attack power *and* spell power. **Tauren** is newly attractive for both damage specs purely because **Endurance now grants +1% hit**. **Troll**'s Berserking is a flat +10% casting and attack speed. **Windshaper Skyborne** is the pick for Elemental and Restoration, because Wind Blessed's passive +1% haste is valuable precisely *because* haste is otherwise scarce.
## The live controversy is not tanking
It is the 24 September Elemental tree swap, and Enhancement is angry. Blizzard swapped Elemental Fury from row 3 to row 6, and conceded the side effect outright: *"We understand that this affects Enhancement Shaman's critical strike damage with spells, but we will use this room created to add more to Enhancement Shaman in the future."* Pushing Elemental Fury out of dip range is argued to have destroyed the two-handed spell-Enhancement build.
**The biggest design objection is totem twisting**, put well in the European feedback thread: *"Totems should support the rotation. They shouldn't become the rotation."* Other unmet asks: Maelstrom Weapon should work with Chain Lightning; Lava Burst should be a guaranteed crit and baseline rather than a capstone; dual wield should be added without removing two-handers.
Universally liked: the 60-minute imbues, indoor Ghost Wolf, 30-yard five-minute totems, the call and recall suite, and Riptide. On tanking the mood is persistent and unanswered across sixteen threads, framed against Season of Discovery — which *did* ship Shaman tanks.
## Still unknown
Chain Heal's jump count and falloff. Whether Enhancement keeps two-handed access. **Whether Stoneclaw Totem taunts** — one guide says yes, a beta player insists it only builds small threat, and the client text is vanilla's threat-based version. Haste's mechanics, since it is on no gear yet.
One caution: a search for Shaman block talents will confidently return a +1% block chance and +5% block value talent. **That is vanilla's Shield Specialization, which Forever removed.**
