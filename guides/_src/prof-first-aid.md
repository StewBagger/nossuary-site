**First Aid makes the healing potions now.** The six standard healing potions moved here from Alchemy. It also gained cures for disease and bleeds, next to the bandages and anti-venom it always made.
:::scope
Ranks, trainers, recipe skill levels and the levelling routes are Wowhead's (November 10) and Icy Veins' (November 3), which agree except where noted. Camp values are Blizzard's (October 21) and the client's. The recipe count is foreverchanges.pro's reading of the launch client. **Thin:** several manuals have no confirmed drop or vendor, and the sites disagree on who teaches the last three bandages. ## Is First Aid worth it?
It costs no primary slot, so the question is only whether to spend the cloth.
Icy Veins (November 3) says it is worth learning on any character, and that Warriors and Rogues get particular value from bandages while levelling, since they can heal between pulls without stopping to eat.
Wowhead (November 10) says it is "particularly useful for non-Healers".
**A class with no dispel can now clear disease and bleeds** off a secondary skill, as well as poison.
**You do not need First Aid to drink the potions.** Anyone can buy and use them. Bandages do need the skill, on the person applying them.
## What it makes
**Bandages**, as in Classic, from Linen to `Heavy Runecloth Bandage`. That one needs 290 skill to craft and 225 to use, and restores 2,000 health over 8 seconds (Icy Veins). The target cannot be bandaged again for 1 minute.
The client still carries a [[Crystal Infused Bandage]] recipe. Neither site lists it and it has no known source, so plan on Heavy Runecloth as the top bandage.
**Healing potions.** They heal instantly and share the 2-minute potion cooldown:
| Skill | Potion | Restores | Recipe from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[Minor Healing Potion]] | 70 to 90 | Trainer |
| 55 | `Lesser Healing Potion` | 140 to 180 | Trainer |
| 110 | `Healing Potion` | 280 to 360 | Trainer |
| 155 | `Greater Healing Potion` | 455 to 585 | Manual, drops from Syndicate Mercenaries in Arathi Highlands |
| 215 | `Superior Healing Potion` | 700 to 900 | Manual, source unconfirmed |
| 275 | [[Major Healing Potion]] | 1,050 to 1,750 | Manual, sold by Pexmit in Mount Hyjal |
They cost herbs, a vial and spices. Minor is a Peacebloom, an Empty Vial and a Mild Spices.
**Cures.** Each tier removes effects up to a level, and all three kinds share a 1-minute cooldown:
| Skill | Item | Cures | In combat? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 80 / 130 / 215 / 300 | [[Anti-Venom]], Strong, Potent, Powerful | Poison up to level 25 / 35 / 45 / 65 | **Yes** |
| 90 / 140 / 210 / 280 | [[Simple Poultice]], Clever, Superior, [[Powerful Poultice]] | Disease up to level 25 / 35 / 45 / 65 | No |
| 120 / 200 / 265 | [[Woolen Tourniquet]], Leather, [[Surgical Tourniquet]] | Bleeds up to level 30 / 45 / 65 | No |
Anti-venom works mid-fight. Poultices and tourniquets only work out of combat, so use them between pulls.
The first tier of each is trained. The rest come from manuals, and Icy Veins has confirmed only some:
- **Strong Anti-Venom**: world drop.
- **Potent Anti-Venom**: Lost One Muckdwellers, Swamp of Sorrows.
- **Powerful Anti-Venom**: Argent Dawn quartermasters at Honored. It needs 300 First Aid to use.
- **Clever Poultice**: Defias Enchanters, Duskwood.
- **The other poultice and tourniquet manuals**: not yet confirmed.
## Ranks and where to learn them
| Rank | Skill cap | Needs | How |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice | 75 | Nothing | Any First Aid trainer |
| Journeyman | 150 | Skill 50 | Any First Aid trainer |
| Expert | 225 | Skill 125 | The book *Expert First Aid - Under Wraps* |
| Artisan | 300 | Skill 225, level 35 | The quest *Triage* |
- **Trainers** stand in every capital, in the first town of each starting zone, in Menethil Harbor and Dalaran, and at Valanaar and Shen'dar Village on Zephras Isle.
- **The Expert book** is sold by Deneb Walker in Stromgarde Keep, Arathi Highlands (Alliance), and Balai Lok'Wein in Brackenwall Village, Dustwallow Marsh (Horde). It is tradeable.
- **Buy two manuals on the same trip**: *Manual: Heavy Silk Bandage* and *Manual: Mageweave Bandage*. You read them at 180 and 210.
- **Triage** comes from Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen on Theramore Isle (Alliance) or Doctor Gregory Victor in Hammerfall (Horde). Save 15 patients before 6 die. The doctor supplies the bandages. Treat Critically Injured first, then Badly Injured, then Injured.
## Levelling it
**The bandage route**, which Wowhead calls usually the cheapest. Craft counts are Wowhead's:
1. **1 → 40**: Linen Bandage, 45 crafts. 1 Linen Cloth each
2. **40 → 80**: Heavy Linen Bandage, 65. 2 Linen Cloth each
3. **80 → 115**: Wool Bandage, 50. 1 Wool Cloth each
4. **115 → 150**: Heavy Wool Bandage, 50. 2 Wool Cloth each
5. **150 → 180**: Silk Bandage, 45. 1 Silk Cloth each
6. **180 → 210**: Heavy Silk Bandage, 45. 2 Silk Cloth each
7. **210 → 240**: Mageweave Bandage, 50. 1 Mageweave Cloth each. Stop at 225 for *Triage*
8. **240 → 260**: Heavy Mageweave Bandage, 30. 2 Mageweave Cloth each
9. **260 → 290**: Runecloth Bandage, 50. 1 Runecloth each
10. **290 → 300**: Heavy Runecloth Bandage, 15. 2 Runecloth each
Wowhead's shopping list for the whole route is 175 Linen, 150 Wool, 135 Silk, 110 Mageweave and 80 Runecloth.
**The potion route to 150** is Icy Veins' (November 3). It trades cloth for herbs, and you can drink or sell what you make:
1. **1 → 75**: Minor Healing Potion, about 85 crafts. Peacebloom, Empty Vial, Mild Spices
2. **75 → 110**: Lesser Healing Potion, about 50. Briarthorn, Empty Vial, 2 Mild Spices
3. **110 → 150**: Healing Potion, about 45. Bruiseweed, Leaded Vial, Hot Spices
4. **150 onward**: switch to the bandage route at step 5
Notes:
- **Train ahead of each cap.** Journeyman from 50, the Expert book from 125.
- **Who teaches the bandages at 240, 260 and 290 is disputed.** Wowhead (November 10) says the Triage doctors teach all three, so bring the cloth with you. Icy Veins (November 3) says that is Classic's arrangement and is unconfirmed in Forever.
- **Icy Veins' craft counts run lower past 225**: 25, 25, 45 and 15 for the last four steps.

## What Forever changed
- **31 recipes, 17 of them new** (foreverchanges.pro, launch client).
- **Healing potions moved here from Alchemy.** Alchemy keeps only the Discolored line, which heals and then deals 75% of it back. See [Alchemy](/guides/wow-forever/professions/alchemy/).
- **Poultices and tourniquets are new.** Classic First Aid cured poison only. `Potent Anti-Venom` is a new tier of that.
- **Camp objects**, above.
- **`Field Medicine`**, a Legacy perk with two ranks, cuts Recently Bandaged by up to 10 seconds. It is *"ineffective in dungeons, raids, and battlegrounds"*, so it is a solo and levelling perk.
- **First Aid earns no Legacy Points.** Wowhead lists no First Aid challenges.
