**The most changed secondary skill in Forever**, with 17 of its 31 recipes new. It is no longer just bandages: First Aid now cures poison, disease and bleeds, and makes healing potions.
:::scope
Recipe names and skill levels come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. One open question has real consequences: nobody has published whether Alchemy keeps its own healing potions now that First Aid makes them. Trainer rank thresholds are unpublished.
:::
## Is First Aid worth it?
It costs no primary slot. Forever added cures for poison, disease and bleeds and craftable healing potions to what was only a bandage skill. That makes it worth taking, and materially more so than in Classic *(ours, not a source's)*.
If your class has no dispel, you can now buy your way out of poison, disease and bleed effects off a free secondary skill. That was not possible in vanilla.
For a Warrior or a Rogue it is still **the only self-heal for a long time**, because neither class has one. Take it at level 1 and keep it current *(ours, not a source's)*.
## What it makes
**Bandages**, as in Classic, topping out at the new [[Crystal Infused Bandage]] at skill 285, which heals 2500 over 10 seconds.
**Cures are the new category**, and the reason to care:
| Skill | Item | Cures | In combat? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 80 / 130 / 215 / 300 | [[Anti-Venom]] | Poison, up to level 25 / 35 / 45 / 65 | **Yes** |
| 90 / 140 / 210 / 280 | [[Simple Poultice]] → [[Powerful Poultice]] | Disease | No |
| 120 / 200 / 265 | [[Woolen Tourniquet]] → [[Surgical Tourniquet]] | Bleeds | No |
Anti-Venom works mid-fight; poultices and tourniquets do not, so plan those two lines for between pulls.
**Healing potions**, from [[Minor Healing Potion]] at skill 55 up to [[Major Healing Potion]] at 275, which restores 1050 to 1750.

## What Forever changed
- **31 recipes, 17 of them new**: proportionally the largest overhaul of any profession.
- **Three categories that did not exist:** curatives for poison, disease and bleeds; craftable healing potions; and camp objects.
- **`Field Medicine`**, a Legacy perk in the Adventure tree, cuts the Recently Bandaged cooldown by 5 or 10 seconds across two ranks. It is *"ineffective in dungeons, raids, and battlegrounds"* — solo and levelling only.
## What is not known
Whether **Alchemy still makes healing potions** now that First Aid does. If it does not, Alchemy loses much of its value, and nobody has published it either way.
Trainer rank thresholds, as for every profession except Fishing.
