Herbs, for Alchemy and now for First Aid's healing potions. **The 28 Classic herbs keep their skill requirements.** Forever added three endgame herbs, three scarce dye materials and four tiers of seeds, and it rewrote Tauren Cultivation.
:::scope
Herb requirements, zones and the levelling brackets are Wowhead's (November 9, to 225) and Icy Veins' (November 3 and 4, to 300). The two agree on the brackets. Camp values are Blizzard's (October 21) and the client's. Cultivation is Blizzard's own notes. **Thin:** Demonsage and Marefoil have no published node, seed yields are unpublished, and Death Lotus is tied to Hyjal, whose raids open December 9.

## Is Herbalism worth taking?
Wowhead (November 9) calls it "a great option for making gold, particularly when you are leveling", because you pass herb nodes all the way to 60 and more crafting professions use herbs than before.
Icy Veins (November 3) frames it by what you craft: if you make potions or flasks every week, picking the herbs yourself saves a lot of gold. It also suggests putting it on a farming character to keep two crafting professions on your main.
**It earns no Legacy Points.** Wowhead lists no Herbalism Legacy challenges; only crafting professions have them.
The camp object is the [[Incense Candle]] at skill 20, an Intellect buff for everyone sitting nearby. The values are in the table below.
## What it pairs with
- **Alchemy.** Icy Veins names it the best pairing, since most Alchemy recipes use herbs.
- **Mining.** Wowhead says it is the profession most often taken alongside, because you gather both on the same trip.
- **First Aid** now uses herbs for healing potions, so a herbalist feeds that too.
- **Tauren** get `Cultivation`, described below.

Thresholds are Icy Veins' (November 4). Its table gives no character-level requirement for Herbalism.
- **One trainer teaches all four ranks.** Every general Herbalism trainer does, in the capitals, in towns such as Lakeshire, Tarren Mill and Booty Bay, and at Valanaar and Shen'dar Village on Zephras Isle.
- **Starting-area trainers teach Apprentice only**: Northshire, Coldridge Valley, Shadowglen, Valley of Trials, Red Cloud Mesa and Deathknell.
- **Or skip the trainer.** An early starting-zone quest offers the manual *Wild Harvest* as a reward choice. It teaches Apprentice Herbalism if you have a primary slot free.
Learning the skill gives you Find Herbs. No tool is needed.
## Levelling it
Both sites publish the same brackets. Wowhead stops at 225, so steps 6 and 7 are Icy Veins' alone. The number after each herb is the skill it needs:
1. **1 → 70**: Peacebloom (1), Silverleaf (1), Earthroot (15). Any starting zone, Zephras Isle included
2. **70 → 115**: Mageroyal (50), Briarthorn (70), Stranglekelp (85, underwater). Darkshore, Loch Modan, Silverpine Forest, The Barrens
3. **115 → 170**: Bruiseweed (100), Wild Steelbloom (115), Kingsblood (125), Liferoot (150). Hillsbrad Foothills, Wetlands, Stonetalon Mountains
4. **170 → 205**: Fadeleaf (160), Goldthorn (170), Khadgar's Whisker (185). Stranglethorn Vale, Arathi Highlands
5. **205 → 230**: Firebloom (205), Purple Lotus (210), Wintersbite (195, Alterac Mountains only). Tanaris, Searing Gorge, Alterac Mountains
6. **230 → 270**: Sungrass (230), Ghost Mushroom (245, in caves), Golden Sansam (260). Feralas, The Hinterlands
7. **270 → 300**: Dreamfoil (270), Mountain Silversage (280), Plaguebloom (285). Felwood, Eastern Plaguelands
Train the next rank before each cap: Journeyman from 50, Expert from 125, Artisan from 200.
**How long a herb keeps giving points.** Both sites agree a herb gives a point every time for 25 points past its requirement and turns grey 100 points past it. They disagree on when it turns green. Wowhead (November 9) prints 65 points past, and says Forever moved this later than Classic. Icy Veins (November 3) prints 50, Classic's figure.

## What Forever changed
- **The 28 Classic herbs are unchanged** in what skill they need (Wowhead).
- **Three endgame herbs: `Demonsage`, `Marefoil` and `Death Lotus`.** All three go into Alchemy's four new raid flasks. On Wowhead's recipe lists Death Lotus is also a reagent in Enchanting, Engineering and Blacksmithing, and Marefoil in Engineering.
- **Demonsage and Marefoil are picked from nodes, and nobody has published where.** The client has a gathering spell for each. Wowhead (November 9) and Icy Veins (November 3) both list their locations as unknown.
- **Death Lotus is not a node.** A glove enchant, `Enchant Gloves - Lotus Claw`, gives "a small chance to acquire Death Lotus when gathering any herb in Hyjal". It is an [Enchanting](/guides/wow-forever/professions/enchanting/) formula that needs skill 295 in the client. No site lists where the formula comes from.
- **Three scarce materials come as bonus loot from ordinary herbs**: `Frilled Lichen`, `Rosecap` and `Stranglevine`. Alchemists turn them into Cerulean, Magenta and Viridian Dye. Wowhead's node table shows Frilled Lichen on every herb from Earthroot to Kingsblood except Grave Moss. It places Rosecap and Stranglevine on no node yet.
- **`Bountiful Harvest`**, a Legacy perk with 5 ranks, raises your chance at those scarce materials. At full rank the client reads "100% more likely".
- **Seeds drop from herb nodes too.** There are four tiers: Commonplace, Uncommon, Unusual and Scarce Herb Seeds. Wowhead shows Commonplace seeds on Peacebloom, Silverleaf and Earthroot, and Uncommon on Briarthorn and Bruiseweed. Each reads: "Grow in a Greenhouse or combine at a Seed Hybridizer at a camp to unlock its full potential." What a seed grows into is unpublished.
- **Tauren `Cultivation` is an active ability now.** Classic gave a passive +15 Herbalism. Forever gives: *"Cultivate a nearby herb, growing a duplicate you can harvest without requiring Herbalism skill. Each herb may only be cultivated once."* The cooldown is 15 minutes.
- **Cultivation has a level requirement.** You need a character level of the herb's skill requirement divided by 5, and level 60 for any herb above 300 skill.
- **Its cooldown now starts when the cast completes** (Blizzard, November 9), so an interrupted cast no longer costs you the 15 minutes.
- **Herbs fill supply crates.** *Waylaid Crate: Apprentice Herbs* takes 20 Peacebloom, 20 Silverleaf, 10 Earthroot, 10 Mageroyal or 10 Briarthorn, and pays [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/). The crate needs level 10 to use.
- **Crates now drop inside dungeons.** Blizzard fixed that on November 9, along with Craftsman's Writs.
- **There is no Herbalism specialisation.**
## Camp objects
| Skill | Object | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Incense Candle]] | Intellect to everyone sitting nearby, by the sitter's level: **2 below level 14, 6 from 14, 12 from 28, 18 from 42, 25 from 56**. Exclusive with [[Arcane Intellect]]. Trained. Costs a Peacebloom and a Silverleaf |
| 140 | [[Greenhouse]] | Grows herbs over time from planted seeds, and gives the candle's buff. Blueprint drops from Shadetooth in Excavation Site: Wetlands. Costs 2 Simple Wood, an Arthas' Tears and a Purple Lotus |
| 300 | [[Seed Hybridizer]] | Multiplies seeds or combines them into rarer tiers, and gives the candle's buff. Blueprint source unknown. Costs 4 Mountain Silversage, a Crystal Vial and 2 Runecloth |
Blizzard prints the candle as "2-25 increased Intellect (level dependent), mutually exclusive with Arcane Intellect". The steps between are the client's. A Mage's buff and the candle do not add together.
Blizzard says camp items at skill 140 and above drop in dungeons and raids. No site has found the Seed Hybridizer Blueprint.
