Mage is a cloth caster with no self-heal whose answers to trouble are [[Frost Nova]], [[Blink]] and [[Polymorph]]. Forever added a Research and scroll system from level 6, and gave Arcane two new spells, [[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage`; Icy Veins heads its Arcane levelling section *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day, so **no level-60 Mage build has been tested.** Ability training levels below come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client. Note that caster base damage was cut across the board and spell coefficients raised to compensate, a lower tooltip number than Classic is usually not a nerf. Neither Wowhead nor ClassicWoW.gg publishes a levelling score for the three specs. Icy Veins is the only one of the three that rates them, and it gives both a number out of five and a heading, so this page reports both.
:::
## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Levelling | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Arcane](/guides/wow-forever/mage/arcane/)** | Ranged DPS | **Icy Veins 4/5 — "A Solid Leveling Spec"** | [[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage`, both new — ClassicWoW.gg: *"build around Arcane-school damage itself"* |
| **[Fire](/guides/wow-forever/mage/fire/)** | Ranged DPS | Icy Veins 2.5/5 — "Usable, But Better Options Are Available" | `Heating Up` (renamed from `Hot Streak` on October 1) cuts [[Pyroblast]]'s cast from 6 seconds to 1.5 at 3 stacks |
| **[Frost](/guides/wow-forever/mage/frost/)** | Ranged DPS | **Icy Veins 3.5/5 — "Smooth and Steady, but Not Fast"** | Freeze, then [[Ice Lance]] for +300%. Icy Veins: *"slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe"* |
**Arcane first, Frost second**, on the only numbers any of the three publishes: Icy Veins scores Arcane **4/5** for levelling against Frost's **3.5** and Fire's **2.5**. Its Frost guide heads the leveling section *"Smooth and Steady, but Not Fast"*: *"Frost Mages are going to have a comfortable time leveling. They might not be able to burst enemies down as quickly as other classes and Mage specs, but slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe."* [[Frostbite]] often kills melee enemies before they reach you. Arcane gets the more enthusiastic heading, *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*, outright: *"Arcane Mages in Forever level up very well."* Arcane is the genuinely new option: Forever gave it `Arcane Meditation` at **50% mana regeneration while casting** (Classic gave 15%), and Icy Veins names that talent as its reason — it *"significantly reduces downtime between fights"*.
**Icy Veins rates Fire playable but slower**, in its own heading — *"Usable, But Better Options Are Available"* — and it names the reason: *"the Fire spec relies a lot on scoring Critical Strikes... at low levels, your Critical Strike chance is not very high and you only gain high levels of Critical Strike chance through gear at or near the Level 60 content."* Its payoff talents sit deep in the tree as well.
**One claim worth flagging rather than stating flat:** none of the three sources confirms or denies a target cap or damage falloff on Mage area spells in Forever. Treat that as genuinely open rather than settled either way. The slows attached to area damage were cut hard, though; `Improved Blizzard` maxes at **40%**, down from Classic's 65% (ClassicWoW.gg's talent tooltip: rank 3 reads "lowers the target's movement speed by 40% for 1.5 sec").
## Is Mage for you?
Play Mage if you want burst damage from range and can accept cloth armour, no self-heal and a mana bar you have to manage. *(Our own reading, not a source's.)*
:::strengths
- [[Ice Lance]] hits a frozen target for **+300%**, and `Heating Up` makes [[Pyroblast]] castable inside a fight from 25
- Conjures its own drink from level 4 and food from level 6, so neither is ever bought
- [[Polymorph]] at level 8 takes one enemy out of a fight entirely
:::
:::weaknesses
- Cloth armour, and the only armour spells are [[Frost Armor]], [[Ice Armor]] at 30 and [[Mage Armor]] at 34
- Mana regeneration stops while you cast until [[Mage Armor]] at 34 or Arcane's `Arcane Meditation`, both 50%
- No self-heal of any kind
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** The published level 20 rotations run five or six steps: a nuke, a freeze, a retreat, a wand finish.
**Is it forgiving?** No. You have cloth armour, no self-heal, and mana that does not regenerate while you cast.
**Does it level well?** Icy Veins scores the specs 4/5 Arcane, 3.5/5 Frost, 2.5/5 Fire. Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish no levelling score.
**Does it need a group?** No. Every spec has a published solo build, and Arcane and Frost also have published dungeon variants.
:::

## Which race
**Read this section in proportion.** Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Where racials genuinely decide things is **PvP**, and specifically the ones that break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm), `Escape Artist` (Gnome, roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance — Human or Gnome.** Human's `Sword Specialization` is **+2% spell and ability crit off a sword held purely as a stat stick**, which is a real change to the vanilla answer, though compare it honestly against a staff with better stats, because at levelling levels the staff often wins. Gnome brings `Expansive Mind` and `Eureka!`, but **as of October 1 `Eureka!` no longer benefits periodic effects at all** — a channeled cast like [[Arcane Missiles]] or [[Evocation]] doesn't count as a periodic, so those still get the bonus, but a damage-over-time tick (Fire's `Ignite`, for instance) no longer does. *(Known issue: Eureka currently doesn't affect every damaging or healing spell for some classes; Blizzard says it's fixed in a later build.)*
**Horde — Orc is new to Mage in Forever**, and `Blood Fury` now gives **+10% spell power**, so it is finally a caster racial. Its axe racial is dead weight, since Mages cannot use axes.
**Skyborne Mage is Alliance-only.** Troll's `Berserking` is now a flat +10% cast speed, and Undead's `Cannibalize` restores **mana as well as health**.
## Quality of life
- **Conjured water is free drink from level 4**, upgrading through the gem ladder, so there is never a reason to buy it.
- **Teleports come from a separate portal trainer** standing near your class trainer, and **every teleport consumes a Rune of Teleportation** at about nine and a half silver. **[[Slow Fall]] eats a Light Feather per cast**, carry a stack of both.
- **Pick up books from level 6.** Forever's new **Research** system unlocks from finding books in the world and returning them to librarians, and it pays real gear, a ring for twenty book donations, and a pendant from its quest. **Failing to decipher a scroll turns you into a sheep** until your skill improves. *(Known issue, October 1: some Mages who learn Research find themselves locked out of accessing it — that's a bug, not something you did; it's worth knowing before you chase it early.)*
- **Every Comprehension scroll lost move-casting on October 1.** All Mage scrolls from the Research system had a balance pass and can no longer be cast while moving — budget the stand-still time like any other cast.
- **Get a wand early** and use it instead of [[Fire Blast]].

## At level 60
Unknown. No source publishes a Mage build past 30, so whether Arcane's new engine holds up, whether Fire takes over once Pyroblast is usable, and how far the scroll system goes are all untested. Raids open December 9.
