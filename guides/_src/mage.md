Mage is a cloth caster with no self-heal whose answers to trouble are [[Frost Nova]], [[Blink]] and [[Polymorph]]. Forever added scrolls and a Research system from level 6. It also gave Arcane the new [[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage`, Fire a [[Pyroblast]] it can cast inside a fight, and Frost [[Ice Lance]].
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Mage build has been tested. Ability training levels below come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client. Caster base damage was cut across the board and spell coefficients raised to compensate, so a lower tooltip number than Classic is usually not a nerf. Neither Wowhead nor ClassicWoW.gg publishes a levelling score for the three specs. Icy Veins is the only one of the three that rates them, giving each a number out of five and a heading; those appear on each spec's own page. Blizzard's caveat covers its October 7 deep dive throughout: *"class abilities and talents may continue to evolve throughout the beta test and are subject to additional changes."*
:::
## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Arcane](/guides/wow-forever/mage/arcane/)** | Ranged DPS | `Arcane Meditation` keeps **50%** of mana regeneration running while you cast, where Classic gave 15% | [[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage`, both new — ClassicWoW.gg: *"build around Arcane-school damage itself"* |
| **[Fire](/guides/wow-forever/mage/fire/)** | Ranged DPS | `Heating Up` at 25: a non-periodic crit from your other Fire casts cuts [[Pyroblast]]'s cast 25%, so 3 stacks make it 1.5 s | Pure damage and nothing else, and `Improved Scorch`'s Fire vulnerability is personal, not shared |
| **[Frost](/guides/wow-forever/mage/frost/)** | Ranged DPS | `Frostbite` 3/3 gives every Chill effect a **15%** chance to freeze outright, which arms `Shatter` | Freeze, then [[Ice Lance]] for +300%. Icy Veins: *"slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe"* |
The three specs do different things, and Blizzard frames the pick as three answers to the same weakness: *"Arcane to manipulate and consume Mana for more damage. Fire to produce devastatingly heavy hits and critical damage. Or Frost to grant levels of combat control still unrivaled by any other class."* Each spec page carries Icy Veins' score and heading.
No source confirms or denies a **target cap or damage falloff** on Mage area spells in Forever, and Blizzard's deep dive does not mention one either.
The slows attached to area damage were cut hard, and Blizzard says why. `Improved Blizzard` now maxes at 40%, down from 65%, to stop Frost Mages farming instances, *"which was widely used by gold farmers and boosters to disrupt the economy of the game."* [[Cone of Cold]]'s duration was shortened for the same reason.
## Is Mage for you?
Play Mage if you want burst damage from range and can accept cloth armour, no self-heal and a mana bar you have to manage. *(Our own reading, not a source's.)*
Blizzard's own summary gives both halves. Mages *"remain one of the most powerful and resourceful damage dealing classes"* and *"masters of ranged area damage which is stronger and more varied than any other class"*. They are also *"extremely fragile and die easily if caught in melee combat"*, and the spec you pick is how you answer that.
:::strengths
- Two new damage engines land while levelling: Frost's [[Ice Lance]] at 20, Fire's `Heating Up` at 25
- Conjures its own drink from level 4 and food from level 6, so neither is ever bought
- [[Polymorph]] at level 8 takes one enemy out of a fight entirely — Blizzard: *"one of the best crowd control abilities"* in the game
:::
:::weaknesses
- Cloth armour, and the only armour spells are [[Frost Armor]], [[Ice Armor]] at 30 and [[Mage Armor]] at 34
- Mana regeneration stops while you cast until [[Mage Armor]] at 34 or Arcane's `Arcane Meditation`, both 50%
- No self-heal of any kind
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** The published level 20 rotations run five or six steps: a nuke, a freeze, a retreat, a wand finish.
**Is it forgiving?** No. You have cloth armour, no self-heal, and mana that does not regenerate while you cast.
**What carries you while levelling?** Conjured drink from level 4 and food from level 6, so you buy neither. [[Polymorph]] from 8 takes one enemy out of a fight.
**Does it need a group?** No. Every spec has a published solo build, and Arcane and Frost also have published dungeon variants.
:::

[[Detect Magic]] is the **only Mage spell deleted in Forever**.
[[Frostfire Bolt]] counts as both a Fire and a Frost spell: it takes the higher of your two spell damage bonuses and the lower of the target's two resistances. Its 3-second cast lands Fireball-sized damage plus a damage-over-time and a slow, and Blizzard's stated intent is to make mixed Frost-and-Fire builds work. **It is learned at 40, above the beta cap, so nobody has cast it.**
## Which race
**Read this section in proportion.** Race is the smallest choice on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and no race is a wrong answer for levelling *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP. The ones that matter there break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm), `Escape Artist` (Gnome, roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance — Human or Gnome.** Human's `Sword Specialization` gives +2% spell and ability crit off a sword held purely as a stat stick, a real change from the vanilla answer. Compare it against a staff with better stats anyway: at levelling levels the staff often wins. Gnome brings `Expansive Mind` and `Eureka!`. Blizzard's October 1 notes made `Eureka!` a flat 10% cost discount on every class. The Mage's own client entry covers your next 3 damaging abilities and carries no periodic restriction, where other classes' copies of it exclude periodic effects. A channeled cast like [[Arcane Missiles]] or [[Evocation]] benefits either way; whether a damage-over-time tick such as Fire's `Ignite` does is unsettled. *(Known issue: Eureka currently doesn't affect every damaging or healing spell for some classes; Blizzard says it's fixed in a later build.)*
**Horde — Orc is new to Mage in Forever.** `Blood Fury` now gives +10% spell power, so it is finally a caster racial. Its axe racial is dead weight: Mages cannot use axes.
**Skyborne Mage is Alliance-only.** Troll's `Berserking` is now a flat +10% cast speed, and Undead's `Cannibalize` restores mana as well as health.
## Quality of life
- Conjured water is **free drink from level 4**, upgrading through the gem ladder, so there is never a reason to buy it.
- **Teleports come from a separate portal trainer** standing near your class trainer, and every teleport consumes a Rune of Teleportation at about nine and a half silver. [[Slow Fall]] eats a Light Feather per cast, so carry a stack of both.
- **Loot scrolls, and pick up books from level 6.** Blizzard calls Comprehension a Mage-only minor tradeskill for deciphering scrolls dropped by creatures, and leaves the rewards for *"players to discover in their adventures"*. [[Comprehend Scroll]] sits in the General tab of your spellbook, and failing to decipher a scroll turns you into a sheep until your skill improves. The separate Research system is described by the guide sites and the client, and absent from Blizzard's deep dive. It unlocks from finding books in the world and returning them to librarians, and pays a ring for twenty donations and a pendant from its quest. *(Known issue, October 1: some Mages who learn Research find themselves locked out of accessing it. That is a bug, not something you did; check it works before you chase Research early.)*
- **Comprehension scrolls cannot be cast while moving.** Budget the stand-still time like any other cast.
- **Get a wand early** and use it instead of [[Fire Blast]].
- **Respeccing means a class trainer in a major city**, Blizzard's stated route for Legacy talents too. Dual Specialization arrives at level 40, above the beta cap and untested; below it you pay for every reset.

Unknown. No source publishes a Mage build past 30. Whether Arcane's new engine holds up, whether Fire takes over once Pyroblast is usable, and how far the scroll system goes are all untested. Raids open December 9.
