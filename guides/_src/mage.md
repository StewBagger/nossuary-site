Mage is a cloth caster with no self-heal whose answers to trouble are [[Frost Nova]], [[Blink]] and [[Polymorph]]. Forever added scrolls and a Research system from level 6. It also gave Arcane the new [[Arcane Blast]], [[Arcane Wrath]] and `Missile Barrage`, Fire a [[Pyroblast]] it can cast inside a fight, and Frost [[Ice Lance]].
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. The beta closed on October 23 at level 30, so nothing above 30 was tested by players; talents and spells past 30 come from the launch client and Blizzard's posts. These pages follow the hotfix Blizzard pushed to the launch realms on October 30, read from the game's hotfix data. Blizzard has not announced it, so it may still move before November 4. Where a spell's tooltip disagrees with the text here, trust the text: tooltips can lag behind a hotfix. Ability training levels below come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the launch client. Caster base damage was cut across the board and spell coefficients raised to compensate, so a lower tooltip number than Classic is usually not a nerf. Neither Wowhead nor ClassicWoW.gg publishes a levelling score for the three specs. Icy Veins is the only one of the three that rates them, giving each a number out of five and a heading; those appear on each spec's own page.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Arcane](/guides/wow-forever/mage/arcane/)** | Ranged DPS | `Arcane Meditation` keeps **50%** of mana regeneration running while you cast, where Classic gave 15% | [[Arcane Blast]], [[Arcane Wrath]] and `Missile Barrage`, all new — ClassicWoW.gg: *"build around Arcane-school damage itself"* |
| **[Fire](/guides/wow-forever/mage/fire/)** | Ranged DPS | `Heating Up` at 25: a non-periodic crit from your other Fire casts cuts [[Pyroblast]]'s cast 25%, so 3 stacks make it 1.5 s | Pure damage and nothing else *(ours)*, and `Improved Scorch`'s Fire vulnerability is personal, not shared |
| **[Frost](/guides/wow-forever/mage/frost/)** | Ranged DPS | `Frostbite` 3/3 gives every Chill effect a **15%** chance to freeze outright, which arms `Shatter` | Freeze, then [[Ice Lance]] for +300%. Icy Veins: *"slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe"* |
The three specs do different things, and Blizzard frames the pick as three answers to the same weakness: *"Arcane to manipulate and consume Mana for more damage. Fire to produce devastatingly heavy hits and critical damage. Or Frost to grant levels of combat control still unrivaled by any other class."* Each spec page carries Icy Veins' score and heading.
No source confirms or denies a **target cap or damage falloff** on Mage area spells in Forever, and Blizzard's deep dive does not mention one either.
The slows attached to area damage were cut hard, and Blizzard says why. `Improved Blizzard` maxes at 40%, where Classic reached 65%, to stop Frost Mages farming instances, *"which was widely used by gold farmers and boosters to disrupt the economy of the game."* [[Cone of Cold]]'s slow was shortened for the same reason, except on the first 4 targets it hits.
## Is Mage for you?
Play Mage if you want burst damage from range and can accept cloth armour, no self-heal and a mana bar you have to manage. *(Our own reading, not a source's.)*
Blizzard's own summary gives both halves. Mages *"remain one of the most powerful and resourceful damage dealing classes"* and *"masters of ranged area damage which is stronger and more varied than any other class"*. They are also *"extremely fragile and die easily if caught in melee combat"*, and the spec you pick is how you answer that.
:::strengths
- Two new damage engines land while levelling: Frost's [[Ice Lance]] at 20, Fire's `Heating Up` at 25
- Conjures its own drink from level 4 and food from level 6, so neither is ever bought
- [[Polymorph]] at level 8 takes one enemy out of a fight entirely — Blizzard: *"one of the best crowd control abilities"* in the game
:::
:::weaknesses
- Cloth armour, and the only armour spells are [[Frost Armor]], [[Ice Armor]] at 30 and [[Mage Armor]] at 34
- Mana regeneration stops while you cast until [[Mage Armor]] at 34 or Arcane's `Arcane Meditation`, both 50%
- No self-heal of any kind
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** The level 20 rotations on the spec pages run five or six steps: a nuke, a freeze, a retreat, a wand finish.
**Is it forgiving?** No *(ours)*. You have cloth armour, no self-heal, and mana that does not regenerate while you cast.
**What carries you while levelling?** Conjured drink from level 4 and food from level 6, so you buy neither. [[Polymorph]] from 8 takes one enemy out of a fight.
**Does it need a group?** No. Every spec levels solo. The builds published before launch predate the tree rework, so the spec pages mostly carry our own reworked lists, and Arcane adds a dungeon one.

`Detect Magic` is the **only Mage spell deleted in Forever**.
[[Frostfire Bolt]] counts as both a Fire and a Frost spell: it takes the higher of your two spell damage bonuses and the lower of the target's two resistances. Its 3-second cast lands about 60% of a [[Fireball]] up front, plus a damage-over-time and a slow, and Blizzard's stated intent is to make mixed Frost-and-Fire builds work. **It is learned at 40, which the beta never reached.**
## Which race
**Read this section in proportion.** Race is the smallest choice on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and no race is a wrong answer for levelling *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP. The ones that matter there break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm) and `Escape Artist` (Gnome, roots and snares). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below *(ours)*.
**Alliance — Human or Gnome.** Human's `Ruthless` gives +1% crit to all spells and attacks with any weapon, and `The Human Spirit` adds 10% Spirit. Neither depends on a weapon type, so pick weapons on their stats: at levelling levels the staff often wins. Gnome brings `Expansive Mind` and `Eureka!`. `Eureka!` is an 8-second buff: 10% lower Mana cost and 10% more damage on your damaging abilities. Blizzard says periodic effects do not benefit and channels do, so [[Arcane Missiles]] counts; the Mage tooltip does not spell out the periodic exclusion that other classes' copies do.
**Horde — Orc is new to Mage in Forever.** `Blood Fury` gives +10% spell power on a 2-minute cooldown, so it is finally a caster racial. `Command` does nothing for a Mage: it only buffs Hunter and Warlock pets.
**Skyborne Mage is Alliance-only.** Troll's `Berserking` is now a flat +10% cast speed for 15 seconds, and Undead's `Cannibalize` restores mana as well as health.
## Quality of life
- Conjured water is **free drink from level 4**, upgrading through the gem ladder, so there is never a reason to buy it.
- **Teleports come from a separate portal trainer** standing near your class trainer, and every teleport consumes a Rune of Teleportation at about nine and a half silver. [[Slow Fall]] eats a Light Feather per cast, so carry a stack of both.
- **Loot scrolls, and pick up books from level 6.** Blizzard calls Comprehension a Mage-only minor tradeskill for deciphering scrolls dropped by creatures, and says of the rewards only that *"players will discover in their adventures"*. [[Comprehend Scroll]] sits in the General tab of your spellbook, and failing to decipher a scroll turns you into a sheep until your skill improves. The separate Research system is described by the guide sites and the client, and absent from Blizzard's deep dive. It unlocks from finding books in the world and returning them to librarians, and pays a ring for twenty donations and a pendant from its quest. *(Blizzard listed a beta known issue that could lock Mages out of Research after learning it. If that happens to you it is a bug, not something you did.)*
- **Comprehension scrolls cannot be cast while moving.** Budget the stand-still time like any other cast.
- **Get a wand early** and use it instead of [[Fire Blast]].
- **Respeccing means a class trainer in a major city**, Blizzard's stated route for Legacy talents too. Dual Specialization arrives at level 40; below it you pay for every reset.

## At level 60
Nothing above 30 was played in the beta, so this is what the launch client shows rather than what players have found. Each tree's 31-point capstone arrives at level 40: `Arcane Power`, [[Combustion]] and [[Ice Barrier]]. Whether Arcane's new engine holds up, whether Fire takes over once Pyroblast is usable, and how far the scroll system goes are all still open. Raids open December 9.
