## Is Destruction worth levelling?
**Viable but distinctly slower, and Icy Veins is the only one of the three sources to score it.** Its ratings, out of 5:
| Spec | Overall | Levelling | Dungeon | Mobility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affliction | 5.0 | **5.0** | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Demonology | 5.0 | **5.0** | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| **Destruction** | **3.5** | **3.5** | 4.0 | **2.0** |
The reasons, in the sources' own words: *"You have less passive sustain than Affliction and less pet support than Demonology"* · *"Most of your damage requires you to stand still"*, and *"taking damage while casting causes spell pushback"* · *"[[Searing Pain]] generates significant threat, however, and this build does not have the threat reduction from `Suppression`"* · *"much of the specialization's new WoW Forever identity comes online at higher levels."*
**The wand argument is real, and Wowhead makes it about the class generally, not Destruction specifically**: *"you'll use your wand more for damage because it deals more DPS than Shadow Bolt, and it's also mana-free."* Destruction is the one spec that pushes back, because `Bane` cuts [[Shadow Bolt]]'s cast time, see Rotation below. **Forever also cut its numbers hard.** [[Conflagrate]] 84–106 where Classic gave 240–306; [[Soul Fire]] 334–420 against 623–783, while `Ruin` went from a 1-rank 100% talent to a 5-rank one that only reaches 100% at 5/5.
**In fairness, its own counter-argument:** *"Destruction does not need as much time to ramp as Affliction, making its direct damage useful when dungeon enemies are dying quickly."* Its **dungeon rating is 4.0, the same as the other two.** This is a soloing weakness.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11, and Icy Veins and Wowhead disagree on how much of the build is fixed.**
**Icy Veins'** build is fully specified: `Bane` 5, `Destructive Reach` 2, `Cataclysm` 3, [[Shadowburn]] 1. *"`Improved Shadow Bolt` and `Aftermath` are both strong alternatives, but this build prioritizes reliable leveling benefits that do not depend on Critical Strike, since we won't have much at this level."*
**Wowhead commits to far less.** Its only fixed pick is `Bane`, *"reduces the cast time of your main source of damage."* Everything else, `Improved Shadow Bolt`, `Destructive Reach`, `Aftermath`, `Molten Skin`, `Improved Corruption`, `Improved Life Tap`, `Soul Harvesting`, is filed as playstyle-dependent, and notably it does **not** put a fixed point into `Cataclysm` or [[Shadowburn]] at 20 the way Icy Veins does; it pushes both toward the Legacy System instead.
**ClassicWoW.gg does not publish a suggested build** for any spec, by design.
**The Legacy System adds points beyond these 11.** Wowhead puts the cap at 7 legacy points from world exploration before level 25, worth 2 extra talent points. Its picks there are [[Shadowburn]], *"instant burst on demand, at the cost of a soul shard. Good for finishing runners and for damage while moving"*, and `Ruin`, *"increases the bonus damage on your destruction crits."*
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source has posted one; the beta has not reached 30. Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs, two readings differ by **exactly one 5-point block**: both take `Bane` 5, `Ruin` 5, [[Shadowburn]] 1, `Agonizing Flames` 3, [[Conflagrate]] 1, [[Bane of Havoc]] 1. Then one adds `Improved Shadow Bolt` 5 and the other adds `Aftermath` 5. `Shadow and Flame` stays out of reach either way.
## Rotation
Icy Veins and Wowhead order the opening DoTs differently, same as the other two specs. Icy Veins leads with Immolate, then Corruption, then Bane of Agony. Wowhead reverses it: *"Bane of Agony first: it deals the least upfront damage, so it's the least threat and requires time to build up to full damage. Immolate last; its initial Hit generates direct threat, so it's the worst opener."* Wowhead also wands less here than for the other two specs, calling wanding *"an equally good option but not really Destruction-oriented"* once `Bane` is trained.
1. **Send the demon in**
2. **Curse**
3. **[[Immolate]]**: *"one of your most important Destruction spells"*, or last on Wowhead's threat-ordered build, see above
4. **[[Corruption]]** *only* *"when the enemy will live long enough for the DoT to be worthwhile"*
5. **[[Bane of Agony]]** on tougher enemies only, or first on Wowhead's build
6. **[[Shadow Bolt]] as your primary filler.** This is the one spec where it beats the wand, because `Bane` cuts its cast time
7. **[[Searing Pain]]** when you need damage faster: *"be careful with the additional threat"*
8. **[[Drain Life]]** for health; wand when the target is nearly dead
9. **[[Shadowburn]] to finish**: *"if a non trivial enemy dies within 8 seconds of being hit by it you gain a Soul Shard"*
10. **[[Drain Soul]]** instead when you specifically need shards
None of the three sources publishes a rotation past level 20; [[Conflagrate]] joins the kit at 25 and [[Bane of Havoc]] at 30, see What defines Destruction below, but how they slot into the priority is untested.
**Multi-target:** [[Rain of Fire]] at 20, and *"In dungeons, let the tank establish threat before channeling it."* From 30 the cleave tool is **[[Bane of Havoc]]**: 15% of your damage copied onto one marked target for 5 minutes. The published rule: *"When an add must die quickly but the boss should keep taking damage, mark the boss and put your casts into the add."*
## Your demon
Same unlocks — [[Imp]] 2, [[Voidwalker]] 10, [[Succubus]] 20, [[Felhunter]] 30. But **Destruction has no pet talents at all.**
**That makes the [[Voidwalker]] matter more here than in the other two specs**: you are a stand-still caster with no threat reduction. Its threat spell is `Torment`, noticeably weaker until rank 2 trains at 20. Beta reports say it *"generated an extremely high amount of threat"* and is *"the safest demon to use while leveling because it can hold enemies away from you while you hard cast."*
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, **Haste**, critical strike, Spirit, Intellect, Stamina.
**Haste is third here and last for the other two specs.** The reason: *"Destruction relies heavily on hard casts, so Haste benefits spells such as [[Shadow Bolt]] and [[Immolate]]."* Hit is called out harder too: *"Destruction does not take `Suppression` at this level, so you receive no Hit from your talent build."*

## What Forever changed for Destruction
- **Five new talents**: `Molten Skin` (−10% all damage taken at 5/5: real survivability, though that is **level 19**; rank 1 at 15 is −2%), [[Bane of Havoc]], `Fire and Brimstone`, `Shadow and Flame`, and the capstone [[Incinerate]].
- **`Shadow and Flame` does three things by rank, not one.** It gates [[Conflagrate]]'s Immolate consumption: a 20% chance to preserve it at rank 1, rising to 100% at rank 5. It links the schools: Conflagrate buffs your Shadow damage and Shadowburn buffs your Fire damage, 2% at rank 1 up to 10% at rank 5, both for 20 seconds. And it gives Shadowburn a chance to refund its Soul Shard immediately, 20% at rank 1 up to 100% at rank 5. It sits at tier 6, so **level 35 at the earliest and unreachable at either beta cap.** Until then every [[Conflagrate]] eats your Immolate; budget for the re-cast.
- **The tree was heavily compressed**: `Destructive Reach` 4→1 (and it now extends the range of *all* your damaging spells), `Bane` 2→1, `Ruin` 5→3, [[Conflagrate]] 7→4.
- **`Improved Shadow Bolt` is now a personal debuff.** The warning attached to it: *"This is a personal benefit, not the original Classic four-charge Shadow vulnerability shared by damage sources… Do not build a group assignment around supplying that old debuff."*
- `Aftermath` was repurposed to +[[Immolate]] initial damage and a [[Conflagrate]] daze. `Improved Searing Pain` was renamed `Agonizing Flames`. **Five removed:** `Improved Immolate`, `Emberstorm`, `Devastation`, `Improved Firebolt`, `Improved Lash of Pain`.
- **[[Curse of Shadow]] no longer exists.** [[Curse of the Elements]] now reduces **all** magic resistances by 30 and increases all magic damage taken by 4%, and it is trained at **20** rather than Classic's **32**. Some sites' rotations still tell you to cast Curse of Shadow; they are stale.
## At level 60
Unknown, and the source admits it: *"[[Bane of Havoc]], `Shadow and Flame`, [[Incinerate]], [[Conflagrate]], and [[Shadowburn]] create a much deeper toolkit than original Classic, but the final rotation will depend heavily on tuning and the encounters available at Level 60."* The longest anyone has played is a level-38 demo, well short of `Shadow and Flame` at 35 with room to rank it up, let alone a full 51-point tree.
