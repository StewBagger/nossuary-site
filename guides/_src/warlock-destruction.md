Hard-cast direct damage instead of ramping damage over time. **The one Warlock spec published guides mark down for levelling** — 3.5 out of 5, against 5 for the other two.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 20** right now, rising to 30 before launch, so no level-60 Destruction build has been tested.
:::
## Is Destruction worth levelling?
**Viable but distinctly slower, and every source that compares the three specs says so.** The numeric ratings are the clearest evidence:
| Spec | Overall | Levelling | Dungeon | Mobility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affliction | 5.0 | **5.0** | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Demonology | 5.0 | **5.0** | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| **Destruction** | **3.5** | **3.5** | 4.0 | **2.0** |
The reasons, in the sources' own words: *"You have less passive sustain than Affliction and less pet support than Demonology"* · *"Most of your damage requires you to stand still"*, and *"taking damage while casting causes spell pushback"* · *"[[Searing Pain]] generates significant threat, however, and this build does not have the threat reduction from `Suppression`"* · *"much of the specialization's new WoW Forever identity comes online at higher levels."*
**The sharpest version is the wand argument**, and it is why one guide publishes no level-20 Destruction build at all: *"Wands are a great early replacement for [[Shadow Bolt]]: they deal nearly equal damage, or more with a good wand, at no mana cost."* A free wand competing with a mana-costing hard cast guts the entire point of the spec. **Forever also cut its numbers hard** — [[Conflagrate]] 84–106 where Classic gave 240–306; [[Soul Fire]] 334–420 against 623–783 — while `Ruin` went from a 1-rank 100% talent to a 5-rank one that only reaches 100% at 5/5.
**In fairness, its own counter-argument:** *"Destruction does not need as much time to ramp as Affliction, making its direct damage useful when dungeon enemies are dying quickly."* Its **dungeon rating is 4.0, the same as the other two.** This is a soloing weakness specifically.
## Your talent points
**Level 20 — 0/0/11**, and here the prose and the calculator agree exactly:
1. `Bane`, 5 points
2. `Destructive Reach`, 2 points
3. `Cataclysm`, 3 points
4. **[[Shadowburn]]** at 20
**Named alternatives:** *"`Improved Shadow Bolt` and `Aftermath` are both strong alternatives, but this build prioritizes reliable leveling benefits that do not depend on Critical Strike, since we won't have much at this level."*
**Level 30 — 0/0/21.** Two published builds differ by **exactly one 5-point block**: both take `Bane` 5, `Ruin` 5, [[Shadowburn]] 1, `Agonizing Flames` 3, [[Conflagrate]] 1, [[Bane of Havoc]] 1 — then one adds `Improved Shadow Bolt` 5 and the other adds `Aftermath` 5. One notes: *"Sadly, `Shadow and Flame` is still out of reach."*
## Rotation
1. **Send the demon in**
2. **Curse**
3. **[[Immolate]]** — *"one of your most important Destruction spells"*
4. **[[Corruption]]** *only* *"when the enemy will live long enough for the DoT to be worthwhile"*
5. **[[Bane of Agony]]** on tougher enemies only
6. **[[Shadow Bolt]] as your primary filler.** This is the one spec where it beats the wand, because `Bane` cuts its cast time
7. **[[Searing Pain]]** when you need damage faster — *"be careful with the additional threat"*
8. **[[Drain Life]]** for health; wand when the target is nearly dead
9. **[[Shadowburn]] to finish** — *"if a non trivial enemy dies within 8 seconds of being hit by it you gain a Soul Shard"*
10. **[[Drain Soul]]** instead when you specifically need shards
From 30: maintain [[Immolate]] → [[Conflagrate]] → [[Shadow Bolt]] → [[Shadowburn]] below the execute threshold → [[Life Tap]].
**Multi-target:** [[Rain of Fire]] at 20, and *"In dungeons, let the tank establish threat before channeling it."* From 30 the cleave tool is **[[Bane of Havoc]]** — 15% of your damage copied onto one marked target for 5 minutes. The published rule for it: *"When an add must die quickly but the boss should keep taking damage, mark the boss and put your casts into the add."*
## Your demon
Same unlocks — [[Imp]] 2, [[Voidwalker]] 10, [[Succubus]] 20, [[Felhunter]] 30 — but **Destruction has no pet talents at all.**
**That makes the [[Voidwalker]] matter more here than in the other two specs**, not less: you are a stand-still caster with no threat reduction. Beta reports say it *"generated an extremely high amount of threat"* and is *"the safest demon to use while leveling because it can hold enemies away from you while you hard cast."*
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, **Haste**, critical strike, Spirit, Intellect, Stamina.
**Note the divergence: Haste is third here and last for the other two specs.** The reason is stated: *"Destruction relies heavily on hard casts, so Haste benefits spells such as [[Shadow Bolt]] and [[Immolate]]."* Hit is called out harder too: *"Destruction does not take `Suppression` at this level, so you receive no Hit from your talent build."*
## What defines Destruction
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Bane` 5/5** | 14 | −0.5 s on [[Shadow Bolt]], [[Immolate]] and [[Incinerate]], −2 s on [[Soul Fire]]. **Moved to tier 1** and now covers Incinerate. Universal in every published build |
| **[[Shadowburn]]** | 20 | Your only instant at the low cap, and a shard on a kill inside 8 seconds |
| **[[Conflagrate]]** | ~25 | **Moved from tier 7 to tier 4 and no longer requires `Improved Immolate`** — the single biggest structural change, and why the spec's identity is reachable at 30 at all |
| **`Ruin`** | ~25 | **5 ranks in Forever where Classic had 1, and moved from tier 5 to tier 3** — a 25-point Classic talent now reachable at 30 |
## What Forever changed for Destruction
- **Five new talents**: `Molten Skin` (−10% all damage taken at 5/5 — real survivability, though that is **level 19**; rank 1 at 15 is −2%), [[Bane of Havoc]], `Fire and Brimstone`, `Shadow and Flame`, and the capstone [[Incinerate]].
- **`Shadow and Flame` gates [[Conflagrate]]'s behaviour**: a 20% chance not to consume [[Immolate]] at rank 1, **100% at rank 5** — but it sits at tier 6, so **level 34 at the earliest and unreachable at either beta cap.** Until then every [[Conflagrate]] eats your Immolate; budget for the re-cast.
- **The tree was heavily compressed**: `Destructive Reach` 4→1 (and it now extends the range of *all* your damaging spells), `Bane` 2→1, `Ruin` 5→3, [[Conflagrate]] 7→4.
- **`Improved Shadow Bolt` is now a personal debuff.** The warning attached to it: *"This is a personal benefit, not the original Classic four-charge Shadow vulnerability shared by damage sources… Do not build a group assignment around supplying that old debuff."*
- `Aftermath` was repurposed to +[[Immolate]] initial damage and a [[Conflagrate]] daze. `Improved Searing Pain` was renamed `Agonizing Flames`. **Five removed:** `Improved Immolate`, `Emberstorm`, `Devastation`, `Improved Firebolt`, `Improved Lash of Pain`.
- **[[Curse of Shadow]] no longer exists.** [[Curse of the Elements]] now reduces **all** magic resistances by 30 and increases all magic damage taken by 4%, and it is trained at **20** rather than Classic's **32**. Some sites' rotations still tell you to cast Curse of Shadow; they are stale.
## At level 60
Unknown, and honestly so: *"[[Bane of Havoc]], `Shadow and Flame`, [[Incinerate]], [[Conflagrate]], and [[Shadowburn]] create a much deeper toolkit than original Classic, but the final rotation will depend heavily on tuning and the encounters available at Level 60."* In practice nothing past roughly 15 points in this tree has been played by anyone.
