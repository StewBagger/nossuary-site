## What Destruction is for
**Direct damage whose timing you control.** `Bane` 5/5 from level 14 takes half a second off [[Shadow Bolt]], [[Immolate]] and [[Incinerate]], and two seconds off [[Soul Fire]]. [[Shadowburn]] at 20 is an instant that hands back a Soul Shard when a non-trivial target dies within 8 seconds of being hit.
**Icy Veins is the only one of the three sources to score it**, and it marks this spec down where it gives the other two full marks. Its ratings, out of 5:
| Spec | Overall | Levelling | Dungeon | Mobility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Affliction | 5.0 | 5.0 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| Demonology | 5.0 | 5.0 | 4.0 | 3.0 |
| **Destruction** | 3.5 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 2.0 |
The reasons, in the sources' own words: *"You have less passive sustain than Affliction and less pet support than Demonology"* · *"Most of your damage requires you to stand still"* · *"taking damage while casting causes spell pushback"* · *"[[Searing Pain]] generates significant threat, however, and this build does not have the threat reduction from `Suppression`"* · *"much of the specialization's new WoW Forever identity comes online at higher levels."*
The tree's own answer to the pushback is `Intensity`, now 3 points and covering **every** Destruction spell where Classic's covered three.
**Wowhead makes the wand argument about the class generally, not Destruction specifically**: *"you'll use your wand more for damage because it deals more DPS than Shadow Bolt, and it's also mana-free."* Destruction is the one spec that pushes back, because `Bane` cuts [[Shadow Bolt]]'s cast time. See Rotation below.
**Forever also cut its numbers hard.** [[Conflagrate]] 84–106 where Classic gave 240–306; [[Soul Fire]] 334–420 against 623–783. `Ruin` went from a 1-rank 100% talent to a 5-rank one that only reaches 100% at 5/5.
Icy Veins also gives the counter-argument: *"Destruction does not need as much time to ramp as Affliction, making its direct damage useful when dungeon enemies are dying quickly."* Its dungeon rating is 4.0, the same as the other two, so on its own numbers the gap is in solo levelling, not dungeons.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Icy Veins and Wowhead disagree on how much of the build is fixed.
**Icy Veins'** build is fully specified: `Bane` 5, `Destructive Reach` 2, `Cataclysm` 3, [[Shadowburn]] 1. *"`Improved Shadow Bolt` and `Aftermath` are both strong alternatives, but this build prioritizes reliable leveling benefits that do not depend on Critical Strike, since we won't have much at this level."*
**Wowhead commits to far less.** Its only fixed pick is `Bane`, *"reduces the cast time of your main source of damage."* Everything else, `Improved Shadow Bolt`, `Destructive Reach`, `Aftermath`, `Molten Skin`, `Improved Corruption`, `Improved Life Tap`, `Soul Harvest`, is playstyle-dependent. It puts no fixed point into `Cataclysm` or [[Shadowburn]] at 20 the way Icy Veins does, pushing both toward the Legacy System instead.
**ClassicWoW.gg does not publish a suggested build** for any spec, by design.
The Legacy System adds points beyond these 11. Wowhead puts the cap at 7 legacy points from world exploration before level 25, worth 2 extra talent points. Its picks there are [[Shadowburn]], *"instant burst on demand, at the cost of a soul shard. Good for finishing runners and for damage while moving"*, and `Ruin`, *"increases the bonus damage on your destruction crits."* **Those points cannot be spent at 20 as printed.** Blizzard's Known Issues list opening the Legacy System before level 25 as a bug: the “Y” hotkey works but throws LUA errors on tabs 1 and 3. Read these as what to spend Legacy points on from 25, not as part of a level-20 build.
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source has posted one; the cap only reached 30 on October 1. Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs, with the running total after each pick: `Bane` 5 (5) → `Improved Shadow Bolt` 5 (10) → `Ruin` 5 (15, a 5-point talent on tier 3 now) → [[Conflagrate]] 1 (16, the 16-point milestone) → [[Shadowburn]] 1 (17) → `Cataclysm` 3 (20) → [[Bane of Havoc]] 1 (21, the 21-point milestone). The second reading swaps two blocks for crit-dependent ones: `Aftermath` 5 for `Improved Shadow Bolt`, `Molten Skin` 3 for `Cataclysm`.
**The last point is a straight choice.** `Fire and Brimstone` sits on tier 5 behind [[Conflagrate]] and needs the same 20 points spent, so it and [[Bane of Havoc]] compete for the 21st. `Agonizing Flames` stays out because the dive never places it, and at its Classic tier 5 there is no room for three points. `Shadow and Flame` and [[Incinerate]] are further out, at 35 and 40.
## Rotation
Icy Veins and Wowhead order the opening DoTs differently, same as the other two specs. Icy Veins leads with Immolate, then Corruption, then Bane of Agony. Wowhead reverses it: *"Bane of Agony first: it deals the least upfront damage, so it's the least threat and requires time to build up to full damage. Immolate last; its initial Hit generates direct threat, so it's the worst opener."*
Wowhead also wands less here than for the other two specs, calling wanding *"an equally good option but not really Destruction-oriented"* once `Bane` is trained.
1. **Send the demon in**
2. **Curse**
3. **[[Immolate]]**: *"one of your most important Destruction spells"*, or last on Wowhead's threat-ordered build, see above
4. **[[Corruption]]** *only* *"when the enemy will live long enough for the DoT to be worthwhile"*
5. **[[Bane of Agony]]** on tougher enemies only, or first on Wowhead's build
6. **[[Shadow Bolt]] as your primary filler**, once `Bane` cuts its cast time — see the wand note above
7. **[[Searing Pain]]** when you need damage faster: *"be careful with the additional threat"*
8. **[[Drain Life]]** for health; wand when the target is nearly dead
9. **[[Shadowburn]] to finish**: *"if a non trivial enemy dies within 8 seconds of being hit by it you gain a Soul Shard"*
10. **[[Drain Soul]]** instead when you specifically need shards
None of the three sources publishes a rotation past level 20. [[Conflagrate]] joins the kit at 25 (see What defines Destruction below), but how it slots into the priority is untested.
**Multi-target:** [[Rain of Fire]] at 20, and *"In dungeons, let the tank establish threat before channeling it."* That is the whole of it at the cap. The intended cleave tool is [[Bane of Havoc]]: 15% of the damage you do elsewhere copied onto one marked target for 5 minutes, and being a Bane it displaces [[Bane of Agony]] there. It arrives with your 21st point, so at the cap exactly, and no source has played it: the published rule is read off its tooltip: *"When an add must die quickly but the boss should keep taking damage, mark the boss and put your casts into the add."*
## Your demon
Same unlocks — [[Imp]] 2, [[Voidwalker]] 10, [[Succubus]] 20, [[Felhunter]] 30. But **Destruction has no pet talents at all.**
**You are a stand-still caster with no `Suppression` threat reduction**, and beta reports point at the [[Voidwalker]]. It *"generated an extremely high amount of threat"* and is *"the safest demon to use while leveling because it can hold enemies away from you while you hard cast."* Its threat spell is `Torment`, noticeably weaker until rank 2 trains at 20.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, **Haste**, critical strike, Spirit, Intellect, Stamina.
**Haste is third here**, and last for the other two specs. The reason: *"Destruction relies heavily on hard casts, so Haste benefits spells such as [[Shadow Bolt]] and [[Immolate]]."* Hit is called out harder too: *"Destruction does not take `Suppression` at this level, so you receive no Hit from your talent build."*

| **[[Shadowburn]]** | 20 | Your only instant at the low cap, and a shard on a kill inside 8 seconds |

## What Forever changed for Destruction
- **Five new talents**: `Molten Skin` (−10% all damage taken at 5/5, though 5/5 is level 19; rank 1 at 15 is −2%), [[Bane of Havoc]], `Fire and Brimstone`, `Shadow and Flame`, and the capstone [[Incinerate]]. Incinerate is Fire damage on a 2.5-second cast at 30 yards, +25% against a target carrying your [[Immolate]]. It requires [[Bane of Havoc]] and is the 31-point capstone, so level 40 and out of reach at the cap.
- **`Shadow and Flame` does three separate things by rank**, each reaching full value at 5/5. [[Conflagrate]] buffs your Shadow damage and [[Shadowburn]] buffs your Fire damage by up to 10% for 20 seconds. Conflagrate gains up to a 100% chance *not* to consume [[Immolate]], and Shadowburn gains up to a 100% chance to refund its Soul Shard instantly. It sits at tier 6, so level 35 at the earliest and unreachable at either beta cap. Until then every Conflagrate eats your Immolate; budget for the re-cast.
- **The tree was heavily compressed**: `Destructive Reach` 4→1 (and it now extends the range of *all* your damaging spells, so every spec can reach it), `Bane` 2→1, `Ruin` 5→3, [[Conflagrate]] 7→4. `Cataclysm` went the other way, tier 1→2, and now cuts the mana cost of your Destruction spells by up to 10%.
- **`Improved Shadow Bolt` is now a personal debuff.** The warning attached to it: *"This is a personal benefit, not the original Classic four-charge Shadow vulnerability shared by damage sources… Do not build a group assignment around supplying that old debuff."*
- `Aftermath` was repurposed to +[[Immolate]] initial damage and a [[Conflagrate]] daze. `Improved Searing Pain` was renamed `Agonizing Flames`, cut to 3 points, and it now adds damage to *all* your Destruction spells on top of the [[Searing Pain]] crit chance. **Five removed:** `Improved Immolate`, `Emberstorm`, `Devastation`, `Improved Firebolt`, `Improved Lash of Pain`.
- **[[Curse of Shadow]] no longer exists**, removed outright in Blizzard's deep dive. [[Curse of the Elements]] now reduces *all* magic resistances by 30 and increases all magic damage taken by 4%, and it is trained at 20 rather than Classic's 32. Some sites' rotations still tell you to cast Curse of Shadow; they are stale.
## At level 60
Unknown, and the source admits it: *"[[Bane of Havoc]], `Shadow and Flame`, [[Incinerate]], [[Conflagrate]], and [[Shadowburn]] create a much deeper toolkit than original Classic, but the final rotation will depend heavily on tuning and the encounters available at Level 60."* The longest anyone has played is a level-38 demo, well short of `Shadow and Flame` at 35 with room to rank it up, let alone a full 51-point tree.
