## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Beast Mastery](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/beast-mastery/)** | Ranged DPS | Your pet holds the target from level 10 while you shoot, and `Focused Fire` raises your damage and the pet's together | `Summon Hawk` at 25 adds up to two more attackers, and `Bestial Wrath` at 40 gives the pet +50% damage for 18 seconds |
| **[Marksmanship](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/marksmanship/)** | Ranged DPS | `Careful Aim`: up to 100% of your Intellect added to **both** melee and ranged Attack Power | `Lone Wolf` gives +20% damage with all attacks, but only while you have **no pet out** |
| **[Survival](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/survival/)** | Melee or weaving DPS | `Strider Kick` at level 30 and `Expose Prey` turn [[Raptor Strike]] and [[Mongoose Bite]] into a rotation | Ranged like the other two until the melee talents arrive, then in and out of melee |
**Beast Mastery** spends its points on the pet and the aspects: `Deadly Aspects` off [[Aspect of the Hawk]], `Focused Fire`, `Unleashed Fury` and `Ferocity` for pet and hawk damage, then `Summon Hawk` and `Bestial Wrath`.
**Marksmanship** buys shot damage. `Lethal Attacks` and `Careful Aim` come first, `Rapid Killing` and `Rapid Recuperation` pay Mana back, and [[Sniper Shot]] closes the tree at level 40. `Lone Wolf` is the one talent that pays you for fighting with no pet.
**Survival** buys the melee kit and the trap kit: `Savage Strikes`, `Predator's Edge`, `Strider Kick`, `Expose Prey`, and `Entrapment` making every trap root what it catches.
Through level 20 the **rotation is the same across all three specs**, and the difference is where the points go. They separate at 25, when `Summon Hawk` arrives, and again at 30 with `Strider Kick`. Each spec page carries its own builds and rotation.
Dual Specialization opens at level 40, per Blizzard. Wowhead reports it is bought from the class trainer for 50 gold.
Blizzard frames the three trees as a spectrum. A Hunter can *"double down on ... ranged combat and their pets, choose not to have a pet at all, or ... dance back and forth between ranged and melee combat."*
## Is Hunter for you?
Play Hunter if you want the pet, not you, holding the enemy, and you do not mind managing ammunition, a quiver and pet food.
Blizzard's own framing of the class: Hunters have *"an amazing toolkit of problem-solving Aspects and attacks but cannot Stun or Interrupt,"* and in solo or small-group play the pet does the tanking.
:::strengths
- Blizzard credits the class with *"an amazing toolkit of problem-solving Aspects and attacks"*
- Your pet holds the enemy, so you take very little damage
- Ranged attacks take no glancing blows, which Wowhead gives as the reason Hunters can fight enemies above their level
:::
:::weaknesses
- Ammunition, a quiver and pet food all cost bag space and money
- An 8-yard minimum range you cannot shoot inside, which Forever did not remove
- No Stun and no Interrupt in the trained kit, which Blizzard states as a limit of the class. Talents add some control later: `Intimidation` (pet stun) and `Scatter Shot` (disorient)
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes. Mark, send the pet, sting, shoot. Each spec adds one or two buttons past level 25.
**Is it forgiving?** Solo, your pet holds the target. In a dungeon the pet is not the tank, so that cover is gone.
**What carries you while levelling?** Your pet tanks for you from level 10.
**Does it need a group?** No, solo the pet does the tanking.
:::

**Talent abilities arrive by points, and their higher ranks are trained.** `Summon Hawk`, `Trueshot Aura` and `Counterattack` open at 16 points, level 25. `Strider Kick` opens at 21 points, level 30. [[Sniper Shot]], `Bestial Wrath` and `Lacerating Strikes` open at 31 points, level 40. The trainer then sells `Summon Hawk` ranks at 36, 48 and 60, `Trueshot Aura` ranks at 32, 40, 50 and 60, and [[Sniper Shot]] ranks at 48 and 58.
**Misdirection is not in Forever.** It is a Burning Crusade ability and it is absent from the client. Icy Veins' class overview still mentions it.
**Wyvern Sting is gone as a talent.** Blizzard folded it, together with Killer Instinct and Improved Feign Death, into the new Survival talent `Resourcefulness` (see the Survival guide).
## Which race
Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*.
Racials decide things in **PvP**, specifically the ones that break crowd control: `Will to Survive` (Human, removes all stuns), `Shatter Curse` (Orc, removes Curses and Banes) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Horde — Orc.** `Command` makes your pet deal 5% more damage.
**Horde — Tauren.** `Plainsrunning` ramps to +60% movement speed, not while mounted, but it needs continuous movement: stutter-stepping does not keep it up, and taking damage or standing still reduces it. Since November 5 it cannot stack during battleground preparation or while you carry a flag. `Endurance` is +5% Health and nothing else.
**Alliance — Human**, new to Hunter in Forever. `Ruthless` gives +1% crit with all spells and attacks. It needs no weapon, so it applies to every shot.
**Alliance — Dwarf.** The new racial is `Artisan's Precision`, +1% hit with spells and attacks.
**Skyborne is new on both factions** and can play Hunter. Its racials are combat-adjacent rather than Hunter-specific: +2% haste, +5% damage to Elemental creatures, `Walk on Air` (glide) on both factions, and a faction-split button: `Read Leyline` for Alliance (Health and Mana regeneration), `Skysight` for Horde (a movement-speed burst).
## Quality of life
- **Level 10 is the class.** Do the pet chain the moment you reach it. [[Tame Beast]] does not work on beasts above your own level.
- **Ammunition and the quiver are real costs.** The quiver gives a ranged attack-speed bonus and does not stack, so carry exactly one. Buy ammunition in stacks whenever you pass a vendor. Wowhead notes that Engineers craft the best ammunition in the game.
- **[[Aspect of the Cheetah]] is your mount until 40** and should be up almost all the time in the world; `Pathfinding` pushes it to +36%. Taking damage in it dazes you for 4 seconds. [[Aspect of the Pack]] carries the same daze to your whole group.
- **Mail opens at 40**, and a weapon master teaches anything you did not start with, including polearms from 20.
- **Train what you use.** Wowhead's levelling guide lists the ranks worth buying when gold is short: the shots, the stings, [[Aspect of the Hawk]], [[Mend Pet]], the traps and [[Raptor Strike]].
## Pet families
**Forever widened every family's kit.** Blizzard confirms every pet family can now learn either [[Bite]] or [[Claw]] (some families both), and either [[Dive]] or [[Dash]]. Pets also gain stats from your own gear. On top of that shared kit, every family has exactly one ability nothing else has:

| --- | --- | --- |

| Crab | `Pinch` | Damage and slows the target's movement |
| Crocolisk | `Dismember` | Damage and reduces the target's healing received by 25% for 6 seconds, on a 15-second cooldown |
| **Fox** *(new family)* | `Trickster's Dance` | Increases the pet's dodge chance and attack speed for 12 seconds |
| Gorilla | `Thunderstomp` | Damages enemies in an area |
| Hyena | `Tendon Rip` | Movement slow and a bleed over time on the target |

**Whether a family still changes a pet's damage is disputed, and the client sides with yes.** Icy Veins says pets *"do not have an inherent damage buff or reduction based on type."* Three sources say otherwise. foreverchanges.pro's reading of the client keeps Classic's family table. Wowhead writes that Cats and Raptors *"have a 1.1x damage modifier."* WoWSims fitted a level 30 Cat's logged swings with that same 10% *(WoWSims' model)*. **Attack speed belongs to the beast you tame.** Every pet swings every 2.0 seconds, and a passive the beast brings with it changes that. Blizzard's notes say untraining a pet no longer strips the passive.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Pets get increased stats from your gear** | Blizzard publishes no coefficients for it. A pet can no longer be stat-buffed directly: Blizzard confirms pets cannot receive player buffs that increase their stats, so gear the Hunter, not the pet |
| **Pet Health, tuned November 10** | Blizzard found pets had 150% of their normal Health by a bug and set it to 115%. Its stated aim is pet durability *"relatively the same as in Classic Era"* |
| [[Aimed Shot]] trained at 20, not talented | Every Hunter gets it, and it shares a cooldown with [[Multi-Shot]] |
| **[[Arcane Shot]] no longer shares a cooldown with [[Aimed Shot]]** | You can press both; the shared cooldown moved onto [[Multi-Shot]] instead, per Blizzard |
| **[[Arcane Shot]] costs 11% of base Mana at every rank** | Since November 10 its cost follows your level, not the rank you trained |
| **Traps usable in combat**, on a 30-second cooldown | Every trap, Fire or Frost, shares that one cooldown, so placing one trap puts all of them on cooldown; **you can still only place a trap at your own location**. One Fire trap and one Frost trap can be out at once |
| **[[Volley]] no longer triggers procs** | Since November 11. It also stops when you move |
| **[[Disengage]]'s threat reduction is doubled** | Blizzard confirms the drop-threat tool is stronger than it was |
| **[[Aspect of the Beast]] now also grants melee Attack Power** | The Aspect for fighting in melee |
| Misdirection absent | There is no threat-transfer tool in Forever, for anyone |
| Survival gained a melee kit | `Strider Kick`, `Expose Prey` and `Lacerating Strikes` are talents at 21, 21 and 31 points (see the Survival guide) |
| The 8-yard dead zone remains | Forever did not fix it, despite heavy feedback |
| **Summon Hawk**, new Beast Mastery talent | Deals an initial hit then attacks on its own for 18 seconds; shares its cooldown with [[Arcane Shot]], and only two hawks can be up at once. On November 10 its cost became 11% of base Mana and the hawk's melee damage rose about 80% |
**Stand still for Auto Shot.** Blizzard's notes say [[Auto Shot]] needs 0.5 seconds standing still before it fires, and that it keeps firing while you cast [[Aimed Shot]] or [[Sniper Shot]]. So a cast costs you no Auto Shots and walking does.
## At level 60
The three trees end in different places, and the spec pages print the published level 60 builds.
- **Beast Mastery** takes 31 points to `Bestial Wrath` and the rest in Marksmanship for `Lethal Attacks` and `Careful Aim`.
- **Marksmanship** has two shapes: with `Lone Wolf` and no pet, or with a pet and a Beast Mastery second tree. Icy Veins prints both.
- **Survival** has three: full melee, weaving in and out, or ranged. Wowhead prints the first two and WoWSims models all three *(WoWSims' model)*.
The two sites disagree on the hit you need at 60. Wowhead says 9%, Icy Veins 8%.
In a dungeon the pet is not the tank and you out-threaten it on several targets. Icy Veins' advice is to play defensively with [[Frost Trap]], [[Wing Clip]] and [[Feign Death]], and to run a loose enemy back to the tank. Wowhead adds the jobs a group hands you: traps on extra enemies, [[Viper Sting]] where Mana drain matters, `Tranquilizing Shot` on an enrage.
Raids open December 9. Nothing on these pages has been tested in one.
