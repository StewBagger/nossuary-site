Hunter is the easiest class in the game to level alone and one of the hardest to play well in a group. Your pet tanks while you shoot, and Forever made pets scale with your own gear.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta caps at level 30, so **no level-60 Hunter build has been tested.** Ability training levels below are read from the beta client and are reliable. One live disagreement worth knowing: published guides differ on whether shots still clip your auto-attack — see *What changed* below.
:::
## Is Hunter for you?
Play Hunter if you want to kill things above your own level from the first hour, and you do not mind managing ammunition, a quiver and a pet.
:::strengths
- The strongest solo levelling class in the game
- Your pet holds the enemy, so you take very little damage
- Very low downtime — you rarely stop to recover
:::
:::weaknesses
- Ammunition, a quiver and pet food all cost bag space and money
- An 8-yard minimum range you cannot shoot inside, which Forever did not remove
- The pet covers your mistakes solo, then stops covering them in a group
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes — mark, send the pet, sting, shoot.
**Is it forgiving?** Extremely, while solo. Much less so in a dungeon, where the pet is not the tank.
**Does it level well?** Better than any other class.
**Does it need a group?** No. Hunter is the class to pick if you mostly play alone.
:::
## Your first talent points
**Beast Mastery**, because the pet is doing most of the work while you level. Marksmanship is a fair alternative if you mean to run dungeons.
1. **`Deadly Aspects`, 5 points** — the single best opening talent and a clear outlier.
2. **`Pathfinding`, 2 points** — +6% movement speed, which matters more than it sounds before you have a mount.
3. **`Improved Revive Pet`, 2 points.**
4. **`Endurance Training`, 1 point** as filler.
5. **`Bestial Swiftness`** at 11.
**Do not level as Survival.** It is now a melee specialisation and its payoffs sit deep in the tree; published guidance is explicit that it is the wrong choice if you want to level quickly. Marksmanship's `Lone Wolf` is likewise a dungeon-cleave talent, not a levelling one.
With the Legacy **Talented** perk, `Deadly Aspects` finishes around level 9 and `Pathfinding` by 11 — so you have your best damage talent and the movement speed **before the pet arrives at 10**, which softens the only stretch where Hunter is not easy.
## Levelling rotation
1. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull.
2. **Send the pet in first** and let it build threat.
3. **[[Serpent Sting]]** once the pet has landed damage.
4. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown.
5. **[[Auto Shot]]** as filler until it dies.
6. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull off the pet.
7. **[[Arcane Shot]]** only when you are mana-capped.
**Two or more enemies:** **[[Multi-Shot]] instead of [[Aimed Shot]]** — they share a cooldown, so it is a swap rather than an addition. [[Explosive Trap]] adds damage, and **traps are usable in combat in Forever**.
**Before level 10, with no pet:** maximise auto-shots while walking backwards, then [[Raptor Strike]] when it reaches you.

## Stat priority
1. **Ranged weapon DPS** — the slowest, highest-damage one you can hold
2. **Agility**
3. **Hit**
4. **Critical strike**
5. Intellect if you have taken `Careful Aim`, then Spirit, then Stamina
Slower weapons are better because [[Aimed Shot]] takes its base damage from the weapon. Stamina is your least important stat while levelling.
## Which race
**Horde — Tauren**, and it is called the single strongest Hunter race across both factions. **`Plainsrunning` ramps to +30% movement speed**, which lets you stutter-step and shoot from level 1 while keeping [[Aspect of the Hawk]] up instead of swapping to Cheetah. `Endurance` also now gives **+1% hit**.
**Alliance — Human, which is new to Hunter in Forever.** `Sword Specialization` is **+2% crit**, the largest weapon racial, and it is general crit — so it applies to your shots off a sword carried purely as a stat stick.
**Forever changed the answer.** Tauren Hunter was the weak vanilla pick and is now the strongest. **Dwarf lost Gun Specialization** and gained a mace racial it cannot use, which is a genuine grievance.
## Quality of life
- **Level 10 is the class. Do the pet chain the moment you reach it.** **Tame a Bear first** — `Swipe` is one of the few pet abilities that holds threat on several targets at once. Note that in Forever **you cannot tame a beast above your own level**.
- **Ammunition and the quiver are real costs.** The quiver gives a ranged attack-speed bonus and **does not stack, so carry exactly one**. Buy ammunition in stacks whenever you pass a vendor.
- **[[Aspect of the Cheetah]] is your mount until 40** and should be up almost all the time in the world; `Pathfinding` pushes it to +36%.
- **Two trainers matter:** your class trainer gives **mail at 40**, and a weapon master teaches anything you did not start with — **polearms only from 20**. As a Human, the trip worth making is one-handed swords, for the crit.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Pets inherit 10% of your attack power and 100% of your crit** | Your gear now makes your pet stronger |
| [[Aimed Shot]] trained at 20, not talented | Every Hunter gets it, and it shares a cooldown with [[Multi-Shot]] |
| **Traps usable in combat**, cooldowns 15s → 30s | Fire and Frost traps have separate limits, so you can hold one of each |
| [[Misdirection]] absent | There is no threat-transfer tool in Forever, for anyone |
| Survival became a melee specialisation | It is not a levelling spec |
| The 8-yard dead zone remains | Forever did not fix it, despite heavy feedback |
**One unresolved disagreement:** one major guide states your abilities no longer clip auto-shot, which would remove vanilla's hardest Hunter skill; another still advises shot-weaving. Blizzard has said the missing cast time is a **bug it intends to fix**, so assume weaving will matter.
## At level 60
Unknown. Nothing past level 20 has been played, so the 31-point builds, whether `Lone Wolf` beats a live pet, whether [[Sniper Shot]]'s four-second cast is a damage loss, and whether melee Survival is viable at all are open questions. Raids open 9 December.
