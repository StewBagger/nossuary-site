## What Frost is for
**`Frostbite` 3/3 gives every Chill effect you apply a 15% chance to freeze the target.** [[Ice Lance]] is instant, on an 8-second cooldown, and hits a frozen target for +300%. `Shatter` 3/3 then adds 50% crit against that frozen target for three points. From 30, `Fingers of Frost` gives the same bonuses against targets that cannot be frozen.
**Icy Veins rates it 4/5 for levelling**, under the heading *"Smooth and Steady"*; the score measures solo kill speed, and Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish none. Its verdict: *"Frost Mages are going to have a comfortable time leveling… slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe."*
The drawback, in the same source's words, is *"the constant need to stop for mana"*. You will drink often. Icy Veins also says Frost *"doesn't become fully effective until level 30+"*, and offers levelling as Arcane first and respeccing at about 31 as an option.
On damage the published line is *"Frost Mage won't top the damage meters, but few specs are as safe or as consistent."*
**In dungeons** Icy Veins scores it 4/5. You bring [[Cone of Cold]] and [[Blizzard]] for packs, and [[Ice Barrier]] and [[Ice Block]] for the moments you pull threat.
## Your talent points
Two sources publish a levelling build from 10 to 60. Icy Veins' ends 7/0/44 and is printed point by point. Wowhead shows its build as an interactive calculator and describes it in words.
**Level 60, Icy Veins' levelling order, 7/0/44 (rebuilt November 9):** `Precision` 2 (10–11), `Improved Frostbolt` 3 (12–14), `Frostbite` 3 (15–17), `Improved Frostbolt` to 5/5 (18–19), [[Ice Lance]] 1 (20), `Ice Shards` 4 (21–24), `Shatter` 3 (25–27), `Ice Shards` to 5/5 (28), [[Ice Block]] 1 (29), `Fingers of Frost` 2 (30–31), `Cold Snap` 1 (32), `Improved Cone of Cold` 3 (33–35), `Frost Channeling` 3 (36–38), `Improved Blizzard` 1 (39), [[Ice Barrier]] 1 (40), `Precision` to 3/3 (41), `Winter's Chill` 5 (42–46), `Arctic Reach` 2 (47–48), `Permafrost` 2 (49–50), `Improved Blizzard` to 3/3 (51–52), `Frost Warding` 1 (53), `Arcane Resilience` 3 (54–56), `Wand Specialization` 2 (57–58), `Arcane Subtlety` 2 (59–60).
**Wowhead's build (page dated November 11)** is a calculator on [its levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/dps-leveling-tips); its text gives no order past the opening and no final split. The first two points go in `Wand Specialization`, which it calls *"insanely valuable and worth delaying everything else for at the start"*. Past that it says to choose by what you do: [[Ice Block]] as soon as possible for world PvP, `Improved Blizzard` and the [[Frost Nova]] talents for dungeons or area pulls.
- **`Frostbite` and `Fingers of Frost` are reported not to stack.** Icy Veins: the two *"share the same proc chance"*. Wowhead says the same. Both suggest a respec once you have `Fingers of Frost`, moving the three `Frostbite` points to `Improved Frost Nova` and `Permafrost` (Icy Veins) or towards [[Ice Barrier]] (Wowhead). The client shows each as a 15% chance from your Chill effects and does not state the rule, so this is the two sites' reading, not a confirmed mechanic.
- **Two points of `Precision` are enough until about 40.** On both sites' figures they remove the 4% miss chance against a same-level target. The third point is for enemies above your level.
- **Two Blizzard changes shape the middle of the tree**: `Shatter` no longer requires `Improved Frost Nova`, and `Fingers of Frost` needs [[Ice Lance]]. Sites still printing the old prerequisite are out of date.
- **`Cold Snap` is the 21-point milestone** and the prerequisite for [[Ice Barrier]]. It is on a **10-minute cooldown**.
- **One point of `Improved Blizzard` is enough to start with.** It makes [[Blizzard]] a Chill effect, so the channel can proc `Fingers of Frost`.
## Rotation
1. **[[Frost Armor]]**, or [[Ice Armor]] from 30, and [[Arcane Intellect]] up. Blizzard's notes confirm both armours proc `Frostbite` and `Fingers of Frost` when you are hit. In a group where nothing hits you, [[Mage Armor]] from 34 is the mana choice
2. **[[Frostbolt]]** from maximum range, and keep casting. Any rank of [[Frostbolt]] procs your talents at the full chance; only the [[Blizzard]] spell loses proc chance at low ranks (see What Forever changed)
3. **[[Ice Lance]] into the freeze.** Press it just before the [[Frostbolt]] you are casting finishes, so the two land together on the frozen target. Do not cancel the Frostbolt for it. Icy Veins reports that [[Ice Lance]] snapshots the target's frozen status when cast, so it keeps its bonus even though the Frostbolt breaks the freeze; no tooltip states it
4. Melee on you → **[[Frost Nova]]**, back away, then [[Frostbolt]] and [[Ice Lance]] again
5. **Wand** to finish low-health enemies rather than spending mana
6. From 30, **`Fingers of Frost` charges go on [[Frostbolt]] and [[Ice Lance]] together.** A proc gives two charges. Cast one [[Frostbolt]] on the first. On the second, queue [[Ice Lance]] behind the next Frostbolt, and both spells use it. Icy Veins and WoWSims' list both play it this way
7. Once you have `Winter's Chill`, **[[Cone of Cold]] at 5 stacks**, on a single target too
- **Never press [[Ice Lance]] on an unfrozen target.** At 25, before spell damage, it hits for about 30 unfrozen and 119 frozen, where [[Frostbolt]] hits for 73. Frozen it beats the bolt and takes no cast; unfrozen it costs you the bolt's damage.
- **[[Fire Blast]] and [[Ice Lance]] share a cooldown.** Casting either puts 8 seconds on both, so a Fire Blast finisher can leave you without Ice Lance for the next freeze. Use the wand instead.
- **[[Frost Nova]] is the freeze you can schedule.** `Frostbite` 3/3 rolls 15% per Chill, which averages 6.7 bolts, or about 17 seconds at a 2.6-second cast. Frost Nova is on a 25-second cooldown, so use it when a target reaches you. In a group, ask first: a frozen enemy the tank walks away from turns on whoever is nearest.
- **[[Cone of Cold]] waits for 5 stacks of `Winter's Chill` on a single target.** At 5/5 each [[Frostbolt]] adds a stack, and five stacks are 100% more damage on the Cone. WoWSims casts it only at 5 stacks *(WoWSims' model)*, and Icy Veins says the same; Wowhead's levelling list accepts 3 or more. Use it early on a target that will die to it. It reaches 10 yards, so it only fits a target you have let close.
- **From 40, keep [[Ice Barrier]]'s damage bonus running.** The 15% lasts 60 seconds whether or not the shield breaks, on a 30-second cooldown. Icy Veins and WoWSims both recast with 3 seconds left.
- **Spell damage does not raise wand damage.** Icy Veins' levelling page says it does; Blizzard's notes say wands *"no longer gain any additional damage from the user's spell damage."*
**Multi-target**, on Icy Veins' list for three or more enemies: [[Frost Nova]] to freeze them, [[Cone of Cold]] on cooldown, `Cold Snap` for a second Cone straight away, [[Blizzard]] between Cones, and [[Arcane Explosion]] when you hold threat and cannot channel. A Cone that hits several enemies rolls `Frostbite` and `Fingers of Frost` on each. Spend those procs on [[Ice Lance]], because [[Blizzard]] gets nothing from them.
**Kiting a pack alone** follows one pattern on both of Icy Veins' pages: shields up, gather, [[Cone of Cold]], [[Frost Nova]], [[Blink]] away, [[Blizzard]] as they come, Cone again, then [[Arcane Explosion]] while moving until Frost Nova returns. [[Cone of Cold]] keeps its first 4 targets slowed almost all the time, and all the time with `Permafrost`, so four is the comfortable number.
**Spells to keep trained past 30:** [[Frostbolt]] at 32, 38, 44, 50, 56 and 60; [[Ice Lance]] at 34, 42, 48 and 56; [[Cone of Cold]] at 34, 42, 50 and 58; [[Ice Armor]] at 30, 40, 50 and 60; [[Blizzard]] at 36, 44, 52 and 60; [[Ice Barrier]] at 46, 52 and 58.
## Stat priority
While levelling, Icy Veins' order is:
1. **Hit** — *"capping your Hit Chance is a high priority"*. Two points of `Precision` cover same-level targets
2. **Spell Power (Frost)** — scales every spell
3. **Critical strike** — *"Frost Mage is a crit-based spec"*: `Ice Shards` doubles the crit-damage bonus at 5/5
4. **Spirit** — *"Frost Mages have limited tools for resource management, so Spirit will be a good stat to increase where you can"*
5. **Intellect** — a bigger mana pool for a whole pull, plus a small crit bonus
6. **Stamina** — survival only
At 60 the two sites differ, and both call their lists provisional. Icy Veins: Hit to 17%, Spell Power, Critical strike, Intellect, MP5, Spirit. Wowhead: Hit, of which you need 11 to 12% from gear after talents, then Critical strike, Spell Damage, Intellect, Spirit, Stamina. The 17% is the miss chance against a raid boss.

## What Forever changed for Frost
- **[[Ice Lance]] and `Fingers of Frost` are both brand new**, and together they are the spec.
- **The slows were cut hard**, and Blizzard says why. `Improved Blizzard` maxes at 40% (Classic: 65%; rank 1 is 15% against Classic's 30%), cut to stop Frost Mages AoE-farming instances *"widely used by gold farmers and boosters to disrupt the economy of the game"*. [[Cone of Cold]] lost duration against the same kiting: 40% over 6 seconds, where Classic gave 50% over 8. The first 4 targets hit take an extra 10% for 3 seconds longer, so 50% over 9 seconds on small pulls. `Permafrost` now extends Chill effects by 11/22/33% instead of a flat 1/2/3 seconds, so it cannot stretch `Improved Blizzard` any further; it still adds 10% slow at 3/3.
- **`Winter's Chill` is now a [[Cone of Cold]] set-up.** [[Frostbolt]] and `Fingers of Frost`-empowered [[Ice Lance]] apply it, up to 5 stacks, and your next [[Cone of Cold]] spends them for 20% more damage per stack plus 7% of your spell damage. Its application does not roll separately to resist. Blizzard's reasoning: the triggering spell already had to hit, so a second resist roll was redundant.
- `Frost Channeling` moved up to row 3, and [[Ice Block]] moved up to row 4 and is now the **16-point milestone talent**. `Precision` (renamed from Elemental Precision) has 3 ranks and covers all spells. `Piercing Ice` is gone from the tree. Blizzard: *"Removing Piercing Ice gives the player a few more talents to play around with early on in the tree, and empowering Ice Barrier gives the Mage a reason to go all the way down the tree for PvE."* [[Ice Barrier]] now requires `Cold Snap` rather than Ice Block, a change the deep dive does not mention either way.
- **[[Frostfire Bolt]] counts as both a Frost and a Fire spell**, so most Frost and Fire talents apply to it. It uses the higher of your two spell damage bonuses and the *lower* of the target's two resistances. Rank 1 is level 40. The build made for it is on the Fire page.
- **Downranking [[Blizzard]] costs you procs.** A [[Blizzard]] rank learned more than 12 levels below your level has a reduced chance to trigger `Frostbite`, `Fingers of Frost` and `Arcane Concentration`. Every other spell triggers your talents at the full chance whatever its rank, though a spell cast well below your level gets less benefit from Spell Power.
- **`Frostbite` freezes have diminishing returns**, like every root that comes from a proc, so repeated freezes on one target get shorter. `Frost Warding` raises what [[Ice Barrier]] and [[Frost Ward]] absorb, and gives [[Frost Ward]] a chance to fully negate a spell's damage without depleting the ward. It does not reflect spells.
- On a **target cap or falloff** for area spells, Blizzard has said nothing. Icy Veins states that none of the Mage's area damage falls off with target count; no other source confirms it.
## At level 60
Icy Veins on Frost at endgame: *"It's hard to say where Frost Mage lands until we see what end-game is like."* The published builds split by purpose.
**Level 60, Wowhead's raid and dungeon build, 18/0/33 (November 4):** `Wand Specialization` 2, `Arcane Resilience` 3, `Arcane Subtlety` 2, `Arcane Concentration` 5, `Arcane Impact` 3, `Arcane Meditation` 3, `Improved Frostbolt` 5, `Precision` 3, `Ice Shards` 5, `Frost Channeling` 3, `Improved Blizzard` 1, [[Ice Lance]] 1, `Shatter` 3, `Improved Cone of Cold` 3, `Cold Snap` 1, `Fingers of Frost` 2, `Winter's Chill` 5, [[Ice Barrier]] 1.
- **It has no `Frostbite`.** Bosses cannot be frozen, so the build relies on `Fingers of Frost` alone.
- **Wowhead names its loose points**: the `Arcane Impact` three, `Arcane Meditation` if mana is not a problem, and `Frost Channeling` if threat is not.
- **Icy Veins publishes four builds.** Its 7/0/44 Deep Frost build is the levelling order above carried to 60, for the open world and dungeons. Two [[Cone of Cold]] raid builds follow: 18/0/33 with `Arcane Meditation` for progression, and 10/5/36 with `Incineration` for when mana proves comfortable. The fourth is an 18/0/33 build for area farming only.
Icy Veins' single-target priority at 60:
1. **[[Ice Barrier]]** before the pull, and again when its damage bonus has 3 seconds left
2. **[[Cone of Cold]]** at 5 stacks of `Winter's Chill`, queued behind a [[Frostbolt]]
3. **[[Ice Lance]]** on the last `Fingers of Frost` charge, queued behind a [[Frostbolt]]
4. **[[Frostbolt]]** otherwise
WoWSims' Frost list is the same, and adds `Cold Snap` when [[Cone of Cold]] has 7 seconds or more left on its cooldown *(WoWSims' model)*. Icy Veins would rather keep `Cold Snap` for a missed Cone or an emergency. Against three or more enemies: [[Cone of Cold]] on cooldown, `Cold Snap` to reset it, [[Blizzard]] as filler.
No raid is open before December 9, so none of this has a raid log behind it.
