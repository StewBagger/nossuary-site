## What Frost is for
**`Frostbite` 3/3 gives every Chill effect you apply a 15% chance to freeze the target.** [[Ice Lance]] is instant, on an 8-second cooldown, and hits a frozen target for +300%. `Shatter` 3/3 then adds 50% crit against that frozen target for three points.
**Icy Veins rates it 3.5/5 for levelling**, under the heading *"Smooth and Steady, but Not Fast"*; Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish no levelling score. Its verdict: *"Frost Mages are going to have a comfortable time leveling… slowing and chilling everything they fight keeps them safe."*
The drawback, in the same source's words, is *"the constant need to stop for mana"* — the *"not Fast"* half of its heading. You will drink often.
On damage the published line is *"Frost Mage won't top the damage meters, but few specs are as safe or as consistent."*
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Two builds, differing in rows 1 and 2. Build A is our own extension, not a published build; Build B is a published list under the launch talent name.
**Build A** (hit and consistency; adjusted because the published list spent 5 points in a talent that has 3, which left it short of [[Ice Lance]]):
1. `Precision`, 3 points. It gives 2% hit with all spells per rank
2. `Improved Frostbolt`, 2 points
3. `Frostbite`, 3 points
4. `Ice Shards`, 2 points
5. **[[Ice Lance]]** at 20
**Build B** (faster casts):
1. `Precision`, 2 points, 4% hit with all spells
2. `Improved Frostbolt`, 5 points
3. `Frostbite`, 3 points
4. **[[Ice Lance]]** at 20
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** Per-level: `Improved Frostbolt` 4 (levels 10–13), `Precision` 3 (14–16), `Frostbite` 3 (17–19), [[Ice Lance]] 1 (20). Then `Ice Shards` 4 (21–24), [[Ice Block]] 1 (25), `Shatter` 3 (26–28), `Ice Shards` 1 more (29), `Fingers of Frost` 1 (30).
Two Blizzard changes make that order legal: `Shatter` no longer requires `Improved Frost Nova`, and `Fingers of Frost` needs [[Ice Lance]], which you took at 20. Sites still printing that prerequisite are out of date. The alternative 21st point is `Cold Snap`, the 21-point milestone down in row 5, on a **10-minute cooldown**.
## Rotation
1. **[[Frost Armor]]** and [[Arcane Intellect]] up. Frost Armor itself procs `Frostbite` when you are hit
2. **[[Frostbolt]]** until `Frostbite` freezes the target. Cast at or near your trained rank: a low-rank [[Frostbolt]] has a reduced chance to proc `Frostbite`, down to a flat 0% at a large enough level gap (see What Forever changed)
3. **[[Ice Lance]] into the freeze** when it is off cooldown. A freeze that lands inside its 8 seconds goes to [[Frostbolt]], and `Shatter` still applies. One source is specific: *"Do not cancel Frostbolt to cast Ice Lance. Finish your current cast, then queue up Ice Lance next"*. The same source reports that [[Ice Lance]] snapshots the target's frozen status, so a queued cast still lands as if frozen; no tooltip states it. Cancelling a [[Frostbolt]] costs you more than a late Ice Lance anyway
4. Melee on you → **[[Frost Nova]]**, back away, [[Ice Lance]] as you retreat
5. **Wand** to finish low-health enemies rather than spending mana
6. From 30, a `Fingers of Frost` charge treats your next spell as if the target were frozen. Spend it on [[Ice Lance]] when that is ready and on [[Frostbolt]] when it is not
**Multi-target:** [[Flamestrike]] while you hold no threat, then [[Arcane Explosion]] once you do: *"Arcane Explosion… has higher DPS than Blizzard until we get more talent points."*
## Stat priority
Icy Veins is the only one of the three tracked sources that ranks Frost's stats; its order:
1. **Hit** — *"capping your Hit Chance is a high priority"*. Nobody publishes the actual cap: *"There will be a cap where you can stop investing in this, but we don't know what it is yet."*
2. **Spell Power (Frost)** — scales every spell. It does not scale your wand, per Blizzard's notes
3. **Critical strike** — *"Frost Mage is a crit-based spec"*: `Ice Shards` doubles the crit-damage bonus at 5/5
4. **Spirit** — *"Frost Mages have limited tools for resource management, so Spirit will be a good stat to increase where you can"*
5. **Intellect** — a bigger mana pool for a whole pull, plus a small crit bonus
6. **Stamina** — a dump stat that comes along for free on most gear (Agility and Strength trail further behind, and Strength is explicitly "not useful")

## What Forever changed for Frost
- **[[Ice Lance]] and `Fingers of Frost` are both brand new**, and together they are the spec.
- **The slows were cut hard**, and Blizzard says why. `Improved Blizzard` maxes at 40% (Classic: 65%; rank 1 is 15% against Classic's 30%), cut to stop Frost Mages AoE-farming instances *"widely used by gold farmers and boosters to disrupt the economy of the game"*. [[Cone of Cold]] lost duration against the same kiting: 40% over 6 seconds, where Classic gave 50% over 8. The first 4 targets hit take an extra 10% for 3 seconds longer, so 50% over 9 seconds on small pulls. `Permafrost` now extends Chill effects by 11/22/33% instead of a flat 1/2/3 seconds, so it cannot stretch `Improved Blizzard` any further; it still adds 10% slow at 3/3.
- **`Winter's Chill` is now a [[Cone of Cold]] set-up.** [[Frostbolt]] and `Fingers of Frost`-empowered [[Ice Lance]] apply it, up to 5 stacks, and your next [[Cone of Cold]] spends them for 20% more damage per stack plus 7% of your spell damage. Its application does not roll separately to resist. Blizzard's reasoning: the triggering spell already had to hit, so a second resist roll was redundant.
- `Frost Channeling` moved up to row 3, and [[Ice Block]] moved up to row 4 and is now the **16-point milestone talent**. `Precision` (renamed from `Elemental Precision`) has 3 ranks and covers all spells. `Piercing Ice` is gone from the tree, a change Blizzard's notes do not mention. [[Ice Barrier]] now requires `Cold Snap` rather than Ice Block, a change the deep dive does not mention either way. It also raises your Frost damage by 15% for 1 minute, and by 5% more while the shield holds.
- **[[Frostfire Bolt]] counts as both a Frost and a Fire spell**, so most Frost and Fire talents apply to it. It uses the higher of your two spell damage bonuses and the *lower* of the target's two resistances. Rank 1 is level 40, which the beta never reached.
- **Downranking is no longer free, and this spec feels it first.** A spell cast well below your level gets less benefit from Spell Power and a reduced chance to trigger your own talents. Blizzard's own example is this spec's: a rank 1 [[Frostbolt]] at level 60 has a 0% chance to proc `Frostbite`. Rank-1 Frostbolt spam for a cheap slow was a standard Frost habit; cast near your trained rank now if you want the freeze.
## At level 60
Nothing above 30 was played in the beta. [[Ice Barrier]] is the 31-point capstone at level 40. The open question is whether [[Ice Lance]], `Fingers of Frost` and a [[Cone of Cold]]-spending `Winter's Chill` make Frost a real raid specialisation, or leave it the kiting spec it was in Classic.
