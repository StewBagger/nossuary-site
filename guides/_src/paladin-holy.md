## What Holy is for
**Keeping a party standing while in plate, with the mana tools to keep doing it.** Blizzard's own summary is that Holy sustains heals on a single target better than anything else in the game, that healing a whole party at once takes more planning, and that the new talents make Holy a viable healer across every form of group play. `Reverence` keeps 30% of your Spirit-driven mana regeneration running while you cast. `Illumination` refunds 50% of a spell's base cost when it procs.
The new `Light's Vigil` capstone is the party-healing half of that. Mark an ally, and your next [[Holy Shock]] on them triggers no cooldown and heals their whole party. It is friendly-only: Blizzard removed its damage half in the November 2 notes.
Underneath all of that you are still a melee Paladin: keep a seal up and press [[Holy Strike]] and [[Judgement]] on cooldown. Blizzard made that easier to hold together. [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your active Seal, and [[Holy Strike]] trains at level 6 for 50% of your weapon damage plus a flat Holy bonus that grows with each rank, though the rank you actually train there reads 25% of weapon damage plus 12 Holy in the client, on a 10-second cooldown.
**Dungeons are where a healer levels.** Wowhead's levelling page says so outright: aim at dungeons and dungeon quests, ideally beside friends on damage specs, and expect solo questing to be slow. Dungeon quests reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values. Kills inside dungeons launched at 40% of their Classic Era experience and went to 60% on November 8.
**Our own reading, not a sourced one:** most of what Holy produces lands on other people, because the Blessings, auras and heals all apply to the party. The tree does buy damage, and that part is Blizzard's: `Consecrated Ground` raises Holy spell damage, `Divine Precision` supplies its Hit, and the dive says Holy gains new tools for spell-focused damage.

## Rotation
**Healing, as both sites order it:**
1. **`Oath of Light` on the tank**, once you have it. It lasts 5 minutes, costs 8% of base mana and has no cooldown. Icy Veins puts it first
2. **Judge `Seal of Light`** on the main enemy from level 30, or `Seal of Wisdom` from 38 if another Paladin has Light covered. Your swings keep the Judgement up
3. **`Light's Vigil`, then [[Holy Shock]] on the same ally,** when the whole party is about to take damage. Cast the Vigil before the damage lands
4. **[[Flash of Light]]** as your primary heal, cheap and fast: a 1.5-second cast out to 40 yards, 35 mana at rank 1
5. **[[Holy Light]]** on someone in real danger, and whenever an `Infusion of Light` proc has shortened it. A 2.5-second cast at the same 40 yards, and its cost climbs hard with each rank
6. **[[Holy Shock]]** as a heal only when there is no time to cast. Both sites call it expensive for what it heals
7. **[[Lay on Hands]]** as the emergency: it heals for your full health and drains all your mana, though the client notes your mana regeneration itself keeps running. It sits on a 20-minute cooldown and trains at 10. The ranks at 30 and 50 also restore 250 and 550 mana to the target
8. Keep a Blessing on everyone and an aura up: [[Devotion Aura]], or [[Retribution Aura]] if no tank or damage dealer is running it
**Why Flash of Light leads.** Icy Veins prints healing per mana at +715 healing, crediting DoubleZug for the figures: [[Flash of Light]] 5.75, `Light's Vigil` 4.44, [[Holy Light]] 4.15, [[Holy Shock]] 2.79 *(Icy Veins' figures, a model)*. The Vigil is efficient per cast and costs 730 mana at rank 1, 1,000 at 50 and 1,340 at 60, so you cannot lean on it.
**One reported exception while levelling.** DoubleZug reports that with healing gear on, low ranks of [[Holy Light]] are more mana-efficient than [[Flash of Light]] in levelling dungeons [until roughly level 30 to 35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88d4Etzi9t4&t=735), because the long cast takes more of your bonus healing. That is one creator's report and we have not confirmed it *(reported, unconfirmed)*. Without healing gear he says to heal the usual way.
**Damage, when nobody needs healing:** your rotation is **Retribution's minus [[Seal of Command]]**. Keep [[Seal of Righteousness]] up, press [[Judgement]] and [[Holy Strike]] on cooldown, add [[Exorcism]] on Undead and Demons, then [[Holy Shock]], then [[Consecration]] if mana allows. [[Judgement]] leaves your Seal standing, so judge on cooldown and keep swinging.
**[[Holy Shock]] reaches 20 yards on an enemy and 40 on an ally.** The damage half is the short one.
**Multi-target healing arrives with the capstone.** Before `Light's Vigil`, at 40 on the healing order or 52 on the questing one, area healing is spreading [[Flash of Light]] around. One Vigil per Paladin per party.
## Stat priority
**Healing Power, then Intellect.** Both sites open with those two. After that they differ. Icy Veins' level-60 list continues Spirit, MP5, critical strike. Wowhead's continues critical strike, then Spirit, and says its list is speculative because the beta stopped at 30. Icy Veins' levelling list puts MP5 and critical strike ahead of Spirit.
Spirit is worth more to Holy than to the other two specs because `Reverence` keeps 30% of your mana regeneration running while you cast. Critical strike feeds `Illumination` and `Infusion of Light`, and both sites note most of it comes from Intellect, not from gear.
If you are not planning to heal seriously, gear **Weapon Damage, Hit and critical strike** early, then Spell Power, and play the Holy-damage path. Icy Veins gives that same early order for a levelling Holy Paladin.
Blizzard says it does not plan to itemize Hit chance on plate healing gear, and that `Divine Precision` is there to supply it instead: up to 8% Hit with Holy spells and 20% shorter Silences, 2 points in row 4, with no [[Holy Shock]] requirement. On the Holy-damage path that talent is where your spell Hit comes from, not your gear.

## What Forever changed for Holy
- **Nine new talents:** `Reverence`, `Purifying Power` (up to 20% off the mana cost of `Cleanse` and `Purify`, and up to 33% off the [[Exorcism]] and [[Holy Wrath]] cooldowns), `Holy Conduit` (up to 40% off the mana cost of [[Consecration]], [[Holy Wrath]], [[Exorcism]] and `Hammer of Wrath`), `Divine Precision`, `Infusion of Light`, `Oath of Light` (swear an Oath on one ally at a time for 5 minutes, at 40 yards for 8% of base mana with no cooldown, and your [[Holy Light]], [[Flash of Light]] and [[Holy Shock]] heal them for more; Blizzard says the bonus grows with your level, and the client's level-60 figures are in the table above), `Consecrated Ground` (your Holy spells hit the first 4 enemies in your [[Consecration]] for up to 10% more), `Boundless Light` (up to 30% more critical strike chance for [[Holy Shock]] on allies without your Oath) and the `Light's Vigil` capstone.
- **`Voice of Truth` is gone.** It was a Holy talent in the beta, then a trained spell for a few days at the end of October. Blizzard's November 2 notes removed it. Wowhead and Icy Veins still list it as a utility button on pages dated November 4 and 5.
- **`Improved Seals` replaces `Improved Seal of Righteousness`.** It raises the damage of every Seal and Judgement, up to 15% at 5 points.
- **The tree was re-laid-out.** [[Holy Shock]] is the 21-point gold medal now, with a 10-second cooldown. `Divine Favor` is the 11-point gold medal and no longer needs `Illumination`, and the client has it guaranteeing a critical on your next [[Flash of Light]], [[Holy Light]] or [[Holy Shock]] on a 2-minute cooldown. `Oath of Light` takes the 16-point slot. `Illumination` sits in row 3 under `Reverence`, at 20% per rank to 100% at 5 ranks, and its proc refunds 50% of the spell's base cost rather than all of it. `Healing Light` moved up to row 2, `Divine Intellect` moved to Protection and `Unyielding Faith` to Retribution.
- **`Light's Vigil` drags two older talents into its orbit.** `Spiritual Focus` now protects its cast as well, up to 70% at 14% per rank, and `Illumination` can proc off it.
- **[[Consecration]] left the tree entirely** to become baseline at 20 for every spec, redesigned so it deals moderate damage to the first 4 enemies that enter it and very little to anyone after them. Blizzard's stated reason is to hold Paladin area threat level with a Warrior's Thunderclap and keep Forever out of expansion-style AoE play. The client agrees: rank 1 does 16 Holy damage over 8 seconds to anything in the area, with an extra 32 over 8 seconds for the first four.
- **`Holy Power` is a critical healing talent:** it raises the critical healing bonus of your Holy spells, up to 50% at 5 points in row 6.
- **Baseline changes a healer feels.** [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your Seal, and since November 11 it is a ranged attack that uses ranged hit chance and sets off no melee procs. [[Lay on Hands]] sits on a 20-minute cooldown. The Fire, Frost and Shadow Resistance Auras reach every raid member within 30 yards.
- **Blizzard removed `Blessing of Light`**, and trainers teach neither it nor `Greater Blessing of Light`, so neither is part of a healer's kit.
- **Salvation is gone.** Blizzard removed `Salvation`, `Mass Salvation` and `Revoke`, so a Paladin has no threat-reduction spell to learn.
- **Three talents left the tree and Blizzard deleted none of them.** `Improved Blessing of Wisdom` and `Lasting Judgement` are baseline effects now, and `Improved Lay on Hands` is gone from the tree with its shorter [[Lay on Hands]] cooldown baseline. Over in Retribution, `Sacred Arbiter` refreshes Judgement effects through [[Holy Strike]], other Paladins' Judgements included.
- **Seal twisting is no longer part of the baseline class.** Retribution's 31-point capstone is `Unsealed Wrath`, and Blizzard placed Holy's 21-point [[Holy Shock]] as the deliberate alternative, so a hybrid can skip that capstone and spend into Holy instead. The same thinking drove the `Champion of the Light` nerf: Blizzard trimmed how much spell damage your Intellect buys there to stop Holy Paladins who give up Holy talents for deep Retribution getting too much out of it. It gives up to 60% of your Intellect as spell damage at 5 ranks.
## At level 60
**The published builds are the two above**, plus Wowhead's 31/7/13 raid build. All three take every healing talent down to `Light's Vigil`. They were written at launch, before level-60 gear existed, and no raid opens until December 9, 2026. Nothing measured stands behind them yet.
**What a raid asks of you, by Icy Veins' and Wowhead's pages:** `Oath of Light` on a tank, a `Seal of Light` or `Seal of Wisdom` Judgement kept up on the boss, a Greater Blessing per class, and the right aura. Wowhead's rotation page says a raid wants several Paladins for those buffs even though a Paladin's total healing can be on the low side.
**The Holy-damage "Shockadin" path is published as a Retribution build**, not a Holy one: 21 to 23 points in Holy for [[Holy Shock]], the rest in Retribution. It is on the [Retribution page](/guides/wow-forever/paladin/retribution/). Blizzard intends it to exist. The dive names the playstyle twice and supplies its spell Hit through `Divine Precision`, because plate healing gear will not carry any.
