## What Holy is for
**Keeping a party standing while in plate, with the mana tools to keep doing it.** Blizzard's own summary is that Holy sustains heals on a single target better than anything else in the game, that healing a whole party at once takes more planning, and that the new talents make Holy a viable healer across every form of group play. `Reverence` keeps 30% of your Spirit-driven mana regeneration running while you cast. `Illumination` refunds 50% of a spell's base cost when it procs.
The new `Light's Vigil` capstone is the party-healing half of that. Mark an ally, and your next [[Holy Shock]] on them triggers no cooldown and heals their whole party.
Underneath all of that you are still a melee Paladin: keep a seal up and press [[Holy Strike]] and [[Judgement]] on cooldown. Blizzard made that easier to hold together. [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your active Seal, and [[Holy Strike]] trains at level 6 for 50% of your weapon damage plus a flat Holy bonus that grows with each rank, though the rank you actually train there reads 25% of weapon damage plus 12 Holy in the client, on a 10-second cooldown.
**Dungeon quests reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values**, which Blizzard framed as a deliberate reduction in levelling speed. Kills inside dungeons pay about 20% more, so dungeons stay open to a Holy Paladin *(ours, not a source's)*.
No source publishes a levelling score for any Paladin spec. A score would only measure solo kill speed, not whether the spec works.
**Our own reading, not a sourced one:** most of what Holy produces lands on other people, because the Blessings, auras and heals all apply to the party. The tree does buy damage, and that part is Blizzard's: `Consecrated Ground` raises Holy spell damage, `Divine Precision` supplies its Hit, and the dive says Holy gains new tools for spell-focused damage.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Our own extension, not a published build. Icy Veins and Wowhead both opened with `Divine Intellect`, which now sits in Protection, so we adjusted the shape.
1. `Spiritual Focus`, 5 points. Row 1 holds only this and `Divine Strength`, and it gives your heals up to a 70% chance not to lose casting time when you take damage
2. `Healing Light`, 3 points. Blizzard moved it up to row 2, where it raises [[Holy Shock]] healing alongside [[Holy Light]] and [[Flash of Light]]
3. `Reverence`, 2 points, its full 30% and the prerequisite for `Illumination`
4. `Divine Favor`, 1 point, the 11-point gold medal
Wowhead preferred `Improved Seals` for the extra Judgement damage. It takes 5 points for its full 15%, so that route gives up `Healing Light` and `Reverence`.
`Healing Light`'s [[Holy Shock]] component does nothing until you train [[Holy Shock]] at **exactly level 30**. The level 30 build below trains it.
**Level 30: 21/0/0.** Our own extension, not a published build, reaching [[Holy Shock]]: `Spiritual Focus` 5, `Healing Light` 3, `Reverence` 2, `Purifying Power` 2, `Divine Favor` 1, `Illumination` 5, `Infusion of Light` 2, [[Holy Shock]] 1.
That line reaches [[Holy Shock]] with 20 points already spent, which the 21-point gold medal needs. `Oath of Light` is the 16-point gold medal, and this build passes over it because the game data carries no healing amounts for it yet *(ours, not a source's)*. [[Holy Shock]] runs on a 10-second cooldown, so treat it as part of your rotation rather than as a cooldown you save.
## Rotation
**Healing:**
1. **[[Flash of Light]]** as your primary heal, cheap and fast: a 1.5-second cast out to 40 yards, 35 mana at rank 1
2. **[[Holy Light]]** only with mana to spare, on someone in real danger. A 2.5-second cast at the same 40 yards, and its cost climbs hard with each rank
3. **[[Lay on Hands]]** as the emergency: it heals for your full health and drains all your mana, so you are finished healing for that fight, though the client notes your mana regeneration itself keeps running. Blizzard has it on a 20-minute cooldown, and it trains at 10
4. Maintain [[Blessing of Might]] on the party, plus [[Devotion Aura]] or [[Retribution Aura]]
While soloing, your damage rotation is **Retribution's minus [[Seal of Command]]**. Because [[Judgement]] leaves your Seal standing, you can judge on cooldown and keep swinging with the Seal still up.
**Multi-target: there effectively is none below 30.** `Light's Vigil` is a 31-point capstone and [[Consecration]] costs 135 mana at rank 1 on an 8-second cooldown, so area healing is spreading [[Flash of Light]] around.
## Stat priority
Healing Power, then Intellect, MP5, critical strike, Haste, Spirit.
If you are not planning to heal seriously, gear **Weapon Damage, Hit and critical strike** instead and play the Holy-damage path.
Blizzard says it does not plan to itemize Hit chance on plate healing gear, and that `Divine Precision` is there to supply it instead: up to 8% Hit with Holy spells and 20% shorter Silences, 2 points in row 4, with no [[Holy Shock]] requirement. On the Holy-damage path that talent is where your spell Hit comes from, not your gear.

## What Forever changed for Holy
- **Nine new talents:** `Reverence`, `Purifying Power` (up to 20% off the mana cost of `Cleanse` and `Purify`, and up to 33% off the [[Exorcism]] and [[Holy Wrath]] cooldowns), `Holy Conduit` (up to 40% off the mana cost of [[Consecration]], [[Holy Wrath]], [[Exorcism]] and `Hammer of Wrath`), `Divine Precision`, `Infusion of Light`, `Oath of Light` (swear an Oath on an ally for 5 minutes and your heals on them grow, with no amounts in the game data yet), `Consecrated Ground` (your Holy spells hit the first 4 enemies in your [[Consecration]] for up to 10% more), `Boundless Light` (up to 30% more critical strike chance for [[Holy Shock]] on allies without your Oath) and the `Light's Vigil` capstone.
- **[[Voice of Truth]] is trained at level 20**, not a talent: 6 seconds of immunity to Silence and Interrupt on a 3-minute cooldown.
- **`Improved Seals` replaces `Improved Seal of Righteousness`.** It raises the damage of every Seal and Judgement, up to 15% at 5 points.
- **The tree was re-laid-out.** [[Holy Shock]] is the 21-point gold medal now, with a 10-second cooldown. `Divine Favor` is the 11-point gold medal and no longer needs `Illumination`, and the client has it guaranteeing a critical on your next [[Flash of Light]], [[Holy Light]] or [[Holy Shock]] on a 2-minute cooldown. `Oath of Light` takes the 16-point slot. `Illumination` sits in row 3 under `Reverence`, at 20% per rank to 100% at 5 ranks, and its proc refunds 50% of the spell's base cost rather than all of it. `Healing Light` moved up to row 2, `Divine Intellect` moved to Protection and `Unyielding Faith` to Retribution.
- **`Light's Vigil` drags two older talents into its orbit.** `Spiritual Focus` now protects its cast as well, up to 70% at 14% per rank, and `Illumination` can proc off it.
- **[[Consecration]] left the tree entirely** to become baseline at 20 for every spec, redesigned so it deals moderate damage to the first 4 enemies that enter it and very little to anyone after them. Blizzard's stated reason is to hold Paladin area threat level with a Warrior's Thunderclap and keep Forever out of expansion-style AoE play. The client agrees: rank 1 does 16 Holy damage over 8 seconds to anything in the area, with an extra 32 over 8 seconds for the first four.
- **`Holy Power` is a critical healing talent:** it raises the critical healing bonus of your Holy spells, up to 50% at 5 points in row 6.
- **Baseline changes a healer feels.** [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your Seal. [[Lay on Hands]] sits on a 20-minute cooldown. The Fire, Frost and Shadow Resistance Auras reach every raid member within 30 yards.
- **`Blessing of Light` and `Greater Blessing of Light` are not taught**, so neither is part of a healer's kit.
- **Salvation is gone.** Trainers teach no `Blessing of Salvation`, `Salvation`, `Mass Salvation` or `Revoke`, so a Paladin has no threat-reduction spell to learn.
- **Three talents left the tree and Blizzard deleted none of them.** `Improved Blessing of Wisdom` and `Lasting Judgement` are baseline effects now, and `Improved Lay on Hands` is gone from the tree with its shorter [[Lay on Hands]] cooldown baseline. Over in Retribution, `Sacred Arbiter` refreshes Judgement effects through [[Holy Strike]], other Paladins' Judgements included.
- **Seal twisting is no longer part of the baseline class.** Retribution's 31-point capstone is `Unsealed Wrath`, and Blizzard placed Holy's 21-point [[Holy Shock]] as the deliberate alternative, so a hybrid can skip that capstone and spend into Holy instead. The same thinking drove the `Champion of the Light` nerf: Blizzard trimmed how much spell damage your Intellect buys there to stop Holy Paladins who give up Holy talents for deep Retribution getting too much out of it. It gives up to 60% of your Intellect as spell damage at 5 ranks.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether the Holy-damage "Shockadin" path holds up as a raid specialisation. Blizzard clearly intends it to exist. The deep dive names the playstyle twice, supplies its spell Hit through `Divine Precision` because plate healing gear will not carry any, and says moving [[Holy Shock]] earlier was partly to give hybrid builds room. Nothing published says whether a 51-point Holy build holds up in a level-60 raid.
