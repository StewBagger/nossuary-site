## What Holy is for
**Keeping a party standing while in plate, with the mana tools to keep doing it.** `Reverence` keeps a share of your mana regeneration running while you cast. `Illumination` refunds 50% of a spell's base cost when it procs. The new `Light's Vigil` capstone turns a [[Holy Shock]] on a marked ally into a heal for their whole party.
Underneath all of that you are still a melee Paladin: keep a seal up and press [[Holy Strike]] and [[Judgement]] on cooldown.
**Forever cut dungeon experience hard, and the October 1 build halved the extra experience on dungeon *quests* as well.** That closes Holy's usual escape hatch: "just level in dungeons" is largely gone.
No source publishes a levelling score for any Paladin spec. A score would only measure solo kill speed, not whether the spec works.
**Our own reading, not a sourced one:** Holy's output lands on other people. The Blessings, auras and heals all apply to the party, and no talent point in the tree buys damage.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Icy Veins and Wowhead agree on the shape, but not on the third talent.
1. `Divine Intellect`, 5 points
2. `Healing Light`, 3 points
3. `Spiritual Focus` or `Improved Seals`, 2 points. Icy Veins defaults to `Spiritual Focus` for pure healing. Wowhead's own build spends them on `Improved Seals` instead, for the extra Judgement damage. Either is defensible
4. `Reverence`, 1 point
`Healing Light`'s [[Holy Shock]] component does nothing until you train [[Holy Shock]] at **exactly level 30**. The level 30 build below trains it.
**Level 30: 21/0/0.** Our own extension, not a published build, reaching [[Holy Shock]]: `Divine Intellect` 5, `Healing Light` 3, `Spiritual Focus` 2, `Reverence` 3, `Purifying Power` 2, `Infusion of Light` 2, `Divine Favor` 1, `Illumination` 2, [[Holy Shock]] 1.
## Rotation
**Healing:**
1. **[[Flash of Light]]** as your primary heal, cheap and fast
2. **[[Holy Light]]** only with mana to spare, on someone in real danger
3. **[[Lay on Hands]]** as the emergency: it heals for your full health and consumes all your mana, so you are finished healing for that fight
4. Maintain [[Blessing of Might]] on the party, plus [[Devotion Aura]] or [[Retribution Aura]]. **Live bug as of October 1:** [[Retribution Aura]] reflects off the *target's* spell power instead of yours. Blizzard has not called that intended, so treat its output as unreliable until it is fixed
While soloing, your damage rotation is **Retribution's minus [[Seal of Command]]**.
**Multi-target: there effectively is none below 30.** `Light's Vigil` is a 31-point capstone and [[Consecration]] is expensive, so area healing is spreading [[Flash of Light]] around.
## Stat priority
Healing Power, then Intellect, MP5, critical strike, Haste, Spirit.
If you are not planning to heal seriously, gear **Weapon Damage, Hit and critical strike** instead and play the Holy-damage path.

## What Forever changed for Holy
- **Seven new talents**, including `Voice of Truth` (6 seconds of silence and interrupt immunity), `Reverence`, `Purifying Power`, `Infusion of Light`, `Divine Precision`, and the `Light's Vigil` capstone.
- **`Improved Seals` replaces `Improved Seal of Righteousness`**. It now buffs all seals and Judgements, but was cut from 5 ranks to 3.
- **The tree was re-laid-out.** [[Holy Shock]] moved from tier 7 to tier 5. `Divine Favor` moved up and no longer needs `Illumination`. `Illumination` now requires `Reverence`, and its proc refunds 50% of the spell's base cost rather than all of it, on a 20% chance per rank, so 40% at the two points this build spends.
- **[[Consecration]] left the tree entirely** to become baseline at 20 for every spec.
- **Three talents deleted:** `Improved Lay on Hands`, `Improved Blessing of Wisdom`, `Lasting Judgement`. The duration-extension role of that last one is now handled by `Sacred Arbiter`, which refreshes Judgement effects through [[Holy Strike]] over in Retribution.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether the Holy-damage "Shockadin" path is a real raid specialisation at all. `Light's Vigil`'s mechanics are no longer in doubt. Wowhead's beta tooltip and both other sources now agree on how the party heal, the subgroup limit and the mana refund work. Nothing published says whether a 51-point Holy build holds up in a level-60 raid.
