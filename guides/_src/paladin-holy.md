## What Holy is for
**Keeping a party standing while in plate, with the mana tools to keep doing it.** Blizzard's own summary is that Holy sustains heals on a single target better than anything else in the game, that healing a whole party at once takes more planning, and that the new talents make Holy a viable healer across every form of group play. `Reverence` keeps 30% of your Spirit-driven mana regeneration running while you cast. `Illumination` refunds 50% of a spell's base cost when it procs.
The new `Light's Vigil` capstone is the party-healing half of that. Mark an ally and their whole party gets healed, and your [[Holy Shock]] on them costs you no cooldown.
Underneath all of that you are still a melee Paladin: keep a seal up and press [[Holy Strike]] and [[Judgement]] on cooldown. Blizzard made that easier to hold together. [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your active Seal, and [[Holy Strike]] trains at level 6 for 50% of your weapon damage plus a flat Holy bonus that grows with each rank, though the rank you actually train there reads 25% of weapon damage plus 12 Holy in the client, on a 10-second cooldown.
**Forever cut dungeon experience hard, and the October 1 notes halved the extra experience on dungeon *quests* as well.** That closes Holy's usual escape hatch: "just level in dungeons" is largely gone.
No source publishes a levelling score for any Paladin spec. A score would only measure solo kill speed, not whether the spec works.
**Our own reading, not a sourced one:** most of what Holy produces lands on other people, because the Blessings, auras and heals all apply to the party. The tree does buy damage now, and that part is Blizzard's: `Consecrated Ground`, `Holy Power` and `Divine Precision` all feed Holy spell damage, and the dive says Holy gains new tools for spell-focused damage.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Icy Veins and Wowhead agree on the shape, but not on the third talent.
1. `Divine Intellect`, 5 points
2. `Healing Light`, 3 points. Blizzard moved it up to row 2, where it now raises [[Holy Shock]] healing alongside [[Holy Light]] and [[Flash of Light]]
3. `Spiritual Focus` or `Improved Seals`, 2 points. Icy Veins defaults to `Spiritual Focus` for pure healing. Wowhead's own build spends them on `Improved Seals` instead, for the extra Judgement damage. Either is defensible, and Blizzard has shrunk both: `Spiritual Focus` buys its full effect for 2 points and `Improved Seals` for 3
4. `Reverence`, 1 point
`Healing Light`'s [[Holy Shock]] component does nothing until you train [[Holy Shock]] at **exactly level 30**. The level 30 build below trains it.
**Level 30: 21/0/0.** Our own extension, not a published build, reaching [[Holy Shock]]: `Divine Intellect` 5, `Healing Light` 3, `Spiritual Focus` 2, `Reverence` 3, `Purifying Power` 2, `Infusion of Light` 2, `Divine Favor` 1, `Illumination` 2, [[Holy Shock]] 1.
That line reaches [[Holy Shock]] at 21 points because Blizzard moved it down to the 21-point gold medal and moved `Divine Favor` to the new 16-point one. It runs on a 10-second cooldown, so treat it as part of your rotation rather than as a cooldown you save.
## Rotation
**Healing:**
1. **[[Flash of Light]]** as your primary heal, cheap and fast: a 1.5-second cast out to 40 yards, 35 mana at rank 1
2. **[[Holy Light]]** only with mana to spare, on someone in real danger. A 2.5-second cast at the same 40 yards, and its cost climbs hard with each rank
3. **[[Lay on Hands]]** as the emergency: it heals for your full health and drains all your mana, so you are finished healing for that fight, though the client notes your mana regeneration itself keeps running. Blizzard has it on a 20-minute cooldown, and it trains at 10
4. Maintain [[Blessing of Might]] on the party, plus [[Devotion Aura]] or [[Retribution Aura]]. **Live bug as of October 1:** [[Retribution Aura]] reflects off the *target's* spell power instead of yours. Blizzard has not called that intended, so treat its output as unreliable until it is fixed
While soloing, your damage rotation is **Retribution's minus [[Seal of Command]]**. Because [[Judgement]] leaves your Seal standing, you can judge on cooldown and keep swinging with the Seal still up.
**Multi-target: there effectively is none below 30.** `Light's Vigil` is a 31-point capstone and [[Consecration]] costs 135 mana at rank 1 on an 8-second cooldown, so area healing is spreading [[Flash of Light]] around.
## Stat priority
Healing Power, then Intellect, MP5, critical strike, Haste, Spirit.
If you are not planning to heal seriously, gear **Weapon Damage, Hit and critical strike** instead and play the Holy-damage path.
Blizzard says it does not plan to itemize Hit chance on plate healing gear, and that `Divine Precision` is there to supply it instead: up to 18% Hit with Holy spells, in row 5 behind [[Holy Shock]]. On the Holy-damage path that talent is where your spell Hit comes from, not your gear.

## What Forever changed for Holy
- **Seven new talents**, all of them Blizzard's: `Voice of Truth` (the 11-point gold medal, 6 seconds of immunity to Silence and Interrupt on a 3-minute cooldown), `Reverence`, `Purifying Power` (up to 20% off the mana cost of `Cleanse` and `Purify`, and up to 33% off the [[Exorcism]] and [[Holy Wrath]] cooldowns, where the client reads 50%), `Infusion of Light`, `Divine Precision`, `Consecrated Ground` (your Holy spells hit the first 4 enemies in your [[Consecration]] for up to 10% more) and the `Light's Vigil` capstone.
- **`Improved Seals` replaces `Improved Seal of Righteousness`.** Blizzard has it raising the damage of every Seal and Judgement, and it costs 3 points for the full effect rather than 5.
- **The tree was re-laid-out.** [[Holy Shock]] is the 21-point gold medal now, with a 10-second cooldown. `Divine Favor` is the new 16-point gold medal and no longer needs `Illumination`; Blizzard moved its position, not its effect, and the client has it guaranteeing a critical on your next [[Flash of Light]], [[Holy Light]] or [[Holy Shock]] on a 2-minute cooldown. `Illumination` requires `Reverence`, and its proc refunds 50% of the spell's base cost rather than all of it; the client's row for it carries no text, so there is no proc chance to read off the game. `Healing Light` and `Unyielding Faith` both moved up to row 2.
- **`Unyielding Faith` does a different job.** It shortens Fear and Disorient effects instead of improving your chance to resist them, and the client puts that at 30% off their duration.
- **`Light's Vigil` drags two older talents into its orbit.** `Spiritual Focus` now protects its cast as well — the client gives that a 14% chance not to lose casting time when you take damage — and `Illumination` can proc off it.
- **[[Consecration]] left the tree entirely** to become baseline at 20 for every spec, redesigned so it deals moderate damage to the first 4 enemies that enter it and very little to anyone after them. Blizzard's stated reason is to hold Paladin area threat level with a Warrior's Thunderclap and keep Forever out of expansion-style AoE play. The client agrees: rank 1 does 16 Holy damage over 8 seconds to anything in the area, with an extra 32 over 8 seconds for the first four.
- **`Holy Power` does two jobs now:** up to 15% critical strike chance on [[Holy Shock]] and [[Holy Strike]], and up to 5% on your other spells.
- **Baseline changes a healer feels.** [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your Seal. [[Lay on Hands]] sits on a 20-minute cooldown. The Fire, Frost and Shadow Resistance Auras reach every raid member within 30 yards.
- **`Salvation` is no longer a Blessing**, so a target can carry a Blessing and `Salvation` at once, and the raid version that covers a whole class is renamed `Mass Salvation`. Blizzard has published this and the game does not have it: the dive says the change "is not yet live and is going into a future beta build", and the client still holds [[Blessing of Salvation]] at level 26 cutting threat by 30%, with neither new name present.
- **Three talents left the tree and Blizzard deleted none of them.** `Improved Blessing of Wisdom` and `Lasting Judgement` are baseline effects now, and `Improved Lay on Hands` is listed as altered rather than removed because the shorter [[Lay on Hands]] cooldown went baseline — the client holds no row for that talent at all, so it is published and absent rather than gone from the tree on Blizzard's word. Over in Retribution, `Sacred Arbiter` refreshes Judgement effects through [[Holy Strike]], other Paladins' Judgements included.
- **Seal twisting is no longer part of the baseline class.** Blizzard rebuilt it as Retribution's 31-point `Twist of Light` and placed Holy's 21-point [[Holy Shock]] as the deliberate alternative, so a hybrid can skip twisting and spend into Holy instead. The same thinking drove the `Champion of the Light` nerf: Blizzard trimmed how much spell damage your Intellect buys there to stop Holy Paladins who give up Holy talents for deep Retribution getting too much out of it. Blizzard prints two sizes for that talent inside one article and the client prints a third, so the Retribution page carries all three and no hybrid build should lean on the figure.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether the Holy-damage "Shockadin" path holds up as a raid specialisation. Blizzard clearly intends it to exist. The deep dive names the playstyle twice, supplies its spell Hit through `Divine Precision` because plate healing gear will not carry any, and says moving [[Holy Shock]] earlier was partly to give hybrid builds room. `Light's Vigil`'s mechanics are no longer in doubt either: Blizzard's own description now carries the party heal, the one-per-party limit and the 75% refund that the three sites had pieced together from beta tooltips. Nothing published says whether a 51-point Holy build holds up in a level-60 raid.
