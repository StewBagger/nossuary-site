## Is Discipline worth levelling?
**Slower to solo, excellent in a group.** The ratings only measure the first. The published heading is *"Struggles To Solo Level"*: *"Discipline Priest has decent single-target damage, but they lack in AoE damage. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Discipline, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
Ratings: **Overall 4.5, Levelling 2.5, Dungeon 5.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0.** Best dungeon healer in the class, among the worst soloers in the game.
**If you are soloing, level Shadow and respec.** That is what the guides recommend. **`Dual Specialization` becomes available at level 40**, so above that you keep both; below it, respeccing is a trainer visit. In the beta that visit costs **1 silver** if you have not changed talents recently, much more for a second reset, and it falls back to 1 silver once an hour has passed — Blizzard calls the price *"temporary for the Beta only."*
**If you are levelling in groups, you do not need to.** Discipline is rated 5.0 out of 5 for dungeons, the highest of any Priest spec, and a healer who is always wanted never waits for a group. The October 1 build cut dungeon **quest** experience, though — *"Dungeon quests now reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values"* — so the dungeon route pays less than it did, and what recommends it is the instant group a healer gets, not the experience per hour.
**The ratings ignore one solo pattern: shield and wand.** [[Power Word: Shield]] absorbs the damage, your wand does the killing, and you spend almost no mana. It is slow and close to unkillable. Discipline has soloed this way since vanilla.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: Icy Veins and Wowhead each publish a build, and they do not spend the same 11 points.**
**Icy Veins' build:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → **`Spirit Tap` 5** (Shadow) → the remaining 4 into `Twilight Focus` and/or `Improved Power Word: Shield`, whichever you are short on. The first two are called *"the most critical"* and *"the second most important"*; Icy Veins does not pin down the exact split of the last 4.
**Wowhead's build** is the same shape it gives every Priest spec at 20, since the early rows are generic: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Twilight Focus` 3 (Holy tree) → `Improved Renew` 2 → `Divine Fury` 4. No `Spirit Tap`, and no Discipline-tree talent beyond the wand point.
**ClassicWoW.gg publishes no level-20 point order** — its talent page is the full level-60 tree, and it says plainly that this is "the full tree, not a suggested build." Its prose on the damage path: lead with `Power in Light` and `Holy Precision` for Holy-spell hit and the Holy Fire/Smite damage bonus, then strengthen those casts with `Holy Specialization`, `Divine Fury` and `Searing Light`.
**No source publishes a level-30 Discipline build, healing or damage.**
**Level 30, 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, in order: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Power in Light` 5 → `Holy Precision` 1 → `Martyrdom` 2 → [[Inner Focus]] 1 → `Meditation` 3 → `Mental Agility` 1 → `Mental Strength` 5 → **[[Penance]]** at 30.
## Rotation
**Solo damage:**
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]]** and **[[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Holy Fire]]** on the furthest or highest-priority target, which sets up `Power in Light`
4. **[[Smite]]** as it closes
5. **[[Mind Blast]]**
6. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
7. **Wand until it dies**: *"generally recommended to use your wand to finish off a foe rather than [[Smite]] or [[Mind Blast]]"*
**Healing:** [[Power Word: Shield]] to buy yourself a cast → [[Heal]] → [[Renew]] for chip damage. From 30, [[Penance]] on a 12-second cooldown.
**Multi-target: none is published, and the spec's one listed weakness is *"lacks group/AoE healing."***
## Stat priority
**Healing:** Healing Power, then MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike — Icy Veins' order.
**Critical strike is talked up here where Holy talks it down**: `Renewed Hope` and `Divine Aegis` both trigger off healing crits.
**Do not itemise Hit for healing gear.** `Holy Precision` grants up to 18% Holy spell hit from the talent tree alone, and Blizzard states the intent outright: the talent exists *"to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance."*
**For the Smite damage build:** Spell Power or Holy power, then Hit, Intellect, critical strike. [[Smite]] and [[Holy Fire]] are Holy-school spells too, so `Holy Precision` chips away at this build's Hit need as well, just without Blizzard's explicit healer framing.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** `Spiritual Guidance` in the Holy tree now gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"*; nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.
## What defines Discipline
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Penance]]** | **exactly 30** | New, the 21-point gold medal talent. 12-second cooldown, channelled over 2 seconds; targets an enemy for damage each second or an ally for healing each second |
| **`Soul Warding`** | **25** | The 16-point milestone: reduces [[Power Word: Shield]]'s cooldown and its Mana cost, by amounts Blizzard does not publish. Requires `Improved Power Word: Shield`, and in turn gates `Renewed Hope` |
| **`Divine Aegis`** | 35 | Critical heals leave an absorb based on a percentage of the amount healed — Blizzard does not say what percentage — for 12 seconds, non-stacking |
| **`Power in Light`** | **10** | Tier 1, so available with your first point. The engine of the Smite build, and why [[Holy Fire]] opens the rotation |
| **`Holy Precision`** | — | Up to 18% Holy spell hit. Blizzard's stated purpose: healing Priests should not need to itemise Hit at all |
## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Power in Light`, `Twin Disciplines`, `Holy Precision`, `Soul Warding`, [[Penance]], `Renewed Hope`, `Divine Aegis`. **Removed:** `Unbreakable Will` (folded into `Silent Resolve`), `Improved Power Word: Fortitude` (baseline now), `Force of Will`.
- **`Renewed Hope`** (new, requires `Soul Warding`): raises crit chance of [[Flash Heal]], `Binding Heal`, [[Lesser Heal]], [[Heal]], [[Greater Heal]] and [[Penance]] against a target carrying Weakened Soul, and each of those casts also shortens Weakened Soul's remaining duration.
- **`Silent Resolve` now also shortens Stun, Fear and Silence durations on you** — the effect `Unbreakable Will` used to cover alone.
- **`Mental Agility` moved from row 4 to row 3**, now only 3 points for the same mana-cost reduction, and now also discounts [[Smite]] and [[Holy Fire]].
- **`Improved Power Word: Shield` gives slightly more per point** (and gates `Soul Warding`, above). **`Improved Inner Fire`'s armor bonus is larger and now adds charges.** **`Improved Mana Burn`'s cast-time cut is bigger.** None of the three are in the level-30 build above, which spends its points on the Smite-damage line instead.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is no longer a talent. It is trained from level 30.**
- **`Inner Focus` no longer boosts periodic effects, fixed in the October 1 build.** Its Critical Strike increase (and free next cast) now applies only to your next non-periodic cast — a HoT or DoT tick landing under it, including [[Penance]]'s own channelled ticks, gets nothing extra from it. Pop it before a direct cast, not to fish for a crit on a periodic one.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, which is why every wand-levelling build front-loads it. Separately, **wands no longer gain spell power** as of the September 24 patch.
- **`Meditation`'s mana benefit from Spirit is *"increased substantially"*** — Blizzard's phrasing, and the post attaches no number to it, so the per-rank figures circulating are the client's rather than Blizzard's. `Mental Strength` now grants **+3% Intellect per rank, 15% at 5/5**, which this build takes.
- **[[Power Word: Shield]] can now overwrite an existing shield** on a target who has no Weakened Soul.
- **The client makes [[Mind Flay]] talent-only in Forever**, gated on 10 points in Shadow, where Classic taught it at level 20. **No published Discipline build reaches it**, so in practice you will not have it. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether absorb-stacking (`Divine Aegis` plus `Renewed Hope` plus `Soul Warding`) beats raw throughput in raids. [[Penance]]'s numbers scale off gear whose stats are **hidden until it drops**, and no raid has been tested.
