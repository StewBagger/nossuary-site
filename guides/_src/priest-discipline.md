## What Discipline is for
**Preventing damage instead of repairing it.** [[Power Word: Shield]] absorbs the hit before it lands, and `Soul Warding` at 25 cuts that shield's cooldown and its Mana cost. `Divine Aegis` leaves a fresh absorb behind every critical heal. From exactly 30, [[Penance]] channels damage or healing each second on a 12-second cooldown.
**The same mechanic is the solo pattern: shield and wand.** No source scores it, so that reading is ours. [[Power Word: Shield]] absorbs the damage, your wand does the killing, and you spend almost no mana. It is slow and close to unkillable. Discipline has soloed this way since vanilla.
The published ratings: Overall 4.5, Levelling 2.5, Dungeon 5.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0. That 5.0 is the highest dungeon score of any Priest spec, and the 2.5 is the second-lowest levelling score of the three. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** The published heading is *"Struggles To Solo Level"*: *"Discipline Priest has decent single-target damage, but they lack in AoE damage. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Discipline, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
**If you are soloing, level Shadow and respec.** That is what the guides recommend. `Dual Specialization` becomes available at level 40, so above that you keep both; below it, respeccing is a trainer visit. The 1-silver price was for the beta only, and Blizzard has published no launch price.
**If you are levelling in groups, you do not need to.** That 5.0 out of 5 is the highest dungeon rating of any Priest spec. Blizzard did trim dungeon quest experience: *"Dungeon quests now reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values"*. That a healer finds a group quickly is our own reading, not a published rating.
## Your talent points
Level 20: **Icy Veins and Wowhead each publish a build**, and they do not spend the same 11 points.
**Icy Veins' build, adjusted — our own extension, not a published build:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 (Shadow) → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Improved Renew` 1. Icy Veins put the last 4 into `Twilight Focus` and `Improved Power Word: Shield`, but that talent now sits in row 3, behind 10 Discipline points, so we moved its share to `Improved Renew`. Icy Veins calls the first two *"the most critical"* and *"the second most important"*.
**Wowhead's build** is a generic early shape: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Twilight Focus` 3 (Holy tree) → `Improved Renew` 2 → `Divine Fury` 4. No `Spirit Tap`, and no Discipline-tree talent beyond the wand point.
**ClassicWoW.gg publishes no level-20 point order.** Its talent page is the full level-60 tree, and it says plainly that this is "the full tree, not a suggested build." Its prose on the damage path: lead with `Power in Light` and `Holy Precision` for Holy-spell hit and the Holy Fire/Smite damage bonus. Then strengthen those casts with `Holy Specialization`, `Divine Fury` and `Searing Light`.
No source had published a level-30 Discipline build, healing or damage, when this page was written.
**Level 30, 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** In order: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Power in Light` 5 → `Holy Precision` 1 → `Martyrdom` 2 → [[Inner Focus]] 1 → `Meditation` 3 → `Mental Agility` 1 → `Mental Strength` 5 → [[Penance]] at 30.
## Rotation
**Solo damage:**
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Holy Fire]]** or **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]** on the furthest or highest-priority target, either of which sets up `Power in Light`. Holy Fire is a 3.5-second cast and Pain is instant, so the order is your call *(ours)*
4. **[[Smite]]** as it closes
5. **[[Mind Blast]]**
6. **Wand until it dies**: *"generally recommended to use your wand to finish off a foe rather than [[Smite]] or [[Mind Blast]]"*
**Healing:** [[Power Word: Shield]] to buy yourself a cast → [[Heal]] → [[Renew]] for chip damage. From 30, [[Penance]] on a 12-second cooldown.
**Multi-target: none is published.** The spec's one listed weakness is *"lacks group/AoE healing."*
## Stat priority
**Healing:** Healing Power, then MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike — Icy Veins' order.
**Critical strike is talked up here where Holy talks it down**: `Divine Aegis` triggers off critical heals, and `Renewed Hope` raises the crit chance feeding it.
**Do not itemise Hit for healing gear.** `Holy Precision` grants up to 18% Holy spell hit from the talent tree alone. Blizzard states the intent outright: the talent exists *"to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance."*
**For the Smite damage build:** Spell Power or Holy power, then Hit, Intellect, critical strike. [[Smite]] and [[Holy Fire]] are Holy-school spells too, so `Holy Precision` chips away at this build's Hit need as well, just without Blizzard's explicit healer framing.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** `Spiritual Guidance` in the Holy tree now gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"*; nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.
## What defines Discipline
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Penance]]** | exactly 30 | New, the 21-point gold medal talent. 12-second cooldown, channelled over 2 seconds; targets an enemy for damage each second or an ally for healing each second |
| **`Soul Warding`** | 25 | The 16-point milestone. The client takes 4 seconds off [[Power Word: Shield]]'s cooldown and 15% off its Mana cost; Blizzard publishes no figure. The shield's cooldown is 4 seconds, so that clears it, and only the target's 15-second Weakened Soul still limits you *(our reading)*. Requires `Improved Power Word: Shield`, and in turn gates `Renewed Hope` |
| **`Divine Aegis`** | 35 | Non-periodic critical heals leave an absorb for 12 seconds, non-stacking. The client reads 5/10/15% of the amount healed, up to 1200 damage; Blizzard publishes no percentage |
| **`Power in Light`** | 10 | Tier 1, so available with your first point. 2% per rank on [[Smite]] and [[Penance]] against a target with your [[Holy Fire]] or [[Shadow Word: Pain]]. The engine of the Smite build |
| **`Holy Precision`** | 15 | Up to 18% Holy spell hit. Blizzard's stated purpose: healing Priests should not need to itemise Hit at all |
## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Power in Light`, `Twin Disciplines`, `Holy Precision`, `Soul Warding`, [[Penance]], `Renewed Hope`, `Divine Aegis`. *Removed:* `Unbreakable Will` (folded into `Silent Resolve`), `Improved Power Word: Fortitude` (baseline now), `Force of Will`.
- **`Renewed Hope`** (new, requires `Soul Warding`): raises crit chance of [[Flash Heal]], [[Lesser Heal]], [[Heal]], [[Greater Heal]] and [[Penance]] by 2% per rank against a target carrying Weakened Soul. Each of those casts also takes 1 second per rank off Weakened Soul's remaining duration.
- **`Silent Resolve`** now also shortens Stun, Fear and Silence durations on you, the effect `Unbreakable Will` used to cover alone.
- **`Mental Agility`** moved from row 4 to row 3, now only 3 points for the same mana-cost reduction, and it now also discounts [[Smite]] and [[Holy Fire]].
- **`Improved Power Word: Shield`** gives slightly more per point, and gates `Soul Warding` above. `Improved Inner Fire`'s armor bonus is larger and now adds charges. `Improved Mana Burn`'s cast-time cut is bigger. None of the three are in the level-30 build above, which spends its points on the Smite-damage line instead.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is no longer a talent.** It is trained from level 30.
- **`Inner Focus` does not boost periodic effects.** Its Critical Strike increase, and the free cast that comes with it, now apply only to your next non-periodic cast. A HoT or DoT tick landing under it gets nothing extra, including [[Penance]]'s own channelled ticks. Pop it before a direct cast.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, so every wand-levelling build front-loads it. Wands no longer gain spell power.
- **`Meditation`'s mana benefit from Spirit is *"increased substantially"*.** That is Blizzard's phrasing, with no number attached. The client reads 17/33/50% of your Mana regeneration continuing while casting. `Mental Strength` is row 2 and grants +2% Intellect per rank, 10% at 5/5, which this build takes.
- **[[Power Word: Shield]] can now overwrite an existing shield** on a target who has no Weakened Soul.
- **The client makes [[Mind Flay]] talent-only**, gated on 10 points in Shadow and on level 20, exactly as Classic did. No published Discipline build reaches it, so in practice you will not have it. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether absorb-stacking (`Divine Aegis` plus `Renewed Hope` plus `Soul Warding`) beats raw throughput in raids. [[Penance]]'s numbers scale off gear whose stats are **hidden until it drops**, and no raid has been tested.
