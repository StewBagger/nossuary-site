Shields and mitigation rather than repair: *"there cannot be Light without Dark."* **The spec is built on [[Power Word: Shield]]**, an absorb that stops damage before it lands. `Soul Warding` cuts its cooldown and Mana cost, [[Penance]] is the heal-or-nuke on a 12-second cooldown, and `Power Infusion` sits at the top of the tree.

## What Discipline is for
**Preventing damage instead of repairing it.** [[Power Word: Shield]] absorbs the hit before it lands, and `Soul Warding` cuts that shield's cooldown and its Mana cost. `Divine Aegis` leaves a fresh absorb behind every critical heal. [[Penance]] channels damage or healing each second on a 12-second cooldown. From 40 at the earliest, `Power Infusion` gives one ally 20% spell damage and healing for 15 seconds.
**The same mechanic is the solo pattern: shield and wand.** [[Power Word: Shield]] absorbs the damage, your wand does the killing, and you spend almost no Mana. Wowhead's levelling page calls the result *"near-unkillable"* and lists the cost: *"Rotation is dominated by auto-attacking with your wand early on."*
Icy Veins' ratings: Overall 4.5, Levelling 2.5, Dungeon 5.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** Its heading is *"No AoE, Struggles To Solo Level"*: *"Discipline Priest has decent single-target damage, but they lack in AoE damage. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Discipline, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
**The two sites do not agree on how slow it is.** Wowhead's levelling page (November 4) calls Discipline *"a reasonable choice to level as"* and *"slightly slower"* than Shadow, most of all past 40. Icy Veins recommends Shadow talents for a solo player. Both agree it is the easier of the two healing specs to solo with, because its tree carries damage talents.
**If you are levelling in groups, you do not need Shadow.** Icy Veins rates Discipline 5.0 out of 5 for dungeons. Blizzard trimmed dungeon quest experience before launch: *"Dungeon quests now reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values"*. On November 8 it raised kill experience inside dungeons by 50%.
A respec is a trainer visit. Icy Veins puts the first at 1 gold, rising each time. `Dual Specialization` is bought from the class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold (Wowhead's report; Blizzard gives the level and no price).
## Your talent points
**Icy Veins publishes a full 10-to-60 order** (its talent builds were last updated November 4). The four lines below are that one order, cut at 20, 30, 40 and 60.
**Level 20, 3/3/5 — Icy Veins' levelling order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Power in Light` 1.
**Level 30, 13/3/5 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Power in Light` 5 → `Mental Strength` 3 → `Meditation` 3.
**Level 40, 23/3/5 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Power in Light` 5 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Meditation` 3 → `Improved Power Word: Shield` 3 → `Soul Warding` 1 → `Mental Agility` 1 → [[Penance]] → `Renewed Hope` 2.
**Level 60, 33/13/5 — the same order, finished:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Power in Light` 5 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Meditation` 3 → `Improved Power Word: Shield` 3 → `Soul Warding` 1 → `Mental Agility` 3 → [[Penance]] → `Renewed Hope` 5 → `Divine Aegis` 3 → `Power Infusion` 1 → `Holy Specialization` 5 → `Divine Fury` 5 → [[Inner Focus]].
**That order reaches [[Penance]] at 38, not 30**, because eight early points sit in Shadow and Holy. `Meditation` lands at 30, `Soul Warding` at 36 and `Power Infusion` at 48. Icy Veins offers a respec at 35 to take Penance sooner: heavy Discipline, keeping `Spirit Tap` 5.
**In a group, Icy Veins swaps `Twilight Focus` for `Silent Resolve`**: threat reduction instead of pushback protection, since someone else is being hit.
**Wowhead's levelling build (Voulk, updated November 4)** is published as an interactive calculator on [its levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/discipline/healer-leveling-tips); its text gives no split and no order. It names its required picks: `Wand Specialization`, `Meditation`, `Improved Inner Fire` and [[Penance]], then `Twilight Focus` and `Divine Fury` in Holy *"before we start to transition from a wand-playstyle to a heavier cast-based one"*. It treats `Spirit Tap` as optional and says those five points can go to Discipline for `Power Infusion` instead.
**Level 60, 31/20/0 — Wowhead's raid build (Voulk, November 4):** `Twin Disciplines` 5 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Mental Agility` 3 → [[Inner Focus]] → `Improved Power Word: Shield` 3 → `Meditation` 3 → `Soul Warding` 1 → [[Penance]] → `Renewed Hope` 5 → `Divine Aegis` 3 → `Power Infusion` 1 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Holy Specialization` 5 → `Divine Fury` 5 → `Blessed Recovery` 1 → `Inspiration` 3 → `Improved Healing` 3.
That line is listed tree by tree, not in the order you would level it. Wowhead's dungeon build is 33/18/0: it adds `Wand Specialization` 2, one point of `Holy Precision` and [[Holy Nova]], and gives up one `Mental Agility`, two `Improved Renew` and `Blessed Recovery`.
**Icy Veins' level-60 build, "The Mitigation Machine", is also 31/20/0** and has the same 31 Discipline points. Its 20 Holy points differ: `Twilight Focus` 3, [[Holy Nova]], `Searing Light` 2 and one `Holy Reach` where Wowhead takes `Improved Renew`, `Blessed Recovery` and `Improved Healing`. WoWSims' Discipline preset is 32/19/0 and takes `Holy Binding` *(a sim preset, not a recommendation)*.
**All three skip `Power in Light`, `Wand Specialization` and `Spirit Tap` at 60.** Those are levelling picks, so expect a respec at 60 whichever order you levelled on.
## Rotation
**Solo, while the wand does the work.** Wowhead's open-world list:
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up, and [[Divine Spirit]] from 30
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Penance]]** to open once you have it, or **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]** when other players are competing for the tag, because it is instant
4. **Wand until it dies.** Refresh [[Shadow Word: Pain]] on a long fight
5. **[[Renew]]** on yourself if your health worries you
**Solo, once your casts out-damage the wand.** Wowhead puts the switch at about level 41 on its own build and says gear moves it. Its list, with Icy Veins' additions marked:
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]], [[Divine Spirit]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Holy Fire]]** from range. It is a 3.5-second cast, and it arms `Power in Light`
4. **[[Penance]]** on the burning target
5. **[[Smite]]** while Penance is on cooldown
6. **[[Mind Blast]] and [[Shadow Word: Pain]]** as the enemy reaches you *(Icy Veins' list has these; Wowhead's does not)*
7. **Wand to finish** *(Icy Veins: "It's generally a good idea to use your wand to finish off a foe")*
**Healing a dungeon.** Wowhead's list:
1. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** on the tank before the pull
2. **[[Renew]]** on the tank, and on anyone with light damage
3. **[[Penance]] and [[Heal]]** as your main single-target heals, [[Greater Heal]] from 40
4. **[[Flash Heal]]** only when someone is about to die. Both sites call it the expensive one
5. **[[Prayer of Healing]]** from 30 when the party is low. It heals the party of the target you cast it on
6. **Wand** when nobody needs healing, and drink between pulls
**Cooldowns:** [[Inner Focus]] makes your next spell free and adds 25% crit chance to it. Wowhead pairs it with [[Prayer of Healing]] or [[Greater Heal]] and says not to hoard it. `Power Infusion` is a 3-minute cooldown you can put on an ally.
**Keep a low rank of [[Renew]] and [[Heal]] on your bars.** Wowhead says a lower rank is often the more efficient cast and that the game penalises ranks learned more than 18 levels ago. Doublezug's launch-week video makes the same case for levelling dungeons: low ranks on damage dealers and on yourself, top ranks for a tank taking heavy damage *(one creator's advice, [at 4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88d4Etzi9t4&t=245))*. Low ranks lose no talent procs in Forever.
**Multi-target: [[Prayer of Healing]] is the only area heal.** Icy Veins: *"The only thing that Discipline lacks is group/AoE healing."* [[Holy Nova]] is a Holy talent some Discipline builds take.
## Stat priority
**Healing, Icy Veins' order:** Healing Power, MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike.
**Healing, Wowhead's order:** Healing Power, Intellect, Spirit, critical strike. Wowhead labels its list speculative and wrote it before launch.
**They differ on Intellect.** Both give the same numbers: 15 Mana a point, and about 59 Intellect for 1% spell crit. Spirit regenerates Spirit divided by 4, plus 12.5, Mana every 2 seconds when you have not cast for 5 seconds, and `Meditation` keeps half of that running while you cast.
**Critical strike is worth more here than on Holy**: `Divine Aegis` and `Inspiration` trigger off critical heals, and `Renewed Hope` raises the crit chance feeding them. Icy Veins makes that point on its stat page.
**Do not itemise Hit for healing gear.** `Holy Precision` grants up to 18% Holy spell hit from the talent tree alone. Blizzard states the intent outright: the talent exists *"to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance."*
**For Smite damage:** [[Smite]], [[Holy Fire]] and [[Penance]] are Holy spells, so `Holy Precision` 3/3 covers the 17% a caster needs against a raid boss. No site publishes a stat order for a Smite build.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** `Spiritual Guidance` in the Holy tree gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"*; nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Power in Light`, `Twin Disciplines`, `Holy Precision`, `Soul Warding`, [[Penance]], `Renewed Hope`, `Divine Aegis`. *Removed:* `Unbreakable Will` (folded into `Silent Resolve`), `Improved Power Word: Fortitude` (baseline now), `Force of Will`.
- **`Power Infusion` is still the top of the tree**, and now requires [[Penance]].
- **`Silent Resolve`** is 3 ranks: 10% less threat a rank, and it now also shortens Stun, Fear and Silence on you by 5% a rank.
- **`Mental Agility`** moved from row 4 to row 3, now only 3 points for the same 10% Mana-cost reduction on instant spells, and it now also discounts [[Smite]] and [[Holy Fire]].
- **`Mental Strength`** is row 2 and grants 2% Intellect a rank, 10% at 5/5, where Classic gave Mana.
- **`Improved Power Word: Shield`** reaches 20% at 3/3 and gates `Soul Warding`. `Improved Inner Fire` reaches 45% more armour and 12 more charges at 3/3. `Improved Mana Burn` takes a full second off the cast at 2/2.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is no longer a talent.** It is trained from level 30, and `Prayer of Spirit` at 60.
- **[[Inner Focus]] does not boost periodic effects.** Its crit bonus applies only to a non-periodic spell. The free cast applies to anything. Pop it before a direct cast.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5. Wands no longer gain spell power.
- **`Meditation`'s Mana benefit from Spirit is *"increased substantially"*.** That is Blizzard's phrasing. The client reads 17/33/50% of your Mana regeneration continuing while casting.
- **[[Power Word: Shield]] can replace a shield already on the target** when yours is of equal or higher rank, on a target who has no Weakened Soul.
- **Shielding a Warrior or Druid tank no longer starves their Rage.** Blizzard's class post says Rage from taking damage now ignores all absorbs.
- **[[Penance]] hits and heals for less than its first beta numbers.** Blizzard's development notes say it had been treated as a 3-second channel rather than 2, cut its damage and healing to match, and raised rank 1's Mana cost by half. Blizzard's stated aim is that it keeps a consistent Mana-efficiency edge over [[Heal]] and [[Greater Heal]].
- **`Renewed Hope` no longer names Binding Heal**, because Blizzard removed that spell on November 2.
- **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only**, gated on 10 points in Shadow. No published Discipline build reaches it. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
**The published builds agree on the Discipline half.** Wowhead's raid build, Icy Veins' build and WoWSims' preset all take `Soul Warding`, [[Penance]], `Renewed Hope` 5, `Divine Aegis` 3 and `Power Infusion`, and spend the rest in Holy. Wowhead notes a deep-Shadow alternative for `Dark Inspiration` and calls it very expensive.
**What nobody has measured:** whether stacked absorbs beat raw healing in a raid. Wowhead's rotation page says plainly that it has not seen endgame content. Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9, 2026.
**A Holy-damage build is talked about and not published.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both raise a Smite, [[Holy Fire]] and [[Penance]] damage build as a possibility. WoWSims carries a 31/17/3 Smite test preset *(a model's test build)*. No site recommends one.
