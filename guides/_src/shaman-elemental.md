Ranged caster mixing hard lightning casts with Shocks, interrupts and totems. **Its new talents arrive from 25 on**: `Earthbound` is the 16-point milestone at 25, `Lightning Overload` and `Call of Thunder` sit at 21 points, level 30, and `Lightning Mastery` is the 31-point capstone at 40.

## What Elemental is for
**Lightning burst at range.** [[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds from rank 4 at level 20. `Elemental Focus` at exactly 20 gives a 10% Clearcasting chance on any Fire, Frost or Nature spell, and `Lightning Overload` at 30 adds a chance at a free, half-damage, no-threat duplicate of the Bolt. [[Chain Lightning]] is the other half of that burst, a 2.5-second cast that arrives at level 32.
**Weak at low levels, and the source says so directly.** Its heading is *"Weak Damage"*, and under it: *"Because they are denied access to some of their best talents on the Level 20 bracket, such as Lava Burst and `Elemental Alacrity`, Elemental is quite weak at low levels. This is also not helped by the lowering of base spell damage in WoW Forever in favor of gear with more Spell Damage."*
**That quotation is out of date on both of its examples.** `Elemental Alacrity` sits at row 2 now, so your 6th point reaches it at level 15, and 3/3 lands at 17. It is also cheaper and weaker than the talent the quote assumes: 3 points for 0.5 seconds off [[Lightning Bolt]] and [[Chain Lightning]]. Blizzard cut it because it *"was twice as strong as similar talents with other classes, causing a spell power scaling problem."* Lava Burst is no longer in the game. The base-spell-damage half of the quotation still holds for [[Lightning Bolt]].
**The base-damage half matters more than it reads.** Blizzard's November 2 notes raised class damage across the game and gave higher-level creatures more health to match. The Shocks deal Classic's base damage, but each rank of [[Lightning Bolt]] deals roughly 78% of Classic's, with a shorter cast, and the rest of the power sits on gear. One guide spells out the consequence: *"all caster specs, and for Shaman that's Elemental, will need to constantly upgrade their gear to deal decent damage."* An Elemental Shaman in stale gear is genuinely weak in a way a Classic one was not *(ours, not a source's)*.

## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Two builds, both legal.
**Build A — our own extension, not a published build.** We adjusted Icy Veins' list because its last 2 points went to a row 3 talent this list cannot reach yet, so we use the `Convection` swap the same source offers.
1. `Concussion`, 5 points
2. `Call of Flame`, 3 points
3. `Convection`, 2 points
4. **`Elemental Focus`** at 20
**Build B:** `Convection` 5 / `Concussion` 5 / `Elemental Focus` 1.
**A PvP variant:** `Convection` 1 / `Concussion` 5 / `Reverberation` 5.
**Wowhead itself disagrees with both.** Its own Level 20 build spends these 11 points outside the Elemental tree entirely, into Restoration for `Mindfulness` and [[Water Shield]] and the mana sustain they give. It treats `Concussion`, `Call of Flame` and `Elemental Focus` as what you spend Legacy System bonus points on instead. Icy Veins spends the base 11 in the Elemental tree itself. Both approaches are legal: the sites disagree on where the points go, not on what exists.
Wowhead's case for spending in Restoration is that mana, and [[Water Shield]] fires on any spell crit, so your damage crits restore it too. **But Wowhead's split cannot be assembled at level 20 as printed.** Blizzard's Known Issues listed opening the Legacy System before level 25 as a bug: the "Y" hotkey worked and threw LUA errors on tabs 1 and 3. The Elemental-tree points Wowhead defers to Legacy are not available until 25.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, in order: `Concussion` 5 → `Elemental Alacrity` 3 → `Call of Flame` 2 → `Elemental Focus` 1 → `Call of Flame` 1 → `Convection` 3 → `Eye of the Storm` 3 → `Trial by Fire` 2 → `Lightning Overload` 1 at 30. No source has posted a level 30 build, and the one circulating for Elemental cannot be entered on the launch client. It opens differently from both level 20 builds above, so start it at level 20 if you mean to follow it. The 21st point can go to `Call of Thunder` instead, which `Lightning Mastery` needs at 40. One point of `Lightning Overload` is a 10% chance at a half-damage copy, about 5% more Bolt damage, where `Call of Thunder`'s 5% critical strike chance adds about half that *(ours, not a source's)*. `Earthbound` can replace one point of `Eye of the Storm` at 16.
## Rotation
1. **Pre-place your totems** before the pull. [[Call of the Elements]] at 20 does it in one button. [[Searing Totem]] goes last, because it starts attacking
2. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** to pull
3. **[[Flame Shock]]**, refreshed on anything long-lived
4. **Back to [[Lightning Bolt]]**
**Why this order.** Figures here are base numbers before gear and talents: the Shocks at Classic's values, where Blizzard's notes put them, and Bolt at Blizzard's roughly 78% of Classic *(our arithmetic)*.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] is your Mana spell, and Shocks buy speed.** Around level 28, Bolt rank 5 returns about 1.28 damage per point of Mana, [[Flame Shock]] rank 3 returns 1.19 over its full 12 seconds, and [[Earth Shock]] rank 4 returns 0.90. That is why Flame Shock waits for something long-lived.
- **You get one Shock at a time.** The three share a 6-second cooldown. WoWSims' list recasts Flame Shock only after it has fallen *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*.
- **Spend Clearcasting on the expensive spell.** `Elemental Focus` makes your next damage spell free, so a proc saves 160 Mana on rank 3 Flame Shock and 85 on rank 5 Bolt *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Multi-target: [[Fire Nova]] on cooldown at three or more enemies, with [[Chain Lightning]] between from level 32.** It requires an active Fire totem and hits softly early, 32 to 38 damage at rank 1. Rank 3 at 32 hits about 130 on every enemy near the totem, about 390 across three. WoWSims' list also puts it ahead of Chain Lightning, from two targets *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, Haste, critical strike, Intellect, MP5, Spirit, Stamina.
**Nature damage and plain spell power lead equally.** Then comes *"Intellect, which is how many bolts you get before you have to sit down."* Take leather over cloth where the stats are otherwise equal.
Forever moved caster power from base spells onto gear, so the published advice is to *"constantly upgrade"*. Replace pieces aggressively.

## What Forever changed for Elemental
- **New:** `Lightning Overload`, `Earthbound` and `Lightning Mastery`, which takes Classic's `Elemental Mastery` slot: it is the 31-point capstone above, not Classic's guaranteed free crit. **Lava Burst is not in the game**; older guides and the base client's tooltips still show it.
- **[[Fire Nova Totem]] became [[Fire Nova]]**: an instant eruption at your active Fire totem's location, rather than a totem you drop and wait on. The client gives the spell a 30-yard range, a 10-second cooldown and level 12, and a 10-yard blast around the totem. `Trial by Fire` replaces `Improved Fire Totems`: 2 ranks that raise [[Fire Nova]]'s damage, cut its cooldown and add 10 or 20 seconds to Magma Totem. It changes how you open a pull.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds instead of 3.0** from rank 4 (level 20) upward, for roughly 78% of Classic's base damage per rank. [[Chain Lightning]] keeps Classic's 2.5-second cast; its first target takes about 145% of a comparable Bolt rank's base damage, for about 225% of that Bolt's Mana.
- `Elemental Alacrity` (renamed from `Lightning Mastery`) **sits at row 2, and `Elemental Fury` at row 6** behind `Lightning Overload`, which needs `Elemental Alacrity`. `Call of Thunder` is a single point at row 5, and `Earthbound` is the 16-point milestone at row 4. That chain is what rules out the circulating level-30 build.
- **`Concussion` no longer boosts [[Flame Shock]] or [[Frost Shock]]**, only your Lightning spells and [[Earth Shock]]. Both builds above spend 5 points there, and it now buys less than they assume.
- **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes instead of 5.** Buff totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards instead of 20.
- **Hit and critical strike are unified** across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
No source has measured Elemental at 60. [[Lightning Bolt]] rank 10 at 56 deals about 340 to 380 base, by Blizzard's figure of 80% more for the top rank *(our arithmetic)*. Whether `Lightning Overload`, `Call of Thunder` and `Lightning Mastery` are enough to make Elemental a raid caster is untested against raid gear whose stats are **hidden until it drops**.
