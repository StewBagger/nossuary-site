## What Elemental is for
**Cleave and area damage at range, with burst on a cooldown.** [[Chain Lightning]] hits three targets from level 32. [[Fire Nova]] bursts around your Fire totem from level 12. `Lightning Mastery` at 40 takes 0.5 seconds off [[Lightning Bolt]] and removes Chain Lightning's cooldown for 15 seconds.
**The sources agree single-target damage is its weak side.** Wowhead: *"So far Elemental appears poised to be a strong Cleave and AoE DPS, but a fairly mediocre Single Target DPS."* Icy Veins: *"Unfortunately Elemental is rather weak in single-target compared to most other caster options"*. Icy Veins rates it 3 of 5 overall, 3 for levelling, 3.5 for dungeons and 4.5 for utility and mobility; a levelling score measures solo kill speed.
**Mana is the other limit.** Wowhead's levelling page: Elemental *"struggles with high Mana costs that require support from the other two trees while leveling."* Every published build below spends points in Restoration or Enhancement for that reason.
**Your power sits on gear more than it did in Classic.** Blizzard's November 2 notes set each rank of [[Lightning Bolt]] to *"roughly 78% of the base damage as the Classic Era version (but cast time is lower than Classic Era)"*, with the Shocks at Classic's values. An Elemental Shaman in stale gear loses more than a Classic one did *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Wowhead recommends not levelling as a caster at all.** Its levelling page calls a melee hybrid built in the Enhancement tree *"the strongest way to level as a Shaman if you're willing to play a hybrid role"*, and respecs to Elemental later. That advice is dated November 4 and leans on Intellect through `Mental Dexterity`, which Blizzard cut from 150% to 100% on November 12. See [Enhancement](/guides/wow-forever/shaman/enhancement/).

## Rotation
Before the pull:
1. **[[Lightning Shield]]** up, or [[Water Shield]] once you have it
2. **Totems**, with the Fire totem last: [[Searing Totem]] on one target, `Magma Totem` on a pack. [[Call of the Elements]] drops four at once on a 3-second cast
3. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** to pull from range, or [[Chain Lightning]] on a pack from 32
On one or two targets, the priority Wowhead, Icy Veins and WoWSims' model share:
1. **[[Flame Shock]]**, recast when it has fallen off
2. **`Lightning Mastery`** from 40, with trinkets and racials
3. **[[Chain Lightning]]** when `Lightning Mastery` is not running, on a Clearcasting proc or when your Mana can afford it
4. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** as the filler
On three or more:
1. **`Magma Totem`** down
2. **[[Chain Lightning]]** on cooldown
3. **[[Fire Nova]]** on cooldown
4. **[[Flame Shock]]** on up to three targets
5. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** if the Mana is there
**Why this order.** Figures are the client's rank values before gear and talents *(our arithmetic)*.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] is your Mana spell.** At level 32, Bolt rank 6 deals 144 to 162 for 110 Mana. [[Chain Lightning]] rank 1 deals 208 to 236 to its first target for 245 Mana, on the same 2.5-second cast. That is Blizzard's design: about 145% of the Bolt's damage for about 225% of its Mana. On one target Chain Lightning is damage you pay extra for, which is why all three sources gate it on Mana or a free cast.
- **Spend Clearcasting on the expensive spell.** `Elemental Focus` makes your next damage spell free. A proc saves 245 Mana on Chain Lightning and 110 on that Bolt.
- **Chain Lightning waits out `Lightning Mastery` on one target.** The cooldown makes [[Lightning Bolt]] 0.5 seconds faster, so the sources fill those 15 seconds with Bolts. WoWSims' list also holds single-target Chain Lightning below 40% Mana *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*.
- **One Shock at a time.** The three share a 6-second cooldown. [[Flame Shock]] lasts 12 seconds, and each point of `Call of Flame` adds 3 more, so at 3/3 one cast covers 21 seconds. [[Earth Shock]] is your interrupt: every rank stops the cast and locks the school for 2 seconds, and rank 1 costs 30 Mana.
- **[[Fire Nova]] needs a Fire totem down and enemies near it.** Rank 1 hits for 32 to 38 for 65 Mana. Rank 3 at 32 hits 126 to 143 for 210, and rank 5 at 52 hits 232 to 259 for 375, on every enemy within 10 yards of the totem. `Trial by Fire` 2/2 adds 20% and cuts the cooldown from 10 seconds to 6.
- **Downrank when Mana is short.** Wowhead: *"The damage difference between ranks in WoW: Forever is far lower than in Vanilla WoW, meaning you're more incentivized to downrank to save on Mana."* WoWSims' list drops to Bolt rank 8 below 20% Mana *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*.
## Stat priority
**The two guide sites publish different lists, and both are dated November 4.**
Wowhead:
1. Spell Piercing
2. Haste
3. Hit, to 17%
4. Spell Damage
5. Critical strike
6. Intellect, then Spirit
Icy Veins:
1. Spell Power
2. Hit, to 17%
3. Critical strike
4. Intellect
5. MP5
**They agree on Hit and Spell Power and differ on Spell Piercing and Haste**, which Icy Veins does not list. WoWSims' weights side with Wowhead: per point of Spell Damage, Spell Piercing 1.81, Haste 1.23, Hit 1.18, critical strike 0.61, Intellect 0.19 *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*. Spell Piercing is Forever's new stat. It lowers the target's resistance, and Wowhead puts 10 points against an unresisting target at about 1.8% damage at 60.
**Hit cap.** A raid boss three levels above you is missed 17% of the time. `Tidal Focus` 5/5 supplies 5% of that, leaving 12% from gear. Dwarves get another 1% from `Artisan's Precision`.
**While levelling**, Wowhead's short version is Spell Power for damage, Intellect for the pool and Spirit for regeneration, with Hit, critical strike and Spell Piercing *"extremely strong, but less common"* on low-level gear.

## What Forever changed for Elemental
- **New:** `Lightning Overload`, `Earthbound` and `Lightning Mastery`. The capstone took the old `Elemental Mastery` node on November 3 and is not Classic's guaranteed free crit. **Lava Burst is not in the game.** Blizzard removed it in the November 2 notes and put `Call of Thunder` in its place.
- **Fire Nova Totem became [[Fire Nova]]**: an instant burst at your active Fire totem, on a 10-second cooldown, rather than a totem you drop and wait on. `Trial by Fire` replaces `Improved Fire Totems`: 2 ranks that raise [[Fire Nova]]'s damage, cut its cooldown and add 10 or 20 seconds to Magma Totem.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds at every rank from 3 up**, where Classic went to 3.0 at rank 4. Rank 10, trained at 56, deals 342 to 381 for 220 Mana.
- **[[Chain Lightning]] casts in 2.5 seconds**, set by the November 2 notes and in the client since November 5. Rank 4 at 56 deals 495 to 552 to the first target for 495 Mana; each jump deals 30% less.
- **`Lightning Overload` now copies the right damage.** Blizzard's November 9 hotfix: it *"now deals the correct amount of damage for Lightning Bolt."*
- `Elemental Alacrity` (renamed from `Lightning Mastery`) **sits at row 2, and `Elemental Fury` at row 6** behind `Lightning Overload`. `Call of Thunder` is a single point at row 5, and `Earthbound` is the 16-point milestone at row 4.
- **`Concussion` no longer boosts [[Flame Shock]] or [[Frost Shock]]**, only your Lightning spells and [[Earth Shock]].
- **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes instead of 5.** Buff totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards instead of 20.
- **Hit and critical strike are unified** across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
**Sourced:** the builds and priority lists above. Wowhead describes the raid role as support first: *"offering strong utility to a raid group instead of strong damage."* Icy Veins expects *"a good dungeon member"* on the strength of [[Chain Lightning]] and [[Fire Nova]], and both sites warn that Mana runs short on longer fights.
**Not known:** how any of it measures up in a raid. None opens until December 9, so no raid log exists. Wowhead reports a developer interview in which Kris Zierhut said of Shaman talents: *"I wouldn't be surprised if early next year we revisit Shaman talents a little bit. It's not going to be sooner than that."* That is Wowhead's account of an interview, not a Blizzard post.
