Ranged caster mixing hard lightning casts with Shocks, interrupts and totems. **Its new talents all sit at level 30 or above**: `Lightning Overload`, `Earthbound` and [[Lava Burst]]. That is the whole story of this spec in the beta.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at level 30 as of the October 1 build, raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Elemental build has been tested. One warning is specific to this spec: a widely-published level-30 Elemental build **is not legal on the current client.** It spends 5 points on `Elemental Fury`, which Blizzard moved down to row 6 on September 24, so it now needs 26 points, not 21. That site's data predates the change, and its prose and its own screenshot disagree besides. Nothing circulating counts as a published level-30 Elemental build.

## What Elemental is for
**Lightning burst at range.** [[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds from rank 4 at level 20. `Elemental Focus` at exactly 20 gives a 10% Clearcasting chance on any Fire, Frost or Nature spell, and `Lightning Overload` at 30 adds a chance at a free, half-damage, no-threat duplicate of the Bolt. [[Chain Lightning]] is the other half of that burst at 2.0 seconds a cast, but it is level 32, so it sits outside the beta cap.
**Viable but weak at the current cap, and the source says so directly.** Its heading is *"Weak Damage"*, and under it: *"Because they are denied access to some of their best talents on the Level 20 bracket, such as [[Lava Burst]] and `Elemental Alacrity`, Elemental is quite weak at low levels. This is also not helped by the lowering of base spell damage in WoW Forever in favor of gear with more Spell Damage."*
**That quotation is pre-patch on one of its two examples.** `Elemental Alacrity` sits at row 3 now, so your 11th point reaches it at level 20. It is also cheaper and weaker than the talent the quote assumes: 3 points for 0.5 seconds off [[Lightning Bolt]] and [[Chain Lightning]], and it now shortens [[Lava Burst]] as well. Blizzard cut it because it *"was twice as strong as similar talents with other classes, causing a spell power scaling problem."* [[Lava Burst]] is still level 40, and the base-spell-damage half of the quotation stands.
**The base-damage half matters more than it reads.** Forever cut base spell damage across the board and moved the power onto gear. One guide spells out the consequence: *"all caster specs, and for Shaman that's Elemental, will need to constantly upgrade their gear to deal decent damage."* An Elemental Shaman in stale gear is genuinely weak in a way a Classic one was not.
[[Chain Lightning]] is level 32, so you do not have it at any beta cap. Ratings: Overall 3.0, Levelling 3.0.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Two published builds, both legal.
**Build A:**
1. `Concussion`, 5 points
2. `Call of Flame`, 3 points
3. `Elemental Warding`, 2 points
4. **`Elemental Focus`** at 20
`Convection` is offered as a swap for `Elemental Warding`. Take it if mana is your problem rather than survival.
**Build B:** `Convection` 5 / `Concussion` 5 / `Elemental Focus` 1.
**A PvP variant:** `Convection` 1 / `Concussion` 5 / `Reverberation` 5.
**Wowhead itself disagrees with both.** Its own Level 20 build spends these 11 points outside the Elemental tree entirely, into Restoration for `Mindfulness` and [[Water Shield]] and the mana sustain they give. It treats `Concussion`, `Call of Flame` and `Elemental Focus` as what you spend Legacy System bonus points on instead. Icy Veins' Build A above spends the base 11 in the Elemental tree itself. Both builds are legal: the sites disagree on where the points go, not on what exists.
[[Totemic Recall]] is not the free alternative it looks like, because Blizzard says its Mana refund is bugged in beta and pays out nothing. Wowhead's case for spending in Restoration therefore stands for now. **But Wowhead's split cannot be assembled at level 20 as printed.** Blizzard's Known Issues list opening the Legacy System before level 25 as a bug: the "Y" hotkey works and throws LUA errors on tabs 1 and 3. The Elemental-tree points Wowhead defers to Legacy are not available until 25.
**Level 30: nothing to print.** No source has posted a level 30 build, and the one circulating for Elemental cannot be entered on the current client, so this page gives no level 30 order.
## Rotation
1. **Pre-place your totems** before the pull. [[Call of the Elements]] at 20 does it in one button. [[Searing Totem]] goes last, because it starts attacking
2. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** to pull
3. **[[Flame Shock]]**, refreshed on anything long-lived
4. **Back to [[Lightning Bolt]]**
**Multi-target: [[Fire Nova]] on cooldown at three or more enemies.** It requires an active Fire totem. [[Chain Lightning]] is level 32 and is not available in this beta at any cap.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, Haste, critical strike, Intellect, MP5, Spirit, Stamina.
**Nature damage and plain spell power lead equally.** Then comes *"Intellect, which is how many bolts you get before you have to sit down."* Take leather over cloth where the stats are otherwise equal.
Forever moved caster power from base spells onto gear, so the published advice is to *"constantly upgrade"*. Replace pieces aggressively.

## What Forever changed for Elemental
- **New:** [[Lava Burst]], `Lightning Overload`, `Earthbound`. Removed: `Elemental Mastery` and `Totemic Mastery`. The first is certain: Blizzard could not raise Elemental's PvE damage while it existed without the spec becoming *"much too strong in PvP"*, and it is gone from the game outright. `Totemic Mastery` rests on weaker ground, because Blizzard names it in no removal list. But every totem now reaches 30 yards with no talent spent, so treat that talent as gone *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **[[Fire Nova Totem]] became [[Fire Nova]]**: an instant eruption at your active Fire totem's location, rather than a totem you drop and wait on. The client gives the spell a 30-yard range, a 10-second cooldown and level 12, and publishes no blast radius; neither does Blizzard. `Improved Fire Nova` replaces `Improved Fire Totems`, and now raises [[Fire Nova]]'s damage and cuts its cooldown. It changes how you open a pull.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds instead of 3.0** from rank 4 (level 20) upward, so the buff is live well inside the level-30 cap. [[Chain Lightning]] casts in 2.0 instead of 2.5 at every rank. Blizzard cut both spells' spell power coefficients to match, so you gain cast time rather than damage per cast.
- `Elemental Alacrity` (renamed from `Lightning Mastery`) **moved up to row 3, and `Elemental Fury` moved down to row 6.** `Call of Thunder` sits between them at row 4 as the tree's new 16-point milestone: one rank, 3% critical strike on [[Lightning Bolt]] and [[Chain Lightning]]. It requires `Elemental Alacrity` and is in turn required by `Elemental Fury`. That chain is what invalidated the published level-30 build.
- **`Concussion` no longer boosts [[Flame Shock]] or [[Frost Shock]]**, only your Lightning spells and [[Earth Shock]]. Both builds above spend 5 points there, and it now buys less than they assume.
- **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes instead of 5.** Totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards instead of 20.
- **Hit and critical strike are unified** across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether [[Lava Burst]] plus `Lightning Overload` is enough to make Elemental a raid caster. Both are untuned against raid gear whose stats are **hidden until it drops**, and there is no [[Chain Lightning]] or mana benchmark to compare against.
