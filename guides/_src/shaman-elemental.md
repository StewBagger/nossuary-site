Ranged caster mixing hard lightning casts with Shocks, interrupts and totems. **Its new talents arrive from 25 on**: `Earthbound` is the 16-point milestone at 25, `Lightning Overload` and [[Lava Burst]] sit at 21 points, level 30, and `Elemental Mastery` is the 31-point capstone at 40.

## What Elemental is for
**Lightning burst at range.** [[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds from rank 4 at level 20. `Elemental Focus` at exactly 20 gives a 10% Clearcasting chance on any Fire, Frost or Nature spell, and `Lightning Overload` at 30 adds a chance at a free, half-damage, no-threat duplicate of the Bolt. [[Chain Lightning]] is the other half of that burst at 2.0 seconds a cast, and it arrives at level 32.
**Weak at low levels, and the source says so directly.** Its heading is *"Weak Damage"*, and under it: *"Because they are denied access to some of their best talents on the Level 20 bracket, such as [[Lava Burst]] and `Elemental Alacrity`, Elemental is quite weak at low levels. This is also not helped by the lowering of base spell damage in WoW Forever in favor of gear with more Spell Damage."*
**That quotation is out of date on both of its examples.** `Elemental Alacrity` sits at row 2 now, so your 6th point reaches it at level 15, and 3/3 lands at 17. It is also cheaper and weaker than the talent the quote assumes: 3 points for 0.5 seconds off [[Lightning Bolt]], [[Chain Lightning]] and [[Lava Burst]]. Blizzard cut it because it *"was twice as strong as similar talents with other classes, causing a spell power scaling problem."* [[Lava Burst]] sits at 21 points, so you can have it at 30. The base-spell-damage half of the quotation stands.
**The base-damage half matters more than it reads.** Forever cut base spell damage across the board and moved the power onto gear. One guide spells out the consequence: *"all caster specs, and for Shaman that's Elemental, will need to constantly upgrade their gear to deal decent damage."* An Elemental Shaman in stale gear is genuinely weak in a way a Classic one was not *(ours, not a source's)*.
Ratings: Overall 3.0, Levelling 3.0.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Two builds, both legal.
**Build A — our own extension, not a published build.** We adjusted Icy Veins' list because its last 2 points went to a row 3 talent this list cannot reach yet, so we use the `Convection` swap the same source offers.
1. `Concussion`, 5 points
2. `Call of Flame`, 3 points
3. `Convection`, 2 points
4. **`Elemental Focus`** at 20
**Build B:** `Convection` 5 / `Concussion` 5 / `Elemental Focus` 1.
**A PvP variant:** `Convection` 1 / `Concussion` 5 / `Reverberation` 5.
**Wowhead itself disagrees with both.** Its own Level 20 build spends these 11 points outside the Elemental tree entirely, into Restoration for `Mindfulness` and [[Water Shield]] and the mana sustain they give. It treats `Concussion`, `Call of Flame` and `Elemental Focus` as what you spend Legacy System bonus points on instead. Icy Veins spends the base 11 in the Elemental tree itself. Both approaches are legal: the sites disagree on where the points go, not on what exists.
Wowhead's case for spending in Restoration is that mana, and [[Water Shield]] fires on any spell crit, so your damage crits restore it too. **But Wowhead's split cannot be assembled at level 20 as printed.** Blizzard's Known Issues listed opening the Legacy System before level 25 as a bug: the "Y" hotkey worked and threw LUA errors on tabs 1 and 3. The Elemental-tree points Wowhead defers to Legacy are not available until 25.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, in order: `Concussion` 5 → `Elemental Alacrity` 3 → `Call of Flame` 2 → `Elemental Focus` 1 → `Call of Flame` 1 → `Convection` 3 → `Eye of the Storm` 3 → `Trial by Fire` 2 → [[Lava Burst]] at 30. No source has posted a level 30 build, and the one circulating for Elemental cannot be entered on the launch client. It opens differently from both level 20 builds above, so start it at level 20 if you mean to follow it. The 21st point can go to `Lightning Overload` instead of [[Lava Burst]], and `Earthbound` can replace one point of `Eye of the Storm` at 16.
## Rotation
1. **Pre-place your totems** before the pull. [[Call of the Elements]] at 20 does it in one button. [[Searing Totem]] goes last, because it starts attacking
2. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** to pull
3. **[[Flame Shock]]**, refreshed on anything long-lived
4. **Back to [[Lightning Bolt]]**
**Multi-target: [[Fire Nova]] on cooldown at three or more enemies.** It requires an active Fire totem and hits softly, 32 to 38 damage at rank 1, so [[Chain Lightning]] takes over from level 32 *(ours, not a source's)*.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, Haste, critical strike, Intellect, MP5, Spirit, Stamina.
**Nature damage and plain spell power lead equally.** Then comes *"Intellect, which is how many bolts you get before you have to sit down."* Take leather over cloth where the stats are otherwise equal.
Forever moved caster power from base spells onto gear, so the published advice is to *"constantly upgrade"*. Replace pieces aggressively.

## What Forever changed for Elemental
- **New:** [[Lava Burst]], `Lightning Overload`, `Earthbound`. `Elemental Mastery` keeps its name and little else: it is the 31-point capstone above, not Classic's guaranteed free crit.
- **[[Fire Nova Totem]] became [[Fire Nova]]**: an instant eruption at your active Fire totem's location, rather than a totem you drop and wait on. The client gives the spell a 30-yard range, a 10-second cooldown and level 12, and a 10-yard blast around the totem. `Trial by Fire` replaces `Improved Fire Totems`: 2 ranks that raise [[Fire Nova]]'s damage, cut its cooldown and add 10 or 20 seconds to Magma Totem. It changes how you open a pull.
- **[[Lightning Bolt]] casts in 2.5 seconds instead of 3.0** from rank 4 (level 20) upward. [[Chain Lightning]] casts in 2.0 instead of 2.5 at every rank. Blizzard cut both spells' spell power coefficients to match, so you gain cast time rather than damage per cast.
- `Elemental Alacrity` (renamed from `Lightning Mastery`) **sits at row 2, and `Elemental Fury` at row 6** behind `Lightning Overload`, which needs `Elemental Alacrity`. `Call of Thunder` is removed, and `Earthbound` is the 16-point milestone at row 4. That chain is what rules out the circulating level-30 build.
- **`Concussion` no longer boosts [[Flame Shock]] or [[Frost Shock]]**, only your Lightning spells and [[Earth Shock]]. Both builds above spend 5 points there, and it now buys less than they assume.
- **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes instead of 5.** Buff totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards instead of 20.
- **Hit and critical strike are unified** across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
No source has measured Elemental at 60, but the client holds the ladder: [[Lava Burst]] rank 4 at level 60 deals 192 to 248, and [[Chain Lightning]] rank 4 at 56 deals 119 to 133 for 315 Mana. Whether [[Lava Burst]], `Lightning Overload` and `Elemental Mastery` are enough to make Elemental a raid caster is untested against raid gear whose stats are **hidden until it drops**.
