## Is Restoration worth levelling?
**Viable but the slowest of the four, and the source says so without hedging:** *"While it is very possible to level as a healing spec like Restoration Druid, it will result in a much longer journey than levelling as Feral or Balance instead. You will be very hard to kill, but our damage is just too low to do the job efficiently. If you're open to the other specs, then it can be best to level as one of those, and you can still heal dungeons while you level if you like."* Blizzard's own class deep dive backs the same shape of complaint at the class level: Druid healing "relies on healing-over-time spells that require more planning" than a Priest's toolkit.
Level Feral, then respec to Restoration when you want to heal. Druid respeccing costs the same as anyone's.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Two genuinely different builds, depending on what you are doing.
**Dungeon healing.** Two sources publish this exact same eleven points independently:
1. `Nature's Focus`, 5 points
2. `Naturalist`, 5 points
3. **`Gift of the Earthmother`** at 20
The stated trade: *"This build will **forgo `Improved Wrath`** so that it can get `Gift of the Earthmother` at Level 20 — a massive increase to how responsive our [[Rejuvenation]] is."*
**Solo and world content:** `Nature's Focus` 5 / `Genesis` 5 / `Nature's Reach` 1, putting five points into a Balance row-1 talent for damage and downtime. ⚠ **That page's prose names `Improved Wrath` where its own calculator encodes `Genesis`.** Both sit in the same row; treat either as the intended solo pick.
**A third published build** takes `Nature's Focus` 5 / `Naturalist` 3 / `Natural Shapeshifter` 3 and **no `Gift of the Earthmother` at all.**
The warning attached to the dungeon build is fair: *"You can definitely heal dungeons 100% fine with the World Content build… this alternative will be much worse at killing enemies by yourself."*
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source has posted one, and the tree allows two readings that **disagree on whether to keep `Gift of the Earthmother`**: one takes `Reflection` 3 · `Gift of Nature` 5 · `Swiftmend` 1 plus 2 Feral points and **drops it**; the other keeps it and adds `Improved Rejuvenation` 3 and `Nature's Swiftness` 1.
**A separate Legacy System grants extra talent points on top of your level-based total** — Wowhead notes that a Restoration druid with enough Legacy points to reach "5/5 Talented" has enough to grab `Swiftmend` at level 20 as well, ahead of the normal 11-point allocation. Details on how Legacy points are earned are not yet published.
## Healing priority
**Level 20:**
1. **Pre-cast [[Thorns]] on the tank before the run starts**
2. **[[Rejuvenation]]** on the tank and anyone taking light damage
3. **[[Healing Touch]]** to top up
4. Spare global cooldowns → **[[Wrath]] or [[Moonfire]]** on the boss
**Level 30:**
1. **`Nature's Swiftness` + [[Healing Touch]]** as your instant emergency
2. **[[Tranquility]]** for party-wide damage
3. **`Swiftmend`** as the instant spot heal
4. **Blanket [[Rejuvenation]] ahead of damage you know is coming**
5. **[[Regrowth]]** on tanks; [[Regrowth]] or [[Healing Touch]] single-target
**Three rules that will catch you out:**
- **[[Wild Growth]] is not an eligible effect for `Swiftmend`**. A tank with only Wild Growth ticking is *not* prepared
- **`Swiftmend` heals for the full periodic duration of one eligible effect, not merely the ticks left on its timer**, and **in Forever it no longer consumes the effect**
- **`Gift of the Earthmother` does not shorten `Swiftmend`'s own 15-second cooldown**
**Multi-target: [[Tranquility]] at 30 is your only party-wide heal until [[Wild Growth]] at 40.** Before that, pre-blanket [[Rejuvenation]]. `Gift of the Earthmother` trimming the global cooldown to 1 second is what makes that affordable.
## Stat priority
Healing Power and Spell Power equally, then Intellect, Spirit, critical strike, Haste.
*"Intellect decides how long you can keep casting, Spirit how quickly the bar refills once you stop. Stamina counts for very little here, and spell damage for nothing."* Icy Veins places Haste last in its list for exactly that reason: **healing over time does not scale with haste at all**, and there are no haste breakpoints for it.
Item level first while levelling: take the higher piece.
## What defines Restoration
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Gift of the Earthmother`** | **exactly 20** | New, and **the earliest meaningful new-talent payoff any Druid spec has in the beta.** Cuts the global cooldown of [[Rejuvenation]], `Swiftmend` and [[Wild Growth]] by 0.5 s, so 1 second flat, which *"allows us to cover a group in Rejuvenation extremely quickly"* |
| **`Swiftmend`** | ~25 | **Moved up from Classic's tier 7 to row 4, where it is now the 16-point gold medal talent.** Redesigned so that it **no longer consumes** the [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] effect it spends |
| **[[Wild Growth]]** | **40** | New, the 31-point capstone, and the spec's first party-wide heal over time. Heals the target's **party, including pets** (within 43 yards), starting at 280 and **tapering down every second** over 7 seconds rather than ticking flat; 6-second cooldown, high Mana cost |
## What Forever changed for Restoration
- **[[Wild Growth]] and `Gift of the Earthmother` are both new, and `Swiftmend` no longer consumes its trigger.** Together that is the "modern Resto" rework. `Swiftmend` also moved up, from Classic's tier 7 to **row 4**, where it is now the **16-point gold medal talent**.
- **[[Wild Growth]] follows the *target's party*, and it tapers rather than ticking flat.** It will not seek out the raid's lowest health bars, so do not treat it as smart healing. Blizzard's own description: it "heals every 1 second for 7 seconds, decreasing the healing amount every second," hits the target's whole party including pets, and carries a **high Mana cost** on its 6-second cooldown.
- **Healing over time can now critically strike**: *"[[Rejuvenation]] can now land critical hits, as can [[Tranquility]] and [[Wild Growth]]"*, per the September 24 patch. One guide's FAQ is stale on this and still says only [[Regrowth]] can crit.
- **Three new talents:** `Gift of the Earthmother` (the new 11-point gold medal talent), `Living Spirit` (the new row 5 talent, a flat Spirit percentage) and [[Wild Growth]] (the new 31-point gold medal talent). **`Improved Tranquility` is not one of them** — it is an existing talent that Blizzard changed, and it now also cuts [[Tranquility]]'s cooldown by up to 60%. **`Reflection`** was also rebuilt: 50% of mana regeneration now continues while you cast at 3/3, where Classic's 3/3 gave 15%.
- **`Naturalist` (formerly `Improved Healing Touch`) now also grants +5% to all your damage** at 5/5, where Classic only cut [[Healing Touch]]'s cast time — and it absorbed `Natural Weapons`, which Blizzard folded into it rather than deleting. That is why Feral and Balance both want it.
- `Nature's Focus` (moved row 2 → row 1) and `Subtlety` were broadened from a named-heal list to all Arcane and Nature spells; `Subtlety` also dropped from 5 points to **3**, for a maximum **30%** threat reduction, and now covers Arcane and Nature threat, not just healing. `Gift of Nature` moved from row 5 to row 3. `Improved Regrowth` now requires `Improved Rejuvenation` as a prerequisite.
- **The Cat energy trick (`Furor`) is gone, and Blizzard says that was deliberate — but they built a replacement, not a flat removal.** Blizzard's own words: *"We realize this removed the 'power shifting' gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the `Shifting Power` talent in the Feral talent tree."* **That talent went live in the October 1 build.** Furor's Bear half is untouched. See the [Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/) page for what `Shifting Power` does.
- **[[Revive]] was added**: out-of-combat resurrection at level 12. [[Regrowth]]'s mana cost was cut from 120 to 70 at rank 1. `Natural Shapeshifter` moved in from Balance (to row 2); [[Insect Swarm]] moved out to Balance. `Improved Mark of the Wild` and `Improved Enrage` are gone as talents, and [[Enrage]] now front-loads 10 rage baseline, which is what `Improved Enrage` used to buy. **[[Mark of the Wild]] now lasts 1 hour** and [[Gift of the Wild]] covers party **and** raid.
- **No source mentions any version of Tree Form, or Lifebloom.** Blizzard’s deep dive lists neither, which is silence rather than confirmation they are out, so build without them but do not call them cancelled.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether [[Wild Growth]] plus [[Rejuvenation]] blanketing on a 1-second global cooldown is mana-sustainable in a raid is untested, since nobody has cast Wild Growth. One guide still does not know whether all healing over time will crit at launch.
