## What Restoration is for
**Keeping a party up on heals that are already running before the damage lands.** Every [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] that runs its full duration pays out once more through `Bloom`, so blanketing a group ahead of time is worth the Mana *(ours, not a source's)*. `Swiftmend` then turns one running [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] into an instant heal for its full periodic duration, and leaves it ticking.
The published verdict measures solo kill speed, and it **also says you can heal dungeons the whole way**: *"While it is very possible to level as a healing spec like Restoration Druid, it will result in a much longer journey than levelling as Feral or Balance instead. You will be very hard to kill, but our damage is just too low to do the job efficiently. If you're open to the other specs, then it can be best to level as one of those, and you can still heal dungeons while you level if you like."* Blizzard's own class deep dive backs the same complaint at class level: Druid healing "relies on healing-over-time (HoT) spells that require more planning."
Level Feral, then respec to Restoration when you want to heal *(ours, not a source's)*. Druid respeccing costs the same as anyone's.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Two genuinely different builds, depending on what you are doing.
**Dungeon healing.** Two sources publish `Nature's Focus` 5 and `Naturalist` 5 independently. Their eleventh point went on a talent that has since left the tree, so this list is our adjustment, not a published build, and `Bloom` takes that point:
1. `Nature's Focus`, 5 points
2. `Naturalist`, 5 points
3. **`Bloom`**, 1 point, at 20
This forgoes `Improved Wrath`, as both published lists did, so it heals better than it kills.
**Solo and world content:** `Nature's Focus` 5 / `Genesis` 5 / `Nature's Reach` 1, putting five points into a Balance row-1 talent for damage and downtime. ⚠ That page's prose names `Improved Wrath` where its own calculator encodes `Genesis`. Both sit in the same row, so either one is a reasonable solo pick.
**A third published build** takes `Nature's Focus` 5 / `Naturalist` 3 / `Natural Shapeshifter` 3, and no `Bloom`.
The published warning attached to the dungeon build: *"You can definitely heal dungeons 100% fine with the World Content build… this alternative will be much worse at killing enemies by yourself."*
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source had posted one. `Nature's Focus` 5 · `Naturalist` 5 · `Reflection` 3 · `Gift of Nature` 5 · `Bloom` 1 · `Swiftmend` 1 · `Nature's Swiftness` 1. That holds every button the level 30 priority below names.
To 40, also ours, it adds `Living Spirit` 3, `Improved Rejuvenation` 3, `Improved Regrowth` 3 and [[Wild Growth]] 1, which makes 31 points. `Living Spirit` opens [[Wild Growth]], and `Improved Rejuvenation` opens `Improved Regrowth`.
**The Legacy perk `Talented` starts talent points up to 5 levels earlier**, with the 51-point cap unchanged. Wowhead notes that a Restoration druid with enough Legacy points to reach "5/5 Talented" can also grab `Swiftmend` at level 20, ahead of the normal 11-point allocation. Legacy Points come from 65 account challenges.
## Healing priority
**Level 20:**
1. **Pre-cast [[Thorns]] on the tank before the run starts**
2. **[[Rejuvenation]]** on the tank and anyone taking light damage
3. **[[Healing Touch]]** to top up
4. Spare global cooldowns → **[[Wrath]] or [[Moonfire]]** on the boss
**Level 30:**
1. **`Nature's Swiftness` + [[Healing Touch]]** as your instant emergency
2. **[[Tranquility]]** for party-wide damage
3. **`Swiftmend`** as the instant spot heal
4. **Blanket [[Rejuvenation]] ahead of damage you know is coming**
5. **[[Regrowth]]** on tanks; [[Regrowth]] or [[Healing Touch]] single-target
**Two rules that will catch you out:**
- **[[Wild Growth]] is not an eligible effect for `Swiftmend`**. A tank with only Wild Growth ticking is *not* prepared
- **`Swiftmend` needs your own [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]], and heals for the full periodic duration of one eligible effect**, not merely the ticks left on its timer. Blizzard's design leaves that effect running. The current tooltip says *"the consumed spell"*, but only its wording changed, and ForeverChanges reads it that way too. No player has tested it
**Multi-target: [[Tranquility]] at 30 is your only party-wide heal until [[Wild Growth]] at 40.** Before that, pre-blanket [[Rejuvenation]].
## Stat priority
Healing Power and Spell Power equally, then Intellect, Spirit, critical strike, Haste.
*"Intellect decides how long you can keep casting, Spirit how quickly the bar refills once you stop. Stamina counts for very little here, and spell damage for nothing."* Icy Veins places Haste last for the same reason: **healing over time does not scale with haste at all**, and there are no haste breakpoints for it.
Item level first while levelling: take the higher piece.
## What defines Restoration
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Bloom`** | **exactly 20** | New, and reachable at exactly 11 points. When [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] runs its duration, the target is healed for 100% of your Spirit, 50% if the effect was dispelled |
| **`Swiftmend`** | ~25 | The **16-point gold medal talent**. It does not consume the [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] effect it spends |
| **[[Wild Growth]]** | **40** | New, the 31-point capstone, and the spec's first party-wide heal over time. It requires `Living Spirit`, and it has a 3-second cast, 2.5 with `Naturalist` 5/5. Heals the target's **party, including pets** (within 43 yards), starting at 280 and tapering down every second over 7 seconds rather than ticking flat; 6-second cooldown, high Mana cost |
## What Forever changed for Restoration
- **[[Wild Growth]] and `Bloom` are both new**, and `Swiftmend` no longer consumes its trigger. Together that is the "modern Resto" rework. `Swiftmend` also moved up, from Classic's tier 7 to row 4, where it is now the 16-point gold medal talent.
- **[[Wild Growth]] follows the *target's party*, and it tapers rather than ticking flat.** It will not seek out the raid's lowest health bars, so do not treat it as smart healing. Blizzard's own description: it "heals every 1 second for 7 seconds, decreasing the healing amount every second," hits the target's whole party including pets, and carries a high Mana cost on its 6-second cooldown. It takes a 3-second cast, 2.5 with `Naturalist` 5/5, so it goes out ahead of the damage.
- **Healing over time can now critically strike**: *"[[Rejuvenation]] can now land critical hits, as can [[Tranquility]] and [[Wild Growth]]"*, per the September 24 patch. One guide's FAQ is stale on this and still says only [[Regrowth]] can crit.
- **Three new talents:** `Bloom` (row 3, the 11-point slot), `Living Spirit` (the new row 5 talent, a flat Spirit percentage) and [[Wild Growth]] (the new 31-point gold medal talent). `Improved Tranquility` is not one of them: it is an existing talent that Blizzard changed, and it now also cuts [[Tranquility]]'s cooldown by up to 60%. `Reflection` was also rebuilt, so that 50% of mana regeneration continues while you cast at 3/3, where Classic's 3/3 gave 15%.
- **`Naturalist` (formerly `Improved Healing Touch`) now also grants +10% to all your damage** at 5/5, and takes 0.5 sec off [[Healing Touch]] and [[Wild Growth]], where Classic only cut [[Healing Touch]]'s cast time. It also absorbed `Natural Weapons`, which Blizzard folded into it rather than deleting, and that is why Feral and Balance both want it.
- `Nature's Focus` (moved row 2 → row 1) and `Subtlety` were broadened from a named-heal list to all Arcane and Nature spells. `Subtlety` also dropped from 5 points to 3, for a maximum 30% threat reduction, and now covers Arcane and Nature threat rather than healing alone. `Gift of Nature` moved from row 5 to row 3. `Improved Regrowth` now requires `Improved Rejuvenation`.
- **`Heart of the Wild` arrived from Feral**, at tier 1, third column, and `Furor` went the other way. `Reflection` sits in row 2 and `Subtlety` in row 3.
- **[[Revive]] was added**: out-of-combat resurrection at level 12. [[Regrowth]]'s mana cost is 70 at rank 1, against Classic's 120. `Natural Shapeshifter` moved in from Balance (to row 2); [[Insect Swarm]] moved out to Balance. `Improved Mark of the Wild` and `Improved Enrage` are gone as talents, and [[Enrage]] now front-loads 10 rage baseline, which is what `Improved Enrage` used to buy. [[Mark of the Wild]] now lasts 1 hour, and [[Gift of the Wild]] covers party and raid.
- **No source tells you how to reach Tree Form or Lifebloom.** Blizzard’s deep dive lists neither. The client carries both, with Forever-shaped text: a [[Tree of Life]] shapeshift that boosts [[Wild Growth]] healing, and a Lifebloom that stacks and refunds half its Mana when it ends. Neither has a learn level or a place in a tree in any source, so there is no way to take them. That is silence rather than confirmation they are out, so build without them and do not call them cancelled.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether [[Wild Growth]] plus [[Rejuvenation]] blanketing is mana-sustainable in a raid is untested, since the beta stopped before Wild Growth. When we last read it, one guide did not know whether all healing over time would crit at launch.
