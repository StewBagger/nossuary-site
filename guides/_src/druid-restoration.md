## What Restoration is for
**Keeping a party up on heals that are already running before the damage lands.** Every [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] that runs its full duration pays out once more through `Bloom`. `Swiftmend` turns one running [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] into an instant heal for its full periodic amount, and leaves it running.
**Both sites say the same thing about levelling in it.** Icy Veins: *"If you want to speed to Level 60, then we recommend leveling as Balance or Feral instead (you will still be able to heal dungeons)."* Wowhead: a dedicated dungeon group is the case for it, and outside dungeons *"you will find being specialized in Restoration to be a hindrance to questing and solo content."* Its strengths in both lists: very hard to kill, and dungeons become easy.
**The usual route** is to level Feral or Balance and heal dungeons in that build. From 40 your class trainer sells Dual Specialization for 50 gold, per Wowhead, which is the cheap way to hold a healing build beside a levelling one.

## Healing priority
**To 30, in a dungeon:**
1. **[[Mark of the Wild]] on the group and [[Thorns]] on the tank** before the pull
2. **[[Rejuvenation]]** on the tank, and on anyone taking light damage
3. **[[Regrowth]]** for a spike. It heals at once and leaves a 21-second heal over time
4. **[[Healing Touch]]** only when the tank is still dropping with both running
5. Spare time: **[[Wrath]]**, or stop casting so Mana regenerates
**From the levels you hold `Swiftmend` and `Nature's Swiftness`:**
1. **`Swiftmend`** on anyone with your [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] who drops fast. With no heal over time on them, [[Rejuvenation]] then `Swiftmend` is a one-second save
2. **`Nature's Swiftness`** then **[[Healing Touch]]** when that is on cooldown, or then **[[Wild Growth]]** when the whole party is low
3. **[[Rejuvenation]] ahead of damage you know is coming**
4. **[[Regrowth]]** on tanks and on spikes
5. **[[Wild Growth]]**, once you have it, when three or more in one party are hurt
6. **[[Insect Swarm]]** on the boss and **[[Wrath]]** in quiet moments
**Use `Swiftmend` freely.** Icy Veins: *"It is too efficient to save it for emergencies only."* It has a 15-second cooldown and does not use up the heal it reads.
**Rules that will catch you out:**
- **`Swiftmend` needs your own [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]]** on the target, per Blizzard's November 2 notes. Another Druid's does not count, and [[Wild Growth]] is not an eligible effect.
- **`Swiftmend` does not consume the effect.** Blizzard: *"This ability does not consume the Rejuvenation or Regrowth effect on the target."* The tooltip said otherwise until a November 6 fix.
- **[[Wild Growth]] heals the target's party, not the lowest health bars in the raid**, and it has a 6-second cooldown and a high Mana cost. It is a 3-second cast, 2.5 with `Naturalist` 5/5, unless `Nature's Swiftness` makes it instant. Wowhead reports it can no longer be cast while moving.
- **`Bloom` only pays when the heal runs out.** Refreshing a [[Rejuvenation]] early skips it *(ours, read from the tooltip)*.
**Downranking is worth a second set of keybinds.** [[Rejuvenation]] takes the same share of your healing power at every rank and [[Regrowth]] has the same 2-second cast, so a low rank on a lightly hit player costs a fraction of the Mana. Icy Veins and Wowhead both say to keep lower ranks on your bars. DoubleZug, healing live dungeons, [keeps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88d4Etzi9t4&t=507) top ranks for the tank and low-rank [[Rejuvenation]] and [[Regrowth]] for everyone else.
**[[Tranquility]]: the sites disagree.** It trains at 30 and heals the party every 2 seconds for 10 seconds on a 5-minute cooldown. Icy Veins: *"quite strong in dungeon groups."* Wowhead: *"Not a useful button to press in WoW: Forever."* Neither takes `Improved Tranquility`.
**Mana.** Innervate trains at 40. Icy Veins: use it on yourself on cooldown while levelling, and drink between packs. `Reflection` 3/3 keeps half your Spirit regeneration running while you cast, and stopping for 5 seconds brings back the rest.
## Stat priority
**Healing Power first. After that the two sites differ**, and both say the order is provisional until level 60 fights have been seen.
1. **Icy Veins (November 4):** Healing Power, Intellect, Spirit, Mana per 5 seconds, critical strike
2. **Wowhead (November 1, labelled speculative):** Spell Healing, critical strike, spell haste, Spirit, Intellect, Mana per 5 seconds
**Spirit is worth more than it looks.** `Living Spirit` 3/3 is +15% Spirit, `Reflection` keeps half its regeneration going in combat, and `Bloom` heals for your Spirit. Wowhead puts `Bloom` at roughly 1 Spell Healing per point of Spirit when the heals are allowed to expire.
**Haste does not speed up healing over time**, and there are no haste breakpoints. Heal-over-time effects can crit, which both sites now confirm.
**While levelling**, Icy Veins says to take Spell Power as well as Healing Power, because your damage sets your levelling speed. Item level first: take the higher piece.

| **`Improved Regrowth` 5/5** | 35 | +50% crit chance on [[Regrowth]]. Requires `Improved Rejuvenation` 3/3 |
| **[[Wild Growth]]** | **40** | The 31-point talent. Heals the target's **party, pets included**, within 43.5 yards for 336 over 7 seconds at rank 1, front-loaded. 6-second cooldown, 550 Mana. Ranks 2 and 3 train at 50 and 60 |
## What Forever changed for Restoration
- **[[Wild Growth]] since launch.** November 10: *"Nature's Swiftness now also makes Wild Growth instant."* November 12: each tick takes 0.066 of your spell power, up from 0.033, on all three ranks. Wowhead reports that the same hotfix stopped it being cast on the move. Blizzard posted no note for the November 12 change.
- **`Bloom` was fixed on November 6** to heal the target; before that it did not.
- **[[Wild Growth]] and `Bloom` are new, and `Swiftmend` was redesigned** and moved from Classic's last row to row 4.
- **Healing over time can crit**: [[Rejuvenation]], [[Tranquility]] and [[Wild Growth]] as well as [[Regrowth]].
- **New talents:** `Bloom`, `Living Spirit` and [[Wild Growth]]. `Improved Tranquility` now also cuts [[Tranquility]]'s cooldown by 60% at 2/2. `Reflection` 3/3 lets 50% of Mana regeneration continue while you cast, where Classic's gave 15%.
- **`Naturalist`**, Classic's Improved Healing Touch, takes 0.5 seconds off [[Healing Touch]] and [[Wild Growth]] and adds +10% to all your damage at 5/5. That is why Feral and Balance builds take it.
- `Nature's Focus` and `Subtlety` cover all Arcane and Nature spells. `Subtlety` is 3 points for 30% less threat. `Gift of Nature` moved from row 5 to row 3. `Improved Regrowth` requires `Improved Rejuvenation`.
- **`Heart of the Wild` arrived from Feral** at row 1, and `Furor` went the other way. `Natural Shapeshifter` moved in from Balance; [[Insect Swarm]] moved out to Balance.
- **[[Revive]]**: an out-of-combat resurrection at level 12. [[Regrowth]] costs 70 Mana at rank 1, against Classic's 120. [[Mark of the Wild]] lasts 1 hour, and [[Gift of the Wild]] covers party and raid.
- **Tree of Life and Lifebloom are not available.** The client holds rows for both, but neither is in the talent tree or on the trainer's list at this build. Build without them.
## At level 60
**What is published:** the two builds above and both sites' rotation pages. They agree on the core: [[Rejuvenation]] and [[Regrowth]] kept on the tanks, [[Regrowth]] as the main answer to a spike, `Swiftmend` on cooldown when it is useful, [[Wild Growth]] for a whole party, and `Nature's Swiftness` held for an emergency.
**In dungeons** both sites call the healing requirement low, and suggest damage between heals. Wowhead publishes two dungeon builds on that idea, a 35/0/16 that casts [[Starfire]] with `Eclipse` between heals and a 0/32/19 that shifts into Cat Form.
**What is not known:** whether [[Wild Growth]] and [[Rejuvenation]] blanketing hold up on Mana in a raid, and which of the two level 60 builds heals more there now that [[Wild Growth]] can be instant and scales twice as hard. Raids open December 9.
