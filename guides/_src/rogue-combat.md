Sustained weapon damage with recurring burst windows, and **the only Rogue cleave in the game.** Two buttons while levelling, and it is the consensus pick.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 20** right now, rising to 30 before launch, so no level-60 Combat build has been tested. Talent rows unlock at levels 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40 — **every tree now has a single-point talent in row 4, the new 16-point milestone.**
:::
## Is Combat worth levelling?
**Yes, and it is the safe answer.** Rated 4 out of 5 for levelling: *"an incredibly simple class to play and to optimize damage. Your rotation will consist primarily of two buttons, [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]]."* And: *"Combat is likely going to continue being an amazing spec for leveling and endgame PvE content."*
**Sources disagree about how to build it**, and the disagreement is philosophical rather than factual — one publishes a pure damage build and states outright *"we have made to ignore talents that offer us more defensive stats, specifically Dodge and Parry"*, while the other publishes a `Riposte` survivability build. Both are legal; pick by whether you solo elites.
## Your talent points
**Level 20 — 0/11/0.** Two published builds, and they genuinely contradict each other.
**The damage build**, whose allocation is forced because the max ranks sum to exactly eleven:
1. `Improved Sinister Strike`, 2 points
2. `Improved Eviscerate`, 3 points
3. `Precision`, 3 points
4. `Puncturing Wounds`, 3 points
**The `Riposte` build**, for survivability:
1. `Lightning Reflexes`, 3 points
2. `Improved Sinister Strike`, 2 points
3. `Deflection`, 3 points
4. `Precision`, 2 points
5. **`Riposte`** at 20
Its stated use: *"High amount of survivability… best for soloing elites or clearing areas with multiple melee mobs packed closely together."*
**Level 30 — 0/21/0**, in this order: `Improved Sinister Strike` 2 → `Improved Eviscerate` 3 → `Precision` 3 → `Deflection` 3 → `Riposte` 1 → `Endurance` 2 → `Improved Sprint` 1 → `Flawless Execution` 1 → `Dual Wield Specialization` 4 → **[[Blade Flurry]]** at 30.
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play the same**, and the source says so: *"During the early stages of Rogue leveling, it is difficult to really differentiate the specializations."*
1. **From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons**
2. **Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]**
3. **[[Backstab]] if you are behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]**
4. At 5 combo points, **[[Slice and Dice]]** if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise **[[Eviscerate]]** at 5 combo points
**Multi-target: nothing until level 30.** [[Blade Flurry]] is 25 energy, +20% melee speed, and **one extra nearby target** for 15 seconds. Two useful framings: it is *"two-target pressure, not damage across an unlimited pack"*, and *"always use it on cooldown for boss fights"* — the haste alone justifies it single-target.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
Published gear advice adds: **slow main hand, fast off hand**, and *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are now secondary skills** — *"like First Aid"* — not Rogue class skills. Poison numbers are lower with more charges: [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"* charges. **Poison and a sharpening stone now stack.**
- **Rogues can learn axes.** Note that the skill **starts at 1**, so a new axe will miss constantly until you raise it.
- **`Weapon Expertise` grants no weapon skill at all** — it now reduces enemy dodge and parry by 1% per rank.
- The tree *"trades the dagger, fist and mace specializations for `Flawless Execution` and `Puncturing Wounds`."* `Improved Backstab` was removed. `Improved Eviscerate` moved **in** from Assassination; `Improved Gouge` moved **out** to it.
- `Precision` is 3 ranks not 5 (3% hit maximum, was 5%). `Dual Wield Specialization` gives 5% per rank, not 10%. `Endurance` is now percentage-based at 30% per rank.
- **Baseline nerfs worth knowing:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. **[[Expose Armor]] was buffed** — rank 5 is *"450 instead of 340"*.
- **Spell, melee and ranged hit are now one stat**, as are all three critical strike chances.
## At level 60
Unknown, and for Combat the open question is concrete: how `Hack and Slash`'s per-weapon-family effects and the new `Weapon Expertise` interact with raid weapons. **Item stats are hidden until they drop**, so the weapon-choice question is not merely untested — it is currently unanswerable.
