## What Combat is for
**`Riposte` lands at exactly level 20:** 10 energy after a parry for 150% weapon damage and a 6-second disarm, on a 6-second cooldown. Every button the level-20 rotation uses is a trained ability rather than a talent. [[Blade Flurry]] at 30 adds the cleave: 25 energy for +20% melee speed and one extra nearby target for 15 seconds, on a 2-minute cooldown.
**Icy Veins and Wowhead both land on Combat**; ClassicWoW.gg does not score the specs. Icy Veins rates it 4 out of 5 for levelling, 3.0 Overall, tied with Assassination on both scores: *"an incredibly simple class to play and to optimize damage. Your rotation will consist primarily of two buttons, [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]]."* It adds: *"Combat is likely going to continue being an amazing spec for leveling and endgame PvE content."* Wowhead agrees from the other direction, calling Combat *"probably the most 'complete' Rogue specialization at level 20,"* with all three of its main abilities already trained. Assassination and Subtlety each miss their signature kit.
**Blizzard's own framing** is *"hard hitting direct damage and little bit of cleave damage with Blade Flurry"*. `Aggression` raises [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]] damage by 5% per rank, 15% at 3/3, and `Flash of Steel` in row 6 makes `Shank`, trained at 24, cheaper to press and richer in Energy.
**No published build spends a point on defence.** Icy Veins states outright *"we have made to ignore talents that offer us more defensive stats, specifically Dodge and Parry"*, judging them unnecessary at this level bracket. `Riposte` itself is real, but no source among our three publishes a defensive "Riposte build" at level 20.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: Wowhead's Sword Build, 3/8/0, or its Dagger Build, 0/11/0.** The max ranks of the non-negotiable three sum with either flex to exactly eleven:
1. `Efficient Strikes`, 2 points, Classic's `Improved Sinister Strike`
2. `Coup de Grace`, 3 points, Classic's `Improved Eviscerate`
3. `Precision`, 3 points
4. Flex the last 3: Sword Build puts 2 into `Remorseless Attacks` and 1 into `Malice` solo, or 3 into `Malice` in a group. Dagger Build puts all 3 into `Puncturing Wounds` instead, for Backstab crit and a 45% chance of a bonus combo point. Blizzard lists no Energy discount on that talent, which some guide sites still show
**Level 30: 0/21/0 — our own extension of the Dagger Build, not a published build.** No respec needed: `Efficient Strikes` 2 → `Coup de Grace` 3 → `Precision` 3 → `Puncturing Wounds` 3 → `Endurance` 2 → `Improved Sprint` 2 → `Flawless Execution` 1 at 25 → `Aggression` 3 → `Weapon Expertise` 1 → [[Blade Flurry]] at 30. `Dual Wield Specialization` shares [[Blade Flurry]]'s row and needs `Precision`, so its 2 points come next. The Sword Build cannot get there without a respec: its 3 Assassination points leave 18 for Combat, and [[Blade Flurry]] needs 20. This line leaves out `Riposte`; a build that wants it swaps `Puncturing Wounds` 3 and `Endurance` 1 for `Deflection` 3 and `Riposte` 1, a respec from either level 20 build. None of our three sources has tested a build that reaches [[Blade Flurry]].
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play the same.** Icy Veins says so on its Assassination page. Wowhead's own three level-20 guides confirm it by being largely interchangeable: *"During the early stages of Rogue leveling, it is difficult to really differentiate the specializations."*
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons
2. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]], or [[Garrote]] without a dagger in the main hand
3. [[Backstab]] if you are behind the target with a dagger in the main hand, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]
4. At 5 combo points, [[Slice and Dice]] if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points
**Multi-target: nothing until [[Blade Flurry]] is reachable.** It costs 25 energy for +20% melee speed and one extra nearby target for 15 seconds, on a 2-minute cooldown. ClassicWoW.gg calls it *"two-target pressure, not damage across an unlimited pack"*. It adds that *"the attack-speed benefit applies even on a single enemy, so do not hold it through a whole boss fight just because no add is present."*
**What changes the answer:**
- **[[Backstab]] or [[Sinister Strike]].** With the same dagger in hand, Backstab deals more per Energy at every level, even against the 40-Energy [[Sinister Strike]] that `Efficient Strikes` gives you, because its 150% also multiplies its flat bonus. It costs more per combo point, 60 Energy against 45, and it needs you behind the target. `Puncturing Wounds` at 3 points brings Backstab to about 41 Energy per point, close to the 40 of a talented [[Sinister Strike]]. The Sword Build has neither choice: [[Backstab]] and [[Ambush]] both need a dagger in the main hand, so [[Sinister Strike]] is its only builder.
- **[[Slice and Dice]] speeds up your swings and nothing else.** It adds no Energy and no builder damage, so its 25 Energy has to come back through auto attacks: +20% melee speed at rank 1, +30% at rank 2, trained at 42. Five points last 21 seconds, one point lasts 9. Wowhead's advice is to refresh it as close to expiry as possible. At 60, WoWSims' list does not wait for 5: it recasts on any number of points the moment the buff falls off *(WoWSims' model)*. Icy Veins' levelling rotation spends at 5, and that is the list above.
- **Hold Energy for the refresh.** At 60, with [[Slice and Dice]] under 2 seconds and at least one combo point, WoWSims' list stops building until it has recast, unless you are at 75 Energy or more *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Leave [[Rupture]] alone.** At 60, WoWSims' list casts it only with `Hemorrhage` on the target, so a Combat Rogue never presses it *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Shank`, from 24, on cooldown.** 5 Energy for 15 back on a 10-second cooldown, and it cannot miss. `Flash of Steel` at 5/5 makes rank 1 return 30 every 8 seconds *(our arithmetic from the talent)*.
- **`Adrenaline Rush`, from 40, is wasted near full Energy.** It doubles Energy regeneration for 15 seconds on a 5-minute cooldown, and Energy past the top of the bar is lost. WoWSims' list waits until you are at 50 Energy or less with [[Slice and Dice]] running *(WoWSims' model)*.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
Published gear advice adds: **slow main hand, fast off hand**, and *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are now secondary skills**: *"like First Aid"*, not Rogue class skills. Poison numbers are lower with more charges: [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"* charges, and Blizzard adds 1% of your Attack Power to it. Poison and a sharpening stone now stack.
- **Rogues can learn axes.** The skill starts at 1, so a new axe will miss constantly until you raise it.
- **Sword specialisation went into `Hack and Slash` too**, which is one more than the guide sites print: one still describes the tree as trading *"the dagger, fist and mace specializations"*. `Improved Backstab` was not removed either; it is `Puncturing Wounds` now, with [[Mutilate]]'s crit chance added to what it already did for [[Backstab]]. `Flawless Execution` is the new 16-point gold medal talent, 15 Energy off [[Eviscerate]]. `Coup de Grace` is Classic's `Improved Eviscerate`, moved in from Assassination with a larger damage bonus, and it also strengthens Subtlety's `Death Mark`; `Improved Gouge` moved out to Assassination. `Efficient Strikes` is Classic's `Improved Sinister Strike`, and its 3 and 5 Energy discount covers `Hemorrhage` too.
- `Precision` now raises hit with everything you do, Poisons included, not just melee swings. The client reads 1% hit per rank across 3 ranks, 3% total, against Classic's 5% across five ranks. The builds above take all three. `Deflection` gives twice the Parry per point. `Endurance` is percentage-based now and takes 60% off the [[Sprint]] and [[Evasion]] cooldowns, further than the flat version did. `Weapon Expertise` was redesigned and grants no weapon skill at all: it cuts your target's Dodge and Parry by 2%.
- **`Dual Wield Specialization` is 2 ranks for 25% and 50% off-hand damage**, Classic's total for fewer points, in row 5 behind `Precision`. No off-hand *hit* change has been published; the conflicting claims about that describe the Warrior talent of the same name, which is a different spell.
- **Baseline nerfs:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. [[Expose Armor]] was buffed instead, to match five [[Sunder Armor]]s at the same level, and it no longer stops the tank using Sunder. The separate `Improved Expose Armor` talent no longer adds armor at all; it cuts Energy cost and refunds combo points (see the Assassination guide, where the talent sits). Fixed October 1: a missed Expose Armor no longer costs you all your combo points (Blizzard), so the finisher is safe to throw at low hit chance.
- **New in Forever:** `Shank` at 24, `Poised` at 18, which takes 10 Energy off your first ability when you start a fight at full Energy, and `Composure` in row 3, 2 ranks, which adds 5 and 10 to that discount. `Aggression` and `Weapon Expertise` sit in row 4, `Improved Sprint` in row 2.
- **Spell, melee and ranged hit are now one stat**, as are all three critical strike chances.
## At level 60
Unplayed, and for Combat the open question is concrete: how `Hack and Slash`'s per-weapon-family effects and the new `Weapon Expertise` interact with raid weapons. **Item stats are hidden until they drop**, so nobody can answer the weapon-choice question yet.
