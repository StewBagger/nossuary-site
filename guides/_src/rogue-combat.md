## What Combat is for
**`Riposte` lands at exactly level 20:** 10 energy after a parry for 150% weapon damage and a **6-second disarm**, on a 6-second cooldown. Every button the level-20 rotation uses is a trained ability rather than a talent, and [[Blade Flurry]] at 30 adds the cleave — 25 energy for +20% melee speed and one extra nearby target for 15 seconds, on a 2-minute cooldown.
**Icy Veins and Wowhead both land on Combat; ClassicWoW.gg does not score the specs.** Icy Veins rates it 4 out of 5 for levelling (3.0 Overall, tied with Assassination on both scores): *"an incredibly simple class to play and to optimize damage. Your rotation will consist primarily of two buttons, [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]]."* And: *"Combat is likely going to continue being an amazing spec for leveling and endgame PvE content."* **Wowhead agrees from the other direction** — it calls Combat *"probably the most 'complete' Rogue specialization at level 20,"* with all three of its main abilities already trained, versus Assassination and Subtlety each missing their signature kit.
**Wowhead publishes two level 20 builds that differ by weapon, not by risk.** Both take `Improved Sinister Strike`, `Improved Eviscerate` and `Precision` as non-negotiable; the **Sword Build** flexes its last 3 points between `Remorseless Attacks` and `Malice`, the **Dagger Build** puts them in `Puncturing Wounds` instead. Icy Veins publishes the same Dagger-style allocation and states outright *"we have made to ignore talents that offer us more defensive stats, specifically Dodge and Parry"* — at this level bracket it judges them unnecessary. `Riposte` itself is real (see below) but no source among our three publishes a defensive "Riposte build" at level 20.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/11/0, from Wowhead** — the max ranks of the non-negotiable three sum with either flex to exactly eleven:
1. `Improved Sinister Strike`, 2 points
2. `Improved Eviscerate`, 3 points
3. `Precision`, 3 points
4. Flex the last 3: **Sword Build** puts 2 into `Remorseless Attacks` and 1 into `Malice` solo, or 3 into `Malice` in a group; **Dagger Build** puts all 3 into `Puncturing Wounds` instead, dropping Backstab's Energy cost to 41 — on par with Sinister Strike
Icy Veins publishes the same Dagger-style allocation independently, with the same four talents.
**Level 30: 0/21/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source has posted one; the cap only reached 30 on October 1. Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs: `Improved Sinister Strike` 2 → `Improved Eviscerate` 3 → `Precision` 3 → `Deflection` 3 → `Riposte` 1 → `Endurance` 2 → `Improved Sprint` 1 → `Flawless Execution` 1 → `Dual Wield Specialization` 4 → **[[Blade Flurry]]** at 30. `Riposte` and [[Blade Flurry]] are both confirmed real talents for when it does (see the table below), but none of our three sources has tested a build that reaches them.
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play the same.** Icy Veins says so on its Assassination page, and Wowhead's own three level-20 guides confirm it by being largely interchangeable: *"During the early stages of Rogue leveling, it is difficult to really differentiate the specializations."*
1. **From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons**
2. **Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]**
3. **[[Backstab]] if you are behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]**
4. At 5 combo points, **[[Slice and Dice]]** if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise **[[Eviscerate]]** at 5 combo points
**Multi-target: nothing until [[Blade Flurry]] is reachable.** It costs 25 energy for +20% melee speed and **one extra nearby target** for 15 seconds, on a 2-minute cooldown. ClassicWoW.gg's framing: it is *"two-target pressure, not damage across an unlimited pack,"* but *"the attack-speed benefit applies even on a single enemy, so do not hold it through a whole boss fight just because no add is present."*
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
Published gear advice adds: **slow main hand, fast off hand**, and *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are now secondary skills**: *"like First Aid"*, not Rogue class skills. Poison numbers are lower with more charges: [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"* charges. **Poison and a sharpening stone now stack.**
- **Rogues can learn axes.** Note that the skill **starts at 1**, so a new axe will miss constantly until you raise it.
- **`Weapon Expertise` grants no weapon skill at all**: it now reduces enemy dodge and parry by 1% per rank.
- The tree *"trades the dagger, fist and mace specializations for `Flawless Execution` and `Puncturing Wounds`."* `Improved Backstab` was removed. `Improved Eviscerate` moved **in** from Assassination; `Improved Gouge` moved **out** to it.
- `Precision` is 3 ranks not 5 (3% hit maximum, was 5%). `Dual Wield Specialization` gives 5% per rank, not 10%. `Endurance` is now percentage-based at 30% per rank.
- **Baseline nerfs worth knowing:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. **The base [[Expose Armor]] ability was buffed**, rank 5 is *"450 instead of 340"* armor per combo point — the separate `Improved Expose Armor` talent no longer adds to that value; it now cuts Energy cost and refunds combo points instead (see the Assassination guide, where the talent sits). **Fixed October 1: a missed Expose Armor no longer costs you all your combo points** (Blizzard) — the finisher is safe to throw at low hit chance.
- **Spell, melee and ranged hit are now one stat**, as are all three critical strike chances.
- **New capstone-adjacent talent:** `Restless Blades` cuts the cooldown of [[Adrenaline Rush]], [[Blade Flurry]], [[Evasion]], [[Sprint]] and [[Vanish]] by 2 seconds per combo point spent (Icy Veins) — not reachable at 20.
## At level 60
Unknown, and for Combat the open question is concrete: how `Hack and Slash`'s per-weapon-family effects and the new `Weapon Expertise` interact with raid weapons. **Item stats are hidden until they drop**, so the weapon-choice question is not merely untested, it is currently unanswerable.
