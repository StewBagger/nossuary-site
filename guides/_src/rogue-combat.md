## What Combat is for
**Steady damage from whatever weapon you are holding, with three talents that change how a fight goes.** `Riposte` at 20 costs 10 Energy after a parry, for 150% weapon damage and a 6-second disarm, on a 6-second cooldown. [[Blade Flurry]] at 30 costs 25 Energy for +20% melee speed and one extra nearby target for 15 seconds, on a 2-minute cooldown. [[Adrenaline Rush]] at 40 doubles Energy regeneration for 15 seconds, on a 5-minute cooldown.
**Blizzard's own framing** is *"hard hitting direct damage and little bit of cleave damage with Blade Flurry"*. Icy Veins, updated November 9, calls it *"some of the highest sustained single-target damage output in the game"* and *"the only Rogue specialization that can cleave"*. Wowhead's levelling page, updated November 6, says Combat Rogues *"feel very smooth to level with"*, and its overview gives the reason the spec travels well: it does *"not rely on Poisons or Bleed effects"*, which some enemies are immune to.
**Both sites build their levelling around `Riposte`.** Icy Veins takes `Deflection` and `Riposte` by level 20. Wowhead: *"This will be the bread and butter for Combat Rogues while leveling."*

## Rotation
**While levelling**, Wowhead's whole rotation is [[Sinister Strike]], then [[Eviscerate]] when the enemy is nearly dead, or [[Slice and Dice]] if another enemy is close enough to use it on. In order:
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons
2. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Cheap Shot]] or [[Garrote]]. [[Ambush]] needs a dagger in the main hand
3. [[Sinister Strike]], or [[Backstab]] behind the target with a dagger
4. `Riposte` whenever it lights up after a parry
5. At 5 combo points, [[Slice and Dice]] if there is more to fight, otherwise [[Eviscerate]]
**From 24, press [[Shank]] every 10 seconds** unless it would take you over the Energy cap.
**At 60, Icy Veins' priority, updated November 4:**
1. [[Instant Poison]] on the main hand and [[Deadly Poison]] on the off hand
2. [[Expose Armor]] only if the group has no stronger armour reduction
3. [[Slice and Dice]] up at all times
4. [[Rupture]] if the target will survive its duration
5. Open with [[Garrote]]; [[Shank]] on cooldown *"if it will not send you over Energy cap"*
6. [[Sinister Strike]] to build, or [[Backstab]] in the dagger build; [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points when the buffs above are running
**Multi-target: [[Blade Flurry]] and nothing else.** ClassicWoW.gg calls it *"two-target pressure, not damage across an unlimited pack"*. Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg agree that on a single target you press it anyway for the attack speed, to start the cooldown.
**What changes the answer:**
- **[[Rupture]] or not: the sources disagree.** Icy Veins keeps it up. WoWSims' list never casts it in the Combat presets: it wants `Blood Splatter` or [[Hemorrhage]] behind it, and skips it for any build holding `Flawless Execution` *(WoWSims' model)*. Neither rests on a log.
- **[[Adrenaline Rush]] is wasted near full Energy.** Energy past the top of the bar is lost. Icy Veins uses it once the opening Energy is spent. WoWSims' list waits until 50 Energy or less with [[Slice and Dice]] running *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Flash of Steel` makes [[Shank]] your Energy engine.** At 5/5 it adds 15 Energy and takes 2 seconds off the cooldown: 30 Energy every 8 seconds at rank 1, and 40 from level 44 with rank 2 *(our arithmetic from Blizzard's figures)*. [[Shank]] cannot miss. WoWSims' list presses it at 80 Energy or less *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Slice and Dice]] speeds up your swings and nothing else.** +20% melee speed at rank 1, +30% at rank 2, trained at 42. Five points last 21 seconds, one point lasts 9. WoWSims' list recasts it on any number of points the moment it falls off *(WoWSims' model)*; Icy Veins refreshes at 5.
- **`Cold Blood` goes on a 5-point [[Eviscerate]].** Icy Veins and WoWSims' list agree.
- **[[Backstab]] or [[Sinister Strike]].** [[Backstab]] and [[Ambush]] need a dagger in the main hand. Backstab costs 60 Energy against 45, or 40 with `Efficient Strikes`, and needs you behind the target. `Puncturing Wounds` at 3 points gives it +30% crit and a 45% chance of a second combo point.
## Stat priority
**While levelling**, from Icy Veins: weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina. Both sites' [[Sinister Strike]] builds assume a slow main hand.
**At 60 the two sites order the top differently, and both say it is early.**
- Icy Veins: Expertise to 6.5%, Hit to 8%, Agility, weapon damage, critical strike.
- Wowhead, which calls its own list *"purely speculative for endgame"*: Hit to 8%, Expertise to 6.5%, weapon skill, then Hit on to 17% for poisons, Agility, critical strike.
- WoWSims' stored weights put Expertise and Hit first, then Agility, then Haste and critical strike level with each other. They were not retuned after the November 3 talent rework *(WoWSims' model)*.
`Precision` gives 3% Hit and `Weapon Expertise` takes 2% off the target's Dodge and Parry, so count both before chasing either stat on gear.
## What defines Combat
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Riposte`** | 11 points (level 20) | Needs `Deflection` 3/3. 10 Energy, after a parry: 150% weapon damage and a 6-second disarm, 6-second cooldown |
| **[[Blade Flurry]]** | 21 points (level 30) | 25 Energy, +20% melee speed and one extra target for 15 seconds, 2-minute cooldown |
| **`Hack and Slash`** | 21 points (level 30) | 5 ranks. Axe or sword: 5% chance of an extra attack. Dagger or fist: 5% crit. Mace: ignore 15% of armour |
| **`Flash of Steel`** | 26 points (level 35) | 5 ranks: [[Shank]] gives back 3 more Energy per rank and its cooldown is 0.4 seconds shorter per rank |
| **[[Adrenaline Rush]]** | 31 points (level 40) | Needs [[Blade Flurry]]. Energy regeneration doubled for 15 seconds, 5-minute cooldown |
**How `Hack and Slash` counts your two weapons**, from Wowhead's talent page, updated November 11:
- **Mace:** the armour ignore applies to both weapons, even with a mace in only one hand.
- **Dagger or fist:** the crit applies only to the hand holding it.
- **Sword or axe:** the extra attack always comes from the main hand, and only a sword or axe hit can set it off. [[Sinister Strike]] needs the sword or axe in the main hand to do so.
Wowhead's conclusion is that a dagger main hand with a mace off hand is *"extremely strong for a Combat Backstab build"*.
## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **`Hack and Slash` absorbed all four weapon specialisations**, dagger, mace, sword and fist, and added axes on the sword bonus. The mace bonus is armour ignore now rather than a chance to stun. Rogues can learn one-handed axes.
- **`Aggression` is 3 ranks for 5%, 10% and 15%** on [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]], and no longer covers [[Backstab]]. `Coup de Grace` is Classic's Eviscerate talent, moved in from Assassination at 7%, 13% and 20%, and it also strengthens Subtlety's `Death Mark`. `Efficient Strikes` is Classic's Sinister Strike discount, 3 and 5 Energy, now covering [[Hemorrhage]] too. `Flawless Execution` is the new 16-point talent, 15 Energy off [[Eviscerate]].
- `Precision` raises hit with everything you do, Poisons included: 1% per rank, 3 ranks. `Endurance` takes 30% and 60% off the [[Sprint]] and [[Evasion]] cooldowns. `Weapon Expertise` grants no weapon skill: it cuts your target's Dodge and Parry by 1% per rank, 2 ranks.
- **`Dual Wield Specialization` is 2 ranks for 25% and 50% off-hand damage**, in row 5.
- **New in Forever:** [[Shank]] at 24, `Poised` at 18, which takes 10 Energy off your first ability when you start a fight at full Energy, and `Composure` in row 3, 2 ranks, which adds 5 and 10 to that discount.
- **Poisons are a secondary skill**, and a poison and a sharpening stone go on the same weapon. Blizzard adds 1% of your Attack Power to [[Instant Poison]].
- [[Expose Armor]] matches five [[Sunder Armor]]s and no longer stops the tank using Sunder. A missed Expose Armor no longer costs your combo points.
## At level 60
**In a group you are single-target damage, one cooldown of cleave, and control.** Icy Veins, updated November 9: Combat *"is likely to find a spot in parties forming for any kind of content"*, and its contribution on trash packs is *"sadly very limited"*. Wowhead expects most Rogues to keep a Combat spec beside another one now that dual specialisation exists.
**The weapon question has a first answer** in Wowhead's `Hack and Slash` notes above. What is not known yet is how the sword and dagger builds compare on real bosses: that waits for logs, and the first raids open on December 9.
