## Is Leatherworking worth taking?
It earns **Legacy Points** at skill 150, 225 and 300, it makes Bind-on-Pickup armour for the four leather and mail classes from skill 60, and its [[Camp Tent]] tops a group's rested experience up to 5% of a level once an hour.
Icy Veins recommends it by class. For Hunters (November 4): *"one of the strongest and most natural combinations for Hunter will be Skinning and Leatherworking."* For Rogues (November 4): *"Leatherworking is an incredible choice, especially when paired with Skinning."* Its Elemental Shaman levelling page recommends it too. Wowhead (November 7) names Druids, Rogues and Hunters as the classes its levelling sets suit.
For leather and mail wearers it is close to mandatory *(ours, not a source's)*. No published verdict goes that far.
If you only want an armour kit, buy one. Kits are tradeable; the Bind-on-Pickup armour is not.
## What it pairs with
**Skinning.** Leatherworking eats leather, hides and scales, and Skinning is the only profession that gathers them. Wowhead also names Engineering and Enchanting as second choices.
**Pristine Leather ties the two together.** Every 30-Favor armour pattern and 21 of the Tanning Rack recipes need it. It is a Scarce material that a skinned beast sometimes gives on top of its leather. See [Skinning](/guides/wow-forever/professions/skinning/) for what is known about where it drops.
## Camp objects and the Tanning Rack
| Skill to place | Object | What it does | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Camp Tent]] | Raises rested experience to 5% of a level for everyone sitting nearby. No effect if you already have more. The same at every character level | 5 Light Leather |
| 125 | `Tanning Rack` | A crafting station, and keeps the tent's benefit | 5 Medium Leather, 3 Fine Thread, 2 Simple Wood |
| 300 | `Sewing Machine` | A crafting station for recipes such as Chromatic Cloak, and keeps the tent's benefit | Undeath Engine, 5 Rugged Leather, Rune Thread |
**The Tanning Rack needs skill 125, not 140.** Blizzard lowered it on November 6 so the rack stops blocking the patterns that open at 125, and lowered its skill-ups by 15 points to match. Wowhead and Icy Veins still print 140.
**48 recipes can only be made beside a rack** (foreverchanges.pro's count). They include the six kilts and legguards at 125, most of the Prowler's-to-Warden's pieces from 150 to 225, Wolfshead Helm, Cured Rugged Hide and three Dragonscale pieces.
How a rack behaves is foreverchanges.pro's account (October 16), not Blizzard's:
1. A rack needs a campfire, lasts 15 minutes and is used up when placed
2. Every camp object you place shares a one-hour cooldown, so put the whole batch's reagents in your bags first
3. The rack is tradeable. Below 125 you can craft at another leatherworker's rack
4. The Blueprint drops from Razorclaw the Butcher in Shadowfang Keep and can be sold. foreverchanges.pro puts it at about one in 900 kills, which is its figure and not Blizzard's
The Sewing Machine's Blueprint source is not published.

## Armour you make while levelling
The Merchant's Favor patterns are the reason to level this early. All of them are Bind on Pickup.
| Favor each | Skill | Lines | Slots |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 | 60–125 | Brawler's, Defender's, Stormrider's, Totemic, Trapper's, Wisdom's | Belt, gloves, boots, hood, tunic, legs |
| 60 | 175–210 | Mender's, Prowler's, Skirmisher's, Skulker's, Skycaller's, Stalker's, Warden's | Gloves, shoulders, plus `Forceful Thick Armor Kit` |
| 90 | 225–250 | The same seven, in leather and mail | Bracers, boots, belt, plus `Mystic Rugged Armor Kit` |
There are 81 patterns and the Certification. Daniel Stitchsong sells them at Three Corners in Redridge, and Pawani outside the Crossroads. See [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/).
Some of the same patterns also drop in dungeons. Blizzard's October 1 build made available *"a number of recipes intended for Blacksmithing and Leatherworking as random drops in dungeons"*. foreverchanges.pro has seen the Brawler's Leather Hood pattern on Lady Sarevess in Blackfathom Deeps and the Trapper's on Skum in Wailing Caverns. No full drop list exists.
**Vendors pay less for some of this armour since November 10.** Blizzard cut the vendor price of Bind-on-Pickup crafted armour that *"a player was intended to craft only once for themselves"*. Do not plan on vendoring spares.
## What else Forever changed
- **Armour kits split into three lines.** Stamina kits remain. Forceful kits give attack power and Mystic kits give spell power, at Medium, Heavy, Thick and Rugged tiers. [[Wild Leather Armor Kit]] is new as well
- **Reagent bags are new to Leatherworking**, starting with `Light Leather Reagent Bag`
- **Fel Leather and Fel Hide are new endgame leathers.** They go into pieces such as Warbear Helm and Tranquil Wristguards. Where they are skinned is not published
- **Cured Rugged Hide now needs a Tanning Rack and Refined Deeprock Salt**, on foreverchanges.pro's reading
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor gives the title *"the Leatherworker"*
- **Craftsman's Writs** cover 30 Leatherworking items. Since November 9 writs and Waylaid Crates drop inside dungeons too

`Dragonscale`, `Elemental` and `Tribal` Leatherworking are in the game. Wowhead (November 7) lists the recipes each one gates, with skill and materials.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Dragonscale` | 13 | 235–300 | Mail: Green, Blue, Black and Red Dragonscale, Chromatic Cloak, Onyxia Scale Breastplate |
| `Elemental` | 15 | 245–300 | Leather: Living, Volcanic, Stormshroud, Molten. Also [[Storm Gauntlets]] and [[Invulnerable Mail]] |

## Level-60 crafted gear
- **Tier-set pieces.** Wowhead's new-recipe list has Grimstitch (4 patterns), Grovekeeper (16), Spiritcaller (12) and Wildstalker's (4), each in boots, gloves, legs and shoulders. Wildstalker is the Hunter set. The others read as Rogue, Druid and Shaman from their armour type and pattern counts, which is our inference
- **What they cost is only half published.** Wowhead lists reagents for Tailoring's equivalents: 3 [[Malleable Essence of Nature]] a piece and 5 for legs. It lists none for Leatherworking's. The client's item text says the essence is *"acquired from disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps"*, and both raids open December 9
- **New Dragonscale and endgame patterns**: Black and Blue Dragonscale Helm, Red Dragonscale Leggings, Warbear Helm, Living Crown, Outlaw's Collar. Their pattern sources are not recorded
- **Raid materials are unplayed.** Core Leather, Fiery Core, Lava Core, Scale of Onyxia and Dreamscale recipes wait on raids, which open December 9
- The Dire Maul library still holds the three specialisation cloak patterns at skill 300, on Wowhead's Dire Maul North page
## Levelling it
**1 to 225, Icy Veins' route (November 4).** It uses trainer recipes only and needs no Tanning Rack, so no hotfix has changed it.
1. **1 → 30**: Light Armor Kit, about 29. Turn Ruined Leather Scraps into Light Leather first while that still gives skill
2. **30 → 55**: Cured Light Hide, about 27. Keep them all
3. **55 → 75**: Embossed Leather Gloves, about 21
4. **75 → 90**: Medium Leather, then 10 Cured Medium Hide. Keep the hides
5. **90 → 100**: Fine Leather Belt, about 16. Keep ten belts
6. **100 → 115**: Light Leather Pants, about 22, using the saved Cured Light Hide
7. **115 → 125**: Dark Leather Belt, 10, each from a saved belt and a saved hide
8. **125 → 130**: Heavy Leather. Train Expert now
9. **130 → 155**: 20 Cured Heavy Hide, then Heavy Armor Kit
10. **155 → 175**: Barbaric Shoulders ×10, then Guardian Gloves ×10
11. **175 → 195**: Thick Armor Kit, about 20
12. **195 → 210**: Nightscape Headband, about 20
13. **210 → 225**: Nightscape Pants, about 15, taught by the Artisan trainers
**Icy Veins' shopping list to 225:** 440 Light Leather, 410 Thick Leather, 198 Heavy Leather, 27 Medium Leather, 27 Light Hide, 10 Medium Hide, 20 Heavy Hide, 97 Salt, 130 Silken Thread, 79 Fine Thread, 74 Coarse Thread, 10 Gray Dye.
If Heavy Hide is dear, Icy Veins swaps 130 to 175 for Heavy Armor Kit to 160 and 15 Dusky Bracers, at about 450 Heavy Leather.
**225 to 300.** Icy Veins says it does not have enough information to give a route yet. foreverchanges.pro computes one from the client's recipes:
1. **225 → 239**: Rugged Armor Kit, about 23, from the trainer
2. **239 → 267**: Wicked Leather Gauntlets, about 34. Pattern from Leonard Porter or Werg Thickblade
3. **267 → 300**: Runic Leather Headband, about 45. Pattern from Jase Farlane in Eastern Plaguelands, in limited stock
That stretch takes about 1,017 Rugged Leather, 450 Runecloth, 79 Rune Thread and 34 Black Dye. The counts are averages. The site prices reagents to pick the cheapest path, so its route can change, and it says the skill each trainer recipe is learned at is Classic's because the client does not hold Forever's.
**Rugged Leather is the wall.** A thousand of it comes from the level-50-plus zones: Winterspring, Felwood, Un'Goro Crater and the Plaguelands. With Skinning, Leatherworking is the cheapest crafting profession to level; without it, the dearest *(ours, not a source's)*.
## What is not known
The requirements for the three specialisations. What Leatherworking's tier pieces cost. Where Fel Leather is skinned. Where the Sewing Machine Blueprint and the new level-60 patterns drop. What 80 of its recipes make. A played route from 225 to 300.
