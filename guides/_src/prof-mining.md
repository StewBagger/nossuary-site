Ore, stone and smelting. **The vein table is unchanged from Classic**, so your old route still works. Blizzard did thin the copper in the starter areas on October 1, where that route begins. What changed otherwise is what the ore is worth, plus a camp object that hands your group up to 90 attack power.
:::scope
Vein types, skill thresholds and camp objects come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. **Professions are capped at skill 225 in the beta.** The zone recommendations below are Classic spawn data; Forever's new zones, including Zephras Isle, are not counted yet. Node density, respawn rules and skill-up rates are unpublished, apart from the October 1 cut to Poor Copper Vein nodes in the starter areas.
:::
## Is Mining worth taking?
Waylaid Crates give low-level ore a guaranteed buyer it never had in vanilla, which makes Mining a better money profession than it was *(ours, not a source's)*.
Mining crafts nothing itself; its entire value is feedstock and gold. It also **earns no Legacy Points**, since only the six crafting professions do. If you are chasing the 18 Tradeskill points, this is the wrong pick.
Beyond gold, the camp object is the argument. [[Lodestone]] at skill 20 gives **90 melee attack power** to everyone sitting nearby and does not stack with [[Blessing of Might]], so a melee group without a Paladin wants a miner at the fire. The 90 is the figure at level 52 and above. The client scales it by level, and at the beta's level-30 cap the Lodestone gives 32. Blizzard publishes no camp-object value and no equivalence to a class buff.
## What it pairs with
Blacksmithing and Engineering both eat ore, and **Mining is the only profession that gathers it.** Neither has another feed, and nothing published suggests Forever changed that.

## Levelling it
1. **1 → 65**: Copper. Durotar, Westfall, Duskwood, Silverpine
2. **65 → 125**: Tin and Silver. Arathi, Wetlands, Duskwood, Silverpine
3. **125 → 175**: Iron and Gold. Arathi, Alterac, Stranglethorn, Duskwood
4. **175 → 245**: Mithril and Truesilver. Badlands, Hinterlands, Alterac, Searing Gorge
5. **245 → 300**: Small and Rich Thorium. Winterspring, Azshara, Swamp of Sorrows, Blasted Lands
**Smelting what you mine gives skill points too**, until the recipe turns grey. Do not vendor raw ore while you are levelling.
**Step 1 got slower on October 1**: with fewer Poor Copper Vein nodes in the starter areas, budget more travel between copper than a Classic route predicts.
**The bottleneck is still 175 to 245**, the familiar Mithril desert. It eats travel time rather than gold.
## What Forever changed
- **Fewer copper nodes in the starter areas.** Blizzard's October 1 build *"reduced the amount of Poor Copper Vein nodes available in starter areas"*. It is the only node-density change Blizzard has published for any gathering profession.
- **Two new smelting recipes:** [[Azerothium Bar]] at skill 275 (2 Pitchblende + 1 Coal) and [[Heavy Thorium Bar]] at 300 (1 Heavy Thorium Ore).
- **Two new ores with no published source.** `Pitchblende` and `Heavy Thorium Ore` are in the client's item table and in no vein's published drop list. The client does name a Heavy Thorium Lode, carrying the same gather spell the ten Classic lodes carry. So that ore has a node type, even though no zone or skill level is published for it. `Pitchblende` has no lode of any kind. Where you get either is the biggest open question in Mining.
- **Three camp objects**, all new:
  | Skill | Object | What it does |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 20 | [[Lodestone]] | **90 melee attack power** nearby at 52 and above, 32 at level 30. Mutually exclusive with [[Blessing of Might]] |
  | 140 | [[Rock Garden]] | Blueprint. Spawns a common mining node over time, and keeps the Lodestone buff |
  | 300 | [[Molten Foundry]] | Blueprint. Required by recipes that call for it, and keeps the Lodestone buff |
- **Ore now buys recipes.** Waylaid Crates take raw ore and convert it into [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/).
- **`Bountiful Harvest`**, a Legacy perk, gives up to +100% more Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning across five ranks.
- **There is no Mining specialisation.** Claims that you can specialise into extra ore or rare materials are not in the client data. Do not go looking.
## What is not known
Where Pitchblende comes from, and which zones carry the Heavy Thorium lode. What counts as a "Scarce material". Node locations, respawn rules and gathering thresholds in Forever's own zones. And whether vanilla's passive Toughness bonus survives. Nothing mentions it either way.
