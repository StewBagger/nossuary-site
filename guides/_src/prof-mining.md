Ore, stone and smelting. **Every Classic vein still asks the same skill**, so an old route still works. What is new sits at both ends: easy copper in the starting zones, and a skill-300 lode on Mount Hyjal that feeds the level-60 crafted sets. In between, ordinary veins now drop Scarce materials, and a camp object hands your group up to 90 attack power.
:::scope
Vein skills, loot and zones are Wowhead's (November 9) and Icy Veins' (November 3 and 4), named where they differ. The levelling route is Icy Veins'. Smelting colours, camp-object figures and the Molten Foundry requirement are client data at build 70938. Blizzard's hotfixes through November 12 changed nothing in Mining. **Still thin:** the Heavy Thorium lode rests on Wowhead alone, and Icy Veins still calls its location unknown. Which veins drop Pitchblende is a guess in Wowhead's own words. Node density and respawn times are unpublished.

## Is Mining worth taking?
Mining crafts no gear. Its value is feedstock and gold. Wowhead (November 9) calls it *"an excellent source of gold in Forever, requiring no upfront investment to get started."* Waylaid Crates also give low-level ore a buyer it never had in vanilla *(ours, not a source's)*.
It **earns no Legacy Points**. Only the six crafting professions have Legacy challenges, and Wowhead confirms Mining has none.
Two things argue for it beyond gold:
- **The camp object.** [[Lodestone]] at skill 20 gives melee attack power to everyone sitting nearby and is exclusive with [[Blessing of Might]], so a melee group without a Paladin wants a miner at the fire
- **The level-60 supply chain.** Heavy Thorium Ore is mined at skill 300 and smelted only at a miner's Molten Foundry. Every crafted set piece in Blacksmithing needs the bars *(ours, from the client's reagent lists)*
## What it pairs with
[Blacksmithing](/guides/wow-forever/professions/blacksmithing/) and [Engineering](/guides/wow-forever/professions/engineering/) both eat ore and stone, and Mining is the only profession that gathers them. Icy Veins calls those two *"the most direct matches"*. Wowhead also suggests Herbalism as a second gathering profession, since you pass herbs on every mining lap.

- **Poor Copper Veins are new.** Icy Veins says they were *"added with WoW Forever to help players quickly level up their Mining skill early on."* They give Copper Ore and Rough Stone only
- **Veins stay useful longer.** Wowhead says the colour thresholds moved, so a vein *"changes from yellow to green slightly later"* than in Classic. The two sites print different thresholds, so trust your tracking colours over either table
- **A node takes several hits but gives one skill point at most**, per Wowhead. Icy Veins notes that a group of miners can each take a hit and each get a point
- **Dark Iron** is still only in Burning Steppes, Searing Gorge and the Blackrock instances
## Levelling it
**This is Icy Veins' route**, dated November 4. It starts at a forge, because copper ore is cheap to buy.
1. **1 → 65**: smelt 120 to 140 Copper Ore. Or mine copper in your starting zone: Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal, Elwynn, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil or Zephras Isle
2. **65 → about 105, still at the forge**: smelt 70 Tin Ore, then 70 Bronze from the tin and 70 of your copper bars. Train Journeyman at 50 first
3. **To 125**: Tin from 65, and Silver from 75. Silverpine, Hillsbrad and the Barrens for the Horde. Wetlands and Westfall for the Alliance. Ashenvale and Stonetalon for both. Copper veins turn grey at 100
4. **125 → 175**: Iron, and Gold from 155. Arathi Highlands, Desolace and Thousand Needles. Train Expert at 125
5. **175 → 245**: Mithril, and Truesilver from 230. Tanaris and the Hinterlands. Train Artisan at 200, or you stop at 225
6. **245 → 275**: Small Thorium. Un'Goro Crater or Felwood
7. **275 → 300**: Rich Thorium, with Small Thorium still giving points. Winterspring or Eastern Plaguelands
**The starting-area trainers teach Apprentice only, and they do not teach smelting.** Visit a full trainer in town before step 1.
**Smelting stops paying early.** In the client, Smelt Copper is grey at 70, Tin at 75, Bronze at 115, Silver at 125, Iron at 140 and Gold at 185. Steel, Mithril, Truesilver, Dark Iron and Thorium are grey the moment you learn them. Wowhead puts it plainly: *"Past level 185, the only way to level is by mining."*
**The slow stretch is still Mithril, 175 to 245** *(ours, not a source's)*. Nothing smelts for points there and the route has only two zones.
foreverchanges.pro's zone list reads differently, with Winterspring, Azshara and the Blasted Lands for thorium. It counts Classic's spawn points and says so. Icy Veins' route was written for the live game.

These are Icy Veins' figures (November 3). It gives gathering professions lower level gates than crafting ones. foreverchanges.pro still lists the crafting gates of 10, 20 and 35, taken from Classic.
Every full Mining trainer teaches all four ranks. That includes the capital cities, Pikkle in Gadgetzan, Grungle at Powderfuse Port in Riverglades, and both trainers on Zephras Isle.
**Skill boosts** let you mine a vein early (Wowhead):
- Miner's Cape, from Miner Johnson in the Deadmines: +2
- Goblin Mining Helmet, a Goblin Engineering craft: +5
- `Enchant Gloves - Mining` +2 and `Enchant Gloves - Advanced Mining` +5
Rockslicer lets anyone, miner or not, mine Copper, Tin, Silver and Iron veins. It drops with 30 charges.
## The new ores and bars
- **Heavy Thorium Ore** comes from the Heavy Thorium Lode at skill 300. Wowhead (November 9) places it on Mount Hyjal. Icy Veins (November 3) still says the location is unknown, and Wowhead's page is the newer one
- **[[Heavy Thorium Bar]]** is 1 Heavy Thorium Ore at skill 300. **It is smelted at a Molten Foundry, not at a forge.** The client's recipe requires it and Icy Veins says the same
- **[[Azerothium Bar]]** is 2 Pitchblende and 1 Coal at skill 275, at an ordinary forge
- **Legionite Bar is not a Mining recipe.** Alchemists transmute it from Heavy Thorium Bars, and Wowhead knows no other source
- **Pyrite, Bauxite and Pitchblende are Scarce materials.** You get them by chance on top of the normal ore, and which one depends on the vein. Alchemists turn Pyrite into Sulfuric Acid. Bauxite goes into new recipes, Enchanting's among them
- **Where Pitchblende drops is not confirmed.** Wowhead: *"Pitchblende is likely from Truesilver and higher deposits, but this is unconfirmed."* Neither site's vein table lists it on any vein
## At camp
| Skill | Object | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Lodestone]] | Melee attack power to everyone sitting nearby. Trainer-taught, 1 Rough Stone and 1 Copper Bar |
| 140 | [[Rock Garden]] | Blueprint. Spawns a common mining node over time, and keeps the Lodestone buff |
| 300 | [[Molten Foundry]] | Blueprint. The station for Smelt Heavy Thorium, and keeps the Lodestone buff. 5 Thorium Bar, 2 Dense Stone, 1 Truesilver Bar |
**The Lodestone scales with your level.** The client pays 12 attack power below level 12, 20 from 12, 32 from 22, 49 from 32, 67 from 42 and **90 from level 52**. The tooltip prints only the 90. Blizzard's October 21 camping article lists ten camp objects with their ranges, and the Lodestone is the one it leaves out, so these figures are the client's alone.
The Rock Garden Blueprint drops in Excavation Site: Wetlands. Wowhead and Icy Veins agree, and Icy Veins names the Relic Guardian. **No source says where the Molten Foundry Blueprint drops.**
## What else Forever changed
- **Blizzard thinned the easy copper once.** Its October 1 notes *"reduced the amount of Poor Copper Vein nodes available in starter areas"*. It is the only node-density change Blizzard has published for any gathering profession
- **Ore now buys recipes.** Waylaid Crates take raw ore and bars and convert them into [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/)
- **`Bountiful Harvest`**, a Legacy perk with five ranks. The client text makes you 20% more likely per rank to discover Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning, 100% at rank five. Wowhead words it as 20% more materials per rank. The client's wording is a chance
- **`Working Overtime`** adds 4% skill-up chance per rank, 20% at five ranks
- **There is no Mining specialisation.** Claims that you can specialise into extra ore or rare materials are not in the client data
## What is not known
Which veins drop Pitchblende, and how often any Scarce material drops. Whether Mithril drops Bauxite, since the two sites disagree. Where the Molten Foundry Blueprint comes from. Node density, respawn rules and how many Heavy Thorium lodes Mount Hyjal holds.
