## What Shadow is for
**The damage a Priest does not otherwise get: [[Mind Flay]].** The client makes it talent-only, gated on 10 points in the Shadow tree, and the single point grants all 6 spell ranks. No published Holy or Discipline build has it, so their rotations end in [[Smite]], [[Mind Blast]] and a wand.
The published ratings put Shadow at 3 out of 5 for levelling, the highest of the three Priest specs, and 2.5 for dungeons, the lowest of the three. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** Shadow is slower than that sounds early on:
*"Slow Starters, Better Later — In lower levels, Shadow has a complicated leveling curve. The spec is incredibly reliant on `Spirit Tap` for its mana regeneration. Failing to get last hits on enemies will generally lead you to drinking more often."*
**It gets better twice, at known levels.** From 25, 16 points in the tree can buy [[Vampiric Embrace]]: party healing off your own Shadow damage, effectively always up, and an enemy dying under it can also fire `Spirit Tap`. At 40, [[Shadowform]]'s 50% mana cut improves the mana curve *"drastically."*
**Land the killing blow.** `Spirit Tap` is your mana economy. It has a 20% chance per rank to fire when you kill a non-trivial target, or when an enemy carrying your [[Vampiric Embrace]] dies, whoever lands the blow. Grey mobs do not count.
## Your talent points
Level 20: **Icy Veins and Wowhead publish the same 11 points, in the same order**, and neither takes [[Mind Flay]]. The two wand points are Discipline, which leaves 9 in Shadow at the very most, short of the 10-point gate either way.
1. `Wand Specialization`, 2 points
2. `Spirit Tap`, 5 points
3. `Improved Shadow Word: Pain`, 2 points
4. Final 2 points: *"there aren't a lot of good options: `Twilight Focus` if you find yourself playing mostly solo; `Blackout` if you're playing in a PvP environment; `Shadow Reach`… for some extra range."* `Shadow Reach` is now only 2 points for the same effect, down a row to row 2, so this option now buys the full talent instead of a partial rank.
Blizzard moved `Shadow Focus` up to row 1 and changed what it does: it now increases the chance for Shadow spells to hit, rather than reducing the target's chance to resist. Neither the published build nor the line below spends on it.
No source had published a build that spends into [[Mind Flay]] at 20, or a level-30 Shadow build, when this page was written.
**Level 30, 2/0/19 — our own extension, not a published build.** In order: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 3 → [[Mind Flay]] → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 2 more, for 5/5 → `Shadow Weaving` 3 → [[Vampiric Embrace]]. The first 9 points are the published level 20 build, its final 2 go into `Improved Mind Blast`, and [[Mind Flay]] lands at 22. The two opening points are Discipline, where `Wand Specialization` lives, so the spread reads 2/0/19 and not 0/0/21. It also keeps the wand habit the rotation below is built on.
## Rotation
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Smite]]** to pull
4. **[[Mind Blast]]**
5. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
6. **Wand until it dies.** No habit matters more: *"The main reason you'll die while playing as a Priest is running out of mana while still fighting against a healthy foe"*
[[Mind Flay]] slots in as your channelled filler if your build bought it. The published rotation omits it because that build does not take it.
**Multi-target:** *"you can deal damage to multiple enemies by applying [[Shadow Word: Pain]] to multiple targets… this can be extremely mana-intensive, especially if you miss last hits to proc `Spirit Tap`."* Pain everything, then wand them down.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then critical strike, Hit, Intellect, Stamina, MP5.
Hit sits lower than you might expect while levelling: *"While fighting enemies at your level, you'll need very little hit chance… Hit will take center stage when talking about endgame content."* It sits lower still now that `Shadow Focus` grants Shadow hit chance directly from the talent tree, instead of its old job of lowering target resist. That talent is row 1, 5 points, and the build above leaves it out for `Spirit Tap`, our call while levelling.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only, as it was in Classic.** In the client it is the Shadow tree's one-point talent, and no trainer teaches it. What Forever changed is the damage: 63 at rank 1, from 75. The client puts the gate at 10 points in Shadow rather than at your spec, so a Holy or Discipline Priest can dip for it. No published Holy or Discipline build reaches it, and their rotations contain no Mind Flay at all.
- **`Shadow Focus`** moved from row 2 to row 1, and now increases Shadow spell hit chance instead of reducing the target's resist chance. See Stat priority above.
- **`Shadow Reach`** moved from row 4 to row 2, and now costs only 2 points for the same range bonus.
- **`Improved Mind Flay`** (new, row 3, requires [[Mind Flay]]): 2 ranks, each tick of Mind Flay deals 10/20% more than the tick before. It has no range or slow effect.
- **`Dark Inspiration`** (new, row 4): [[Mind Blast]] and a fully channelled [[Mind Flay]] raise your and your party's Spirit by 3/6/9/12/15% for 10 seconds.
- **`Shadow Weaving`** now stacks on the target but counts only your own Shadow damage: 33/67/100% chance per rank, 2% a stack, 5 stacks, 10% total. It cannot fail to apply, and it feeds no other Shadow caster or Warlock, so do not take a raid assignment on it. The 3 points in the build buy the 100% chance.
- **`Devouring Contagion`** (new, row 5): 2 ranks, cuts [[Devouring Plague]]'s Mana cost by 25/50%, and when a target dies while afflicted, Devouring Plague jumps to 2 enemies within 5/10 yards for the remaining duration, free and without triggering your cooldown.
- **`Early Demise`** (new, row 6, needs 25 Shadow points): raises [[Shadow Word: Death]]'s critical strike chance against targets under 20% health, and only under that threshold.
- **[[Silence]] no longer requires `Improved Psychic Scream`** as a prerequisite. `Improved Psychic Scream` now requires `Blackout` instead.
- **Removed:** `Improved Vampiric Embrace`, folded into the base `Vampiric Embrace` redesign above, plus `Shadow Affinity` and `Improved Fade`.
- **[[Devouring Plague]] is no longer Undead-only.** It is *"now available to all Priests at level 20, regardless of race"*, Gnome included, with a 24-second cooldown over a 24-second duration, against 3 minutes in Classic. That makes it a rolling damage-over-time from 20 *(our reading)*. It can critically strike, and it is baseline for every spec at 20, so that raises damage across the whole class rather than only Shadow's.
- **[[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every Priest**, learned at 20 *"regardless of race"*, and that is all Blizzard's post says about it. The 3-minute duration and 3-minute cooldown quoted for it are the client reading, not a published nerf.
- **[[Shadow Word: Death]] is new**, for all races at level 32. Instant cast, 18-second cooldown; if it does not kill the target you take 30% of your max health in damage. Its talent `Early Demise` above needs 25 Shadow points.
- **[[Shadowform]] now bars *healing* spells rather than Holy spells**, so [[Smite]], [[Holy Fire]], [[Resurrection]], `Cure Disease` and `Abolish Disease` are usable inside it. See What defines Shadow above for the Mana-cost, crit-damage and flat-damage numbers.
- **New race-locked Priest spells:** `Divine Grace` (Human), `Chastise` (Dwarf), `Dark Sacrifice` (Undead), and `Confounding Flash` plus `Contingency Plan` for Gnome. Gnome is a new Priest race in Forever and needed racials of its own; vanilla Priests were Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Troll and Undead.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, so every wand-levelling build front-loads it. Wands no longer gain spell power.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether Shadow has a raid slot at all. Losing the Shadow Vulnerability debuff role removes what it was *for* in Classic, and what is left is single-target damage plus a [[Vampiric Embrace]] that heals only the party and `Dark Inspiration`'s party Spirit buff.
