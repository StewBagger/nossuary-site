## What Shadow is for
**The damage a Priest does not otherwise get, and healing as a by-product of it.** [[Mind Flay]] is the channelled filler no other tree has. [[Vampiric Embrace]] heals every party member for 20% of the Shadow damage you deal to the marked target. [[Shadowform]] halves your Shadow Mana costs and doubles your Shadow crit bonus.
Icy Veins' ratings: Overall 3.5, Levelling 3.0, Dungeon 2.5, Utility 5.0, Mobility 1.0. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** Its own summary of the levelling curve:
*"In lower levels, Shadow has a complicated leveling curve. The spec is incredibly reliant on `Spirit Tap` for its mana regeneration. Failing to get last hits on enemies will generally lead you to drinking more often."*
**It gets better three times.** [[Mind Flay]] replaces [[Smite]] as your filler from 20 at the earliest. [[Vampiric Embrace]] heals you as you fight, and an enemy dying under it fires `Spirit Tap` whoever lands the blow. At [[Shadowform]] the Mana curve improves *"drastically"*, in Icy Veins' word. Wowhead lists the cost of that last one as a con: *"Substantial power locked behind the level 40 talent"*.
**Land the killing blow.** `Spirit Tap` is your Mana economy while levelling. It has a 20% chance a rank to fire when you kill a target that gives experience, or when an enemy carrying your [[Vampiric Embrace]] dies. It doubles your Spirit for 15 seconds and lets half your regeneration continue while you cast.
A respec is a trainer visit. Icy Veins puts the first at 1 gold, rising each time. `Dual Specialization` is bought from the class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold (Wowhead's report; Blizzard gives the level and no price).
## Your talent points
**Icy Veins publishes a full 10-to-60 order** (updated for launch on November 4). The four lines below are that one order, cut at 20, 30, 40 and 60.
**Level 20, 2/0/9 — Icy Veins' levelling order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 2.
**Level 30, 2/0/19 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 3 → [[Mind Flay]] → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` to 4/5 → `Shadow Reach` 1 → [[Vampiric Embrace]] → `Dark Inspiration` 3.
**Level 40, 2/0/29 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 3 → [[Mind Flay]] → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` to 4/5 → `Shadow Reach` 1 → [[Vampiric Embrace]] → `Dark Inspiration` 5 → `Devouring Contagion` 2 → [[Silence]] → `Shadow Weaving` 1 → `Darkness` 4.
**Level 60, 13/3/35 — the same order, finished:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 3 → [[Mind Flay]] → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` to 4/5 → `Shadow Reach` 1 → [[Vampiric Embrace]] → `Dark Inspiration` 5 → `Devouring Contagion` 2 → [[Silence]] → `Shadow Weaving` 1 → `Darkness` 5 → [[Shadowform]] → `Twin Disciplines` 3 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Meditation` 3 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Shadow Weaving` to 3/3 → `Shadow Focus` 2.
**On that order [[Mind Flay]] arrives at 22, [[Vampiric Embrace]] at 27 and [[Shadowform]] at 42.** The tree allows them at 20, 25 and 40. The two `Wand Specialization` points are the difference, and Wowhead says so in terms: the wand talent *"delays picking up Shadowform by two levels."*
**Wowhead's levelling build (Jereico, updated November 8)** is published as an interactive calculator on [its levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/shadow/dps-leveling-tips); its text gives no split and no order. It describes the same shape: `Wand Specialization` first, then *"a primary focus on Shadow talents"* with no respec. *"After level 53"* it leaves the last points to you, between `Twilight Focus`, `Shadow Focus` and `Mental Strength`. It offers `Dark Inspiration` for group play and [[Silence]] as a one-point option.
**`Blackout` or `Shadow Focus` in row 1 is a choice.** Icy Veins prefers `Blackout` while levelling and `Shadow Focus` if dungeons and hard quests have you missing casts. `Blackout` is 5 ranks: a 2% chance a rank, 10% at 5/5, for any Shadow damage spell to stun the target for 3 seconds. `Shadow Focus` is 1% Shadow hit a rank. Neither is in Icy Veins' order before 59.
**The respec-at-40 route.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both describe it, and Wowhead says many players prefer it: level on Discipline's Mana talents, then respec the moment [[Shadowform]] is available. Icy Veins' order for it: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twin Disciplines` 5 → `Mental Strength` 3 → `Meditation` 3, then Shadow in the usual order, and a respec to deep Shadow at 40. That puts `Meditation` at 27 and [[Shadowform]] at 40 instead of 42, for the price of a respec.
**Level 60, 15/3/33 — Icy Veins' "Cookie Cutter" build (November 4):** `Shadow Focus` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 4 → `Shadow Reach` 1 → [[Mind Flay]] → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → [[Vampiric Embrace]] → `Dark Inspiration` 5 → `Shadow Weaving` 3 → `Devouring Contagion` 2 → [[Silence]] → `Darkness` 5 → [[Shadowform]] → `Twin Disciplines` 5 → `Silent Resolve` 3 → `Mental Strength` 4 → `Meditation` 3 → `Twilight Focus` 3.
That line is listed tree by tree, not in the order you would level it. WoWSims' test preset is also 15/3/33: it takes `Shadow Reach` 2, `Mental Strength` 3 and [[Inner Focus]] where Icy Veins takes `Shadow Reach` 1, `Mental Strength` 4 and [[Silence]] *(a sim preset, not a recommendation)*. Neither takes `Spirit Tap`, `Blackout`, `Early Demise` or `Wand Specialization`.
**Wowhead's raid build (Jereico, November 4)** is published as an interactive calculator on [its talent page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/shadow/talent-builds-pve-dps); its text gives no split. Wowhead calls the build *"still quite speculative"*: it assumes one main boss target and regular raid damage. It leaves out `Spirit Tap`, which it expects *"to provide limited value in raids"*, and does not recommend `Early Demise` or [[Inner Focus]]. It names `Twilight Focus`, `Mental Strength` and `Shadow Reach` as the flexible picks and [[Silence]] as *"a reasonable optional talent for one point"*.
**Why `Improved Mind Blast` stops at 4.** Wowhead leaves it at 4/5 on purpose, and Icy Veins' build does the same. Wowhead's reasoning: four points bring [[Mind Blast]] from 8 seconds to 6, which fits two full [[Mind Flay]] channels between casts. The fifth point's 5.5 seconds fits nothing extra.
**Wowhead's dungeon build** is its raid build with `Spirit Tap` added, for Mana when you keep [[Shadow Word: Pain]] on several enemies. It is a calculator too, and the text gives no split.
## Rotation
**Levelling.** Icy Veins' list. Wowhead's is the same order:
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull, unless the enemy will die before it reaches you
3. **[[Mind Blast]]** to pull. Before you own [[Mind Flay]], pull with **[[Smite]]** and follow with Mind Blast
4. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
5. **[[Devouring Plague]]**, if Mana allows
6. **[[Mind Flay]]**, if Mana allows
7. **Wand until it dies**
Icy Veins says you will rarely cast steps 5 and 6 before 30, and that they come into their own with [[Shadowform]] and `Devouring Contagion`. The wand finish is the point of the list: a wand costs no Mana, so your Spirit regeneration restarts sooner and `Spirit Tap` lands on a full five seconds of it.
**Level 60, single target.** Icy Veins and Wowhead publish the same priority, and WoWSims' default list runs the same order *(a model)*:
1. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]** up. Recast it as it expires, not before
2. **[[Devouring Plague]]** up
3. **[[Mind Blast]]** on cooldown
4. **[[Shadow Word: Death]]** on cooldown
5. **[[Vampiric Embrace]]** up, when the group needs the healing
6. **[[Mind Flay]]**, channelled to the end
Both sites open by casting [[Fade]] and then [[Mind Blast]] before the pull, for threat.
**Several targets.** Same priority, with [[Shadow Word: Pain]] on each target that will live at least 9 seconds *(Icy Veins' figure)*. With `Devouring Contagion`, put [[Devouring Plague]] on the enemy that will die first so it spreads. Put [[Vampiric Embrace]] on the one that will live longest.
**Why this order.** The figures are the client's base numbers, before gear and talents.
- **[[Mind Flay]] and [[Shadow Word: Pain]] are your best Mana.** Around level 28, Flay rank 2 returns 1.46 damage per point of Mana and Pain rank 4 returns 1.16, or 1.55 with `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2/2 adding two ticks. [[Mind Blast]] rank 4 returns about 1.0 and [[Smite]] about 0.63. At 60 the top ranks read 1.90 for Flay, 1.62 for Pain, 1.40 for Mind Blast, 1.35 for [[Shadow Word: Death]] and 0.63 for Smite *(our arithmetic on the client's numbers)*.
- **[[Shadowform]] doubles every Shadow figure above**, because it halves the cost. [[Smite]] gets nothing from it.
- **[[Devouring Plague]] is poor on Mana until two talents fix it.** Rank 6 returns 0.86 per point of Mana at 985 Mana. [[Shadowform]] and `Devouring Contagion` 2/2 each halve the cost, which takes it to about 3.4 *(our arithmetic)*. Wowhead's levelling page says to skip it before `Devouring Contagion` when Mana is short.
- **Let Pain run out before you recast it.** Both sites say so: an early recast throws away the ticks still to come.
- **Finish the channel.** With `Improved Mind Flay` 2/2 each tick deals 20% more than the last: 100, 120, 144. The third tick is about 40% of the channel. `Dark Inspiration` also needs a full channel.
- **From 32, [[Shadow Word: Death]] is a killing blow while levelling.** It is instant, and a kill can fire `Spirit Tap`. If it hits and does not kill, you take 30% of your maximum health; a miss costs you no health. The level-60 lists fire it on cooldown, and Icy Veins notes that [[Vampiric Embrace]] heals the backlash back. Solo, with no healer, save it for the kill *(ours, not a source's)*.
## Healing dungeons as Shadow
**Before 40 you are a Priest with every healing spell, and Shadow talents cost you little.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both note that a levelling Shadow Priest often heals early dungeons.
**Past 40, one creator reports healing dungeons from inside [[Shadowform]].** Doublezug's November 10 video, recorded at level 48 in Zul'Farrak, is the only source, so treat it as a report and not a measurement. The mechanics he leans on are the client's:
- [[Vampiric Embrace]] heals **each** party member for 20% of your Shadow damage to the marked target, and the marked target's death fires `Spirit Tap` ([his explanation, at 3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KXZBawGxSs&t=183))
- [[Power Word: Shield]] can be cast in [[Shadowform]]. [[Renew]] and every other heal cannot
- Entering [[Shadowform]] costs 40% of your base Mana, so each time a heal drops you out, going back in is expensive
His pattern: shield the tank and enter Shadowform before the pull, put [[Shadow Word: Pain]] on everything, put [[Vampiric Embrace]] and [[Devouring Plague]] on the enemy that will live longest, then [[Mind Blast]] and [[Shadow Word: Death]] into it. When the tank drops, leave Shadowform for [[Greater Heal]] and [[Renew]] and stay out for the rest of the pull ([at 7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KXZBawGxSs&t=425)). He says it needs a sturdy tank, smaller pulls and a lot of water, and that it is weakest when the tank alone takes heavy damage.
## Stat priority
**While levelling, Icy Veins' order:** Spell Power, critical strike, Hit, Intellect, Spirit, Stamina, MP5.
Hit sits low while levelling: *"While fighting enemies at your level, you'll need very little hit chance."* An enemy two levels above you is a 6% miss.
**At 60, Icy Veins' order:** Hit to 17%, Spell Power, critical strike, Spell Pierce, Spirit.
**At 60, Wowhead's order:** Hit to 17%, critical strike, Spell Damage, Intellect, Spirit, Stamina. Wowhead labels its list speculative and wrote it before launch.
**They agree on Hit and differ on what comes second.** A caster misses a raid boss 17% of the time. `Shadow Focus` 5/5 covers 5, so gear has to find 12%. A missed [[Devouring Plague]] still costs its Mana and its 24-second cooldown.
**Critical strike is worth more in [[Shadowform]]**: Shadow crits deal 200% instead of 150%.
**Spell power per spell, from the client:** [[Shadow Word: Pain]] takes 20% of it per tick, [[Mind Flay]] 16.7% per tick, [[Mind Blast]] and [[Shadow Word: Death]] 42.9%, [[Devouring Plague]] 10% per tick.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only, as it was in Classic.** What Forever changed is the damage: 63 at rank 1, from 75. The gate is 10 points in Shadow rather than your spec, so a Holy or Discipline Priest can dip for it. No published Holy or Discipline build does.
- **`Shadow Focus`** moved from row 2 to row 1, and now increases Shadow spell hit chance instead of reducing the target's resist chance.
- **`Shadow Reach`** moved from row 4 to row 2, and now costs only 2 points for the same 20% range.
- **`Improved Mind Flay`** (new, row 3, requires [[Mind Flay]]): 2 ranks, each tick of Mind Flay deals 10/20% more than the tick before. It has no range or slow effect in the client, though Blizzard's September post described both.
- **`Shadow Weaving`** now stacks on the target but counts only your own Shadow damage: 33/67/100% chance by rank, 2% a stack, 5 stacks, 10% total, for 15 seconds. It cannot fail to apply. It feeds no other Shadow caster or Warlock.
- **`Early Demise`** (new, row 6, needs 25 Shadow points): 2 ranks, 15/30% more critical strike chance for [[Shadow Word: Death]] against targets at or below 20% health. No published level-60 raid build takes it.
- **[[Silence]] no longer requires `Improved Psychic Scream`**. It is one point in row 5: 5 seconds, 45-second cooldown. `Improved Psychic Scream` now requires `Blackout` 5/5.
- **Removed:** `Improved Vampiric Embrace`, folded into the base redesign above, plus `Shadow Affinity` and `Improved Fade`.
- **[[Devouring Plague]] is no longer Undead-only.** It is *"now available to all Priests at level 20, regardless of race"*, with a 24-second cooldown over a 24-second duration, against 3 minutes in Classic. It can critically strike, and it heals you for the damage it deals.
- **[[Shadow Word: Death]] is new**, for all races at level 32, with ranks at 40, 48 and 56. Instant cast, 18-second cooldown in the client. If it hits and does not kill the target you take 30% of your maximum health in damage, and a miss deals you nothing.
- **[[Shadowform]] now bars *healing* spells rather than Holy spells**, so [[Smite]], [[Holy Fire]], [[Resurrection]], `Cure Disease`, `Abolish Disease`, `Dispel Magic` and [[Power Word: Shield]] are usable inside it.
- **[[Fear Ward]] no longer drops [[Shadowform]].** Blizzard's November 9 hotfix. It is baseline for every Priest at 20, with a 3-minute duration and cooldown in the client.
- **Blizzard's November 10 hotfix** stopped [[Vampiric Embrace]] healing from triggering the `Revelation` enchant. **November 11:** Gnome `Confounding Flash` and the `Twilight Focus` talent now work in [[Shadowform]].
- **New race-locked Priest spells:** `Divine Grace` (Human), `Chastise` (Dwarf), `Dark Sacrifice` (Undead), and `Confounding Flash` plus `Contingency Plan` for Gnome. Gnome is a new Priest race in Forever; vanilla Priests were Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Troll and Undead.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, so every published levelling order opens with it. Wands no longer gain spell power.
## At level 60
**The published builds are settled; their value is not measured.** Icy Veins and WoWSims both sit on 15/3/33, and Wowhead calls its own build speculative. Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9, 2026, so no raid log exists.
**What Shadow brings to a group, from the client:** [[Vampiric Embrace]] healing for the party only, `Dark Inspiration`'s party-only Spirit, and the three raid-wide Priest buffs. `Shadow Weaving` no longer raises anyone else's Shadow damage.
**Mana at 60.** Icy Veins' rotation page says Mana stops being a problem: [[Shadowform]] halves costs and `Meditation` keeps half your Spirit regeneration running while you cast. Its exception is heavy multi-target [[Shadow Word: Pain]] casting, where it points to `Spirit Tap`.
**No debuff limit.** Icy Veins states that Forever has no cap on debuffs per target, so your damage-over-time spells are not competing for slots.
