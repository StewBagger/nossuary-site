*"A vampiric DPS specialization, healing allies based on how much shadow damage they deal."* **[[Mind Flay]] is here and nowhere else — the client makes it talent-only, 10 points into this tree — and the published advice for the other two specs is to use these talents until you can heal in groups.**
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Shadow build has been tested. Talent rows unlock at levels 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40. The one-point milestone talents are gated on **points spent**, not on level: 11, 21 and 31 points as in Classic, now joined by a new **16-point** milestone. **[[Shadowform]] is row 7, so level 40 and unreachable in the beta.** No source has posted a level 30 build either — the cap only reached 30 on October 1 and none of the three has published one since — so the level 30 line below extends the published level 20 build using the full talent tree's point costs, not a build any guide has recommended. Blizzard's own September 30, 2026 Class Deep Dive post is folded in below and takes priority over the three guide sites; its own caveat applies: *"class abilities and talents may continue to evolve throughout the beta test and are subject to additional changes."*

## What Shadow is for
**The damage a Priest does not otherwise get: [[Mind Flay]].** The client makes it talent-only, gated on 10 points in the Shadow tree, and the single point grants all **6 spell ranks** — which is why no published Holy or Discipline build has it, and why their rotations end in [[Smite]], [[Mind Blast]] and a wand.
The published ratings put Shadow at **3 out of 5 for levelling**, the highest of the three Priest specs, and **2.5 for dungeons**, the lowest of the three. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** It is slower than it sounds early on:
*"Slow Starters, Better Later — In lower levels, Shadow has a complicated leveling curve. The spec is incredibly reliant on `Spirit Tap` for its mana regeneration. Failing to get last hits on enemies will generally lead you to drinking more often."*
**It gets better twice, at known levels.** At **25** the 16 points are in for [[Vampiric Embrace]] — party healing off your own Shadow damage, on a cooldown you can hold for the fights that need it. At **40**, [[Shadowform]]'s 50% mana cut improves the mana curve *"drastically."*
**Learn one habit immediately: land the killing blow.** `Spirit Tap` is your mana economy and it fires only when *you* kill something worth experience. Grey mobs do not count.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: Icy Veins and Wowhead publish the same 11 points, in the same order, neither taking [[Mind Flay]]** — the two wand points are Discipline, which leaves 9 in Shadow at the very most, short of the 10-point gate either way.
1. `Wand Specialization`, 2 points
2. `Spirit Tap`, 5 points
3. `Improved Shadow Word: Pain`, 2 points
4. Final 2 points: *"there aren't a lot of good options: `Twilight Focus` if you find yourself playing mostly solo; `Blackout` if you're playing in a PvP environment; `Shadow Reach`… for some extra range."* `Shadow Reach` is now only 2 points for the same effect, down a row to row 2, so this option now buys the full talent instead of a partial rank.
**Blizzard moved `Shadow Focus` up to row 1 and changed what it does**: it now increases the chance for Shadow spells to hit, rather than reducing the target's chance to resist. The 5 points spent on it below buy Shadow hit chance, not resist reduction.
**No source publishes a build that spends into [[Mind Flay]] at 20**, or a level-30 Shadow build.
**Level 30, 2/0/19 — our own extension, not a published build**, in order: `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Shadow Focus` 5 → `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` 2 → `Shadow Affinity` 3 → **[[Mind Flay]]** → `Improved Mind Flay` 2 → `Improved Mind Blast` 2 → `Shadow Weaving` 3 → **[[Vampiric Embrace]]**. The two opening points are Discipline — `Wand Specialization` lives in that tree — which is why this is 2/0/19 and not 0/0/21, and it keeps the wand habit the rotation below is built on.
## Rotation
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]]** and **[[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Smite]]** to pull
4. **[[Mind Blast]]**
5. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
6. **Wand until it dies.** This is the single most important habit: *"The main reason you'll die while playing as a Priest is running out of mana while still fighting against a healthy foe"*
**[[Mind Flay]] slots in as your channelled filler if your build bought it.** The published rotation omits it only because that build does not take it.
**Multi-target:** *"you can deal damage to multiple enemies by applying [[Shadow Word: Pain]] to multiple targets… this can be extremely mana-intensive, especially if you miss last hits to proc `Spirit Tap`."* Pain everything, then wand them down.
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then critical strike, Hit, Intellect, Stamina, MP5.
Hit sits lower than you might expect while levelling: *"While fighting enemies at your level, you'll need very little hit chance… Hit will take center stage when talking about endgame content."* It sits lower still now that `Shadow Focus` — row 1, 5 points, the first pick in the build above — grants Shadow hit chance directly from the talent tree instead of its old job of lowering target resist.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only in Forever, where Classic taught it at level 20.** The client is explicit: *"Comes with the talent point, no trainer teaches it."* The damage changed too, 63 from 75 at rank 1. **The client puts the gate at 10 points in Shadow Magic, not at your spec**, so a Holy or Discipline Priest can dip for it. **No published Holy or Discipline build reaches it**, and their rotations contain no Mind Flay at all.
- **`Shadow Focus` moved from row 2 to row 1, and now increases Shadow spell hit chance instead of reducing the target's resist chance.** See Stat priority above.
- **`Shadow Reach` moved from row 4 to row 2, and now costs only 2 points for the same range bonus.**
- **`Improved Mind Flay`** (new, row 3, requires [[Mind Flay]]): increases Mind Flay's damage and range, but reduces how much it slows the target's movement.
- **`Shadow Affinity`'s threat reduction on Shadow spells increased slightly.**
- **`Shadow Weaving` is now a personal stacking buff, not the raid-wide Shadow Vulnerability debuff.** Blizzard adds that its **damage percentage is reduced moderately** — *"Percentage increase to damage decreased moderately"*, with no figure attached to it. It no longer feeds other Shadow casters or Warlocks. Do not take a raid assignment on it. It always lands, on top of being personal-only and weaker per stack.
- **`Devouring Contagion`** (new, row 5): cuts [[Devouring Plague]]'s Mana cost, and when a target dies while afflicted, Devouring Plague jumps to a new enemy within 5 yards carrying its remaining duration.
- **`Early Demise`** (new, row 6, needs 25 Shadow points): raises [[Shadow Word: Death]]'s critical strike chance against targets under 20% health, and only under that threshold. Moot for now either way — see below.
- **[[Silence]] no longer requires `Improved Psychic Scream`** as a prerequisite. **`Improved Psychic Scream` now requires `Blackout`** instead.
- **Removed:** `Improved Vampiric Embrace` — folded into the base `Vampiric Embrace` redesign above.
- **[[Devouring Plague]] is no longer Undead-only**: it is *"now available to all Priests at level 20, regardless of race"* — every race, Gnome included. Blizzard publishes no change to its cooldown. **The October 1 build also fixed it to correctly critically strike** — and since it's baseline for every spec at 20, this is a flat damage increase across the whole class, not a Shadow-only buff.
- **[[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every Priest**, learned at 20 *"regardless of race"* — which is all Blizzard's post says about it. The 3-minute duration and 3-minute cooldown quoted for it are the client reading, not a published nerf.
- **[[Shadow Word: Death]] is new**, for all races at **level 32**, so out of reach at any beta cap. Instant cast, 15-second cooldown; if it does not kill the target you take 10% of your max health in damage. Its talent `Early Demise` above needs 25 Shadow points.
- **[[Shadowform]] now bars *healing* spells rather than Holy spells**, so [[Smite]], [[Holy Fire]] and [[Resurrection]] are usable inside it. See What defines Shadow above for the Mana-cost, crit-damage and flat-damage numbers.
- **New race-locked Priest spells:** `Divine Grace` (Human), `Chastise` (Dwarf), `Dark Sacrifice` (Undead), and `Confounding Flash` plus `Contingency Plan` for **Gnome**, which is a new Priest race in Forever and needed racials of its own — vanilla Priests were Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Troll and Undead.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%** where Classic needed 5, which is why every wand-levelling build front-loads it. Separately, **wands no longer gain spell power** as of September 24.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether Shadow has a raid slot at all. Losing the Shadow Vulnerability debuff role removes what it was *for* in Classic, and what is left is single-target damage plus a [[Vampiric Embrace]] that heals only the party.
