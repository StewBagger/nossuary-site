## What Assassination is for
**Poison and bleed pressure on one target, opened from [[Stealth]].** [[Ambush]] at level 18 is where the build starts to work. At 21 Assassination points [[Mutilate]] becomes the builder: 60 Energy, both weapons at once, 2 combo points, and extra damage on a poisoned target. Blizzard attaches no figures to it: the 75% weapon damage per hand and the +20% in the table below are client readings. `Cold Blood` is now the 16-point gold medal talent, so level 25. It guarantees a crit on your next [[Sinister Strike]], [[Backstab]], [[Ambush]], [[Eviscerate]] or [[Mutilate]].
**The sources disagree about this spec.**
**For:** Icy Veins rates its levelling 4 out of 5, tied with Combat's 4 out of 5, and both specs sit at 3.0 Overall. It says *"Assassination is likely to be very strong especially in this level bracket due to the potency of talents like `Remorseless Attacks`."*
**Against:** Wowhead, updated 2026/09/29, says *"Assassination is wholly underwhelming at this level bracket. We don't have any of our essential kit, such as [[Mutilate]], `Venom`, and `Seal Fate`. It will ultimately play the same as the Combat Rogue for now until we can gain access to some more impactful abilities."*
**Both agree on why 21 points matters:** that is what [[Mutilate]] costs in the tree (ClassicWoW.gg), and that is exactly level 30, the beta cap. Below that, Assassination leans on `Malice`, `Remorseless Attacks` and `Lethality`: good passive numbers with no signature button. Our own reading, not a source's: Combat is the lower-risk levelling pick, and Assassination pays off once [[Mutilate]] is reachable.
## Your talent points
**Icy Veins' level 20 build is 11/0/0**, with the click order published:
1. `Remorseless Attacks`, 2 points
2. `Malice`, 3 points
3. `Ruthlessness`, 3 points
4. `Malice` to 5/5
5. `Relentless Strikes` at 20
**`Improved Slice and Dice` is explicitly rejected:** *"mobs and even bosses in this level bracket are unlikely to survive long enough."*
**Wowhead publishes two different level 20 builds**, and neither is this one. Its Backstab Build puts 3 points into `Puncturing Wounds`, for +30% Backstab crit and a 45% chance of a bonus combo point. It adds `Improved Sinister Strike` "solely for when you inevitably pull aggro." Its Relentless Strikes Build spends those points on `Relentless Strikes` and `Ruthlessness` for Energy economy instead, on either a dagger or a slow one-hander.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs: `Malice` 5 → `Remorseless Attacks` 2 → `Ruthlessness` 3 → `Relentless Strikes` 1 → `Lethality` 5 → `Vile Poisons` 4 → [[Mutilate]] at 30. [[Mutilate]] is the 21st point and nothing beyond it. `Venom` needs 31 points, so level 40. One Blizzard change opens the rows below: `Seal Fate` no longer requires `Cold Blood` first.
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play the same.** Icy Veins says so on its own Assassination guide. Wowhead's three level-20 guides confirm it by being largely interchangeable: *"During the early stages of Rogue leveling, it is difficult to really differentiate the specializations."*
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons
2. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]
3. [[Backstab]] behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]
4. At 5 combo points, [[Slice and Dice]] if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points
**Once [[Mutilate]] is reachable**, it becomes your builder in place of Backstab/Sinister Strike, with [[Slice and Dice]] upkeep unchanged.
**Multi-target: no source publishes an Assassination area rotation.** `Remorseless Attacks` *"heavily incentivizes pulling multiple mobs"*, but that rewards chain-killing rather than cleaving.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
Published gear advice adds: **slow main hand, fast off hand**, and *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are secondary skills now**, *"like First Aid"*, not class skills. Poison damage is lower with more charges: [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"*. Blizzard has since raised the charges on every poison again. A poison and a sharpening stone now go on the same weapon. `Improved Poisons` gives a large chance for a poison not to spend a charge when it fires, because Blizzard wants *"Assassination to be a Poison-focused playstyle."*
- **[[Mutilate]] and `Venom` are both new**, and `Malice`, `Lethality`, `Remorseless Attacks` and `Cold Blood` were each extended to cover [[Mutilate]]. `Lethality`'s critical-damage bonus came down in the same pass.
- `Malice` now raises crit with all attacks and your Poisons, not melee alone. `Murder` gained Giants and lost Beasts and Dragonkin, a loss on levelling targets. `Vigor` is a 2-point row 5 talent instead of the 31-point capstone, for the same total effect.
- `Improved Gouge` moved in from Combat to row 1; `Improved Eviscerate` moved out to Combat. `Puncturing Wounds` is Combat's renamed `Improved Backstab`, and it now raises [[Mutilate]]'s crit chance too, so the two trees talk to each other.
- **Rogues can learn axes**, and the skill starts at 1.
- **Baseline nerfs:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. [[Expose Armor]] was buffed instead, to match five [[Sunder Armor]]s at the same level. It no longer stops a Warrior using Sunder, which cannot overwrite your Expose Armor unless yours is the weaker effect. The `Improved Expose Armor` talent no longer adds armor reduction at all: it cuts the Energy cost and refunds combo points when you spend five. Fixed October 1: a missed Expose Armor no longer costs you all your combo points, so it is safe to throw at low hit chance.
- Hit and critical strike are now unified across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether poison scaling and `Venom` upkeep make Assassination competitive with Combat in raids. There is no raid PTR, **item stats are hidden until the items drop**, and both 31-point capstones are untested.
