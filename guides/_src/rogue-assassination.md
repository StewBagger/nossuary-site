## What Assassination is for
**Poison damage on one target, with a builder that does not need you behind it.** [[Mutilate]] at level 40 costs 60 Energy, strikes with both daggers for 90% weapon damage plus 40 each, awards 2 combo points, and deals 20% more to a poisoned target. Before 40 the tree gives you crit and poison passives: `Malice`, `Lethality`, `Vile Poisons`, and from 21 points `Venom`, which turns each [[Shank]] into 6 seconds of +30% poison damage and +20% chance to apply poisons. `Cold Blood` at 16 points guarantees a crit on your next [[Sinister Strike]], [[Backstab]], [[Ambush]], [[Eviscerate]] or [[Mutilate]].
**The sources agree on the shape and differ on the levelling.** Icy Veins, updated November 9, calls its single-target damage *"some of the strongest in the game"*, says `Venom` *"is a bit weak in its current state"*, and warns that some enemies are immune to poison. Wowhead's levelling page, updated November 6, calls Assassination *"by far the least viable leveling option"*; that page still places [[Mutilate]] at 30, so read the verdict with the wait to 40 in mind. Our own reading, not a source's: levels 10 to 39 are the cost of this spec, and Wowhead's levelling build below does not take [[Mutilate]] at all.

## Rotation
**Levels 10 to 39.** Wowhead: *"You'll start the first 30 levels of your journey very similarly to Combat."* Icy Veins' levelling list:
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons, or [[Crippling Poison]] against enemies that run
2. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Cheap Shot]] if the target can be stunned, or [[Garrote]] for damage. [[Ambush]] needs a dagger in the main hand
3. [[Backstab]] behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]
4. [[Slice and Dice]] if there will be something to hit for its duration
5. Otherwise [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points
**From 24, press [[Shank]] every 10 seconds**: 5 Energy for 15 back, and from 30 each one starts `Venom`'s 6 seconds. Icy Veins: *"Try to avoid using it when your Energy is near full."* WoWSims' list presses it at 80 Energy or less *(WoWSims' model)*.
**From 40, [[Mutilate]] replaces [[Backstab]] and [[Sinister Strike]]** as the builder. It needs a dagger in each hand.
**At 60, Icy Veins' priority, updated November 4:**
1. [[Instant Poison]] on the main hand and [[Deadly Poison]] on the off hand, to hold 5 stacks of [[Deadly Poison]]
2. [[Expose Armor]] only if the group has no stronger armour reduction
3. [[Slice and Dice]] up at all times
4. [[Rupture]] if the target will survive its duration
5. Open with [[Garrote]]; [[Shank]] on cooldown for `Venom`
6. [[Mutilate]] to build; [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points when the buffs above are running
**Multi-target: none.** Icy Veins: Assassination *"has no AoE or means of hitting multiple targets at the same time."*
**What changes the answer:**
- **[[Rupture]] or not: the sources disagree.** Icy Veins keeps it up. WoWSims' list skips it for any build holding `Flawless Execution`, which makes [[Eviscerate]] 15 Energy cheaper, and both level 60 builds above hold it *(WoWSims' model)*. Neither rests on a log.
- **[[Mutilate]] wins on cost per point.** 60 Energy for 2 points is 30 per point. A cast at 4 points wastes one, so WoWSims' list finishes at 4 once [[Mutilate]] is known *(WoWSims' model)*; Icy Veins finishes at 5.
- **[[Mutilate]]'s bonus needs a poison on the target.** [[Instant Poison]] deals its damage and leaves nothing behind. [[Deadly Poison]], from level 30, stays on the target, which is why every source puts it on one weapon.
- **Does [[Shank]] itself apply poisons? Not settled.** WoWSims reads the client row as suppressing weapon procs and poisons on the strike, and marks that for checking in game. Gaunerh, playing at launch, [says it can apply one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rz4_nxWRFRg&t=64). `Venom` works either way, since it buffs the poisons your other hits apply.
- **Finish at 5 with `Relentless Strikes`.** Each rank is a 4% chance per combo point of 25 Energy back, so at 5/5 a 5-point finisher always refunds and a 4-point one does 80% of the time.
- **[[Slice and Dice]] speeds up your swings and nothing else.** +20% melee speed at rank 1, +30% at rank 2, trained at 42. Five points last 21 seconds, one point lasts 9. WoWSims' list recasts it on any number of points the moment it falls off *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Cold Blood` goes on a 5-point [[Eviscerate]].** Icy Veins and WoWSims' list agree.
- **[[Backstab]] or [[Sinister Strike]] before 40.** With the same dagger, Backstab deals more per Energy, because its 150% also multiplies its flat bonus *(our arithmetic)*. It costs 60 Energy against 45 and needs you behind the target.
## Stat priority
**While levelling**, from Icy Veins: weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina. [[Mutilate]] needs two daggers, so from the high 30s keep a second dagger with high top-end damage.
**At 60 the two sites order the top differently, and both say it is early.**
- Icy Veins: Expertise to 6.5%, Hit to 8%, Agility, weapon damage, critical strike.
- Wowhead, which calls its own list *"purely speculative for endgame"*: Hit to 8%, Expertise to 6.5%, weapon skill, then Hit on to 17% for poisons, Agility, critical strike.
- WoWSims' stored weights put Hit and Expertise level with each other, then Agility and critical strike. They were not retuned after the November 3 talent rework *(WoWSims' model)*.
Wowhead's reason for chasing Hit past 8% is specific to this spec: poisons use the spell hit cap. `Precision` gives 3% of it.

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons are a secondary skill** and a poison and a sharpening stone now go on the same weapon. [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 deals 13 to 17 with 60 charges, and every poison carries more charges than in Classic. Attack Power counts for more: [[Instant Poison]] adds 1% of it and [[Deadly Poison]] 0.9%, per Blizzard's November 2 notes. Blizzard on `Improved Poisons`: *"We want Assassination to be a Poison-focused playstyle."*
- **[[Mutilate]], `Venom`, `Blood Splatter` and the trained [[Shank]] are all new.** `Blood Splatter`, 1 point in row 3, adds 6 seconds to [[Rupture]] and 9 to [[Garrote]].
- `Malice` raises crit with all attacks and your Poisons, not melee alone. `Lethality` is 2 ranks for +20% critical damage. `Vigor` is a 2-point row 3 talent for +10 maximum Energy. `Relentless Strikes` is a 5-rank row 5 talent.
- `Puncturing Wounds`, in Combat's row 2, is the old Backstab crit talent with [[Mutilate]]'s crit chance added, so the two trees feed each other.
- `Improved Expose Armor` no longer adds armour reduction: it cuts the Energy cost by 10 and refunds 2 combo points when you spend five. [[Expose Armor]] itself now matches five [[Sunder Armor]]s and no longer stops a Warrior using Sunder.
- **`Poised`, from 18:** out of combat at full Energy, your next Energy ability costs 10 less.
## At level 60
**In a group you are single-target damage and control.** Icy Veins, updated November 9: Assassination is *"likely to struggle in encounters that consistently spawn small mobs"*, and giving up [[Blade Flurry]] *"can make it a bit more challenging to fit into groups."* Both sites list poison-immune enemies as the spec's specific weakness.
**What is not known yet:** how the Icy Veins and Wowhead builds compare, whether [[Rupture]] earns its place, and how far Hit should go past 8%. Those wait for logs, and the first raids open on December 9.
