## Is Assassination worth levelling?
**Sources genuinely disagree, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.**
**For:** one publishes a pure level-20 Assassination build, rates levelling **4 out of 5**, and says *"Assassination is likely to be very strong especially in this level bracket due to the potency of talents like `Remorseless Attacks`."*
**Against:** another says the level-20 options are the Combat `Riposte` build or the Subtlety hybrid, and that only *"as the level cap progresses to 30"* do you get *"even having a viable Assassination build."*
**Both agree on why 30 matters: [[Mutilate]] needs 21 points in the tree.** Below that, Assassination is `Malice` and `Lethality` — good passive numbers with no signature button. If you want a decision: **Combat is lower-risk, and this spec pays off at 30.**
## Your talent points
**Level 20 — 11/0/0**, with the click order published:
1. `Remorseless Attacks`, 2 points
2. `Malice`, 3 points
3. `Ruthlessness`, 3 points
4. `Malice` to 5/5
5. **`Relentless Strikes`** at 20
**`Improved Slice and Dice` is explicitly rejected:** *"mobs and even bosses in this level bracket are unlikely to survive long enough."*
**A dungeon alternative** abandons the tree entirely — `Malice` 3 / `Improved Eviscerate` 3 / `Improved Sinister Strike` 2 / `Precision` 3, labelled *"Combat for damage in dungeons."* That is a fair reflection of how weak pure Assassination is at 20.
**Level 30 — 21/0/0**, in order: `Malice` 5 → `Remorseless Attacks` 2 → `Ruthlessness` 3 → `Relentless Strikes` 1 → `Lethality` 5 → `Vile Poisons` 4 → **[[Mutilate]]** at 30. *"The level 30 cap unlocks Mutilate… extremely strong in both dungeons and solo play."*
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play identically.**
1. **[[Instant Poison]] on both weapons**
2. **Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]**
3. **[[Backstab]] behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]**
4. At 5 combo points, **[[Slice and Dice]]** if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise **[[Eviscerate]]** at 5 combo points
**From 30**, [[Mutilate]] becomes your builder, with [[Slice and Dice]] upkeep — and one piece of practical advice: *"sometimes it's better to use [[Sinister Strike]] to add the last Combo Point and conserve energy."*
**Multi-target: no source publishes an Assassination area rotation.** The only relevant note is that `Remorseless Attacks` *"heavily incentivizes pulling multiple mobs"* — it rewards chain-killing, which is not the same as cleaving.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
**Slow main hand, fast off hand.** *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*
## What defines Assassination
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Mutilate]]** | **exactly 30** | New in Forever. 60 energy, strikes with **both weapons**, 2 combo points, **+20% against a poisoned target** |
| **`Remorseless Attacks`** | 10 | The chain-pull crit engine the level-20 build is designed around |
| **`Venom`** | **40** | The 31-point capstone — a finisher that buffs poison damage 30% and application chance 10%. Not in the beta |
## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are secondary skills now**, *"like First Aid"*, not class skills. Poison damage is lower with more charges — [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"*. **Poison and a sharpening stone now stack.**
- **[[Mutilate]] and `Venom` are both new.**
- `Improved Gouge` moved **in** from Combat; `Improved Eviscerate` moved **out** to Combat. `Puncturing Wounds` is a new **Combat** talent that buffs [[Mutilate]] — which is why the two trees now talk to each other.
- **Rogues can learn axes**, and the skill starts at 1.
- **Baseline nerfs:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. **[[Expose Armor]] was buffed** to *"450 instead of 340"* at rank 5.
- **Hit and critical strike are now unified across spell, melee and ranged.**
