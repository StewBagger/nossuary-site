## What Assassination is for
**Poison pressure on one target, opened from [[Stealth]].** [[Ambush]] at level 18 is where the build starts to work *(ours, not a source's)*. At 21 points `Venom` arrives: a passive that turns each `Shank`, trained at 24, into 6 seconds of +30% poison damage and +20% chance to apply poisons. At 31 points, level 40, [[Mutilate]] becomes the builder: 60 Energy, both weapons at once for 90% weapon damage each plus a flat bonus, 2 combo points, and extra damage on a poisoned target. `Cold Blood` is the 16-point gold medal talent, so level 25. It guarantees a crit on your next [[Sinister Strike]], [[Backstab]], [[Ambush]], [[Eviscerate]] or [[Mutilate]].
**The sources disagree about this spec.**
**For:** Icy Veins rates its levelling 4 out of 5, tied with Combat's 4 out of 5, and both specs sit at 3.0 Overall. It says *"Assassination is likely to be very strong especially in this level bracket due to the potency of talents like `Remorseless Attacks`."*
**Against:** Wowhead, updated 2026/09/29, says *"Assassination is wholly underwhelming at this level bracket. We don't have any of our essential kit, such as [[Mutilate]], `Venom`, and `Seal Fate`. It will ultimately play the same as the Combat Rogue for now until we can gain access to some more impactful abilities."*
**Both reviews were written for the beta's tree**, where [[Mutilate]] sat at 21 points. Blizzard's November 2 notes moved it to row 7, 31 points, so level 40, and `Seal Fate` to 3 ranks at level 35. Below that, Assassination leans on `Malice`, `Remorseless Attacks` and `Lethality`, with `Venom` from 30: good passive numbers and one cheap button, `Shank`. Our own reading, not a source's: Combat is the lower-risk levelling pick, and Assassination pays off once [[Mutilate]] is reachable.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** Icy Veins published an 11/0/0 click order for the beta's tree, and its first ten points still fit; its eleventh, `Relentless Strikes`, now sits in row 5, 20 points deep. Our line keeps the ten and spends the eleventh in row 3:
1. `Remorseless Attacks`, 2 points
2. `Malice`, 3 points
3. `Ruthlessness`, 3 points
4. `Malice` to 5/5
5. `Lethality`, 1 point at 20, for +10% critical damage on [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Backstab]]. It needs `Malice` at 5/5
**Icy Veins explicitly rejects `Improved Slice and Dice`:** *"mobs and even bosses in this level bracket are unlikely to survive long enough."*
**Wowhead published two level 20 builds for the beta's tree.** Its Backstab Build puts 3 points into Combat's `Puncturing Wounds`, for +30% Backstab crit and a 45% chance of a bonus combo point, and still fits. It adds `Improved Sinister Strike`, now `Efficient Strikes`, "solely for when you inevitably pull aggro." Its Relentless Strikes Build no longer fits: `Relentless Strikes` needs 20 points in the tree.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** Extending the level 20 line on the tree's own point costs: `Malice` 5 → `Remorseless Attacks` 2 → `Ruthlessness` 3 → `Lethality` 2 → `Vigor` 2 → `Blood Splatter` 1 → `Cold Blood` 1 at 25 → `Vile Poisons` 4 → `Venom` at 30. `Venom` is the 21st point. `Relentless Strikes` shares its row and takes the next five points; [[Mutilate]] needs 31.
## Rotation
**At level 20 all three Rogue specs play the same.** Icy Veins says so on its own Assassination guide. Wowhead's three level-20 guides confirm it by being largely interchangeable: *"During the early stages of Rogue leveling, it is difficult to really differentiate the specializations."*
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons
2. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]], or [[Garrote]] without a dagger in the main hand
3. [[Backstab]] behind the target with a dagger in the main hand, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]
4. At 5 combo points, [[Slice and Dice]] if the target will survive its duration
5. Otherwise [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points
**From 24, press `Shank` on cooldown**: 5 Energy for 15 back, and from 30 each one starts `Venom`'s 6 seconds, out of a 10-second cooldown *(our arithmetic)*.
**Once [[Mutilate]] is reachable at 40**, it becomes your builder in place of Backstab/Sinister Strike, with [[Slice and Dice]] upkeep unchanged and [[Deadly Poison]] on one weapon.
**Multi-target: no source publishes an Assassination area rotation.** `Remorseless Attacks` *"heavily incentivizes pulling multiple mobs"*, but that rewards chain-killing rather than cleaving.
**What changes the answer:**
- **[[Backstab]] or [[Sinister Strike]].** With the same dagger in hand, Backstab deals more per Energy at every level, even against the 40-Energy [[Sinister Strike]] that `Efficient Strikes` gives you, because its 150% also multiplies its flat bonus. It costs more per combo point, 60 Energy against 45, and it needs you behind the target.
- **Finish at 5 with `Relentless Strikes`, from 30.** Each rank is a 4% chance per combo point of 25 Energy back, so at 5/5 a 5-point finisher always refunds and a 4-point one does 80% of the time. A 5-point [[Slice and Dice]] can refund its whole cost.
- **[[Slice and Dice]] speeds up your swings and nothing else.** It adds no Energy and no builder damage, so its 25 Energy has to come back through auto attacks: +20% melee speed at rank 1, +30% at rank 2, trained at 42. Five points last 21 seconds, one point lasts 9. Wowhead's advice is to refresh it as close to expiry as possible. At 60, WoWSims' list does not wait for 5: it recasts on any number of points the moment the buff falls off *(WoWSims' model)*. Icy Veins' levelling rotation spends at 5, and that is the list above.
- **[[Mutilate]] wins on cost per point.** 60 Energy for 2 points is 30 per point, and it does not need you behind the target. A cast at 4 points wastes one, so WoWSims' list finishes at 4 once [[Mutilate]] is known *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Mutilate]]'s bonus needs a poison on the target.** [[Instant Poison]] deals its damage and leaves nothing behind, so on both weapons it earns no +20%. [[Deadly Poison]], from level 30, stays on the target. WoWSims' preset is Instant on the main hand and Deadly on the off hand, and it counts only Deadly and Wound as poisoned *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Venom` costs no finisher.** It rides on `Shank`, so your combo points go to [[Slice and Dice]] and [[Eviscerate]]. At 60, WoWSims' list keeps [[Slice and Dice]] first, then spends on [[Eviscerate]], never [[Rupture]], and saves `Cold Blood` for a 5-point [[Eviscerate]] *(WoWSims' model)*.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, critical strike, Strength, Stamina.
Published gear advice adds: **slow main hand, fast off hand**, and *"Intellect and Spirit are wasted on a rogue."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are secondary skills now**, *"like First Aid"*, not class skills. Poison damage is lower with more charges: [[Instant Poison]] rank 1 is *"13 to 17 instead of 19 to 25, 60 instead of 40"*. Blizzard's dive says charges rose on all poisons; 60 against 40 is that increase. Attack Power counts for more: [[Instant Poison]] adds 1% of it and [[Deadly Poison]] 0.9%, per Blizzard's November 2 notes. A poison and a sharpening stone now go on the same weapon. `Improved Poisons` gives a large chance for a poison not to spend a charge when it fires, because Blizzard wants *"Assassination to be a Poison-focused playstyle."*
- **[[Mutilate]], `Venom`, `Blood Splatter` and the trained `Shank` are all new**, and `Malice`, `Lethality`, `Remorseless Attacks` and `Cold Blood` each cover [[Mutilate]]. `Lethality` is 2 ranks for +20% critical damage, short of Classic's 30%.
- `Malice` raises crit with all attacks and your Poisons, not melee alone. `Murder` is gone. `Vigor` is a 2-point row 3 talent instead of the 31-point capstone, for the same total effect. `Relentless Strikes` is a 5-rank row 5 talent. `Blood Splatter`, 1 point in row 3, adds 6 seconds to [[Rupture]] and 9 to [[Garrote]].
- `Improved Gouge` moved in from Combat to row 1; `Improved Eviscerate` moved out to Combat as `Coup de Grace`. `Puncturing Wounds` is Combat's renamed `Improved Backstab`, and it now raises [[Mutilate]]'s crit chance too, so the two trees talk to each other.
- **Rogues can learn axes**, and the skill starts at 1.
- **`Poised`, from 18:** out of combat at full Energy, your next Energy ability costs 10 less.
- **Baseline nerfs:** [[Rupture]] rank 6 is *"159 instead of 272"*; [[Eviscerate]] rank 6 was lowered. [[Expose Armor]] was buffed instead, to match five [[Sunder Armor]]s at the same level. It no longer stops a Warrior using Sunder, which cannot overwrite your Expose Armor unless yours is the weaker effect. The `Improved Expose Armor` talent no longer adds armor reduction at all: it cuts the Energy cost and refunds combo points when you spend five. Fixed October 1: a missed Expose Armor no longer costs you all your combo points, so it is safe to throw at low hit chance.
- Hit and critical strike are now unified across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether poison scaling, `Venom` and [[Mutilate]] make Assassination competitive with Combat in raids. There is no raid PTR, **item stats are hidden until the items drop**, and no player has used [[Mutilate]] or `Venom` in the tree's current shape.
