## What Arcane is for
`Arcane Meditation` 3/3 keeps **50% of your regeneration** running through every cast, and [[Mage Armor]] at 34 adds its own 50%. Icy Veins and Wowhead both count the two as full regeneration while casting. That is what pays for [[Arcane Blast]], whose mana cost climbs +100% per cast.
**Icy Veins rates it 4/5 for levelling**, a score that measures solo kill speed; Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish no levelling score. Its heading is *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*: *"The `Arcane Meditation` talent significantly reduces downtime between fights as it regenerates a lot of mana even during combat."* Wowhead's levelling guide calls it *"great for low-effort questing"* and lists its costs: fewer tools than Frost in world PvP, and more damage taken, because enemies reach you more often.
In Classic, Arcane Power raid builds spent their damage casts on Frostbolt instead of the Arcane tree's own spells; ClassicWoW.gg puts it plainly: *"Forever gives you Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage to build around Arcane-school damage itself."*
**In dungeons** Icy Veins scores it 5/5 and names two drawbacks. Your burst can pull threat: *"if the tank is not used to your burst or unable to handle large AOE pulls, you are likely to pull aggro."* And you have no slow of your own until [[Cone of Cold]] at 26.
## Your talent points
Two sources publish a levelling build from 10 to 60. Icy Veins prints its order point by point. Wowhead shows its build as an interactive calculator and describes it in words. They take broadly the same talents and differ on the opening.
**Level 60, Icy Veins' levelling order, 31/17/3 (page dated November 4):** `Wand Specialization` 2 (10–11), `Improved Channeling` 3 (12–14), `Missile Barrage` 2 (15–16), `Arcane Concentration` 3 (17–19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20), `Precision` 2 (21–22), `Improved Channeling` to 5/5 (23–24), `Arcane Concentration` to 5/5 (25–26), [[Arcane Wrath]] 1 (27), `Arcane Meditation` 3 (28–30), `Arcane Subtlety` 1 (31), [[Presence of Mind]] 1 (32), `Arcane Mind` 5 (33–37), `Arcane Instability` 3 (38–40), `Arcane Subtlety` to 2/2 (41), `Arcane Power` 1 (42), `Incineration` 5 (43–47), `Accelerant` 5 (48–52), `Improved Fire Blast` 2 (53–54), `Ignite` 2 (55–56), [[Pyroblast]] 1 (57), `Heating Up` 1 (58), `Ignite` to 3/5 (59), `Precision` to 3/3 (60).
**Wowhead's build (page dated November 10)** is a calculator on [its levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/arcane/dps-leveling-tips), described in words. It opens with `Wand Specialization` 2 and one point of `Precision`, then `Improved Channeling`, `Missile Barrage` and `Arcane Subtlety`. It states [[Arcane Blast]] at 21 and [[Arcane Wrath]] at 26, and has the last points of `Improved Channeling` ready by 24, when rank 3 of [[Arcane Missiles]] gives the channel its fifth missile. It then takes `Arcane Concentration` and `Arcane Meditation`, a second point of `Precision` *"at either level 32 or at level 27"*, the Arcane damage and crit talents, `Accelerant` and `Incineration`, and `Arcane Power` once it has all of those. It gives no level for `Arcane Power` and no final split.
- **Two points of `Precision` are enough while levelling.** Wowhead and Icy Veins both put the miss chance against a same-level target at 4%, which two points remove. `Precision` is a Frost talent; Arcane has no hit talent of its own.
- **`Arcane Subtlety` is a damage talent now**, on both sites' reading. At 2/2 it cuts **15** from the target's resistance to your spells, with a 30% threat cut on Arcane spells. It is row 2, so it needs 5 points in the tree first.
- **Three prerequisites set the order**: [[Arcane Wrath]] needs [[Arcane Blast]], `Arcane Meditation` needs `Arcane Concentration` 5/5, and `Arcane Power` needs [[Presence of Mind]].
- **The Fire points are not filler.** `Incineration` raises [[Arcane Blast]]'s crit chance and `Accelerant` takes 0.5 seconds off [[Arcane Wrath]]'s cast.
## Rotation
`Missile Barrage` is a row 2 talent: first point at level 15, both at 16. Use the steps below as soon as you have it.
1. **Pull with [[Frostbolt]]** so the enemy arrives slowed, and it can proc `Missile Barrage`
2. `Missile Barrage` up → **[[Arcane Missiles]] immediately**. It is free and channels in half the time
3. From the level you take it, **[[Arcane Wrath]] on its 6-second cooldown**. Both guide sites cast it on cooldown while levelling
4. Otherwise **[[Arcane Blast]]** to fish for `Missile Barrage`. Wowhead's levelling guide stops at 2 stacks on ordinary enemies and goes higher only on elites and bosses; Icy Veins casts it 1 to 3 times and lets the buff drop. Four is the cap
5. **A proc mid-sequence means spend it** before the next [[Arcane Blast]]
6. Moving: [[Fire Blast]], a [[Presence of Mind]]-instant cast, or wand
7. **`Arcane Concentration` procs go on your most expensive cast**: a stacked [[Arcane Blast]] or [[Arcane Wrath]]
**Each [[Arcane Blast]] adds spell damage for everything you cast, itself included,** for 18 seconds from the first stack; no other spell spends it. Its cost is what limits stacking.
- **[[Fire Blast]] beats [[Arcane Blast]] whenever it is ready.** Each holds you for 1.5 seconds, and at 25 Fire Blast hits for about 104 against Arcane Blast's 35, before spell damage. WoWSims ranks it above Arcane Blast in both Arcane lists *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Arcane Blast]] earns its place through `Missile Barrage`, not its hit.** At 2/2 each cast has a 40% chance of a free channel, where a 2.6-second [[Frostbolt]] at 25 has 20%. A free channel at 25 is 243 damage, so that 40% averages 97, nearly three times the hit itself.
- **Four stacks is a hard stop.** The first four casts cost 1, 2, 3 and 4 times the base price, 15% of your base mana, so reaching the cap spends 150% of it. A fifth costs 5 times and adds nothing.
- **[[Arcane Missiles]] is the better use of a stacked buff when you have no proc.** Stacked spell damage reaches both spells equally per second, and Missiles' own damage per second is higher at almost every level: 49 against 23 at 25, and 175 for a rank 7 channel against 157 at 60. Only at 30 and 31 does Arcane Blast lead, on its new rank. At 60 WoWSims never casts Missiles without a proc and fills with Arcane Blast *(WoWSims' model)*, because a channel cannot trigger `Missile Barrage`.
- **With a free channel and an `Arcane Concentration` proc both up, neither is wasted.** An [[Arcane Missiles]] channel under `Missile Barrage` does not spend Clearcasting, so take the free channel and keep the proc for step 7. Both last 15 seconds. `Missile Barrage` is spent as the channel starts, so cutting the channel short does not save it.
- **Standing still, [[Presence of Mind]] goes on [[Arcane Wrath]]**: that saves 3.5 seconds where Arcane Blast saves 1.5. WoWSims' lists spend it the same way *(WoWSims' model)*.
**Multi-target.** Tanked: both guide sites say to stand by the tank and spam [[Arcane Explosion]], with a [[Flamestrike]] cast first if threat allows. Wait for the tank to have the pack. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Cone of Cold]] to slow, then [[Arcane Explosion]]. Icy Veins' warning: against three or fewer enemies, area spells are usually not worth the mana.
**Spells to keep trained past 30:** [[Arcane Blast]] at 30, 40, 50 and 60; [[Arcane Missiles]] at 32, 40, 48 and 56; [[Arcane Wrath]] at 36, 48 and 60; [[Mage Armor]] at 34; [[Arcane Explosion]] at 30, 38, 46 and 54; and the mana gems at 38, 48 and 58.
## Stat priority
Both sites that rank Arcane's stats do so for level 60, and both call their lists provisional.
1. **Hit**, to 17% against a raid boss. Icy Veins gives 4% as the figure against a same-level target; `Precision` supplies up to 6%
2. **Spell Power**, including Arcane damage
3. **Critical strike**: `Arcane Mind` 5/5 doubles the crit bonus of Arcane spells
4. **Intellect**: mana, a little crit, and 12% of it as spell damage per [[Arcane Blast]] stack. Wowhead places Spell Piercing and Haste above it, and says Spell Piercing's worth at 60 is not yet confirmed
5. **Spirit**: it keeps working while you cast, through `Arcane Meditation` and [[Mage Armor]]
6. **MP5** (Icy Veins) or **Stamina** (Wowhead) last
While levelling, Icy Veins' Fire guide makes the case for Spirit on any Mage with `Arcane Meditation`: time not spent drinking is worth more than a little spell damage. That is its judgement, not a measured figure.

## What Forever changed for Arcane
- **`Arcane Mind` was rewritten**: now +10% Intellect at 5/5 and +100% Arcane critical damage, where Classic gave a percentage of maximum mana.
- `Arcane Impact` (renamed from Improved Arcane Explosion) now buffs **all** Arcane spells' crit, 6% at 3/3. `Improved Channeling` (renamed from Improved Arcane Missiles) now protects [[Arcane Blast]] and [[Arcane Wrath]] too, 70% at 5/5.
- **`Arcane Concentration` gives a 10% chance at 5/5, and an [[Arcane Missiles]] channel now shares one chance.** Blizzard's November 9 hotfix: the chance *"now correctly splits across the cast and every bolt (was granting 10% on cast and per bolt)."* Before it, a five-missile channel rolled six times. The same hotfix stopped [[Flamestrike]]'s ground ticks from spending Clearcasting.
- **`Arcane Concentration` procs at its full chance from any rank of any spell except the [[Blizzard]] spell.** A [[Blizzard]] rank learned more than 12 levels below your level has a reduced chance to trigger it, as it does `Frostbite` and `Fingers of Frost`.
- **`Arcane Geometry` is a new row 3 talent** and adds 6 yards to the range of your Arcane spells at 2/2. `Arcane Shielding` (formerly Improved Mana Shield) now adds resistances to [[Mage Armor]] as well.
- `Arcane Focus` is gone from the tree; spell hit now comes from `Precision` in Frost. `Wand Specialization` costs **2 points** for its full 25% and sits in row 1. `Arcane Resilience` is row 1 with 3 ranks, up to 75% of your Intellect as Armor, and `Magic Attunement` is baseline. `Magic Absorption` has 2 ranks and triggers at most once a second. [[Improved Counterspell]] no longer rolls a chance: rank 1 always silences for 2 seconds and rank 2 for 4, where Classic gave a 50% chance at rank 1.
- **Several talent tooltips lag or show one rank's figure.** `Wand Specialization`, `Arcane Resilience`, `Arcane Instability` and `Improved Channeling` hover at less than the full talent; the totals are the ones in the text.
- **[[Arcane Explosion]] was barely touched**: rank 6 is 239–258, against Classic's 249–270.
## At level 60
**Level 60, the Arcane and Fire build, 32/16/3:** `Improved Channeling` 5, `Arcane Subtlety` 2, `Arcane Concentration` 5, `Missile Barrage` 2, `Arcane Impact` 3, [[Arcane Blast]] 1, `Arcane Meditation` 3, [[Arcane Wrath]] 1, [[Presence of Mind]] 1, `Arcane Mind` 5, `Arcane Instability` 3, `Arcane Power` 1, `Incineration` 5, `Accelerant` 2, `Ignite` 5, [[Pyroblast]] 1, `Improved Fire Blast` 2, `Heating Up` 1, `Precision` 3.
Wowhead (November 4), Icy Veins and WoWSims' preset all publish this same build. Wowhead also carries a 35/13/3 build that casts only Arcane spells, for a boss immune to Fire, and says it performs worse otherwise.
The two sites' priority lists for it agree on the top:
1. **[[Pyroblast]]** when `Heating Up` is at 3 stacks
2. **[[Fire Blast]]** on cooldown, for the stacks
3. **[[Arcane Missiles]]** on a `Missile Barrage` proc
4. **[[Arcane Blast]]** to 4 stacks, then as filler while mana allows
5. **[[Arcane Wrath]]** on cooldown. Icy Veins puts it above Missiles; Wowhead puts it last
- **How long to keep casting [[Arcane Blast]] at 4 stacks is a mana decision.** Wowhead: it *"depends on your gear, and mana remaining vs fight length remaining."* WoWSims' list stacks to four, casts [[Arcane Wrath]], then keeps casting Arcane Blast only while mana is ahead of the time left in the fight, and fills with [[Scorch]] when it is not *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Arcane Power` goes out once a `Missile Barrage` proc is banked**, in Wowhead's opener for the Arcane-only build: [[Arcane Wrath]] before the pull, [[Arcane Blast]] until the proc, `Arcane Power`, then the free channel.
- **[[Evocation]]** when you are close to empty. WoWSims' lists use it at 10% mana *(WoWSims' model)*.
No raid is open before December 9, so none of this has a raid log behind it.
