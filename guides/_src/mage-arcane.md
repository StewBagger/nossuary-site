## What Arcane is for
`Arcane Meditation` 3/3 keeps **50% of your regeneration** running through every cast. That is what pays for [[Arcane Blast]], whose mana cost climbs +100% per cast.
**Icy Veins rates it 4/5 for levelling**; Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish no levelling score. Its heading is *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*: *"Arcane Mages in Forever level up very well… The `Arcane Meditation` talent significantly reduces downtime between fights… and `Missile Barrage` reduces the mana cost of Arcane Missiles by 100%."*
In Classic, Arcane Power raid builds spent their damage casts on Frostbolt instead of the Arcane tree's own spells; ClassicWoW.gg puts it plainly: *"Forever gives you Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage to build around Arcane-school damage itself."*
**The dungeon caveat:** *"if the tank is not good enough, you will pull aggro… the Arcane Mage cannot slow enemies as effectively as a Frost Mage (but still sufficient)."*
## Your talent points
Solo and dungeon builds are given at both 20 and 30, and they **differ a lot**.
**Level 20, solo, 11/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build:** `Improved Channeling` 5 (10–14), `Missile Barrage` 2 (15–16), `Arcane Concentration` 3 (17–19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20). We adjusted the published lists because `Arcane Focus`, which they all spent points on, is not in the launch tree.
**Level 20, dungeon, 11/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build:** `Wand Specialization` 2 (10–11), `Improved Channeling` 3 (12–14), `Arcane Subtlety` 2 (15–16), `Missile Barrage` 2 (17–18), `Arcane Concentration` 1 (19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20). It trades sustain for resistance shred plus a threat cut (`Arcane Subtlety`). Arcane has no hit talent of its own: spell hit comes from `Precision` in Frost, and this build has no spare point for it.
`Arcane Subtlety` is a row 2 talent, so it needs 5 points in the tree first and cannot open a build. At 2/2 it gives **15 spell resistance reduction** and a 30% threat cut.
**Level 30, solo, 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build:** `Improved Channeling` 5 (10–14), `Missile Barrage` 2 (15–16), `Arcane Concentration` 3 (17–19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20), `Arcane Concentration` 2 more (21–22), `Arcane Impact` 2 (23–24), [[Arcane Wrath]] 1 (25), `Arcane Meditation` 3 (26–28), `Arcane Impact` 1 more (29), `Arcane Mind` 1 (30). Three prerequisites set the order: [[Arcane Wrath]] needs [[Arcane Blast]], `Arcane Meditation` needs `Arcane Concentration`, and `Arcane Mind` no longer needs `Arcane Resilience`. That last change is what makes the final point legal. The alternative at 30 is [[Presence of Mind]] in place of `Arcane Mind`.
**Level 30, dungeon, 21/0/0 — likewise ours:** `Wand Specialization` 2 (10–11), `Improved Channeling` 3 (12–14), `Arcane Subtlety` 2 (15–16), `Missile Barrage` 2 (17–18), `Arcane Concentration` 1 (19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20), `Arcane Concentration` 4 more (21–24), [[Arcane Wrath]] 1 (25), `Arcane Meditation` 3 (26–28), `Arcane Impact` 1 (29), `Arcane Mind` 1 (30).
## Rotation
`Missile Barrage` is a row 2 talent: first point at level 15, both at 16. Use the steps below as soon as you have it.
1. **Pull with [[Frostbolt]]** so the enemy arrives slowed, and it can proc `Missile Barrage`
2. `Missile Barrage` up → **[[Arcane Missiles]] immediately**. It is free and channels in half the time
3. Otherwise **[[Arcane Blast]] to 4 stacks maximum**, then cast [[Arcane Missiles]] while the bonus runs
4. From 25, **[[Arcane Wrath]] on its 6-second cooldown** while the bonus runs *(ours)*
5. **A proc mid-sequence means spend it**: *"do not cast another Arcane Blast, as the mana cost drastically increases with each cast"*
6. Moving: [[Fire Blast]], a [[Presence of Mind]]-instant [[Arcane Blast]] (if you took it over `Arcane Mind` at 30), or wand
7. **`Arcane Concentration` procs go on [[Arcane Blast]]**, which is the most expensive thing you cast
**Each [[Arcane Blast]] adds spell damage for everything you cast, itself included,** for 18 seconds from the first stack; no other spell spends it. Its cost is what limits stacking.
**Multi-target.** Tanked: spam [[Arcane Explosion]]. It is the highest dungeon damage *and* the highest threat, so wait for the tank. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Flamestrike]] plus [[Cone of Cold]], kite with [[Arcane Explosion]].
## Stat priority
Icy Veins is the only one of the three tracked sources that ranks Arcane's stats; its order:
1. **Hit** — *"your best stat until the cap"*, though low-level gear rarely carries much of it
2. **Spell Power (Arcane)** — *"the most sought-after stat"*
3. **Spirit** — ranked above Intellect on purpose: *"Spirit is a powerhouse… works while you cast, especially with [[Mage Armor]] and `Arcane Meditation`"*
4. **Intellect** — a bigger mana pool and a small crit bonus
5. **Critical strike** — take it when it's on gear, but *"only gets really powerful when you have 5 talent points in `Arcane Mind`"*
6. **Stamina** — comes along for free on most gear
7. **MP5** — lowest priority; usually a healer stat

## What Forever changed for Arcane
- **`Arcane Mind` was rewritten**: now +10% Intellect at 5/5 and +100% Arcane critical damage, where Classic gave a percentage of maximum mana.
- `Arcane Impact` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Explosion`) now buffs **all** Arcane spells' crit. `Improved Channeling` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Missiles`) now protects [[Arcane Blast]] and [[Arcane Wrath]] too, 70% at 5/5.
- **`Arcane Concentration` procs less from low-rank casts.** Blizzard blames gold farmers fishing for procs with cheap ranks, and says to expect the same penalty on *"any similar talents that trigger a free effect"*. Cast near your trained rank.
- **`Arcane Geometry` is a new row 3 talent** and extends the range of your Arcane spells. `Arcane Shielding` (formerly `Improved Mana Shield`) now adds resistances to [[Mage Armor]] as well.
- `Arcane Focus` is gone from the launch tree; spell hit now comes from `Precision` in Frost. `Wand Specialization` costs **2 points** for its full effect and sits in row 1. `Arcane Resilience` is row 1 with 3 ranks, up to 75% of your Intellect as Armor, and `Magic Attunement` is baseline. `Magic Absorption` has 2 ranks and triggers at most once a second. [[Improved Counterspell]] no longer rolls a chance: rank 1 always silences for 2 seconds and rank 2 for 4, where Classic gave a 50% chance at rank 1. At 2/2 it is unchanged.
- **[[Arcane Explosion]] was barely touched**: rank 6 is 239–258, against Classic's 249–270.
## At level 60
Nothing above 30 was played in the beta. The open question is whether [[Arcane Blast]]'s +100%-per-stack mana curve survives level-60 mana pools and raid buffs, or whether Arcane stays a burst and levelling spec. `Arcane Power`, the 31-point capstone at level 40, **requires [[Presence of Mind]]**, and `Arcane Instability` no longer does.
