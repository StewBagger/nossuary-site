## Is Arcane worth levelling?
**Yes, and it is the biggest change to the class.** Icy Veins' heading is literally *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*: *"Arcane Mages in Forever level up very well… The `Arcane Meditation` talent significantly reduces downtime between fights… and `Missile Barrage` reduces the mana cost of Arcane Missiles by 100%."*
[[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage` are both new — in Classic, Arcane Power raid builds spent their damage casts on Frostbolt instead of the Arcane tree's own spells; ClassicWoW.gg puts it plainly: *"Forever gives you Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage to build around Arcane-school damage itself."*
**The dungeon caveat:** *"if the tank is not good enough, you will pull aggro… the Arcane Mage cannot slow enemies as effectively as a Frost Mage (but still sufficient)."*
## Your talent points
**There are separate solo and dungeon builds at both 20 and 30, and they differ a lot.**
**Level 20, solo, 11/0/0:** `Improved Channeling` 1 → `Arcane Focus` 1→2 → `Improved Channeling` 2→3 → `Arcane Concentration` 1→5 → **[[Arcane Blast]]**.
**Level 20, dungeon, 11/0/0:** `Arcane Focus` 1→4 → `Wand Specialization` 1→2 → `Arcane Subtlety` 1→2 → `Arcane Concentration` 1→2 → **[[Arcane Blast]]**. The dungeon build trades sustain for **hit** (`Arcane Focus`) and **threat reduction plus resistance shred** (`Arcane Subtlety`).
A third published build opens `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Arcane Focus` 3 → `Arcane Subtlety` 2 → `Arcane Concentration` 3 → [[Arcane Blast]], and takes `Arcane Subtlety` for its **15 spell resistance reduction** rather than its threat cut. Its flex point is named: *"the other 3 points either, to your preference, in `Arcane Focus` or `Arcane Channeling`."*
**Level 30, solo, 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build:** `Improved Channeling` 1→4 (10–13), `Arcane Focus` 1 (14), `Arcane Concentration` 1→5 (15–19), **[[Arcane Blast]] at 20**, `Arcane Focus` 2→3 (21–22), `Arcane Impact` 1→2 (23–24), **`Missile Barrage` at 25**, `Arcane Meditation` 1→3 (26–28), `Improved Channeling` 5/5 (29), `Arcane Mind` 1 (30).
**Level 30, dungeon, 21/0/0 — likewise ours.** Front-loading `Arcane Focus` 5 and `Arcane Subtlety` 2 **delays [[Arcane Blast]] to level 22**, on purpose.
## Rotation
`Missile Barrage` only exists from 25, so the sequence changes at that level.
1. **Pull with [[Frostbolt]]** so the enemy arrives slowed, and it can proc `Missile Barrage`
2. **`Missile Barrage` up → [[Arcane Missiles]] immediately.** It is free and channels in half the time
3. Otherwise **[[Arcane Blast]] to 4 stacks maximum**, then spend into [[Arcane Missiles]]
4. **A proc mid-sequence means spend it**: *"do not cast another Arcane Blast, as the mana cost drastically increases with each cast"*
5. Moving: [[Fire Blast]], a [[Presence of Mind]]-instant [[Arcane Blast]], or wand
6. **`Arcane Concentration` procs go on [[Arcane Blast]]**, which is the most expensive thing you cast
**[[Arcane Blast]] does not increase its own damage.** Each cast empowers *the next different spell*. And *"building a bigger bonus is wasted if you cannot afford the spell meant to spend it."*
**Multi-target.** Tanked: **spam [[Arcane Explosion]]** — highest dungeon damage *and* highest threat, so wait for the tank. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Flamestrike]] plus [[Cone of Cold]], kite with [[Arcane Explosion]]. Spend a leftover [[Arcane Blast]] stack on the first area cast.
## Stat priority
Icy Veins is the only one of the three tracked sources that ranks Arcane's stats; its order:
1. **Hit** — *"your best stat until the cap"*, though low-level gear rarely carries much of it
2. **Spell Power (Arcane)** — *"the most sought-after stat"*
3. **Spirit** — ranked above Intellect on purpose: *"Spirit is a powerhouse… works while you cast, especially with [[Mage Armor]] and `Arcane Meditation`"*
4. **Intellect** — a bigger mana pool and a small crit bonus
5. **Critical strike** — take it when it's on gear, but *"only gets really powerful when you have 5 talent points in `Arcane Mind`"*
6. **Stamina** — comes along for free on most gear
7. **MP5** — lowest priority; usually a healer stat
## What defines Arcane
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Arcane Blast]]** | **20** (22 in the dungeon build) | New. +10% to all your *other* spells per cast, **+175% mana cost per cast**, 4 stacks, 8 seconds **or until you cast any other damage spell** |
| **`Missile Barrage`** | **25** | New. 40% from [[Arcane Blast]], 20% from [[Fireball]] or [[Frostbolt]]: next [[Arcane Missiles]] is half the channel and **free** |
| **`Arcane Meditation` 3/3** | 27 | **50% of mana regeneration continues while casting**, up from Classic's 15% (ClassicWoW.gg gives the per-rank curve: 17% at rank 1, 33% at rank 2, 50% at rank 3) |
## What Forever changed for Arcane
- **[[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage` are both new.** Together they give the spec a main damage spell and a free follow-up it never had in Classic.
- **`Arcane Meditation` went from 15% to 50%.** This is what makes Arcane a levelling spec.
- **`Arcane Mind` was rewritten**: now +10% Intellect at 5/5 **and +100% Arcane critical damage**, where Classic gave flat maximum mana.
- `Arcane Impact` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Explosion`) now buffs **all** Arcane spells' crit. `Improved Channeling` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Missiles`) now protects [[Arcane Blast]] too, 70% at 5/5.
- `Wand Specialization` moved to tier 1. `Arcane Focus` was rewritten from resistance reduction to plain Arcane hit. `Magic Absorption` went from 5 ranks to 2. **[[Improved Counterspell]] no longer rolls a chance.** Rank 1 always silences for 2 seconds and rank 2 for 4, where Classic gave a 50% chance at rank 1. At 2/2 it is unchanged.
- **[[Arcane Explosion]] was barely touched**: rank 6 is 232–252, against Classic's 243–263.
## At level 60
Unknown. The open question: whether [[Arcane Blast]]'s +175%-per-stack mana curve is survivable with level-60 mana pools and raid buffs, or whether Arcane stays a burst and levelling spec.
