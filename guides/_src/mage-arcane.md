Burst damage on a mana curve you have to manage. **Arcane is genuinely new in Forever** — in Classic its tree had nothing worth spending points on, and now it is a levelling spec.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 20** right now, rising to 30 before launch, so no level-60 Arcane build has been tested.
:::
## Is Arcane worth levelling?
**Yes, and this is the change most worth knowing about the class.** The published heading is literally *"A Solid Leveling Spec"*: *"Arcane Mages in Forever level up very well… The `Arcane Meditation` talent significantly reduces downtime between fights… and `Missile Barrage` reduces the mana cost of Arcane Missiles by 100%."* One source puts it level with Frost — *"you should mainly pick between Arcane or Frost builds."*
The reason it was not a spec in Classic is worth stating plainly: the Arcane tree had *"no way to use [its talents] other than empowering [[Arcane Explosion]] and [[Arcane Missiles]]."* [[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage` are both new.
**The dungeon caveat is real:** *"if the tank is not good enough, you will pull aggro… the Arcane Mage cannot slow enemies as effectively as a Frost Mage (but still sufficient)."*
## Your talent points
**There are separate solo and dungeon builds at both 20 and 30, and the difference is not cosmetic.**
**Level 20, solo — 11/0/0:** `Improved Channeling` 1 → `Arcane Focus` 1→2 → `Improved Channeling` 2→3 → `Arcane Concentration` 1→5 → **[[Arcane Blast]]**.
**Level 20, dungeon — 11/0/0:** `Arcane Focus` 1→4 → `Wand Specialization` 1→2 → `Arcane Subtlety` 1→2 → `Arcane Concentration` 1→2 → **[[Arcane Blast]]**. The dungeon build trades sustain for **hit** (`Arcane Focus`) and **threat reduction plus resistance shred** (`Arcane Subtlety`).
A third published build opens `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Arcane Focus` 3 → `Arcane Subtlety` 2 → `Arcane Concentration` 3 → [[Arcane Blast]], and takes `Arcane Subtlety` for its **15 spell resistance reduction** rather than its threat cut. Its flex point is named: *"the other 3 points either, to your preference, in `Arcane Focus` or `Arcane Channeling`."*
**Level 30, solo — 21/0/0:** `Improved Channeling` 1→4 (10–13), `Arcane Focus` 1 (14), `Arcane Concentration` 1→5 (15–19), **[[Arcane Blast]] at 20**, `Arcane Focus` 2→3 (21–22), `Arcane Impact` 1→2 (23–24), **`Missile Barrage` at 25**, `Arcane Meditation` 1→3 (26–28), `Improved Channeling` 5/5 (29), `Arcane Mind` 1 (30).
**Level 30, dungeon — 21/0/0**, and note the consequence: front-loading `Arcane Focus` 5 and `Arcane Subtlety` 2 **delays [[Arcane Blast]] to level 22.** That is a deliberate trade, not an error.
## Rotation
`Missile Barrage` only exists from 25, so the sequence changes at that level.
1. **Pull with [[Frostbolt]]** so the enemy arrives slowed — and it can proc `Missile Barrage`
2. **`Missile Barrage` up → [[Arcane Missiles]] immediately.** It is free and channels in half the time
3. Otherwise **[[Arcane Blast]] to 4 stacks maximum**, then spend into [[Arcane Missiles]]
4. **A proc mid-sequence means spend it** — *"do not cast another Arcane Blast, as the mana cost drastically increases with each cast"*
5. Moving: [[Fire Blast]], a [[Presence of Mind]]-instant [[Arcane Blast]], or wand
6. **`Arcane Concentration` procs go on [[Arcane Blast]]**, which is the most expensive thing you cast
**The one misconception to clear up: [[Arcane Blast]] does not increase its own damage.** Each cast empowers *the next different spell*. And *"building a bigger bonus is wasted if you cannot afford the spell meant to spend it."*
**Multi-target.** Tanked: **spam [[Arcane Explosion]]** — highest dungeon damage *and* highest threat, so wait for the tank. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Flamestrike]] plus [[Cone of Cold]], kite with [[Arcane Explosion]]. Spend a leftover [[Arcane Blast]] stack on the first area cast.
## Stat priority
Spell Power and Arcane spell power, then Hit, spell Haste, Intellect, critical strike, Spirit, MP5.
**Intellect is unusually important here** — *"it directly interacts with how many times you can cast Arcane Blast."* One source reorders to put **Spirit** third instead, and the reason is sound: *"Spirit is a powerhouse… works while you cast, especially with [[Mage Armor]] and `Arcane Meditation`."* Both orderings are defensible; the disagreement is about how much you lean on regeneration versus pool size.

| **`Missile Barrage`** | **25** | New. 40% from [[Arcane Blast]], 20% from [[Fireball]] or [[Frostbolt]]: next [[Arcane Missiles]] is half the channel and **free** |

## What Forever changed for Arcane
- **[[Arcane Blast]] and `Missile Barrage` are both new.** The published summary: *"The former now finally grants Arcane Mage a main spell that it can rely on, while the latter introduces an RNG mechanic that empowers Arcane Missiles."*
- **`Arcane Meditation` went from 15% to 50%.** This is what makes Arcane a levelling spec.
- **`Arcane Mind` was rewritten** — now +10% Intellect at 5/5 **and +100% Arcane critical damage**, where Classic gave flat maximum mana.
- `Arcane Impact` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Explosion`) now buffs **all** Arcane spells' crit. `Improved Channeling` (renamed from `Improved Arcane Missiles`) now protects [[Arcane Blast]] too, 70% at 5/5.
- `Wand Specialization` moved to tier 1. `Arcane Focus` was rewritten from resistance reduction to plain Arcane hit. `Magic Absorption` went from 5 ranks to 2. **[[Improved Counterspell]] no longer rolls a chance** — rank 1 always silences for 2 seconds and rank 2 for 4, where Classic gave a 50% chance at rank 1. At 2/2 it is unchanged.
- **[[Arcane Explosion]] was barely touched** — rank 6 is 232–252, against Classic's 243–263.
## At level 60
Unknown. The specific open question: whether [[Arcane Blast]]'s +175%-per-stack mana curve is survivable with level-60 mana pools and raid buffs, or whether Arcane stays a burst and levelling spec. One source also notes *"it is yet unknown how Haste will interact with Missile Barrage."*
