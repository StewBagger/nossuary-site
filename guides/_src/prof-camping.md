**New in Forever.** A cook lights a fire, everyone drops one crafted object beside it, and the whole group carries the buffs for an hour, including buffs that normally need a specific class.
:::scope
Camp objects, skill levels and buff values come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. **Several mechanics are genuinely unsettled**: whether camps work in cities and instances, whether you must be grouped to receive someone else's buff, and where each Blueprint drops. Blizzard's own panel and the beta client also disagree on two numbers, flagged below.
:::
## How camping works
1. **A cook places a campfire.** [[Basic Campfire Kit]] takes 1 Simple Wood and Flint and Tinder. It burns 15 minutes, and no other campfire can be within 100 yards
2. **Each player adds one object** from their own profession, learned at skill 20
3. **Wait a minute at the fire.** Since the September 24 build, sitting, sitting in a chair, sleeping, lying down and the kneeling emote all count. You may buff, cast and craft while you wait
4. The buffs last an hour, and you can then walk away
Camp objects share a **one-hour cooldown**. You set a camp up before a session, not between pulls.
Objects are tradeable, but **placing one needs the skill**. You cannot buy your way to a full camp.
## What each profession brings
Every profession has three objects, at skill **20, 140 and 300**. The 140 and 300 ones come from Blueprints that drop from dungeon bosses, and each replacement keeps the buff of the object it replaces.

Every object, value and non-stacking rule in that table is **foreverchanges.pro's reading of the client**. Blizzard describes camping itself, names the campfire and the [[Boosted Rest]] debuff, and has rewritten its tooltips. It has published no camp object, no buff value and no equivalence to a class buff.
**Every stat buff in that table is the figure at the level beside it, and the client scales the rest by character level.** Each object climbs on its own ladder: the Lodestone pays 12 attack power below level 12, then 20, 32, 49 and 67, before reaching 90 at 52. At the beta's level-30 cap a full camp hands out 32 attack power, 12 Intellect, 21 Stamina and 15 mana every 5 seconds. Add 11 Strength, 14 Spirit, and the Lute's 163 armour with +7 all stats and +6 all resistances. The three percentage buffs do not scale: the Fish Bowl's 8%, the Camp Chair's 2% and the Camp Tent's 5% read the same at every level.
**A camp hands a group the class buffs it does not have.** A party with no Paladin can still have Blessing of Kings, Might and Wisdom. None of them stacks with the real thing, so you get one or the other. The Druid crit aura appears twice because Blizzard made [[Moonkin Form]] and [[Leader of the Pack]] the same effect, mutually exclusive with each other.
The [[Camp Tent]] tops up rather than stacks: *"does nothing if you already have more."* The [[Faction Banner]] only buffs your own faction, and has separate Horde and Alliance recipes.

You can learn a Blueprint well before you can craft the kit, which is why guides quote different numbers for the same thing. Both are right, printed from different columns.
## Two published numbers that disagree
- The [[Sharpening Wheel]] is widely reported as giving attack power. **The beta client says Strength.** The client is what is live, and both guide sites follow it. The attack-power claim is attributed to Blizzard's What's Next panel, but the recap this page cites does not contain it, so treat it as reported rather than published.
- A basic campfire is likewise reported as allowing **three objects**. The client shows 3, 5 and 10 for the three kit tiers, so that reading fits the basic kit only — and again, the cited recap does not say it.
## What changed in the October 1 build
- **Campfires no longer damage players below level 5**, and they deal reduced damage to players under 90% health.
- **The [[Boosted Rest]] debuff has a new icon and a rewritten tooltip**, which Blizzard says is *"intended to make the effect clearer."* No value changed. Leatherworking's [[Camp Tent]] is the only rest-related camp object on this page. Blizzard's note names the debuff but not the object it belongs to, so that link is inferred here rather than stated.
- **Camping tooltips now say "mutually exclusive" instead of just "exclusive"**, which Blizzard did *"to reduce some confusion around functionality."* This page describes effects as not stacking, so nothing above needed correcting. Four profession pages did: the camp-object tables on [Alchemy](/guides/wow-forever/professions/alchemy/), [First Aid](/guides/wow-forever/professions/first-aid/), [Herbalism](/guides/wow-forever/professions/herbalism/) and [Mining](/guides/wow-forever/professions/mining/) each read "Exclusive with", and all four now carry Blizzard's term.
## What is not known
Whether camps function in cities and in instances. Whether you must be **grouped** to receive another player's buff, which nobody has answered. How many objects one craft produces. And where each of the 140 and 300 Blueprints drops, beyond "specific dungeon bosses". Two are known: Alchemy's [[Fermenter]] Blueprint drops in Wailing Caverns and Ruins of Lordaeron.
