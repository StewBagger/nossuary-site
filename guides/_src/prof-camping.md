**New in Forever.** A cook lights a fire, players drop one crafted object each beside it, and everyone who rests there carries the buffs for an hour, including buffs that normally need a specific class.
:::scope
The objects, their skill levels, the buff ranges and what each buff is exclusive with are **Blizzard's**, from its October 21 article and its hotfixes through November 12. Tooltip text and reagents are the client's, as Wowhead (November 13) and foreverchanges.pro (build 70938) print them. Blueprint drop sources are Icy Veins' list, dated October 19, before launch. **Still thin:** where the skill-300 Blueprints drop. Blizzard says dungeons and raids, no raid opens until December 9, and no site has published a source for any of them.
:::
## How camping works
1. **A cook places a campfire.** [[Basic Campfire Kit]] takes 1 Simple Wood and a reusable Flint and Tinder, both sold by the Cooking trainer. Campfire kits share a 5-minute cooldown
2. **Each player adds one object** from their own profession. The first one comes at skill 20, from a short trainer quest or bought from the trainer
3. **Sit or craft at the fire for a minute.** Sitting, sitting in a chair, sleeping, lying down and kneeling all count, and you may buff, cast and craft while you wait
4. The buffs last an hour, and you can then walk away
**You do not need to be grouped.** Blizzard describes the buffs going to *"nearby players"* and *"passing adventurers"*, and Wowhead says anyone can receive them, with or without the profession.
Camp objects share a **one-hour cooldown**, separate from the campfire's five minutes. Placing an object uses it up, and foreverchanges.pro times a placed Tanning Rack or Spinning Wheel at 15 minutes on the ground. You set a camp up before a session, not between pulls.
Objects are tradeable, but **placing one needs the skill**. You cannot buy your way to a full camp.
**Where you can camp.** Outdoors and out of combat. Icy Veins says anywhere in the outdoor world except major cities, with a minimum distance between campfires. No source gives that distance.
The quest `The Great Outdoors`, around level 5, introduces the system and sends you to learn Cooking.

## The buffs scale with your level
The stat buffs are level dependent. The tooltip prints only the top value, so a low-level character gets less than it reads.
| Object | Buff | Value by character level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Lodestone]] | Melee attack power | 12 below level 12, 20 from 12, 32 from 22, 49 from 32, 67 from 42, 90 from 52 |
| [[Mana Well]] | Mana every 5 seconds | 10 below level 24, 15 from 24, 20 from 34, 24 from 44, 29 from 54 |
| [[Sharpening Wheel]] | Strength | 6 below level 24, 11 from 24, 20 from 38, 34 from 52 |
| [[Incense Candle]] | Intellect | 2 below level 14, 6 from 14, 12 from 28, 18 from 42, 25 from 56 |
| [[First Aid Kit]] | Stamina | 3 below level 12, 8 from 12, 21 from 24, 34 from 36, 45 from 48, 56 at 60 |
| [[Faction Banner]] | Spirit | 14 below level 40, 19 from 40, 27 from 50, 32 at 60 |
| [[Enchanted Lute]] | Armour | 28 below level 10, 71 from 10, 114 from 20, 163 from 30, 211 from 40, 260 from 50, 308 at 60 |
| [[Enchanted Lute]] | All stats | None below level 10, 2 from 10, 4 from 20, 7 from 30, 9 from 40, 12 from 50, 13 at 60 |
| [[Enchanted Lute]] | All resistances | None below level 30, 6 from 30, 12 from 40, 16 from 50, 22 at 60 |
These ladders are the client's own, read from the spell text at build 1.60.1.70938. Blizzard's article prints the same lowest and highest values for every object it lists, with one exception.
**The Lute's armour is 308 at level 60, not 260.** Blizzard's article says 28 to 260. In the client 260 is the value from level 50 to 59, and 308 is the level-60 step.
The three percentage buffs do not scale: the Fish Bowl's 8%, the Camp Chair's 2% and the Camp Tent's 5% read the same at every level.
**A camp hands a group the class buffs it does not have.** A party with no Paladin can still have the effects of Blessing of Kings, Might and Wisdom. Each is mutually exclusive with the real thing, so you get one or the other.
The [[Camp Tent]] tops up rather than stacks: *"No effect if Rested experience already exceeds that value."* Icy Veins describes it as once per hour. The client also carries a one-hour [[Boosted Rest]] debuff, which we read as the tent's lockout *(ours, not a source's)*. The [[Faction Banner]] buffs your own faction only.

**Two of these were hotfixed on November 10.** The [[Arcane Salvager]] is now crafted with a Runed Golden Rod, and its disenchanting bonus now works for lower-level enchanters. The [[Fishing Rack]] had one lure bug fixed, and Blizzard says other issues with it remain, including *"There is no loot"* errors.

## Campfire capacity
| Kit | Cooking skill | Blueprint | Objects supported |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Basic Campfire Kit]] | 1 | None, learned with Cooking | 3 |
| [[Journeyman Campfire Kit]] | 90 | Lord Serpentis, Wailing Caverns | 5 |
| [[Expert Campfire Kit]] | 200 to learn | Source not published | 10 |
**The Journeyman kit is placed at Cooking 90.** It used to need 140 to place although the recipe was learned at 90. Blizzard matched the two on November 10. Guides written before that date still print 140.
**The Expert kit may have the same gap.** Its recipe is learned at 200. Icy Veins, on November 3, lists 220 to place it. Blizzard's hotfix named only the Journeyman kit, so check the kit's tooltip.
A cook's two other objects, [[Cookie's Feast]] at 140 and the [[Iron Oven]] at 300, go on the shared one-hour cooldown like everyone else's.
## Pouch of Violet Dust
A launch toy that turns a campfire purple and **adds an hour to all camp benefits, two hours in total.** Everyone resting at that camp gets the extension, not only the owner.
- It was a Twitch drop, claimable from November 4 to November 12. It can no longer be earned
- It works only during the Launch Celebration, which Blizzard says ends in early December. After that the toy is removed from collections
- Until November 9 it failed to add the hour. Blizzard fixed that in a hotfix
- The client gives its use a 30-minute cooldown
## Legacy perks that touch camping
- **`Field Guide`**, three ranks: cuts the cooldown on adding camp objects by 8%, 17% and 25%. At full rank the hour becomes 45 minutes
- **`Permanence`**, two ranks: camp benefits last 50% and then 100% longer
- **`Reagent Economy`**: Wowhead and Icy Veins both say it also makes your skill-20 camp objects cost no reagents. The client text foreverchanges.pro prints for it mentions only class reagents
## Small rules
- Campfires do not damage players below level 5, and deal reduced damage to players under 90% health
- Crafting a secondary profession's camp object makes two of them, per Wowhead
- On a PvP realm a camp can be attacked. Blizzard's own advice is to choose the spot carefully
## What is not known
- **Where the skill-300 Blueprints drop**, and the Expert Campfire's. Icy Veins lists every one as unknown
- **Whether the Expert kit still needs 220 to place**, as above
- **The minimum distance between campfires.** Icy Veins confirms there is one. No source gives a figure
- **Whether camps work inside dungeons.** Icy Veins says outdoor areas. Nobody has published a test
