Warrior builds its own resource by fighting, and spends it on three jobs: two-handed damage, dual-wield damage, and shield tanking. You start every fight with an empty bar and fill it by swinging. Rage from dealing damage is now fully normalized to weapon speed, and a critical strike generates **100% increased Rage**.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Arms](/guides/wow-forever/warrior/arms/)** | Melee DPS | [[Rend]] into `Bloodthrill` into [[Overpower]] from 30, at 4% per rank (20% at 34): hits on your own bleed proc it, no dodge needed | Two-handed bleeds plus a 50% healing debuff, and eleven points that go to Fury at 20 |
| **[Fury](/guides/wow-forever/warrior/fury/)** | Melee DPS | Dual-wielding from 20 swings more often, and `Unbridled Wrath` gives ~60% of those hits +1 Rage | A rage dump that waits on its own kit: dual wield at 20, [[Whirlwind]] at 36 |
| **[Protection](/guides/wow-forever/warrior/protection/)** | Tank | `Shield Specialization` 5/5 returns 5 Rage per block, where Classic gave 1, and that funds [[Revenge]] | Shield tanking, and three of its talents now require a shield |
**At level 20 the published Arms and Fury builds are identical**: eleven points in the Fury tree, because nothing in Arms is worth reaching yet. Protection's is its own. Pick by what you want at 30 and beyond, not by what you can feel at 20. Dual Specialization opens at level 40 (Blizzard), so the pick is not permanent.
## Is Warrior for you?
Play Warrior if you want to watch the enemy rather than your own bars. Icy Veins describes the trade: one of the harder classes to level, one of the strongest once geared. **Wowhead takes the opposite tone on levelling itself.** Forever's changes make Arms "feel phenomenal," a deliberate contrast with Vanilla's reputation.
Set Blizzard's own Rage work against that "hardest class to level" reputation. Its changes target the early-game Rage starvation reviewers describe: Rage from taking damage no longer shrinks for wearing Armor, Blizzard fixed several bugs in that calculation on October 8, and a critical hit gives 100% increased Rage. None of that touches the other reasons Warrior levels slowly — no self-heal before 20, no ranged attack, full dependence on a current weapon. No source had re-rated the class after those changes when this page was written, and that the reputation still holds, on a narrower set of reasons, is our own reading rather than a source's.
Blizzard's closing assessment of the class, in its own words:
> Warriors remain the masters of melee combat. They can both dish out melee damage and mitigate damage done to them. They have a unique strength in dealing sustained damage to multiple melee targets, found on no other melee class. They have a powerful toolkit to solve nearly any problem, but they lack the tools to easily recover from taking too much damage. And they have unique buffs and debuffs that only Warriors can supply.
:::strengths
- Blizzard's own framing: Warriors *"can both dish out melee damage and mitigate damage done to them"*
- Weapon damage leads every published stat priority, so an upgrade is felt immediately
- No resource to manage before a fight starts, you begin at zero and build
:::
:::weaknesses
- Nothing in the kit heals you or reaches at range before 20
- [[Victory Rush]] at level 20 is the first self-heal, and it needs a kill before it is available
- Dependent on keeping your weapon current — Icy Veins' difficulty rating is *"especially when gear falls behind"*
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Four or five buttons while levelling, and you react rather than follow a sequence.
**Is it forgiving?** No self-heal before 20 and no escape, so a second enemy on you is a real risk.
**What carries you while levelling?** Rage from being hit as well as hitting, plus the 100% crit bonus above. Expect to sit and eat often before level 20.
**Does it need a group?** Rage from damage taken pays best when a healer lets you pull two.
:::

**Talent points are your level minus nine**: one at 10, eleven at 20, twenty-one at 30, fifty-one at 60. Tiers open at levels 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40. The one-point milestone talents sit at 11, 21 and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point tier (Blizzard). The Legacy perk Talented moves your first point earlier, down to level 5 at full rank. That is 26 points at level 30 rather than 21.
**Devastate is not in Forever, and it was not in Classic either.** It is a Burning Crusade ability, so lists that expect it are reading forward, not back. [[Sunder Armor]] is the ability you use instead, and its threat scales with Attack Power.
## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest choice on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP. The ones that count break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm), `Escape Artist` (Gnome, roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Alliance — Night Elf.** `Elune's Light` gives +10% crit for 15 seconds and lines up with your cooldowns, `Shadowmeld` drops threat, and `Wisp Spirit` speeds corpse runs.
**Horde — Orc.** `Blood Fury` is +10% attack power and spell power for 15 seconds on a 2-minute cooldown, and `Hardiness` shortens stuns on you by 20%. We keep it as the Horde pick for that pair, a short damage cooldown you can use often plus less time stunned *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Forever changed the answer.** Vanilla's Human and Orc weapon-skill picks are gone and no racial cares what weapon you hold. Human keeps a flat 1% crit in `Ruthless`, and Night Elf gained an offensive cooldown it never had.
## Quality of life
- **Do the level-10 class quest immediately, and the level-30 one on time.** They are how you get [[Defensive Stance]], [[Sunder Armor]], [[Taunt]] and later [[Berserker Stance]]. A Warrior who skips them has no tanking kit at all.
- **Keep the weapon current and its skill trained.** A new weapon type will not perform as expected while its skill lags.
- **Carry a one-hander and shield permanently** for [[Shield Bash]] interrupts.
- **Mining and Blacksmithing** lets you craft your own weapons and armour. First Aid is your only fast self-heal before 20. And 103 cooked dishes now give +5% experience from kills while you are well fed, 15 minutes a serving; quest experience is unaffected.
- **Group when you can, and pull two.** Blizzard rebalanced Rage from damage taken around questing Warriors, and the place it pays best is levelling alongside a healer *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **[[Thunder Clap]] now works from [[Defensive Stance]]** (Blizzard), so AoE threat no longer forces a stance swap.
- **[[Rend]] and [[Sunder Armor]] now instantly tap the enemy onto your threat table** — useful for grabbing a pull before another melee does, and for a tank racing to establish threat.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Rage from dealing damage normalized to weapon speed | Raw damage dealt no longer feeds rage, swing speed does |
| Critical hits give increased Rage | **100% increased Rage**, set by Blizzard on October 2. A Rage formula is not something a tooltip shows, so that figure is Blizzard's |
| **Armor no longer reduces Rage from taking damage** | Blizzard's formula assumes a fixed mitigation for your level, 20% to 40%, whatever you wear. Your own gear stops mattering to your Rage income, and absorbs like Power Word: Shield no longer block Rage gain either |
| Yellow attacks generate no rage | [[Heroic Strike]] and [[Cleave]] are net rage losses while levelling |
| [[Victory Rush]] added at 20 | **Your first self-heal**, and the only one you get without spending a point. Blizzard adds two talented ones: `Blood Craze`, which no longer fires off [[Bloodthirst]] casts, and `Gore Drinker` at Fury row 6 |
| [[Tactical Mastery]] baseline at 14 | You keep 10 rage through a stance change without spending a point |
| The three major cooldowns no longer share a lockout | Retaliation and Shield Wall dropped to 15 minutes, but **Recklessness is still 30** |
| Plate armour at 40 | Cloth, leather and mail until then |
## At level 60
Unknown, and we will not guess. Nobody has played the class at 60 yet, and no source had published a Warrior build past level 20 when this page was written. Icy Veins flags Fury as the spec the rage rework hit hardest, and says outright: *"we are unsure currently if there will be additional ways to generate Rage at Level 60, as these changes alone greatly reduce Fury Warrior's Rage generation."* Raids open December 9.
