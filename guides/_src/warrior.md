Warrior is the slowest class to level in Forever and one of the strongest once geared. Rage now comes from weapon speed rather than damage dealt, so the early game is about not running dry.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta caps at level 30 and never goes higher before launch, so **no level-60 Warrior build has been tested by anyone.** Talent trees and ability training levels below are read from the beta client and are solid; anything about endgame is not, and we have left it out rather than guessed.
:::
## Is Warrior for you?
Play Warrior if you want to watch the enemy rather than your own bars, and you do not mind a hard first thirty levels in exchange for being formidable later.
:::strengths
- The best tank in the game once you have [[Defensive Stance]] and a shield
- Enormous damage ceiling with a good two-handed weapon
- No resource to manage before a fight starts — you begin at zero and build
:::
:::weaknesses
- Both published guides rate it the worst leveller in the game
- No self-heal until [[Victory Rush]] at level 20, and no ranged attack worth the name
- Utterly dependent on keeping your weapon current
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes — four or five buttons while levelling, and you react rather than follow a sequence.
**Is it forgiving?** No. Pulling two enemies alone will often kill you, and you have no escape.
**Does it level well?** It is the slowest. Expect to sit and eat often before level 20.
**Does it need a group?** It benefits more than any other class. A Warrior with a healer is transformed.
:::
## Your first talent points
**Spend all eleven in Fury, not Arms.** Arms is the levelling *spec*, but its damage talents arrive later than Fury's resource fix, and rage is the problem you actually have.
1. **`Cruelty`, 5 points** — +5% critical strike. Hard to pass up in any spec.
2. **`Unbridled Wrath`, 5 points** — extra rage on hit, and **2 rage with a two-hander rather than 1**. This directly fixes the class's worst levelling problem.
3. **`Piercing Howl`, 1 point** — a 50% area slow. It costs rage, so it is situational; on a PvP realm take it anyway.
An alternative published build stays in Arms and reaches `Deep Wounds` by 20, which procs easily off `Improved Overpower`. It is respectable and slower to come online.
At 30, Arms takes `Deep Wounds`, `Impale`, `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` and a point in **`Sweeping Strikes`** — your first real area damage, and the tree is short of it.
If you have the Legacy **Talented** perk, points start at level 5, so `Cruelty` finishes around level 9 instead of 14. That lands your crit and rage fix before [[Defensive Stance]] arrives, which noticeably eases levels 10 to 20.
## Levelling rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** — keep it up at all times. [[Bloodrage]] between pulls.
2. **[[Charge]]** to open. Out of combat only, so pull a pack with a ranged weapon instead.
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on anything that is not a caster.
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** the moment it procs — 20-second window, and it restores 10% of your health.
5. **[[Overpower]]** immediately when they dodge you.
6. **[[Rend]]** early, and only if the target will live out the bleed.
7. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice.
8. **Auto-attack to finish.** Never spend rage on something already dying.
**Two or more enemies:** [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Demoralizing Shout]], [[Piercing Howl]] to peel, [[Retaliation]] from 20 when several are on you. The real answer is `Sweeping Strikes` with [[Whirlwind]], which needs level 36.
**Do not use [[Heroic Strike]] while levelling.** It converts your white swing into a yellow one, and **yellow attacks generate no rage in Forever** — so it is a net rage loss. It is an endgame button for when you have rage to spare.

## Stat priority
1. **Weapon DPS** — slow, with a high top end
2. **Hit**
3. **Critical strike**
4. **Strength**
5. Agility, then Stamina
Rage is normalized to **weapon speed**, not damage dealt. A slow two-hander gives bigger individual hits and **2 rage per `Unbridled Wrath` proc instead of 1**.
**Dual wielding actually generates more rage** — about 5.2 per second against a two-hander's 4.5, rising further with `Dual Wield Specialization`. You cannot dual wield until **level 20**, and hit becomes your most valuable stat once you do, because a missed swing is rage you never earn.
## Which race
**Alliance — Night Elf.** `Elune's Light` gives +10% crit for 15 seconds and lines up with your cooldowns, `Shadowmeld` drops threat, and `Wisp Spirit` speeds corpse runs.
**Horde — Orc.** `Blood Fury` is +10% attack power and `Axe Specialization` gives +1% crit, which makes axes worth seeking out.
**Forever changed the answer.** Vanilla's Human and Orc weapon-skill picks are gone — all three weapon racials became critical-strike racials, and Night Elf gained an offensive cooldown it never had.
## Quality of life
- **Do the level-10 class quest immediately, and the level-30 one on time.** They are how you get [[Defensive Stance]], [[Sunder Armor]], [[Taunt]] and later [[Berserker Stance]]. A Warrior who skips them has no tanking kit at all.
- **Keep the weapon current and its skill trained.** A new weapon type will not perform as expected while its skill lags — practise on low-level enemies after switching.
- **Carry a one-hander and shield permanently** for [[Shield Bash]] interrupts and [[Mocking Blow]] pulls.
- **Mining and Blacksmithing** lets you craft your own weapons and armour. **First Aid is your only fast self-heal** before 20, and food now grants **+5% experience**.
- **Group when you can, and pull two.** Rage from damage taken survives in Forever but is small, so the one situation it pays is levelling alongside a healer — published guidance is to pull two enemies at a time in that case, specifically for the rage.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Rage normalized to weapon speed | Damage dealt no longer feeds rage. Slow weapons are better for the resource |
| Critical hits give no bonus rage | The vanilla feedback loop of gear → damage → rage is gone |
| Yellow attacks generate no rage | [[Heroic Strike]] and [[Cleave]] are net rage losses while levelling |
| [[Victory Rush]] added at 20 | Your first and only self-heal |
| [[Tactical Mastery]] made baseline at 14 | You keep 10 rage through a stance change without spending a point |
| [[Devastate]] absent | [[Sunder Armor]] is the ability, now scaling with Attack Power |
| `Weaponmaster` folds the weapon specs into one talent | You can switch weapon family without respeccing |
| Plate armour at 40 | Cloth, leather and mail until then |
## At level 60
Unknown, and we will not guess. No source publishes a Warrior build past level 30. What is settled is that **critical hits giving no bonus rage is intentional** — a Blizzard designer closed the report as working as designed. How much rage you get from *taking* damage is community reverse-engineering rather than anything Blizzard has published. Rage generation is currently low enough that one published guide says outright you *"will be struggling to press buttons on cooldown in endgame content, but this is likely to change before launch"* — so neither the endgame rotation nor whether Arms or Fury wins can be stated yet. Raids open 9 December.
