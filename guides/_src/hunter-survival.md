The durable Hunter, parry, a defensive cooldown, and traps that are genuinely hard to play against. The published advice is **not to level it if damage or speed is what you want** (see below).
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Survival build has been tested. Survival's melee payoff is `Strider Kick`, the 21-point gold medal talent. 21 points fit inside the level-30 budget, so it is in-beta content rather than something out of reach. The sources disagree about when it starts to matter. Wowhead's spell data attaches level 32 to `Strider Kick` and `Expose Prey`. Icy Veins says only that you will "need to play as a ranged DPS for at least the first 40 levels if not more." **Blizzard's own caveat applies to everything on this page:** *"class abilities and talents may continue to evolve throughout the beta test and are subject to additional changes."*

## What Survival is for
**Defences and control the other two trees do not carry.** `Deflection` 5/5 gives +5% base parry, a stat Hunters otherwise never get. [[Deterrence]] at 20 is +25% dodge and parry for 10 seconds on a 5-minute cooldown. `Entrapment` makes every trap you trigger root what it catches, which Blizzard confirms is a Forever redesign: a chance became a guarantee, with the duration scaling instead. `Survivalist's Discipline` cuts the cooldown of your traps and [[Deterrence]] together.
Deeper in, `Strider Kick` at 21 points is an instant weapon-damage attack that also grants +30% movement speed for 3 seconds.
The sources place that kit in PvP. `Deflection` plus [[Deterrence]] gives you 5% base parry and an on-use defensive cooldown. Icy Veins calls that *"extremely strong in PvP against other melee"*, the matchup a Hunter normally fears most. The October 1 build then halved Deflection's parry, which was 10%, so read that verdict against the stronger pre-patch number. Add `Entrapment` and your traps become, in the source's words, *"exceptionally more annoying to deal with."*
The published verdict comes with a condition attached: *"you should not go Survival if all you care about is damage or leveling quickly"*, and Beast Mastery or Marksmanship *"both offer far better damage talents at low levels."*
**Our own reading, which no source states:** that is a statement about solo kill speed, not about whether the spec works. In a group, where something else is holding the target, the durability costs you very little.
If solo speed is what you care about, the published advice above points to Beast Mastery, with a respec to Survival when you want to duel. **Our own reading, not a source's:** if you level in groups, play whichever you enjoy, because the gap is small.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11**, a durability build rather than a damage one.
1. **`Deflection`, 5 points**: +5% base parry, 1% per rank
2. **`Entrapment`, 3 points**: every trap you own gets worse to be caught in
3. **[[Deterrence]]**, and the remaining points toward it
There is no published level-20 Survival damage build, because no source recommends one.
## Rotation
**Ranged is correct at level 20 regardless of your talents.** Icy Veins says you will *"need to play as a ranged DPS for at least the first 40 levels if not more."*
1. **[[Aspect of the Hawk]]** up; [[Aspect of the Cheetah]] when kiting
2. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull
3. **Send the pet in first**, abilities on auto-cast
4. **[[Serpent Sting]]** once the pet has the target
5. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown
6. **[[Auto Shot]]** as filler
7. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull aggro; **[[Arcane Shot]]** with spare mana
**If you insist on melee**, there is a published rotation for it, and it needs [[Aspect of the Beast]] rather than Hawk:
1. **[[Hunter's Mark]]**, then send the pet
2. **[[Serpent Sting]]**
3. **Close to melee and [[Raptor Strike]]** on cooldown
4. **[[Disengage]]** if you take the target off your pet
Below level 10, walk backwards between shots to stay out of the dead zone.
## Stat priority
**Weapon damage, then Agility**, then item level, the same as the other two specs. The slower weapon still wins, because [[Aimed Shot]] scales off damage per hit. Agility does double duty here: it feeds dodge alongside attack power.

## Survival talent changes, Classic to Forever
Beyond the level-20 build above, Blizzard's own changelog for the tree:
- `Improved Tracking`, new, combines the old `Monster Slaying` and `Humanoid Slaying` into one talent that increases damage to whatever creature type you are currently tracking
- `Savage Strikes` redesigned: now +2/4% to **all** melee damage, instead of +10/20% to Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite specifically. The client has not caught up here: the in-game tooltip still reads a 20% melee critical strike chance, so believe Blizzard
- **`Survivalist` moved from row 3 up to row 2**
- `Improved Wing Clip` now reaches its maximum 20% chance in 3 points instead of more
- `Surefooted` moved from row 4 up to row 3; its hit-chance bonus now applies to all attacks, and it reduces the duration of movement-impairing effects instead of just reducing their chance to land on you
- **`Survival Tactics`** (formerly `Trap Mastery`) now also increases [[Feign Death]]'s chance to hit, in addition to Traps
- `Predator's Edge`, new, increases melee critical strike damage and boosts off-hand damage while dual-wielding
- `Counterattack` moved from row 5 up to row 4, and is now the 16-point gold medal talent
- `Resourcefulness`, new, reduces the Mana cost of all Traps and melee abilities, and gives your critical strikes a chance to regenerate Mana from Spirit while casting
- **`Survivalist's Discipline`, new, reduces the cooldown of Traps and [[Deterrence]]**
- `Lightning Reflexes`'s Agility bonus is smaller, to make room for the new talent powers above
- **Removed:** `Wyvern Sting`, `Killer Instinct` and `Improved Feign Death` are combined into the new `Resourcefulness`; `Monster Slaying` and `Humanoid Slaying` are combined into the new `Improved Tracking`
## What Forever changed for Hunters
- **Pets lost their per-family damage/armour modifiers.** Icy Veins is explicit: *"Pets no longer have an inherent damage buff or reduction based on type."* What each family carries instead is one unique ability on top of a wider shared kit. Blizzard confirms the mechanism: pets now get increased stats from your gear, and can no longer receive player buffs that increase their stats.
- **`Improved Serpent Sting` became `Improved Stings`** (Marksmanship, moved from row 4 up to row 2). Build lists naming the old talent cannot be entered as printed.
- **Survival's melee toolkit is new and deep in the tree.** `Strider Kick` is the 21-point gold medal talent, an instant weapon-damage attack. `Expose Prey` turns your own attacks into [[Mongoose Bite]] procs off a marked target. `Lacerating Strikes` is the new 31-point gold medal talent: Mongoose Bite also bleeds the target over 21 seconds, for damage based on the strength of the bite that applied it. None of the three is in the level-20 build, but 21 points reach `Strider Kick` inside the current cap — see the scope box above.
- The utility kit is intact: [[Feign Death]], [[Freezing Trap]], [[Frost Trap]], [[Concussive Shot]], [[Disengage]]. Survival is the spec built around it.
## At level 60
Unknown, and this is the spec with the most riding on it: whether Forever intends Survival as a melee Hunter, a trap-and-control Hunter, or a ranged spec with a defensive tree bolted on. Nothing published tests that. Icy Veins reads the talent kit (bleeds off marked targets, real melee crit and offhand support) as arguing for melee as the intended destination, just not the levelling path.
