The Hunter that fights in melee, or in and out of it, with the trap talents alongside. It plays as a ranged Hunter until its melee talents arrive at level 30.

## What Survival is for
**A melee rotation the other two trees do not have.** `Strider Kick` at 21 points is an instant attack for 100% weapon damage on an 8-second cooldown that also grants +30% movement speed for 3 seconds. `Expose Prey` gives your attacks on a [[Hunter's Mark]]ed target a 10% chance at 2/2 to open [[Mongoose Bite]], which otherwise needs a dodge. `Predator's Edge` raises melee ability crit damage and off-hand damage. `Lacerating Strikes` at 31 points makes Mongoose Bite bleed for 40% of its damage over 21 seconds.
**The speed from `Strider Kick` is what makes weaving work**: kick, then run back out to shoot. Icy Veins: learning to weave [[Raptor Strike]] and `Strider Kick` *"will make a significant difference in your kill times,"* at the cost of Mana and of pulling threat off your pet when a melee ability crits.
**The trap and defence talents sit beside it.** `Entrapment` makes every trap you trigger root what it catches, 1 second per rank. `Clever Traps` lengthens the Frost traps and raises Fire trap damage. `Survivalist's Discipline` cuts the cooldown of traps and [[Deterrence]] by 40% at 2/2. `Deflection` gives up to +5% parry, and [[Deterrence]] is +25% dodge and parry for 10 seconds on a 5-minute cooldown.
**The sources agree the first 30 levels are the weak part.** Icy Veins: *"you should not go Survival if all you care about is damage or leveling quickly,"* and Beast Mastery or Marksmanship *"both offer far better damage talents at low levels."* Wowhead recommends playing Beast Mastery until level 32. Both describe solo kill speed before the melee talents exist. Icy Veins adds that fully built at level 30, melee *"will just start to surpass ranged damage"* on a single target.

- **`Lethal Attacks` first**, five Marksmanship points for +5% crit, before anything in Survival. That delays `Strider Kick` to level 35.
- **`Improved Tracking`** is +5% damage at 5/5 to the creature type you are tracking. Track what you are fighting.
- **`Surefooted`** is +3% hit at 3/3.
- **After 40** the same page goes on to `Lightning Reflexes` 5/5 and `Lacerating Strikes` by 45, then Marksmanship.
**Level 30, to reach `Strider Kick` on time: 0/0/21**, Icy Veins' level 30 build from its Survival guide, page dated November 9, 2026. `Improved Tracking` 5 · `Savage Strikes` 2 · `Entrapment` 3 · `Surefooted` 3 · `Clever Traps` 2 · `Predator's Edge` 5 · `Strider Kick` 1.

- **The weaving build takes `Lone Wolf`**, which pays only with no pet out. It uses a two-handed weapon, which Wowhead gives as the reason `Lacerating Strikes` earns its point there: a bigger [[Mongoose Bite]] makes a bigger bleed.
- **The melee build keeps the pet** and reaches `Summon Hawk`. Wowhead rates `Lacerating Strikes` poorly for it and takes it anyway as the 31st point.
- **WoWSims' default Survival preset is the weave**, 0/20/31 with `Deflection` 3 where Wowhead has `Survivalist` 3 *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Defensive points are yours to trade in** *(ours, not a source's)*. `Entrapment`, [[Deterrence]] and `Counterattack` appear in Wowhead's PvP build, not in any published PvE one.
## Rotation
**Before the melee talents, play it ranged.** Icy Veins says you will *"need to play as a ranged DPS for at least the first 40 levels if not more."*
1. **[[Aspect of the Hawk]]** up; [[Aspect of the Cheetah]] when kiting
2. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull
3. **Send the pet in first**, abilities on auto-cast
4. **[[Serpent Sting]]** once the pet has the target
5. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown, or [[Multi-Shot]] on two or more
6. **[[Auto Shot]]** as filler
7. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull aggro; **[[Arcane Shot]]** with spare Mana
**Weaving, from `Strider Kick`.** Icy Veins' levelling list:
1. **[[Hunter's Mark]]**, send the pet, **[[Serpent Sting]]**, **[[Arcane Shot]]**
2. **Queue [[Raptor Strike]]** and run in to land it
3. **[[Mongoose Bite]]** if it is lit
4. **`Strider Kick`**, and use its speed to run back out
5. **[[Arcane Shot]]** again at range, or [[Multi-Shot]] on several targets
**Full melee at 60.** Wowhead's priority, in [[Aspect of the Beast]]:
1. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** kept on the target
2. **[[Raptor Strike]]** always queued
3. **[[Mongoose Bite]]**
4. **`Strider Kick`**
5. **[[Immolation Trap]]**
6. **`Summon Hawk`** when talented
Wowhead's weaving rotation for 60 is marked work in progress. Below level 10, kite backwards between shots to stay out of the dead zone.
**What decides each press:**
- **Stand still for Auto Shot.** Blizzard's notes say Auto Shot needs 0.5 seconds standing still before it fires, and that it keeps firing while you cast [[Aimed Shot]]. So the cast costs you no Auto Shots and walking does.
- **When to walk in.** WoWSims' weave walks in once [[Auto Shot]] has fired and back out once the melee swing has landed, so neither timer is wasted *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Raptor Strike]] goes off on your next main-hand swing**, in place of that swing, so press it as you close rather than on arrival *(WoWSims' model)*. Its cooldown is 6 seconds.
- **[[Serpent Sting]] or [[Arcane Shot]].** The sting is the better use of Mana if the target lives. Rank 3 costs 50 Mana and deals 70 over 15 seconds, against 50 for Arcane Shot at level 20. Rank 4 costs 80 and deals 130, against 85 at level 28. Each tick of the sting adds 5% of attack power, 25% in all, against 15% for Arcane Shot. The sting passes Arcane Shot on its fourth tick, at 12 seconds. Since November 10 Arcane Shot costs 11% of base Mana at every rank.
- **[[Immolation Trap]] is cheap damage.** Traps work in combat on a 30-second cooldown that every trap shares, so an [[Immolation Trap]] down means no [[Freezing Trap]] for 30 seconds. Rank 1 burns for 105 over 15 seconds for 50 Mana, and rank 2 at 26 for 215 for 90. It burns the first enemy to reach it, so in melee drop it at your feet. `Clever Traps` adds to it and `Resourcefulness` cuts trap and melee costs.
- **Melee before level 30.** [[Aspect of the Beast]] is trained at 30, so before that melee has no Attack Power Aspect. [[Mongoose Bite]] can only follow a dodge, and [[Aspect of the Monkey]] adds dodge, which makes it the natural stand-in *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **[[Hunter's Mark]] in melee.** It raises Ranged Attack Power only, so it adds nothing to [[Raptor Strike]]. It pays for the shots, and for `Expose Prey`, whose [[Mongoose Bite]] procs need the Mark on the target.
- **`Lacerating Strikes` replaces itself.** Each new bleed overwrites your old one on the target, even a weaker one, per Blizzard's notes.
- **Mana.** Icy Veins warns that weaving is Mana-hungry. `Resourcefulness` at 2/2 cuts trap and melee costs by 60%, and a crit from a melee or ranged ability lets half your Mana regeneration run while casting for 30 seconds. Traps never trigger that part, per Blizzard's notes.
- **Threat.** A melee crit can pull the target off your pet. [[Disengage]] drops threat, and [[Feign Death]] from 30 clears it.
## Stat priority
**While levelling: weapon damage, then Agility.** Icy Veins asks for a slow bow and a slow two-hander, or a slow main hand and fast off hand if you dual wield, with Strength and Intellect as secondary gains.
**At 60 both sites list hit, then crit, then Agility.** Wowhead says 9% hit for abilities and labels its list speculative. Icy Veins says 8%, and notes `Surefooted` supplies 3 of it. Dual wielding wants more hit than that for its white swings, on both sites' account.

## Survival talent changes, Classic to Forever
Blizzard's own changelog for the tree:
- `Improved Tracking`, new, combines the old `Monster Slaying` and `Humanoid Slaying` into one talent that increases damage to whatever creature type you are currently tracking
- `Savage Strikes` redesigned, from +10/20% on Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite to a smaller bonus on all melee (see the table above)
- **`Survivalist` moved from row 3 up to row 2**
- `Improved Wing Clip` now reaches its maximum 20% chance in 3 points instead of more
- `Surefooted` moved from row 4 up to row 3; its hit-chance bonus now applies to all attacks, and it reduces the duration of movement-impairing effects instead of just reducing their chance to land on you
- **`Survival Tactics`** (formerly `Trap Mastery`) now also increases [[Feign Death]]'s chance to hit, in addition to Traps
- `Predator's Edge`, new, increases the critical strike damage of your melee abilities, not auto attacks, and boosts off-hand damage while dual-wielding
- `Counterattack` moved from row 5 up to row 4, and is now the 16-point gold medal talent
- `Resourcefulness`, new, reduces the Mana cost of all Traps and melee abilities, and gives your critical strikes a chance to regenerate Mana from Spirit while casting
- **`Survivalist's Discipline`, new, reduces the cooldown of Traps and [[Deterrence]]**
- `Lightning Reflexes` keeps Classic's Agility bonus, 3/6/9/12/15% by rank
- **Removed:** Wyvern Sting, `Killer Instinct` and `Improved Feign Death` are combined into the new `Resourcefulness`; `Monster Slaying` and `Humanoid Slaying` are combined into the new `Improved Tracking`
## What Forever changed for Hunters
- **Pets scale their stats off your gear**, and can no longer receive player buffs that increase their stats. On November 10 Blizzard set pet Health to 115% of normal, down from a bugged 150%. Icy Veins' Survival levelling page recommends a Cat or Raptor with [[Claw]]; the client's family table is on the [Hunter overview](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/#pet-families).
- **`Improved Serpent Sting` became `Improved Stings`** (Marksmanship, moved from row 4 up to row 2). Build lists naming the old talent cannot be entered as printed.
- **Survival's melee toolkit is new**: `Strider Kick`, `Expose Prey` and `Lacerating Strikes`, with `Predator's Edge` and `Savage Strikes` behind them.
- **Traps work in combat**, one Fire and one Frost trap out at a time, all on one 30-second cooldown.
- The utility kit is intact: [[Feign Death]], [[Freezing Trap]], [[Frost Trap]], [[Concussive Shot]], [[Disengage]].
## At level 60
Three ways to play it are published or modelled: Wowhead's full melee build, Wowhead's weaving build, and a ranged list in WoWSims beside its melee and weave ones *(WoWSims' model)*. No raid opens until December 9, so none has a raid result behind it. Icy Veins' view of dungeons is that melee Survival has little area damage beyond [[Explosive Trap]], and that its trap control is where it helps a group: [[Freezing Trap]] and [[Frost Trap]] with `Entrapment` on top.
