## What Fire is for
**[[Pyroblast]] becomes a spell you cast inside a fight.** `Heating Up` cuts its cast time by 25% per stack, so 3 stacks turn 6 seconds into 1.5. Non-periodic Fire crits build stacks, and every [[Fire Blast]] adds one. It arrives at 25; before that there is *"no reliable way to use Pyroblast other than a pre-pull cast."* Below 25 Fire is [[Fireball]], [[Fire Blast]] and [[Pyroblast]] on the pull.
**Icy Veins rates Fire 2.5/5 for levelling**, under the heading *"Usable, But Better Options Are Available"*; Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish no levelling score.
It names the reason: *"the Fire spec relies a lot on scoring Critical Strikes... at low levels, your Critical Strike chance is not very high and you only gain high levels of Critical Strike chance through gear at or near the Level 60 content."* The payoff talents sit deep as well: *"the bread-and-butter talents that make Fire work very well are deep down in the Fire and Frost talent trees."* Those tiers are levels 25 through 40.
**Forever also nerfed Fire's baseline damage.** [[Fireball]] rank 12 hits for 425–541 where Classic gave 596–760; [[Scorch]] and [[Pyroblast]] were cut similarly. The talents that pay that damage back sit deeper in the tree *(ours)*.
Fire is not written off, though: *"The AoE leveling potential of Fire is also respectable."* One published guide carries no level-20 Fire build at all.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/11/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** We adjusted the published list because one of its row 1 talents moved to row 3, which put [[Pyroblast]] out of reach at 20.
1. `Accelerant`, 5 points
2. `Ignite`, 5 points
3. **[[Pyroblast]]** at 20
`Incineration` 5 can replace `Ignite` at the same gate if you would rather lift [[Fire Blast]] and [[Scorch]] crit *(ours)*. Icy Veins warns that *"on low levels we have such a low chance for a Critical Strike, the talent barely adds any dps."*
**Level 30: 0/21/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, per-level: `Accelerant` 5 (10–14), `Ignite` 5 (15–19), [[Pyroblast]] 1 (20), `Incineration` 3 (21–23), `Burning Soul` 1 (24). Then `Heating Up` 1 (25), `Improved Scorch` 3 (26–28), `Burning Soul` 1 more (29), `Critical Mass` 1 (30). The [[Pyroblast]] point is not optional in any Fire build that wants `Heating Up`: Blizzard made it the prerequisite. An alternative for the two `Burning Soul` slots, in the same row, is `Improved Fire Blast`, since every [[Fire Blast]] feeds `Heating Up` *(ours)*.
## Rotation
1. **[[Frost Armor]]** and [[Arcane Intellect]] up, or [[Mage Armor]] from 34 if mana is tight
2. **Open with [[Pyroblast]]** ([[Fireball]] before 20) to land the damage-over-time component on the pull
3. **[[Fireball]]** as filler
4. **[[Fire Blast]] to finish** anything non-trivial
5. **Wand heavily** at low level rather than spending mana
From 25, use [[Fire Blast]] on cooldown to build stacks: *"Use `Hot Streak` [now `Heating Up`] as soon as it reaches 3 stacks"*, and keep the `Improved Scorch` stack up on long fights.
**Multi-target.** Tanked: [[Flamestrike]], then [[Blast Wave]] (if you took it at 30), then [[Arcane Explosion]]. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Flamestrike]] plus [[Cone of Cold]], then kite with [[Arcane Explosion]] and [[Blast Wave]].
## Stat priority
Icy Veins is the only one of the three tracked sources that ranks Fire's stats; its order:
1. **Hit** — *"your best stat until the cap"*, though low-level gear rarely carries much of it
2. **Spell Power (Fire)** — *"the most sought-after stat"*
3. **Critical strike** — ranked above Spirit and Intellect here, the same spot Frost gives it: *"a great damage increase for Fire Mages"* because `Ignite`, `Heating Up` and `Combustion` all trigger off it
4. **Spirit** — reduces downtime between fights more than it looks
5. **Intellect** — a bigger mana pool and a small crit bonus
6. **Stamina** — comes along for free on most gear
7. **MP5** — lowest priority; usually a healer stat
Below 60, crit is limited by what the gear offers rather than by where it ranks.

## What Forever changed for Fire
- **`Heating Up` is new and it changes the spec** *(ours)*. Blizzard renamed it from `Hot Streak`, *"since it is no longer dependent on having a 'streak'."* Its deep dive calls it *"Forever's variation on the ideas found in the Hot Streak talent"*, and other guide sites may still use the older name. The buff lasts 20 seconds, so a 15 printed elsewhere is out of date. It is a *reduction* rather than an instant-cast proc: *"this is not a two-crits-then-instant-Pyroblast rule."*
- **`Incineration`** (renamed from Incinerate) sits in row 1 with 5 ranks, up to 10% crit, and buffs [[Fire Blast]], [[Ice Lance]], [[Arcane Blast]] and [[Scorch]], deliberately cross-school.
- [[Combustion]] ends after 3 non-periodic Fire crits, so treat any "4 charges" claim as out of date. [[Blast Wave]] no longer requires [[Pyroblast]] and now dazes for 50%. `Impact` has 3 ranks, where Classic had 5, and like `Arcane Concentration` it now procs less from low-rank casts. `Burning Soul` takes 3 points for the effect 2 used to buy. `Accelerant` (renamed from `Improved Fireball`) also cuts the cast time of [[Frostfire Bolt]], which matters from 40, and of [[Arcane Wrath]].
- **`Fire Power` was redesigned** to 3 ranks: 1/2/3% more Fire damage per Fire effect of yours on the target, up to 12%. `Critical Mass` has 3 ranks, up to 6%.
- Whether Mage area spells keep a **target cap or falloff** in Forever is unconfirmed. None of the three tracked sources states it either way, and neither does Blizzard's deep dive. Do not build a farming plan on an assumption in either direction.
## At level 60
Nothing above 30 was played in the beta. [[Combustion]] is the 31-point capstone at level 40. Icy Veins ties the spec to a critical strike rate *"you only gain… through gear at or near the Level 60 content"*, which nobody has yet.
