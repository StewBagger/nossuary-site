## What Fire is for
**[[Pyroblast]] becomes a spell you cast inside a fight.** `Heating Up` cuts its cast time by 25% per stack, so 3 stacks turn 6 seconds into 1.5. Non-periodic Fire crits build stacks, and every [[Fire Blast]] adds one. It arrives at 25 at the earliest. Below that Fire is [[Fireball]], [[Fire Blast]] and [[Pyroblast]] on the pull.
**Icy Veins rates Fire 3/5 for levelling**, under the heading *"Unreliable and Fun"*; the score measures solo kill speed, and Wowhead and ClassicWoW.gg publish none. Its reason is mana: without `Arcane Meditation`, and before [[Mage Armor]] at 34, *"you will be stuck drinking after nearly every mob."* Its answer is to start in the Arcane tree and move to Fire later.
Wowhead's levelling guide calls Fire *"a good but not amazing leveling specialization"* and lists what it lacks: Frost's control, Arcane's mana talents, and area damage. What it has is range and single-target damage that needs no ramp.
**Forever also cut Fire's baseline damage.** [[Fireball]] rank 12 hits for 425–541 where Classic gave 596–760; [[Scorch]] and [[Pyroblast]] were cut similarly, and spell damage counts for more to compensate. Wowhead draws the conclusion: Fire *"requires a lot of spell power to get back to its normal baseline Classic WoW damage"*, so it feels weak until you have a good weapon or dungeon gear.
**In dungeons** Icy Veins scores it 4.5/5 and warns about threat: a run of crits with `Ignite` *"can quickly overtake a Tank in threat."* It still calls Fire's area levelling respectable: *"The AoE leveling potential of Fire is also respectable."*
## Your talent points
Two sources publish a levelling build from 10 to 60, and they are different routes. Wowhead starts in Fire. Icy Veins levels in Arcane to 29 and only then moves to Fire. Icy Veins prints its order point by point. Wowhead shows its build as an interactive calculator and describes it in words.
**Wowhead's Fire-first route (page dated November 4).** The build itself is a calculator on [Wowhead's levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/fire/dps-leveling-tips); its text gives no split and no levels. In its words, and in its order: `Accelerant` *"as soon as possible"*; `Improved Fire Blast` and `Incineration` ready by the time you have both `Heating Up` and [[Pyroblast]]; then the Arcane tree for `Improved Channeling`, `Missile Barrage` and `Arcane Subtlety`; then *"pretty much all the critical strike and passive damage talents you can get"*; and last the mana talents, `Arcane Concentration` and `Arcane Meditation`.
**Level 60, Icy Veins' Arcane-first order, 18/31/2 (page dated November 4):** `Wand Specialization` 2 (10–11), `Improved Channeling` 3 (12–14), `Missile Barrage` 2 (15–16), `Arcane Concentration` 3 (17–19), [[Arcane Blast]] 1 (20), `Precision` 2 (21–22), `Improved Channeling` to 5/5 (23–24), `Arcane Concentration` to 5/5 (25–26), `Arcane Meditation` 3 (27–29), `Incineration` 5 (30–34), `Accelerant` 5 (35–39), `Improved Fire Blast` 2 (40–41), `Ignite` 2 (42–43), [[Pyroblast]] 1 (44), `Heating Up` 1 (45), `Ignite` to 5/5 (46–48), `Improved Scorch` 1 (49), [[Blast Wave]] 1 (50), `Critical Mass` 3 (51–53), `Improved Scorch` to 2/3 (54), `Fire Power` 3 (55–57), `Improved Scorch` to 3/3 (58), `Master of Elements` 1 (59), [[Combustion]] 1 (60).
- **The choice between them is Fire talents first or mana first.** Wowhead's route takes the Fire talents up to `Heating Up` before anything in Arcane and leaves the mana talents to the end. Icy Veins' has the mana talents by 29 and no `Heating Up` until 45.
- **Wowhead says its route can be rearranged**: take `Arcane Concentration` and `Arcane Meditation` straight after `Arcane Subtlety` if mana is the problem.
- **The [[Pyroblast]] point is not optional** in any build that wants `Heating Up`: Blizzard made it the prerequisite.
- **Both stop at 4% hit.** Two points of `Precision` remove the miss chance against a same-level target, on both sites' figures.
- **`Improved Fire Blast` matters twice.** It takes 2 seconds off the cooldown that feeds `Heating Up`, and its 15 yards bring [[Fire Blast]] out to the range of your other Fire spells.
## Rotation
1. **[[Frost Armor]]** and [[Arcane Intellect]] up, or [[Mage Armor]] from 34 if mana is tight
2. **Open with [[Pyroblast]]** ([[Fireball]] before you have it) to land the damage-over-time component on the pull
3. **[[Fireball]]** as filler
4. **[[Fire Blast]] to finish** anything non-trivial
5. **Wand heavily** at low level rather than spending mana
Once you have `Heating Up`, both guide sites give the same top of the list: [[Pyroblast]] at 3 stacks, and [[Fire Blast]] on cooldown to build them. If you took `Missile Barrage`, a proc goes on [[Arcane Missiles]] next.
- **Hold [[Pyroblast]] for 3 stacks while the target has health to spare.** A cast spends every stack unless [[Presence of Mind]] made it instant, so a Pyroblast at 1 stack throws them away. At 25 the hit does about 90 damage per second of casting at 3 stacks and 23 at none, where a [[Fireball]] with `Accelerant` 5 does 40. That gap is why an uncut Pyroblast belongs on the pull.
- **Two stacks will do on a dying target.** That is a 3-second cast, the same as a [[Fireball]] with `Accelerant` 5, and Pyroblast's hit is the larger at every level from 20 to 60.
- **[[Fire Blast]] is the stack source you can count on.** It adds one even without a crit, so three in a row take 16 seconds, or 12 with `Improved Fire Blast` 2/2, inside the 20-second buff. That holds on any fight long enough, whatever your crit.
- **[[Fireball]] unless [[Fire Blast]] is about to come back.** WoWSims casts Fireball only when Fire Blast will stay on cooldown through the cast, and [[Scorch]] otherwise *(WoWSims' model)*. A 1.5-second Scorch cannot hold Fire Blast back the way a 3-second Fireball can. Icy Veins' levelling list uses [[Scorch]] as the filler outright, and both sites switch to short casts or the wand as the enemy gets close or low.
- **Build the `Improved Scorch` stack when a fight will outlast its cost.** Five Scorches followed by Fireballs catch up with Fireballs alone after 8 to 15 seconds, across levels 28 to 50 with no spell damage *(ours, not a source's)*. Wowhead's levelling guide sets the bar higher: only on enemies that live 20 to 30 seconds, such as dungeon bosses, and only once you have both `Improved Scorch` and `Fire Power`. The stack lasts 30 seconds on that target; WoWSims refreshes it with under 5 left *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **From 40, [[Frostfire Bolt]] is an opener, not a filler, in a Fire build.** Icy Veins' levelling list opens with it. WoWSims' Fire list keeps [[Fireball]], and only its Fire and Frost hybrid fills with Frostfire Bolt *(WoWSims' model)*.
**Multi-target.** Tanked: [[Flamestrike]] first, then [[Blast Wave]] if you took it, then [[Arcane Explosion]]. Solo: gather, [[Frost Nova]], [[Flamestrike]], then [[Blast Wave]] or [[Cone of Cold]], then [[Arcane Explosion]]. Icy Veins adds two cautions: do not use both slows back to back, or the pack reaches you with both on cooldown, and do not spend the mana on fewer than three enemies. Wowhead rates Fire's area damage as a weakness while levelling and says spamming [[Arcane Explosion]] is the best option most of the time.
**Spells to keep trained past 30:** [[Fireball]] and [[Pyroblast]] at 30, 36, 42, 48, 54 and 60; [[Fire Blast]] at 30, 38, 46 and 54; [[Scorch]] at 34, 40, 46, 52 and 58; [[Flamestrike]] at 32, 40, 48 and 56; [[Frostfire Bolt]] at 40, 50 and 60.
## Stat priority
Both sites that rank Fire's stats do so for level 60, and both call their lists provisional.
1. **Hit**, to 17% against a raid boss. Icy Veins gives 4% as the figure against a same-level target
2. **Spell Power**, including Fire damage
3. **Critical strike**: `Ignite`, `Heating Up` and [[Combustion]] all run on it
4. **Intellect**. Wowhead places Spell Piercing and Haste above it, and says Spell Piercing's worth at 60 is not yet confirmed
5. **Spirit**
6. **MP5** (Icy Veins) or **Stamina** (Wowhead) last
Below 60, crit is limited by what the gear offers rather than by where it ranks. Icy Veins' levelling guide argues for Spirit on the way up if you have `Arcane Meditation`: time not spent drinking is worth more than a little spell damage. That is its judgement, not a measured figure.

## What Forever changed for Fire
- **`Heating Up` is new and it changes the spec** *(ours)*. Blizzard renamed it from Hot Streak, *"since it is no longer dependent on having a 'streak'."* Its deep dive calls it *"Forever's variation on the ideas found in the Hot Streak talent"*, and older pages may still use that name. The buff lasts 20 seconds, so a 15 printed elsewhere is out of date. It is a *reduction* rather than an instant-cast proc: *"this is not a two-crits-then-instant-Pyroblast rule."*
- **`Incineration`** (renamed from Incinerate) sits in row 1 with 5 ranks, up to 10% crit, and buffs [[Fire Blast]], [[Ice Lance]], [[Arcane Blast]] and [[Scorch]], deliberately cross-school. Its tooltip still reads 6%; Blizzard's November 2 notes give the 10%.
- [[Combustion]] ends after 3 non-periodic Fire crits, so treat any "4 charges" claim as out of date. [[Blast Wave]] no longer requires [[Pyroblast]] and now dazes for 50%. `Impact` has 3 ranks, where Classic had 5, and procs at its full chance from any rank you cast. `Burning Soul` takes 3 points for the effect 2 used to buy. `Accelerant` (renamed from Improved Fireball) also cuts the cast time of [[Frostfire Bolt]], which matters from 40, and of [[Arcane Wrath]].
- **`Fire Power` was redesigned** to 3 ranks: 1/2/3% more Fire damage per Fire effect of yours on the target, up to 12%. `Critical Mass` has 3 ranks, up to 6%.
- On a **target cap or falloff** for Mage area spells, Blizzard has said nothing. Icy Veins' Frost levelling guide (November 10) states that none of the Mage's area damage falls off with target count; no other source confirms it. Do not build a farming plan on it alone.
## At level 60
Two builds are published, and they play differently.
**Level 60, the Combustion build, 16/32/3:** `Improved Channeling` 5, `Arcane Subtlety` 2, `Arcane Concentration` 5, `Missile Barrage` 2, [[Arcane Blast]] 1, `Arcane Meditation` 1, `Incineration` 5, `Accelerant` 5, `Ignite` 5, [[Pyroblast]] 1, `Improved Fire Blast` 2, `Improved Scorch` 3, `Heating Up` 1, `Master of Elements` 3, `Critical Mass` 3, `Fire Power` 3, [[Combustion]] 1, `Precision` 3.
Wowhead (November 4), Icy Veins and WoWSims' preset all publish this same build. Icy Veins' priority for it:
1. Open with **[[Pyroblast]]**, then **[[Scorch]]** to 5 stacks of `Improved Scorch`, and refresh it inside its 30 seconds
2. **[[Pyroblast]]** whenever `Heating Up` reaches 3 stacks, even mid-way through the Scorches
3. **[[Fire Blast]]** on cooldown
4. Keep **one [[Arcane Blast]] stack** running for its spell damage. Every spell you cast benefits
5. **[[Arcane Missiles]]** on a `Missile Barrage` proc
6. **[[Fireball]]** as filler
WoWSims' Fire list runs much the same order and holds [[Combustion]] and other cooldowns until the `Improved Scorch` stack is at 5 *(WoWSims' model)*.
**Level 60, the Frostfire Bolt build, 0/29/22 (Wowhead's):** `Incineration` 5, `Accelerant` 5, `Ignite` 5, [[Pyroblast]] 1, `Improved Fire Blast` 2, `Improved Scorch` 3, `Heating Up` 1, `Master of Elements` 1, `Critical Mass` 3, `Fire Power` 3, `Frost Warding` 2, `Precision` 3, `Ice Shards` 5, `Frost Channeling` 3, `Improved Blizzard` 1, [[Ice Lance]] 1, `Arctic Reach` 1, [[Ice Block]] 1, `Shatter` 3, `Fingers of Frost` 2.
It gives up [[Combustion]] for `Shatter` and `Fingers of Frost`, which [[Frostfire Bolt]] can use because it counts as a Frost spell. Icy Veins' version moves two of the Frost points. The list is the same as above with [[Frostfire Bolt]] as the filler and no Arcane spells. Blizzard's own example of the idea: with `Ignite` and `Ice Shards` together, a Frostfire Bolt crit is worth 280% of a normal hit.
- **A `Fingers of Frost` proc carries two charges.** Icy Veins' advice is to press an instant spell, [[Fire Blast]] for choice, just before the second charged cast finishes, so that three spells land as if the target were frozen. WoWSims' list holds [[Fire Blast]] back while two charges are up *(WoWSims' model)*.
- Wowhead calls this build *"far more locked in"*, with few points to move. If it runs out of mana, its suggestion is to move 2 points from `Incineration` to `Master of Elements`.
No raid is open before December 9, so neither build has a raid log behind it.
