## Is Beast Mastery worth levelling?
**Yes, and nothing beats it.** The published verdict on taming a pet at level 10 is blunt: *"the single greatest leveling tool that any class gets without exception."*
The reason Beast Mastery specifically is the answer at low level is `Deadly Aspects` — *"one of the best 5 point talents you can take"* — and the fact that the tree's other early points are movement speed and pet reliability, which is exactly what levelling wants.
**One thing to learn immediately: the dead zone.** Below level 10, walk backwards between shots. You want the maximum number of ranged attacks in before the enemy closes.
## Your talent points
**Level 20 — 10/1/0 or 11/0/0.** Hunter is the one class with no single obvious level-20 capstone, so the published advice is genuinely flexible.
1. **`Deadly Aspects`, 5 points** — this is the outlier. It procs a **30% haste buff for 12 seconds**
2. **`Pathfinding`, 2 points** — +6% movement speed. Not a damage talent, and the published reasoning is honest about that: *"the extra 6% movement speed is going to be so much more noticeable than anything else while you run around the world without a mount"*
3. **The last 4 points are subjective.** Named options, with what each is for:
   - `Improved Revive Pet` — makes bringing your pet back reliable **in combat**. The safe pick
   - `Focused Fire` — *"the strongest DPS talent available at this point"*
   - `Lethal Attacks` — *"a close second"*
   - `Hawk Eye` — extra range, for kiting and PvP
   - `Bestial Swiftness` — if all 10 go into Beast Mastery, your pet closes on targets far faster
## Rotation
The honest version first: **early Hunter is mostly Auto Shot**, and that is by design.
1. **[[Aspect of the Hawk]]** up before you engage — unless you are kiting, in which case [[Aspect of the Cheetah]]
2. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull
3. **Send the pet in first**, abilities on auto-cast
4. Once the pet has the target's attention, **[[Serpent Sting]]**
5. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown
6. **[[Auto Shot]]** the rest of the time
7. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull the target off your pet
8. **[[Arcane Shot]]** only with mana to spare
**Multi-target:** [[Multi-Shot]] in place of [[Aimed Shot]].
**The mana rule that matters most:** *"Even if you are out of mana, it will always be more efficient to just attack the next target and regenerate your mana slowly while using basic attacks."* Spend what mana you have on [[Mend Pet]] and [[Serpent Sting]] first.
## Stat priority
**Weapon damage, then Agility** — and past that, take the higher item level.
Weapon damage is *"far and away your most important stat"*, and there is a subtlety worth knowing: **prefer the slower weapon.** [[Aimed Shot]] calculates from your weapon's damage per hit, not its DPS, so a slow high-damage bow makes your abilities hit harder. Hit is strong but barely exists on gear at this level.
## Your pet
Forever changed pets more than it changed the Hunter.
- **Every pet is Ferocity, Cunning or Tenacity** now, and **type no longer carries an inherent damage bonus or penalty.** Pick for the abilities, not the label
- **Pets inherit some of your stats**, so they scale as you gear up
- Happiness and loyalty still exist — feed your pet
- **Each family has a unique ability**, and you still learn ranks by taming a higher-level pet that knows them. Once learned, you can teach it to any future pet that can use it
**The early recommendation is a Bear**, for one specific reason: its **Swipe** is a 5-second-cooldown frontal cleave, and *"one of the rare pet abilities capable of dealing consistent AoE damage to hold aggro on multiple targets."*

## What Forever changed for Hunters
- **Pet classifications replaced the old type bonuses** (above), and **pets scale off your stats.**
- **`Improved Serpent Sting` was removed from the client** and Blizzard did not note it. Stale build lists still spend points on it — those builds cannot be entered as printed.
- The utility kit is intact and still the class's signature: [[Feign Death]] drops threat completely (*"one of the most valuable leveling spells"* — though enemies will keep hitting your pet), [[Freezing Trap]], [[Frost Trap]], [[Concussive Shot]], and [[Disengage]] as a threat dump rather than a leap.
