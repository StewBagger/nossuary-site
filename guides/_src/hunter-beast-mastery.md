## What Beast Mastery is for
**The pet tanks and hits, and the tree makes both better.** `Focused Fire` raises your damage and your pet's together while the pet is out. `Unleashed Fury` and `Ferocity` raise pet and hawk damage and crit. `Endurance Training` gives the pet Health and Armor in one talent.
`Deadly Aspects` gives [[Auto Shot]] a 10% chance at 5/5 of +30% ranged attack speed for 12 seconds, **and only while [[Aspect of the Hawk]] is up.** Those figures are the client's. Icy Veins calls it *"one of the strongest Hunter talents anywhere on the tree."* The talent has a melee half too, off [[Aspect of the Beast]].
`Summon Hawk` at 16 points adds a second and third attacker. `Intimidation` at 21 is a 3-second pet stun on a 1-minute cooldown that also gives the pet's next attack +100% crit chance. `Bestial Wrath` at 31 points, level 40, gives the pet +50% damage for 18 seconds on a 2-minute cooldown and makes it immune to crowd control.
Icy Veins' verdict on taming a pet at level 10: *"the single greatest leveling tool that any class gets, without exception."* Wowhead notes the first solo level 60 on launch realms was a Beast Mastery Hunter using the pet to tank.
**Learn the dead zone immediately.** Below level 10, kite backwards between shots. You want as many ranged attacks in as possible before the enemy closes.

What the order is doing:
- **`Summon Hawk` lands at 25**, the first level it can. Icy Veins calls it *"the single most important point you will want to rush for."*
- **`Pathfinding` is a travel talent.** It adds 6% to [[Aspect of the Cheetah]] and [[Aspect of the Pack]] at 2/2 and nothing in combat. Wowhead treats it, `Improved Revive Pet` and `Endurance Training` as interchangeable for the points that open rows 2 and 3.
- **`Improved Revive Pet`** cuts the cast by 6 seconds and the Mana cost by 40% at 2/2, which is what makes a revive possible mid-fight.
- **`Frenzy` needs `Ferocity` 5/5, and `Bestial Wrath` needs `Intimidation`.** `Intimidation` in turn needs `Bestial Swiftness`.
- **After 31 points the sources agree on Marksmanship.** `Lethal Attacks` is +5% crit with all attacks, and `Careful Aim` turns Intellect into Attack Power.

## Rotation
**Early Hunter is mostly Auto Shot**, and that is by design. Icy Veins' levelling priority:
1. **[[Aspect of the Hawk]]** up before you engage, unless you are kiting, in which case [[Aspect of the Cheetah]]
2. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull
3. **Send the pet in first**, abilities on auto-cast
4. Once the pet has the target's attention, **[[Serpent Sting]]**
5. **`Summon Hawk`** on cooldown from level 25, keeping two hawks out
6. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown
7. **[[Auto Shot]]** the rest of the time
8. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull the target off your pet
9. **[[Arcane Shot]]** only when Mana is full and both hawks have more than 6 seconds left
**Multi-target:** [[Multi-Shot]] in place of [[Aimed Shot]], an [[Explosive Trap]] from level 34, and [[Volley]] from 40 on large groups. Both sites list those three as the Hunter's only area tools.
**Cooldowns:** [[Rapid Fire]] from 26 and `Bestial Wrath` from 40. Wowhead says to use both whenever they are up. Icy Veins suggests holding them for elites while levelling.
**At 60** Wowhead and Icy Veins print the same single-target list: [[Hunter's Mark]], [[Serpent Sting]] kept up, `Summon Hawk`, [[Aimed Shot]]. They differ on [[Arcane Shot]]: Wowhead gives it every third press of the shared cooldown, Icy Veins only when both hawks have more than 6 seconds left.
**What decides each press:**
- **Stand still for Auto Shot.** Blizzard's notes say Auto Shot needs 0.5 seconds standing still before it fires, and that it keeps firing while you cast [[Aimed Shot]]. So the cast costs you no Auto Shots and walking does. When kiting, walk between shots and stop before the next one is due.
- **[[Serpent Sting]] or [[Arcane Shot]].** The sting is the better use of Mana if the target lives. Rank 3 costs 50 Mana and deals 70 over 15 seconds, against 50 for Arcane Shot at level 20. Rank 4 costs 80 and deals 130, against 85 at level 28. All before attack power, which widens the gap: each tick of the sting adds 5% of it, 25% over the full sting, against 15% for Arcane Shot. Those shares are Blizzard's November 2 figures, and WoWSims reports level 34 and 36 combat logs fit both. The sting ticks every 3 seconds and passes Arcane Shot on its fourth tick, at 12 seconds. On a target with less time than that, skip it.
- **[[Arcane Shot]] costs 11% of base Mana at every rank.** Since November 10 the cost follows your level, not the rank. `Summon Hawk` costs the same 11%.
- **[[Multi-Shot]] or [[Aimed Shot]].** They share a 6-second cooldown, so you get one or the other. Aimed Shot is your weapon hit plus a flat bonus: 40 at rank 1, 70 at rank 2, up to 350 at rank 6. Multi-Shot is the weapon hit with no bonus, on up to three targets. Each extra target adds a whole weapon hit, so two targets beat Aimed Shot once your weapon hit is larger than that bonus.
- **`Summon Hawk` or [[Arcane Shot]].** The shared cooldown is 6 seconds and a hawk lasts 18, with two up at most, so two presses in every three keep both hawks out and the third is free. The dive alone is the weaker hit: 32 plus 5% of ranged attack power at rank 1. A hawk's worth is in the 18 seconds after it. On a target about to die, press Arcane Shot.
- **The hawks were retuned on November 10.** Blizzard raised hawk melee damage by about 80%. WoWSims had dropped the hawks from its level 60 Beast Mastery list on November 3, when the buffed Arcane Shot beat them. After the hotfix it re-read the hawk from level 48 combat logs and put the loop back: 912.6 DPS with hawks against 886.4 without *(WoWSims' model)*. Its list casts [[Serpent Sting]] first, then hawk, hawk, Arcane Shot, then [[Aimed Shot]].
- **Keep the pet alive and go back to Hawk.** `Focused Fire` works only while your pet is active, so a dead pet costs your damage as well as its own. `Deadly Aspects` rolls only on [[Auto Shot]] in [[Aspect of the Hawk]], so return to Hawk when the kite ends.
- **[[Volley]] since November 11.** It no longer triggers procs and it stops if you move. In WoWSims' level 34 and 36 logs each of its six waves hit every enemy in the area, 14 in one wave, and no Auto Shot fired during the channel *(WoWSims' reading of the logs)*.
- **Mana.** Most of your damage is Auto Shot and the pet, which cost nothing. Icy Veins' rule is to scale back abilities until you break even, or carry water. `Bestial Discipline` lets half your Mana regeneration continue while casting.
## Stat priority
**While levelling: weapon damage, then Agility**, and past that, take the higher item level.
Weapon damage is *"far and away your most important stat"* (Icy Veins), and **the slower weapon is better.** [[Aimed Shot]] calculates from your weapon's damage per hit, not its DPS.
**At 60 the two sites agree on the order and disagree on the hit cap.** Both list hit, then crit, then Agility, then Intellect, then Attack Power. Wowhead says 9% hit and labels its list speculative. Icy Veins says 8%. Intellect counts because `Careful Aim` turns it into Attack Power one for one at 5/5.
## Your pet
- **Pets get increased stats from your own gear**, and Blizzard confirms that is now the only way to buff them. Pets can no longer receive player buffs that increase their stats.
- **Pet Health was tuned on November 10.** Blizzard found pets at 150% of normal Health by a bug and set them to 115%, aiming for Classic Era's durability.
- **Family still matters for damage, on the client's reading.** Icy Veins says pets have no inherent damage difference by type. The client's family table, Wowhead and WoWSims all say Cats and Raptors deal 10% more and Bears 9% less. The table is on the [Hunter overview](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/#pet-families).
- **Which pet.** Icy Veins recommends a Bear for [[Swipe]], a 5-second-cooldown frontal cleave and *"one of the rare pet abilities capable of dealing consistent AoE damage to hold aggro on multiple targets."* Wowhead recommends a Cat or Raptor for damage, or a Carrion Bird for `Demoralizing Screech`. WoWSims' presets use a Raptor *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **The pet's attack power is set when you call it.** In WoWSims' level 34 to 36 logs the pet's attack power held through 20 [[Aspect of the Hawk]] toggles and a gear swap, so it reads the inherited attack power and Health as taken at the summon *(WoWSims' reading of the logs)*. Call the pet in the gear you fight in.
- **[[Rapid Fire]] speeds the pet up too.** The same logs show the pet swinging 40% faster under it, and faster again with its own `Frenzy`. Ranged-only haste does not reach the pet *(WoWSims' reading of the logs)*.
- Happiness and loyalty still exist. Feed your pet: Wowhead notes an unhappy pet deals less damage.
- **Ability ranks are taught, not inherited.** Tame a beast that already knows the rank, use it until the chat line says you learned it, take your own pet back, and teach it with Beast Training. Wowhead suggests new ranks of [[Claw]] and [[Bite]] about every 10 levels. [[Tame Beast]] does not work on beasts above your own level.
- **Every family can now learn either [[Bite]] or [[Claw]]** (some both), and either [[Dive]] or [[Dash]], on top of one ability unique to that family.
- **Aggressive Mode is back**, in the pet tab of your spellbook.
- **Threat.** Send the pet first and give it a couple of attacks before you shoot. On several targets you out-threaten it, which is the case for a Bear, or for fewer targets.

## Beast Mastery talent changes, Classic to Forever
Blizzard's own changelog for the tree:
- `Deadly Aspects` (formerly `Improved Aspect of the Hawk`) now also carries a chance for melee attacks to grant increased attack speed while [[Aspect of the Beast]] is active
- `Endurance Training` absorbed the old `Thick Hide` talent, so it now grants your pet both Health and Armor in one talent
- `Focused Fire`, new, increases all damage you **and** your active pet deal
- `Aspect of the Monkey` now grants twice the Dodge, and your pet gains half the benefit of it
- `Pathfinding` sits on row 3, where it sat in Classic, and Blizzard's November 2 notes confirm that row
- `Unleashed Fury`'s pet-damage bonus is slightly smaller, but now also benefits a summoned Hawk
- `Improved Mend Pet` now also cuts Mend Pet's Mana cost
- `Ferocity`'s pet critical-strike bonus is slightly smaller, but now also benefits a summoned Hawk
- `Intimidation` now requires `Bestial Swiftness` first, and now also grants your pet +100% critical strike chance on its next attack
- `Bestial Discipline` now also regenerates Mana from Spirit while you cast
- **Removed:** `Improved Eyes of the Beast` is a baseline ability now, and `Thick Hide` no longer exists as its own talent
## What Forever changed for Hunters
- **Pets scale off the Hunter's gear**, and every family gained a wider shared kit and exactly one unique ability.
- **`Summon Hawk` is new**: a Beast Mastery talent, not a baseline ability, and it competes with [[Arcane Shot]] for the same cooldown rather than stacking with it.
- **`Improved Serpent Sting` became `Improved Stings`** (Marksmanship, moved from row 4 up to row 2). It carries a larger Serpent Sting damage increase than the talent it replaced, cuts the [[Viper Sting]] cooldown and lengthens [[Scorpid Sting]].
- The utility kit is intact. [[Feign Death]] at 30 drops you out of the fight, *"one of the most valuable leveling spells"* (Icy Veins), though enemies keep hitting your pet. **Cancel it: left running its full 6 minutes it actually kills you.** The rest: [[Freezing Trap]], [[Frost Trap]], [[Concussive Shot]], and [[Disengage]] as a threat dump.
## At level 60
The build and the rotation above are published. What is not yet known is how they hold in a raid: none opens until December 9. Both sites' level 60 builds predate the November 10 hawk and pet tuning, and only WoWSims has re-run since *(WoWSims' model)*. In a dungeon your pet is not the tank: keep it on the tank's target, and use [[Feign Death]] when you pull threat.
