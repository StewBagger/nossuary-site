## What Subtlety is for
**Openers, bought early.** `Opportunity` in row 1 adds 4% per rank, 20% at 5/5, to [[Backstab]], [[Garrote]], [[Ambush]] and [[Mutilate]]. `Improved Ambush` and `Camouflage` sit in row 2, and `Premeditation` in row 3, all within reach of an 11-point build. `Ghostly Strike` lands at 16 points, so level 25, for 125% weapon damage plus 15% dodge for 7 seconds, and your next [[Ambush]] or [[Garrote]] inside those 7 seconds needs no [[Stealth]]. It is non-positional: the one builder here that does not ask you to be behind the target.
Icy Veins rates it **the lowest Rogue spec overall**, 2.5 out of 5 Overall against 3.0 for the other two, while rating all three the same 4 out of 5 for levelling. Its heading is *"Weakest Among Rogue Specs"*: *"Subtlety gets the short end of the stick, with most of its higher-impact talents being unavailable until you can put more talent points into the tree."* Wowhead agrees in stronger language, calling the spec *"wholly underwhelming at this level bracket"*. It notes the Rogue lacks *"our primary builder `Hemorrhage`"* and the talents that support [[Rupture]] this early.
The main problem is positional: *"One of the biggest issues the spec has is its positional requirement… you won't be able to do so very often when leveling solo."*
**The top of the tree runs on `Shank`**, trained at 24. `Boot Knife` in row 6, level 35, gives it a 20% chance per rank, 100% at 5/5, to add 2 combo points. `Death Mark`, the 31-point talent at 40, is a finisher that stores part of your damage and deals it 6 seconds later.
## Your talent points
Icy Veins built a **pure-Subtlety level 20 build** around `Camouflage`, `Improved Ambush` and `Ghostly Strike` for the beta's tree. It no longer fits: `Ghostly Strike` needs 15 points in the tree. Its chain-pulling flex is `Remorseless Attacks`, because *"this gives you close to 100% Crit Chance with Ambush."* For the levels before [[Ambush]] at 18, Icy Veins borrows `Improved Sinister Strike`, now `Efficient Strikes`, from Combat and uses a slow main-hand.
Wowhead published **two level 20 builds** for the beta's tree, and both still fit. Its Backstab Build is the same dagger and `Puncturing Wounds` allocation it publishes on the Assassination and Combat pages: Backstab as primary builder, 3 points into `Puncturing Wounds`, some into `Improved Sinister Strike` "solely for when you inevitably pull aggro." Its Ambush Build spends points on `Improved Ambush` instead, for *"a strong opener every time you're able to get back into Stealth."*
**Level 20: 0/0/11 — our own extension, not a published build.** `Opportunity` 5 → `Improved Ambush` 3 → `Camouflage` 2 → `Premeditation` 1 at 20, for 2 combo points on the target that last 20 seconds.
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** Extending the level 20 line, no respec needed: `Opportunity` 5 → `Improved Ambush` 3 → `Camouflage` 2 → `Premeditation` 1 → `Initiative` 3 → `Serrated Blades` 1 → `Ghostly Strike` 1 at 25 → `Serrated Blades` 3/3 → `Camouflage` 3/3 → `Dirty Deeds` 1 → `Hemorrhage` at 30. `Setup` is left out: at 15% per rank it adds a combo point only after you dodge or fully resist.
## Rotation
1. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]: this requires a dagger
2. Weapon-swap macro to a slow main hand, or stay on the dagger
3. Build with [[Backstab]] behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]. From 25, **`Ghostly Strike` is your non-positional builder**, and the [[Ambush]] it unlocks without [[Stealth]] is a second opener mid-fight
4. [[Slice and Dice]] on durable targets, [[Eviscerate]] otherwise. *"[[Rupture]] is rarely used at lower levels."*
5. **Dagger-only alternative:** [[Gouge]] then [[Backstab]], but wait for near-maximum energy first
**Once `Hemorrhage` is reachable:** use it to build, then [[Rupture]] immediately on long fights. Gaunerh picks the finisher by the target's Health: [[Rupture]] if it will live through the bleed, [[Eviscerate]] to finish it off. That `Hemorrhage` and `Serrated Blades` both raise [[Rupture]] damage is in the client; the Health cut-off is his judgement.
**Multi-target: none.** Wowhead: *"No AoE capabilities whatsoever."*
**What changes the answer:**
- **[[Backstab]] or [[Sinister Strike]].** With the same dagger in hand, Backstab deals more per Energy at every level, even against the 40-Energy [[Sinister Strike]] that `Efficient Strikes` gives you, because its 150% also multiplies its flat bonus. It costs more per combo point, 60 Energy against 45, and it needs you behind the target.
- **`Ghostly Strike` cannot be your only builder.** It costs 40 Energy and has a 20-second cooldown. At 60, WoWSims' list presses it ahead of every other builder whenever it is ready *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Hemorrhage` is the cheap point, and its debuff only serves your bleeds.** 35 Energy, 30 with `Efficient Strikes` from Combat, no cooldown, no need to be behind the target. The target takes +15% from your [[Rupture]] and [[Garrote]] for 15 seconds, and a 5-point [[Rupture]] lasts 16. At 60, WoWSims' list casts [[Rupture]] on 3 or more points with the debuff up, without waiting for 5, and does not recast it while it is running *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Premeditation` is lost if you sit on it.** 2 points on a 2-minute cooldown, gone after 20 seconds unless you add to them or spend them. You hold 5 at most, so WoWSims' list presses it only at 3 points or fewer *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Boot Knife`, from 35, makes `Shank` a builder.** At 5/5, each `Shank` adds 2 combo points for 5 Energy, on a 10-second cooldown.
- **`Death Mark`, from 40, wants bleeds running.** 25 Energy, 20 yards, a 10-second cooldown: it stores part of the damage your attacks deal for 6 seconds, more per combo point, and [[Garrote]] and [[Rupture]] damage at twice the rate. The stored total is capped, and the cap rises with Attack Power and critical strike chance.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage **favouring a slow main hand**, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, attack power, critical strike, Strength.
This spec's list differs from the other two. The published example shows why: *"Between a 15 DPS weapon that has a 1.9 speed, and a 14 DPS weapon with a 2.8 speed, the 2.8 speed one will serve you better."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- `Hemorrhage` is no longer a **shared physical-damage debuff**. It is a personal [[Rupture]] amplifier now, and does nothing for the rest of your group. Do not take a raid assignment on it.
- `Improved Ambush` sits in **row 2**, which is why an 11-point Subtlety build can reach it at all. `Setup` sits in row 1 at 15% per rank, 45% at 3/3, and only awards its combo point when the target you dodged or resisted a spell from is also your current target. `Initiative` reaches 100% at 3 points. `Camouflage` is 3 ranks in row 2, for up to 15% Stealth speed and 6 seconds off its cooldown. `Opportunity` is 5 ranks for up to 20%. `Elusiveness` sits in row 4 and `Heightened Senses` in row 5. `Master of Deception` costs 3 points instead of 5 for the same effect.
- **Using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**, so the opener it sets up is still yours, and it cannot set off weapon procs. It is still a Stealth ability. Blizzard lists it with [[Pick Pocket]], [[Distract]] and [[Garrote]] as what Rogues do from Stealth, and wants it used as *"a crowd control benefit they can provide to parties and raids."*
- `Dirty Tricks` is the redesigned `Improved Sap`, moved up to row 2: it now takes a large bite out of the Energy cost of [[Sap]] and [[Blind]].
- **Genuinely new:** `Quick Getaway` in row 4, 2 ranks: one more target can be Sapped at once, and you move 15% faster per rank for 3 seconds after a [[Sap]]. `Boot Knife` in row 6 and `Death Mark` in row 7, both above. Removed outright: `Sleight of Hand` and `Deadliness`.
- `Dirty Deeds`, in row 2, allows **[[Garrote]] from the front**, a real help while levelling *(ours, not a source's)*. `Serrated Blades` ignores a percentage of the target's armour now, where the flat value it replaces rose with your level.
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are secondary skills now**, not class skills, and poison stacks with a sharpening stone. Rogues can learn axes, starting at skill 1.
- Hit and critical strike are unified across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether the `Death Mark` and `Hemorrhage` bleed cycle works as a raid specialisation; no player has used `Death Mark`. It and [[Mutilate]] are each 31-point talents, 62 points together against Forever's 51-point budget, so **the two cannot coexist in one build.**
