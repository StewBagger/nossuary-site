## What Subtlety is for
**Openers, bought early, and from 30 a builder that does not care where you stand.** `Opportunity` in row 1 adds 4% per rank, 20% at 5/5, to [[Backstab]], [[Garrote]], [[Ambush]] and [[Mutilate]]. `Improved Ambush` in row 2 adds up to 45% crit to [[Ambush]]. `Ghostly Strike` at 16 points, level 25, deals 125% weapon damage, gives 15% dodge for 7 seconds, and lets your next [[Ambush]] or [[Garrote]] inside those 7 seconds skip [[Stealth]]. [[Hemorrhage]] at 21 points, level 30, costs 35 Energy from any side with any weapon, and makes the target take 15% more from your [[Rupture]] and [[Garrote]] for 15 seconds.
**The top of the tree runs on [[Shank]].** `Boot Knife` in row 6 gives it a 20% chance per rank, 100% at 5/5, to add 2 combo points. `Death Mark`, the 31-point talent, is a finisher that stores part of your damage for 6 seconds and then deals it.
**Icy Veins, updated November 7,** says Forever's talent changes *"position it as one of the most fun and high burst specs in the game, even in PvE"*, and that it struggles a little until 30. Its named problem is position: before [[Hemorrhage]] the damage is built around [[Backstab]], and *"you won't be able to do so very often when leveling solo."* Wowhead's levelling page, updated November 6, calls Subtlety *"a very burst-focused class while leveling"*, built on an [[Ambush]] that almost always crits.

## Rotation
**While levelling**, Icy Veins' list:
1. From 20, [[Instant Poison]] on both weapons
2. From 18, open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]], which needs a dagger in the main hand. In a group, let the tank get threat first
3. Before 30, build with [[Sinister Strike]] from the front, or [[Gouge]] and step behind for a [[Backstab]]
4. From 24 and 25, [[Shank]] and `Ghostly Strike` on cooldown. Against a durable enemy, follow `Ghostly Strike` with [[Garrote]]
5. [[Slice and Dice]] is the finisher, unless the target is almost dead
6. From 30, **[[Hemorrhage]] is always your builder**, with any weapon, and [[Rupture]] becomes the finisher unless the target is close to dying
**The weapon swap.** Before [[Hemorrhage]], Icy Veins opens with a dagger and swaps to a slow main hand for [[Sinister Strike]] with a macro. It notes that a swap costs a global cooldown and resets your swing timer.
**At 60, Icy Veins' priority, updated November 4:**
1. [[Instant Poison]] on the main hand, [[Deadly Poison]] on the off hand
2. [[Rupture]] up at all times, cast at 5 combo points
3. `Death Mark` as often as possible, at 5 combo points
4. [[Garrote]] up if the target will survive it
5. [[Slice and Dice]] as much as possible, at 5 combo points
6. [[Shank]] on cooldown at 3 combo points or fewer, so `Boot Knife`'s 2 points are not wasted
7. `Ghostly Strike` on cooldown, followed by [[Garrote]], which gives 2 points with `Initiative`
8. `Premeditation` on cooldown, also 2 points
9. [[Hemorrhage]] to build; [[Ambush]] in the opener
**Multi-target: none.** Icy Veins: pick the most dangerous or highest-Health target and kill it while others clean up the rest. With spare Energy you can put a second [[Rupture]] on another target.
**What changes the answer:**
- **`Death Mark` at 4 or at 5.** Icy Veins casts it at 5 combo points. WoWSims' list casts it at 4 or more, on every cooldown, and never casts [[Eviscerate]] once `Death Mark` is known *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Death Mark` wants bleeds running.** 25 Energy, 20 yards, a 10-second cooldown. It stores 12% of your damage per combo point for 6 seconds, and [[Garrote]] and [[Rupture]] damage at twice that rate. The stored total is capped, and the cap rises with Attack Power and critical strike chance. Icy Veins' advice is to pool Energy before each one.
- **[[Rupture]] early.** WoWSims' list casts it on 3 or more points whenever it is not running, without waiting for 5 *(WoWSims' model)*. Icy Veins waits for 5.
- **`Ghostly Strike` is how you re-apply [[Garrote]].** It costs 40 Energy on a 20-second cooldown. WoWSims' list holds it until [[Garrote]] has 4 seconds or less left *(WoWSims' model)*; Icy Veins presses it on cooldown.
- **[[Hemorrhage]]'s debuff only serves your bleeds.** 35 Energy, 30 with `Efficient Strikes`. A 5-point [[Rupture]] lasts 16 seconds, 22 with `Blood Splatter`, against a 15-second debuff, so keep building with it.
- **`Premeditation` is lost if you sit on it.** 2 points on a 2-minute cooldown, gone after 20 seconds unless you add to them or spend them. WoWSims' list presses it at 3 points or fewer *(WoWSims' model)*; Icy Veins says on cooldown, without overcapping.
- **Combo points are easy to waste here.** [[Garrote]], [[Ambush]], `Premeditation` and [[Shank]] can each give 2. Icy Veins lists using them at 4 points as a common mistake.
## Stat priority
**While levelling**, from Icy Veins: weapon damage **favouring a slow main hand**, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, Attack Power, critical strike, Strength. Its example: *"Between a 15 DPS weapon that has a 1.9 speed, and a 14 DPS weapon with a 2.8 speed, the 2.8 speed one will serve you better."* The Wowhead and WoWSims level 60 build accepts any weapon type; a [[Backstab]] build needs a dagger in the main hand.
**At 60 the two sites order the top differently, and both say it is early.**
- Icy Veins: weapon damage, Expertise to 6.5%, Hit to 8%, weapon skill, Agility.
- Wowhead, which calls its own list *"purely speculative for endgame"*: Hit to 8%, Expertise to 6.5%, weapon skill, then Hit on to 17% for poisons, Agility, critical strike.
- WoWSims' stored weights put Expertise ahead of Hit, then Agility, critical strike and Haste. They were not retuned after the November 3 talent rework *(WoWSims' model)*.
Icy Veins adds that enemies cannot parry you from behind, so stay behind the boss.

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- [[Hemorrhage]] is no longer a **shared physical-damage debuff**. It raises your own [[Rupture]] and [[Garrote]] damage, and does nothing for the rest of your group. Do not take a raid assignment on it.
- `Improved Ambush` sits in **row 2**. `Setup` sits in row 1 at 15% per rank, 45% at 3/3. `Initiative` reaches 100% at 3 points. `Camouflage` is 3 ranks in row 2, for up to 15% Stealth speed and 6 seconds off its cooldown. `Opportunity` is 5 ranks for up to 20%. `Master of Deception` costs 3 points.
- **Using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**, so the opener it sets up is still yours. Blizzard wants it used as *"a crowd control benefit they can provide to parties and raids."*
- `Dirty Tricks`, in row 2, takes 25% per rank off the Energy cost of [[Sap]] and [[Blind]]. `Quick Getaway` in row 4 needs it: 2 ranks, one more target can be Sapped at once, and you move 15% faster per rank for 3 seconds after a [[Sap]].
- `Dirty Deeds`, in row 2, allows **[[Garrote]] from the front** and takes up to 20 Energy off [[Cheap Shot]] and [[Garrote]]. `Serrated Blades` ignores 3% of armour per rank and adds 10% [[Rupture]] damage per rank.
- **New in Forever:** [[Shank]] at 24, `Poised` at 18, `Quick Getaway`, `Boot Knife` and `Death Mark`.
- **Poisons are a secondary skill**, and a poison stacks with a sharpening stone.
## At level 60
**In a group you are single-target damage and control.** Icy Veins, updated November 7, describes the dungeon job as [[Sap]] before the pull, [[Distract]] on patrols, and [[Kick]], [[Gouge]] and [[Kidney Shot]] during it, and notes the trade: Energy spent on control is Energy not spent on damage. Wowhead lists bleed-immune enemies as this spec's specific weakness.
**`Death Mark` and [[Mutilate]] cannot share a build.** Each is a 31-point talent, 62 points together against a 51-point budget.
**What is not known yet:** how the bleed cycle performs on a raid boss, and whether `Death Mark` is better at 4 points or 5. Those wait for logs, and the first raids open on December 9.
