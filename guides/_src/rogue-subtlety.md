Stealth openers and a [[Rupture]]-driven bleed cycle. **Icy Veins' lowest-rated Rogue spec overall**, and it has no area damage of any kind.

Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Subtlety build has been tested. ## What Subtlety is for
**Mobility and stealth, bought early.** `Camouflage` sits in row 1 now, shortening the [[Stealth]] cooldown and raising move speed. `Improved Ambush` strengthens the opener from row 2, within reach of an 11-point build. `Ghostly Strike` lands at exactly level 20 for 125% weapon damage, 180% with a dagger, plus 15% dodge for 7 seconds. It is non-positional: the one builder here that does not ask you to be behind the target.
Icy Veins rates it **the lowest Rogue spec overall**, 2.5 out of 5 Overall against 3.0 for the other two, while rating all three the same 4 out of 5 for levelling. Its heading is *"Weakest Among Rogue Specs"*: *"Subtlety gets the short end of the stick, with most of its higher-impact talents being unavailable until you can put more talent points into the tree."* Wowhead agrees in stronger language, calling the spec *"wholly underwhelming at this level bracket"*. It notes the Rogue lacks *"our primary builder `Hemorrhage`"* and the talents that support [[Rupture]] this early.
The main problem is positional: *"One of the biggest issues the spec has is its positional requirement… you won't be able to do so very often when leveling solo."*
Blizzard's stated intent is **two builds in this tree**, one on [[Sinister Strike]] and one on [[Backstab]]: the new row 6 talents `Quietus` and `Cutthroat` serve them in turn. Both are level 35, so neither helps while you level.
## Your talent points
Icy Veins publishes a **pure-Subtlety build**, around `Camouflage`, `Improved Ambush` and `Ghostly Strike`. It names an explicit flex: if you are chain-pulling, drop `Ghostly Strike` and `Setup` for `Remorseless Attacks`, because *"this gives you close to 100% Crit Chance with Ambush."* The build does not come together until [[Ambush]] at 18. For the levels before that, Icy Veins borrows `Improved Sinister Strike` from Combat and uses a slow main-hand.
Wowhead publishes **two different level 20 builds**, and neither is Subtlety-flavoured in the way Icy Veins' is. Its Backstab Build is the same dagger and `Puncturing Wounds` allocation it publishes on the Assassination and Combat pages: Backstab as primary builder, 3 points into `Puncturing Wounds`, some into `Improved Sinister Strike` "solely for when you inevitably pull aggro." Its Ambush Build spends points on `Improved Ambush` instead, for *"a strong opener every time you're able to get back into Stealth."*
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build.** Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs: `Opportunity` 2 → `Camouflage` 3 → `Setup` 3 → `Improved Ambush` 3 → `Ghostly Strike` 1 → `Initiative` 3 → `Serrated Blades` 3 → `Camouflage` 5/5 → `Hemorrhage` at 30. `Premeditation` is a live option in that range too: Blizzard moved it from the 31-point capstone to the 16-point gold medal, so level 25, for 2 combo points on the target that last 20 seconds.
## Rotation
1. Open from [[Stealth]] with [[Ambush]]: this requires a dagger
2. Weapon-swap macro to a slow main hand, or stay on the dagger
3. Build with [[Backstab]] behind the target, otherwise [[Sinister Strike]]. **`Ghostly Strike` is your non-positional builder**
4. [[Slice and Dice]] on durable targets, [[Eviscerate]] otherwise. *"[[Rupture]] is rarely used at lower levels."*
5. **Dagger-only alternative:** [[Gouge]] then [[Backstab]], but wait for near-maximum energy first
**Once `Hemorrhage` is reachable:** use it to build, then [[Rupture]] immediately on long fights.
**Multi-target: none.** Wowhead: *"No AoE capabilities whatsoever."*
## Stat priority
Weapon damage **favouring a slow main hand**, then Hit and Expertise, Agility, attack power, critical strike, Strength.
This spec's list differs from the other two. The published example shows why: *"Between a 15 DPS weapon that has a 1.9 speed, and a 14 DPS weapon with a 2.8 speed, the 2.8 speed one will serve you better."*

## What Forever changed for Rogues
- `Hemorrhage` is no longer a **shared physical-damage debuff**. It is a personal [[Rupture]] amplifier now, and does nothing for the rest of your group. Do not take a raid assignment on it.
- `Improved Ambush` moved from row 3 to **row 2**, which is why an 11-point Subtlety build can reach it at all. `Heightened Senses` moved up to row 4. Blizzard does not say `Setup` moved, but Icy Veins' 11-point build takes it, which row 4 would not allow. `Setup` and `Initiative` now both reach 100% at 3 points. `Setup` only awards its combo point when the target you dodged or resisted a spell from is also your current target (October 1 build), and levelling solo is single-target anyway. `Master of Deception` costs 3 points instead of 5 for the same effect.
- **Using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**, so the opener it sets up is still yours. It is still a Stealth ability. Blizzard lists it with [[Pick Pocket]], [[Distract]] and [[Garrote]] as what Rogues do from Stealth, and wants it used as *"a crowd control benefit they can provide to parties and raids."*
- `Dirty Tricks` is the redesigned `Improved Sap`, moved up to row 2: it now takes a large bite out of the Energy cost of [[Sap]] and [[Blind]].
- **Genuinely new:** `Improved Distract` in row 3, which widens [[Distract]] and deepens the stealth-detection penalty on anything it hits. `Quietus` sits at row 6, needs `Dirty Deeds`, and makes [[Sinister Strike]], `Ghostly Strike` and `Hemorrhage` hit harder below 35% health. `Thousand Cuts` needs level 40, so nobody has used it. Removed outright: `Sleight of Hand` and `Deadliness`.
- `Dirty Deeds` now allows **[[Garrote]] from the front**, worth more while levelling than anything else in its row. `Serrated Blades` ignores a percentage of the target's armour now, where the flat value it replaces rose with your level.
- **Poisons and Lockpicking are secondary skills now**, not class skills, and poison stacks with a sharpening stone. Rogues can learn axes, starting at skill 1.
- Hit and critical strike are unified across spell, melee and ranged.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether the `Thousand Cuts` and `Hemorrhage` bleed cycle works as a raid specialisation. Blizzard describes `Thousand Cuts` as knocking 3 Energy off your next `Hemorrhage` or [[Backstab]] per [[Rupture]] tick, stacking five times. ClassicWoW.gg puts `Thousand Cuts` at 31 Subtlety points minimum, since it requires `Preparation`, and [[Mutilate]] at 21 Assassination points. Together that is 52, one more than Forever's 51-point budget, so **the two cannot coexist in one build.**
