## What Demonology is for
**A demon that carries part of the fight.** `Unholy Power` raises all its damage by 10%. `Demonic Energies` heals it for 15% of the spell damage you deal and passes it all the Mana from your [[Life Tap]]. `Fel Vitality` gives it 15% more Health and Mana and gives you 15% more Mana.
**It comes together at 25 to 27 with `Demonic Brand`.** [[Searing Pain]] brands the target for 10 seconds, and your demon's next attacks on it deal extra damage, two per talent point. Wowhead: until then Demonology *"plays like a weaker Affliction"*, and from then on the demon does a large share of the damage and holds threat well.
**At 40, `Demonic Pact` lets you keep a [[Demonic Sacrifice]] bonus with a different demon out.** Sacrifice the Imp for +15% Shadow damage, then summon the Succubus or Incubus.
**[[Corruption]] keeps its 2-second cast** in both published orders until the Affliction points arrive in the 40s.
Icy Veins rates the spec 5 out of 5 for levelling and 4 for dungeons (November 9). A levelling score measures solo kill speed and nothing else. Wowhead's costs: the early talents do little before 25, and losing your demon costs its damage, [[Soul Link]] and the time to summon it again.
## Your talent points
Both guide sites publish a full 1 to 60 order, and they match closely to level 40.
**Level 60, Wowhead's order (November 6): 20/31/0.** `Unholy Power` 5 (levels 10 to 14) → `Demonic Energies` 2 (15, 16) → `Fel Vitality` 3 (17 to 19) → `Master Summoner` 2 (20, 21) → `Improved Sayaad` 3 (22 to 24) → `Demonic Brand` 3 (25 to 27) → [[Demonic Sacrifice]] 1 (28) → [[Fel Domination]] 1 (29) → [[Soul Link]] 1 (30) → `Demonic Knowledge` 3 (31 to 33) → `Demonic Embrace` 1 (34) → `Master Demonologist` 5 (35 to 39) → `Demonic Pact` 1 (40) → `Improved Corruption` 5 (41 to 45) → `Improved Life Tap` 2 (46, 47) → `Suppression` 5 (48 to 52) → `Improved Drains` 3 (53 to 55) → `Pandemic` 3 (56 to 58) → `Amplify Curse` 1 (59) → `Malevolence` 1 (60).
That order is read from the talent embed on [Wowhead's levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/dps-leveling-tips). The page's own text confirms `Demonic Brand` at 25 to 27 and `Demonic Pact` at 40. Wowhead says to take the three `Demonic Brand` points in a row. For instant [[Corruption]] early it offers a detour: `Improved Corruption` at 10 to 14, then a respec at 25 into this build.
**Level 60, Icy Veins' order (November 4): 20/31/0.** `Unholy Power` 5 (levels 10 to 14) → `Demonic Energies` 2 (15, 16) → `Fel Vitality` 3 (17 to 19) → `Master Summoner` 2 (20, 21) → `Improved Voidwalker` 3 (22 to 24) → `Demonic Brand` 3 (25 to 27) → [[Fel Domination]] 1 (28) → [[Demonic Sacrifice]] 1 (29) → [[Soul Link]] 1 (30) → `Demonic Knowledge` 3 (31 to 33) → `Demonic Aegis` 1 (34) → `Master Demonologist` 5 (35 to 39) → `Demonic Pact` 1 (40) → `Suppression` 4 (41 to 44) → `Improved Corruption` 5 (45 to 49) → `Improved Life Tap` 1 (50) → `Improved Bane of Agony` 2 (51, 52) → `Pandemic` 3 (53 to 55) → `Malevolence` 5 (56 to 60).
**Where they differ.** At 22 to 24 Wowhead takes `Improved Sayaad` for the Succubus and Icy Veins takes `Improved Voidwalker`. That is the choice of which demon you level with. Wowhead notes that `Improved Sayaad` raises only `Lash of Pain`, not the demon's whole damage, and that `Improved Voidwalker` is for players who keep the Voidwalker out. After 40 Wowhead goes to instant [[Corruption]] first and Icy Veins to hit.
**[[Soul Link]] requires [[Demonic Sacrifice]], and `Demonic Pact` requires [[Soul Link]] and 30 points in the tree.** Both orders spend exactly 31 in Demonology, so none of those points can move.
## Rotation
**Levelling, one enemy** (Wowhead, November 6). Use the Voidwalker until 20, then the Succubus or Incubus.
1. **Send the demon in**
2. **[[Searing Pain]]** on the demon's target, once you have `Demonic Brand`
3. **[[Curse of the Elements]] first, from 40**
4. **[[Corruption]]** on every enemy
5. **[[Bane of Agony]]** if the enemy will live long enough for it to ramp up
6. **[[Immolate]]** on enemies with a lot of health
7. **Fill.** Your wand before 30, [[Drain Life]] from 30
8. **[[Searing Pain]] again** when the demon has used up the brand, even mid-channel
9. **Finish with the wand.** [[Life Tap]] in place of the drain when you are low on mana and high on health
**From 40:** sacrifice the Imp with [[Demonic Sacrifice]], then summon the Succubus or Incubus. `Demonic Pact` keeps the bonus. Summoning an Imp again cancels it.
**Two enemies at once:** load the first with effects and [[Fear]] it, send the demon at the second, brand that one and kill it, then finish the first. Brand only the demon's target. A brand on the feared enemy is wasted.
**Dungeon packs** (Wowhead): [[Bane of Agony]] on each enemy as they come in, skip the brand, then channel [[Hellfire]] once the tank has them.
**Why this order.**
- **`Demonic Brand` is damage and threat.** At 3/3 [[Searing Pain]] causes 50% less threat and brands for 10 seconds or six of your demon's attacks. At level 60 each attack adds 65 to 68 damage plus 7.8% of your Shadow spell power, Fire or Shadow by demon. Blizzard tuned the threat so *"a non-tanking pet like the Warlock's Imp does not gain extra threat, but a tanking-oriented pet like the Voidwalker does."*
- **Your effects are your best Mana.** At the ranks you hold around level 30, a full [[Bane of Agony]] deals 168 for 90 Mana and a full [[Corruption]] 132 for 100, before gear and talents.
- **[[Life Tap]] feeds the Imp.** With `Demonic Energies` each tap refills the demon's Mana. Wowhead: an Imp with no Mana stops casting `Firebolt`.
- **[[Hellfire]] is the strongest area spell and it costs you.** Rank 1 at 30 deals the same damage to you each second as to each enemy, for 645 Mana. [[Rain of Fire]] is weaker and works from range.
## Stat priority
**Wowhead (November 3, from sim stat weights, and by its own word a best estimate for 60):** Spell Power, or Fire or Shadow damage matching your build, then Hit, critical strike, Haste, Spell Pierce, Intellect, Spirit.
**Icy Veins (November 4):** Hit to the cap, then Spell Power, critical strike, Haste, Intellect.
- **Hit ranks higher here than for the other two specs.** Wowhead: your demon uses your Hit chance, so each miss you remove helps both of you. The cap is 17% against raid bosses and 4% against an enemy of your own level. Wowhead's raid builds take a single point of `Suppression`, so nearly all of it comes from gear.
- **Spell Power reaches the demon too.** Wowhead: the demon scales from your Spell Power. Blizzard gives one ratio only, 2 Health per point of your Stamina.
- **Haste** speeds up your casts, not the demon's.
- **Spirit** adds 1 Mana per point to every [[Life Tap]], and `Demonic Embrace` no longer reduces it.
## Your demon
[[Summon Imp]] in your starting zone, [[Voidwalker]] at 10, [[Summon Succubus]] or [[Summon Incubus]] at 20, [[Summon Felhunter]] at 30, all from class quests.
- **Demon Health.** A demon gets 2 Health per point of your Stamina since November 2 (7.5 in the beta). Blizzard then added about 40 base Health to the Voidwalker on November 6 and about 15% base Health to the Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus and Incubus on November 10.
- **Keep it alive between pulls.** Wowhead: a bandage or a new summon is usually better than [[Health Funnel]]. A new Succubus costs a Soul Shard, and you must cast [[Soul Link]] again.
- **`Master Summoner` and [[Fel Domination]] are the recovery tools.** 2/2 takes 4 seconds and 40% of the Mana off a summon. [[Fel Domination]] takes another 5.5 seconds and 50% off the next one, on a 5-minute cooldown.
- **`Master Demonologist` depends on the demon out.** Imp: +10% Fire damage. Voidwalker: 10% less Physical damage taken. Succubus or Incubus: +10% Shadow damage. Felhunter: 10% less Magic damage taken. Both you and the demon get it.

## What Forever changed for Demonology
- **Demons scale with your gear**, confirmed by Blizzard: *"Pets now scale from their summoner's stats."*
- **New talents:** `Demonic Aegis` (+30% to [[Demon Skin]] and Demon Armor at 2/2), `Fel Vitality`, `Demonic Energies`, `Decimation`, `Demonic Brand`, `Improved Felhunter`, `Demonic Knowledge`, `Demonic Pact`.
- **The tree was moved up:** `Unholy Power` to row 1, `Master Summoner` to row 3, [[Soul Link]] from row 7 to row 5. [[Fel Domination]] is the 16-point milestone and needs `Master Summoner`.
- **`Improved Health Funnel`** adds 40% to the health transferred, cuts its cost by 30% and removes its threat at 2/2, and works whatever your demon's health.
- **`Improved Sayaad` is a rename.** Sayaad is the demon family covering Succubus and Incubus. The summon spells are still [[Summon Succubus]] and [[Summon Incubus]].
- **Removed:** `Improved Healthstone`, `Fel Intellect`, `Fel Stamina`, `Improved Firestone`, `Improved Spellstone`, `Improved Subjugate Demon`.
## At level 60
**Wowhead publishes two raid builds, both 2/31/18 (November 4).** WoWSims ships the same two as presets.
- **[Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/talent-builds-pve-dps): sacrifice the Succubus, keep the Imp.** [[Searing Pain]] is the filler and keeps the brand up by itself. It takes `Improved Imp` 3/3, and `Demonic Energies` so [[Life Tap]] keeps the Imp casting. Weapon stone: Firestone.
- **Shadow: sacrifice the Imp, keep the Succubus.** [[Shadow Bolt]] is the filler with `Improved Shadow Bolt` 5/5, and [[Searing Pain]] is cast only to renew the brand. Weapon stone: Spellstone.
Wowhead's comparison: the Shadow build is ahead on fights of about a minute or less and the Fire build slightly ahead on long ones, and [[Rain of Fire]] and [[Hellfire]] are Fire, so the Fire build is the one for dungeons. That page predates launch.
**Icy Veins publishes one, the Shadow version with a Succubus (November 4).**
**The priority** (Wowhead; WoWSims' lists run the same order *(a model)*):
1. **[[Searing Pain]]** if the brand is not on the boss
2. **Your Bane:** [[Bane of Doom]] with a minute or more left in the fight, [[Bane of Agony]] with less
3. **Your assigned Curse**
4. **[[Immolate]]**
5. **[[Soul Fire]] while `Decimation` is up**, Fire build only. Wowhead skips it in the Shadow build
6. **[[Corruption]]**
7. **[[Life Tap]]** before a refresh, while moving, or when the Imp is nearly out of Mana
8. **Filler:** [[Searing Pain]] in the Fire build, [[Shadow Bolt]] in the Shadow build
On three or more enemies within reach both sources go to [[Hellfire]]. Wowhead's raid pages say demons take 50% less damage from boss area effects, which is what lets the demon you keep survive.
