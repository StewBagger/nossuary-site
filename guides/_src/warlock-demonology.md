## What Demonology is for
**`Demonic Energies`: your damage heals the demon, and [[Life Tap]] feeds its mana.** At 2/2, 15% of all your spell damage is mirrored to your pet as healing, and 100% of the mana from a Life Tap goes to it too. The thing absorbing the hits is topped up by casting you were doing anyway.
*"Demonology is an excellent solo leveler because your demon can take a lot of pressure off you while you cast from range."* **Icy Veins rates its levelling 5 out of 5**, joint best in the class.
**[[Corruption]] keeps its cast time here.** In the source's own words: *"Unlike Affliction, this build does not take `Improved Corruption`, so [[Corruption]] still has a cast time."* That means *"setting up multiple enemies takes longer than it does for Affliction."*
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 11/0/0.** Icy Veins and Wowhead disagree on whether `Improved Corruption` belongs in it at all.
**Icy Veins:** `Improved Corruption` 5 (Affliction) + `Unholy Power` 5 + `Demonic Energies` 1. Its own case for the Affliction points: [[Corruption]] keeping its cast time (above) is a cost worth paying to get `Unholy Power` and `Demonic Energies` online.
**Wowhead puts `Improved Corruption` in the flexible pile instead.** Its fixed picks are `Unholy Power` first, then `Suppression` to 4/5 for the hit cap, *"it helps if you push slightly higher-level content."* Everything else, including `Improved Corruption`, `Improved Life Tap`, `Soul Harvest`, `Demonic Embrace`, `Improved Voidwalker`, `Fel Vitality`, `Demonic Energies` and `Destructive Reach`, is playstyle-dependent.
**ClassicWoW.gg does not publish a suggested build** for any spec, by design.
The Legacy System adds points beyond these 11. Wowhead puts the cap at 7 legacy points from world exploration before level 25, worth 2 extra talent points. Its top pick there is `Improved Sayaad`, *"the strongest per point benefit"* for Succubus damage; it calls `Demonic Sacrifice` *"likely not worth it due to losing out on your pet's DPS"* this early. **Those points cannot be spent at 20 as printed.** Blizzard's Known Issues list opening the Legacy System before level 25 as a bug: the “Y” hotkey works but throws LUA errors on tabs 1 and 3. Read these as what to spend Legacy points on from 25, not as part of a level-20 build.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** No source has posted one; the cap only reached 30 on October 1. Extending the level 20 build on the tree's own point costs, with the running total after each pick: `Unholy Power` 5 (5) → `Fel Vitality` 3 (8) → `Demonic Energies` 2 (10) → `Improved Sayaad` 3 (13) → `Master Summoner` 2 (15) → [[Fel Domination]] 1 (16, the 16-point milestone, now behind `Master Summoner`) → `Demonic Brand` 3 (19) → `Decimation` 1 (20) → [[Soul Link]] 1 (21, the 21-point milestone, and the last point a level-30 character has).
**Skipping [[Demonic Sacrifice]] is deliberate.** It is the 11-point milestone, so it fits from level 20, but it consumes the demon that `Demonic Brand` and [[Soul Link]] both work through. Take it only if you want the sacrifice build instead.
## Rotation
Icy Veins and Wowhead order the opening DoTs differently, same as the other two specs.
**Icy Veins' order** is Immolate, then Corruption, then Bane of Agony.
**Wowhead reverses it**: *"Bane of Agony first: it deals the least upfront damage, so it's the least threat and requires time to build up to full damage. Immolate last; its initial Hit generates direct threat, so it's the worst opener."*
1. **Send the demon in**: *"Your demon should be attacking throughout the entire fight"*
2. **Curse**
3. **[[Immolate]]**: or last, on Wowhead's threat-ordered build, see above
4. **[[Corruption]]**: it has a cast time in this build
5. **[[Bane of Agony]]** if the target will live long enough: or first, on Wowhead's build
6. **[[Drain Life]]** when you need health. *"The spell damage will also heal your demon through `Demonic Energies`"*
7. **Wand as filler** at level 20
8. **[[Life Tap]]** when you need mana: *"With `Demonic Energies`, this will also restore Mana to your demon"*
9. **[[Drain Soul]]** for shards
**Multi-target:** spread [[Corruption]] and [[Bane of Agony]] while the demon holds the pack, then [[Rain of Fire]].
## Stat priority
Spell Power, then Hit, Intellect, Spirit, critical strike, Stamina, Haste.
Same order as Affliction, with **different reasoning at two places**. Spell Power because *"More spell damage also means more healing for your demon through `Demonic Energies`."* Hit is flagged harder: *"This build does not take `Suppression`, so you do not have the additional Hit Chance from the Affliction tree."* Intellect is lifted by `Fel Vitality`'s +15% maximum mana. Spirit costs you nothing to carry now: `Demonic Embrace` no longer reduces it, and [[Life Tap]] scales off it.
## Your demon
Same unlocks as every Warlock — [[Imp]] 2, [[Voidwalker]] 10, [[Succubus]] 20, [[Felhunter]] 30. But *"Your Demonology talents make every one of them stronger, so the best choice depends on what you are doing."* The Voidwalker's threat spell is `Torment`; Wowhead notes it is noticeably weaker until rank 2 trains at 20.
The [[Imp]] *"can run out of Mana quickly"*, which `Demonic Energies` answers every time you [[Life Tap]].

## What Forever changed for Demonology
- **Demons now scale with your gear**, confirmed by Blizzard as a baseline change: *"Pets now scale from their summoner's stats."* A guide's hands-on reading of it: they *"deal much more damage and become much tankier."*
- **Eight new talents**: `Demonic Aegis` (up to +30% to Demon Skin and Demon Armor), `Fel Vitality`, `Demonic Energies`, `Decimation`, `Demonic Brand`, `Improved Felhunter`, `Demonic Knowledge`, and the capstone `Demonic Pact`: *"Your [[Demonic Sacrifice]] effect is no longer cancelled by summoning a different Demon pet. Resummoning the sacrificed pet will still cancel the effect."* So you keep the sacrifice buff *and* a demon, just not the one you sacrificed. It requires [[Soul Link]] and sits at 31 points, so the published reaction, *"At last, we can play with our pet out instead of sacrificing it"*, is to a tooltip nobody has reached.
- **`Demonic Brand` moves threat as well as damage.** [[Searing Pain]] generates less threat and brands the target, so your pet's next 2 attacks deal Fire or Shadow damage, the Shadow version at high threat. Blizzard tuned it so *"a non-tanking pet like the Warlock's Imp does not gain extra threat, but a tanking-oriented pet like the Voidwalker does."*
- **`Decimation` is a [[Soul Fire]] finisher**, cutting its cooldown by up to 90% and, below 35% target health, making the next cast faster and free of Soul Shards.
- **[[Demonic Sacrifice]] lasts 2 hours** (Classic: 30 minutes) and its bonuses swapped: [[Imp]] gives +15% Shadow where Classic gave Fire, [[Succubus]] gives +15% Fire where Classic gave Shadow. The other two are utility: [[Voidwalker]] restores mana, [[Felhunter]] health.
- **`Master Demonologist` was rewritten**: Imp gives Fire damage, Voidwalker reduces physical damage taken, Succubus gives Shadow damage, Felhunter reduces magic damage taken.
- **The tree was front-loaded:** `Unholy Power` 4→1, `Improved Health Funnel` 2→1, `Master Summoner` 4→3, [[Soul Link]] 7→5. `Improved Health Funnel` also sheds up to 100% of its own threat now and works whatever your demon's health. Six removed: `Improved Healthstone`, `Fel Intellect`, `Fel Stamina`, `Improved Firestone`, `Improved Spellstone`, `Improved Subjugate Demon`.
- `Improved Sayaad` is a rename, and Sayaad is the demon *family* covering Succubus and Incubus. **The summon spells are still [[Summon Succubus]] and [[Summon Incubus]]**, and the talent's own tooltip still says "your Succubus' and Incubus' Lash of Pain."
## At level 60
Unknown, and the gap is pet scaling: *"Pet balance is one of the clearest areas to watch during beta… Demonology is much more dependent on its active pet than it was in original Classic"*, and nobody has established how your stats convert into demon output.
