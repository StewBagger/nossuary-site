Priest is the archetypal healer and always wanted for groups. The biggest change in Forever is that **Mind Flay is now a talent, so a Holy or Discipline priest never gets it.**
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta caps at level 30, so **no level-60 Priest build has been tested.** A specific caution for this class: much of what is written about Priest elsewhere comes from the BlizzCon demo build rather than the live beta, and Blizzard has shipped only a handful of small Priest changes since. Ability training levels below are read from the client.

## Is Priest for you?
Play Priest if you want to be the person keeping everyone alive, and you are happy watching health bars rather than the fight.
:::strengths
- The broadest healing toolkit in the game, and always in demand for groups
- A shadow specialisation that kills perfectly well on its own
- A shield that prevents damage rather than repairing it
:::
:::weaknesses
- Slow and fragile at low level, with a great deal of sitting and drinking
- Mana drains faster than you expect
- The commonest way a Priest dies is watching everyone else's health
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes — a damage-over-time, a nuke, then your wand.
**Is it forgiving?** Not solo. You are cloth-wearing and squishy.
**Does it level well?** Slowly alone, comfortably in a group.
**Does it need a group?** It does not require one, but healers are scarce and you will never wait for an invitation.
:::
## Your first talent points
**Shadow** is the solo answer; **Discipline or Holy** if you intend to group from the start. Every published levelling build spends its early points on mana and wand damage rather than on spec identity.
1. **`Wand Specialization`, 2 points** — and note Forever cut this from five ranks to two for the same bonus, so it is far cheaper than it was.
2. **`Spirit Tap`, 5 points** — your only real source of extra mana regeneration early on.
3. **`Improved Shadow Word: Pain`, 2 points.**
4. The last two points have no strong home — `Twilight Focus` solo, `Blackout` on a PvP realm.
Discipline and Holy take the same first seven points, then `Twilight Focus` or `Improved Renew`.
With the Legacy **Talented** perk, `Wand Specialization` is only two points and lands by about level 6 — **the wand comes online almost as soon as you can hold one**, which is the largest practical gain of that perk for any class.
## Levelling rotation
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** before you engage. Pulling without either is described as a death sentence.
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** on yourself.
3. **[[Smite]]** to pull.
4. **[[Mind Blast]]**.
5. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**.
6. **Wand it down.** Finishing with the wand rather than another spell is the difference between two pulls and four.
**Two or more enemies:** put [[Shadow Word: Pain]] on all of them back to back, then wand each in turn. Bunching your casts matters, because the five-second rule runs from your **last completed cast** — so casting together starts your regeneration sooner.

**The change that matters most: [[Mind Flay]] is no longer a trainer spell.** It comes with the talent point and nothing else teaches it, so **a Holy or Discipline Priest never gets Mind Flay in Forever.**
## Stat priority
**Shadow:** Spell Power, then Critical strike, Hit, Intellect, Stamina, MP5.
**Holy and Discipline:** Healing Power, then MP5, Spirit, Intellect, Critical strike.
**Spirit is worth far more than in vanilla.** `Meditation` now returns **17/33/50%** of your regeneration while casting, where Classic gave 5/10/15%; `Spirit Tap` adds more after a kill; and `Spiritual Guidance` turns Spirit into healing *and* spell damage.
**Read tooltips carefully before comparing them to Classic.** Caster base damage was cut across the board and the spell coefficients raised to compensate — [[Smite]] reads far lower than its Classic number and is not a nerf.
## Which race
**Kill the vanilla answer first: you used to pick Dwarf for [[Fear Ward]], and Fear Ward is now baseline for everyone.** That reason is gone.
**Alliance — Gnome**, a brand-new Priest combination, for `Contingency Plan` and `Confounding Flash` — an area confuse on up to five enemies, which is the best panic button any Priest race has. **Dwarf is the stronger pure leveller**, though: `Desperate Prayer` at level 10 is a full instant self-heal nothing else on Alliance has that early, and **`Mace Specialization` is the only weapon-crit racial a Priest can actually use** — Human's larger sword racial is dead weight on a class that cannot equip swords.
**Horde — Undead**, for `Cannibalize` and `Touch of the Grave`. One correction worth carrying: **`Dark Sacrifice` is on a ten-minute cooldown**, so it is an emergency mana button, not a repeatable trade.
**Skyborne cannot be a Priest.**
## Quality of life
- **The wand is the levelling mechanic.** Wand skill is default, so a wand works the moment you loot one, and `Wand Specialization` is now two points instead of five. Get one early.
- **Re-drag your action bars, or use macros.** Before level 20 nearly every even level brings a new rank of Smite, Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Pain or Power Word: Shield, and **bars do not update themselves**. A `/cast Spell Name` macro always fires the highest rank you know.
- **Tailoring and Enchanting**, and note the good Forever crafted pieces are **bind-on-pickup, so you can only make them for yourself**.
