Priest is a cloth-wearing caster with two healing specialisations and one ranged damage specialisation. Before you pick a spec, know that **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only**, as it was in Classic. It is a Shadow talent, gated on 10 points in that tree and so on level 20. A Holy or Discipline Priest does not have it in practice.
:::scope
A guide to the live game, levels 1 to 60. Forever launched on November 4, 2026 and this page was checked against client 1.60.1.70938 on November 13. Spell numbers, training levels and the talent tree are read from the client. Blizzard's Priest Class Deep Dive of September 30, its November 2 development notes and its launch hotfixes outrank the client and the guide sites wherever they disagree. Blizzard has hotfixed Priest three times since launch: November 9 ([[Fear Ward]] no longer breaks [[Shadowform]], and `Power Infusion` persists on allies), November 10 and November 11 (small fixes, listed on the spec pages). Advice on how to play is attributed to the site that publishes it. No raid opens before December 9, 2026, so nothing here rests on a raid log. Where a tooltip differs from the text, go by the text: tooltips can lag a hotfix.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Discipline](/guides/wow-forever/priest/discipline/)** | Healer | [[Power Word: Shield]] stops damage before it lands, and from 40 `Power Infusion` gives an ally 20% spell damage and healing for 15 seconds | Prevention and Mana efficiency, with [[Penance]] as a heal or a nuke |
| **[Holy](/guides/wow-forever/priest/holy/)** | Healer | [[Holy Nova]] heals the party with no threat, and [[Prayer of Mending]] jumps between injured party members. Both are talents: 20 and 40 at the earliest | Icy Veins: *"great all-rounders… very beginner-friendly"* |
| **[Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/)** | Ranged DPS | [[Vampiric Embrace]] heals every party member for 20% of the Shadow damage you deal, and [[Shadowform]] halves the Mana cost of Shadow spells | `Spirit Tap` is the Mana economy while levelling: your own kills trigger it, and so does an enemy dying under your [[Vampiric Embrace]] |
**The published advice for soloing is to level with Shadow talents and respec to heal.** Icy Veins' Discipline and Holy guides both say it in the same words: *"it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."* Wowhead's Holy levelling page says the same and adds that Discipline levels faster than Holy.
**A second published route runs the other way.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both describe levelling on `Meditation` in the Discipline tree until 40, then respeccing into Shadow the moment [[Shadowform]] is available. The [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page has the point order.
The published ratings sit on the three spec pages, each alongside the caveat that the levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.
This is not a permanent commitment. A respec is a visit to a class trainer in a major city. Icy Veins puts the first one at 1 gold, rising each time you do it. **`Dual Specialization` is bought from your class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold** (Wowhead's report, November 11; Blizzard's own post gives the level and no price). Your mount is also a level-40 purchase.
## Is Priest for you?
Play Priest if you want to be the person keeping everyone alive, and you are happy watching health bars rather than the fight.
:::strengths
- A shield that prevents damage rather than repairing it, and a [[Power Word: Shield]] of equal or higher rank can replace one already on the target
- [[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every race from level 20, so no race is locked out of it
- `Dual Specialization` from level 40 carries a damage spec and a healing spec at once
:::
:::weaknesses
- Icy Veins' headings for the two healing specs while levelling: *"No AoE, Struggles To Solo Level"* and *"Pitiful AoE and Lackluster Damage"*
- No movement ability at all. Wowhead and Icy Veins both list it, and your mount is level 40
- [[Desperate Prayer]] is Dwarf-only now, so most Priests have no instant self-heal
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes: a damage-over-time spell, a nuke, then your wand.
**Is it forgiving?** Not solo. Cloth armour, and Mana is the limit: Wowhead's Holy page has you drinking every couple of pulls without a wand finish.
**What carries you while levelling?** [[Mind Blast]], [[Shadow Word: Pain]] and a wand, then [[Mind Flay]] from the Shadow tree and [[Shadowform]] at 40.
**Does it need a group?** No, but both healing specs are published as group specs. Solo, the advice is Shadow talents.
:::

The trainer has something new every even level. Ranks that matter most while levelling: [[Mind Blast]] at 16, 22, 28, 34, 40, 46, 52 and 58, [[Shadow Word: Pain]] at 10, 18, 26, 34, 42, 50 and 58, and [[Power Word: Shield]] every six levels from 12 to 60. [[Shadow Word: Death]] ranks up at 40, 48 and 56.
**Talent spells are not on this table because points buy them, not levels.** [[Mind Flay]] needs 10 points in Shadow, [[Vampiric Embrace]] 15, [[Penance]] 20 in Discipline, and [[Shadowform]], `Power Infusion` and [[Prayer of Mending]] 30 in their own trees. A healing Priest can dip into Shadow for [[Mind Flay]]; the gate is points in the tree, not your spec.
**Binding Heal is not in the game.** Blizzard's November 2 notes removed it and put the talent `Holy Binding` in its slot. No trainer level teaches it, whatever older guide pages still say.
## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP, and specifically the ones that break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm), `Will to Survive` (Human, stuns), `Escape Artist` (Gnome, roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below *(ours)*.
**You used to pick Dwarf for [[Fear Ward]].** Fear Ward is now baseline for everyone, so that reason is gone.
**The two Priest racial spells come from class quests at 10 and 20**, not from the trainer. Wowhead's quest table still marks the Undead level-20 quest for `Dark Sacrifice` as coming soon.
**Alliance: Gnome** *(our pick)*. Its two Priest racials are new in Forever: `Confounding Flash` and `Contingency Plan`, the only area confuse and the only automatic below-35% absorb in the table below. `Expansive Mind` adds 5% maximum Mana, and `Eureka!` takes 10% off the Mana cost of non-periodic spells and adds 10% damage or healing for 8 seconds. Since November 11 `Confounding Flash` can be cast in [[Shadowform]]. Human's case is `The Human Spirit`, 10% more Spirit. Dwarf's case is a self-heal instead. `Desperate Prayer` at level 10 is a full instant self-heal, and nothing else on Alliance has one that early. Dwarf also picks up a new level-20 racial, `Chastise` (damages and immobilises a Humanoid target for 2 seconds, instant, 2-minute cooldown), and `Artisan's Precision` gives every Dwarf 1% hit.
**Horde: Undead, for `Touch of Weakness` and `Dark Sacrifice`** *(our pick)*. Blizzard's class post: Undead keeps `Touch of Weakness` unchanged and gains a new level-20 spell, `Dark Sacrifice`. It converts your own health to Mana over time, with the Mana gain increased by Spirit. In the client rank 1 takes 440 Health over 15 seconds and returns 440 Mana plus your Spirit, on a 10-minute cooldown, so it is an emergency Mana button. Blizzard publishes no cooldown for it. `Touch of the Grave` is a general Undead racial and not a Priest spell: your spells and attacks have a 30% chance to drain Health, up to 5% of your maximum Health, on a 15-second internal cooldown. [[Fade]]'s doubled threat reduction is not part of the case for Undead, because Blizzard lists it under Baseline Abilities, so every Priest of every race has it. Every Undead character has the general-race ability `Cannibalize`, Priest or not. It restores Mana as well as Health, and it works while you are immune to Physical damage. `Dark Sacrifice` does not break crowd control effects.
**Skyborne cannot be a Priest.**
**Every Priest race's spells, as Blizzard's class post describes them:**

The live client reads 10 minutes, not 5, on both `Divine Grace` and `Contingency Plan`, and no Blizzard note since has mentioned either. Plan around the 10 minutes on your tooltip. The ward's 30 seconds and the 35% threshold agree. Rank 1 of `Contingency Plan` absorbs 155 damage and heals 125 over 15 seconds.
## Quality of life
- **The wand is the levelling mechanic.** Wand skill is default, so a wand works the moment you loot one, and `Wand Specialization` is now two points instead of five. Wowhead's levelling pages name the Enchanting-made `Lesser Magic Wand` at level 5 and `Greater Magic Wand` at 13.
- **Wanding does not stop your Mana regeneration.** Spirit regeneration restarts five seconds after your last spell that cost Mana, and a wand costs none. Both sites give the same reason for finishing every kill with it.
- **Re-drag your action bars, or use macros.** Nearly every even level brings a new rank of something, and the bars do not update themselves. New spells do not place themselves on your bars either. A `/cast Spell Name` macro always fires the highest rank you know.
- **Low ranks lose nothing on talent procs.** Talents that trigger from your spells no longer have a lower chance when you cast a low rank, so downranking a heal or a nuke costs you no talent procs.
- **Training costs are flattened.** Blizzard puts the cost of every class ability from 1 to 60 at 206 to 207 gold for every class, with abilities learned at the same level costing the same. The Priest's race-specific spells are the exception.
- **Trainers are in starting zones and capitals only.** Wowhead's levelling guide lists them; there is none out in the levelling zones.
- **Tailoring and Enchanting.** Both sites recommend the pair. The good Forever crafted cloth pieces are bind-on-pickup, so you can only make them for yourself.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **[[Mind Flay]] hits for less** | It is talent-only, as in Classic: one Shadow talent point, 10 points in, level 20 at the earliest. Its damage reads 63 at rank 1, against Classic's 75. The new `Improved Mind Flay` sits behind it |
| [[Fear Ward]] baseline for all races | Every Priest learns it at 20 regardless of race. 3-minute duration and cooldown in the client. Since November 9 casting it no longer drops [[Shadowform]] |
| [[Devouring Plague]] available to all races | It was Undead-only. It can now critically strike |
| [[Divine Spirit]] now trained at 30 | It was a talent, and Blizzard says baseline in so many words |
| **[[Lightwell]] is no longer a talent** | Blizzard lists it under the Holy tree's removed talents and does not say what became of the spell. The client carries it as a trained Holy spell at 40 (225 Mana, 10-minute cooldown), and foreverchanges and Icy Veins' level-40 tables list it. No Blizzard post confirms it, so check your trainer |
| `Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks, not 5 | Same bonus for three fewer points |
| `Meditation`'s Mana benefit from Spirit is *"increased substantially"* | Blizzard's words. The client reads 50% of your Mana regeneration continuing while you cast at 3/3 |
| [[Shadow Word: Death]] added at 32 | With a backlash of 30% of your maximum health if it hits and fails to kill; a miss deals you nothing |
| Binding Heal added, then removed | It was a Holy talent in the beta. Blizzard's November 2 notes removed it, and `Holy Binding` took its slot |
| [[Prayer of Healing]] now heals the **target's** party | It no longer heals your own party. Read the target before you cast |
| [[Renew]] can stack from multiple Priests | Two Priests renewing the same tank is no longer wasted. It can also critically strike |
| **[[Desperate Prayer]] is Dwarf-only now** | A Human Priest loses the self-heal it had in Classic |
| [[Power Word: Shield]] no longer starves a tank's Rage | Rage from taking damage ignores absorbs, in Blizzard's class post |
## At level 60
**Published builds now exist for all three specs.** Wowhead and Icy Veins each publish level-60 raid and dungeon builds, and WoWSims carries a preset for each spec. The spec pages give them with their dates. All of them predate any raid: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9, 2026, so no raid log exists to test them against.
**What a Priest brings to a group at 60, from the client:** `Prayer of Fortitude`, `Prayer of Spirit` and `Prayer of Shadow Protection` for the whole raid, [[Fear Ward]], `Dispel Magic`, `Abolish Disease`, and one of `Power Infusion` (Discipline), [[Prayer of Mending]] (Holy) or [[Vampiric Embrace]] with `Dark Inspiration` (Shadow).
