Professions still run 1 to 300 and you still pick two primaries. What changed is everything around them: **929 new recipes**, a camping system, a second currency, and a food buff that gives +5% experience from level one.
:::scope
The game is live and the cap is 60, so skill 225 to 300 is played content. Hotfixes and camp-object rules below are **Blizzard's**. Recipe counts and Legacy perk ranks are the client's, as foreverchanges.pro prints them at build 70938. Trainer ranks, specialisations and skill-300 recipes are Wowhead's pages (November 7 to 11) and Icy Veins' (November 3 and 4), named where they disagree. **Still thin:** anything that comes out of a raid. Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9, so raid-sourced materials and recipes are unplayed. Node density, respawn timers and skill-up rates are unpublished for every gathering profession, and the zone recommendations in the gathering guides are Classic spawn data.

## Start here: the three things that matter most
1. **Take Cooking at level 1.** Cooked food gives +5% experience from kills while you are well fed, and the first dishes come at Cooking skill 1 on a level-1 character. It costs you no primary slot, and nothing else in the profession system comes close for a levelling player *(ours, not a source's)*.
2. **Only six professions earn Legacy Points**: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring, one point each at skill 150, 225 and 300. Gathering professions and secondary skills earn none. If you care about account-wide perks, that is your list.
3. **Camping is new.** Every profession gets camp objects, and the buffs last an hour.
:::new
**How many can I have?** Two primary professions, plus all three secondary skills: Cooking, First Aid and Fishing, which cost you nothing.
**Can I change later?** Yes. Unlearn a primary from the Professions tab and take another. You lose all its skill and recipes.
**Do I need one to play?** No. Cooking and First Aid cost no primary slot and both land effects while you level, so our own advice is to take them regardless.
**Which two primaries?** The Legacy Points come only from a crafting pair; that part is mechanical. Our own answer for money is a gathering pair — see below.

## Trainer ranks
| Rank | Train at skill | Character level | Raises your cap to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice | — | — | 75 |
| Journeyman | 50 | 10 | 150 |
| Expert | 125 | 20 | 225 |
| Artisan | 200 or 225 | 35 | 300 |
**There is no Master rank and no 275 tier.** That is Burning Crusade. Blizzard's own list ends at Artisan.
**Artisan needs character level 35**, which is the gate people forget: a crafting profession cannot pass 225 until then no matter how much you craft.
**The two sites disagree on Artisan's skill.** Icy Veins (November 3) says 200. Wowhead (November 7) says 225. Check with the trainer at 200.
**Gathering professions unlock earlier**, per Icy Veins: no level for Journeyman, level 10 for Expert, level 25 for Artisan.
**Three professions break the pattern.** Cooking's Expert rank is a vendor book and its Artisan rank is a quest in Gadgetzan at skill 225. Enchanting's Artisan trainer stands inside the Uldaman dungeon, per Wowhead.

**The classic pairings are unchanged**: Mining with Blacksmithing or Engineering, Herbalism with Alchemy, Skinning with Leatherworking, each crafting profession beside the profession that gathers its feed. Icy Veins recommends the same pairs. A crafting profession that has to buy its own materials is expensive.
**Two gathering professions is still the money answer** if you want gold rather than gear, and it is the cheapest way to level *(ours, not a source's)*.
## The secondary skills are free
| Skill | Why |
| --- | --- |
| [Cooking](/guides/wow-forever/professions/cooking/) | **+5% experience from kills** while well fed, from skill 1. Also owns the campfire |
| [First Aid](/guides/wow-forever/professions/first-aid/) | Heavily expanded, bandages, plus cures for **poison, disease and bleeds**, and craftable healing potions |
| [Fishing](/guides/wow-forever/professions/fishing/) | Feeds Cooking's fish dishes, including the level-60 attack power and critical strike food. Cooking itself can reach 300 on meat alone |
**Rogues: Poisons and Lockpicking are now tradeskills, not class spells.** The client describes them as secondary skills *"like First Aid"*, but they sit in a separate class-based bucket and do not count against your three secondaries. Lockpicking now levels like a tradeskill.
## What Forever changed
- **929 new recipes** out of 2,188, against Blizzard's published *"over 600"*. Leatherworking gained the most (270 new of 511), then Blacksmithing (197 of 437) and Tailoring (180 of 414).
- **[Camping](/guides/wow-forever/professions/camping/) is new.** A cook lights a campfire, and every profession can then place a camp object: the first at skill 20, then upgrades from Blueprints, at 140 and 300 for most. Leatherworking's second object is at 125, and Cooking's campfires are at 1, 90 and 200. Sit or craft at the fire for a minute and the buffs last an hour.
- **[Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/) is new**: a second currency, and 311 recipes that no trainer teaches. Open from level 10 to any class and any profession, including none.
- **A new material class, "Scarce materials"**, drops alongside what you gather. Mining gives Pyrite, Bauxite and Pitchblende. Herbalism gives Frilled Lichen, Rosecap and Stranglevine. Skinning gives Pristine Leather, Pristine Hide and Pristine Scale.
- **The Legacy system** has a Professions tree. It includes `Working Overtime` (4% more skill-up chance per rank, 20% at five ranks), `Bountiful Harvest` (20% more likely to find Scarce materials per rank, 100% at five) and `Master Chef` (10% chance of an extra cooking result per rank, 50% at five). A character can spend 16 Legacy Points at launch.
- **No profession was added or removed.** Nine primary, three secondary, exactly as Classic. There is no Jewelcrafting and no Inscription.
## 225 to 300, and crafting at 60
**The top recipes need a crafting station.** Each crafting profession's skill-300 camp object is a station its best recipes require: the `Master Forge`, `Loom`, `Sewing Machine`, [[Alchemy Laboratory]], `Arcane Forge` and `Anarchist's Workbench`. Wowhead counts 65 Blacksmithing recipes that need a Master Forge. You can craft at another player's station, but someone at your camp has to be able to place one. No site has published where the skill-300 Blueprints drop.
**New endgame metals.** Heavy Thorium Bar is smelted from Heavy Thorium Ore at a [[Molten Foundry]]. Azerothium Bar is smelted from Pitchblende and Coal at Mining 275. Legionite Bar is an Alchemy transmute from Heavy Thorium Bars.
**Skill-300 gear that takes [[Malleable Essence of Nature]].** Wowhead lists it as a reagent for Blacksmithing's `Justice` and `Glory` plate pieces and several weapons, Tailoring's `Manaflare`, `Conviction` and `Demonheart` cloth, six Enchanting relics and a wand, and one Engineering gun. The Tailoring patterns are sold by a vendor in Mount Hyjal. Wowhead's Leatherworking page lists no recipe that uses it. The client's item text says it is *"acquired from disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps"*, and both raids open December 9, so none of this gear can be made yet and all of it runs through an enchanter.
**Engineering's raid ammunition can be sold.** Blizzard made `Swiftstrike Shot` and `Swiftfeather Arrow` tradeable on November 10, and said they are not available until raids unlock.
**Certifications and titles.** At skill 300, 1,000 [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/) buys an account-wide title: *the Alchemist*, *the Blacksmith*, *the Enchanter*, *the Engineer*, *the Leatherworker*, *the Tailor*. Six crafting professions only; no gathering title, no Cooking title. Only a character with 300 skill can display it.
## There are no self-only profession perks
Lists of Wrath-style perks have circulated for Forever: belt buckles, ring enchants, bracer kits, cloak embroidery. **Wowhead's November pages say they are not in the game.** Its Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Enchanting pages state there are no such perks, and its Tailoring and Engineering pages say it has found no source for the claims.
What a crafter does get is **Bind on Pickup recipes**: gear only the maker can wear. Icy Veins lists these as each profession's perk, such as `Sentinel's Sabatons` for blacksmiths and the `Emergency Field Cloak` trinket for engineers. Enchanters can also enchant necklaces. The Bind on Pickup armour families are [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/) recipes.
## A warning about stats
**Forever uses rating stats where vanilla used flat percentages.** Client item text carries defense rating and hit rating. Spell Power and Healing Power are separate stats: there is +44 Healing Power food and +28 spell damage food. If you read a vanilla stat budget onto these items you will misjudge them.
## Specialisations
Only three professions have them, exactly as in vanilla: Blacksmithing (Armorsmith, Weaponsmith, and the three Master smiths), Leatherworking (Dragonscale, Elemental, Tribal) and Engineering (Gnomish, Goblin). **Tailoring, Enchanting and Alchemy have none.** If you remember Elixir or Potion Mastery, that is Burning Crusade.
- **Engineering** is the one with a published route. Wowhead: both specialisations open through a three-step quest chain at **character level 30 and Engineering 200**. Goblin starts in Ratchet. Gnomish starts from Engineering trainers. Each ends with a membership card that unlocks that school's trainer
- **Blacksmithing.** Wowhead lists 18 Armorsmith recipes, 8 Weaponsmith recipes, and 5 to 7 each for Master Axesmith, Hammersmith and Swordsmith. Its section on the specialisation quests is empty
- **Leatherworking.** Wowhead lists 13 Dragonscale, 15 Elemental and 18 Tribal recipes, the Dragonscale ones from skill 235. Neither site says how you choose
For Blacksmithing and Leatherworking, the skill and level needed, whether the choice is exclusive and what switching costs are all unpublished.
## What Blizzard has changed since launch
- **November 5:** trade vendors on Zephras Isle now sell apprentice tradeskill bags
- **November 6:** the Tanning Rack needs Leatherworking 125, down from 140
- **November 9:** Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs now drop in dungeons. The Pouch of Violet Dust now adds its hour to camp buffs
- **November 10:** the Journeyman Campfire Kit is placed at Cooking 90, down from 140. Coarse Grinding Stone gives skill-ups to 90. The Arcane Salvager is crafted with a Runed Golden Rod and works for lower-level enchanters. The vendor price of Iron Grenades is 1 silver 25 copper, down from 5 silver. *Recipe: Elixir of Greater Firepower* is renamed *Recipe: Elixir of Holy Power*. One Fishing Rack lure bug is fixed and others remain
- **November 11:** five Tailoring bags cost one more bolt and are larger: Woolen bags 10 slots, Silk packs 12, Red Mageweave Bag 14
Each profession's page has the detail.
## What is not known
- **Where two new herbs grow.** Marefoil and Demonsage are reagents for the raid flasks, and Wowhead (November 9) and Icy Veins (November 3) both list their locations as unknown. Heavy Thorium Ore is mined at skill 300 on Mount Hyjal per Wowhead (November 9); Icy Veins (November 3) still says unknown. Death Lotus is not a node: the `Lotus Claw` glove enchant gives a chance at it when gathering herbs in Hyjal. Wowhead guesses that Pitchblende comes from Truesilver and higher deposits and marks that unconfirmed.
- **Gathering node density, respawn rules and skill-up rates.** Unpublished for all three gathering professions.
- **Where the skill-300 Blueprints drop.**
- **How Blacksmithing and Leatherworking specialisations are chosen**, as above.
- **What a Legacy perk reset costs on live realms.** Perks can be unlearned. Blizzard set the cheapest reset to 1 silver in October and said that price applied to the beta only.
- **Whether the +5% food experience stacks with rested experience.** Wowhead says it combines with the Cozy Sleeping Bag's +3%. No source addresses rested experience.
