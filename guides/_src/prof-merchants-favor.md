**New in Forever: a second currency, and 317 recipes no trainer teaches.** It is open from level 10 to any class and any profession, including none at all.
:::scope
Recipe counts, costs and mechanics come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. **One count is disputed:** the figure of 317 recipes comes from the client diff, while another source's breakdown totals about 305. The seven trades involved are agreed. Treat the number as roughly 300 to 320.
:::
## What it is
A reputation-free currency you earn by handing in **Waylaid Crates**, and spend on recipes that no trainer sells.
1. **Waylaid Crates drop** from ordinary monsters roughly level 7 to 26, and from chests and dungeon mobs
2. **Fill one line of the crate's material list**: for example *Waylaid Crate: Apprentice Herbs* asks for 20 Peacebloom, or 20 Silverleaf, or 10 Earthroot
3. **Hand it in** at Three Corners in Redridge (Alliance, the Azeroth Commerce Authority) or west of the Crossroads in the Barrens (Horde, Durotar Supply and Logistics)
A crate pays 5 to 20 Favor and most recipes cost 30 to 240, so **every recipe is several crates' worth.**
**Crates are tradeable**, so most players buy them at the auction house rather than hunting them. Crates that take crafted goods are green and pay double the white raw-material ones.
**Favor is per-character, not account-wide.** Every alt starts from zero.
**Two crate bugs are still live.** Blizzard's Known Issues post, last edited October 1, carries *"Waylaid Crates do not function on items with a maximum stack size smaller than the quantity required"* and *"Waylaid Crate: Apprentice Curiosities does not accept Greater Magic Essence, and incorrectly allows you to target Lesser Magic Essence"*. Both are annotated *"This is fixed in the next Beta build"*, but neither the September 24 nor the October 1 change log carries the fix. In the beta a correct turn-in can still be refused, and one crate asks for the wrong essence.
## Why it matters even if you have no profession
**You need no profession to fill a crate or earn Favor**, and it opens at level 10. It is the only part of the profession system that is open to everyone.
It also gives low-level raw materials a guaranteed buyer they never had in vanilla. **Peacebloom and copper ore now have a price floor**, subject to the two crate bugs above, unfixed as of the October 1 build. If you are levelling a gathering profession, this is where the early junk goes.
## What you can buy
**317 recipes across seven trades**, the six crafting professions plus Cooking:
| Trade | Recipes |
| --- | --- |
| Leatherworking | 82 |
| Tailoring | 68 |
| Blacksmithing | 62 |
| Enchanting | 40 |
| Alchemy | 30 |
| Engineering | 29 |
| Cooking | 6 |
**Gathering professions buy no recipes.** They feed the crates instead.
**This is where most of the good level-20 gear lives** *(ours, not a source's)*, by the best-in-slot counts on those three pages. The role-templated armour families in Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring are all Merchant's Favor recipes, and they are Bind on Pickup. Nobody can sell you the finished piece: you buy the recipe and make it yourself.
## Certifications and titles
These come only from this system. At skill 300, **1,000 Merchant's Favor** buys a Certification that unlocks an account-wide title: *the Alchemist*, *the Blacksmith*, *the Enchanter*, *the Engineer*, *the Leatherworker*, *the Tailor*.
**Six crafting professions only.** There is no gathering title and no Cooking title. The title is account-wide, but only a character with 300 skill in that profession can display it.
## Craftsman's Writs
A separate item, and the optional reputation path: **150 different crafting orders**, 30 each for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring. Enchanting has none.
## Crate tiers
Apprentice and Journeyman from level 10, Expert from 20, Artisan from 35. **Only Apprentice and Journeyman crates had been seen in the beta as of September 30**, because the level cap sat right at Expert's level-20 threshold. The cap rose to 30 on October 1, clearing that threshold, so Expert crates should now be reachable. No source has confirmed one has actually dropped. Artisan still needs level 35, so it stays out of reach.
## The Legacy perk
**`Performance Bonus`**, in the Professions tree, gives a 5%, 10% or 15% chance of doubled Merchant's Favor on a crate turn-in across three ranks.
## What is not known
The exact recipe count, as above. Whether Expert crates behave as expected now that the October 1 cap rise puts level 20 in reach. Artisan is untested too, and at level 35 it stays beyond the level-30 cap. And real gold values for any of it. There are no prices before launch, so every cost estimate in circulation is weighed by vendor value rather than by what players will actually pay.
