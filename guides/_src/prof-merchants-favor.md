**New in Forever: a second currency, and 311 recipes no trainer teaches.** It is open from level 10 to any class and any profession, including none at all.
:::scope
The vendor lists, prices and skill levels are Wowhead's tables, dated November 11, and we counted them. Crate contents, crate tiers and the reputation each writ pays are foreverchanges.pro's reading of the client, on a page dated September 23, before launch. The dungeon-drop fix is **Blizzard's**, November 9. **Still thin:** what Expert and Artisan crates pay. Wowhead's payout table stops at Journeyman, and its Writs page says it has only seen Journeyman writs so far.

## What it is
A currency you earn by handing in **Waylaid Crates**, and spend on recipes that no trainer sells. It needs no reputation.
1. **Waylaid Crates drop** in the open world and in dungeons. Wowhead says the crate you find matches your level and the zone's
2. **Fill one line of the crate's material list**: for example *Waylaid Crate: Apprentice Herbs* asks for 20 Peacebloom, or 20 Silverleaf, or 10 Earthroot. Put the whole amount in one stack
3. **Hand it in** at Three Corners in Redridge (Alliance, the Azeroth Commerce Authority) or just outside the Crossroads in the Barrens (Horde, Durotar Supply and Logistics). Both camps sell the same things at the same prices
**Dungeons dropped no crates or writs for the first five days.** Blizzard fixed *"a bug that prevented Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs from dropping inside dungeons"* on November 9.
| Crate | Quality | Favor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice | White | 5 |
| Apprentice | Green | 10 |
| Journeyman | White | 10 |
| Journeyman | Green | 20 |
Crates that take raw materials are white. Crates that take crafted goods are green and pay double. Your first hand-in also gives a Shipping Label, a one-time quest that foreverchanges.pro puts at 50 Favor.
Most recipes cost 30 to 360, so **every recipe is several crates' worth.**
**Crates are tradeable**, so most players buy them at the auction house rather than hunting them. The Alliance camp has a neutral auctioneer of its own, per Wowhead.
**Favor is per-character, not account-wide.** Every alt starts from zero.
## Why it matters even if you have no profession
**You need no profession to fill a crate or earn Favor**, and crates can be used from level 10. It is the only part of the profession system that is open to everyone. Without a crafting profession, Favor still buys a pet, a toy licence and a mount.
It also gives low-level raw materials a guaranteed buyer they never had in vanilla. **Peacebloom and copper ore now have a price floor.** If you are levelling a gathering profession, this is where the early junk goes.
The two crate bugs from the beta are gone from Blizzard's live Known Issues list. One refused materials whose stack size was smaller than the crate's quantity. The other had *Apprentice Curiosities* asking for the wrong essence.
## What you can buy
**311 recipes across seven trades**, the six crafting professions plus Cooking, and six Certifications. Wowhead's vendor tables total 317 entries with the Certifications included, which is where the figure of 317 in circulation comes from.
| Trade | Recipes | Skill range | Price in Favor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leatherworking | 81 | 60 to 235 | 30 to 90 |
| Tailoring | 67 | 60 to 235 | 30 to 90 |
| Blacksmithing | 61 | 45 to 300 | 30 to 120 |
| Enchanting | 39 | 130 to 300 | 45 to 240 |
| Alchemy | 29 | 65 to 300 | 45 to 240 |
| Engineering | 28 | 25 to 300 | 45 to 360 |
| Cooking | 6 | 25 to 250 | 30 to 90 |
**Gathering professions buy no recipes.** They feed the crates instead.
**The lists run from the first levels to 60.** The role-templated armour families in Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring start at skill 45 to 60 and climb to 235 or 250. Above them sit skill-300 recipes: `Enriched Thorium` plate and the `Legionite Glaive` for blacksmiths, fifteen enchanting formulas at 240 Favor each, and four engineering schematics at 360.
The armour families are Bind on Pickup. Nobody can sell you the finished piece: you buy the recipe and make it yourself. Blizzard cut the vendor sale price of some of these on November 10, noting they were *"bind-on-pickup items that a player was intended to craft only once for themselves."*
**Buy what you will wear now** *(ours, not a source's)*. A 30-Favor pattern is two to six crates. Enchanters get three class relics at skill 130 for 45 Favor each.

## Certifications and titles
These come only from this system. At skill 300, **1,000 Merchant's Favor** buys a Certification that unlocks an account-wide title: *the Alchemist*, *the Blacksmith*, *the Enchanter*, *the Engineer*, *the Leatherworker*, *the Tailor*.
**Six crafting professions only.** There is no gathering title and no Cooking title. The title is account-wide, but only a character with 300 skill in that profession can display it.
## Craftsman's Writs
A separate item, and the way to raise reputation with the camp. A writ is a rare-quality world drop that starts a quest: someone wants one crafted item delivered.
1. **Find or buy a writ.** It drops in the open world and in dungeons, and it is tradeable
2. **Get the item.** Craft it, or buy it at the auction house
3. **Deliver it.** The customer is out in the world, often a goblin in a levelling zone. Wowhead lists a hand-in for every Journeyman writ, in Wetlands, Duskwood, Hillsbrad, Arathi, Alterac, Ashenvale, Stonetalon, Stranglethorn and Thousand Needles
Each writ quest can be done once a day.
There are **150 writs**, 30 each for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring. Enchanting has none.
| Writ | Reputation | How many | Skill to make the item, usually |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Journeyman | 75 | 45 | 100 to 170 |
| Expert | 125 | 60 | 180 to 225 |
| Artisan | 200 | 45 | 230 to 310 |
The Journeyman figure is confirmed by Wowhead, which also reports a coin pouch with each one. The Expert and Artisan figures are foreverchanges.pro's reading of the client. Wowhead says it has only seen Journeyman writs so far and lists a single Expert hand-in.
**What reputation is for.** Two things: the signpost licence at Honored and the tabard at Exalted. The sources disagree on whether any recipe needs it. foreverchanges.pro says none does. Icy Veins, on November 3, says the `Wicked Smoothie` recipe requires Honored. Wowhead lists that recipe at 90 Favor with no requirement.
## Crate tiers
Apprentice and Journeyman crates can be used from level 10, Expert from 20, Artisan from 35, per the client. **No source has published what an Expert or Artisan crate pays.** Wowhead's November 11 table lists only Apprentice and Journeyman.
## The Legacy perk
**`Performance Bonus`**, in the Professions tree, gives a 5%, 10% or 15% chance of doubled Merchant's Favor on a crate turn-in. The client's rank texts show three ranks. Wowhead's overview lists five. The perk opens after 5 points in the tree.
## What is not known
- **Expert and Artisan crate payouts**, as above
- **Whether any recipe is gated by reputation**, as above
- **Real gold values.** No source in our evidence publishes live auction prices for crates, writs or the crafted pieces
