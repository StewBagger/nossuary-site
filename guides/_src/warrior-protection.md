## What Protection is for
**The shield is the engine.** `Shield Specialization` at 5/5 returns 5 Rage on every block, where Classic gave 1, and `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all now check that you are holding one.
**Icy Veins holds both halves at once:** *"extremely slow when it comes to solo leveling,"* but *"takes less damage and can handle dangerous pulls more safely."* Solo levelling is more forgiving even while it is slower: fewer kills per minute, far less time sitting down. Its Levelling score of 2.5/5 is the highest of the three Warrior specs on Icy Veins' scale, and no other source scores them. That dungeons are where the spec pays off is our own reading; no source ranks the three Warrior specs for group play.
**You do not need a full Protection spec to tank at low level.**
**The Rage rework favours Protection specifically.** Blizzard kept the old formula for Rage gained from taking damage, but now calculates it as if every Warrior always had 50% damage reduction from Armor. Stacking Armor, which is what this spec does, no longer shrinks your Rage income the way it did in Classic. Absorbs like Power Word: Shield stop blocking Rage gain too.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Wowhead and Icy Veins agree on the first ten points and split on the last.
1. `Shield Specialization`, 5 points, taken mainly for **5 rage on every block**
2. **`Improved Thunder Clap`, 3 points** for cheaper area threat, now usable in Defensive Stance
3. `Improved Bloodrage`, 2 points — row 1, so it needs no point parked elsewhere first
4. The eleventh point is where the two sources part ways. **Icy Veins** spends it on `Master of Defense`, a chance of 5 rage when you Dodge or Parry with a shield equipped; Blizzard publishes no proc chance. Wowhead puts it in `Improved Revenge` instead, row 2, redesigned to increase Revenge's damage rather than stun, with no figure from Blizzard. Wowhead also argues against `Master of Defense` at level 20: ~10% total avoidance makes its proc too rare to be worth a point yet. It names `Last Stand` as its own alternative for that point.
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build**, reaching `Vanguard`: `Shield Specialization` 5, `Anticipation` 4, `Improved Bloodrage` 2, `Master of Defense` 2, `Defiance` 3, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Vanguard` 1. That is twenty points, and the twenty-first is a choice we will not make for you. `Bastion` is out of reach: the September 24 swap puts it in row 6, which is 25 points into the tree. `Focused Rage` takes its place in row 5 once that swap reaches the client. Until it does, a point in any row you have already opened is the honest answer. No source publishes a level-30 Protection build, and we are not inventing a row for a talent Blizzard has not placed. Order matters in one place: `Improved Bloodrage` is row 1 and `Anticipation` is row 2, so bank `Improved Bloodrage` first and `Anticipation` once you have unlocked row 2.
**`Iron Will` sits in row 1**, and it is not in either build above. It cuts the duration of Stun and Fear effects on you by 15% rather than resisting them. The Fear half is the client's reading: Blizzard's own note on the change mentions only Stuns, while the client's tooltip and the effects behind it both list the two. Worth a point if you expect PvP; skip it for pure levelling and dungeon tanking.
## Rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** always up, [[Bloodrage]] between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]** to open in Battle Stance, then swap straight back to Defensive, until you have `Vanguard`
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on non-casters. A live bug, not a reason to skip it: Blizzard's own Known Issues list for this build says multiple debuffs including Demoralizing Shout are not generating threat right now. Keep using it for the damage reduction, which works, but do not count on it for aggro until Blizzard says it is fixed
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** on proc
5. **[[Revenge]]** on every block, dodge or parry: very cheap, a lot of threat, and your best button
6. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice, spread evenly across targets. It also taps every target it lands on onto your threat table instantly, which is how you grab adds while the Demoralizing Shout threat bug above lasts
**Multi-target is the payoff, and the rage talents are why:** with the recommended build you can afford far more [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Cleave]] than a Warrior otherwise could.
Differences from the damage specs: **[[Revenge]] replaces [[Overpower]]**, and [[Rend]] and [[Slam]] drop out entirely.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, **Hit**, Strength, Agility, Armor, Stamina, then crit — though crit goes above Agility if you care only about threat (Icy Veins' published order).
**Hit now improves [[Taunt]] reliability**, which is a Forever-specific reason to value it.

## What Forever changed for Protection
- **[[Thunder Clap]] is usable in Defensive Stance**, and `Improved Thunder Clap` moved in from Arms with its rage reduction doubled.
- **[[Rend]] and [[Sunder Armor]] now instantly tap the enemy onto your threat table** (October 1 build); see Rotation. Blizzard's own Known Issues list adds that several debuffs, Demoralizing Shout included, currently generate no threat at all — a live bug, not intended design.
- **The whole tree was row-reshuffled on October 1**, and Blizzard said why: to give non-shield users a clearer path through the middle of the tree as a PvP-secondary spec (quoted in full below). `Iron Will` is new to row 1. `Anticipation` moved row 1 → row 2. `Improved Bloodrage` moved row 2 → row 1. `Improved Revenge` moved row 3 → row 2. `Improved Disarm` moved row 4 → row 3. `Improved Shield Bash` moved row 5 → row 4. None of the point costs changed, only which row gates them — see Your talent points for what that does to build order.
- **`Toughness` is gone**, in Blizzard's own words: "Toughness has been removed." If you are carrying an older build from elsewhere that budgeted a point for it, that point is free again. A `Toughness` entry does survive in the client, and it settles nothing: Paladin and Shaman both still have a talent by that name. The tree in game is no help either. At build 1.60.1.70245 it is still Classic's, offering `Deflection`, `Improved Taunt` and `Improved Shield Block`, with none of Forever's new Protection talents in it. A talent can exist in the client and still be unreachable in the tree.
- **Three talents now check for a shield.** `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all require one, so Classic's dual-wield threat setup loses its best threat talents. The tree itself is not gated. Blizzard's stated goal for the October 1 reshuffle: *"Our goal is to give non-shield users a clear path to the middle of the tree, where Protection could serve as a PvP tech secondary specialization, with more access to Improved Bloodrage, Iron Will, Improved Disarm, Last Stand, and Improved Shield Bash."* `Defiance` was also cut from 5 ranks to 3.
- **Four new talents:** `Master of Defense`, `Vanguard`, `Bastion` and `Focused Rage`. `Bastion` replaces `One-Handed Weapon Specialization` at +10% damage with a shield equipped.
- **`Improved Revenge` no longer grants a stun**: Blizzard redesigned it to increase Revenge's damage instead, and [[Concussion Blow]] carries the stun now. Blizzard published no percentage; the client's own tooltip reads +60% at full rank.
- `Improved Taunt` and `Improved Shield Block` are gone from the tree because **both effects became baseline**. [[Taunt]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds without a point, and [[Shield Block]] blocks 2 attacks over 7 seconds where Classic blocked 1 over 5. You lose the talents and keep the effects.
- `Anticipation` now grants **twice as much Defense Skill per point**. `Improved Bloodrage` now boosts both the initial Rage from [[Bloodrage]] *and* its Rage-over-time tick, where Classic only touched the initial amount.
- `Improved Disarm` now reduces the cooldown on [[Disarm]] instead of extending its duration. `Improved Shield Wall` was redesigned to cut [[Shield Wall]]'s cooldown by a large amount rather than its old effect. `Focused Rage`, the new talent that the September 24 swap moves into row 5, reduces the Rage cost on all offensive abilities.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether shield-only tanking holds up now that `Defiance` and `Bastion` require a shield is untested. Icy Veins is explicit that its stat priority and gear picks are Level 20 snapshots, not an endgame ranking. Forever reworked dungeon loot and item stats wholesale, and nothing past 20 has been playtested by any of the three sources.
