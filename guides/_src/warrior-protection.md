## What Protection is for
**Shield tanking, and one of Forever's three tanks.** The shield is the engine: `Shield Specialization` at 5/5 returns **5 Rage on every block**, where Classic gave 1, and `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all now check that you are holding one.
**Icy Veins holds both halves at once:** *"extremely slow when it comes to solo leveling,"* but *"takes less damage and can handle dangerous pulls more safely."* This makes solo levelling more forgiving even while it's slower, and its Levelling score (2.5/5) is the highest of the three Warrior specs on Icy Veins' scale — no other source scores them. Fewer kills per minute, far less time sitting down. **That dungeons are where the spec pays off is our own reading; no source ranks the three Warrior specs for group play.**
Worth knowing before you commit: **a full Protection spec is not required to tank at low level.**
**The Rage rework favours Protection specifically.** Blizzard kept the old formula for Rage gained from taking damage but now calculates it as if every Warrior always had 50% damage reduction from Armor — so stacking Armor, which is what this spec does, no longer shrinks your Rage income the way it did in Classic. Damage-absorption effects like Power Word: Shield also stop blocking Rage gain.
**The October 1 build also reshuffled this tree, and said why:** *"Our goal is to give non-shield users a clear path to the middle of the tree, where Protection could serve as a PvP tech secondary specialization, with more access to Improved Bloodrage, Iron Will, Improved Disarm, Last Stand, and Improved Shield Bash."* That is a PvP framing more than a levelling one, but it moved rows our build order below assumed — see Your talent points and What Forever changed.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Wowhead and Icy Veins agree on the first ten points and split on the last.
1. `Shield Specialization`, 5 points, taken mainly for **5 rage on every block**
2. **`Improved Thunder Clap`, 3 points** for cheaper area threat, now usable in Defensive Stance
3. `Improved Bloodrage`, 2 points — **the October 1 build moved this talent to row 1** (it was row 2), so as of this build it no longer needs a single point parked elsewhere first; the published Wowhead/Icy Veins order already takes it this early regardless
4. The eleventh point is where the two sources part ways. **Icy Veins** spends it on `Master of Defense`, which grants **a chance** of 5 rage when you Dodge or Parry with a shield equipped — Blizzard publishes no proc chance. **Wowhead** puts it in `Improved Revenge` instead (redesigned to increase Revenge's damage rather than stun, with no figure published; now **row 2**, moved down from row 3 on October 1 — so this pick got easier to reach, not harder) and explicitly argues against `Master of Defense` at level 20, reasoning that ~10% total avoidance makes its proc too rare to be worth a point yet; Wowhead names `Last Stand` as its own stated alternative for that point.
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build**, reaching `Vanguard`: `Shield Specialization` 5, `Anticipation` 4, `Improved Bloodrage` 2, `Master of Defense` 2, `Defiance` 3, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Vanguard` 1 — twenty points, and **the twenty-first is a choice we will not make for you.** `Bastion` is out of reach: the September 24 swap puts it in row 6, which is 25 points into the tree. `Focused Rage` takes its place in row 5 once that swap reaches the client, and until it does, a point in any row you have already opened is the honest answer — no source publishes a level-30 Protection build, and we are not inventing a row for a talent Blizzard has not placed. **One ordering note from the October 1 reshuffle:** `Anticipation` moved from row 1 to row 2 (it now needs points spent elsewhere first) while `Improved Bloodrage` moved the opposite way, row 2 to row 1 — the total points above are unchanged, but if you are levelling this live, bank `Improved Bloodrage` first and `Anticipation` once you've unlocked row 2, not the other way around as the old row layout implied.
**New in this tree as of October 1: `Iron Will`, landing in row 1.** It reduces the duration of Stuns against you rather than resisting them, and it is not in either build above — Blizzard's own framing for the reshuffle (see above) pitches it at a non-shield PvP-secondary path, not the shield-tanking line this page follows. Worth a point if you expect PvP; skip it for pure levelling and dungeon tanking.
## Rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** always up, **[[Bloodrage]]** between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]** to open in Battle Stance, then **swap straight back to Defensive**, until you have `Vanguard`
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on non-casters — **currently a live bug, not a reason to skip it:** Blizzard's own Known Issues list for this build says multiple debuffs including Demoralizing Shout are not generating threat right now. Keep using it for the damage-reduction (that part works); don't count on it for aggro until Blizzard says it's fixed
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** on proc
5. **[[Revenge]]** on every block, dodge or parry, very cheap and a large amount of threat, so it is your best button
6. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice, **spread evenly across targets** — as of October 1 it also instantly taps every target it lands on onto your threat table, useful for grabbing adds before Demoralizing Shout's threat bug above gets fixed
**Multi-target is the payoff, and it is why the rage talents matter:** with the recommended build you can afford far more [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Cleave]] than a Warrior otherwise could.
Differences from the damage specs: **[[Revenge]] replaces [[Overpower]]**, and [[Rend]] and [[Slam]] drop out entirely.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, **Hit**, Strength, Agility, Armor, Stamina, then crit, crit above Agility if you care only about threat (Icy Veins' published order).
**Hit now improves [[Taunt]] reliability**, which is a Forever-specific reason to value it.

## What Forever changed for Protection
- **[[Thunder Clap]] is usable in Defensive Stance**, and **`Improved Thunder Clap` moved in from Arms** with its rage reduction doubled.
- **[[Rend]] and [[Sunder Armor]] now instantly tap the enemy onto your threat table** (October 1 build) — see Rotation. Separately, Blizzard's own Known Issues list says several debuffs, Demoralizing Shout included, currently generate **no threat at all** — a live bug, not intended design (see Rotation).
- **The whole tree was row-reshuffled on October 1**, and Blizzard said why: to give non-shield users a clearer path through the middle of the tree as a PvP-secondary spec. `Iron Will` is new to row 1 — it started in Fury row 2, the September 30 Class Deep Dive placed it in Arms row 2, and the October 1 build landed it here. `Anticipation` moved row 1 → row 2. `Improved Bloodrage` moved row 2 → row 1. `Improved Revenge` moved row 3 → row 2. `Improved Disarm` moved row 4 → row 3. `Improved Shield Bash` moved row 5 → row 4. None of the point costs changed, only which row gates them — see Your talent points for what that does to build order.
- **`Toughness` is gone — deleted outright** in the same pass. It was never referenced on this page before, so nothing here needed rewriting for it, but if you're carrying an older build from elsewhere that budgeted a point for it, that point is free again.
- **Three talents now check for a shield.** `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all require one, so Classic's dual-wield threat setup loses its best threat talents. The tree itself is not gated: Blizzard's stated goal for the October 1 reshuffle was *"to give non-shield users a clear path to the middle of the tree."* `Defiance` was also cut from 5 ranks to 3.
- **Four new talents:** `Master of Defense`, `Vanguard`, `Bastion` and `Focused Rage`. **`Bastion` replaces `One-Handed Weapon Specialization`** at +10% damage with a shield equipped.
- **`Improved Revenge` no longer grants a stun**: Blizzard redesigned it to increase Revenge's damage instead, without publishing a percentage, and [[Concussion Blow]] carries the stun now.
- `Improved Taunt` and `Improved Shield Block` are gone from the tree because **both effects became baseline**: [[Taunt]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds without a point, and [[Shield Block]] blocks 2 attacks over 7 seconds where Classic blocked 1 over 5. You lose the talents and keep the effects.
- `Anticipation` now grants **twice as much Defense Skill per point**. `Improved Bloodrage` now boosts both the initial Rage from [[Bloodrage]] *and* its Rage-over-time tick, where Classic only touched the initial amount.
- `Improved Disarm` now reduces the cooldown on [[Disarm]] instead of extending its duration. `Improved Shield Wall` was redesigned to cut [[Shield Wall]]'s cooldown by a large amount rather than its old effect. `Focused Rage`, the new talent that the September 24 swap moves into row 5, reduces the Rage cost on all offensive abilities.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether shield-only tanking holds up now that `Defiance` and `Bastion` require a shield is untested, and Icy Veins is explicit that its stat priority and gear picks are Level 20 snapshots, not an endgame ranking — Forever reworked dungeon loot and item stats wholesale, so nothing past 20 has been playtested by any of the three sources.
