Shield tanking, and one of Forever's three tanks. **Three of its talents now check for a shield**, which costs Classic's dual-wield threat build its best threat talents. Blizzard has deliberately kept a non-shield path to the middle of the tree.

## What Protection is for
**The shield is the engine.** `Shield Specialization` at 5/5 returns 5 Rage on every block, where Classic gave 1, and `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all now check that you are holding one.
**Icy Veins holds both halves at once:** *"extremely slow when it comes to solo leveling,"* but *"takes less damage and can handle dangerous pulls more safely."* Solo levelling is more forgiving even while it is slower: fewer kills per minute, far less time sitting down. Its Levelling score of 2.5/5 is the highest of the three Warrior specs on Icy Veins' scale, and no other source scores them. That dungeons are where the spec pays off is our own reading; no source ranks the three Warrior specs for group play.
**You do not need a full Protection spec to tank at low level.**
**The Rage rework favours Protection specifically** *(ours, not a source's)*. Armor you wear does not reduce Rage from taking damage: Blizzard's formula assumes a fixed mitigation for your level, 20% to 40%. Stacking Armor, which is what this spec does, no longer shrinks your Rage income the way it did in Classic. Absorbs like Power Word: Shield stop blocking Rage gain too.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.** Wowhead and Icy Veins agree on the first ten points and split on the last.
1. `Shield Specialization`, 5 points, taken mainly for **5 rage on every block**
2. **`Improved Thunder Clap`, 3 points** for cheaper area threat, now usable in Defensive Stance
3. `Improved Bloodrage`, 2 points — row 1, so it needs no point parked elsewhere first
4. The eleventh point is where the two sources part ways. **Icy Veins** spends it on `Master of Defense`, a 50% chance at rank 1 of 5 rage when you Dodge or Parry with a shield equipped (client; Blizzard publishes no figure). Wowhead puts it in `Improved Revenge` instead, row 2, redesigned to increase Revenge's damage rather than stun, with no figure from Blizzard. Wowhead also argues against `Master of Defense` at level 20: ~10% total avoidance makes its proc too rare to be worth a point yet. It names `Last Stand` as its own alternative for that point.
**Level 30: 0/0/21 — our own extension, not a published build**, reaching `Focused Rage`: `Shield Specialization` 5, `Improved Bloodrage` 2, `Improved Thunder Clap` 3, `Anticipation` 1, `Master of Defense` 2, `Defiance` 3, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Vanguard` 1, `Focused Rage` 1. It keeps the level 20 list, so there is no respec; if you followed Wowhead, your `Improved Revenge` point stands in for `Anticipation`. `Concussion Blow` is the alternative twenty-first point, and you need it by 40 because `Shield Slam` requires it. `Bastion` is out of reach: it sits in row 6, which is 25 points into the tree.
**`Iron Will` sits in row 1**, and it is not in either build above. It cuts the duration of Stun and Fear effects on you by 3% per point, 15% at 5/5, rather than resisting them. The Fear half is the client's reading: Blizzard's own note on the change mentions only Stuns, while the client's tooltip and the effects behind it both list the two. Worth points if you expect PvP; skip it for pure levelling and dungeon tanking.
## Rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** always up, [[Bloodrage]] between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]** to open in Battle Stance, then swap straight back to Defensive, until you have `Vanguard`
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on non-casters: it puts some threat on everything it hits (Blizzard, October 8)
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** on proc
5. **[[Revenge]]** on every block, dodge or parry: very cheap, a lot of threat, and your best button *(ours, not a source's)*. It scales with Attack Power, 22.5% at every rank (client; Blizzard posted no figure)
6. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice, spread evenly across targets. It also taps every target it lands on onto your threat table instantly, which is how you grab adds
**Multi-target is the payoff, and the rage talents are why:** with the recommended build you can afford far more [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Cleave]] than a Warrior otherwise could.
Differences from the damage specs: **[[Revenge]] replaces [[Overpower]]**, and [[Rend]] and [[Slam]] drop out entirely.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, **Hit**, Strength, Agility, Armor, Stamina, then crit — though crit goes above Agility if you care only about threat (Icy Veins' published order).
**Hit now improves [[Taunt]] reliability**, which is a Forever-specific reason to value it.

## What Forever changed for Protection
- **[[Thunder Clap]] is usable in Defensive Stance**, and `Improved Thunder Clap` moved in from Arms and takes up to 6 Rage off it, where Classic's took 4.
- **[[Rend]] and [[Sunder Armor]] now instantly tap the enemy onto your threat table**; see Rotation.
- **The whole tree was row-reshuffled**, and Blizzard said why: to give non-shield users a clearer path through the middle of the tree as a PvP-secondary spec (quoted in full below). `Iron Will` is new to row 1. `Anticipation` moved row 1 → row 2. `Improved Bloodrage` moved row 2 → row 1. `Improved Revenge` moved row 3 → row 2. `Improved Disarm` moved row 4 → row 3. `Improved Shield Bash` moved row 5 → row 4. None of the point costs changed, only which row gates them.
- **`Toughness` is gone**, in Blizzard's own words: "Toughness has been removed." If you are carrying an older build from elsewhere that budgeted a point for it, that point is free again.
- **Three talents now check for a shield.** `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense` all require one, so Classic's dual-wield threat setup loses its best threat talents. The tree itself is not gated. Blizzard's stated goal for the reshuffle: *"Our goal is to give non-shield users a clear path to the middle of the tree, where Protection could serve as a PvP tech secondary specialization, with more access to Improved Bloodrage, Iron Will, Improved Disarm, Last Stand, and Improved Shield Bash."* `Defiance` was also cut from 5 ranks to 3.
- **Four new talents:** `Master of Defense`, `Vanguard`, `Bastion` and `Focused Rage`. `Bastion` replaces `One-Handed Weapon Specialization` at +10% damage with a shield equipped.
- **`Improved Revenge` no longer grants a stun**: Blizzard redesigned it to increase Revenge's damage instead, and [[Concussion Blow]] carries the stun now. Blizzard published no percentage; the client's own tooltip reads +60% at full rank.
- `Improved Taunt` and `Improved Shield Block` are gone from the tree because **both effects became baseline**. [[Taunt]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds without a point, and [[Shield Block]] blocks 2 attacks over 7 seconds where Classic blocked 1 over 5. You lose the talents and keep the effects.
- `Anticipation` now grants **twice as much Defense Skill per point**. `Improved Bloodrage` now boosts both the initial Rage from [[Bloodrage]] *and* its Rage-over-time tick, where Classic only touched the initial amount.
- `Improved Disarm` now reduces the cooldown on [[Disarm]] instead of extending its duration. `Improved Shield Wall` was redesigned to cut [[Shield Wall]]'s cooldown by a large amount rather than its old effect. `Focused Rage`, row 5, reduces the Rage cost of offensive abilities by 1/2/3.
## At level 60
Unknown. Nobody has played level 60 yet, so whether shield-only tanking holds up now that `Defiance` and `Bastion` require a shield is untested. Icy Veins is explicit that its stat priority and gear picks are Level 20 snapshots, not an endgame ranking. Forever reworked dungeon loot and item stats wholesale.
