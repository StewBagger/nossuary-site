## What Protection is for
**Taking hits and turning them into Rage and threat.** `Shield Specialization` at 5/5 returns 5 Rage on every block, where Classic gave 1. `Master of Defense` does the same on every Dodge and Parry. That Rage pays for [[Revenge]], which was rebuilt as a damage button.
**It levels well, and the sources say so in strong terms.** Icy Veins: "Protection is the fastest Warrior specialization for leveling in WoW Forever and the one we recommend." Wowhead: Arms and Fury Warriors "will be looking to Protection for the first 35-40 levels". Both name the same limits: a slow start before [[Shield Block]] at 16, and trouble with casters and elementals, whose attacks cannot be blocked. Kargoz [argues the other side](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ZpQvbxDFlw&t=100): a shield is safer than dungeons need, and Arms kills faster. That is one creator's report.
**Threat is yours to hold.** Blessing of Salvation was removed (Blizzard), so damage dealers have no blessing to shed threat with. [[Defensive Stance]] adds 80% to all threat you generate (Blizzard, live November 4), and `Defiance` adds up to 20% more with a shield. The two multiply (WoWSims' reading of the client).
**The Rage rework favours Protection** *(ours, not a source's)*. Armor you wear does not reduce Rage from taking damage: the formula assumes 20% to 40% mitigation for your level. One limit: a hit blocked in full gives no Rage from damage taken (WoWSims' measurement). The two block talents are what pay you for it.
## Your talent points
The lines below are **Icy Veins' Protection levelling build**, in its order, as published on November 4. Every point is legal on the live tree. Each line is the whole build at that level.
**Level 20, 0/0/11:** `Shield Specialization` 5, `Improved Revenge` 3, `Improved Thunder Clap` 2, `Master of Defense` 1.
**Level 30, 0/0/21:** `Shield Specialization` 5, `Improved Revenge` 3, `Improved Thunder Clap` 3, `Master of Defense` 2, `Last Stand` 1, `Defiance` 2, `Vanguard` 1, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Focused Rage` 1.
**Level 40, 0/0/31:** `Shield Specialization` 5, `Improved Revenge` 3, `Improved Thunder Clap` 3, `Master of Defense` 2, `Last Stand` 1, `Defiance` 3, `Vanguard` 1, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Focused Rage` 3, `Concussion Blow` 1, `Bastion` 5, `Shield Slam` 1.
**Level 60, 4/16/31:** `Shield Specialization` 5, `Improved Revenge` 3, `Improved Thunder Clap` 3, `Master of Defense` 2, `Last Stand` 1, `Defiance` 3, `Vanguard` 1, `Improved Sunder Armor` 3, `Focused Rage` 3, `Concussion Blow` 1, `Bastion` 5, `Shield Slam` 1, `Improved Rend` 3, `Deflection` 1, `Cruelty` 5, `Unbridled Wrath` 5, `Enrage` 5, `Reckless Swings` 1.
**The order inside those lines:** `Shield Specialization` at 10 to 14, `Improved Revenge` at 15 to 17, two points of `Improved Thunder Clap`, then `Master of Defense` at 20 and 21. `Last Stand` at 23, `Vanguard` at 25, `Improved Sunder Armor` at 26 to 28, `Focused Rage` at 30 to 32, `Concussion Blow` at 33, `Bastion` at 35 to 39 and `Shield Slam` at 40. Then `Improved Rend`, `Cruelty`, `Unbridled Wrath` and `Enrage`, with `Reckless Swings` at 59.
Icy Veins suggests a two-hander until you train [[Revenge]] at 14, then a one-hander and shield for the rest.
**Wowhead agrees on the first step and then offers three roads.** Its priority is `Shield Specialization`, `Improved Revenge` and `Master of Defense` as early as possible. After that: stay deep Protection (its recommendation for most people), go into Arms for `Sweeping Strikes`, `Impale` and `Weaponmaster` and a stance-dancing cleave build for dungeons (28/5/18 at 60), or go into Fury for `Blood Craze`, `Enrage` and `Gore Drinker`. Kargoz [reports the last](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ZpQvbxDFlw&t=543) as the common community build: Protection down to `Master of Defense`, then Fury.
**Level-60 builds for dungeons.** Icy Veins publishes 5/15/31, Wowhead 4/15/32, and WoWSims' preset is 5/15/31. All three share `Shield Specialization` 5, `Anticipation` 5, `Improved Revenge` 3, `Last Stand`, `Master of Defense` 2, `Defiance` 3, `Concussion Blow`, `Focused Rage`, `Bastion` 5 and `Shield Slam`, with `Deflection`, `Cruelty` 5 and `Enrage` 5 outside the tree. They differ on Rage. Wowhead takes `Unbridled Wrath` 5; Icy Veins takes `Lingering Rage` 5 and says to change it for `Unbridled Wrath` if Rage runs short. Both skip `Improved Thunder Clap` and `Improved Sunder Armor` at 60 on the view that a tank being hit by a boss has Rage to spare. Wowhead's dungeon build puts `Improved Thunder Clap` 3 back in place of three `Anticipation` points.
**`Iron Will` sits in row 1** and is in none of these PvE builds. It cuts the duration of Stun and Fear effects on you by 3% per point, 15% at 5/5. Wowhead's PvP build takes all 5.
## Rotation
**Levelling, one target** (Icy Veins' order; Wowhead's agrees except where noted):
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** up, [[Bloodrage]] on cooldown, ideally between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]**, then [[Defensive Stance]]. With `Vanguard`, from 25 in the published order, you Charge without leaving the stance
3. **[[Victory Rush]]** before its 20 seconds run out
4. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** early. It puts threat on everything it hits (Blizzard)
5. **`Shield Slam`** from 40, ahead of [[Revenge]] when you have the Rage and behind it when you are low. Wowhead puts Revenge first always
6. **[[Revenge]]** every time it lights up
7. **[[Shield Block]]** against anything that melees you. Skip it against casters
8. **[[Rend]]** early if the target will live through it
9. **[[Sunder Armor]]** with spare Rage; five stacks only on elites and bosses
10. **[[Thunder Clap]]** on hard-hitting melee enemies
11. **[[Heroic Strike]]** with Rage to burn. Wowhead: above 30
**Packs:** [[Thunder Clap]] first, then the same list with [[Cleave]] in place of Heroic Strike. Wowhead's reminder: [[Retaliation]] against several enemies is a strong cooldown, and stronger with a two-hander equipped for its duration.
**While questing, front-load.** Wowhead: [[Demoralizing Shout]] and [[Thunder Clap]] together cut enemy damage by 30 to 40% depending on level, which is why they are worth the Rage at the start of a fight.
**The reasons behind the order:**
- **[[Revenge]] is the cheapest threat and damage you have.** It costs 5 Rage. Rank 2 at level 24 deals 31 to 37 plus 22.5% of Attack Power in [[Defensive Stance]], and `Improved Revenge` adds 60% at 3/3. Its threat is 2.25 times its damage (WoWSims' measurement on ranks 2 to 4). Rank 2 [[Sunder Armor]] makes 75 threat for 15 Rage before its Attack Power share.
- **[[Shield Block]] is free at 5/5 `Shield Specialization`.** It costs 10 Rage and blocks twice, and each block returns 5 Rage and opens [[Revenge]]. With fewer points the return is only 20% per point. At 60 the list presses it when no Revenge window is open and Revenge is within 1.5 seconds of ready, or at 40 Rage or more *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Shield Slam` hits for its tooltip plus twice your block value** (WoWSims' measurement). At 60 the list still casts [[Revenge]] ahead of it *(WoWSims' model)*, which matches Wowhead and differs from Icy Veins' "Shield Slam first if you have the Rage".
- **[[Rend]] or [[Sunder Armor]] on one target comes down to how long it lives.** Rank 3 Rend deals 45 over 15 seconds for 10 Rage before its Attack Power share. Sunder's threat lands at once, so grab adds with Sunder. With `Improved Sunder Armor` 3/3 it costs 12.
- **[[Demoralizing Shout]] is pack threat.** Rank 2 at level 24 puts 27 threat on each enemy it hits for 10 Rage: 108 across four, against 75 on one target from a Sunder.
- **[[Thunder Clap]] and [[Revenge]] do not compete for long.** Clap hits up to 4 targets on a 6-second cooldown for 14 Rage with `Improved Thunder Clap` 3/3. Its threat is equal to its damage with no flat bonus (WoWSims' measurement at levels 43 to 45). At 60 the list claps ahead of Revenge on three or more targets and after it on two *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Heroic Strike]] and [[Cleave]] are for surplus Rage.** Each replaces a white hit, and that hit earns no Rage. At 60 the list queues Heroic Strike from 20 Rage on one target and Cleave from 40 on a pack *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Execute]] means leaving the stance.** Wowhead notes it is your hardest hit below 20% health but needs Battle or Berserker Stance, so do not swap with a full Rage bar or under heavy damage.
## Stat priority
**While levelling:** the best one-hander you can get, a shield, then Strength and Stamina. Wowhead's levelling page: Rage is normalized, so take the higher-level weapon, and between two equal weapons take the faster one.
**At 60 both sites give more than one list**, because tank gear is a trade between threat and survival.
- **Icy Veins, offensive:** Expertise, Hit, Agility, Strength, Stamina. **Defensive:** Stamina, Armor, Agility, Dodge and Parry, Defense. It puts Defense last because [[Shield Block]] already keeps ordinary melee crits off you.
- **Wowhead, balanced:** Stamina, Expertise to 6.5%, Hit to 8%, Defense to 440, then Expertise to 14%, Armor, Strength, Agility. It also publishes a defensive and an offensive list and says to start from the balanced one.
The two disagree on Defense: Wowhead wants 440 early, Icy Veins ranks it last. Neither has raid data yet. Expertise is a new stat in Forever; 6.5% removes a boss's dodges and 14% its parries from the front (both sites).
Strength raises block value as well as Attack Power, and `Shield Slam` uses block value twice (WoWSims' measurement). ClassicWoW.gg's beta-era page says Hit gear also makes [[Taunt]] more reliable; nothing newer confirms it.

## What Forever changed for Protection
- **[[Thunder Clap]] is usable in Defensive Stance**, and `Improved Thunder Clap` moved in from Arms and takes up to 6 Rage off it, where Classic's took 4.
- **[[Rend]] and [[Sunder Armor]] instantly tap the enemy to you** (Blizzard).
- **[[Defensive Stance]] adds 80% threat**, against Classic's 30% (Blizzard). The client and its tooltip both read 80% since November 4.
- **Three talents check for a shield:** `Defiance`, `Bastion` and `Master of Defense`. Blizzard kept a path for non-shield users on purpose: *"Our goal is to give non-shield users a clear path to the middle of the tree, where Protection could serve as a PvP tech secondary specialization, with more access to Improved Bloodrage, Iron Will, Improved Disarm, Last Stand, and Improved Shield Bash."*
- **New talents:** `Master of Defense`, `Vanguard`, `Bastion` and `Focused Rage`. `Bastion` replaces `One-Handed Weapon Specialization`. `Toughness` is gone (Blizzard).
- **`Improved Revenge` no longer grants a stun.** [[Concussion Blow]] carries it: 5 seconds, 10 Rage, 45-second cooldown. It makes no threat (WoWSims' measurement).
- `Improved Taunt` and `Improved Shield Block` are gone because **both effects became baseline**. [[Taunt]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds, and [[Shield Block]] blocks 2 attacks over 7 seconds on a 5-second cooldown.
- `Anticipation` gives 4 Defense per point, 20 at 5/5. `Improved Bloodrage` adds 25/50% to all the Rage [[Bloodrage]] generates.
- `Improved Disarm` takes 7/13/20 seconds off [[Disarm]]'s 60-second cooldown. `Improved Shield Wall` takes 5.5/11 minutes off [[Shield Wall]]'s 15, leaving 4 minutes at 2/2. `Improved Shield Bash` gives [[Shield Bash]] a 50/100% chance to silence for 3 seconds.
- **Damage ranges were narrowed on November 8** with no Blizzard note: [[Revenge]] and `Shield Slam` roll closer to their average. The averages did not move.
## At level 60
Dungeons are the level-60 game until raids open on December 9. Both sites publish a shield build ending in `Shield Slam`, above. Wowhead also describes two builds without a deep Protection tree: a Fury and Protection hybrid (3/31/17) that keeps a shield and takes `Death Wish`, `Flurry` and [[Bloodthirst]], and a pure dual-wield build for enemies whose attacks cannot be blocked. It rates the hybrid a low single-digit damage gain unless you [[Execute]] in [[Berserker Stance]], and warns the dual-wield build takes about 50% more damage on a normal boss. Its author adds that endgame tuning is unknown: if bosses hit harder than Classic's, the builds will change. No raid log exists yet.
