## Is Blacksmithing worth taking?
Blacksmithing makes plate and mail, so Warriors and Paladins are its main wearers. It earns one **Legacy Point** each at skill 150, 225 and 300.
Icy Veins (November 3) calls it *"a good fit for Warriors and Paladins who want to make their own weapons as they level and plate armor later on"*, and adds that if you only want one weapon or a shield spike, buying it *"leaves your profession slot open."*
What you cannot buy is the reason to take it *(ours, not a source's)*:
- **The role-templated armour binds on pickup.** The trade camp's plans, plus a second line learned outside it, come in five stat profiles per slot and are wearable from level 12 to level 50
- **Three level-60 weapons and four armour pieces bind on pickup**, listed under level 60 below
- **Two blacksmith-only thrown weapons** that double as your hammer
**There is no profession perk.** Wowhead (November 7) says *"there are no WoTLK-style Profession Perks in WoW: Forever"*. Claims of stat belt buckles or a free repair cooldown date from the early beta. Neither is in the client.
## What it pairs with
Blacksmithing eats ore, and [Mining](/guides/wow-forever/professions/mining/) is the only profession that gathers it. Icy Veins pairs the two. Wowhead (November 7) prefers Engineering as the partner, because both level on the same bars and it adds bombs and gadgets. That choice means buying your ore.
Its camp object is [[Sharpening Wheel]] at skill 20. It gives Strength to everyone sitting at the fire and is exclusive with [[Strength of Earth Totem]]. The client scales it by character level: **6 Strength below level 24, 11 from 24, 20 from 38 and 34 from level 52.** Blizzard's October 21 camping article gives the same range, 6 to 34.
Two Blueprints replace it and keep its buff:
- **Anvil** at skill 140: a working anvil at camp. Icy Veins (October 19) has the Blueprint dropping from Gilnid in the Deadmines
- **Master Forge** at skill 300: the station most level-60 recipes need. It costs a Golem Heart, 5 Thorium Bar and 2 Rugged Leather. No source says where the Blueprint or the Golem Heart drops
The [Camping guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/camping/) has the full table.

## Levelling it
**To 225 this is Icy Veins' route**, dated November 4.
1. **1 → 25**: Rough Sharpening Stone, about 30. Each adds 2 damage to a sharp weapon for 30 minutes, so keep some
2. **25 → 50**: Rough Grinding Stone, about 30. Keep them for the copper steps
3. **50 → 67**: Coarse Grinding Stone. Make 36 and keep them all for steps 6 and 8
4. **67 → 80**: Runed Copper Gauntlets, then Runed Copper Belt. Train Journeyman before 75
5. **80 → 95**: Rough Bronze Leggings
6. **95 → 100**: Patterned Bronze Bracers, using the stones from step 3
7. **100 → 112**: Heavy Grinding Stone. Make 20 and keep them for step 10
8. **112 → 125**: Patterned Bronze Bracers again
9. **125 → 165**: Bronze Warhammer, about 44. Train Expert before 150
10. **165 → 185**: Green Iron Bracers, then Golden Scale Bracers
11. **185 → 200**: Heavy Mithril Gauntlet
12. **200 → 225**: Mithril Plate Pants, then Heavy Mithril Breastplate
**Blizzard changed step 3 after that route was written.** Since the November 10 hotfix, Coarse Grinding Stone gives skill-ups to 90, where it used to stop at 75. If stone is cheaper than copper on your realm, keep grinding past 67 and skip some gauntlets *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Shopping list to 225** (Icy Veins): 532 Bronze Bar, 478 Mithril Bar, 114 Copper Bar, 90 Iron Bar, 50 Steel Bar, 90 Rough Stone, 72 Coarse Stone, 60 Heavy Stone, 60 Mageweave Cloth, 44 Medium Leather, 44 Strong Flux and 15 Green Dye.
**Above 225 no guide site has a route.** Icy Veins says it does not yet have enough information to recommend one. The only published path is foreverchanges.pro's, computed from the client's skill colours at build 70938 and not from play:
1. **225 → 235**: Dense Grinding Stone. Keep them, and make more for the steps below
2. **235 → 242**: Heavy Mithril Boots
3. **242 → 272**: Ebon Shiv, about 41
4. **272 → 293**: Ornate Thorium Handaxe, about 34
5. **293 → 300**: [[Huge Thorium Battleaxe]], about 21
All three weapon plans are sold by Magnus Frostwake in Western Plaguelands, per the same page. **Budget for thorium**: its whole 1 to 300 list holds 1,520 Thorium Bar, 964 Mithril Bar, 776 Dense Stone and 256 Truesilver Bar.
**These counts ignore `Working Overtime`**, the Legacy perk that adds 4% skill-up chance per rank, 20% at five ranks.
## Specialisations
[[Armorsmith]] and [[Weaponsmith]] are live, along with vanilla's three Weaponsmith branches: Master Swordsmith, Hammersmith and Axesmith. Wowhead (November 7) lists what each one alone can make:
| Specialisation | Recipes | Skill range | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Armorsmith | 18 | 260 to 300 | Lionheart Helm, the Enchanted Thorium set, Stronghold Gauntlets, Dark Iron Plate |
| Weaponsmith | 8 | 240 to 300 | Dark Iron Pulverizer, Blood Talon, Heartseeker |
| Master Axesmith | 5 | 250 to 300 | Dawn's Edge, Arcanite Reaper, Nightfall |
| Master Hammersmith | 6 | 255 to 300 | Masterwork Stormhammer, Hammer of the Titans, Persuader |
| Master Swordsmith | 7 | 255 to 300 | Corruption, Arcanite Champion, Sageblade |
Those are the vanilla specialisation recipes. None of the new Forever plans is tied to one in that list.
**How you specialise is not published.** Wowhead's page has a heading for the specialisation quests and nothing under it. Icy Veins says only that *"as you gain skill, you can specialize in Armorsmithing or Weaponsmithing"*. Vanilla asked for level 40 and skill 200. Treat that as a guess until a source confirms it.
## At level 60
- **Most level-60 recipes need a Master Forge.** Wowhead counts 65 of them and foreverchanges.pro 62. That covers Lionheart Helm, the Enriched Thorium pieces, every piece of the crafted sets and all the new Legionite weapons. Either reach 300 and place one, or stand at somebody else's
- **Dark Iron recipes still need the Black Anvil** in Blackrock Depths, 20 of them in Wowhead's list
- **Most level-60 plate is Bind on Equip**, so anyone can buy it. At item level 58 and above the client binds only seven Blacksmithing items on pickup: Enriched Thorium Helm, Leggings and Breastplate (item level 62 to 63, Defense, Stamina and Spell Power), Dark Iron Plate, and three weapons, Corruption, Blood Talon and Legionite Glaive
- **The Bind on Pickup tank line starts lower.** Sentinel's pieces run from skill 150 to 250, ending in Sentinel's Gloves and Sentinel's Waistguard at item level 55 with Defense, Stamina and Strength
- **Seven new pieces are item level 65 and Bind on Equip**: Ironwood Blade, Forest Defender's Axe, Greenhammer, Evergreen Shield, Depleted Thorium Sabatons, Heavy Thorium Gauntlets and Azerothium Legplates. Each takes 2 to 4 [[Malleable Essence of Nature]], a Legionite Bar and 10 to 20 [[Heavy Thorium Bar]]. No source records where their plans come from
- **Five crafted sets at skill 300.** Three are named Justice and two Glory, four pieces each: legs, hands, shoulders and feet. foreverchanges.pro has all 20 plans sold by Aisarra Nightmeadow on Mount Hyjal. A piece costs 3 or 5 Malleable Essence of Nature, 2 or 4 Arcanite Bar, 12 or 18 Heavy Thorium Bar and an Elemental Flux. The client does not describe the items, so their stats, set bonuses and binding are unknown
**None of the Malleable Essence pieces can be made yet.** The client's item text says the essence is *"acquired from disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps"*, and Blizzard opens both raids on December 9. You will need an enchanter.
**The new bars are a chain across three professions:**
1. A miner at skill 300 mines Heavy Thorium Ore on Mount Hyjal
2. The ore is smelted into [[Heavy Thorium Bar]] at a Molten Foundry, Mining's own skill-300 camp object
3. An alchemist transmutes Heavy Thorium Bars into Legionite Bars. Wowhead knows no other source
4. [[Azerothium Bar]] is smelted from 2 Pitchblende and 1 Coal at skill 275
The [Mining guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/mining/) covers the ore. Do not build a shopping list for these from a vanilla guide.
**What Engineering locks is the reverse**: items you cannot *use* without the skill, where Blacksmithing's are mostly items you can buy. The [Engineering guide](/guides/wow-forever/professions/engineering/) lists them.
## What Forever changed
- **A role-templated armour line at the trade camp.** 61 plans bought with [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/): five mail lines from skill 45 to 175 at 30 and 60 Favor, five plate lines from 210 to 250 at 60 and 90, two weapons at 250, and three Enriched Thorium pieces and the Legionite Glaive at 300 for 120 each. Each line is one stat profile, so the tank version carries Defense Rating and the healer version Spell Damage and Healing. They bind on pickup
- **A second role line outside the camp**: Chain Shirt at skill 80, Chain Leggings at 95 and Chain Helm at 125 in mail, then Belt at 150, Boots at 200 and Gloves at 250. The client turns each one grey five points after you learn it, so they are gear, not levelling fodder. foreverchanges.pro records a source for the Belt plans only
- **Blizzard cut what vendors pay for that gear.** The November 10 hotfix *"reduced the price vendors pay for some crafted armor items"*, all of them Bind on Pickup pieces *"a player was intended to craft only once for themselves."* Wowhead's list of changed items includes the Chain and the Belt, Boots and Gloves pieces. Do not craft them to vendor
- **Blacksmith hammers you can throw.** [[Mithril Blacksmith Hammer]] at skill 195 and [[Arcanite Blacksmith Hammer]] at 270 are Bind on Pickup thrown weapons that also *"serve as a Blacksmith Hammer"*. The Mithril one can slow its target and the Arcanite one can stun for 1 second
- **At level 20 the helm is the piece to make.** foreverchanges.pro's level-20 gear lists, dated September 23, put the Silvered Chain Helm first for every Warrior and Paladin spec: Veteran's for Retribution, Arms and Fury, Acolyte's for Holy, Crusader's for Protection Paladin and [[Guard's Silvered Chain Helm]] for Protection Warrior. Each is skill 95 and 30 Favor
- **All five Weightstones lost their cloth component.** They now cost only the stone. Those are five of only six recipes in 437 whose reagents changed
- **Certification** at skill 300 for 1,000 Merchant's Favor unlocks the account-wide title "the Blacksmith"
- **Plans also drop in dungeons.** Blizzard's October 1 notes added *"a number of recipes intended for Blacksmithing and Leatherworking as random drops in dungeons"*. No drop list has been published
## What is not known
The specialisation quest and its requirements. Where the Master Forge Blueprint and the Golem Heart drop. Where the plans for the seven Malleable Essence pieces and the outside role line come from. What the crafted sets do. What 70 further recipes make: foreverchanges.pro leaves that many out because the client does not describe their item. And whether any vanilla recipe was removed, since no source publishes a removal list.
