Two-handed melee that feeds spellcasting: [[Stormstrike]], a weapon imbue and `Maelstrom Weapon` turn swings into Shocks and instant [[Lightning Bolt]]s. **Blizzard retuned the spec on November 12**, and most stat and talent advice written before that date is out of date.

## What Enhancement is for
**A slow two-hander with a weapon imbue that lasts an hour.** [[Rockbiter Weapon]] carries you to 30. [[Windfury Weapon]] arrives at 30: each hit has a 20% chance of two extra attacks. [[Stormstrike]] arrives at 25: 100% weapon damage on an 8-second cooldown, and +20% on your next [[Lightning Bolt]], [[Chain Lightning]] or [[Earth Shock]] against that target.
**The November 12 changes pulled the spec back toward its weapon.** Blizzard's reason: the beta changes *"caused Enhancement to favor Spell Power and Intellect gear too heavily"* and *"pushed Enhancement away from Windfury, which we believe should remain at the heart of the specialization's fantasy and gameplay."* What changed:
- [[Stormstrike]] deals 100% weapon damage (was 150%) on an 8-second cooldown (was 6).
- `Mental Dexterity` converts 33/66/100% of Intellect to attack power (was 50/100/150%).
- `Maelstrom Weapon` gives 20% per charge at every rank, and procs 6/7/8/9/10 times a minute by rank (was 20 a minute at any rank).
- **Weapon imbues and weapon totems now work together.** A Shaman with [[Windfury Weapon]] also benefits from [[Windfury Totem]], and can proc both.
**Mana is the limiter while levelling.** Both guide sites say so. Icy Veins lists *"Extremely high Mana costs"* as the first weakness, and Wowhead's levelling page advises that *"If mana is an issue, you can stop using your Shocks and just let your auto attacks carry."*
**The sources agree it is the solo levelling tree.** Wowhead's Restoration levelling page tells solo players to *"look to play Enhancement instead"*, and its Elemental levelling page recommends a hybrid built in the Enhancement tree. Icy Veins rates the spec 4 of 5 overall, 4 for levelling, 4 for dungeons and 4.5 for mobility; a levelling score measures solo kill speed.
**Shamans cannot dual wield in Forever.** Wowhead: *"There won't be any official support for dual-wielding, and the skill has been removed in this version of the game."* Buy one big two-hander.

## Rotation
Before the pull:
1. **Imbue your weapon**: [[Rockbiter Weapon]] before 30, [[Windfury Weapon]] from 30
2. **[[Lightning Shield]]** up, or [[Water Shield]] if you have talented it
3. **Totems**: [[Strength of Earth Totem]], or [[Stoneskin Totem]] if you are taking damage; [[Searing Totem]], or `Magma Totem` if the Mana allows; `Mana Spring Totem`; [[Windfury Totem]] from 32
In combat, the priority Wowhead, Icy Veins and WoWSims' model share:
1. **[[Flame Shock]]** if it is not on the target
2. **[[Stormstrike]]** on cooldown, from 25
3. **[[Lightning Bolt]]** at five `Maelstrom Weapon` charges, from 35 at the earliest
4. **[[Earth Shock]]**
5. **[[Fire Nova]]** with a Fire totem down and Mana to spare
6. **Auto-attack**: most of your damage while levelling
**Pull with rank-1 [[Lightning Bolt]]**, the cheapest thing that pulls. Use `Rage of the Farseer` with trinkets and racials from 40.
**Why this order.**
- **`Shamanistic Focus` makes Shocks your cheap damage.** With it, around level 20, [[Earth Shock]] returns about 1.5 damage per point of Mana, [[Frost Shock]] about 1.6 and [[Flame Shock]] about 2.0 over 12 seconds, against about 1.25 for [[Lightning Bolt]] *(our arithmetic, from the client's rank figures before gear)*.
- **The three Shocks share a 6-second cooldown.** On anything that lives 12 seconds, Flame Shock is the better use of it. On shorter fights Earth Shock lands it all at once *(ours, not a source's)*. Each point of `Call of Flame` adds 3 seconds to Flame Shock.
- **In a group, mind your threat.** [[Earth Shock]] *"causes a high amount of threat"* in its own tooltip. Icy Veins' levelling list switches to [[Frost Shock]] when threat is a problem. `Spirit Weapons` at 30 or later cuts your threat by 30% while Rockbiter is off.
- **Keep rank 1 [[Earth Shock]] for interrupts.** Every rank interrupts the cast and locks that school for 2 seconds, and rank 1 costs 30 Mana before `Shamanistic Focus`.
- **Spend [[Stormstrike]] on something worth it.** Its 20% reaches only your next [[Lightning Bolt]], [[Chain Lightning]] or [[Earth Shock]]. [[Flame Shock]] and [[Frost Shock]] get nothing.
- **Wait for five `Maelstrom Weapon` charges.** Each charge takes 20% off the next Bolt's cast time and Mana at any rank, so five make it instant and free. Icy Veins adds that a Bolt cast below five charges resets your swing timer. Charges last 30 seconds.
- **[[Fire Nova]] is for packs.** It gets no `Shamanistic Focus` discount, and all three sources put it last on one target. On packs it moves to the top of both sites' lists, with [[Flame Shock]] spread across targets.
**Windfury or Rockbiter while levelling.** Icy Veins switches to [[Windfury Weapon]] at 30. WoWSims' level-60 model changed its default from Rockbiter to Windfury on November 12, at 778.8 against 751.5 DPS with [[Windfury Totem]] also up *(WoWSims' model, at 60)*. One creator levelling the spec [prefers Rockbiter's steadier damage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v0mDnMb_apE&t=272), a single report recorded the day before the change landed.
## Stat priority
1. **Weapon DPS on a slow two-hander.** Icy Veins suggests 3.3 to 3.8 speed
2. **Hit**, to 8% for melee, and **Expertise**, 6.5% in Wowhead's figure
3. **Strength and attack power**
4. Intellect
5. Critical strike, Haste and Agility
6. Spell Power
7. Spirit and Stamina
**Strength over Intellect is Blizzard's position, and the guide sites have not caught up.** Blizzard, November 6: *"We plan to make changes so that Strength is always their strongest stat"*, and *"Enhancement Shaman players who are leveling up will want to collect Strength and Attack Power gear from now on."* Wowhead's stat page (November 3) and Icy Veins' (November 4) both still rank Intellect above Strength. Both predate the change.
**The arithmetic behind it.** A point of Strength is 2 attack power. A point of Intellect is 1 attack power through `Mental Dexterity` 3/3, plus 15 Mana, some spell crit, and spell damage through `Mental Quickness`. Before November 12 it was 1.5 attack power. Blizzard still expects *"some Intellect and/or Spell Power"* on your best items. The order of items 4 to 7 above is our own reading; no source has published a list since the change.
**Hit and Expertise first** is the one thing both sites and WoWSims' weights agree on. A missed two-hander swing is a long wait.
**The class quest weapon was changed to match.** `Rage of the Storm` is the two-handed mace from The Tempest's Weapons, the chain that follows the level-30 Air totem quest. Since November 10 it has 16 Strength where it had Intellect and swings at 3.6 where it swung at 3.2, and it adds 10% to [[Stormstrike]] damage. Icy Veins calls the chain *"Difficult at Level 30"*: it needs a Razorfen Kraul run, drops from zones around 35 to 40, and ends in waves of elites.

## What Forever changed for Enhancement
- **Weapon imbues last 60 minutes instead of 5**, and run alongside a sharpening stone, weight stone or oil. Buff totems last 5 minutes instead of 2 and reach 30 yards.
- **[[Windfury Totem]] and Flametongue Totem are raid-wide buffs, not weapon enchants.** The two exclude each other. Since November 12 your own matching imbue no longer blocks the totem.
- **New:** `Mental Dexterity`, `Shamanistic Focus`, `Mental Quickness`, `Improved Stormstrike`, `Maelstrom Weapon`, `Rage of the Farseer`. `Parry` is renamed `Spirit Weapons`. `Shield Specialization` is removed. Now baseline, so no points are spent on them: `Enhancing Totems`, `Two-Handed Axes and Maces`, `Weapon Mastery` and `Improved Weapon Totems`.
- **[[Stormstrike]] is a 16-point talent**, where Classic made it the 31-point capstone on a 20-second cooldown.
- **`Thundering Strikes` is row 1 and `Elemental Weapons` row 3.** `Ancestral Knowledge` grants Intellect rather than maximum Mana. `Flurry` gives 6% attack speed a rank, 30% at 5/5.
- **`Improved Ghost Wolf` sits at row 4**, so 25 at the earliest. Rank 1 makes [[Ghost Wolf]] usable indoors and cuts 1 second off the cast; rank 2 makes it instant.
- **`Rage of the Farseer` does not increase casting speed.** Blizzard's September 24 notes: the talent *"no longer increases the Shaman's Spell Casting Speed"*.
- **A capstone conflict:** `Rage of the Farseer` needs 31 Enhancement points and `Nature's Swiftness` 21 Restoration: 52 against a 51-point budget.
- **Hit and critical strike are unified** across spell, melee and ranged. `Thundering Strikes` now *"increases critical strike chance of all spells and attacks instead of only weapon attacks"*.
## On Enhancement tanking
**Blizzard sanctions it, in one shape only.** Of the redesigned `Spirit Weapons`, the deep dive says it *"will help Enhancement Shaman manage their threat now that their damage is competitive, while also retaining their unique ability to generate high threat and serve as a temporary tank in small group gameplay"*. `Improved Stormstrike` exists partly to give you *"more active Stormstrike usage when serving as a temporary tank."*
**`Spirit Weapons` is the mechanism.** The tooltip: *"Gives a chance to parry enemy melee attacks, reduces all threat generated by your attacks by 30% while [[Rockbiter Weapon]] is not active, and increases all threat generated by 30% while Rockbiter Weapon is active."* You tank in Rockbiter and drop it to go back to damage. There is no taunt and no defensive cooldown. Your survival talents are `Anticipation`, 6% Dodge at 3/3, and `Toughness`, 10% Stamina at 5/5.
**One published guide goes further.** A community guide hosted on Wowhead, by Caperfin and last changed November 10, tanks dungeons with a shield, [[Rockbiter Weapon]] and [[Fire Nova]] for area threat. It publishes two builds, 17/23/11 and 0/20/31, and both are legal on the current tree. It is one author's method and nothing in our evidence tests it. Icy Veins' class overview stops at *"off-tanking duties, or serve as tanks for dungeons in a pinch."* Blizzard names no dungeon or raid tanking role for Shaman.
## At level 60
**Sourced:** the builds above, the shared priority list, and Blizzard's statement that Strength gear is the intended direction. Icy Veins notes the spec *"relies a lot on a Paladin's Judgement of Wisdom to sustain a full rotation"* in raids. Every melee group wants a [[Windfury Totem]], which any Shaman provides.
**Not known:** how the spec performs after November 12. No raid opens until December 9, so there is no raid log, and no guide site has rewritten its build or stat page since the change. Blizzard said it changed the spec before the raid on purpose, so players would not win *"highly contested items that could become much less valuable after a later change."* Blizzard also tunes classes on Tuesdays *"if necessary"*, so check the date on anything you read.
