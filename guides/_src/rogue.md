Energy regenerates continuously, poisons and bleeds can crit, and Mutilate locks out Adrenaline Rush by exactly one talent point.
## Blizzard has published almost nothing about Rogue
The entire Rogue section of the beta development notes is one line: *"Sap now correctly flags the Rogue for PvP when used on a target that is PvP flagged."* Everything below is datamining and community work.
**A trap first.** Searching for Forever Rogue hotfixes surfaces real Blizzard pages mentioning Eviscerate and other Rogue abilities. **Those pages cover retail Midnight, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Season of Discovery, Burning Crusade Classic, Classic Era and Hardcore — there is no WoW Forever section on them at all.** Retail Rogue tuning is not Forever Rogue tuning.
## The headline changes
**All four weapon specializations were deleted and folded into one talent, Hack and Slash.**
| Weapon | At 5 ranks |
| --- | --- |
| Axe or sword | **5% chance** of an extra attack |
| Dagger or fist | **+5% critical strike chance** |
| Mace | attacks ignore **15% of target armor** |
This is structurally identical to Warrior's Weaponmaster. Two consequences: **the mace branch deletes Classic's mace stun proc**, and **one-handed axes are now usable by Rogues**, which is why the axe branch exists and why Orc's axe racial suddenly matters.
**Weapon Expertise keeps its name and does something completely different.** It now reduces the chance your attacks are dodged or parried by up to 2%. Vanilla's version granted *weapon skill*; Forever's grants **none at all**. And a warning worth keeping: **a miss and a dodge are different results, so the second point is not another point of hit.**
**Energy regeneration is continuous, not ticked** — smoothed to roughly 1 energy per 0.1 seconds instead of 20-energy chunks every 2 seconds. The rotational consequence is specific: **Backstab and Ambush are harder to chain**, because you can no longer bank a tick to land exactly when you need it. Note "continuous" is not "fast", and no source publishes an energy-per-second figure.
**Combo points persist per target, but the popular framing overstates it.** They "persist on a target even if you swap to another, but you **cannot** transfer your combo points from an existing target to a new one." Per-target retention, not a pooled resource — which is exactly what the community is angry about, since points are still lost on tab-target and wasted entirely when the mob dies.
## The 16-point milestone
| Tree | The new 16-point talent |
| --- | --- |
| Assassination | **Cold Blood**, moved down |
| Combat | **Flawless Execution** — brand new, Eviscerate costs 10 less energy |
| Subtlety | **Premeditation**, moved down |
Worth knowing how this was resolved, because it is a lesson about datamining: one sweep concluded Forever has **no** 16-point milestone, because no talent carries a required-points value of 16. That is a false negative — **15 points *required* means available at your 16th point.**
## Talents
**Assassination.** *Lethality* was **nerfed from 30% to 20%** crit damage but widened to cover five more abilities. *Improved Expose Armor* was **completely rewritten** — from stronger armor reduction to a cheaper cost plus a **1–2 combo point refund at 5 points**. *Improved Kidney Shot* was **narrowed**: Classic boosted damage from *all sources*, Forever only from **your own** attacks and poisons. *Murder* lost Beasts and Dragonkin. *Improved Poisons* gained a **10–50% chance not to consume a charge**. *Vigor* raises **maximum energy, not regeneration**. *Seal Fate* now reaches 100%.
**Combat took two real nerfs.** ***Precision* was gutted from 5 ranks at 5% hit to 3 ranks at 3%** — a straight −2% hit for every Combat rogue. ***Dual Wield Specialization* was halved**, from 5×10% to 5×5% off-hand damage. Against that, Improved Eviscerate was buffed and *Puncturing Wounds* moved here from Assassination, giving Backstab a **45% chance of a bonus combo point**.
**Subtlety** got the most new material. **Quietus** adds damage below 35% health, **Cutthroat** gives Backstab a chance that your next **Ambush needs no Stealth**, and the capstone **Thousand Cuts** cuts your next Hemorrhage or Backstab cost as Rupture ticks. *Setup* and *Initiative* both reach **100%**, large buffs. *Dirty Deeds* means **Garrote no longer requires being behind the target**.
**Hemorrhage's rework is the headline and it is a loss.** Classic Hemorrhage added damage to *the raid's* physical attacks. Forever's supports **your** bleed instead — +15% Rupture damage from you. **Subtlety no longer brings a group physical-damage debuff.**
Removed from Subtlety: Sleight of Hand, Deadliness, and *Improved Sap* — the last because **Sap no longer breaks Stealth in Forever**, so the talent had nothing to do.
## The two new abilities
**Mutilate** — Assassination 21-point, 60 energy, instant, daggers only. Both weapons for 75% weapon damage plus a flat bonus each, **+20% against poisoned targets**, awards **2 combo points**. It does not consume poison stacks and does not require attacking from behind, and because it uses both weapons directly, **off-hand weapon damage matters for the builder itself**. A live tuning tell: the flat bonus was **+13 in a 17 September snapshot and +17 at the current build**.
**Venom** — the capstone, requires Mutilate. Increases **your poison damage by 30% and application chance by 10%**. Combo points buy **duration only**, not a larger bonus. It is a self-buff, not a damaging finisher, and it is **not eligible for Cold Blood**. It is not Envenom.
## Poisons
**Poison damage now scales with Attack Power, and poisons and bleeds can crit** — a genuine departure from 1.12. That makes crit matter to Rupture, Garrote and every poison tick.
**Poisons still have charges**, now alongside a one-hour duration, and charges run out first in practice. **They stack with Sharpening Stones and with Windfury Totem** — which has the rare distinction of being **Blizzard-confirmed**: *"Windfury and weapon enhancements/poisons stacked on the Blizzcon demo which was level 38."*
One subtlety that will cost you damage: **a coating is not a target debuff.** Instant Poison's immediate damage does not by itself leave a poison *effect*; Deadly Poison does. **Mutilate's +20% needs an actual poison sitting on the enemy.**
Poison base damage was cut across all ranks, with one tester estimating roughly 30% and no compensation.
## Rotation
**At 20, Combat and Assassination share an identical priority**, which tells you the specs have not diverged: Instant Poison on **both** weapons, Stealth into **Ambush**, **Backstab** from behind or **Sinister Strike** otherwise, then **Slice and Dice** at 5 points if the target lives its full duration, else **Eviscerate**. It is described plainly as "two buttons" plus utility, and **Combat is the safest levelling route**.
**Subtlety is the weakest at 20** and **Rupture is usually not worth casting** — *"even on targets that live the full duration, you're better off using Slice and Dice."* The **Gouge into Backstab** setup is the only non-Stealth way behind a target early: wait until you are near max energy before Gouging, or the window closes before you can afford Backstab.
**Weapon-swap macros are genuinely part of level-20 play** — dagger for Ambush, slow main hand for Sinister Strike. Swapping costs a GCD *and* resets your swing timer, and the macros need pressing twice.
### The forced shape at 60
Venom needs 31 Assassination and requires Mutilate. Adrenaline Rush needs 31 Combat. Thousand Cuts needs 31 Subtlety.
> **Mutilate and Adrenaline Rush cannot coexist.** Mutilate needs at least 21 Assassination and Adrenaline Rush needs 31 Combat — a 52-point minimum against a 51-point budget. The same exclusion kills Mutilate plus Thousand Cuts.
Forever forbids the dagger-Mutilate/Adrenaline-Rush hybrid **by exactly one point**. What the spare points buy: **Cold Blood at 16 Assassination** fits alongside Adrenaline Rush, and **Flawless Execution at 16 Combat** fits alongside Venom — though it is worth less to a build spending most finishers on poison upkeep.
## Stats
**Weapon damage is king**, then hit and Expertise, then Agility, then crit. Subtlety's guide is the weapon-speed-aware one: **favour slow main hands** — *"between a 15 DPS weapon that has a 1.9 speed, and a 14 DPS weapon with a 2.8 speed, the 2.8 speed one will serve you better."*
**Does 1.12 hit-cap theory still hold? Partly, and nobody has published the numbers.** A yellow and white distinction appears to survive — *"shared stats still do not mean every attack has an identical hit requirement"* is the two-roll model still speaking — but that is an inference from careful phrasing, not a stated cap split. **No hit cap number exists anywhere for Rogue.** On the dual-wield miss penalty there is no evidence of change and no confirmation of retention; one player asserts 19%, in a thread reporting a **70–80% miss rate with dual daggers at level 10**.
**Two poison-gearing traps** both careful guides shut down: unified hit and crit does **not** mean poisons scale with spell power, and **armor penetration and the Hack and Slash mace branch do nothing for poison damage or bleeds.**
## Race
**Tauren remains the only Classic race that cannot be a Rogue. Skyborne can**, on either faction.
**Human Sword Specialization is +2%, double every other weapon racial.** **Orc Axe Specialization is newly relevant**, because Rogues can use one-handed axes now. **Dwarf gained Mace Specialization**, new to the race.
**Night Elf's Elune's Light — +10% crit for 15 seconds — is arguably the strongest new Rogue racial in the game**, because it is an on-use burst window that pairs with Adrenaline Rush. Dwarf Stoneform now gives flat 10% physical damage reduction plus bleed, poison and disease immunity.
## Sentiment
Negative and specific. **Combat levelling is considered weak**, with an itemised case: Eviscerate feeling weak despite 3/3 Improved Eviscerate, Precision's cut hurting early hit, Dual Wield Spec's halving reducing white damage, poison damage down with no compensation. **Both new capstones are disliked** — *"terrible designs and completely useless in pvp"* — and Thousand Cuts drew *"100% the wrong direction"*. **Combo points are the loudest complaint.** **Dual-wielding at level 10 is reported as a DPS *loss*** versus a single weapon.
Counterpoint, from a tester who finds the class smooth: they prefer spending finishers at 2–3 combo points, because *"building all the way to 5 combo points often feels too expensive in terms of energy and time."*
**No Blizzard reply appears in any Rogue feedback thread.**
## Still unknown
Any hit cap value, and whether the yellow and white split survives. Whether the dual-wield miss penalty changed. Energy regeneration in numbers, base pool, and the GCD. **Cloak of Shadows**, mentioned by no source. **Redirect**, datamined but not on any trainer. And **Restless Blades**, which one guide's prose describes as a Combat talent — it appears **nowhere** in that same site's own talent data. Almost certainly stale pre-beta text.
