Rogue is a leather-wearing melee class with no self-heal that opens fights from stealth. Forever made Lockpicking a level 1 secondary skill, made Poisons a secondary skill too, still from level 20, and **using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. The beta closed on October 23 at level 30, so nothing above 30 was tested by players; talents and spells past 30 come from the launch client and Blizzard's posts. Blizzard published its first Rogue class deep dive on October 7, and it is the authority on this page: where a guide site or the client still carries an older talent name or effect, Blizzard's text wins. Ability training levels and the percentages below are read from the client, which Blizzard mostly does not publish. The 31-point talents `Venom` and `Thousand Cuts` arrive at level 40, past anything the beta reached. If a tooltip on this page differs from the text, go by the text.

| **[Combat](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/combat/)** | Melee DPS | Every level-20 point improves the two buttons you press, [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]] | Any weapon type, per Blizzard, plus `Riposte` |
| **[Subtlety](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/subtlety/)** | Melee DPS | `Ghostly Strike` at 20 is non-positional, so you build combo points without getting behind the target | Stealth openers and a [[Rupture]] bleed cycle, with **no area damage of any kind** |
**All three are melee damage**, so pick on how you want to play rather than on role. The spec pages carry each source's ratings and quotes in full.
## Is Rogue for you?
Play Rogue if you like deciding when a fight starts, and you are willing to level a class wearing leather with no self-heal.
:::strengths
- You choose when every fight starts: [[Stealth]] from level 1, opening with [[Ambush]] from 18
- [[Stealth]] and [[Sprint]] let you walk past a fight instead of winning it
- Lockpicking and Poisons are secondary skills now, so neither costs a talent point
:::
:::weaknesses
- Leather armour, no healing, and no way to protect an ally
- No area damage before [[Blade Flurry]] at 30, and Wowhead says Subtlety has *"no AoE capabilities whatsoever"*
- [[Backstab]] and [[Ambush]] both need you behind the target, and a weapon swap costs a global cooldown
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Icy Veins calls Combat *"two buttons"* — build combo points, then spend them.
**Is it forgiving?** No: no self-heal, and leather armour.
**What carries you while levelling?** [[Stealth]] from level 1, so you choose every fight.
**Does it need a group?** No. [[Stealth]] lets you walk past the fights you would lose.
:::

## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP, specifically the ones that break crowd control. `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead) stops fear, sleep and charm; `Escape Artist` (Gnome) breaks roots and snares; `Stoneform` (Dwarf) clears bleeds, poisons and disease; `Will to Survive` (Human) removes stuns. A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance: Human** for long fights, on `Ruthless` at +1% crit with all spells and attacks. Gnome is the better levelling pick, for `Eureka!` and `Escape Artist`. Both calls are *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Horde: no clear winner** *(ours, not a source's)*. Orc has `Blood Fury`, 10% Attack Power for 15 seconds on a 2-minute cooldown, and `Hardiness`, which makes stuns 20% shorter. Troll has `Berserking`, 10% attack speed for 15 seconds. Undead has `Will of the Forsaken` and `Touch of the Grave`, a 20% chance for a Rogue's attacks to drain Health, with a 15-second internal cooldown.
**Weapon choice is looser in Forever.** No racial depends on a weapon, Rogues can use one-handed axes, and `Hack and Slash` gives axes the same bonus as swords. Your weapon no longer picks your spec for you.
## Quality of life
- **The level-20 poison quest is the one thing you must not skip** *(ours, not a source's)*. Reagents come from a poison vendor near your class trainer. In Forever a poison and a sharpening stone or weightstone go on the same weapon at once, where Classic made you choose. Every poison also carries more charges than it did.
- **You need two weapons and a swap macro:** a dagger main-hand for [[Ambush]] and [[Backstab]], and a slow non-dagger for [[Sinister Strike]]. Swapping costs a global cooldown and resets your swing timer.
- Lockpicking is free money from level 1 now, and pairs with [[Pick Pocket]] at 4. First Aid is near-mandatory with no self-heal *(ours, not a source's)*, but you can [[Gouge]] or [[Blind]] and bandage mid-fight.
- [[Distract]] now cuts enemy stealth detection for 10 seconds, which is how you open a [[Sap]] on one of a pair. Blizzard's own warning for PvP: *"player enemies will now know there is a Rogue nearby to look for."*
- **A known issue, not intended design:** Blizzard's last known-issues list (October 8) had enemies Parrying and Blocking from behind at very close range, and no fix has been announced. Blizzard's workaround is to not crowd the target. Leave a step of room before you open with [[Backstab]].
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]] | Split a pair and keep the opener. It cannot set off weapon procs. Blizzard built it as a party tool for *"when there are too many monsters in a group to be safely tanked"* |
| [[Pick Lock]] at level 1, as a secondary skill | Free money and utility from the start |
| `Hack and Slash` replaced all four weapon specialisations | One talent branches by whichever weapon you hold, **axes included**, on the same bonus swords get |
| Rogues can use one-handed axes | More weapons are usable, and `Hack and Slash` gives axes the same bonus as swords |
| Poisons scale with Attack Power on the published guides' reading, and can crit | Crit is worth more than it was |
| Energy regeneration is continuous, not ticked | You cannot bank a tick to arrive exactly when needed |
| **[[Expose Armor]] now matches five [[Sunder Armor]]s**, and missing it no longer costs your combo points (fixed October 1) | Worth casting in a group, and safe at low hit chance. It no longer locks the tank out of Sunder, which cannot overwrite yours unless yours is the weaker effect |
## At level 60
Unknown. No player has tried a 51-point build, and there are no stat weights and no answer on whether Assassination's poison identity, Combat's `Hack and Slash` or Subtlety's Rupture engine wins. One major site declines even to publish a stat priority. Blizzard states the class has *"the highest potential single-target melee damage in the game."* Nobody has played far enough to test that.
