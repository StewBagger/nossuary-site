Rogue is a leather-wearing melee class with no self-heal that opens fights from stealth. Forever moved Lockpicking to level 1 as a secondary skill, did the same to Poisons, and **using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Rogue build has been tested. Blizzard published its first Rogue class deep dive on October 7, and it is the authority on this page: where a guide site or the client still carries an older talent name or effect, Blizzard's text wins. Ability training levels and the percentages below are read from the client, which Blizzard mostly does not publish. The 31-point talents `Venom` and `Thousand Cuts` arrive at level 40, so nobody in the beta has used them. Blizzard's caveat covers the rest: *"class abilities and talents may continue to evolve throughout the beta test and are subject to additional changes."*
:::
## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Assassination](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/assassination/)** | Melee DPS | Blizzard: it *"specializes in daggers and buffing themselves with Finishing moves"* | Daggers, poison upkeep, [[Ambush]] openers and bleed damage |
| **[Combat](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/combat/)** | Melee DPS | Every level-20 point improves the two buttons you press, [[Sinister Strike]] and [[Eviscerate]] | Any weapon type, per Blizzard, plus `Riposte`, and Wowhead's *"most 'complete'"* Rogue spec at 20 |
| **[Subtlety](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/subtlety/)** | Melee DPS | `Ghostly Strike` at 20 is non-positional, so you build combo points without getting behind the target | Stealth openers and a [[Rupture]] bleed cycle, with **no area damage of any kind** |
**All three are melee damage**, so pick on how you want to play rather than on role. The spec pages carry each source's ratings and quotes in full.
## Is Rogue for you?
Play Rogue if you like deciding when a fight starts, and you are willing to level a class wearing leather with no self-heal.
:::strengths
- You choose when every fight starts: [[Stealth]] from level 1, opening with [[Ambush]] from 18
- [[Stealth]] and [[Sprint]] let you walk past a fight instead of winning it
- Lockpicking and Poisons are secondary skills now, so neither costs a talent point
:::
:::weaknesses
- Leather armour, no healing, and no way to protect an ally
- No area damage before [[Blade Flurry]] at 30, and Wowhead says Subtlety has *"no AoE capabilities whatsoever"*
- [[Backstab]] and [[Ambush]] both need you behind the target, and a weapon swap costs a global cooldown
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Icy Veins calls Combat *"two buttons"* — build combo points, then spend them.
**Is it forgiving?** No: no self-heal, and leather armour.
**What carries you while levelling?** [[Stealth]] from level 1, so you choose every fight.
**Does it need a group?** No. [[Stealth]] lets you walk past the fights you would lose.
:::

## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP, specifically the ones that break crowd control. `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead) stops fear, sleep and charm; `Escape Artist` (Gnome) breaks roots and snares; `Stoneform` (Dwarf) clears bleeds, poisons and disease. A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance: Human** for long fights, on `Sword Specialization` at +2% crit, the largest weapon racial. Gnome is the better levelling pick, for `Eureka!` and `Escape Artist`.
**Horde: Orc, unambiguously.** `Axe Specialization` gives +1% crit, and Rogues can use one-handed axes in Forever, which is new.
**Forever changed the answer three ways.** Weapon racials became crit racials, Rogues gained one-handed axes, and `Hack and Slash` extended the old weapon-specialisation bonus to axes. Your weapon no longer picks your spec for you.
## Quality of life
- **The level-20 poison quest is the one thing you must not skip.** Reagents come from a poison vendor near your class trainer. In Forever a poison and a sharpening stone or weightstone go on the same weapon at once, where Classic made you choose. Every poison also carries more charges than it did.
- **You need two weapons and a swap macro:** a dagger main-hand for [[Ambush]] and [[Backstab]], and a slow non-dagger for [[Sinister Strike]]. Swapping costs a global cooldown and resets your swing timer.
- Train the weapon that matches your racial: swords for Human, one-handed axes for Orc.
- Lockpicking is free money from level 1 now, and pairs with [[Pick Pocket]] at 4. First Aid is near-mandatory with no self-heal, but you can [[Gouge]] or [[Blind]] and bandage mid-fight.
- [[Distract]] now cuts enemy stealth detection for 10 seconds, which is how you open a [[Sap]] on one of a pair. Blizzard's own warning for PvP: *"player enemies will now know there is a Rogue nearby to look for."*
- **A current beta bug, not intended design:** enemies can Parry and Block from behind if you stand too close. Blizzard's workaround is to not crowd the target. Leave a step of room before you open with [[Backstab]].
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]] | Split a pair and keep the opener. Blizzard built it as a party tool for *"when there are too many monsters in a group to be safely tanked"* |
| [[Pick Lock]] at level 1, as a secondary skill | Free money and utility from the start |
| `Hack and Slash` replaced all four weapon specialisations | One talent branches by whichever weapon you hold, **axes included**, on the same bonus swords get |
| Rogues can use one-handed axes | Which is why Orc's racial now matters |
| Poisons scale with Attack Power on the published guides' reading, and can crit | Crit is worth more than it was |
| Energy regeneration is continuous, not ticked | You cannot bank a tick to arrive exactly when needed |
| **[[Expose Armor]] now matches five [[Sunder Armor]]s**, and missing it no longer costs your combo points (fixed October 1) | Worth casting in a group, and safe at low hit chance. It no longer locks the tank out of Sunder, which cannot overwrite yours unless yours is the weaker effect |
## At level 60
Unknown. There is no tested 51-point build, no stat weights, and no answer on whether Assassination's poison identity, Combat's `Hack and Slash` or Subtlety's Rupture engine wins. One major site declines even to publish a stat priority. Blizzard states the class has *"the highest potential single-target melee damage in the game."* Nothing has tested that. Raids open December 9.
