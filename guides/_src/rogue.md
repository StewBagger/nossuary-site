Rogue is a leather-wearing melee class with no self-heal that opens fights from stealth. Forever made Lockpicking and Poisons secondary skills, added [[Shank]] at 24, and **using [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]]**.
:::scope
Written for the live game, levels 1 to 60. Blizzard's development notes of November 2 are the authority on talents, then its Rogue class deep dive of October 7: where a guide site or a tooltip still carries an older talent name or effect, Blizzard's text wins. Training levels and percentages are read from the client, build 1.60.1.70938, which Blizzard mostly does not publish. Blizzard has not tuned Rogue since launch on November 4; its only Rogue hotfixes, on November 6, corrected two tooltips. Builds on the spec pages are Icy Veins', Wowhead's and WoWSims', named and dated. No raid opens before December 9, so there are no raid logs, and nothing here about level 60 damage is measured. Talent levels assume no Legacy `Talented` perk, which starts your points up to five levels earlier. If a tooltip on this page differs from the text, go by the text.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Assassination](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/assassination/)** | Melee DPS | [[Mutilate]] at 40: 2 combo points for 60 Energy with no need to stand behind the target | Two daggers, poisons, and `Venom` riding on [[Shank]] from 30 |
| **[Combat](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/combat/)** | Melee DPS | [[Blade Flurry]] at 30, the only Rogue ability that hits a second target | Any weapon type, `Riposte` at 20, [[Adrenaline Rush]] at 40 |
| **[Subtlety](/guides/wow-forever/rogue/subtlety/)** | Melee DPS | `Ghostly Strike` at 25 lets you [[Ambush]] or [[Garrote]] again in the middle of a fight | Openers, [[Hemorrhage]] at 30 and a bleed cycle, with **no area damage of any kind** |
**All three are melee damage**, so pick on how you want to play rather than on role. Blizzard's own summary: Assassination *"specializes in daggers and buffing themselves with Finishing moves"*, Combat is direct damage with a little cleave, and Subtlety is *"combat tricks and Bleeds"*. Dual specialisation is sold by the class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold, per Wowhead, so the choice is not permanent.
## Is Rogue for you?
Play Rogue if you like deciding when a fight starts, and you are willing to level a class wearing leather with no self-heal.
:::strengths
- You choose when every fight starts: [[Stealth]] from level 1, opening with [[Ambush]] from 18
- [[Stealth]] and [[Sprint]] let you walk past a fight instead of winning it
- Six ways to stop an enemy acting: [[Gouge]], [[Kick]], [[Sap]], [[Cheap Shot]], [[Kidney Shot]], [[Blind]]
- Poisons are a secondary skill now, so they cost no talent point
:::
:::weaknesses
- Leather armour, no healing, and no buff for your group
- No area damage at all outside Combat's [[Blade Flurry]] at 30, which adds one target for 15 seconds
- [[Backstab]] and [[Ambush]] both need a dagger and a position behind the target
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** While levelling, yes: build combo points, spend them, press [[Shank]] every 10 seconds from 24.
**Is it forgiving?** No: no self-heal, and leather armour.
**What carries you while levelling?** [[Stealth]] from level 1, so you choose every fight.
**Does it need a group?** No. [[Stealth]] lets you walk past the fights you would lose.
:::

## Which race
Read this section in proportion. Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP, specifically the ones that break crowd control. `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead) stops fear, sleep and charm; `Escape Artist` (Gnome) breaks roots and snares; `Stoneform` (Dwarf) clears bleeds, poisons and disease; `Will to Survive` (Human) removes stuns. A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance: Human** for long fights, on `Ruthless` at +1% crit with all spells and attacks. Gnome is the better levelling pick, for `Eureka!` and `Escape Artist`. Both calls are *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Horde: no clear winner** *(ours, not a source's)*. Orc has `Blood Fury`, 10% Attack Power for 15 seconds on a 2-minute cooldown, and `Hardiness`, which makes stuns 20% shorter. Troll has `Berserking`, 10% attack speed for 15 seconds. Undead has `Will of the Forsaken` and `Touch of the Grave`, a 20% chance for a Rogue's attacks to drain Health, with a 15-second internal cooldown.
**Weapon choice is looser in Forever.** No racial depends on a weapon, Rogues can use one-handed axes, and `Hack and Slash` gives axes the same bonus as swords. Your weapon no longer picks your spec for you.
## Quality of life
- **The level-20 poison quest is the one thing you must not skip** *(ours, not a source's)*. Icy Veins says to have [[Pick Pocket]] and [[Pick Lock]] trained and Lockpicking at 75 before you start it. In Forever a poison and a sharpening stone or weightstone go on the same weapon at once, where Classic made you choose. Every poison also carries more charges than it did.
- **Lockpicking: two readings.** The client lists [[Pick Lock]] among the spells a Rogue starts with at level 1. Icy Veins' milestone table puts a Lockpicking class quest at 15, and Gaunerh, playing at launch, [trains the skill at 16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P3Y3CJGPC_0&t=20) and levels it on the practice chests of the Ratchet quest. If your spellbook has no [[Pick Lock]] at level 1, that is where it comes from.
- **Carry a dagger and a slow non-dagger.** [[Ambush]] and [[Backstab]] need a dagger in the main hand; [[Sinister Strike]] hits harder with a slow weapon. [[Mutilate]] needs a dagger in each hand. Icy Veins notes that swapping weapons in combat costs a global cooldown and resets your swing timer.
- **Combo points stay on the target you built them on.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both report that you keep them if you switch away and back, and lose them if you start building on someone else.
- First Aid is close to mandatory with no self-heal *(ours, not a source's)*. Icy Veins' tip is to [[Blind]] the enemy and bandage mid-fight.
- [[Distract]] now cuts enemy stealth detection for 10 seconds, which is how you open a [[Sap]] on one of a pair. Blizzard's own warning for PvP: *"player enemies will now know there is a Rogue nearby to look for."*
- **Parries from behind.** Blizzard's beta list of October 8 had enemies Parrying and Blocking from behind at very close range, with the advice not to crowd the target. The live known-issues list of November 5 does not carry it, and no fix note has been posted, so its status is not known. If a [[Backstab]] from behind is parried, take a step back.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| [[Sap]] no longer breaks [[Stealth]] | Split a pair and keep the opener. Blizzard built it as a party tool for *"when there are too many monsters in a group to be safely tanked"* |
| Lockpicking and Poisons are secondary skills | Neither is a class skill any more, and neither costs a talent point |
| `Hack and Slash` replaced all four weapon specialisations | One talent branches by whichever weapon you hold, **axes included**, on the same bonus swords get |
| Poisons scale with Attack Power and can crit: Blizzard puts [[Instant Poison]] at 1% of your Attack Power and [[Deadly Poison]] at 0.9% | Attack Power and crit both raise your poison damage |
| [[Shank]] and `Poised` are new | [[Shank]] is a cheap off-hand strike that returns Energy, and each tree has a talent built on it: `Venom`, `Flash of Steel` and `Boot Knife` |
| Energy regenerates smoothly, not in ticks, per Wowhead; Icy Veins puts it at 10 a second | You cannot bank a tick to arrive exactly when needed |
| **[[Expose Armor]] now matches five [[Sunder Armor]]s**, and missing it no longer costs your combo points | It no longer locks the tank out of Sunder, which cannot overwrite yours unless yours is the weaker effect. Wowhead's reading is that a Warrior covers the same debuff, so it is not a reason to bring a Rogue |
| `Hemorrhage` no longer raises the group's physical damage | It raises your own [[Rupture]] and [[Garrote]] damage by 15% and nothing else |
## At level 60
**What you bring to a group is damage on one target and control.** Icy Veins and Wowhead say the same thing from two directions: no party buff, no area damage, and a long list of stuns, interrupts and incapacitates. Icy Veins' advice for dungeons is to [[Sap]] before the pull, [[Kick]] and [[Gouge]] casters, and kill the most dangerous target while others clean up the rest. Blizzard states the class has *"the highest potential single-target melee damage in the game."*
**Builds, rotations and stat lists for 60 are on the spec pages**, from Icy Veins, Wowhead and WoWSims. None of them rests on a raid log yet: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9. Both sites mark their stat pages as early, and Icy Veins' best-in-slot pages were still empty on November 9 with the note *"It's too early to tell."*
