## What Arms is for
**Killing two enemies at once with one heavy weapon.** `Sweeping Strikes` makes your next 5 melee attacks also strike a second enemy. Before it, the spec runs on [[Rend]] and [[Overpower]]. After 31, `Bloodthrill` lets your swings open Overpower on any target carrying your Rend.
Icy Veins recommends Protection for levelling and calls Arms "a good alternative if you prefer using a two handed weapon". Wowhead's Arms page says the spec struggles with several enemies at once without a healer and is "phenomenal" in dungeons and elite quests. Kargoz [prefers Arms from level 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ZpQvbxDFlw&t=62) for its early two-target damage and the two-handers that dungeon quests hand out; that is one creator's report.

**Wowhead's levelling build ends at 35/16/0.** It takes `Deflection` 5/5 and a `Piercing Howl` point, and leaves out `Spearing Strike`, `Reckless Swings` and `Improved Bloodrage`. Its reason for the last: the health-for-Rage trade is poor value while questing. Both sites skip `Improved Heroic Strike` for levelling.
**Level-60 builds for dungeons.** Icy Veins and Wowhead both publish 32/17/2, and WoWSims' Arms preset is 32/17/2 too. ## Rotation
**Levelling, one target** (Icy Veins' order; Wowhead's is the same in outline):
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** up; [[Bloodrage]] between pulls so the Rage does not decay
2. **[[Charge]]** to open. It works out of combat only
3. **[[Victory Rush]]** before its 20 seconds run out, sooner if you are hurt. Wowhead: press it below 90% health
4. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** early on anything that hits hard
5. **[[Rend]]** early, on anything that will live through most of it. From 31 keep it on the target for `Bloodthrill`
6. **[[Overpower]]** whenever it lights up
7. **[[Mortal Strike]]** on cooldown from 40. Keep 30 Rage for it
8. **[[Slam]]** with spare Rage. Before `Improved Slam`, only straight after a white hit
9. **[[Sunder Armor]]** on elites and bosses only
10. **[[Execute]]** on bosses and runners. On an ordinary enemy, let white hits finish it and keep the Rage
11. **[[Heroic Strike]]** only with Rage to burn. Wowhead: above 40
**Two targets, from 30** (Icy Veins): pool Rage on one enemy, then pull two. Press `Sweeping Strikes`, put [[Rend]] on both, and queue [[Cleave]]. Kill one, pool on the second, repeat. [[Whirlwind]] from 36 needs [[Berserker Stance]]; Icy Veins saves it for groups and warns against it solo.
**The reasons behind the order:**
- **[[Overpower]] comes first whenever it lights up.** 5 Rage buys weapon damage plus a rank bonus, with +50% crit from `Improved Overpower`, and it cannot be blocked, dodged or parried. At 60 the list puts it above [[Mortal Strike]] *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Rend]] beats [[Heroic Strike]] on anything that lives 15 seconds.** At level 24 Rend deals 45 for 10 Rage before its Attack Power share, 4.5 per Rage. Heroic Strike adds 44 to a swing for 15 Rage, 2.9 per Rage, and that swing earns no Rage (Icy Veins and Wowhead both say so). At 60 the list holds Heroic Strike for 90 Rage, and never presses it once you have `Improved Slam` *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Slam]] waits for the swing because it delays the swing.** Blizzard says it stops doing that with `Improved Slam`, which the published build reaches at 35. Wowhead goes further and says not to cast Slam at all before the talent; Icy Veins allows it straight after a white hit. At 60 the list casts an untalented Slam within about 0.3 seconds of a main-hand hit *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **From 31 [[Rend]] does a second job.** `Bloodthrill` works only on a target with your own Rend. The proc comes from your swings, not from the bleed's ticks (Icy Veins). At 60 the list refreshes Rend with one Global Cooldown left and stops refreshing it below 20% health *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Spend [[Execute]] carefully.** Rank 1 deals 125 for 15 Rage and each extra point adds only 3. An Execute that is dodged, parried or misses costs only its base Rage and keeps the rest (WoWSims' measurement, 36 of 36 in combat logs). At 60 the list keeps casting [[Mortal Strike]] and a talented [[Slam]] through the execute phase and Executes after them *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **`Sweeping Strikes` no longer wastes charges.** Since November 10 a charge is not lost when no second target is hit (Blizzard). It costs 30 Rage, holds 5 charges for 20 seconds, and has a 30-second cooldown. A [[Whirlwind]] spends one charge; [[Thunder Clap]] spends none (WoWSims' measurement).
- **`Spearing Strike` fills gaps.** Icy Veins puts it above [[Mortal Strike]] against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, and otherwise saves it for when Rage is about to cap.
## Stat priority
**Weapon damage first.** A slow two-hander: [[Mortal Strike]], [[Slam]] and [[Whirlwind]] all take their base from the weapon's hit, and Rage is normalized to weapon speed.
**At 60 the two sites nearly agree.** Icy Veins: weapon DPS, Hit, Expertise, critical strike, Strength. Wowhead: Hit to 8%, Expertise to 6.5%, critical strike, Strength, Agility, Haste, and it labels its list speculative for endgame. Expertise is new in Forever and reduces enemy dodges and parries. While levelling you rarely get to choose: take the weapon upgrade, then Strength and Hit.
On WoWSims' pre-raid Arms set, one point of weapon DPS is worth about 17.6 Attack Power and one Strength 2.2 *(WoWSims' model)*.

| --- | --- | --- |

| **`Improved Slam`** | 35 | 2.5/5 seconds off [[Slam]]'s 15-second cooldown, 0.25/0.5 seconds off its cast and Global Cooldown, and Slam stops delaying your swing from the first point |

**[[Mortal Strike]] and `Death Wish` are mutually exclusive.** Mortal Strike needs 31 points in Arms, `Death Wish` needs 21 in Fury, and that is 52 against a 51-point budget.
## What Forever changed for Arms
- **`Improved Slam` moved from Fury into Arms** and has 2 ranks where Classic had 5. [[Slam]] itself now has a cooldown, 15 seconds since the November 2 notes.
- **All four weapon-specialisation talents were deleted** and replaced by one `Weaponmaster`. The Classic mace stun proc is gone, replaced by armour penetration.
- **Two new talents:** `Bloodthrill`, and `Spearing Strike`. Spearing Strike is an instant attack for 15 Rage on a 20-second cooldown. It deals 75% weapon damage, plus another 75% against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, and dismounts the target. It needs no two-handed weapon, but it does require [[Battle Stance]].
- `Anger Management` generates 1 Rage every 3 seconds in combat and cuts your out-of-combat Rage loss by 30%. `Impale` requires `Deep Wounds` 3/3, which requires `Improved Rend` 3/3. Impale adds 10% per point to the critical strike damage bonus of your abilities. `Improved Rend` is 12/23/35%. `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` has 3 ranks at 2/4/6% (Blizzard). `Deep Wounds` does not scale with Attack Power and rolls over when refreshed (Blizzard).
- **`Improved Tactical Mastery`** has 3 ranks and keeps 5 more Rage per point through a stance change, 25 in all at 3/3 on top of the 10 that [[Tactical Mastery]] trains at 14 (Blizzard). `Improved Charge` adds 3/6 Rage to [[Charge]].
- `Improved Overpower` moved from row 3 up to row 2. **`Iron Will` is no longer in this tree**: it is Protection row 1 now.
## At level 60
Dungeons are the level-60 game until raids open on December 9. Icy Veins describes Arms at 60 as cleave-first: strongest against two nearby enemies, with sustained damage limited by Rage. No raid log exists, and neither site ranks the build against Fury on measured data. Wowhead's tier list predates launch tuning and calls itself a prediction.
