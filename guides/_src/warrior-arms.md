## What Arms is for
**Bleeds and burst out of one heavy weapon, plus a 50% healing debuff.** Its engine is Rend into `Bloodthrill` into [[Overpower]], which is tier 5 and so level 30 at the earliest, with all five points by 34. [[Mortal Strike]] arrives at level 40.
That is why **the published level-20 build spends all eleven points in the Fury tree**: nothing in Arms is worth reaching yet.
Icy Veins rates Warrior a poor leveller, one of the more difficult classes, "especially when gear falls behind." It scores Arms 2.0/5 for Levelling on the Level 20 toolkit. Neither Wowhead nor ClassicWoW.gg publishes a levelling score for the spec.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/11/0, entirely in Fury.** Not a typo: it is what both published Arms builds do.
1. `Cruelty`, 5 points
2. `Unbridled Wrath`, 5 points — **it no longer grants +2 Rage per proc with a two-handed weapon**, which used to be the reason an Arms Warrior took it. The published builds pre-date that change. Even so, nothing else in Fury row 2 is worth taking instead *(ours, not a source's)*
3. `Piercing Howl`, 1 point
**If you would rather commit to Arms early at 20** — our own extension, not a published build: `Improved Heroic Strike` 3, `Improved Rend` 3, `Improved Charge` 2, `Improved Overpower` 2, then 1 point into `Deep Wounds`. Improved Rend is forced here: it is Deep Wounds' prerequisite.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, reaching `Sweeping Strikes`: `Improved Heroic Strike` 3, `Improved Rend` 3, `Improved Charge` 2, `Improved Tactical Mastery` 2, `Improved Overpower` 2, `Deep Wounds` 3, `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` 3, `Impale` 2, `Sweeping Strikes` 1. Its one row 5 point goes to `Sweeping Strikes` because `Mortal Strike` requires it, so `Bloodthrill` waits until after 30.
## Rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** always up; [[Bloodrage]] on cooldown, ideally between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]** to open, out of combat only, so ranged-pull a group instead
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on non-casters
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** inside its 20-second window
5. **[[Overpower]]** on a dodge
6. **[[Rend]]** early, only on something that lives out the bleed — it taps the enemy onto your threat table the instant it lands
7. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice; stop below 50% health
8. **[[Slam]]**: only immediately after a white hit, until you have `Improved Slam`
9. **[[Execute]]** from level 24, once the target is at 20% health or less
**Multi-target: don't.** Kill one fast and [[Cleave]] once or twice as rage allows. [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Demoralizing Shout]] are damage reduction here, not damage. With a healer, pull two for the extra rage from damage taken.
**Arms is the only Warrior spec whose published priority includes [[Slam]].** Steps 1 to 7 are Fury's seven abilities in the same order, worded a little differently.
**The reasons behind the order:**
- **[[Overpower]] comes first whenever it lights up.** 5 Rage buys weapon damage plus 5 on top of your normal swings, and it cannot be blocked, dodged or parried. The window stays open 5 seconds, so finishing a Global Cooldown first loses nothing.
- **[[Rend]] beats [[Heroic Strike]] on anything that lives 15 seconds.** At level 24 Rend deals 45 for 10 Rage, 4.5 per Rage. Heroic Strike adds 44 to a swing for 15 Rage, 2.9 per Rage, and that swing earns no Rage (Icy Veins says so of [[Cleave]]; WoWSims treats both the same way). At 60 the Arms list holds Heroic Strike for 90 Rage, and never presses it once you have `Improved Slam` *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Slam]] waits for the swing because it delays the swing.** Blizzard says it stops doing that only with `Improved Slam`, so straight after a white hit is when the delay costs least. At 60 the list casts it within about 0.3 seconds of a main-hand hit, and drops that rule once its cast is shorter than a Global Cooldown *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Press [[Execute]] the moment you have 15 Rage.** Rank 1 deals 125 for 15, and each extra point adds only 3, so two at 15 Rage deal 250 where one at 30 deals 170. Stop putting Rage into [[Rend]] here; at 60 the list stops refreshing it and stops [[Slam]] too *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **From 30 [[Rend]] does a second job.** `Bloodthrill` only works on a target with your own Rend, so past 30 keep it rolling on anything that lasts. At 60 the list refreshes it only with one Global Cooldown left, and opens any fight of two or more with `Sweeping Strikes`, 30 Rage for an extra target on your next 5 melee attacks *(WoWSims' model)*.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then **Hit**, critical strike, Strength, Agility, Stamina. Slow and heavy: rage is normalized to weapon speed, and [[Slam]] and [[Mortal Strike]] both take their base from the weapon.
## What defines Arms
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Weaponmaster`** | 30, 5/5 by 34 | Replaces all four weapon specialisations. At 5/5: axe and polearm +5% crit, mace and staff 15% armour ignore, sword a 5% extra-attack proc |
| **`Bloodthrill`** | 30, 5/5 by 34 | Melee hits on your own [[Rend]] target proc [[Overpower]] with no dodge needed: 4% per point, 20% at 5/5, 6-second window. **Main-hand attacks only**, though that does include [[Cleave]] and [[Heroic Strike]] |
| **`Mortal Strike`** | 40 | The capstone. Requires `Sweeping Strikes` |
**[[Mortal Strike]] and `Death Wish` are mutually exclusive.** Mortal Strike needs 31 points in Arms, `Death Wish` needs 21 in Fury, and that is 52 against a 51-point budget.
## What Forever changed for Arms
- **`Improved Slam` moved from Fury into Arms** and has 2 ranks where Classic had 5. Blizzard's own description: it reduces the cooldown, global cooldown *and* cast time of [[Slam]] together, and stops Slam interrupting your swing timer. Blizzard printed 1.5/3 seconds off a cooldown of 18 seconds; the client's tooltip reads 3.0 sec at both ranks and foreverchanges reads that as 3 per rank.
- **All four weapon-specialisation talents were deleted** and replaced by one `Weaponmaster`. The Classic mace stun proc is gone, replaced by armour penetration.
- **Two entirely new talents:** `Bloodthrill`, and `Spearing Strike`. Spearing Strike is an instant attack for 15 Rage on a 20-second cooldown. It deals 40% weapon damage, plus another 80% against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets (client), and dismounts the target. Blizzard's September 30 post says doubled. It needs no two-handed weapon, but it does require [[Battle Stance]], so it is unusable in Berserker or Defensive Stance whatever you are holding.
- `Anger Management` generates 1 Rage every 3 seconds in combat and cuts your out-of-combat Rage loss by 30%, and **that is unchanged from Classic**. Blizzard's own notes say only that its tooltip was clarified, with no functionality change. `Impale` requires `Deep Wounds`, which is itself gated on `Improved Rend`. `Improved Rend` keeps 35% at 3/3; its lower ranks were trimmed. `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` has 3 ranks where Classic had 5.
- **`Improved Tactical Mastery`** (formerly just `Tactical Mastery`) now retains 3 more Rage per point when you change Stances, to a maximum of 25. That sits on top of the 10 Rage baseline Tactical Mastery trains at level 14 (Blizzard).
- `Improved Overpower` moved from row 3 up to row 2. **`Iron Will` is no longer in this tree**: it is Protection row 1 now, and that guide covers its Stun and Fear duration effect.
## At level 60
Unknown. Nobody has tested whether the Rend, `Bloodthrill` and `Improved Slam` package beats a Fury or hybrid split. Gear cannot be planned for either: Forever reworked dungeon loot and item stats wholesale, so Classic equipment lists do not transfer.
