## What Arms is for
**Bleeds and burst out of one heavy weapon, plus a 50% healing debuff.** Its engine is Rend into `Bloodthrill` into [[Overpower]], which is tier 5 and so level 30 at the earliest. [[Mortal Strike]] arrives at level 40.
That is why **the published level-20 build spends all eleven points in the Fury tree**: nothing in Arms is worth reaching yet.
Icy Veins rates Warrior a poor leveller, one of the more difficult classes, "especially when gear falls behind." It scores Arms 2.0/5 for Levelling on the Level 20 toolkit. Neither Wowhead nor ClassicWoW.gg publishes a levelling score for the spec.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/11/0, entirely in Fury.** Not a typo: it is what both published Arms builds do.
1. `Cruelty`, 5 points
2. `Unbridled Wrath`, 5 points — **it no longer grants its old +2-rage-per-proc bonus for two-handed weapons**, which used to be the reason an Arms Warrior took it. The published builds pre-date that change and so does the game: the tooltip in front of you still shows the doubling, so take this from Blizzard's October 1 notes rather than from the talent. Even so, nothing else in Fury row 1 is worth taking instead
3. `Piercing Howl`, 1 point
**If you would rather commit to Arms early at 20** — our own extension, not a published build: `Improved Heroic Strike` 3, `Improved Rend` 3, `Improved Charge` 2, `Improved Overpower` 2, then 1 point into `Deep Wounds`. Improved Rend is forced here: it is Deep Wounds' prerequisite.
**Level 30: 21/0/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, reaching `Sweeping Strikes`: `Improved Heroic Strike` 3, `Improved Rend` 3, `Improved Charge` 2, `Improved Tactical Mastery` 2, `Improved Overpower` 2, `Deep Wounds` 3, `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` 3, `Impale` 2, `Sweeping Strikes` 1.
## Rotation
1. **[[Battle Shout]]** always up; [[Bloodrage]] on cooldown, ideally between pulls
2. **[[Charge]]** to open, out of combat only, so ranged-pull a group instead
3. **[[Demoralizing Shout]]** on non-casters
4. **[[Victory Rush]]** inside its 20-second window
5. **[[Overpower]]** on a dodge
6. **[[Rend]]** early, only on something that lives out the bleed — it taps the enemy onto your threat table the instant it lands
7. **[[Sunder Armor]]** once or twice; stop below 50% health
8. **[[Slam]]**: only immediately after a white hit, until you have `Improved Slam`
**Multi-target: don't.** Kill one fast and [[Cleave]] once or twice as rage allows. [[Thunder Clap]] and [[Demoralizing Shout]] are damage reduction here, not damage. With a healer, pull two for the extra rage from damage taken.
**Arms is the only Warrior spec whose published priority includes [[Slam]].** Steps 1 to 7 are Fury's seven abilities in the same order, worded a little differently.
## Stat priority
Weapon damage, then **Hit**, critical strike, Strength, Agility, Stamina. Slow and heavy: rage is normalized to weapon speed, and [[Slam]] and [[Mortal Strike]] both take their base from the weapon.
## What defines Arms
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Weaponmaster`** | 30 | Replaces all four weapon specialisations. Axe and polearm +5% crit, mace and staff 15% armour ignore, sword a 5% extra-attack proc |
| **`Bloodthrill`** | 30 | Melee hits on your own [[Rend]] target proc [[Overpower]] with no dodge needed: 20% at 5 points, 6-second window. **Main-hand attacks only**, though that does include [[Cleave]] and [[Heroic Strike]] |
| **`Mortal Strike`** | 40 | The capstone. Requires `Sweeping Strikes` |
**[[Mortal Strike]] and `Death Wish` are mutually exclusive.** Mortal Strike needs 31 points in Arms, `Death Wish` needs 21 in Fury, and that is 52 against a 51-point budget.
## What Forever changed for Arms
- **`Improved Slam` moved from Fury into Arms** and was cut from 5 ranks to 2. Blizzard's own description: it reduces the cooldown, global cooldown *and* cast time of [[Slam]] together, and stops Slam interrupting your swing timer. That is 1.5/3 seconds off a cooldown now 18 seconds, up from 15. ClassicWoW.gg and Icy Veins have the talent leaving Slam's own cooldown untouched.
- **All four weapon-specialisation talents were deleted** and replaced by one `Weaponmaster`. The Classic mace stun proc is gone, replaced by armour penetration.
- **Two entirely new talents:** `Bloodthrill`, and `Spearing Strike`. Spearing Strike is an instant attack for 15 Rage on a 20-second cooldown. It deals a percentage of weapon damage and dismounts the target, and Blizzard says that damage is doubled against a mounted target, a Dragon, or a Giant. It no longer requires a two-handed weapon, but it does now require [[Battle Stance]], so it is unusable in Berserker or Defensive Stance whatever you are holding.
- `Anger Management` generates 1 Rage every 3 seconds in combat and cuts your out-of-combat Rage loss by 30%, and **that is unchanged from Classic**. Blizzard's own notes say only that its tooltip was clarified, with no functionality change. `Impale` no longer requires `Deep Wounds`. `Improved Rend` was nerfed and `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` cut from 5 ranks to 3.
- **`Improved Tactical Mastery`** (formerly just `Tactical Mastery`) now retains 3 more Rage per point when you change Stances, to a maximum of 25. That sits on top of the 10 Rage baseline Tactical Mastery trains at level 14 (Blizzard).
- `Improved Overpower` moved from row 3 up to row 2. **`Iron Will` is no longer in this tree**: it is Protection row 1 now, and that guide covers its Stun and Fear duration effect.
## At level 60
Unknown. Nobody has tested whether the Rend, `Bloodthrill` and `Improved Slam` package beats a Fury or hybrid split. Gear cannot be planned for either: Forever reworked dungeon loot and item stats wholesale, so Classic equipment lists do not transfer.
