Permanent bonuses on gear, paid for by destroying other gear. Forever added two enchant slots, class relics, staves and off-hands. It also gave enchanters a new job at level 60: **the reagent the level-60 crafted sets need comes only from disenchanting raid drops.**
:::scope
The hotfixes are **Blizzard's**, through November 12, and so are the wand and oil changes from its beta notes. Enchants, reagents, skill colours, bindings and crafting stations are client data at build 70938, read through foreverchanges.pro and checked against the client's own tables. Trainers and the route to 225 are Icy Veins' (November 3 and 4). The vendor formula list is Wowhead's (November 9). **Still thin:** the route above 225, where the only source is foreverchanges.pro's computed path. How much the Arcane Salvager returns. Where the Arcane Forge Blueprint drops. And everything that rests on Malleable Essence of Nature, because the raids that supply it open December 9.

## Is Enchanting worth taking?
It earns one **Legacy Point** each at skill 150, 225 and 300.
Icy Veins (November 3) names two groups. Mages, Priests and Warlocks can use it at once, because a Novice's Practice Wand is made entirely from vendor materials. And it *"suits players who run dungeons often, regardless of class"*, since a soulbound drop you will never wear still disenchants.
You pour materials in while you level and sell enchants at 60, so it pays back late *(ours, not a source's)*. Two things Forever added cut against that:
- **Relics you make for yourself.** Druids, Paladins and Shamans get an idol, libram or totem at skill 130, again at 210 and again at 300. foreverchanges.pro's level-20 gear lists, dated September 23, rank each of the three skill-130 relics as the best in its slot at that level
- **The essence.** [[Malleable Essence of Nature]] goes into every crafted set piece in Blacksmithing, into Tailoring and Engineering recipes, and into Enchanting's own top relics. The client's item text gives one source: *"disenchanting items from Hyjal Summit or Barrow Deeps."* Both raids open December 9, so none exists yet
**There is no profession perk.** Wowhead (November 9) says *"WoTLK-style Profession Perks do not exist in WoW: Forever"*. Ring enchants for your own rings were early-beta speculation. No ring enchant is in the client.
## What it pairs with
**Tailoring**, for the vanilla reason. Icy Veins: *"Enchanting doesn't rely on a particular gathering profession, so Enchanters occasionally pursue Tailoring to allow them to quickly create magic items to disenchant."* Wowhead agrees and notes that other crafting professions can feed it too.
Check prices before you sew to shard. Icy Veins' advice is to compare the cost of the cloth and thread with the price of the dust you need.

**Blueprint sources.** For the Salvager the sites disagree: Icy Veins (October 19) says Rath'mael in Ruins of Lordaeron, while Wowhead and foreverchanges.pro list it in City of Dalaran. No source says where the Arcane Forge Blueprint or the Maker's Spark drops.

## The rods
Every enchant needs a runed rod in your bags. A blacksmith makes the plain rod and you rune it.
| Rod | Icy Veins' route makes it at | Wowhead lists it at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runed Copper Rod | 1 | 1 |
| Runed Silver Rod | 129 | 130 |
| Runed Golden Rod | 159 | 175 |
| Runed Truesilver Rod | 204 | 220 |
| Runed Arcanite Rod | Not on its route | 290, from a formula |
Wowhead's figures for the middle three are the skill at which the client turns each rod yellow. Icy Veins' route makes each one earlier, for a certain point. **Buy the Silver, Golden and Truesilver Rods before you reach Icy Veins' numbers** and try the trainer then.
The first rod needs a Copper Rod and a `Mote of Magic`, both from an Enchanting Supplies vendor. Motes are new: you also learn `Dust to Motes`, which turns 1 Strange Dust into 3.
## Levelling it
**To 225 this is Icy Veins' route**, dated November 4. You can enchant the same bracers, chest, cloak and boots over and over.
1. **1 → 20**: Runed Copper Rod, then Bracer Inferior Stamina, 1 Mote of Magic each
2. **20 → 70**: Chest Inferior Stamina, 1 Strange Dust each
3. **70 → 100**: Bracer Minor Stamina. Train Journeyman before 75
4. **100 → 129**: Cloak Minor Protection. Make the Runed Silver Rod for the next point
5. **130 → 159**: Bracer Lesser Stamina, 1 Soul Dust each. Train Expert before 150. Make the Runed Golden Rod for the next point
6. **160 → 180**: Boots Lesser Agility
7. **180 → 204**: Bracer Strength, 1 Vision Dust each. Make the Runed Truesilver Rod for the next point
8. **205 → 225**: Cloak Greater Defense, taught by the two Expert trainers
**Shopping list to 225** (Icy Veins): 185 Strange Dust, 51 Soul Dust, 46 Vision Dust, 19 Mote of Magic, 4 Lesser Magic Essence, and the rods with their gems and essences. Disenchant what you loot first and buy less.
**Above 225 no guide site has a route.** Icy Veins says it does not yet have enough information. The only published path is foreverchanges.pro's, computed from the client's skill colours at build 70938 and not from play:
1. **225 → 262**: Gloves Agility, about 68 casts at 1 Vision Dust each
2. **262 → 277**: Bracer Greater Strength, about 20
3. **277 → 300**: Shield Greater Stamina, about 27. Its formula is vendor-sold
Its list for that stretch is 182 Dream Dust and 40 Lesser Nether Essence, plus the Vision Dust for the gloves.
**Your limit is throughput.** You need a steady stream of green items to shard, which is why the Tailoring pairing exists.
**These counts ignore `Working Overtime`**, the Legacy perk that adds 4% skill-up chance per rank, 20% at five ranks.
## What Forever changed
foreverchanges.pro counts 166 enchants at build 70938. 34 are new.
- **Two new enchant slots.** Five Necklace enchants at skill 210 for 120 [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/) each: 5 Agility, 5 Strength, 6 Spell Power, 11 Healing or 5 Defense. And `Enchant Off-Hand - Wisdom` at 300 for 240 Favor: 6 Intellect and 5 Spirit. Neither slot is enchantable in vanilla
- **Three proc weapon enchants at skill 140**, 45 Favor each: Insight, Recovery and Revelation
- **Insight** has a chance on casting to increase Spirit by 100% for 10 seconds
- **Recovery** heals 5% of maximum health when you are parried or dodged, at most once every 10 seconds
- **Revelation** can trigger when a non-periodic spell fails to crit, and gives your next spell 100% more critical strike chance. The chance shrinks as your own crit rises. Blizzard's November 10 hotfix stopped Vampiric Embrace healing from triggering it
- **Fourteen class relics**, all new: three at skill 130, three at 210 and eight at 300. They are listed under level 60 below
- **Staves and off-hands.** Glimmering Staff, Soulstaff and two orbs at skill 140 for 45 Favor. Dreamstaff, Radiant Staff, Truesilver Conduit and Twisting Essence Jar at 220 for 120
- **Seven new wands**, from the Novice's Practice Wand at skill 1 to Torch of Light at 300
- **The wands are not a gold line.** Blizzard's October 1 notes made *"all crafted common and uncommon quality wands"* sell at vendors for 1 copper. Its September 24 notes stopped wands gaining damage from the user's spell damage. Craft them to wear or to disenchant
- **Enchanting is cheaper.** 84 of the 166 enchants changed reagents, most of them downward. Bracer Stamina takes 3 Soul Dust where it took 6. Boots Greater Stamina takes 6 Dream Dust where it took 10
- **Two Spirit enchants were broken until November 10.** Blizzard: `Enchant Boots - Lesser Spirit` and `Enchant Bracer - Lesser Spirit` *"now correctly give their stated bonus to Spirit (was Resist All)."*
- **Wizard oils are at their Classic values.** Blizzard's September 24 notes set Minor Wizard Oil to 8 spell power, Lesser Wizard Oil to 16 and Wizard Oil to 24, and left Brilliant Wizard Oil unchanged
- **Six Season of Discovery enchants are sold at the trade camp**, all at skill 300 for 240 Favor. Twelve more sit in the client with no vendor, including Retricutioner, Dismantle and Excellent Spirit. If you played Season of Discovery, do not assume your enchants came with you
- **[[Lotus Claw]]**, a gloves enchant at 295: *"a small chance to acquire Death Lotus when gathering any herb in Hyjal."* It costs a Death Lotus to cast, and no source records where the formula comes from
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor unlocks the title "the Enchanter"
## At level 60
- **The skill-300 relics need an Arcane Forge.** All eight do, and so does the Torch of Light wand
- **Three of them are sold for Favor and bind on pickup**: Idol of the Dream, Libram of Holy Alacrity and Totem of Thunder, 240 Favor each. So are the six relics below them. Only an enchanter of the right class gets these
- **Five take raid essence and bind on equip.** Idol of Swiftness, Idol of the Ursine Twins, Libram of Infusion, Steadfast Libram and Totem of Urgency are item level 65. Each takes 1 Malleable Essence of Nature and a Nexus Crystal. The client marks them Bind on Equip, so they can be sold. Wowhead's page says every relic binds on pickup, and the client disagrees for these five
- **Twelve skill-300 enchants cost 240 Favor each.** They include `Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Spell Power` at 55 spell power and `Mighty Healing Power` at 100 healing. Both take Fel Crystal and Torbernite, which Wowhead lists as loot from the skill-300 Heavy Thorium lode on Mount Hyjal
- **Three new Heart of the Mountain trinkets** join the vanilla one. They bind on pickup and are made at the Black Forge in Blackrock Depths
- **You are the only source of Malleable Essence of Nature** once the raids open. A piece of a Blacksmithing set takes 3 or 5
## Specialisations
**There is no Enchanting specialisation**, in vanilla or in Forever.
## What is not known
How much more the [[Arcane Salvager]] returns. Where the Arcane Forge Blueprint, the Maker's Spark and the Lotus Claw formula come from. How much essence a raid item yields. Whether Enchanting Vellum exists: Wowhead saw it in the data and never in game. And whether any vanilla enchant was removed.
