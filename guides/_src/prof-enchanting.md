Permanent bonuses on gear, paid for by destroying other gear. **Forever gave enchanters a new role: you are the supply chain for every crafted tier set in the game.**

Recipes, reagents and skill levels are come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. **Professions cap at skill 225 in the beta.** One of the sources below has no Forever Enchanting page at all, so this profession is more thinly covered than the others.

## Is Enchanting worth taking?
**Yes if you are a caster also taking Tailoring, or if you want the most durable gold engine in the game.** It earns **Legacy Points** at 150, 225 and 300.
It is not a levelling-power profession. You pour money in and sell enchants at 60. But **Forever gave it an argument it never had**: **[[Malleable Essence of Nature]], the gating reagent for every crafted tier piece in Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring, is only obtained by disenchanting items from the two new raids.**
At the beta cap its three new **class relics are best in slot**. One source calls each *"the best relic at level 20 in my gear lists."* **That figure is level 20**, which was the beta cap when it was written; the cap rose to 30 on October 1 and it has not been re-checked.
## What it pairs with
**Tailoring**, and for the vanilla reason, unchanged: *"Enchanting doesn't rely on a particular gathering profession, so Enchanters occasionally pursue Tailoring to allow them to quickly create magic items to disenchant."*
**It still has no gathering profession and is still funded by disenchanting.**
## What Forever changed
This profession changed more than any other.
- **Two brand-new enchant slots.** There are now **five Necklace enchants** (Agility, Strength, Spell Power, Healing Power, Deflection, all at skill 210) and an **Off-Hand enchant** at 300. **Neither slot is enchantable in vanilla.**
- **Proc weapon enchants at skill 140**, the first low-level enchants with effects rather than stats:
  - **Insight** has a chance on casting to increase Spirit by 100% for 10 seconds
  - **Recovery** triggers when you are parried or dodged, healing 5% of maximum health
  - **Revelation** can, when a non-periodic spell fails to crit, grant **100% critical strike chance to your next spell**
  One source puts Revelation *"in 17 of my 31 level-20 gear lists."*
- **Enchanters now make class relics.** Eleven new ones at skill 130, 210 and 300: Druid idols, Paladin librams and Shaman totems. Several reference abilities that do not exist in vanilla at all.
- **Disenchanting itself got better**, through the camp: [[Arcane Salvager]] at skill 140 *"enables more efficient disenchanting."* **How much more efficient is not published.**
- **84 of 165 enchants had their reagents changed, overwhelmingly downward.** Enchant Bracer Stamina now takes 3 Soul Dust where it took 6. Boots Greater Stamina takes 6 Dream Dust where it took 10. **Enchanting is materially cheaper in Forever.**
- **Six Season of Discovery enchants are obtainable**, but **twelve more sit dead in the client with no vendor selling them**, including Retricutioner, Dismantle, Excellent Spirit and Superior Intellect. **If you played Season of Discovery, do not assume your enchants came with you.**
- **New craftables**: staves, off-hands, seven new wands, and three new Heart of the Mountain trinkets at 285.
- **The wands are not a gold line.** Blizzard's October 1 build made *"all crafted common and uncommon quality wands"* sell at vendors for **1 copper**, and the September 24 build stopped wands gaining any *additional* damage from the user's spell damage. Craft them to wear or to disenchant; vendoring one pays nothing.
- **New gloves enchant, [[Lotus Claw]]** at 295 — *"a small chance to acquire Death Lotus when gathering any herb in Hyjal."*
- **Profession perk, new: ring enchants** for attack power, spell power or Stamina, **on your own rings only**. A later-expansion perk in a vanilla relaunch. **Values, skill and cost are not published.**
- **Certification** at 300 for 1,000 Favor → the title **"the Enchanter"**.
## Specialisations
**There is no Enchanting specialisation, in vanilla or in Forever.** Do not go looking.
## Levelling it
The four rods gate everything above them, so buy the materials for each before you need it: **Runed Copper Rod**, then **Runed Silver Rod at 130**, **Runed Golden Rod at 173**, **Runed Truesilver Rod at 218**.
1. **1 → 130**: Bracer Inferior Stamina → Chest Inferior Stamina → Bracer Minor Stamina → Shield Minor Stamina
2. **130 → 218**: Bracer Lesser Stamina → Boots Lesser Agility → Bracer Strength
3. **218 → 262**: Cloak Greater Defense, then the wall at **Gloves Agility, ~68 crafts**
4. **262 → 300**: Bracer Greater Strength → Shield Greater Stamina, ~27
**Shopping list to 225:** 176 Strange Dust, 74 Soul Dust, 38 Vision Dust, 9 Lesser Astral Essence, 4 Lesser Magic Essence, 32 Mote of Magic, plus the four rods.
**225 → 300:** 182 Dream Dust, 68 Vision Dust, 40 Lesser Nether Essence.
**The real cost here is throughput.** You need a steady stream of green items to shard, which is why the Tailoring pairing exists.
## What is not known
How much more efficient the [[Arcane Salvager]] actually is. Ring enchant values and requirements. Whether any vanilla enchant was removed.
