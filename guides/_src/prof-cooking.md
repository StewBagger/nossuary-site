**Available at level 1.** Cooked food in Forever gives +5% experience from kills while you are well fed, and the first dishes come at Cooking skill 1 on a level-1 character.

## Is Cooking worth it?
It costs you no primary slot and the payoff starts at skill 1. No other profession improves your levelling *while* you level rather than paying out at 60. That makes it the clearest call in the whole profession system *(ours, not a source's)*.
The buff is **+5% experience from kills**, and it does not apply to quest experience. The client's wording is *"Experience gained from kills increased by 5%"*, and Icy Veins says the same: the bonus does not apply to quest rewards. It rewards grinding and dungeon pulls more than questing.
**How many dishes carry it?** Our count from the client is 103 of 123. Wowhead says all cooked foods give it, and Icy Veins says many do. Check the tooltip for the experience line before you rely on a dish.
**It combines with the Cozy Sleeping Bag.** Wowhead's November 9 article says the bag's 3% and the food's 5% can be held together.
## Start in five minutes
1. **Learn Cooking** from a Cooking trainer. They stand in every capital and most starting towns, and two neutral ones are on Zephras Isle
2. **Buy a recipe from a vendor.** Get [[Slitherskin Mackerel]]: skill 1, and it gives 6 attack power, three times what the trainer's starting dishes give
3. **Get the fish.** Raw Slitherskin Mackerel comes from fishing or from low-level humanoid drops
4. **Cook, eat, stay seated for 10 seconds.** That is what makes you *well fed*. A shorter bite gives you the health back and none of the buff
5. **Re-apply every 15 minutes**

Two of them are ones you might otherwise reach for.
- [[Goblin Deviled Clams]]
- [[Gingerbread Cookie]] and [[Egg Nog]], both Winter Veil items
- **[[Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops]]**, the famous raid food
## The four ranks
| Rank | Skill cap | Learn at skill | How |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice | 75 | — | Cooking trainer |
| Journeyman | 150 | 50 | Cooking trainer |
| Expert | 225 | 125 | The `Expert Cookbook`, a vendor book |
| Artisan | 300 | 225 | The quest `Clamlette Surprise`, at character level 35 |
**Expert is a book, not a trainer.** Alliance buy it from Shandrina in Ashenvale, Horde from Wulan at Shadowprey Village in Desolace. It is tradeable, so the auction house may have it.
**Artisan is a quest.** Dirge Quikcleave in the Gadgetzan inn, Tanaris, gives `Clamlette Surprise` at Cooking 225 and level 35. Bring 12 Giant Egg, 10 Zesty Clam Meat and 20 Alterac Swiss. You can collect all three before you pick the quest up.
## Levelling it, 1 to 300
Two sites publish a route, and they use different ingredients.
**Icy Veins' route, November 4: meat and eggs, about 352 crafts, no Fishing and no oven.**
1. **1 → 50**: [[Roasted Boar Meat]], 52 crafts. `Charred Wolf Meat` works the same
2. **50 → 90**: `Coyote Steak`, 40, from the trainer. `Boiled Clams` covers the same range
3. **90 → 130**: `Dry Pork Ribs`, 42, from the trainer at 80. Read the Expert Cookbook at 125
4. **130 → 175**: `Curiously Tasty Omelet`, 46. Recipe from Kendor Kabonka in Stormwind or Keena at Hammerfall
5. **175 → 225**: `Roast Raptor`, 52. Recipe from Hammon Karwn at Refuge Pointe or Keena at Hammerfall
6. **225 → 300**: `Tender Wolf Steak`, about 120. Dirge sells the recipe after the Artisan quest
The last step is the expensive one. Tender Wolf Steak turns yellow at 265 and green at 285, and Icy Veins expects about 50 crafts for the final 15 points. `Monster Omelet`, sold by Himmik in Everlook, has the same breakpoints if eggs are cheaper on your realm.
**Wowhead's route, November 7: fish, and it stops at 225.**
1. **1 → 50**: [[Brilliant Smallfish]], 52
2. **50 → 100**: [[Longjaw Mud Snapper]], 55
3. **100 → 175**: [[Bristle Whisker Catfish]], 120
4. **175 → 225**: [[Mithril Headed Trout]], 55
Wowhead's case for fish is that the recipes are easy to get, raw fish is cheap and almost none of these dishes needs a second ingredient. All four recipes are vendor-sold. It publishes no step past 225.
**You do not need Fishing or an [[Iron Oven]] to reach 300.** Icy Veins says every dish on its route cooks on an ordinary fire. Older routes that finish on [[Poached Sunscale Salmon]] no longer work alone: Icy Veins marks that dish as needing an Iron Oven, and placing your own oven takes Cooking 300.
`Cookie's Stirring Rod`, a wand from Cookie in the Deadmines, now raises cooking speed by 25% while equipped.

**You can cook at someone else's oven.** Placing an [[Iron Oven]] needs Cooking 300 and its Blueprint. Using one needs only the food recipe and its skill. A cook below 300 has to find one at a camp.
**Where the recipes come from is mostly unpublished.** Wowhead lists the source of nearly every new skill-275 dish as unknown. The known ones: Poached Sunscale Salmon and Grilled Squid from Gikkix in Tanaris, Hot Smoked Bass from Kelsey Yance in Booty Bay, Mightfish Steak, Baked Salmon and Lobster Stew from Vivianna or Sheendra Tallgrass in Feralas, and Wicked Smoothie for 90 [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/).
[[Filet of Redgill]], skill 225, gives +18 Fishing for 15 minutes. `Goldthorn Tea`, skill 175, gives +15 Herbalism.
## What Forever changed
- **123 recipes, 42 of them new**, plus 6 more bought with [Merchant's Favor](/guides/wow-forever/professions/merchants-favor/): the six smoothies, at 30 to 90 Favor.
- **The experience buff itself.** Classic food gave stats; Forever food gives stats *and* 5% more experience from kills.
- **Cooks own the campfire.** [[Basic Campfire Kit]] at skill 1 supports 3 camp objects, [[Journeyman Campfire Kit]] at 90 supports 5, and [[Expert Campfire Kit]], learned at 200, supports 10. [[Cookie's Feast]] arrives at 140 and the [[Iron Oven]] at 300. Without a cook, nobody's camp objects go down. See [Camping](/guides/wow-forever/professions/camping/).
- **The Journeyman kit is placed at 90 since November 10.** It used to need 140 to place. Blizzard matched it to the recipe's skill. Its Blueprint drops from Lord Serpentis in Wailing Caverns, and the Cookie's Feast Blueprint from Cookie in the Deadmines. Icy Veins, writing on November 3, lists the Expert kit at 220 to place; Blizzard's hotfix did not mention it.
- **New buff types that vanilla food never had:** Healing Power (the tea line, up to 44), Spirit (the smoothie line, up to 20), and armour ([[Plated Armorfish]] at 150). There is even zone movement speed: [[Sweetpaw Jam]] gives +15% in Hyjal.
- **`Master Chef`**, a Legacy perk, gives your cooking recipes a chance of an extra result: 10% per rank over five ranks, 50% at full rank. The perk opens after 5 points in the Professions tree.
- **`Gourmand`**, another Legacy perk, extends food buffs by 33%, 67% or 100% across three ranks. At full rank a 15-minute buff lasts 30 minutes.
- **`Working Overtime`** adds 4% per rank to your chance of a skill-up, 20% at five ranks, and it covers Cooking.
## What is not known
- **Where most new skill-275 recipes come from.** Wowhead's table reads "Unknown" for eleven of them
- **Where the Expert Campfire and Iron Oven Blueprints drop.** Neither Wowhead nor Icy Veins names a source
- **Whether Wicked Smoothie needs reputation.** Icy Veins says Honored with the trade camp. foreverchanges.pro says no recipe there needs reputation. Wowhead lists only the 90 Favor price
- **Whether the +5% stacks with rested experience.** No source addresses it
A related fact is settled: **camp buffs are mutually exclusive with the class buff they copy.** The Fish Bowl's 8% to all stats does not add to [[Blessing of Kings]].
