Slow and peaceful, and in Forever no longer optional if you want Cooking: **five of Cooking's seven levelling steps are fish.**
:::scope
Skill levels and camp objects come from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the beta client and are solid. **The rank thresholds below are Classic's**: the source publishing them marks them as Classic values, not confirmed Forever ones. Fishing is still the only profession with any published rank data.

## Is Fishing worth it?
[Cooking](/guides/wow-forever/professions/cooking/) gives +5% experience from kills. Fishing costs no primary slot and feeds that route directly, so if you cook, you should fish *(ours, not a source's)*.
On its own terms it is still the slowest thing in the game. It now has reasons beyond itself, though: the fish, a camp object worth having, and its own Legacy perk *(ours, not a source's)*.
## Getting started
1. **Learn Fishing** from a trainer. There is now one by the pond in Valanaar, the Skyborne city
2. You also get **[[Find Fish]]**, which puts nearby fishing nodes on the minimap, and [[Bait and Tackle]], which lets you place Fishing camp objects
3. Fish in the lowest-level zone you can find until your skill climbs. **57 zones** are tabulated by the skill they require
## Camp objects
| Skill | Object | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Fish Bowl]] | **8% to all stats** for everyone sitting nearby. It does not stack with [[Blessing of Kings]], and that is the client reading rather than a Blizzard figure. Needs a Raw Brilliant Smallfish and an Empty Vial |
| 140 | [[Fishing Rack]] | Blueprint. Produces uncommon fish on a 1-hour cycle, and keeps the Fish Bowl's 8% |
| 300 | [[Fishing Hut]] | Blueprint. Produces rare fish, and keeps the 8% |
**A Fish Bowl at skill 20 stands in for Blessing of Kings when the party has no Paladin**, but never alongside it. On foreverchanges.pro's reading of the client, a camp buff never stacks with the class buff it copies. Blizzard publishes no camp-object values at all, so the 8% is the client's figure.

| Apprentice | 75 |
| Journeyman | 150 |
| Expert | 225, from the book *Expert Fishing – The Bass and You*, needs level 20 and Fishing 125 |

These thresholds and their acquisition methods are Classic's, and **Forever has not confirmed them unchanged.**
## What Forever changed
- **Camp objects**, above — the Fish Bowl is the notable one.
- [[Find Fish]] and [[Bait and Tackle]] come with learning the skill.
- **`Luremaster`**, a Legacy perk: while a lure is active you have a 25% or 50% chance to catch an extra fish across two ranks.
- A trainer in Valanaar, the Skyborne city, which did not exist in Classic.
- **Cooking's levelling route is mostly fish**, which makes Fishing more load-bearing than in vanilla.
## What is not known
Whether the Classic rank thresholds and their acquisition (the book, Nat Pagle) survived into Forever unchanged. Node density and respawn behaviour are unpublished, as they are for every gathering activity.
