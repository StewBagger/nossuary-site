A secondary skill, so it costs no profession slot. Fishing itself changed little. What changed is around it: a camp object worth 8% to all stats, cooked fish that boost Fishing, and a tournament with 50 winners.

## Is Fishing worth it?
Wowhead (November 10) gives two reasons: the catch is "pure profit" because Fishing has no costs, and many Cooking recipes that use fish were buffed or added.
Icy Veins (November 3) is narrower: pick it up if you regularly buy fish for food or oils. If you need a few fish for one recipe, check the auction house first.
**You do not need Fishing to level Cooking.** The two sites publish different Cooking routes. Wowhead's (November 7) is all fish to 225 and calls fish "usually the best way". Icy Veins' (November 4) uses meat and eggs and says you "can reach 300 without leveling Fishing". See [Cooking](/guides/wow-forever/professions/cooking/).
**Hunters** can feed many raw fish to pets (Wowhead).
## Getting started
1. **Learn Apprentice Fishing** from a trainer. No level is required
2. **Buy a Fishing Pole** from a Fishing Supplies or Trade Supplies vendor, and a few Shiny Baubles
3. **Apply a Shiny Bauble** to the pole. It adds 25 Fishing for 10 minutes, enough that nothing gets away in a starting zone
4. **Stop using baubles at 25 skill.** Starter water is a guaranteed catch from there
You also get [[Bait and Tackle]], which lets you place Fishing camp objects. The client lists [[Find Fish]] as a Fishing ability, but no site says how it is learned.

The four quest fish:
1. **Feralas Ahi**: the Verdantis River in Feralas, southeast of Dire Maul
2. **Misty Reed Mahi Mahi**: the east coast of Swamp of Sorrows
3. **Sar'theris Striker**: the west coast of Desolace, south of Shadowprey Village
4. **Savage Coast Blue Sailfin**: the west coast of Stranglethorn Vale
## Levelling it
**Skill comes from catches, not from where you fish.** Both sites say so: a harder zone does not give points faster, and each point takes more catches as you go. Icy Veins notes you could reach 300 in starter water, leaving only to train each rank.
Wowhead's route (November 10), which picks zones for useful fish:
1. **1 → 75**: any starter zone
2. **75 → 150**: a capital city. You need no lure
3. **150 → 225**: Stranglethorn Vale, which Wowhead prefers for Horde, or Dustwallow Marsh for Alliance. Use lures to reach 225 effective skill
4. **At 225**: do Nat Pagle's quest in Dustwallow Marsh
5. **225 → 300**: stay in Dustwallow Marsh, or move to Tanaris for Raw Summer Bass or Raw Winter Squid, depending on the season. Tanaris needs 300 effective skill for a guaranteed catch
Train ahead of each cap: Journeyman from 50, the Expert book from 125.
**What each zone asks** (Wowhead, main zones only). Below the first number you cannot fish there. At the second, nothing gets away:

## Camp objects
| Skill | Object | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | [[Fish Bowl]] | **8% to all stats** for everyone sitting nearby, at every level. Exclusive with [[Blessing of Kings]]. Trained. Costs a Raw Brilliant Smallfish and an Empty Vial |
| 140 | [[Fishing Rack]] | Lets you catch uncommon fish for 1 hour and hands out Fishing lures. Keeps the bowl's 8%. Blueprint drops from Gelihast in Blackfathom Deeps. Costs 2 Simple Wood, a Crystal Vial and 2 Raw Mithril Head Trout |
| 300 | [[Fishing Hut]] | Lets you catch rare fish for 1 hour and hands out lures. Keeps the 8%. Blueprint source unknown. Costs 5 Simple Wood, 2 Bolts of Runecloth and Seasonal Fish Steaks |
- **The bowl is a flat 8%.** It has no level ladder, unlike the other professions' buffs. Blizzard prints the same figure.
- **It is weaker than the buff it copies.** [[Blessing of Kings]] is 10% in the launch client. The two do not add together, so under Blessing of Kings the bowl adds nothing.
- **The Rack and the Hut are buffs on the angler, not fish dispensers.** You still fish; the buff adds catches you could not otherwise get. No site has listed those catches yet.
- **Seasonal Fish Steaks** are prepared from Raw Winter Squid or Raw Summer Bass (Icy Veins).
- **The Fishing Rack was hotfixed on November 10.** Blizzard fixed a case where it gave no lures to eligible players, and says it knows of other Rack problems, including more "There is no loot" errors.
## What Forever changed
- **Camp objects**, above.
- **The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza has 50 winners**, not one. It runs Sundays from 2 to 4 P.M. server time: bring 40 Speckled Tastyfish to Riggle Bassbait in Booty Bay. Wowhead says the quest now always rewards the Arcanite Fishing Pole and an Extravagant Extravaganza Coin, spent on rewards such as Hook of the Master Angler.
- **`Luremaster`**, a Legacy perk: while a lure is active you have a chance to catch an extra fish. The client has two ranks and 50% at the second. Wowhead (November 10) prints five ranks and 25%, which does not match.
- **Cooked food gives 5% more experience from kills** (Wowhead), and many of the cheapest dishes are fish.
- **Whimsyfin** is a new fish, caught east of Lake Everstill in Redridge Mountains. Cooks turn it into Savory Whimsyfin Delight.
- **Three fish still feed Alchemy**: Oily Blackmouth, Firefin Snapper and Stonescale Eel, for the three oils.
- **Fishing earns no Legacy Points.** Wowhead lists no Fishing challenges.
