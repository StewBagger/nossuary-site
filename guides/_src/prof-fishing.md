Slow and peaceful, and in Forever it is no longer optional if you want Cooking — **five of Cooking's seven levelling steps are fish.**
:::scope
Skill levels and camp objects are read from the beta client and are solid. **The rank thresholds below are Classic's**, and the source publishing them marks them as Classic values rather than confirmed Forever ones — Fishing is nonetheless the only profession with any published rank data at all.
:::
## Is Fishing worth it?
**Yes, if you intend to cook** — and you should, because [Cooking](/guides/wow-forever/professions/cooking/) gives +5% experience from kills. Fishing costs no primary slot and it feeds that route directly.
**On its own terms it is the slowest thing in the game**, and that has not changed. What has changed is that it now has a reason beyond itself: the fish, a camp object worth having, and its own Legacy perk.
## Getting started
1. **Learn Fishing** from a trainer. There is now one **by the pond in Valanaar, the Skyborne city**
2. You also get **[[Find Fish]]** — nearby fishing nodes appear on the minimap — and **[[Bait and Tackle]]**, which lets you place Fishing camp objects
3. Fish in the lowest-level zone you can find until your skill climbs; **57 zones** are tabulated by the skill they require
## Camp objects
This is the part worth knowing even if you do not care about fish.
| Skill | Object | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **20** | **[[Fish Bowl]]** | **8% to all stats** for everyone sitting nearby — the same effect as [[Blessing of Kings]]. Needs a Raw Brilliant Smallfish and an Empty Vial |
| 140 | [[Fishing Rack]] | Blueprint. Produces uncommon fish on a 1-hour cycle, and keeps the Fish Bowl's 8% |
| 300 | [[Fishing Hut]] | Blueprint. Produces rare fish, and keeps the 8% |
**A Fish Bowl at skill 20 gives a party Blessing of Kings without a Paladin.** That is the single best argument for Fishing in Forever. **It does not stack with the real thing** — camp buffs never stack with the class buff they copy, so you keep one or the other.
## Ranks
| Rank | Skill |
| --- | --- |
| Apprentice | 75 |
| Journeyman | 150 |
| Expert | 225 — from the book *Expert Fishing – The Bass and You*, needs level 20 and Fishing 125 |
| Artisan | 300 — from Nat Pagle, needs Fishing 225 |
These are Classic's thresholds and the acquisition methods are described as Classic's. **Forever has not confirmed them unchanged**, though Fishing is the only profession with any published rank data.
## What Forever changed
- **Camp objects**, above — the Fish Bowl is the notable one.
- **[[Find Fish]] and [[Bait and Tackle]]** come with learning the skill.
- **`Luremaster`**, a Legacy perk: while a lure is active you have a **25% or 50% chance to catch an extra fish** across two ranks.
- **A trainer in Valanaar**, the Skyborne city, which did not exist in Classic.
- **Cooking's levelling route is mostly fish**, which makes Fishing more load-bearing than it was in vanilla.
## What is not known
Whether the Classic rank thresholds and their acquisition — the book, Nat Pagle — survived into Forever unchanged. Node density and respawn behaviour are unpublished, as they are for every gathering activity.
