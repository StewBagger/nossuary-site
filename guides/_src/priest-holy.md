## What Holy is for
**Raw healing throughput.** [[Holy Nova]] at exactly 20 heals the party while it deals damage, and generates no threat. `Litany of Light` refunds part of a heal's Mana cost every time you alternate heals rather than spam one. `Holy Binding` in row 4 heals you for 50% of any [[Flash Heal]] you land on someone else.
The published ratings: Overall 3.5, Levelling 2.0, Dungeon 4.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** The published heading is *"Zero AoE and Lackluster Damage"*: *"While Holy Priests are great at their role of healing, they lack tools to solo level effectively. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Holy, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
**If you are soloing, level Shadow and respec.** That is the published recommendation, quoted above. `Dual Specialization` becomes available at level 40, so above that you keep both; below it, respeccing is a trainer visit. The 1-silver price was for the beta only, and Blizzard has published no launch price.
**If you are levelling in groups, the published dungeon rating is 4.0 out of 5**, against 2.0 for levelling. That Holy is not slower at *reaching 60* is our own reading, not a published rating.
## Your talent points
Level 20: **Icy Veins and Wowhead each publish a build**, and they lean on different trees.
**Icy Veins' build, leaning on Shadow's mana regeneration:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus`, then the rest into `Improved Renew`. ⚠ Its prose and its own calculator disagree on whether `Spirit Tap` or `Twilight Focus` comes first; the totals are identical either way.
**Wowhead's build, 2/9/0, Holy after the wand point:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Holy Specialization` 2 → `Divine Fury` 4. No `Spirit Tap`: Wowhead frames this as a generic early build, not a Holy-specific one.
No source had published a level-30 Holy build when this page was written.
**Level 30, 0/21/0 — our own extension, not a published build.** In order: `Holy Specialization` 5 → `Divine Fury` 5 → `Inspiration` 3 → [[Holy Nova]] → `Twilight Focus` 1 → `Improved Healing` 3 → `Twilight Focus` 2 more, for 3/3 → [[Spirit of Redemption]]. `Binding Heal` is a trained spell, so it costs no point. We skip `Holy Binding` for the last `Twilight Focus` point because it works only off [[Flash Heal]], which the healing pattern below does not use *(ours)*.
## Rotation
**Solo damage, Discipline's rotation minus [[Holy Fire]]:**
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Smite]]**
4. **[[Mind Blast]]**
5. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
6. **Wand to finish**
**Healing:** [[Power Word: Shield]] → [[Heal]] → [[Renew]]. From 25 add `Binding Heal`; from 30 add [[Prayer of Healing]].
**Multi-target: [[Holy Nova]] is your only area spell**, and no source puts it in a levelling rotation. The page that publishes the build is headed *"Zero AoE and Lackluster Damage"*. Holy Nova is a healing tool with a damage component; do not plan to farm packs with it.
## Stat priority
Healing Power, then MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike.
**Critical strike is explicitly last:** *"Critical Strike is likely the lowest of our stat priorities as a Holy Priest… crits you may get will often translate into accidental overhealing."* It is worth slightly more than that line suggests now that `Inspiration` triggers off every non-periodic critical heal, rather than the old proc list. That reading is ours, and no source has revised its stat order for it.
**Do not itemise Hit for healing gear.** `Holy Precision` is Discipline row 2: 5 Discipline points to unlock it, then 3 ranks at 6% each, up to 18% Holy spell hit. The 0/21/0 build above does not take it. Blizzard states the point outright: the talent exists *"to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance."* Holy's heals get that benefit only if you pay for the points.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** `Spiritual Guidance` is what gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"*; see What Forever changed below. Nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Holy Binding`, `Litany of Light`, [[Prayer of Mending]]. *Removed:* `Improved Prayer of Healing` and `Lightwell` as talents (see below).
- **`Binding Heal`** is a trained spell for every Priest from 25: a 1.5-second cast on **no cooldown** that heals your target and you, 236 to 284 at rank 1 for 155 Mana. Blizzard says it is tuned to be as good as [[Flash Heal]] even when you need no healing yourself.
- **`Twilight Focus`** (formerly `Healing Focus`) now covers *any* spell at 23% per rank, where Classic covered healing spells at 35%. It is pushback protection on all spellcasts, not healing spells only. It costs 3 talent points instead of 2 for that wider coverage, and the level-30 build above spends all three.
- **`Twilight Focus` now covers Night Elf `Starshards` as well:** *"Starshards now correctly gains resistance to spell pushback from the Twilight Focus talent."* If you are a Night Elf, the talent's third point protects your opener too.
- **`Blessed Recovery`** also triggers off any single hit that deals more than 30% of your max health, not only the old proc condition.
- **`Inspiration`** now triggers from all non-periodic critical heals, not a narrower list.
- **`Improved Healing`** now also discounts [[Penance]] and [[Prayer of Mending]], alongside its existing heals.
- **`Searing Light` redesigned**: still raises all Holy damage, but by a smaller percentage, and it now also gives Holy Fire damage a chance to make your next [[Holy Nova]] cost no Mana.
- **`Litany of Light`** refunds part of a heal's Mana cost whenever you cast a different-named healing spell than your last one: alternate your heals rather than spam one.
- **`Spiritual Healing`** now only needs 3 points for the same benefit, where Classic needed more.
- `Spiritual Guidance`'s bonus to spell damage is cut to *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing."* That is Blizzard's wording, and the client's 25% healing against 8% damage at 5/5 fits it. **This is a direct nerf to Holy's solo damage.**
- **[[Renew]] can now critically strike**, and it can be active from multiple Priests on the same target.
- **[[Lightwell]] is no longer a talent.** Blizzard lists it beside `Improved Prayer of Healing` under the Holy tree's removed talents and does not say what became of the spell. foreverchanges reads it as trained at 40 from the client, so check your trainer at 40.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is trained from level 30**, not a talent. [[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every Priest, learned at 20 *"regardless of race"*, and that is all Blizzard's post says about it. The 3-minute duration and cooldown quoted for it are the client reading.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, and wands no longer gain spell power.
- **The client makes [[Mind Flay]] talent-only**, gated on 10 Shadow points and on level 20, exactly as Classic did. No published Holy build reaches it. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether [[Prayer of Mending]] and `Litany of Light` give Holy enough mana efficiency and raid throughput to close the gap Discipline holds in the published Overall and Dungeon scores. Those are 4.5 and 5.0, against Holy's 3.5 and 4.0.
