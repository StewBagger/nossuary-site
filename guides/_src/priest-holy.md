## What Holy is for
**Raw healing throughput and group healing.** [[Holy Nova]] heals the party while it deals damage, and generates no threat. [[Prayer of Mending]] jumps to another injured party or raid member each time it heals. `Litany of Light` refunds part of a heal's Mana cost every time you alternate heals rather than spam one. `Holy Binding` heals you for 50% of any [[Flash Heal]] you land on someone else.
Icy Veins' ratings: Overall 4.0, Levelling 2.0, Dungeon 5.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0. **The levelling number measures solo kill speed and nothing else.** Its heading is *"Pitiful AoE and Lackluster Damage"*: *"While Holy Priests are great at their role of healing, they lack tools to solo level effectively. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Holy, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
**Wowhead says the same.** Its levelling page (November 9): levelling purely as Holy is not the recommended path, Discipline levels faster while still covering dungeons, Shadow is faster still alone, and Holy is workable with friends or in dungeons.
**If you are levelling in groups, Icy Veins rates Holy 5.0 out of 5 for dungeons**, level with Discipline. Its own comparison: Holy thrives when the whole group is taking damage, and Discipline is ahead on single-target healing and overall throughput.
A respec is a trainer visit. Icy Veins puts the first at 1 gold, rising each time. `Dual Specialization` is bought from the class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold (Wowhead's report; Blizzard gives the level and no price).
## Your talent points
**Icy Veins publishes a full 10-to-60 order** (updated for launch on November 4). It is a hybrid for most of the way: Shadow and Discipline Mana talents first, Holy late. The four lines below are that one order, cut at 20, 30, 40 and 60.
**Level 20, 2/4/5 — Icy Veins' levelling order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Improved Renew` 1.
**Level 30, 10/6/5 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Twin Disciplines` 3 → `Mental Strength` 5.
**Level 40, 13/13/5 — the same order:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Twin Disciplines` 3 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Meditation` 3 → `Divine Fury` 5 → [[Holy Nova]] → `Inspiration` 1.
**Level 60, 14/32/5 — the same order, finished:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Spirit Tap` 5 → `Twilight Focus` 3 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Twin Disciplines` 3 → `Mental Strength` 5 → `Meditation` 3 → `Divine Fury` 5 → [[Holy Nova]] → `Inspiration` 1 → `Holy Specialization` 2 → `Searing Light` 2 → `Improved Healing` 3 → [[Spirit of Redemption]] → `Litany of Light` 2 → `Spiritual Guidance` 5 → `Spiritual Healing` 3 → [[Prayer of Mending]] → [[Inner Focus]].
**That order is late on every Holy landmark.** `Meditation` arrives at 33, [[Holy Nova]] at 38, [[Spirit of Redemption]] at 48 and [[Prayer of Mending]] at 58. The tree allows them at 20, 20, 30 and 40. Icy Veins is trading them for Mana while you level.
**Wowhead's levelling build (updated November 9) is the pure-Holy route**, published as an interactive calculator on [its levelling page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/holy/healer-leveling-tips); its text gives no split and no order. It takes `Wand Specialization` 2 first if you are mostly solo, *"the largest damage increase you can buy while questing"*, then stays in Holy. [[Prayer of Mending]] *"needs 31 spent there, so a tree that stays in Holy reaches it in the early 40s."* After that it spends in Discipline, for `Meditation` with [[Inner Focus]] on the way. If you heal dungeons most sessions it says to skip the wand points. Its prose still names Binding Heal, which no longer exists.
**Level 60, 18/33/0 — Wowhead's raid build (Spanklord, November 4):** `Twilight Focus` 1 → `Holy Specialization` 5 → `Divine Fury` 5 → [[Holy Nova]] → `Inspiration` 3 → `Holy Reach` 2 → `Improved Healing` 3 → `Holy Binding` 1 → `Litany of Light` 2 → [[Spirit of Redemption]] → `Spiritual Guidance` 5 → `Spiritual Healing` 3 → [[Prayer of Mending]] → `Twin Disciplines` 5 → `Mental Strength` 5 → [[Inner Focus]] → `Improved Power Word: Shield` 3 → `Meditation` 3 → `Soul Warding` 1.
That line is listed tree by tree, not in the order you would level it.
**Icy Veins' level-60 build, "Full Throttle Group Healer", is 17/34/0.** Against Wowhead's it takes `Improved Renew` 3 and `Searing Light` 2 and skips `Holy Binding`, `Holy Reach` and `Twilight Focus`. In Discipline it takes `Silent Resolve` 3 where Wowhead takes `Improved Power Word: Shield` and `Soul Warding`. WoWSims' Holy preset is 16/35/0 and takes `Holy Binding` *(a sim preset, not a recommendation)*.
**Wowhead's dungeon build is 21/30/0 and gives up [[Prayer of Mending]] for [[Penance]].** It stops Holy at `Spiritual Healing` and spends 21 in Discipline. Wowhead describes it as a hybrid for small-group content where you also deal damage.
## Rotation
**Solo.** Wowhead's list. Icy Veins' is the same shape, opening on [[Smite]]:
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]] and [[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull. It stops pushback, which lets the opener finish
3. **[[Mind Blast]]** to open, or **[[Holy Fire]]** from level 20
4. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
5. **[[Smite]]** if the enemy still has a lot of health
6. **Wand until it dies.** Recast [[Mind Blast]] on a long fight
7. **[[Renew]]** on yourself if you are taking hits
On a weak enemy Wowhead skips the damage-over-time spell: one cast, then wand. Wanding does not interrupt your Spirit regeneration, and every Mana-costing cast does for five seconds.
**Healing a dungeon.** Wowhead's list, with the removed Binding Heal step left out:
1. **[[Renew]]** on the tank before the pull, and keep it there
2. **[[Heal]]** as the main heal. `Divine Fury` shortens it
3. **[[Flash Heal]]** if someone is about to die. It is the emergency button, not the efficient one
4. **[[Prayer of Mending]]** on the tank once you have it, and let it jump
5. **[[Greater Heal]]** from 40 for a big single heal. It is a 3-second cast
6. **[[Prayer of Healing]]** from 30 only when most of the party is low. It is a 3-second cast and heals the party of the target you cast it on
7. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** on the tank with spare Mana. It no longer costs a Warrior or Druid their Rage
**Alternate your heals once you have `Litany of Light`.** Each heal that differs from your last one refunds 5% of its base cost a rank, 10% at 2/2.
**Keep a low rank of [[Heal]], [[Greater Heal]] and [[Renew]] on your bars.** Wowhead names Heal and Greater Heal as the ranks to drop for steady damage and says downranking Flash Heal is pointless. Doublezug's launch-week video makes the same case for levelling dungeons and adds low-rank Renew on damage dealers and yourself *(one creator's advice, [at 4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88d4Etzi9t4&t=245))*. Low ranks lose no talent procs in Forever.
**Multi-target damage: [[Holy Nova]] is your only area spell.** Icy Veins calls it *"a decent option for dealing AoE damage"* when farming and warns that it is costly. Rank 1 is 185 Mana and rank 6 is 750. With `Searing Light`, a [[Holy Fire]] tick can make the next one free.
## Stat priority
**Icy Veins' order:** Healing Power, MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike.
**Wowhead's order:** Healing Power, Intellect, MP5, Spirit, critical strike. Wowhead labels its list speculative and wrote it before launch.
**They differ on Intellect.** Both give the same numbers: 15 Mana a point, and about 59 Intellect for 1% spell crit. Spirit regenerates Spirit divided by 4, plus 12.5, Mana every 2 seconds when you have not cast for 5 seconds. `Meditation` keeps half of that running while you cast, and `Spiritual Guidance` turns Spirit into healing.
**Critical strike is low on both lists.** Icy Veins: *"Critical Strike is likely the lowest of our stat priorities as a Holy Priest… crits you may get will often translate into accidental overhealing."* Both sites add that it is not a dead stat, because `Inspiration` triggers off non-periodic critical heals.
**Hit does nothing for a healer.** Healing spells do not miss, as Wowhead's stat page says. If you Smite, `Holy Precision` in Discipline row 2 gives 6% Holy spell hit a rank.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** `Spiritual Guidance` is what gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"*. Nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.

## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Holy Binding`, `Litany of Light`, [[Prayer of Mending]]. *Removed:* `Improved Prayer of Healing` and `Lightwell` as talents (see below).
- **Binding Heal came and went.** It was the new 16-point Holy talent in the beta. Blizzard's November 2 notes removed it and put `Holy Binding` in its slot. No trainer teaches it. Wowhead's Holy levelling, rotation and overview pages and Icy Veins' Holy pages still describe it.
- **`Twilight Focus`** (formerly `Healing Focus`) now covers *any* spell at 23% a rank, 70% at 3/3, where Classic covered healing spells only. It costs 3 talent points instead of 2. It covers Night Elf `Starshards` too: *"Starshards now correctly gains resistance to spell pushback from the Twilight Focus talent."* Since November 11 it also works in [[Shadowform]].
- **`Blessed Recovery`** also triggers off any single hit that deals more than 30% of your maximum health. It heals 25% of the damage over 6 seconds at 3/3.
- **`Inspiration`** now triggers from all non-periodic critical heals: 25% more armour on the target for 15 seconds at 3/3.
- **`Improved Healing`** now also discounts [[Penance]] and [[Prayer of Mending]], alongside its existing heals. 15% at 3/3.
- **`Searing Light` redesigned**: 2 ranks, it now raises all Holy damage, and each periodic tick of [[Holy Fire]] has a chance to make your next [[Holy Nova]] cost no Mana. The readers of the client disagree on the numbers. foreverchanges reads 2% damage and a 5% chance at rank 1. Icy Veins reads 5% and 10% at 2/2. Our own tooltip reads 10% and 10%. Blizzard says only that the damage bonus is smaller than Classic's.
- **`Spiritual Healing`** now only needs 3 points for the same benefit.
- `Spiritual Guidance`'s bonus to spell damage is cut to *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing."* That is Blizzard's wording, and the client's 25% healing against 8% damage at 5/5 fits it.
- **[[Renew]] can now critically strike**, and it can be active from multiple Priests on the same target.
- **[[Lightwell]] is no longer a talent.** Blizzard lists it beside `Improved Prayer of Healing` under the Holy tree's removed talents and does not say what became of the spell. The client carries it as a trained Holy spell at 40, 225 Mana on a 10-minute cooldown, with ranks at 50 and 60, and foreverchanges and Icy Veins' level-40 table list it. No Blizzard post confirms it, so check your trainer at 40.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is trained from level 30**, not a talent. [[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every Priest, learned at 20 *"regardless of race"*, with a 3-minute duration and cooldown in the client.
- **Blizzard's November 10 hotfix** fixed [[Holy Nova]] staying learned after you untalented it.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%**, where Classic needed 5, and wands no longer gain spell power.
- **[[Mind Flay]] is talent-only**, gated on 10 Shadow points. No published Holy build reaches it. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
**The published raid builds agree on the Holy spine.** Wowhead's raid build, Icy Veins' build and WoWSims' preset all take `Divine Fury` 5, `Inspiration` 3, `Improved Healing` 3, `Litany of Light` 2, `Spiritual Guidance` 5, `Spiritual Healing` 3 and [[Prayer of Mending]], with 16 to 18 points in Discipline for `Meditation`. They split on `Holy Binding`, `Searing Light` and what else to buy in Discipline.
**What nobody has measured:** how [[Prayer of Mending]] and `Litany of Light` hold up over a raid fight. Wowhead's Holy stat page says its list has not been tested at 60. Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on December 9, 2026.
