## Is Holy worth levelling?
**The slowest Priest spec to solo.** The published heading is *"Zero AoE and Lackluster Damage"*: *"While Holy Priests are great at their role of healing, they lack tools to solo level effectively. It's typically recommended to play with a group if you plan to level as Holy, otherwise it is a better idea to primarily use Shadow talents until you're able to get into group content."*
Ratings: **Overall 3.5, Levelling 2.0, Dungeon 4.0, Utility 4.0, Mobility 1.0.**
**If you are soloing, level Shadow and respec.** That is the published recommendation, quoted above. **`Dual Specialization` becomes available at level 40**, so above that you keep both; below it, respeccing is a trainer visit. In the beta that visit costs **1 silver** if you have not changed talents recently, much more for a second reset, and it falls back to 1 silver once an hour has passed — Blizzard calls the price *"temporary for the Beta only."*
**If you are levelling in groups, the published dungeon rating is 4.0 out of 5**, against 2.0 for levelling. *"Slower"* in that published heading means slower at killing things by yourself; that it is not slower at reaching 60 is our own reading, not a published rating.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: Icy Veins and Wowhead each publish a build, and they lean on different trees.**
**Icy Veins' build, leaning on Shadow's mana regeneration:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → **`Spirit Tap` 5** → `Twilight Focus`, then the rest into `Improved Renew`. ⚠ Its prose and its own calculator disagree on whether `Spirit Tap` or `Twilight Focus` comes first; the totals are identical either way.
**Wowhead's build, 2/9/0, Holy after the wand point:** `Wand Specialization` 2 → `Improved Renew` 3 → `Holy Specialization` 2 → `Divine Fury` 4. No `Spirit Tap` — Wowhead frames this as the generic early build it gives every Priest spec at 20, not a Holy-specific one.
**No source publishes a level-30 Holy build.**
**Level 30, 0/21/0 — our own extension, not a published build**, in order: `Holy Specialization` 5 → `Divine Fury` 5 → `Inspiration` 3 → **[[Holy Nova]]** → `Twilight Focus` 1 → `Improved Healing` 3 → **`Binding Heal`** → `Twilight Focus` to 2/3 → **[[Spirit of Redemption]]**.
## Rotation
**Solo damage, Discipline's rotation minus [[Holy Fire]]:**
1. **[[Power Word: Fortitude]]** and **[[Inner Fire]]** up
2. **[[Power Word: Shield]]** before you pull
3. **[[Smite]]**
4. **[[Mind Blast]]**
5. **[[Shadow Word: Pain]]**
6. **Wand to finish**
**Healing:** [[Power Word: Shield]] → [[Heal]] → [[Renew]]. From 25 add `Binding Heal`; from 30 add [[Prayer of Healing]].
**Multi-target: [[Holy Nova]] is your only area spell, and no source puts it in a levelling rotation.** The page that publishes the build is headed *"Zero AoE and Lackluster Damage"*. Treat Holy Nova as a healing tool with a damage component, not as a farming spell.
## Stat priority
Healing Power, then MP5, Spirit, Intellect, critical strike.
**Critical strike is explicitly last:** *"Critical Strike is likely the lowest of our stat priorities as a Holy Priest… crits you may get will often translate into accidental overhealing."* It is worth slightly more than that line suggests now that `Inspiration` triggers off every non-periodic critical heal, not just the old proc list — our own reading, and no source has revised its stat order for it.
**Do not itemise Hit for healing gear.** `Holy Precision` (Discipline tree, one early point) grants up to 18% Holy spell hit, and Blizzard states the point outright: the talent exists *"to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance."* That applies to Holy's own heals exactly as much as Discipline's.
**The one-third figure belongs to a talent, not to gear.** It is `Spiritual Guidance` that gives a bonus to spell damage *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing"* — see What Forever changed below. Nothing in Blizzard's post makes healing bonus on gear grant spell damage.
## What defines Holy
| Talent | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`Binding Heal`** | **25** | New, and the 16-point milestone. A 1.5-second cast, **no cooldown**, that heals **your target and you**. Blizzard says it is tuned to be as good as [[Flash Heal]] even when you need no healing yourself |
| **[[Holy Nova]]** | **exactly 20** | Your only area spell: damage plus a party heal, and **no threat** |
| **[[Prayer of Mending]]** | **40** | New, the 31-point gold medal, requires `Spirit of Redemption`. 10-second cooldown, 30-second duration: heals the target the next time they take damage or a non-periodic heal lands, consumes a charge, then jumps to another party or raid member within 20 yards, up to 5 heals total, one carrier at a time. **Unreachable at either beta cap** |
## What Forever changed for Priests
- **New:** `Binding Heal`, `Litany of Light`, [[Prayer of Mending]]. **Removed:** `Improved Prayer of Healing` and `Lightwell` (see below).
- **`Twilight Focus`** (formerly `Healing Focus`) **now covers *any* spell at 23% per rank**, where Classic covered healing spells at 35%, and Blizzard's post adds the piece our own numbers didn't: it is now pushback protection **on all spellcasts**, not healing spells only, and it costs **3 talent points instead of 2** for that wider coverage — the level-30 build above already spends the third point.
- **`Twilight Focus` now covers Night Elf `Starshards` as well.** Blizzard's Known Issues listed the racial as not benefiting from the talent; the October 1 build fixed it — *"Starshards now correctly gains resistance to spell pushback from the Twilight Focus talent."* If you are a Night Elf, the talent's third point protects your opener too.
- **`Blessed Recovery` also triggers off any single hit that deals more than 30% of your max health**, not only the old proc condition.
- **`Inspiration` now triggers from all non-periodic critical heals**, not a narrower list.
- **`Improved Healing` now also discounts [[Penance]] and [[Prayer of Mending]]**, alongside its existing heals.
- **`Searing Light` redesigned**: still raises all Holy damage, but by a smaller percentage, and it now also gives Holy Fire damage a chance to make your next [[Holy Nova]] cost no Mana.
- **`Litany of Light` refunds part of a heal's Mana cost whenever you cast a different-named healing spell than your last one** — alternating heals, not spamming one.
- **`Spiritual Healing` now only needs 3 points for the same benefit**, where Classic needed more.
- **`Spiritual Guidance`'s bonus to spell damage is cut to *"approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing."*** That is Blizzard's wording; the post publishes no conversion percentages, in Classic's terms or Forever's. **This is a direct nerf to Holy's solo damage.**
- **[[Renew]] can now critically strike**, as of the September 24 patch, and **can now be active from multiple Priests on the same target**.
- **[[Lightwell]] is gone.** Blizzard lists it beside `Improved Prayer of Healing` under the Holy tree's removed talents and puts nothing in its place. Where a talent became baseline instead, the post says so outright — it does for [[Divine Spirit]] and for `Improved Power Word: Fortitude` — and it does not for Lightwell, so there is no trainable version to look for.
- **[[Divine Spirit]] is trained from level 30**, not a talent. **[[Fear Ward]] is baseline for every Priest**, learned at 20 *"regardless of race"* — which is all Blizzard's post says about it. The 3-minute duration and cooldown quoted for it are the client reading.
- **`Wand Specialization` is 2 ranks reaching 25%** where Classic needed 5, and **wands no longer gain spell power.**
- **The client makes [[Mind Flay]] talent-only**, gated on 10 Shadow points, where Classic taught it at level 20. **No published Holy build reaches it**. See the [Shadow](/guides/wow-forever/priest/shadow/) page.
## At level 60
Unknown: whether [[Prayer of Mending]] and `Litany of Light` give Holy enough mana efficiency and raid throughput to close the gap Discipline holds in the published Overall and Dungeon scores — 4.5 and 5.0, against Holy's 3.5 and 4.0.
