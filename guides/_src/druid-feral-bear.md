Leather tanking in Bear Form on rage and dodge: no block, no parry. **This is your only combat form between levels 10 and 20**, so every Druid plays it whether they intend to tank or not.

## What Bear is for
**Bear Form multiplies the armour on your gear and adds +180 health**, and you can drop form to heal yourself with Mana. [[Growl]] on an 8-second cooldown holds what you pull, and rage comes from being hit as well as from hitting. In Forever the weapon's damage counts in Bear Form, so more damage means more threat.
**You do not get a choice before level 20.** Bear Form comes from a class quest at 10; Cat Form does not exist until 20. The published advice: *"As you reach Level 10 you will mainly want to level in Bear Form, using your Mana to heal yourself after killing enemies, and then recovering your Mana while in Bear Form."*
**The class starts slowly:** *"Since Bear Form is not unlocked until Level 10, and Cat Form first at level 20, Feral Druid has a fairly slow start."*
The three-tank line-up of Protection Warrior, Protection Paladin and Feral Druid is **a common assumption rather than a Blizzard taxonomy**. Blizzard's own Druid deep dive credits Druids with the *"ability to serve any combat role"* and publishes no tank list.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/11/0.** Four different level-20 Feral builds are published, and they disagree on most of the eleven points. Here is the one with a published click order:
1. **`Ferocity`, 5 points**: *"helping with both your Rage economy while leveling allowing for more [[Maul]] casts, and reducing your energy cost in Cat Form"*
2. **`Brutal Impact`, 2 points**: *"both strengthen your [[Bash]] and reduces the cooldown by 30 seconds"*
3. **`Feral Instinct`, 1 point**: *"for [[Swipe]] damage and improved [[Prowl]] at Level 20."* Blizzard's class deep dive confirms the mechanism: it no longer increases threat in Bear Form, and instead it increases [[Swipe]]'s damage. The stealth (Prowl) bonuses are unchanged
4. **`Feral Swiftness`, 2 points**: *"a sizable movement speed increase in Cat Form, and an added benefit of granting increased Dodge chance in all forms."* Blizzard's class deep dive confirms both halves directly: the Cat Form speed bonus works indoors, and the Dodge bonus applies all the time, not only in Cat Form
5. **[[Feral Charge]]** at 20, the 11-point gold medal talent. Per Blizzard, it now also grants a Cat Form version, on a 30-second cooldown that does not interrupt or immobilize the target

## Threat and mitigation rotation
1. **[[Growl]]** to pull and to hold. Rage comes from taking hits and auto-attacking
2. **[[Enrage]]**: 30 rage over 10 seconds on a 1-minute cooldown, *"at the cost of reducing your Armor slightly"*
3. **[[Faerie Fire]]** to strip armour. In Forever it is usable in Bear, Dire Bear or Cat Form, which it was not in Classic. Per Blizzard it costs no resources in form but gains a 6-second cooldown, and it does not reset your swing timer. It generates threat in Bear and Dire Bear Form, and it cannot proc [[Omen of Clarity]] there
4. **[[Maul]]** as your rage dump
5. **[[Bash]]** to stun, and with `Brutal Impact` it comes back much faster
Pre-buff [[Mark of the Wild]] and [[Thorns]]. Drop form to self-heal. [[Nature's Grasp]] to disengage: it works in form and indoors. [[Entangling Roots]] works indoors too, but you have to drop form to cast it.
**Multi-target: [[Swipe]], and know its limit.** Swipe hits three targets, and `Primal Bite` at 40 is single-target, so Swipe's three is your whole answer. Swipe scales with Attack Power, gaining 3% of it. Blizzard says the in-game tooltip will not show this until a future update, so the ability's own text is wrong in the meantime.
## Stat priority
**Stamina and armour first**, then Agility (dodge *and* more armour), then Defense.
Bear Form multiplies your gear's armour, so a leather piece with a high armour number beats the same stats on cloth. **The weapon's damage now counts in Bear Form, and more damage means more threat**, so choose the weapon on its stats *and* its damage together. Blizzard describes the mechanism: auto-attack DPS in Bear, Cat or Dire Bear Form equals your weapon's DPS outright. The swing speed is a fixed 2.5 seconds in Bear and Dire Bear, 1.0 second in Cat Form, and weapon-damage abilities use the same numbers. Blizzard publishes the two speeds but not which form takes which, so that pairing is ours, not a source's.
One published tank list orders it Defense > Dodge > Armor > Agility > Strength > Attack Power > crit > Haste. **One source disputes the weapon-damage priority outright**, arguing you should *"Compare the item's form-relevant bonuses, armor, Stamina and defense instead of importing a Warrior list."* Three sources and the client data are on the other side, but the disagreement is real.

## What Forever changed for Bear
- **`Thick Hide` is a different talent under the same name** (above): base armour per level and defense scaling, rather than a percentage of your gear.
- **[[Frenzied Regeneration]] is now a real defensive cooldown:** *"Each point of Rage is converted into 1% health"*, where Classic gave 10 flat health. Ranks 2 and 3 were deleted, and the one remaining rank trains at 36.
- **[[Enrage]] front-loads**: *"Instantly generates 10 Rage and another 20 Rage over 10 sec"*, where Classic gave 20 over 10 with nothing up front.
- **Bear Form and Dire Bear Form generate 100% increased Rage on a critical strike**, which is another reason crit gear is not wasted on a tank. Their threat bonus is 50%.
- **All Shapeshift Forms are now immune to Disarm.** The nuance matters for a tank: if you are disarmed *before* you shift, the form does not clear the Disarm, and you deal reduced damage for its duration. A mob or boss that disarms you before you can shift still works.
- **Bear Form's health bonus is +180.** [[Growl]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds. [[Demoralizing Roar]] takes 16 more attack power than Classic's at the same rank. [[Faerie Fire]] is usable in Bear, Dire Bear and Cat Form.
- **A new rage economy:** `Natural Reaction` gives a chance of rage on dodge, `Blood Frenzy` gives it on crit. `Improved Enrage` and `Faerie Fire (Feral)` are gone as talents because their effects became baseline: [[Enrage]] now front-loads 10 rage itself, and ordinary [[Faerie Fire]] works in form. [[Lacerate]] arrives as a tier 4 talent.
- `Heart of the Wild` **kept its bear clause exactly: +20% Stamina at 5/5, the same as Classic**, while the Intellect and Cat Strength clauses were both halved to +10%. It now sits in Restoration tier 1, so the Bear Stamina clause costs points outside the Feral tree, and `Furor` holds its old slot in Feral.
- **One rename and one merge, both confirmed directly by Blizzard's class deep dive.** `Mangle` is now `Primal Bite`, and it lost its debuff: *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* The rename is finished in the client: *"All talents referencing Mangle now reference Primal Bite."* Mangle is gone from the spellbook. Separately, Classic's `Primal Fury` (the Bear rage talent) and `Blood Frenzy` (the Cat combo-point talent) were merged into one node. Blizzard's own text is explicit that the merged node is called `Blood Frenzy`: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* It carries the Bear rage clause plus a Cat clause Classic did not have. `Primal Fury` is not selectable in the Forever tree, so build lists naming it cannot be entered as printed; ClassicWoW.gg has the direction the other way round. Half the guide sites still use the old Mangle name.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether Bear holds threat and survives without Classic's armour-from-items scaling is untested. The beta stopped at 30, so nobody has played `Primal Bite`, Dire Bear Form, [[Frenzied Regeneration]] or a level-60 defense value.
