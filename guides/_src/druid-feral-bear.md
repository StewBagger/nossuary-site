Leather tanking in Bear Form on rage, armour and dodge: no block, no parry. **This is your only combat form between levels 10 and 20**, so every Druid plays it whether they intend to tank or not.

## What Bear is for
**Bear Form multiplies the armour on your gear by 180% and adds health that grows with level**, 560 on the tooltip from level 40. Dire Bear Form at 40 makes that 360% and +1,240 health at 60. Both forms raise your threat by 115%. You can drop form to heal yourself with Mana. [[Growl]] on an 8-second cooldown holds what you pull, and rage comes from being hit as well as from hitting. The weapon's damage counts in Bear Form, so more damage means more threat.
**You do not get a choice before level 20.** Bear Form comes from a class quest at 10; Cat Form does not exist until 20. Icy Veins on choosing between them later: *"Often it can be faster to use Bear Form against multiple enemies, while Cat Form is better against a single enemy."* Wowhead: *"Can deal damage, tank, and heal between pulls with the same basic toolkit."*
**The class starts slowly.** Wowhead: *"Druids start off feeling a little slow, particularly before unlocking Bear Form at Level 10 and Cat Form at Level 20."*
**Expect to be asked to tank.** Wowhead lists it as a cost of the spec: a Feral Druid *"will frequently be expected to tank, both while leveling and in the endgame."*

## Threat and mitigation rotation
**Before 25:**
1. **[[Enrage]]** before the pull, for rage to start with
2. **[[Faerie Fire]]** to pull and to strip armour, from 18. It costs nothing in form
3. **[[Demoralizing Roar]]** on anything that will be hitting you for a while
4. **[[Swipe]]** from 16, on two targets or more
5. **[[Maul]]** on the rage that is left
**From 25, with [[Lacerate]]:** build it to 5 stacks on a target that will live, keep it from running out, and [[Maul]] with spare rage.
**From 40, with `Primal Bite`**, the order both sites publish:
1. Keep **[[Faerie Fire]]** and **[[Demoralizing Roar]]** up
2. **[[Lacerate]]** when a 5-stack is about to run out
3. **`Primal Bite`** on cooldown
4. **[[Lacerate]]** to build stacks, and as filler
5. **[[Maul]]** above about 35 rage
**Against several targets**, Icy Veins and Wowhead both swap the filler: `Primal Bite` on cooldown, then **[[Swipe]]**, then [[Maul]] on spare rage, with [[Thorns]] and [[Demoralizing Roar]] up. Use [[Growl]] and [[Challenging Roar]] when something runs at another player.
**[[Maul]] costs more than it says.** It replaces an auto-attack, and Icy Veins puts an auto-attack at 11.25 rage, 22.5 on a crit. Wowhead: spending on [[Maul]] so that `Primal Bite` cannot be pressed *"should be avoided at all costs."*
[[Feral Charge]] in Bear Form interrupts the target's cast and roots it for 4 seconds, so it doubles as an interrupt on a caster. [[Bash]] stuns, and `Brutal Impact` shortens its cooldown. Drop form to self-heal. [[Nature's Grasp]] works in form and indoors. [[Entangling Roots]] works indoors too, but you have to drop form to cast it.
**What changes the answer:**
- **What threat each button makes.** WoWSims measured it from a feral druid's threat logs at levels 37 to 51: [[Lacerate]]'s hit and its ticks threaten for 10/3 of their damage, `Primal Bite` for 3 times its damage, and a landed [[Faerie Fire]] in Bear Form for twice your level. Everything is then raised by the form's 115%.
- **When to press [[Enrage]].** Before the pull, or when rage is what stops your next attack. It lowers base armour by 27% in Bear Form and 16% in Dire Bear Form for 10 seconds, and 10 of its 30 rage arrives at once. WoWSims' model holds it for a `Primal Bite` or [[Lacerate]] it cannot afford *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Refreshing [[Lacerate]].** Early, unlike a Cat's bleeds. The stacks are the value: the first application hits for nothing, and each later one adds 10% weapon damage per stack already there. A new stack refreshes the bleed without restarting its ticks. WoWSims' model reapplies a 5-stack with 3 seconds left *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Swipe]] or [[Lacerate]].** [[Swipe]] from two targets. At 30, with `Feral Instinct` 3/3 and `Ferocity` 5, rank 2 deals 32.5 to each of up to three targets for 15 rage, against 20 over 15 seconds from one rank 1 [[Lacerate]]. It also takes 3% of your attack power on each hit. WoWSims' model swaps at two targets too, once one of them bleeds, for `Rend and Tear` *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Holding rage from [[Maul]].** [[Maul]] adds a flat amount to your next swing, 37 at 26 to 33 and 128 at 58. From 40, WoWSims' model leaves it unqueued while `Primal Bite` is about to come back unless the rage covers both *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Rage as health, from 36.** [[Frenzied Regeneration]] converts up to 10 rage a second for 10 seconds at 1% health per point, on a 3-minute cooldown, so it heals by what you are holding. Wowhead: use it under sustained damage rather than at the last moment, because it heals over time. WoWSims' model presses it at 25% health, and [[Barkskin]] at 60% from 44 *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Crits are rage.** `Blood Frenzy` 2/2 gave 5 rage on every non-periodic crit in logs at levels 26 to 33, and both bear forms double the rage of a critical auto-attack.
## Stat priority
**For staying alive, the two sites agree on the stats and not on the order.**
1. **Wowhead (November 3):** Stamina, Defense to 440, armour, Agility, avoidance
2. **Icy Veins (November 4):** armour, Defense to 440, Stamina, Agility, dodge
Both say so with a caveat. Icy Veins: *"we do not actually know if we need 440 Defense since we have not seen Raid Bosses at Level 60 yet."* 440 Defense is the figure that removes a raid boss's chance to crit you. A bear cannot block or parry, and Icy Veins adds that it cannot avoid Crushing Blows, so armour has to carry those. The armour cap against a raid boss is 17,265.
**For threat: weapon DPS, then expertise and hit.** Icy Veins lists weapon DPS, expertise to 6.5% and on to 14% for a tank facing a boss, hit to 8%, then Agility and Strength. WoWSims' bear weights agree on the shape: 1 weapon DPS about 3.0, a point of Stamina 0.97, Strength 0.47, Agility 0.45 *(WoWSims' model)*.
**While levelling:** Stamina and armour on leather, and the highest-DPS weapon you can hold. Bear Form multiplies your gear's armour, so a leather piece with a high armour number beats the same stats on cloth.
**The weapon's damage counts in Bear Form.** Auto-attack DPS in form equals your weapon's DPS at a fixed 2.5-second swing, 1.0 second in Cat Form, so weapon speed does not matter. `Primal Bite`, [[Lacerate]] and [[Maul]] all build on that swing. Sharpening stones and weightstones work in form.

## What Forever changed for Bear
- **Threat is +115% in both bear forms**, up from 50% at launch on November 4. Blizzard: *"This is to compensate for Blessing of Salvation being removed."*
- **Fixes since launch.** November 6: Bear Form's tooltip showed the wrong health figure and was corrected; Blizzard says the effect itself was always right. November 9: `Natural Instinct` now applies to [[Lacerate]]. November 8: [[Swipe]]'s damage range narrowed around the same average, with no Blizzard note.
- **`Thick Hide` is a different talent under the same name**: base armour per level and Defense scaling, rather than a percentage of your gear.
- **[[Frenzied Regeneration]] is a real defensive cooldown:** *"Each point of Rage is converted into 1% health"*, where Classic gave 10 flat health. It has one rank, trained at 36.
- **[[Enrage]] front-loads**: *"Instantly generates 10 Rage and another 20 Rage over 10 sec"*, where Classic gave 20 over 10 with nothing up front.
- **All Shapeshift Forms are immune to Disarm.** If you are disarmed *before* you shift, the form does not clear the Disarm, and you deal reduced damage for its duration.
- **[[Faerie Fire]] works in Bear, Dire Bear and Cat Form.** In form it costs nothing and has a 6-second cooldown. [[Growl]]'s cooldown is 8 seconds. [[Demoralizing Roar]] takes 56 attack power at rank 1 against Classic's 40, and 204 at rank 5.
- **[[Barkskin]] is usable while shapeshifted**, and so are potions and other consumables.
- `Heart of the Wild` **kept its bear clause: +20% Stamina at 5/5**. It moved to Restoration row 1, and `Furor` holds its old slot in Feral. `Furor` 5/5 still gives 10 rage on every shift into Bear Form.
- **Mangle is `Primal Bite`**, and it lost its debuff. Blizzard: *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Classic's Primal Fury was folded into `Blood Frenzy`: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* Berserk left the tree before launch. Lists naming Mangle, Primal Fury or Berserk cannot be entered as printed, and Wowhead's tank rotation page still mentions Berserk.
## At level 60
**What is published:** the tank and dual-role builds above, both sites' rotation and stat pages, and WoWSims' bear model. They agree on the rotation: `Primal Bite` on cooldown, five [[Lacerate]] stacks kept up, [[Maul]] on spare rage, [[Swipe]] on packs.
**Your defensive buttons are few.** [[Barkskin]] every minute, [[Frenzied Regeneration]] every three, and [[Challenging Roar]] every ten. Wowhead: *"most of our survivability [comes] from gear rather than active mitigation."* Innervate and [[Rebirth]] take you out of form, so they are for moments when you are not being hit.
**What is not known:** whether 440 Defense, the armour you can reach and a bear's health are enough for a level 60 raid boss, and how bear threat compares with a Warrior's or Paladin's there. Raids open December 9, and both sites say their tank pages will change after that.
