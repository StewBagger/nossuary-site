Guides for World of Warcraft: Forever, the 1–60 game that launched on **November 4, 2026**.
:::scope
These pages describe the live game on client 1.60.1.70938, with Blizzard's hotfixes through November 12. They cover levelling from 1 to 60 and what is published for level 60 so far. Wowhead and Icy Veins now publish level-60 builds and gear lists for every class. ClassicWoW.gg has not updated a Forever page since October 14. **No raid is open until December 9**, so there are no raid logs, and every level-60 build, gear list and ranking in print is a prediction. Blizzard tunes classes on Tuesdays, so a number here can change within a week. Where a tooltip on a class page differs from the prose, trust the prose: a tooltip can lag a hotfix.
:::
## What is here
**53 pages**, written from the client's own data and Blizzard's posts rather than remembered from vanilla, and written to say plainly where something is unknown instead of guessing.
Every class page **lists the pages it was compiled from**, as does every profession page that rests on a named source. Four profession pages carry no citation, because they make no claim traceable to one. The three guide sites are Wowhead, Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg, cross-checked against each other. Where those three agree we say so. Where they disagree on something you would act on, the page names which said what rather than quietly picking a winner. Where only one of them scores something, we say that too. Anything we worked out ourselves, such as an extended talent build or a judgement no source makes, is labelled as ours on the page. Combat logs and simulators sit above the guide sites for how a class plays, and a sim is always named as a model. Player forum posts, Reddit threads and YouTube videos count as evidence only when they show their numbers or logs, and the page says so when it uses one. A creator's judgement can appear too, but only under their name, never as a fact. Boosting-service blogs are not cited.
**The class guides** cover all nine classes, with a separate page for every specialisation, 28 in total. Each page carries talent builds from level 10 to 60, a rotation, a stat priority, and what its tree does differently in Forever.
**The profession guides** cover all nine primary professions, the three secondary skills, and the two systems Forever added that have no vanilla equivalent: Camping and Merchant's Favor.
## Where this comes from
**We have no copy of the game.** Nothing here is extracted from an install. What we do read is the client's own database tables, and every ability and talent tooltip on these pages is built from them. That matters, because a lot of what follows is stated flatly.
**The client is read through [wago.tools](https://wago.tools)**, a public database-table archive, at build 1.60.1.70938. Talent trees come from the client's own tree tables, which match the tree Raidbots publishes node for node. [foreverchanges.pro](https://foreverchanges.pro), a client-diff site, reads the same build and publishes per-rank tooltips, training levels and a Forever-versus-Classic comparison. We use it for training levels and as a cross-check.
**Hotfixes reach players before they reach the client files.** Blizzard changes the live game from the server, and the base tables do not carry those changes. We read them from Raidbots' hotfix cache and from the WoWSims project's public record of each hotfix. Where the two differ, the prose states what a player sees and says the tooltip lags.
**Blizzard's own posts** are treated as the strongest evidence wherever they say anything. Since launch that means its hotfix notes, dated November 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11 with a further set live on November 12, and its class-tuning posts.
**The three guide sites are used for builds, rotations and opinion, not for mechanics.** Wowhead and Icy Veins are current, and each page checks the date on what it quotes. ClassicWoW.gg's Forever pages date from the beta.
**One source is deliberately excluded.** On our own reading, Warcraft Tavern carries stale talent data and has asserted mechanics from later expansions that Forever does not have, so it is never a sole source here.
**A quotation is fixed at the date it was read.** Guide sites revise their pages without notice. The quotations here were last read on November 13, 2026.
**The known weaknesses in that chain:**
- **A talent existing in the client is not proof it is reachable.** The tables carry leftovers from Season of Discovery and from later expansions. The old talent table is the plainest case: it still lists Blessing of Kings as a Paladin talent, which Blizzard removed. Where that distinction matters we say so rather than inferring.
- **No raid has been played.** Raids open on December 9. Until then nothing about raid performance on these pages, or on any site, comes from a log.
- **We could not read combat logs for this pass**, so claims about how a spec performs rest on Blizzard's posts, the sims and the guide sites.
## If you only read one thing
**Take Cooking at level 1.** 103 of its 123 dishes give +5% experience from kills while you are well fed, and the first one works at Cooking skill 1 on a level-1 character. It costs you no primary profession slot.
