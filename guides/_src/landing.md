Guides for World of Warcraft: Forever, which relaunches the 1–60 game on **November 4, 2026** with everyone starting from scratch on the same day.
:::scope
Everything here is written for levelling, 1 to 60. The beta closed on October 23 at level 30, so nothing above 30 was tested by players; talents and spells past 30 come from the launch client and Blizzard's posts. When we last checked in October, the three sites these guides are compiled from had published no **level-60 build, best-in-slot list or class ranking** for Forever. ClassicWoW.gg published the complete level-60 talent trees and a calculator, and said of them *"this is the full tree, not a suggested build"*. Icy Veins' tier-list directory listed every tier list as *"COMING SOON"*. Wowhead's class guides stopped at level 20. These pages follow the hotfix Blizzard pushed to the launch realms on October 30, read from the game's hotfix data. Blizzard has not announced it, so it may still move before November 4. Where a tooltip on a class page differs from the prose, trust the prose: a tooltip can lag a hotfix. Raids open December 9, and we will write the endgame half then.

## What is here
**53 pages**, taken from foreverchanges.pro's reading of the launch client rather than remembered from vanilla, and written to say plainly where something is unknown instead of guessing.
Every class page **lists the pages it was compiled from**, as does every profession page that rests on a named source. Four profession pages carry no citation, because they make no claim traceable to one. The three guide sites are Wowhead, Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg, cross-checked against each other. Where those three agree we say so. Where they disagree on something you would act on, the page names which said what rather than quietly picking a winner. Where only one of them scores something, we say that too. Anything we worked out ourselves, such as an extended talent build or a judgement no source makes, is labelled as ours on the page. Player forum threads and boosting-service blogs are not cited, because they are downstream of these three when they are not simply wrong.
**The class guides** cover all nine classes, with a separate page for every specialisation, 28 in total. Each page carries its talent order at 20 and 30, a rotation, a stat priority, and what its tree does differently in Forever.
**The profession guides** cover all nine primary professions, the three secondary skills, and the two systems Forever added that have no vanilla equivalent: Camping and Merchant's Favor.
## Where this comes from
**We have no copy of the game.** Nothing here is extracted from an install. What we do read is the client's own database tables, and every ability and talent tooltip on these pages is built from them. That matters, because a lot of what follows is stated flatly.
**The spine of these guides is [foreverchanges.pro](https://foreverchanges.pro)**, a client-diff site. It publishes per-rank tooltips, talent tiers and a Forever-versus-Classic comparison, reading the client through [wago.tools](https://wago.tools), a public database-table archive, and Raidbots' hotfix data alongside it. We read the same tables there directly. Both readings are on launch client 1.60.1.70707, and ours is how the prose here is spot-checked. The launch realms also receive hotfixes the client files do not yet carry: the October 30 one reworks the Druid and Paladin trees, and Blizzard has posted no notes for it.
Secondary sources, used for opinion and build lists rather than mechanics: Icy Veins and classicwow.gg. **Blizzard's own posts** are treated as the strongest evidence wherever they say anything.
**One source is deliberately excluded.** On our own reading, Warcraft Tavern carries stale talent data and has asserted mechanics from later expansions that Forever does not have, so it is never a sole source here.
**Wowhead is cited and quoted throughout, but it blocks automated access.** Its pages are linked so you can read them yourself. We cannot re-read them on demand, so a Wowhead quotation here is fixed at the date it was taken and is not re-checked on every pass.
**The known weakness in that chain:** a talent existing in the client is not proof it is reachable. The same tables carry leftovers from Season of Discovery and from later expansions. The client's talent table is the plainest case. It still lists Blessing of Kings as a Paladin talent, which Blizzard removed. All 27 of its trees are laid out as Classic's seven rows, with no sign of Forever's new 16-point milestone. Where that distinction matters we say so rather than inferring.
## If you only read one thing
**Take Cooking at level 1.** 103 of its 123 dishes give +5% experience from kills while you are well fed, and the first one works at Cooking skill 1 on a level-1 character. It costs you no primary profession slot.
