Paladin is a plate-wearing melee fighter that runs on mana, and it is no longer faction-locked. Undead Paladins are new in Forever, with their own class hub in Tirisfal Glades.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Holy](/guides/wow-forever/paladin/holy/)** | Healer | `Reverence` keeps 30% of your mana regeneration running while you cast, and gates `Illumination` | Plate healer whose Blessings and auras cover the whole party, and now an area-heal capstone |
| **[Protection](/guides/wow-forever/paladin/protection/)** | Tank | `Shield Specialization` turns blocks into mana, which funds [[Consecration]] | A shield tank whose threat is Holy damage, so **Spell Power** sits on its gear lists |
| **[Retribution](/guides/wow-forever/paladin/retribution/)** | Melee DPS | [[Seal of Command]] at 20 procs Holy off each swing, and a stun amplifies its Judgement | Two-hander plus Holy, and [[Holy Strike]] arrives at level 6 |
**Every Paladin now gets [[Holy Strike]] at level 6**, twenty levels earlier than Classic gave you a button. [[Consecration]] and [[Blessing of Kings]] are trained at 20 in every spec, so the three share more of their kit than they did in Classic.
**Dungeon experience was cut, then partly restored.** Dungeon quests reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values, which Blizzard framed as a deliberate reduction in levelling speed. Kills inside dungeons launched at 40% of their Classic Era experience. Blizzard raised that by half on November 8, to 60% of Classic Era, and has said the result fits the rest of the game. The party experience penalty is judged per member, not by the highest-level member.
**Dual Specialization is bought at 40.** Wowhead reports it comes from your class trainer at level 40 for 50 gold. That is the cheap way to hold a tanking or healing set of talents beside a solo one.
**`Crusade` and `Improved Holy Strike` are gone**, and a published build that spends points on either cannot be entered. Blizzard made the shorter cooldown baseline, so [[Holy Strike]] sits on 10 seconds for every Paladin. The dive names what else each spec gave up: `Improved Retribution Aura` and `Sanctity Aura` for Retribution, `Blessing of Sanctuary` and `Improved Concentration Aura` for Protection.
## Is Paladin for you?
Play Paladin if you want a melee class that carries its own heals, wears mail from level 1 and plate from 40, and can train for whichever of the three roles a group is short of.
:::strengths
- Heals itself in every spec: [[Holy Light]] from level 1, [[Lay on Hands]] from 10, [[Divine Shield]] from 34
- Covers all three group roles — [[Seal of Fury]] taunts from 10, and the heals need no talent points
- Blessings and auras apply to the whole party, and Blessings now last a full hour
- Blizzard calls Paladins the strongest single-target healers in the game, and very strong against Undead and Demons
:::
:::weaknesses
- Holy and Protection spend their talent points on healing and threat rather than damage
- Runs on mana: [[Holy Strike]], [[Judgement]] and [[Consecration]] all draw on the same bar
- No area damage at all until [[Consecration]] at 20
- Blizzard's own list of what the class lacks: no interrupt, no way to slow a target, and less reach than its other mana users even with Holy talents
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Three buttons: keep a seal up, judge it, strike on cooldown.
**Is it forgiving?** You heal yourself, wear mail from level 1 and plate from 40, and get [[Divine Shield]] at 34.
**What carries you while levelling?** Retribution carries the damage: [[Holy Strike]] from level 6, a two-hander, and [[Consecration]] added at 20.
**Does it need a group?** No. Every spec trains heals and a taunt, so you can fill whatever a group lacks.
:::

## Which race
Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation. **No race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP. The ones that matter there break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead, fear, sleep and charm), `Will to Survive` (Human, stuns) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf, bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Alliance: Human or Dwarf.** Human's `Ruthless` gives +1% crit to all spells and attacks, which feeds both gearing paths, and `The Human Spirit` adds 10% Spirit. Dwarf's `Stoneform` is a real solo cooldown, and `Artisan's Precision` gives +1% hit with spells and attacks.
**Horde: Undead, and it is forced**, being the only Horde Paladin. It is good anyway *(ours, not a source's)*. `Cannibalize` now restores 35% health *and* mana, which on a mana-using melee class is close to a free recovery on every corpse.
**Paladin is no longer Alliance-only.** Undead Paladins get their own hub at Bandarion Keep in the previously inaccessible Whispering Wood, Tirisfal Glades, and their own level 40 and 60 mount quests.
## Quality of life
- **Seals, Blessings and Auras are three separate always-on systems, and new players drop one.** Set an aura and forget it. Keep a blessing on yourself. Keep a seal up always: Judgement no longer consumes your seal in Forever, so there is no excuse for letting it lapse.
- **Only one-handed maces are known by default.** Every other weapon skill, including all two-handers, must be bought from a weapon master. Polearms from 20.
- **Do the level-20 class quest**, which rewards a deliberately over-budgeted weapon. Alliance get Verigan's Fist from *The Test of Righteousness*. Horde get Wolfsbane, even slower and more powerful, from the chain that opens with *Diplomatic Incident* in Bandarion Keep. Icy Veins has both chains available from level 20 and sends both through the Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep and Blackfathom Deeps.
- **[[Consecration]] at 20 changes the class** from single-target to area-capable, and every spec now gets it. Budget mana for it.
- **[[Judgement]] is a ranged attack since November 11.** Blizzard changed it so it no longer counts as a melee attack for item and effect procs, and it uses your ranged hit chance. It still takes no global cooldown, so press it alongside another button.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Judgement no longer consumes your seal** | You stop re-casting after every Judgement |
| [[Judgement]] is a ranged attack | Since the November 11 hotfix it uses ranged hit chance and sets off no melee item or effect procs |
| [[Consecration]] and [[Blessing of Kings]] trained at 20 | Every spec gets both without spending talent points |
| [[Holy Strike]] at 6 and [[Seal of Fury]] at 10 | Two new baseline buttons inside the first ten levels, and judging the seal taunts at 10 yards, further than a Druid's or a Warrior's taunt reaches |
| Blessings last 1 hour, Greater Blessings too | No more desynced buff timers |
| `Repentance` moved from tier 7 to tier 5 | The Retribution capstone slot is now `Unsealed Wrath`. [[Seal of Command]] is unchanged: tier 3, live at 20, as in Classic |
| Resistance auras are raid-wide | Fire, Frost and Shadow resistance reach every raid member within 30 yards |
| Seal twisting is gone from the baseline class | The nearest thing left is Retribution's capstone `Unsealed Wrath`, which stores your Seal and releases it when you attack under a different one *(ours, not a source's)* |
| [[Consecration]] deals real damage to only 4 enemies | Blizzard capped it there on purpose, to keep the Paladin level with a Warrior's Thunderclap instead of making the class an area-damage class |
| Salvation is gone | Blizzard removed `Salvation`, `Mass Salvation` and `Revoke`, so a Paladin has no threat-reduction spell to learn and the classes you group with keep all their threat |
| [[Righteous Fury]] at 10 | Trained at the same level other tanks get their tanking stance. It raises all your threat by 33% and your Holy damage's threat by another 90%, and the Legacy perk `The Quick and the Dead` makes it free too |
| Undead Paladins exist | With their own hub, quests and mounts |
## At level 60
Published 51-point builds exist for every spec. None is measured: raids open December 9, 2026, and until then no log shows what a Paladin does in one.
- **Retribution** has two published builds. One takes the `Unsealed Wrath` capstone and swaps between [[Seal of Command]] and [[Seal of Righteousness]]. The other skips the capstone for [[Holy Shock]] and gears for Spell Power. Wowhead and Icy Veins both publish both, and WoWSims' default talents match Wowhead's capstone build.
- **Protection** builds agree on 35 points in Protection through `Shieldsworn`, with the rest in Retribution for `Pursuit of Justice` and `Sacred Arbiter`.
- **Holy** builds agree on 31 or more points in Holy through `Light's Vigil`, with `Divine Intellect` from Protection and `Benediction` and `Conviction` from Retribution.
Blizzard's dive closes by calling all three specs playable at the top: effective tanks, competitive damage dealers, the strongest single-target healers. It publishes no level-60 numbers behind that. The spec pages carry the builds, their dates and where the two sites disagree.
