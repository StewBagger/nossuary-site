## What Marksmanship is for
**The tree raises what your own shots do.** `Lethal Attacks` is +5% crit with **all** attacks at 5/5. `Careful Aim` adds up to 100% of your Intellect to both melee and ranged Attack Power. `Mortal Shots` raises ranged crit damage, `Barrage` raises [[Aimed Shot]], [[Multi-Shot]] and [[Volley]] by up to 15%, and `Ranged Weapon Specialization` adds 5% ranged damage.
**It also pays its own Mana.** `Rapid Killing` cuts [[Rapid Fire]]'s cooldown by 2 minutes at 2/2 and gives your next Shot +20% damage after a kill. `Rapid Recuperation` lets Mana regeneration continue while casting after a [[Serpent Sting]]. Icy Veins calls the pair *"incredibly important"* and says it *"will solve almost all of your mana issues while leveling."*
**`Lone Wolf` gives +20% damage with all attacks, and only with no pet out.** It is the one talent in the Hunter trees that pays you for fighting without a pet. Both sites leave it out of their levelling builds. Wowhead: it *"isn't really an option while leveling since you need a pet to tank for you."* At 60, both sites and WoWSims print a `Lone Wolf` build (see below).
Icy Veins' levelling page describes the cost of the spec plainly: it buffs the player instead of the pet, so the pet holds threat less well and you lean more on Mana. That is a statement about solo levelling with a pet tank.

**Level 30: 0/21/0.** `Lethal Attacks` 5 · `Careful Aim` 5 · `Rapid Killing` 2 · `Improved Stings` 3 · `Trueshot Aura` 1 · `Mortal Shots` 4 · `Rapid Recuperation` 1.
**Level 40: 0/31/0.** `Lethal Attacks` 5 · `Careful Aim` 5 · `Rapid Killing` 2 · `Improved Stings` 3 · `Trueshot Aura` 1 · `Mortal Shots` 5 · `Rapid Recuperation` 2 · `Barrage` 2 · `Ranged Weapon Specialization` 5 · [[Sniper Shot]] 1.
**Level 60: 19/32/0.** `Lethal Attacks` 5 · `Careful Aim` 5 · `Rapid Killing` 2 · `Improved Stings` 3 · `Trueshot Aura` 1 · `Mortal Shots` 5 · `Rapid Recuperation` 2 · `Barrage` 3 · `Ranged Weapon Specialization` 5 · [[Sniper Shot]] 1 · `Deadly Aspects` 5 · `Focused Fire` 2 · `Improved Revive Pet` 2 · `Endurance Training` 1 · `Pathfinding` 2 · `Bestial Swiftness` 1 · `Unleashed Fury` 5 · `Summon Hawk` 1.
What the order is doing:
- **`Trueshot Aura` lands at 25**, the 16-point talent. It gives 20 ranged Attack Power to the party or raid at rank 1, and the trainer sells ranks at 32, 40, 50 and 60 for 35, 50, 75 and 100.
- **`Mortal Shots` needs `Careful Aim` 5/5, and `Rapid Recuperation` needs `Rapid Killing` 2/2.** [[Sniper Shot]] needs `Trueshot Aura`.
- **After 31 points Icy Veins goes to Beast Mastery** for `Deadly Aspects`, `Focused Fire` and finally `Summon Hawk`, on the grounds that one hawk *"provides far more damage than a single Arcane Shot."*
- **Wowhead's levelling build differs** (15/36/0, page dated November 4). It takes `Efficiency` 5 and `Improved Arcane Shot` 5, adds `Scatter Shot`, and skips [[Sniper Shot]] until 60: *"incredibly mana hungry and has a really long cast time."*
- **At level 20 Wowhead does not start in Marksmanship at all.** Its level 20 page prints `Deadly Aspects` 5, `Focused Fire` 2 and `Lethal Attacks` 4, with the reason: *"A pet will currently do more damage than the bonus you would get from Lone Wolf, so taking a couple of points in the BM tree is strongly advised."*
**Level 60, the `Lone Wolf` build: 5/41/5**, Wowhead's dungeon and raid build, page dated November 4, 2026. `Lethal Attacks` 5 · `Careful Aim` 5 · `Improved Stings` 3 · `Efficiency` 5 · `Rapid Killing` 2 · `Improved Arcane Shot` 5 · `Lone Wolf` 1 · `Trueshot Aura` 1 · `Mortal Shots` 5 · `Rapid Recuperation` 2 · `Barrage` 1 · `Ranged Weapon Specialization` 5 · [[Sniper Shot]] 1 · `Deadly Aspects` 5 · `Improved Tracking` 5.
Icy Veins' `Lone Wolf` build is the same 5/41/5 split with `Barrage` 3 and `Hawk Eye` 3 in place of `Improved Arcane Shot`. WoWSims' Marksmanship preset is 5/41/5 too *(WoWSims' model)*. Icy Veins also prints a pet build, 16/31/4, which drops `Lone Wolf` and takes Beast Mastery to `Summon Hawk`. Its test for choosing is whether you want a pet, and for the goal *"to maximize your damage for dungeons and raids, especially for AoE"* it recommends going without.
## Rotation
**Through level 24 this is the same list as Beast Mastery's.** Icy Veins' levelling priority:
1. **[[Aspect of the Hawk]]** up; [[Aspect of the Cheetah]] when kiting
2. **[[Hunter's Mark]]** before the pull
3. **Send the pet in first**, abilities on auto-cast. With `Lone Wolf` talented you keep no pet out at all
4. **[[Serpent Sting]]** once the target is engaged. With `Rapid Killing`, you want it on the target when it dies
5. **With the `Rapid Killing` buff up, open with [[Aimed Shot]]**, or [[Sniper Shot]] once you have it
6. **[[Aimed Shot]]** on cooldown
7. **[[Auto Shot]]** as filler
8. **[[Disengage]]** if you pull aggro; **[[Arcane Shot]]** with Mana to spare
**Multi-target:** [[Multi-Shot]] instead of [[Aimed Shot]], an [[Explosive Trap]] from level 34, and [[Volley]] from 40 on large groups.
**At 60, Wowhead's single-target list:** [[Hunter's Mark]], [[Serpent Sting]] kept up, [[Arcane Shot]], [[Aimed Shot]], then [[Sniper Shot]]. Its only damage cooldown is [[Rapid Fire]], used whenever it is up.
Below level 10, kite backwards between shots to stay out of the dead zone.
**What decides each press:**
- **Stand still for Auto Shot.** Blizzard's notes say Auto Shot needs 0.5 seconds standing still before it fires, and that it keeps firing while you cast [[Aimed Shot]] or [[Sniper Shot]]. So a cast costs you no Auto Shots and walking does.
- **[[Serpent Sting]] or [[Arcane Shot]].** The sting is the better use of Mana if the target lives. Rank 3 costs 50 Mana and deals 70 over 15 seconds, against 50 for Arcane Shot at level 20. Rank 4 costs 80 and deals 130, against 85 at level 28. All before attack power, which widens the gap: each tick of the sting adds 5% of it, 25% over the full sting, against 15% for Arcane Shot. Those shares are Blizzard's November 2 figures, and WoWSims reports level 34 and 36 combat logs fit both. The sting passes Arcane Shot on its fourth tick, at 12 seconds. In a group, `Rapid Killing` also triggers when a target dies with your sting on it, so a kill you did not land still pays.
- **[[Arcane Shot]] costs 11% of base Mana at every rank.** Since November 10 the cost follows your level, not the rank. `Improved Arcane Shot` takes 1.5 seconds off its 6-second cooldown at 5/5.
- **[[Multi-Shot]] or [[Aimed Shot]].** They share a 6-second cooldown, so you get one or the other. Aimed Shot is your weapon hit plus a flat bonus: 40 at rank 1, 70 at rank 2, up to 350 at rank 6. Multi-Shot is the weapon hit with no bonus, on up to three targets. Each extra target adds a whole weapon hit, so two targets beat Aimed Shot once your weapon hit is larger than that bonus. `Lone Wolf` and `Barrage` raise both alike, so neither changes that answer.
- **[[Sniper Shot]] goes last.** It is a 4-second cast on a 15-second cooldown for weapon damage plus 320 at rank 1, 450 at 48 and 590 at 58, and it costs 365 Mana. WoWSims casts it only when nothing else is ready, because it deals the least damage per second of casting, and still gains about 43 DPS on its preset by taking it. It also opens the fight with one, started before the pull *(WoWSims' model)*. Wowhead's note is that it forces points into `Efficiency`.
- **[[Sniper Shot]]'s range.** It reaches 45 yards and adds 10 yards to your next 3 Shots within 10 seconds. `Hawk Eye` adds 6 more at 3/3.
- **Dismiss the pet for `Lone Wolf`.** The talent pays only with no active pet. Take the talent and keep a pet out, and you get neither.
- **`Lone Wolf` or a pet at 60.** On November 1 WoWSims had `Lone Wolf` ahead of a Raptor by 18 DPS on its Marksmanship preset. That figure predates the November 10 pet and hawk changes, and the model has not published a re-run of the comparison *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **[[Volley]] since November 11.** It no longer triggers procs and it stops if you move. `Barrage` still raises it. In WoWSims' level 34 and 36 logs each of its six waves hit every enemy in the area and no Auto Shot fired during the channel *(WoWSims' reading of the logs)*.
## Stat priority
**While levelling: weapon damage, then Agility**, then the highest item level you can wear. The slower weapon is better: [[Aimed Shot]] scales off damage per hit, not DPS. Intellect is worth more here than on any other physical spec once `Careful Aim` is in, at level 19 on Icy Veins' order.
**At 60 the two sites agree on the order and disagree on the hit cap.** Both list hit, then crit, then Agility, then Intellect, then Attack Power. Wowhead says 9% hit and labels its list speculative. Icy Veins says 8%.

## Marksmanship talent changes, Classic to Forever
Blizzard's own changelog for the tree:
- `Hawk Eye` moved from row 3 up to row 1
- `Lethal Attacks` (formerly `Lethal Shots`) moved from row 2 up to row 1, and now grants increased critical strike chance to **all** attacks, not just ranged
- `Efficiency` moved from row 1 down to row 2, and its Mana discount is now larger and applies to Stings and melee abilities as well as Shots
- `Careful Aim`, new, increases melee and ranged Attack Power by 20/40/60/80/100% of your Intellect
- `Rapid Killing`, new, reduces `Rapid Fire`'s cooldown and grants a buff that boosts your next Shot's damage on a kill, or when Serpent Sting's target dies while afflicted
- `Rapid Recuperation`, new, requires `Rapid Killing` first: regenerates Mana from Spirit while casting after a Serpent Sting hit, and more during Rapid Killing
- `Improved Arcane Shot` now also cuts [[Arcane Shot]]'s cooldown by up to an extra 0.5 seconds
- `Trueshot Aura` moved from row 7 up to row 4, the 16-point gold medal talent (Blizzard's article says 21, but row 4 is the 16-point row and the client teaches it at level 25), with new lower-rank versions added. It is raid-wide and gives 20, 35, 50, 75 and 100 ranged Attack Power by rank
- `Mortal Shots` now requires `Careful Aim` as a prerequisite
- `Barrage` keeps Classic's 5/10/15% damage bonus, and now also applies to [[Aimed Shot]], on top of the [[Multi-Shot]] and [[Volley]] it already covered
- **Removed:** `Improved Hunter's Mark` and [[Aimed Shot]] are both baseline abilities now, not talents
## What Forever changed for Hunters
- **Pets scale their stats off your gear**, and can no longer receive player buffs that increase their stats. On November 10 Blizzard set pet Health to 115% of normal, down from a bugged 150%. Whether a pet's family changes its damage is disputed between the sites; the client's table is on the [Hunter overview](/guides/wow-forever/hunter/#pet-families).
- **`Improved Serpent Sting` became `Improved Stings`** (moved from row 4 up to row 2), a larger Serpent Sting damage increase that now also shortens the [[Viper Sting]] cooldown and extends [[Scorpid Sting]]'s duration. Build lists naming the old talent are stale.
- **[[Sniper Shot]] is Marksmanship's new capstone**, the 31-point gold medal talent, and it requires `Trueshot Aura` first. Blizzard's November 2 notes doubled its bonus damage.
- The utility kit is unchanged: [[Feign Death]], [[Freezing Trap]], [[Frost Trap]], [[Concussive Shot]], [[Disengage]].
## At level 60
The builds and the rotation above are published. What is not yet known is how they hold in a raid: none opens until December 9. The open question is `Lone Wolf` against a pet after the November 10 tuning. The sites' builds predate it and no source has re-measured. In a dungeon either works: with a pet, keep it on the tank's target; without one, you bring `Trueshot Aura` and the area tools above.
