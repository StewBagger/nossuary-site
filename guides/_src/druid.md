Druid is four roles on one character, and Forever changed a gearing rule outright: **your weapon's damage now scales Bear and Cat form**. In Classic it did not matter at all.

## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Balance](/guides/wow-forever/druid/balance/)** | Ranged DPS | `Nature's Reach` 2/2 adds 20% range and 4% hit to its spells, and [[Moonkin Form]] gives the party or raid +3% to every kind of critical strike | Levels on [[Wrath]] and two damage-over-time spells; `Eclipse` and [[Moonkin Form]] come at 40 or later on the published orders |
| **[Feral — Bear](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-bear/)** | Tank | Multiplies the armour on your gear, raises threat by 115%, and can drop form to heal itself | Leather tanking on rage, armour and dodge, with no block and no parry |
| **[Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/)** | Melee DPS | [[Rip]] scales with attack power, so five combo points keep a 12-second bleed running | Energy and combo points, +30% movement speed, and no area damage |
| **[Restoration](/guides/wow-forever/druid/restoration/)** | Healer | `Swiftmend` turns your running [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] into an instant heal without using it up | Heals over time placed ahead of the damage, and `Bloom` pays out again when one expires |
**Bear and Cat share one tree and one levelling build**, but they get separate pages: the rotations, stat priorities and gear are different. Bear is your only combat form from 10 to 20. [[Aquatic Form]] arrives at 16 and is for swimming. Cat Form does not exist until 20, and it comes from a quest chain in Moonglade rather than a trainer. See the [Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/) page for the chain.
**You do not have to level in the spec you will play.** Both guide sites say to level Feral or Balance and heal dungeons in that build. From 40 your class trainer sells Dual Specialization for 50 gold, per Wowhead.
**Two habits from Classic you must unlearn.** First, your weapon's damage counts in Bear and Cat Form, so pick the weapon for its DPS. Second, the Cat energy trick is gone: `Furor` no longer hands you a flat 40 Energy for entering Cat Form. At 5/5 it restores the Energy you last had plus 10 per second spent outside Bear and Dire Bear Form, capped at 100, so shifting out and back cannot beat staying in Cat. The replacement is `Shifting Power` at 40, which turns Mana into Energy. The Bear half is unchanged: 10 rage on every shift into Bear at 5/5.
## Is Druid for you?
Play Druid if you want one character that can do any job in the game, and you accept a slow first twenty levels. You have no combat form at all until Bear Form at 10, and no Cat Form until 20.
Blizzard's own class deep dive sharpens that trade-off. Druids have the "ability to serve any combat role, given the right equipment and talents," but "that versatility comes at the cost of non-damage utility." They "cannot snare targets, cannot Silence or Interrupt, and have weaker and less reliable crowd control," and their healing "relies on healing-over-time (HoT) spells that require more planning." They "lack the extended utility toolkits found on Rogues, Warriors, or Mages," so they "can perform any role but will need to rely on others to provide the complete toolkit needed for raids and dungeons."
:::strengths
- Blizzard credits Druids with the *"ability to serve any combat role"* — and Bear and Cat need no respec between them
- [[Travel Form]] at 30 is +40% speed outdoors, ten levels before a mount
- Shapeshifting breaks roots and slows
:::
:::weaknesses
- Wowhead on the early levels: *"Leveling as a Feral Druid can start off feeling very slow"*
- Three separate resources, and shifting costs Mana every time
- Blizzard: the versatility *"comes at the cost of non-damage utility"* — no snare, Silence or Interrupt
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes, within a form: a level-20 Cat is three buttons. You have several forms.
**Is it forgiving?** Bear Form multiplies your gear's armour and you can drop form to heal yourself; Cat has neither.
**What carries you while levelling?** Bear Form from a class quest at 10 and Cat Form at 20. In Forever your weapon's damage scales both.
**Does it need a group?** No. But a Feral Druid in a group will be asked to tank, and any Druid can heal a levelling dungeon.
:::

**Tree of Life and Flight Form are not available.** Neither is in a talent tree or on the trainer's list at this build, and Blizzard's deep dive lists neither. [[Moonkin Form]] is the 31-point Balance talent. It gives the party or raid +3% to every kind of critical strike within 45 yards, the same bonus as [[Leader of the Pack]] and exclusive with it, and it doubles your [[Omen of Clarity]] proc rate.
**Training costs about 206 gold from 1 to 60**, the same for every class per Blizzard. Wowhead's Feral pages list the ranks you can skip.
## Which race
**Read this section in proportion.** Race is the smallest choice on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and no race is a wrong answer for levelling *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in PvP, specifically the ones that break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead: fear, sleep and charm), `Escape Artist` (Gnome: roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf: bleeds, poisons and disease). None of those three races can be a Druid.
**Alliance: Night Elf.** `Elune's Light` is +10% critical strike with all spells and attacks for 15 seconds, per the client. [[Shadowmeld]] is usable while shapeshifted, per Blizzard's class deep dive. Since November 6 it no longer lets you use [[Ravage]] or [[Pounce]] from it.
**Horde: Tauren.** `Endurance` is 5% Health. `War Stomp` is usable while shapeshifted, per Blizzard's class deep dive, which gives every form a short area stun.
**`Plainsrunning` gives 2% movement speed per stack, up to 60%, and nothing while mounted.**
**Skyborne can be Druids on both factions**, the only new path into the class, with forms made for the race and 2% haste from their Wind-Blessed racial.
## Quality of life
- **Take the highest-DPS weapon you can get and keep sharpening stones on it.** This reverses the Classic rule. Weapon speed does not matter: forms swing at a fixed speed.
- **Bear Form at 10 comes from a class quest.** Go to your trainer the moment you hit 10. Until then you are a caster in cloth: Snupy spams [[Wrath]] and uses [[Moonfire]], trained at 4, to tag mobs first.
- **[[Travel Form]] at 30 is your mount ten levels early.** Instant, outdoors only, and most world objects can be used without dropping it.
- **Consumables work while shapeshifted**, so a bear can drink a potion and a cat can use a Thistle Tea, per Blizzard's own list. Weapon enchants and procs fire in form too, and most doors and world objects open without dropping your shapeshift. The holdouts are most Engineering bombs, with the SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes as the stated exceptions, and Mining and Fishing, per Icy Veins.
- **Dual Specialization is 50 gold at 40**, from your class trainer, per Wowhead. That is the same level as your mount, so you may have to choose which comes first. Respec costs rise each time you pay them.
- **Spell Power is worth wearing before 20.** Wowhead: it helps your spells before the forms arrive, and [[Thorns]] scales with it.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon damage now scales Bear, Cat and Dire Bear form** | Auto-attack DPS in form equals your weapon's DPS, at a fixed 1.0-second swing in Cat Form and 2.5 seconds in Bear and Dire Bear, and weapon-damage abilities build on it. Chase weapon DPS, not weapon speed |
| Tiger's Fury removed | Replaced by a Feral talent, `Shifting Power` — row 7, behind `Predatory Discipline`, level 40, Cat Form only |
| Bear and Dire Bear threat is +115% | Raised from 50% at launch, to make up for Blessing of Salvation being removed |
| All Shapeshift Forms immune to Disarm | Only once you are already shifted: disarmed *before* you shift, the form does **not** clear the Disarm |
| [[Revive]] added at level 12 | An out-of-combat resurrect, **eight levels before [[Rebirth]]** |
| [[Nature's Grasp]] and [[Omen of Clarity]] now trained | Both were talents; Omen procs off melee as well as spells |
| [[Faerie Fire]] usable in forms | Free in form with a 6-second cooldown, and it does not reset your swing timer |
| `Swiftmend` moved to Restoration row 4 | A healer can have it at 25. It needs a [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] you placed yourself and does not use it up |
| [[Barkskin]] usable while shapeshifted | A bear or a cat can press it without dropping form |
| [[Hurricane]]'s cooldown removed | Balance and Restoration both use it on packs from 40 |
| Consumables usable in form | A bear can drink a potion |
| **Mangle is called `Primal Bite`** | Bear only, no debuff. Older guides use the old name |
## At level 60
**Every spec has a published level 60 build now**, from Icy Veins, Wowhead and, for Balance and Feral, WoWSims. They are on the spec pages with their dates. All of them reach outside their own tree: Feral and Balance take `Naturalist` for +10% damage, and Restoration takes `Nature's Splendor` from Balance.
**Dungeons.** Both sites expect a Feral Druid to tank, call Cat's lack of area damage its weak point, and describe dungeon healing as light enough to add damage between heals.
**Raids open December 9**: Barrow Deeps for 10, Hyjal Summit for 20 and Onyxia's Lair for 40. Until then nobody has a raid log. For bear tanking, both sites aim for 440 Defense to stop boss critical strikes and say that figure is untested; Icy Veins adds that a bear cannot avoid Crushing Blows, so armour and health have to absorb them. The tier lists on both sites were written before launch and before six rounds of hotfixes.
