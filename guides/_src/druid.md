Druid is four classes in one character, and Forever changed a gearing rule outright: **your weapon's damage now scales Bear and Cat form**. In Classic it did not matter at all.
:::scope
Written for levelling, 1 to 60. Forever's beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day, so **no level-60 Druid build has been tested**. Moonkin Form is a 31-point capstone, so **nobody in the beta has ever been in it**. Naming note, confirmed by Blizzard's own class deep dive: the Feral talent older guides call Mangle is **Primal Bite** in the current client — *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Blizzard's own caveat applies throughout: *"class abilities and talents may continue to evolve throughout the beta test and are subject to additional changes."*
:::
## Which spec?
| Spec | Role | Its strongest point | In one line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Balance](/guides/wow-forever/druid/balance/)** | Ranged DPS | `Eclipse` at 30: each [[Wrath]] cuts 0.50 s off your next two [[Starfire]]s, no crit needed | [[Wrath]] got 50% more base damage on September 24, and [[Moonkin Form]] waits until 40 |
| **[Feral — Bear](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-bear/)** | Tank | Multiplies the armour on your gear, adds +180 health, and can drop form to heal itself | Leather tanking on rage and dodge, with no block and no parry |
| **[Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/)** | Melee DPS | [[Rip]] scales with attack power now, so five combo points keep a 12-second bleed running | Energy and combo points, and +30% movement speed permanently |
| **[Restoration](/guides/wow-forever/druid/restoration/)** | Healer | `Swiftmend` turns a running [[Rejuvenation]] or [[Regrowth]] into an instant heal and leaves it ticking | HoTs rather than repair, and a 1-second global cooldown on [[Rejuvenation]] |
**Bear and Cat share one tree and mostly one build**, but they get separate pages: the **rotations, stat priorities and gear are completely different** even where the talents are not. Bear is your only **combat** form from 10 to 20. [[Aquatic Form]] arrives at 16 and is for swimming. **Cat Form does not exist until 20 and now comes from a quest chain**, not a trainer. The level, the Moonglade chain and its three relics come from the guide sites and the client; what Blizzard corroborates is that the quests exist — its October 1 quest fixes name "The Great Cat Spirit" and "The Great Windborne Cat Spirit". See the [Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/) page for the chain.
**Two habits from Classic you must unlearn.** **Your weapon's damage now counts in Bear and Cat Form.** Three sources confirm it, so pick the weapon for its damage. And **the Cat energy trick is gone**: `Furor` no longer hands you a flat 40 energy for entering Cat Form. Blizzard's framing is that five points let you regenerate Energy out of form as if you were in Cat Form — and that **shifting to Bear Form loses all of it**, *"to prevent 'bear-weaving' gameplay."* The client's figures, which Blizzard has not published, are the Energy you last had plus 10 per second out of form, capped at 100. Either way, shifting out and back can no longer beat staying in Cat. **The Bear half is unchanged**: 10 rage on every shift into Bear at 5/5, exactly as in Classic.
## Is Druid for you?
Play Druid if you want to be able to do any job in the game on one character, and you accept what one published guide calls *"a fairly slow start"* — no combat form at all until Bear Form at 10, and no Cat Form until 20.
Blizzard's own class deep dive sharpens that trade-off rather than softening it: Druids "can serve any combat role, given the right equipment and talents," but "that versatility comes at the cost of non-damage utility" — they "cannot snare targets, cannot Silence or Interrupt, and have weaker and less reliable crowd control," their healing "relies on healing-over-time spells that require more planning," and they "lack the extended utility toolkits found on Rogues, Warriors, or Mages," so they "can perform any role but will need to rely on others to provide the complete toolkit needed for raids and dungeons."
:::strengths
- Blizzard: Druids *"can serve any combat role"* — and Bear and Cat need no respec between them
- [[Travel Form]] at 30 is a mount ten levels early, and you can gather herbs in it
- Shapeshifting breaks roots and slows
:::
:::weaknesses
- A published caveat on the early levels: *"Feral Druid has a fairly slow start"*
- Three separate resources that do not talk to each other
- Blizzard: the versatility *"comes at the cost of non-damage utility"* — no snare, Silence or Interrupt
:::
:::new
**Is the rotation simple?** Yes, within a form — a level-20 Cat is two buttons. You have several forms.
**Is it forgiving?** Bear Form multiplies your gear's armour and you can drop form to heal yourself; Cat has neither.
**What carries you while levelling?** Bear Form from a class quest at 10 and Cat Form at 20 — and in Forever your weapon's damage scales both.
**Does it need a group?** No. Blizzard says Druids *"can serve any combat role"*, so groups will take you as tank, healer or damage.
:::

**Nothing in any Forever source mentions [[Tree of Life]] or Flight Form**, so plan without them — Blizzard’s deep dive simply does not list either. Moonkin Form is still the 31-point Balance capstone, so it arrives at 40. It is no longer a dead aura: its party-wide critical strike bonus now applies to **all spells and attacks**, not just spells, it is **exclusive with the same effect from [[Leader of the Pack]]**, and it doubles your [[Omen of Clarity]] proc rate. ## Which race
**Read this section in proportion.** Race is the smallest of the choices on this page, well behind spec, gear and knowing your rotation, and **no race is a wrong answer for levelling** *(ours, not a source's)*. Racials decide things in **PvP**, specifically the ones that break crowd control: `Will of the Forsaken` (Undead: fear, sleep and charm), `Escape Artist` (Gnome: roots and snares) and `Stoneform` (Dwarf: bleeds, poisons and disease). A free trinket slot is worth more than any damage racial below.
**Alliance: Night Elf.** `Elune's Light` is a burst critical-strike cooldown Night Elf never had; its figures are client text, not anything Blizzard has published. The part Blizzard does state is that **[[Shadowmeld]] is usable while shapeshifted**, listed in the class deep dive alongside War Stomp and Walk on Air, so a prowling cat keeps it in form.
**Horde: Tauren.** `Endurance`'s Forever version adds hit chance on top of health, per the client — Blizzard has published no racial figures — and `War Stomp` is usable while shapeshifted in Forever — confirmed directly in Blizzard's class deep dive, which lists it alongside Shadowmeld and Walk on Air as racials now usable in any form — giving every form a short AoE stun.
**`Plainsrunning` does not stack with Cat Form or Travel Form**, so it is worth less to a Druid than to other classes. The same applies to Skyborne's `Skysight`.
**Skyborne can be Druids on both factions**, the only new path into the class, with forms made for the race.
## Quality of life
- **Take the highest-DPS two-hander you can get and keep sharpening stones on it.** This reverses the Classic rule, and three sources confirm the change.
- **Bear Form at 10 comes from a class quest.** Go to your trainer the moment you hit 10. Until then you are a cloth-damage caster.
- **[[Travel Form]] at 30 is your mount ten levels early.** Instant cast, and you can interact with most world objects without dropping it. **You can gather herbs in it.**
- **Consumables now work while shapeshifted**, so a bear can drink a rage potion and a cat can use a Thistle Tea — Blizzard's own list. Weapon enchants and procs (sharpening stones, Crusader, Windfury Totem) now fire in form too, and most doors and world objects can be opened without dropping your shapeshift. The one holdout is most Engineering bombs; the SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes are the stated exceptions. **Dual Specialization becomes available at level 40**, stated in Blizzard’s class deep dive, so from 40 you carry two builds and swap between them instead of paying to respec. Below 40 you pay, at a class trainer in a major city. Respecs cost 1 silver in the beta if you have not changed talents recently, but **the cost after your first reset is much higher and only drops back to 1 silver an hour later** — and the whole price is explicitly a beta value.
## What changed from Classic
| Change | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon damage now scales Bear, Cat and Dire Bear form** | Auto-attack DPS equals your weapon's DPS outright, at a fixed 1.0-second swing in Cat Form and 2.5 seconds in Bear/Dire Bear, and weapon-damage abilities use the same numbers. Chase weapon DPS, not weapon stats — the reverse of Classic |
| `Tiger's Fury` removed | **Live as of October 1, not merely announced.** Replaced by a new Feral talent, `Shifting Power` — Row 4, connected to `Shredding Attacks`, Cat Form only. See [Feral — Cat](/guides/wow-forever/druid/feral-cat/) |
| All Shapeshift Forms immune to Disarm (Oct 1) | Only once you are already shifted: disarmed *before* you shift, the form does **not** clear the Disarm, and you deal reduced damage for its duration |
| [[Revive]] added at level 12 | An out-of-combat resurrect, **eight levels before [[Rebirth]]** |
| [[Nature's Grasp]] and [[Omen of Clarity]] now trained | Both were talents; Omen now procs off melee too |
| [[Faerie Fire]] usable in forms | The separate feral version is gone, and as of October 1 it no longer resets your swing timer when used |
| `Swiftmend` moved to Restoration tier 4 | A healer gets it about fifteen levels earlier, and **it no longer consumes the heal-over-time** |
| [[Barkskin]] usable while shapeshifted | Blizzard’s only stated change to it: a bear or a cat can now pop it without dropping form |
| [[Hurricane]]'s cooldown removed | Fixes the Balance area-damage hole |
| Consumables usable in form | A bear can drink a potion |
| **Mangle is called [[Primal Bite]]** | Older guides use the old name |
## At level 60
Unknown. No source publishes a Druid build past 30, and **nobody has ever been in Moonkin Form**, so whether Forever's reworked version makes Balance a real raid specialisation is untested. For bear tanking specifically, one published assessment is that Feral **remains the only tank with no straightforward way to become immune to critical strikes**, and it cannot block or parry. It has dodge, armour and health only. Raids open December 9.
