Two-handed melee with Holy magic layered on top. **[[Holy Strike]] at level 6** turns it from auto-attack-and-wait into something you actually play, twenty levels earlier than Classic gave a Paladin a button.

Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta closed on October 23 at level 30, so nothing above 30 was tested by players; talents and spells past 30 come from the launch client and Blizzard's posts. Blizzard published its Paladin class deep dive on October 15, the last of the series, and this page is written from it and then read back against the launch client. Where Blizzard speaks, Blizzard is the source; where it says nothing, the client is, and figures that are only a guide site's are marked. These pages follow the hotfix Blizzard pushed to the launch realms on October 30, read from the game's hotfix data. Blizzard has not announced it, so it may still move before November 4.
Talents open at 10. Blizzard keeps the one-point gold medal talents at 11, 21 and 31 points and has added a new one at 16 points, which for Retribution is `Sacred Arbiter` at around level 25. `Repentance` sits at 21 points and `Unsealed Wrath` at 31. Dual Specialization opens at 40.

**`Champion of the Light` converts up to 60% of your Intellect into spell damage** at 5 points, 12% per rank, and takes up to 1 second off `Hammer of Wrath`'s cast. Blizzard's October 15 talent line printed 30%; build against the current tree.

## What Retribution is for
**Layering Holy damage onto a two-handed swing, with a real button to press from level 6.** [[Seal of Command]] at 20 procs Holy damage scaled off the swing, and a stun amplifies its Judgement.
Blizzard's framing for the spec is three things: new ways to sustain Judgements, weapon attacks blended with Holy damage, and a more approachable form of Seal Twisting. Sustaining Judgements starts in row 2 with `Sanctified Judgement`. The capstone in the twisting slot, `Unsealed Wrath`, arrives at 31 points, level 40.
Wowhead's overtuning warning is the only published comment aimed squarely at this spec. It is not a levelling score: no source publishes one for any Paladin spec, and a score would only measure solo kill speed.
**You have no area damage until [[Consecration]] at 20.** Blizzard trains it to every Paladin now instead of selling it as a Holy talent, and it only damages four enemies properly. Pull one target at a time.
**Blizzard states the class has no interrupt and no way to slow an enemy's movement**, and that Holy talents widen its range without matching other mana users. Your own speed comes from one talent, `Pursuit of Justice`. The published guides still call the class *"slow, very slow."*
**Treat mana as an extension of your health bar.** Topping up with [[Holy Light]] and killing the next thing at low mana usually beats sitting down to drink.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.**
1. `Deflection`, 5 points. The client's Paladin row reads **1% parry** a rank, so five points is +5%, and Blizzard has it in row 1, so it is available from level 10
2. `Improved Judgement`, 2 points. The client takes **2 seconds** off [[Judgement]]'s 10-second cooldown
3. `Conviction`, 3 points, and that is forced: [[Seal of Command]] is a tier-3 talent needing 10 points in the tree, and 5+2+1 would not reach it. Three points is the talent's maximum: **6% critical strike chance with all spells and attacks**
4. **[[Seal of Command]]**, 1 point, at 20
**A respec at 20**, for mana rather than damage, is our own extension, not a published build: `Benediction` 5, `Improved Judgement` 2, `Conviction` 3, [[Seal of Command]] 1. The published respec spent two of those points on `Holy Conduit`, which now sits in the Holy tree, so we adjusted it. `Benediction` is broader and shallower than the version those builds were written against: Blizzard applies it to all instant-cast spells and abilities rather than to Seals and [[Judgement]] alone, and the client reads **10%** at 5 points where the old talent took up to 15% off those two.
**The discount is broad but thin** *(ours, not a source's)*. Almost everything a Retribution Paladin presses at 20 is instant, so it touches the whole rotation, but not [[Holy Light]], the mana sink this page tells you to treat as your health bar. `Holy Conduit`, which cheapens [[Consecration]] and [[Exorcism]], is out of reach here: it sits in Holy behind 10 points.
A second published build dropped `Improved Judgement` for more `Conviction`, on the grounds that *"mana is simply not an issue at level 20 with the new changes."* Its points no longer fit the tree, so our version is `Benediction` 5, `Conviction` 3, `Sanctified Judgement` 2, [[Seal of Command]] 1. That position is the better-supported one, which is why the build at the top of this section leads *(ours, not a source's)*.
**Wowhead went further and questioned the whole premise.** Its testers found [[Seal of Command]] *not meaningfully better than* [[Seal of Righteousness]] at 20, once you account for talents and spell power. Its build, written against an older tree, spent that final point elsewhere and aimed at [[Holy Shock]] and `Champion of the Light` later.
Wowhead called that Holy-leaning "Shockadin" build the stronger PvE choice. It rated this page's "Seal Twist" build weaker in PvE at 20, but ahead in PvP burst with a slow two-hander. Blizzard puts [[Holy Shock]] at the Holy tree's 21-point gold medal, ten points earlier in that tree, which makes the hybrid a cheaper plan than it was.
**Neither build can actually twist seals.** Blizzard removed the baseline Seal Twisting that Classic and Classic Anniversary had, and the capstone in its place is `Unsealed Wrath` at 31 points in this tree. A level-20 or level-30 build labelled for twisting is a build for the weapon and the seal choice, not for the twist.
## Rotation
1. **Always have a seal up**: [[Seal of Righteousness]], or [[Seal of Command]] once talented
2. On anything that will survive several Judgements, **judge [[Seal of the Crusader]] first** for the Holy damage amplifier, then switch back. Blizzard states [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your active Seal, so this costs you mana and nothing else, and the debuff lasts 40 seconds. Blizzard also moved the Judgement half of `Improved Seal of the Crusader` into the class, so the amplifier needs no talent points
3. **[[Holy Strike]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
4. **[[Judgement]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
5. With [[Seal of Command]], **[[Hammer of Justice]] first**: Judgement of Command hits far harder into a stunned target
6. **[[Exorcism]]** against Undead and Demons only
7. Maintain a Blessing and an Aura; top up with [[Holy Light]]
**Multi-target: none until 20.** Then [[Consecration]], and it is expensive: **135 Mana** at rank 1, on an 8-second cooldown. Blizzard limits what it can do on purpose: moderate damage to the first 4 enemies that walk into it, very low damage to everything after that, so Paladin threat stays level with a Warrior's Thunderclap. The thin damage on the fifth target is the design, and Blizzard says the limit keeps Forever out of expansion-era AoE pulling. The client agrees. Its rank 1 does 16 Holy damage over 8 seconds to anything in the area, with an extra 32 over 8 seconds for the first four.
Later, `Sacred Arbiter` gives [[Holy Strike]] **+20% damage** and makes it refresh every Judgement effect on the target, **even those from other Paladins**, so staying in melee maintains them without re-judging. Blizzard puts it at the new 16-point gold medal, around level 25; the client's thinner text stops at the Judgements on the target without naming whose they are.
## Stat priority
Weapon DPS, then Hit and Expertise, critical strike, **Spell Power**, Strength, Intellect, Agility, Stamina.
Hit and Expertise barely appear on gear at this level, so they are close to irrelevant below 30. A slower weapon makes [[Holy Strike]] hit harder, but raw weapon DPS usually wins.
**Intellect matters much more from 26 points in the tree, at level 35**, where `Champion of the Light` starts converting it into spell damage. Blizzard places the talent in row 6 of this tree.
**It converts up to 60% of your Intellect at 5 points**, which makes Intellect a damage stat for a Retribution Paladin who goes that deep. Wowhead, Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg may print older figures.
Blizzard confines the effect to damage, so read it as spell damage only.
Published gear lists split into a **Spell Power** build and an Attack Power build, and call both competitive at level 20.

## What Forever changed for Retribution
- **Five new talents:** `Sanctified Judgement` in row 2, `Sacred Arbiter` at the new 16-point gold medal, `Turn Evil` in row 5, `Champion of the Light` in row 6, and `Unsealed Wrath` at 31 points. `Holy Conduit` sits in the Holy tree.
- **Blizzard's removal list for this tree is two talents**, `Improved Retribution Aura` and `Sanctity Aura`, and more have left since. `Improved Seal of the Crusader` is gone with its Judgement bonus baseline. `Improved Holy Strike` is gone with its shorter cooldown baseline. `Improved Blessing of Might`, `Crusade` and `Instrument of Law` are gone too.
- `Unyielding Faith` joins row 3 from the Holy tree. It shortens Fear and Disorient effects instead of improving your chance to resist them, up to 30% off their duration at 2 points.
- `Turn Evil` makes `Turn Undead` cast up to 1 second faster with up to 10 seconds less cooldown, and lets it affect Demons.
- **Seal Twisting is no longer baseline.** `Unsealed Wrath` at 31 points, behind `Repentance`, holds the capstone slot. Your melee attacks store a copy of your active Seal for 15 seconds, stacking up to 3 times, and landing an attack with a different Seal consumes all the stacks, giving the stored Seal its normal chance to activate once per stack. Its text carries no Seal mana discount. It is the nearest the class comes to twisting *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **[[Judgement]] no longer consumes your Seal**, a baseline change Blizzard states for every Paladin. [[Lasting Judgement]] is baseline too, and the client has it adding 10 seconds per rank to `Judgement of Light` and `Judgement of Wisdom`.
- `Vindication` is redesigned. Your damaging melee attacks have a chance to cut the target's Attack Power for 30 seconds. Blizzard tunes the reduction by level to sit close to Demoralizing Shout, and the client's figure at 3 points is 200 Attack Power.
- `Sanctified Judgement` gives [[Judgement]] up to a 100% chance to refund up to 60% of the judged Seal's mana cost, and the client reads the full 100% and 60%. It sits in row 2.
- `Vengeance` sits in row 5 and requires [[Seal of Command]]. It stacks three times at 3% damage each, 9% in total, holds for 30 seconds, and triggers off non-periodic critical strikes only.
- `Eye for an Eye` sits in row 4 and answers every critical strike, not only spells. **Blizzard puts the reflected share at up to 10% of the damage taken**, against Classic's 30%. The client reads the same 10% at 2 points, and caps the reflect at half the Paladin's health.
- `Pursuit of Justice` is worth up to 15% movement speed for the same points. The client confirms it covers mounted speed and that it does not stack with other speed effects.
- `Repentance` is the 21-point gold medal, so it lands around level 30 rather than 40. The client has it incapacitating a Humanoid for up to 6 seconds, broken by any damage.
- `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` gives up to **6% physical damage** and cuts your threat by up to 20% while a two-handed weapon is equipped, 3 points in row 4 at 2% damage per rank.
- [[Consecration]] is baseline at 20 for every Paladin, and Blizzard redesigned it around four targets. See Rotation.
## At level 60
Unknown, and Blizzard frames it as a choice rather than a puzzle. Retribution can take the `Unsealed Wrath` capstone or hybridise into [[Holy Shock]] for Holy damage, and Blizzard positioned [[Holy Shock]] as the alternative so that opting out of the capstone is a real build. Neither side has been tested at 51 points. The author of one published guide says *"it's looking like twisting might not be the way to go for Paladin raid DPS specs atm."* Blizzard's closing line is that Retribution stays a strong damage dealer with steadier damage and more group utility, naming `Sacred Arbiter` and its upkeep of other Paladins' Judgements as the example.
