Two-handed melee with Holy magic layered on top. **[[Holy Strike]] at level 6** turns it from auto-attack-and-wait into something you actually play, twenty levels earlier than Classic gave a Paladin a button.

## What Retribution is for
**Layering Holy damage onto a two-handed swing, with a real button to press from level 6.** [[Seal of Command]] at 20 procs Holy damage scaled off the swing, and a stun amplifies its Judgement.
Blizzard's framing for the spec is three things: new ways to sustain Judgements, weapon attacks blended with Holy damage, and a more approachable form of Seal Twisting. Sustaining Judgements starts in row 2 with `Sanctified Judgement`. The capstone in the twisting slot, `Unsealed Wrath`, arrives at 31 points, level 40.
**It is the solo levelling spec of the three.** Wowhead's levelling page calls it one of the smoother classes to level: little downtime, plate and self-healing, and burst from [[Judgement]] and [[Holy Strike]] that is back up for each new enemy. It lists the costs too: no ranged pull, heavy dependence on your weapon, and real trouble once out of mana. Icy Veins lists the same strengths and adds no answer to casters and no slow.
**You have no area damage until [[Consecration]] at 20.** Blizzard trains it to every Paladin now instead of selling it as a Holy talent, and it only damages four enemies properly. Pull one target at a time.
**Blizzard states the class has no interrupt and no way to slow an enemy's movement**, and that Holy talents widen its range without matching other mana users. Your own speed comes from one talent, `Pursuit of Justice`.
**Treat mana as an extension of your health bar.** Topping up with [[Holy Light]] and killing the next thing at low mana usually beats sitting down to drink.

**Icy Veins' levelling order differs in the first 22 levels and then matches.** It opens `Deflection` 5, `Improved Judgement` 2, `Conviction` 3, takes `Pursuit of Justice` at 20 and 21 and [[Seal of Command]] at 22, and fills `Sanctified Judgement` afterwards. From `Sacred Arbiter` at 25 to `Unsealed Wrath` at 40 the two orders agree. Icy Veins says either row-1 talent works: `Deflection` to take less damage alone, `Benediction` for mana.
**The row-1 choice is mana against safety.** `Benediction` takes up to 10% off every instant-cast spell and ability, which is almost everything you press but not [[Holy Light]]. `Deflection` is 1% parry a rank.
**Whether to keep [[Seal of Command]] depends on your gear.** Icy Veins takes it because it suits early gear, when you replace your weapon every few levels. It adds that a strong fast weapon or a lot of Spell Power makes [[Seal of Righteousness]] the better Seal, and that once you are filling `Champion of the Light` you could move the point elsewhere.

**The two builds cannot be combined.** `Unsealed Wrath` needs 30 points in Retribution and [[Holy Shock]] needs 20 in Holy, and 51 points do not cover both.
## Rotation
1. **Always have a seal up**: [[Seal of Righteousness]], or [[Seal of Command]] once talented
2. On anything that will survive several Judgements, **judge [[Seal of the Crusader]] first** for the Holy damage amplifier, then switch back. Blizzard states [[Judgement]] no longer consumes your active Seal, so this costs you mana, two Seal casts and that Judgement's damage. The debuff lasts 40 seconds and your melee strikes refresh it. Blizzard also moved the Judgement half of `Improved Seal of the Crusader` into the class, so the amplifier needs no talent points
3. **[[Judgement]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
4. **[[Hammer of Wrath]]** from level 44, on anything at 20% health or less
5. **[[Holy Strike]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
6. With `Unsealed Wrath`, **swap Seals when your stored stacks are ready**. See below
7. **[[Exorcism]]** against Undead and Demons only, and [[Consecration]] on packs or with mana to spare
8. Maintain a Blessing and an Aura; top up with [[Holy Light]]
**Why that order, and when it changes:**
- **[[Judgement]] takes no global cooldown,** so press it and [[Holy Strike]] together and do not choose between them. WoWSims' level 60 list also leads with [[Judgement]] *(WoWSims' model)*. When mana runs short, [[Holy Strike]] is the last to go: it costs 12 at level 20 and 20 at 60.
- **[[Judgement]] is a ranged attack since November 11.** Blizzard changed it so it uses your ranged hit chance and no longer counts as a melee attack for item and effect procs. The published stat pages predate that and still list it as a melee ability.
- **The sources disagree on where [[Consecration]] sits.** Wowhead calls it optional on one target and says to drop it first when mana is short. Icy Veins' level-60 list casts it on cooldown, above [[Judgement]]. WoWSims uses it only as a filler when [[Judgement]] and [[Holy Strike]] are both down, and stops below 10% mana *(WoWSims' model)*. While levelling, follow Wowhead: it costs far more than the two instants together.
- **Is the Crusader opener worth it?** Not on a normal quest mob. At level 20 rank 2 adds about 15 to a [[Judgement]] or a [[Holy Strike]] and about 4 to a [[Seal of Righteousness]] swing with a two-hander, because it counts at each ability's spell power coefficient *(WoWSims' model)*. That takes about 11 more seconds of fighting just to repay the [[Judgement]] it spent, before the mana.
- **[[Seal of Command]] against [[Seal of Righteousness]] depends on your white hit.** With a two-hander, Command pulls ahead once a white hit passes about 59 at level 20 and about 98 at 30 *(WoWSims' model, using its measured Righteousness formula and the seven procs a minute it carries from Classic)*. Near those figures the choice is close. Icy Veins gives the reason: [[Seal of Command]] is built to fire about 7 times a minute whatever your weapon speed, where [[Seal of Righteousness]] adds 12% of your Spell Power to every two-handed swing.
- **Recast a Seal only when it would run out before your next swing.** It pays only on swings, and it lasts 30 seconds *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Save [[Judgement]] for the stun when you can.** Judgement of Command hits twice as hard into a stunned or incapacitated target. Rank 1 does about 97 that way at level 20 and grows as you level to 138 to 146, the figure the tooltip shows. [[Hammer of Justice]] costs 30 Mana on a 1-minute cooldown and stuns for only 3 seconds at rank 1. `Repentance` opens the same window on a Humanoid, and Icy Veins notes a macro casting `Repentance` then [[Judgement]] lands the bonus without breaking the effect.
- **[[Hammer of Wrath]] has a 1-second cast, so it resets your swing.** Cast it straight after a swing. Five points in `Champion of the Light` make it instant, which is why WoWSims' list fires it freely only when it is *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Fillers are not equal.** On one target at level 20, [[Exorcism]] deals about 80 for 85 Mana and [[Consecration]] 48 for 135, both before Spell Power.
- **[[Retribution Aura]] runs off your own Spell Power.** It damages any creature that strikes a party member within 30 yards, and Blizzard's notes put its share of the Paladin's Spell Power at 6%, with Vanilla's base damage.
**`Unsealed Wrath`, from level 40.** Each melee attack stores a copy of your active Seal for 15 seconds, up to 3. Landing an attack under a different Seal spends every copy, giving the stored Seal its normal chance to fire once per copy. The talent needs a weapon equipped.
- **Wowhead's version:** stay in [[Seal of Command]], and at 2 to 3 stacks switch to [[Seal of Righteousness]] for one swing, then switch back.
- **Icy Veins' version depends on weapon speed.** With a weapon slower than 3.0, switch to [[Seal of Righteousness]] at 3 stored copies of Command and back to [[Seal of Command]] with 1 or more copies of Righteousness. With a weapon of 2.9 or faster, do the mirror: sit in Righteousness and switch to Command at 3.
- **Windfury Totem no longer feeds it.** Blizzard's November 11 hotfix, which calls the talent Unsealed Fury, stopped it generating copies from Windfury Totem attacks.
- Every swap is a Seal cast, so `Sanctified Judgement` and `Benediction` are what pay for it. Its text carries no Seal discount of its own.
**Multi-target: none until 20.** Then [[Consecration]], and it is expensive: **135 Mana** at rank 1, on an 8-second cooldown. Blizzard limits what it can do on purpose: moderate damage to the first 4 enemies that walk into it, very low damage to everything after that, so Paladin threat stays level with a Warrior's Thunderclap. The thin damage on the fifth target is the design, and Blizzard says the limit keeps Forever out of expansion-era AoE pulling. The client agrees. Its rank 1 does 16 Holy damage over 8 seconds to anything in the area, with an extra 32 over 8 seconds for the first four.
From level 50, [[Holy Wrath]] is a second area spell against Undead and Demons only. It has a 2-second cast, and Wowhead says the spec generally avoids hard casts because they reset the swing timer.
From level 25, `Sacred Arbiter` gives [[Holy Strike]] **+20% damage** and makes it refresh every Judgement effect on the target, **even those from other Paladins**, so staying in melee maintains them without re-judging. Blizzard puts it at the new 16-point gold medal; the client's thinner text stops at the Judgements on the target without naming whose they are.
**There is no damage cooldown.** Wowhead notes Forever has nothing like Avenging Wrath, so there is no burst window to plan around.
## Stat priority
**Your weapon first.** Both sites put the weapon above every stat. After that:
- **Wowhead**, October 31: Weapon Skill, weapon DPS and speed, Hit to 9%, Expertise to 6.5%, Spell Damage, Intellect, Strength and Attack Power, Agility and critical strike, Stamina.
- **Icy Veins**, November 4, `Unsealed Wrath` build: weapon damage, Hit to 8%, Expertise to 6.5%, Strength, Spell Power.
- **Icy Veins**, [[Holy Shock]] build: weapon DPS, Hit to 8%, Spell Power, critical strike, Expertise to 6.5%.
**They disagree twice.** Wowhead gives 9% Hit as the safe target against a raid boss until someone confirms it at 60, and Icy Veins gives 8%. Wowhead ranks Spell Damage and Intellect above Strength for every build, and Icy Veins ranks Strength above Spell Power for the `Unsealed Wrath` build. Both lists were written from beta data, and both say level-60 gear may change them.
**Hit is one stat in Forever.** Melee, ranged and spell Hit are combined, so the November 11 change that moved [[Judgement]] to ranged hit chance does not send you looking for a different stat. Expertise is new and removes dodges, and both sites put its ceiling for a boss at 6.5%.
**Slow or fast weapon.** [[Seal of Command]] wants a slow two-hander, as close to 4.0 as you can find by Icy Veins' account. The [[Holy Shock]] build runs [[Seal of Righteousness]] and wants a fast two-hander.
**While levelling,** Hit and Expertise are rare on gear, so weapon damage decides most upgrades. Icy Veins says Spell Power is worth wearing leather or cloth for, because nearly everything except your white swing scales with it.
**Intellect matters much more from 26 points in the tree, at level 35**, where `Champion of the Light` starts converting it into spell damage. Blizzard places the talent in row 6 of this tree.
**It converts up to 60% of your Intellect at 5 points**, 12% a rank, by Blizzard's November 2 notes. Icy Veins' stat page agrees. ClassicWoW.gg still prints 20, 40 and 60% over 3 ranks, Wowhead's stat page prints 60% "at 3/3", and Icy Veins' class overview says it raises healing too. Blizzard confines the effect to damage.

## What Forever changed for Retribution
- **Five new talents:** `Sanctified Judgement` in row 2, `Sacred Arbiter` at the new 16-point gold medal, `Turn Evil` in row 5, `Champion of the Light` in row 6, and `Unsealed Wrath` at 31 points. `Holy Conduit` sits in the Holy tree.
- **Blizzard's removal list for this tree is two talents**, `Improved Retribution Aura` and `Sanctity Aura`, and more have left since. `Improved Seal of the Crusader` is gone with its Judgement bonus baseline. `Improved Holy Strike` is gone with its shorter cooldown baseline. `Improved Blessing of Might`, `Crusade` and `Instrument of Law` are gone too.
- `Unyielding Faith` joins row 3 from the Holy tree. It shortens Fear and Disorient effects instead of improving your chance to resist them, up to 30% off their duration at 2 points.
- `Turn Evil` makes `Turn Undead` cast up to 1 second faster with up to 10 seconds less cooldown, and lets it affect Demons.
- **Seal Twisting is no longer baseline.** `Unsealed Wrath` at 31 points, behind `Repentance`, holds the capstone slot. See Rotation for how it plays. It is the nearest the class comes to twisting *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **[[Judgement]] no longer consumes your Seal**, a baseline change Blizzard states for every Paladin, and since November 11 it is a ranged attack. [[Lasting Judgement]] is baseline too, and the client has it adding 10 seconds per rank to `Judgement of Light` and `Judgement of Wisdom`.
- `Vindication` is redesigned. Your damaging melee attacks have a chance to cut the target's Attack Power for 30 seconds. Blizzard tunes the reduction by level to sit close to Demoralizing Shout. At 3 points and level 60, Icy Veins' calculator shows 204. Icy Veins says to take it when your raid has no Warrior or bear to shout.
- `Sanctified Judgement` gives [[Judgement]] up to a 100% chance to refund up to 60% of the judged Seal's mana cost, and the client reads the full 100% and 60%. It sits in row 2.
- `Vengeance` sits in row 5 and requires [[Seal of Command]]. It stacks up to 5 times at 3% each at 3 points, so up to 15% more Physical and Holy damage, holds for 30 seconds, and triggers off non-periodic critical strikes only.
- `Eye for an Eye` sits in row 4 and answers every critical strike, not only spells. **Blizzard puts the reflected share at up to 10% of the damage taken**, against Classic's 30%. The client reads the same 10% at 2 points, and caps the reflect at half the Paladin's health.
- `Pursuit of Justice` is worth up to 15% movement speed for the same points. The client confirms it covers mounted speed and that it does not stack with other speed effects.
- `Repentance` is the 21-point gold medal, so it is reachable at 30 where Classic had it at 40. The client has it incapacitating a Humanoid for up to 6 seconds, broken by any damage, for 60 Mana on a 1-minute cooldown.
- `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` gives up to **6% Holy and Physical damage** and cuts your threat by up to 20% while a two-handed melee weapon is equipped, 3 points in row 4 at 2% damage and about 7% threat per rank.
- [[Consecration]] is baseline at 20 for every Paladin, and Blizzard redesigned it around four targets. See Rotation.
## At level 60
**Two builds are published and nobody has measured them against each other.** Raids open December 9, 2026.
- **`Unsealed Wrath`:** Wowhead's 14/6/31, which is the levelling order above carried to 60, and Icy Veins' 12/8/31, which fills `Precision` for Hit and drops `Reverence`. WoWSims' default talents are Wowhead's build exactly. Wowhead calls this the standard build.
- **[[Holy Shock]]:** Icy Veins' 21/0/30 and Wowhead's 23/0/28. A fast two-hander, [[Seal of Righteousness]] alone, [[Holy Shock]] and [[Consecration]] on cooldown. Wowhead says it off-heals better, does slightly more area damage with `Consecrated Ground`, and is stronger against Undead and Demons through `Purifying Power`.
**Wowhead uses the same build in dungeons and raids** and says no change is worth making for dungeons.
**Spell Power and Intellect scale both builds** through `Champion of the Light`, by Wowhead's account more than Strength does. Icy Veins is less sure for the capstone build, as the stat lists above show.
Blizzard frames the choice as deliberate: it positioned [[Holy Shock]] as the alternative so that opting out of the capstone is a real build. Its closing line is that Retribution stays a strong damage dealer with steadier damage and more group utility, naming `Sacred Arbiter` and its upkeep of other Paladins' Judgements as the example.
