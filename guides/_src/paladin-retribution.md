Two-handed melee with Holy magic layered on top. **[[Holy Strike]] at level 6** turns it from auto-attack-and-wait into something you actually play, twenty levels earlier than Classic gave a Paladin a button.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, raised from 20 that day, so no level-60 Retribution build has been tested.
A widely-published level-30 Retribution build spends two points on `Crusade`, and nobody can tell you whether the tree still offers it. No Blizzard note mentions it leaving in the September 24 build (1.60.1.70009), the beta's first balance pass, and build 1.60.1.70245 has not deleted the talent. **But the client cannot tell you which talents a Forever tree can reach.** It also omits `Champion of the Light` and `Sacred Arbiter`, which are both unquestionably live. Check in game whether you can spend a point in `Crusade`, and treat those two points as the least reliable part of that build.
Wowhead reports that beta testers and class discords currently consider Retribution the single most overtuned spec in the level-20 bracket, and expects changes. The level-30 cap opened on October 1, and that same build cut `Champion of the Light`. Treat the talent and gearing advice below as a snapshot rather than a settled answer.

## What Retribution is for
**Layering Holy damage onto a two-handed swing, with a real button to press from level 6.** [[Seal of Command]] at 20 procs Holy damage scaled off the swing, and a stun amplifies its Judgement.
Wowhead's overtuning warning is the only published comment aimed squarely at this spec. It is not a levelling score: no source publishes one for any Paladin spec, and a score would only measure solo kill speed.
**You have no area damage until [[Consecration]] at 20.** Pull one target at a time. Paladin has no movement tools either; the published guides call it *"slow, very slow."*
**Treat mana as an extension of your health bar.** Topping up with [[Holy Light]] and killing the next thing at low mana usually beats sitting down to drink.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.**
1. `Deflection`, 5 points. **+5% parry**, and Forever moved it to tier 1, so it is available from level 10
2. `Improved Judgement`, 2 points
3. `Conviction`, **3 points**, and that is forced: [[Seal of Command]] is a tier-3 talent needing 10 points in the tree, and 5+2+1 would not reach it
4. **[[Seal of Command]]** at 20
**The published respec at 20**, for mana rather than damage: `Benediction` 5, `Holy Conduit` 2, `Improved Judgement` 2, `Conviction` 1, [[Seal of Command]] 1. It makes [[Consecration]] affordable and cheapens [[Exorcism]], which matters in Ruins of Lordaeron and Shadowfang Keep.
A second published build drops `Improved Judgement` for two more `Conviction`, on the grounds that *"mana is simply not an issue at level 20 with the new changes."* Both are defensible.
**Wowhead goes further and questions the whole premise.** Its testers find [[Seal of Command]] *not meaningfully better than* [[Seal of Righteousness]] at 20, once you account for talents and spell power. Its own build spends that final point on `Divine Intellect`, `Voice of Truth` or mana regen instead, then aims at [[Holy Shock]] and `Champion of the Light` later.
Wowhead calls that Holy-leaning "Shockadin" build the stronger PvE choice right now. It rates this page's "Seal Twist" build weaker in PvE at 20, but ahead in PvP burst with a slow two-hander.
## Rotation
1. **Always have a seal up**: [[Seal of Righteousness]], or [[Seal of Command]] once talented
2. On anything that will survive several Judgements, **judge [[Seal of the Crusader]] first** for the Holy damage amplifier, then switch back. This is cheap now: Judgement no longer consumes the seal, and the debuff lasts 40 seconds
3. **[[Holy Strike]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
4. **[[Judgement]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
5. With [[Seal of Command]], **[[Hammer of Justice]] first**: Judgement of Command hits far harder into a stunned target
6. **[[Exorcism]]** against Undead and Demons only
7. Maintain a Blessing and an Aura; top up with [[Holy Light]]
**Multi-target: none until 20.** Then [[Consecration]], and it is expensive. Blizzard publishes no target cap for it. The patchy damage beyond the first few targets was a bug, and the September 24 build fixed Consecration's failure to apply its increased damage effect to some targets, along with its inconsistent Z axis.
Later, `Sacred Arbiter` gives [[Holy Strike]] **+20% damage** and makes it refresh all Judgement debuffs, so staying in melee maintains them without re-judging.
## Stat priority
Weapon DPS, then Hit and Expertise, critical strike, **Spell Power**, Strength, Intellect, Agility, Stamina.
Hit and Expertise barely appear on gear at this level, so they are close to irrelevant below 30. A slower weapon makes [[Holy Strike]] hit harder, but raw weapon DPS usually wins.
**Intellect matters much more from 26 points in the tree, at level 35.** There `Champion of the Light` begins converting it into spell damage: 20% per point spent, up to 60% at max rank. The October 1 build cut that from 33% per point, or 100% at max. Wowhead, Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg may still print the old figure, and so does the client: build 1.60.1.70245 still reads 100% of Intellect. Blizzard's note is the one to believe.
Blizzard also removed a tooltip line that claimed the talent increased Healing too. It never did, so treat this as spell damage only.
Published gear lists split into a **Spell Power** build and an Attack Power build, and call both competitive at level 20.
## What defines Retribution
| Talent or ability | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Holy Strike]]** | 6, baseline | **25% of weapon damage at the rank you train at 6, rising per rank to 50% at rank 8.** The full curve is 25/29/32/36/39/43/46/50%, plus a flat Holy component. 10-second cooldown. The client tags the whole spell Holy, but its text splits the damage into a weapon component and an added Holy one, so we read armour as reducing the weapon half *(ours, not a source's)* |
| **[[Seal of Command]]** | **exactly 20** | Procs Holy damage scaled off the swing, so it wants a slow two-hander. Its Judgement is amplified against stunned targets |
| **`Benediction` + `Holy Conduit`** | 10 and 15 | The mana economy, and the reason the level-20 respec exists |
## What Forever changed for Retribution
- **Six new talents:** `Holy Conduit`, `Sanctified Judgement`, `Sacred Arbiter`, `Champion of the Light`, `Instrument of Law`, and the `Twist of Light` capstone.
- **Four talents deleted:** `Improved Blessing of Might`, `Improved Retribution Aura`, `Improved Seal of the Crusader`, `Sanctity Aura`.
- **`Crusade`'s place in the tree is unsettled.** Guide sites still spend points in it and no Blizzard note mentions removing it, so check in game before you plan a build around it.
- **[[Holy Strike]]'s rank curve was flattened** to 25/29/32/36/39/43/46/50% in the September 24 build because *"early ranks of Holy Strike didn't always feel like an upgrade"*. The client prints exactly that curve across all eight ranks. `Improved Holy Strike` was folded into the class in the same pass, so there is no longer a talent to spend points on.
- `Vindication` now also grants you attack power. `Vengeance` moved a tier and now stacks, up to 3 times rather than Classic's single 8-second buff. The September 24 build made it activate off non-periodic critical strikes only, closing a Known Issue where periodic crits fed it. `Eye for an Eye` reflects 15% of a critical strike's damage in the client, against Classic's 30% at max rank. `Pursuit of Justice` now covers mounted speed. `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` is **2% per rank, 6% at max**, cut from 3/6/9% in the September 24 build. Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg still carry the old 3% in their talent data.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether the `Twist of Light` capstone or a Holy hybrid wins is an untested either-or at 51 points. The author of one published guide now says *"it's looking like twisting might not be the way to go for Paladin raid DPS specs atm."*
