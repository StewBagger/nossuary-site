Two-handed melee with Holy magic layered on top. **This is the Paladin levelling spec**, and Forever improved it more than any other — [[Holy Strike]] at level 6 turns it from auto-attack-and-wait into something you actually play.
:::scope
Levelling guide, 1 to 60. The beta is capped at **level 30** as of the October 1 build, which Blizzard raised from 20 that day and is the cap for the rest of the beta, so no level-60 Retribution build has been tested. **A specific warning for this spec:** a widely-published level-30 Retribution build spends two points on `Crusade`, **a talent that does not exist in the beta client**, it vanished from the Retribution tree in the September 24 build (1.60.1.70009), the beta's first balance pass, with no mention in Blizzard's notes. That build cannot be entered as printed, and no source publishes a legal replacement. **Separately, Wowhead reports that beta testers and class discords currently consider Retribution the single most overtuned spec in the level-20 bracket**, with changes expected before the level-30 cap opens, so treat the talent and gearing advice below as a snapshot, not a settled answer.

## Is Retribution worth levelling?
**Yes, and it is the clear answer for Paladin.** [[Holy Strike]] from level 6 gives you a real button twenty levels before Classic gave you one. Rated good at everything except area damage.
Two honest caveats. **You have no area damage whatsoever until [[Consecration]] at 20**, so do not pull more than you can kill one at a time. And Paladin has no movement tools at all, it is described, fairly, as *"slow, very slow."*
One tip that generalises: **treat mana as an extension of your health bar.** Topping yourself up with [[Holy Light]] and killing the next thing at low mana usually beats sitting down to drink.
## Your talent points
**Level 20: 0/0/11.**
1. `Deflection`, 5 points — **+5% parry**, and Forever moved it to tier 1 so it is available from level 10
2. `Improved Judgement`, 2 points
3. `Conviction`, **3 points**, this is forced, not a preference: [[Seal of Command]] is a tier-3 talent needing 10 points in the tree, and 5+2+1 would not reach it
4. **[[Seal of Command]]** at 20
**The published respec at 20**, for mana rather than damage: `Benediction` 5, `Holy Conduit` 2, `Improved Judgement` 2, `Conviction` 1, [[Seal of Command]] 1. It makes [[Consecration]] affordable and cheapens [[Exorcism]], which matters in Ruins of Lordaeron and Shadowfang Keep.
A second published build drops `Improved Judgement` for two more `Conviction`, on the grounds that *"mana is simply not an issue at level 20 with the new changes."* Both are defensible.
**Wowhead goes further and questions the whole premise.** Its testers find [[Seal of Command]] *not meaningfully better than* [[Seal of Righteousness]] at 20 once you account for talents and spell power, and currently rate a Holy-leaning "Shockadin" build, the final point going to `Divine Intellect`, `Voice of Truth` or mana regen instead, then aiming at [[Holy Shock]] and `Champion of the Light` later, as the **stronger PvE choice right now**, against this page's "Seal Twist" build, which Wowhead rates weaker in PvE at 20 but ahead in PvP burst with a slow two-hander.
## Rotation
1. **Always have a seal up**: [[Seal of Righteousness]], or [[Seal of Command]] once talented
2. On anything that will survive several Judgements, **judge [[Seal of the Crusader]] first** for the Holy damage amplifier, then switch back. This is cheap now: Judgement no longer consumes the seal, and the debuff lasts **40 seconds**
3. **[[Holy Strike]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
4. **[[Judgement]]** on cooldown, as mana allows
5. With [[Seal of Command]], **[[Hammer of Justice]] first** — Judgement of Command hits far harder into a stunned target
6. **[[Exorcism]]** against Undead and Demons only
7. Maintain a Blessing and an Aura; top up with [[Holy Light]]
**Multi-target: none until 20.** Then [[Consecration]], full damage to the **first four targets only**, a trickle beyond, and expensive.
Later, `Sacred Arbiter` gives [[Holy Strike]] **+10% damage** and makes it refresh **all** Judgement debuffs, so staying in melee maintains them without re-judging.
## Stat priority
Weapon DPS, then Hit and Expertise, critical strike, **Spell Power**, Strength, Intellect, Agility, Stamina.
Hit and Expertise barely appear on gear at this level, so they are close to irrelevant below 30. A slower weapon makes [[Holy Strike]] hit harder, but raw weapon DPS usually wins. **Intellect jumps sharply at 26 points in the tree, level 35**, where `Champion of the Light` starts converting it into spell damage, up to **60% of it at the talent's max rank, 20% per point spent** — cut from 33% per point (100% at max) in the October 1 build, so expect Wowhead, Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg to still carry the old number until they update. Blizzard also removed a tooltip line that claimed the talent increased Healing too; it never did, so treat this as spell damage only.
Published gear lists split into a **Spell Power** build and an **Attack Power** build and call both competitive at level 20.
## What defines Retribution
| Talent or ability | Earliest level | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[[Holy Strike]]** | **6, baseline** | **50% weapon damage plus a flat Holy component**, 10-second cooldown. Only the added component is Holy — **armour still reduces the weapon half** |
| **[[Seal of Command]]** | **exactly 20** | Procs Holy damage scaled off the swing, so it wants a slow two-hander. Its Judgement is amplified against stunned targets |
| **`Benediction` + `Holy Conduit`** | 10 and 15 | The mana economy, and the reason the level-20 respec exists |
## What Forever changed for Retribution
- **Six new talents:** `Holy Conduit`, `Sanctified Judgement`, `Sacred Arbiter`, `Champion of the Light`, `Instrument of Law`, and the `Twist of Light` capstone.
- **Four talents deleted:** `Improved Blessing of Might`, `Improved Retribution Aura`, `Improved Seal of the Crusader`, `Sanctity Aura`.
- **`Crusade` is gone from the Retribution tree**, removed in the September 24 build with no note from Blizzard.
- `Vindication` now also grants **you** attack power. `Vengeance` moved a tier and now stacks up to **5 times** rather than Classic's single 8-second buff. `Eye for an Eye` reflect cut from 30% to 10%. `Pursuit of Justice` now covers **mounted** speed. `Two-Handed Weapon Specialization` is **3% per rank, 9% at max**, we found no source for the September nerf some builds cite, and both Icy Veins and ClassicWoW.gg's talent data confirm 3% is still current.
## At level 60
Unknown. Whether the `Twist of Light` capstone or a Holy hybrid wins is an untested either-or at 51 points, and the author of one published guide now says *"it's looking like twisting might not be the way to go for Paladin raid DPS specs atm."*
