## Is Cat worth levelling?
**Yes. It is the strongest published recommendation of the three Druid specs, but only from 20.** `Feral Swiftness` gives *"a permanent 30% speed buff while in Cat Form"*. You also have [[Prowl]], and shapeshifting breaks roots. One guide calls Feral *"one of the fastest and most mobile specializations in the WoW Forever beta."*
**Cat Form now comes from a quest chain, not a trainer.** *"In WoW Forever, druids earn Cat Form with a quest chain at level 20. Your trainer sends you to Moonglade, the Great Cat Spirit asks for three relics hidden in the Stormrage Barrow Dens, and when you bring them back your trainer teaches you Cat Form, [[Prowl]], [[Claw]] and [[Rip]] in one go."* It is 2,885 experience in total, and **the relics are party loot**: *"Five druids in a group loot the relic once instead of five times."*

## Rotation
**At level 20 it is two buttons:**
1. **[[Claw]]** to build combo points
2. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
**From 24 you add [[Rake]].** The published rotation also calls for [[Tiger's Fury]] here *"when below 40 Energy"*, but Blizzard's own class deep dive says [[Tiger's Fury]] is **being removed outright**, replaced by a new Feral talent, `Shifting Power` — see "What Forever changed for Cat" below. Treat the guide sites' [[Tiger's Fury]] step as stale until that lands.
**From 30:**
1. Maintain **[[Faerie Fire]]**, usable in form now
2. **[[Shred]] on [[Omen of Clarity]] procs**. Omen is baseline at 20 in Forever, not a talent
3. Maintain **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
4. Maintain **[[Rake]]**
5. **[[Shred]]** as filler, from behind
**One correction to a widely-copied level-30 rotation: it calls for [[Ferocious Bite]], which trains at level 32.** You will not have it at a level-30 cap.
The target-selection rule: *"pick [[Rip]] when the enemy will live through the ticks, and direct damage when the remaining kill time is too short. Keep an existing bleed running instead of restarting it."*
**Multi-target: there is none in Cat Form.** [[Swipe]] is Bear-only. Either shift to Bear and [[Swipe]], or spread [[Rake]] and [[Rip]] on targets durable enough to be worth it.
## Stat priority
**Agility first**, then Strength, then Stamina.
The reason Agility leads is that *"in cat form it is attack power, critical strike and armor at once."*
**The Forever-specific part reverses a Classic habit: your weapon's damage now counts in Cat Form.** Cat Form's flat attack power was **cut from 40 to 12** plus Agility, and abilities gained weapon-damage terms. Blizzard's own framing of the mechanism: auto-attack DPS in Cat Form now simply equals your weapon's DPS, at a fixed 1.0-second swing speed (2.5 seconds in Bear or Dire Bear Form), and weapon-damage abilities use the same numbers. The result: *"the weapon here is chosen on its damage first, then on its stats."* Published level-20 cat weapon picks are **two-handers on both factions**, held in both hands.
**One source disputes the weapon-DPS priority outright.** Three sources and the client data are against it, but the disagreement exists and no source publishes the actual conversion formula.

## What Forever changed for Cat
- **The old `Furor` powershifting trick is dead, but Blizzard has deliberately reintroduced the gameplay in a different form — this is confirmed by Blizzard, not a player rumour.** `Furor` was rewritten: instead of a flat 40 energy on entering Cat Form, you *"regain 100% of the Energy you had when you were last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy for each second you spent not in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, up to a maximum of 100"* at 5/5 — *"Leaving an empty bar and instantly re-entering supplies no flat refill."* Blizzard's own class deep dive says this was intentional, to stop "bear-weaving," and then adds: *"We realize this removed the 'power shifting' gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the `Shifting Power` talent in the Feral talent tree."*
- **[[Tiger's Fury]] is being removed, not merely reworked.** Blizzard's class deep dive lists it under baseline shapeshifting changes: *"Tiger's Fury: (Removed.) This has been replaced with a new Feral-specific talent called Shifting Power."* `Shifting Power` is the new 16-point gold medal talent described above — it instantly grants 40 Energy for a Mana cost rather than buffing damage, and **Blizzard says it is not yet live in the beta**, so the current client (and the datamined [[Tiger's Fury]] rework some guides describe — no cost, 30-second cooldown, flat +15% physical damage) may still be what you actually see in game until the replacement ships.
- **The spec was rebalanced off [[Shred]] and onto bleeds.** Shred was cut from 225% to **155%** weapon damage; [[Rip]] was buffed enormously. Rank 6 deals 243/396/549/702/855 **over 12 seconds** by combo point, against Classic's 45/73/101/129/157, and it now scales with attack power.
- **[[Claw]] became a weapon-damage multiplier**: *"110% normal damage plus 27"*, where Classic gave a flat *"27 additional damage."* [[Ferocious Bite]] is unchanged from Classic, and trains at 32.
- **Cat Form's flat attack power was cut from 40 to 12** plus Agility. This is the mechanism behind the weapon change.
- **Six new talents:** `Primal Bite`, `Predatory Instincts`, `King of the Jungle`, `Natural Reaction`, `Rend and Tear`, [[Berserk]]. `Heart of the Wild` moved to tier 1. `Feral Instinct` was rewritten from bear threat to **+30% [[Swipe]] damage**.
- **A rename and a merge, both confirmed directly by Blizzard's class deep dive:** `Mangle` is now the `Primal Bite` talent and **is gone from the spellbook** — *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Classic's `Primal Fury` and `Blood Frenzy` were merged into one node, and Blizzard's own text says the merged node **is `Blood Frenzy`**: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* It gained a cat clause giving combo-point generators a chance at an extra point on a crit. **`Primal Fury` is not selectable**, so lists naming it cannot be entered as printed.
- **All damage-over-time effects can now crit.**
## At level 60
Unknown. The open question is whether bleed-centric damage with `Rend and Tear` beats a Rogue once [[Berserk]], `King of the Jungle` and real weapons exist.
