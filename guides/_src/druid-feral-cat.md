## What Cat is for
**Damage out of stealth, at speed, and on bleeds that keep ticking while you reposition.** `Feral Swiftness` gives *"a permanent 30% speed buff while in Cat Form"* plus +4% dodge in all forms, and it now works indoors. You also have [[Prowl]], shapeshifting breaks roots, and [[Rip]] scales with attack power and runs 12 seconds off five combo points.
The published verdict measures solo kill speed, and **it applies only from 20, when Cat Form arrives**. One guide's *"fastest and most mobile"* line describes a cat that already has its form.
**Cat Form now comes from a quest chain rather than a trainer.** *"In WoW Forever, druids earn Cat Form with a quest chain at level 20. Your trainer sends you to Moonglade, the Great Cat Spirit asks for three relics hidden in the Stormrage Barrow Dens, and when you bring them back your trainer teaches you Cat Form, [[Prowl]], [[Claw]] and [[Rip]] in one go."* It is 2,885 experience in total, and the relics are party loot: *"Five druids in a group loot the relic once instead of five times."*

## Rotation
**At level 20 it is three buttons:**
1. **[[Claw]]** to build combo points, or **[[Shred]]** from behind on a free [[Omen of Clarity]] proc
2. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
**From 24 you add [[Rake]] and [[Ferocious Bite]].** The published rotation also calls for [[Tiger's Fury]] here *"when below 40 Energy"*; drop that step, because [[Tiger's Fury]] has been removed. Its replacement `Shifting Power` (below) is a tier 7 talent, so it waits for level 40.
**From 30:**
1. Maintain **[[Faerie Fire]]**, usable in form now, and it does not reset your swing timer
2. **[[Shred]] on [[Omen of Clarity]] procs**. Omen is baseline at 20 in Forever, not a talent
3. Maintain **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
4. Maintain **[[Rake]]**
5. **[[Ferocious Bite]]** at 5 combo points when the target will not live through [[Rip]]
6. **[[Shred]]** as filler from behind, or **[[Claw]]** when you cannot get there; see Claw or Shred below
The target-selection rule: *"pick [[Rip]] when the enemy will live through the ticks, and direct damage when the remaining kill time is too short. Keep an existing bleed running instead of restarting it."*
**Multi-target: there is none in Cat Form.** [[Swipe]] is Bear-only. Either shift to Bear and [[Swipe]], or spread [[Rake]] and [[Rip]] on targets durable enough to be worth it.
**What changes the answer**, on tooltip numbers before attack power and armour:
- **[[Rip]] or [[Ferocious Bite]].** At 30, five-point [[Rip]] ticks for 40 every 2 seconds and five-point [[Ferocious Bite]] deals 134 at once, so [[Rip]] needs 4 of its 6 ticks, 8 seconds, to pass it. At 24 to 27 and 32 to 35 it needs all 6. At 40 to 43, 48 to 51 and 56 to 59 a new [[Ferocious Bite]] rank deals more than a whole [[Rip]] until the next [[Rip]] rank catches up. Without `Predatory Discipline`, WoWSims' model at 60 turns to [[Ferocious Bite]] only with under 8 seconds left *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Energy going into [[Ferocious Bite]].** Bite it close to its 35 Energy cost. Every point above that becomes 0.6 damage at rank 1 and 2.5 at the rank trained at 56, and [[Rake]] pays more per Energy at every level: 1.9 with `Ferocity` 3 at rank 1. `Predatory Discipline` keeps that Energy, and it is the one case for biting high.
- **[[Rake]] or [[Claw]] as a builder.** Either gives one combo point. At 30 with `Ferocity` 3, [[Rake]] deals 71 for 37 Energy, and [[Claw]] matches that rate at an auto-attack of 34, so above that size [[Claw]] leads per Energy. Most of [[Rake]] is its ticks: 48 of the 71 over 9 seconds, so on a target about to die, use [[Claw]]. WoWSims' model drops [[Rake]] with 6 seconds or less to go *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **When to refresh, and at how many points.** Let a bleed end before you cast it again. A [[Rip]] restarted early pays the five combo points twice, which at 30 is 210 Energy of [[Claw]]. WoWSims' model recasts both only after they fall off *(WoWSims' model)*. The fifth point adds about 22% to [[Rip]] at every rank, which is why 5 is the default, and at 60 WoWSims' model will go at 4 rather than wait on Energy it does not have *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **What to spend [[Omen of Clarity]] on.** The dearest thing you can land. With the level 30 build a free [[Shred]] saves 54 Energy, [[Claw]] 42, [[Rake]] 37 and [[Rip]] 30, so take it before refreshing a bleed, and on [[Claw]] if you cannot get behind.
- **Fitting in [[Faerie Fire]].** It costs no Energy in form, so press it while you wait for Energy rather than in place of a builder *(ours, not a source's)*.
## Claw or Shred
**[[Shred]] hits harder and [[Claw]] costs less, and once you own `Shredding Attacks` the two are close on damage per Energy** *(ours, worked out from the game's numbers and not tested in play)*. A Cat usually runs short of Energy before it runs short of global cooldowns, so damage per Energy is the fair comparison. Both percentages apply to the flat bonus as well: rank 5 [[Shred]] is 155% of your auto-attack plus 80, not 155% of the auto-attack with 80 on top.
**Before `Shredding Attacks`, use [[Claw]].** Neither level 20 build has it, so [[Claw]] costs 40 Energy against [[Shred]]'s 60 and wins at any normal auto-attack size.
**After it, [[Shred]] from behind is a fine filler, and [[Claw]] costs you little when you cannot get there.** With `Ferocity` 3 and `Shredding Attacks` 3, [[Claw]] costs 42 and [[Shred]] costs 54. Below the auto-attack size in each row [[Claw]] does more per Energy, and above it [[Shred]] does:
| Level | [[Claw]] rank bonus | [[Shred]] rank bonus | [[Shred]] wins above |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20–27 | 27 | 22 | 30 |
| 28–29 | 39 | 22 | 155 |
| 30–37 | 39 | 32 | 41 |
| 38–45 | 57 | 44 | 91 |
| 46–47 | 57 | 64 | any size |
| 48–53 | 88 | 64 | 186 |
| 54–57 | 88 | 80 | any size |
| 58–60 | 115 | 80 | 285 |
**At 59 with a 150 auto-attack,** [[Claw]] deals 292 for 42 Energy and [[Shred]] deals 356 for 54: 6.9 against 6.6 per Energy, a gap of about 5%. The gap stays around that size at most levels, so moving behind the target is rarely worth losing time for, except at 46 or 47, where [[Shred]] leads by more. With `Ferocity` 5 the balance tips back to [[Claw]].
**WoWSims' level 60 rotation uses [[Shred]] whenever it can land and [[Claw]] only when it cannot.** That is a raid model against a boss, and it is why [[Shred]] stays the default here.
**Always [[Shred]] from behind when [[Omen of Clarity]] makes your next ability free**, because the cost no longer matters. Spend Energy on either rather than sit at full Energy, which wastes it.
## Stat priority
**Agility first**, then Strength, then Stamina.
The reason Agility leads is that *"in cat form it is attack power, critical strike and armor at once."*
**Your weapon's damage now counts in Cat Form, which reverses a Classic habit.** Cat Form's flat attack power is 12 plus Agility, against Classic's 40, and abilities gained weapon-damage terms. Blizzard's framing: auto-attack DPS in Cat Form equals your weapon's DPS, at a fixed 1.0-second swing speed, 2.5 seconds in Bear or Dire Bear Form. Weapon-damage abilities use the same numbers. Blizzard publishes the two speeds but not which form takes which, so that pairing is ours, not a source's. The result: *"the weapon here is chosen on its damage first, then on its stats."* Published level-20 cat weapon picks are two-handers on both factions, held in both hands.
**One source disputes the weapon-DPS priority outright.** Three sources and the client data are against it, but the disagreement exists and no source publishes the actual conversion formula.

## What Forever changed for Cat
- The old `Furor` powershifting trick is dead, and **Blizzard reintroduced the gameplay in a different form on purpose**. `Furor`, now in Feral tier 1, was rewritten: instead of a flat 40 energy on entering Cat Form, five points let you regenerate Energy out of form *"as if they were in Cat Form"*. Energy you hold is kept through Bear and Dire Bear Form, but it does not regenerate in them. The numbers are client text rather than published. At 5/5, shifting into Cat Form restores 100% of the Energy you had when last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy per second spent outside Bear Form or Dire Bear Form, up to 100 Energy, and *"Leaving an empty bar and instantly re-entering supplies no flat refill."* Blizzard then adds: *"We realize this removed the 'power shifting' gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the `Shifting Power` talent in the Feral talent tree."* See `Shifting Power` above for its numbers.
- **[[Tiger's Fury]] is gone.** Blizzard's class deep dive flagged it under baseline shapeshifting changes: *"Tiger's Fury: (Removed.) This has been replaced with a new Feral-specific talent called Shifting Power."* `Shifting Power` (above) is what replaced it.
- **[[Shred]] reads lower than Classic in the launch client, and [[Rip]] stayed close to it.** Blizzard's deep dive says Shred’s *"damage increased significantly to need less talent support to be effective"* and publishes no figure, but rank 5 reads 155% plus 80 where Classic read 225% plus 180. We do not know why the two disagree. [[Rip]] rank 6 deals 243/396/549/702/855 over 12 seconds by combo point, against Classic's 942 at 5 points, while rank 1 sits slightly above Classic's. It scales with attack power, as Classic's tooltip also says.
- **[[Claw]] became a weapon-damage multiplier**: *"110% normal damage plus 27"*, where Classic gave a flat *"27 additional damage."* [[Ferocious Bite]] has a new rank 1 at 24, 33 to 43 damage with 1 combo point and 129 to 139 with 5. Classic's ranks follow as ranks 2 to 6, and their numbers no longer match Classic's.
- **New talents:** `Primal Bite`, `Lacerate`, `Predatory Discipline`, `Natural Instinct`, `Natural Reaction`, `Rend and Tear`, `Shifting Power`. `King of the Jungle`, which older guides list, is gone from the tree, so no build can spend a point on it. `Heart of the Wild` moved to Restoration tier 1, and `Furor` took its slot. `Feral Instinct` was rewritten from bear threat to +30% [[Swipe]] damage.
- **A rename and a merge, both confirmed directly by Blizzard's class deep dive.** `Mangle` is now the `Primal Bite` talent, and it is gone from the spellbook: *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Classic's `Primal Fury` and `Blood Frenzy` were merged into one node. Blizzard's own text says the merged node is `Blood Frenzy`: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* It gained a cat clause giving combo-point generators a chance at an extra point on a crit. `Primal Fury` is not selectable, so lists naming it cannot be entered as printed.
- **Periodic effects were fixed to allow critical hits one at a time**, not as a blanket rule. On the Druid side Blizzard names [[Rejuvenation]], [[Tranquility]] and [[Wild Growth]]; elsewhere Renew, Devouring Plague, Riptide, `Lacerating Strikes`, Hellfire and `Contingency Plan`. One case runs the other way: Gnome’s `Eureka!` *"no longer benefits periodic effects at all."*
