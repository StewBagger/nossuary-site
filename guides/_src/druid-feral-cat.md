## What Cat is for
**Damage out of stealth, at speed, and on bleeds that keep ticking while you reposition.** `Feral Swiftness` 2/2 gives +30% movement speed in Cat Form and +4% dodge in all forms, and it works indoors. You also have [[Prowl]], shapeshifting breaks roots, and [[Rip]] scales with attack power and runs 12 seconds off five combo points.
**It applies only from 20, when Cat Form arrives.** Wowhead: *"Leveling as a Feral Druid can start off feeling very slow, as Cat Form isn't available until Level 20."* Icy Veins lists the same weakness, and one more: *"No AoE damage in Cat Form."*
**Cat Form comes from a quest chain, not a trainer.** *"In WoW Forever, druids earn Cat Form with a quest chain at level 20. Your trainer sends you to Moonglade, the Great Cat Spirit asks for three relics hidden in the Stormrage Barrow Dens, and when you bring them back your trainer teaches you Cat Form, [[Prowl]], [[Claw]] and [[Rip]] in one go."* It is 2,885 experience in total, and a relic looted in a group counts for the whole group.

## Rotation
**At level 20 it is three buttons:**
1. **[[Shred]]** from [[Prowl]] to open, and on a free [[Omen of Clarity]] proc
2. **[[Claw]]** to build combo points
3. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points, if the target will live through it
**From 24 you add [[Rake]] and [[Ferocious Bite]]. From 32 you open with [[Ravage]].**
1. Open from [[Prowl]] with **[[Ravage]]**. `Poised`, trained at 32, takes 10 Energy off your first ability when you start out of combat at full Energy
2. Keep **[[Faerie Fire]]** on the target. It costs nothing in form and does not reset your swing timer
3. **[[Shred]] on [[Omen of Clarity]] procs**
4. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points when it is not running and the target will live. Icy Veins accepts 4 points while levelling
5. **[[Rake]]** when it is not running and the target will live about 9 seconds
6. **[[Ferocious Bite]]** on a target that will die before a [[Rip]] pays out
7. **[[Claw]]** as the builder. [[Shred]] takes over only with `Shredding Attacks`; see below
**From 41, with `Shifting Power` and `Predatory Discipline`**, in Icy Veins' order:
1. **[[Ferocious Bite]]** at 5 combo points, when [[Rip]] has 4 seconds or more left and you hold 70 Energy, or 35 with an [[Omen of Clarity]] proc
2. **`Shifting Power`** at about 50 Energy or less
3. **[[Shred]]** on an [[Omen of Clarity]] proc
4. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points unless it is running
5. **[[Rake]]** unless it is running
6. **[[Claw]]**, or **[[Shred]]** from behind with `Shredding Attacks`
**The sources differ on small numbers.** Wowhead casts `Shifting Power` *"if no Energy will be wasted"* and bites only on a target with 3 seconds or less to live. WoWSims' model presses `Shifting Power` at 57 Energy or less and pools to 70 for a five-point bite *(WoWSims' model)*. All three keep [[Rip]] and [[Rake]] up first.
**`Shifting Power` spends Mana.** Each press costs what shifting into Cat Form costs, 55% of base Mana. Icy Veins: *"without considering how to sustain it, you will eventually run out of Mana."* Wowhead's tank levelling page goes further and says it *"does not benefit leveling too much unless you significantly outgear the content."* Solo, hold it for fights that last.
**Multi-target: there is none in Cat Form.** Every Cat ability hits one target, and combo points stay on the target they were built on. Shift to Bear and [[Swipe]], or spread [[Rake]] on targets durable enough to be worth it. Icy Veins: *"it is often more efficient to fight multiple enemies in Bear Form."*
**What changes the answer**, on tooltip numbers before attack power and armour:
- **[[Rip]] or [[Ferocious Bite]].** At 30, five-point [[Rip]] ticks for about 50 every 2 seconds and five-point [[Ferocious Bite]] deals about 167 at once, so [[Rip]] needs 4 of its 6 ticks, 8 seconds, to pass it. At 24 to 27 and 32 to 35 it needs all 6. At 40 to 43, 48 to 51 and 56 to 59 a new [[Ferocious Bite]] rank deals more than a whole [[Rip]] until the next [[Rip]] rank catches up. Without `Predatory Discipline`, WoWSims' model at 60 turns to [[Ferocious Bite]] only with under 8 seconds left in the fight *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **Energy going into [[Ferocious Bite]].** Without `Predatory Discipline`, bite close to its 35 Energy cost. Every point above that becomes 0.7 damage at rank 1 and 3.1 at the rank trained at 56, and [[Rake]] pays more per Energy at every level: about 2.5 at rank 1 with `Ferocity` 5. `Predatory Discipline` keeps that Energy and still adds the damage, which is why the level 41 list bites at 70.
- **[[Rake]] or [[Claw]] as a builder.** Either gives one combo point. At 30 with `Ferocity` 5, [[Rake]] deals 87 for 35 Energy, and [[Claw]] matches that rate at an auto-attack of about 32, so above that size [[Claw]] leads per Energy. Most of [[Rake]] is its ticks: 60 of the 87 over 9 seconds, so on a target about to die, use [[Claw]]. WoWSims' model drops [[Rake]] with 6 seconds or less to go *(WoWSims' model)*.
- **When to refresh, and at how many points.** Let a bleed end before you cast it again. A [[Rip]] restarted early pays the five combo points twice. WoWSims' model recasts both only after they fall off, and will [[Rip]] at 4 points rather than wait on Energy it does not have *(WoWSims' model)*. The fifth point adds about 22% to [[Rip]] at every rank.
- **What to spend [[Omen of Clarity]] on.** The dearest thing you can land. With `Ferocity` 5 a free [[Shred]] saves 60 Energy, or 42 with `Shredding Attacks`. [[Claw]] saves 40, [[Rake]] and [[Ferocious Bite]] 35 and [[Rip]] 30. So take it on [[Shred]] before refreshing a bleed, and on [[Claw]] if you cannot get behind.
- **Fitting in [[Faerie Fire]].** It costs no Energy in form, so press it while you wait for Energy rather than in place of a builder *(ours, not a source's)*.
- **Missed finishers.** A dodged or parried [[Ferocious Bite]] gives back 80% of its base cost and keeps your combo points and spare Energy, fitted from a levelling combat log by WoWSims.
## Claw or Shred
**It turns on one talent. Without `Shredding Attacks`, [[Claw]] is the builder. With it at 3/3, [[Shred]] from behind is.** `Shredding Attacks` takes 6 Energy off [[Shred]] per point, 18 at 3/3, per the client. An earlier version of this page read that as 6 in total and was wrong.
| Talents | [[Claw]] costs | [[Shred]] costs | Builder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None | 45 | 60 | [[Claw]] |
| `Ferocity` 5, no `Shredding Attacks` (Icy Veins' levelling build) | 40 | 60 | [[Claw]] |
| `Ferocity` 5, `Shredding Attacks` 3 (the Cat-only level 60 builds) | 40 | 42 | [[Shred]] from behind |
**Both percentages apply to the flat bonus as well** *(WoWSims' formula)*: rank 5 [[Shred]] is 200% of your auto-attack plus 80, not 200% of the auto-attack with 80 on top, and rank 5 [[Claw]] is 140% of your auto-attack plus 115.
**At 60 with a 150 auto-attack** *(ours, worked from the game's numbers and not tested in play)*: [[Claw]] deals 371 for 40 Energy, 9.3 per Energy. [[Shred]] deals 460. At 42 Energy that is 11.0 per Energy, about 18% ahead. At 60 Energy it is 7.7, about 17% behind. The same holds at every rank: at 60 Energy [[Shred]] never catches [[Claw]] per Energy, and at 42 it leads at any normal auto-attack size.
**Icy Veins says the same from the other side.** Without `Shredding Attacks`, [[Shred]] is *"much more expensive than Claw and only worth using with Omen of Clarity procs."* Its Cat-only rotation, which has the talent, builds with [[Shred]]. WoWSims' level 60 rotation uses [[Shred]] whenever it can land and [[Claw]] only when it cannot; that preset also has the talent.
**Solo you are usually in front of the target**, so [[Claw]] is what you press either way. [[Shred]] from behind is a group button, plus the opener from [[Prowl]] before [[Ravage]] at 32. Spend Energy on either rather than sit at full Energy, which wastes it.
## Stat priority
**Weapon damage first, then hit and expertise to their caps at 60, then Agility and Strength.**
1. **Weapon DPS.** Icy Veins puts it first. WoWSims' weights rate 1 weapon DPS at about 2.0, against 0.52 for a point of Agility and 0.50 for Strength *(WoWSims' model)*
2. **Hit to 8% and expertise to 6.5%** against raid bosses, per Icy Veins. `Nature's Reach` 2/2 supplies 4% of the hit. Both are rare on gear until the higher levels
3. **Agility**: 1 attack power in Cat Form, and 1% crit and 1% dodge per 20
4. **Strength**: 2 attack power, 10% more with `Heart of the Wild`
5. **Critical strike, then attack power**
**Wowhead's list differs** and predates launch (October 29, labelled speculative): crit, hit, expertise, haste, then weapon DPS, with Agility equal to Strength. While levelling, both sites reduce it to the same thing: the highest-DPS weapon you can hold, then Agility and Strength.
**Your weapon's damage counts in Cat Form, which reverses a Classic habit.** Auto-attack DPS in form equals your weapon's DPS, at a fixed 1.0-second swing in Cat Form and 2.5 seconds in Bear Form, so weapon speed does not matter. [[Claw]], [[Shred]] and [[Ravage]] multiply that auto-attack. Sharpening stones, weapon enchants and chance-on-hit effects work in form. Icy Veins: a weapon with *"slightly lower Weapon DPS"* can still win on a strong proc.
**Bleeds scale with attack power, not weapon damage.** WoWSims' log fits: [[Rake]] takes 6.5% of attack power on the hit and on each tick, [[Rip]] 1.14% per tick per combo point, [[Ferocious Bite]] 3.7% per combo point.

## What Forever changed for Cat
- **Launch tuning, November 4:** [[Shred]] is 200% weapon damage (was 155%), [[Claw]] 140% (was 110%), [[Ravage]] 435% (was 350%). [[Rip]], [[Rake]], [[Ferocious Bite]] and [[Pounce]] gained about 25% base damage and attack power scaling. Five-point [[Rip]] rank 6 is 1,071 over 12 seconds before attack power.
- **Fixes since launch.** November 6: [[Ravage]]'s tooltip was corrected, with no change to damage. Its flat term is 42 at rank 1 and 98 at rank 4. Night Elves in [[Shadowmeld]] no longer get [[Ravage]] or [[Pounce]]. November 9: `Natural Instinct` now applies to [[Pounce]], and the Cat [[Feral Charge]] puts you in combat at once. November 11: `Rend and Tear` works on the [[Pounce]] bleed. November 8: damage ranges on [[Ferocious Bite]], [[Rake]] and [[Shred]] narrowed around the same averages; Blizzard posted no note for that one.
- The old `Furor` powershifting trick is gone, and **Blizzard put the gameplay back in a different form on purpose**. `Furor` no longer hands you 40 Energy on entering Cat Form. At 5/5, shifting into Cat Form restores 100% of the Energy you had when last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy per second spent outside Bear Form or Dire Bear Form, up to 100. Blizzard: *"We realize this removed the 'power shifting' gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the `Shifting Power` talent in the Feral talent tree."*
- **Wolfshead Helm gives 5 extra Energy when you use `Shifting Power`**, per Blizzard's notes, and its Rage half is unchanged.
- **Tiger's Fury is gone.** Blizzard's class deep dive: *"Tiger's Fury: (Removed.) This has been replaced with a new Feral-specific talent called Shifting Power."*
- **[[Claw]] became a weapon-damage multiplier**: 140% weapon damage plus 27 at rank 1, where Classic gave a flat 27. [[Ferocious Bite]] has a new rank 1 at 24, and Classic's ranks follow as ranks 2 to 6.
- **Talents new to Feral:** `Primal Bite`, `Lacerate`, `Predatory Discipline`, `Natural Instinct`, `Natural Reaction`, `Rend and Tear`, `Shifting Power`. King of the Jungle and Berserk, which older guides list, are not in the tree. `Heart of the Wild` moved to Restoration row 1, and `Furor` took its slot. `Feral Instinct` was rewritten from bear threat to +30% [[Swipe]] damage at 3/3.
- **Mangle is `Primal Bite`**, a Bear-only talent with no debuff. Blizzard: *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Classic's Primal Fury was folded into `Blood Frenzy`: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* Lists naming Mangle or Primal Fury cannot be entered as printed.
- **Cat Form's tooltip reads 12 attack power plus Agility**, against Classic's 40 plus Agility. The damage moved into the weapon and the ability multipliers.
## At level 60
**What is published:** the two Cat-only builds above, Icy Veins' and Wowhead's rotation pages, and WoWSims' Cat model. All of them keep [[Rip]] and [[Rake]] running, spend [[Omen of Clarity]] on [[Shred]], and feed Energy through `Shifting Power`. Icy Veins' dual-role alternative keeps `Primal Bite`, `Lacerate` and `Thick Hide` and gives up `Shredding Attacks`, so its cat builds with [[Claw]].
**In dungeons** Cat has no area damage, and both sites expect a Feral Druid to be asked to tank. [[Leader of the Pack]] gives the party +3% to every kind of critical strike, and does not stack with a Moonkin's aura.
**What is not known:** how Cat compares with other melee on a raid boss. Raids open December 9, and the tier lists on both sites were written before launch.
