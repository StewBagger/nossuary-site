## What Cat is for
**Damage out of stealth, at speed, and on bleeds that keep ticking while you reposition:** `Feral Swiftness` gives *"a permanent 30% speed buff while in Cat Form"* plus +4% dodge in all forms, and it now works indoors; you also have [[Prowl]], shapeshifting breaks roots, and [[Rip]] scales with attack power and runs 12 seconds off five combo points.
**The published assessment, which measures solo kill speed — and it only applies from 20, when Cat Form arrives:** one guide calls Feral *"one of the fastest and most mobile specializations in the WoW Forever beta."*
**Cat Form now comes from a quest chain, not a trainer.** *"In WoW Forever, druids earn Cat Form with a quest chain at level 20. Your trainer sends you to Moonglade, the Great Cat Spirit asks for three relics hidden in the Stormrage Barrow Dens, and when you bring them back your trainer teaches you Cat Form, [[Prowl]], [[Claw]] and [[Rip]] in one go."* It is 2,885 experience in total, and **the relics are party loot**: *"Five druids in a group loot the relic once instead of five times."*

## Rotation
**At level 20 it is two buttons:**
1. **[[Claw]]** to build combo points
2. **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
**From 24 you add [[Rake]].** The published rotation also calls for [[Tiger's Fury]] here *"when below 40 Energy"* — **drop that step:** [[Tiger's Fury]] has been removed and no longer exists to use. Its replacement `Shifting Power` (below) sits in row 4 behind `Shredding Attacks` in row 3, so both are out of reach this early.
**From 30:**
1. Maintain **[[Faerie Fire]]**, usable in form now, and it does not reset your swing timer
2. **[[Shred]] on [[Omen of Clarity]] procs**. Omen is baseline at 20 in Forever, not a talent
3. Maintain **[[Rip]]** at 5 combo points
4. Maintain **[[Rake]]**
5. **[[Shred]]** as filler, from behind
**One correction to a widely-copied level-30 rotation: it calls for [[Ferocious Bite]], which trains at level 32.** You will not have it at a level-30 cap.
The target-selection rule: *"pick [[Rip]] when the enemy will live through the ticks, and direct damage when the remaining kill time is too short. Keep an existing bleed running instead of restarting it."*
**Multi-target: there is none in Cat Form.** [[Swipe]] is Bear-only. Either shift to Bear and [[Swipe]], or spread [[Rake]] and [[Rip]] on targets durable enough to be worth it.
## Stat priority
**Agility first**, then Strength, then Stamina.
The reason Agility leads is that *"in cat form it is attack power, critical strike and armor at once."*
**The Forever-specific part reverses a Classic habit: your weapon's damage now counts in Cat Form.** Cat Form's flat attack power was **cut from 40 to 12** plus Agility, and abilities gained weapon-damage terms. Blizzard's own framing of the mechanism: auto-attack DPS in Cat Form now simply equals your weapon's DPS, at a fixed 1.0-second swing speed (2.5 seconds in Bear or Dire Bear Form), and weapon-damage abilities use the same numbers. The result: *"the weapon here is chosen on its damage first, then on its stats."* Published level-20 cat weapon picks are **two-handers on both factions**, held in both hands.
**One source disputes the weapon-DPS priority outright.** Three sources and the client data are against it, but the disagreement exists and no source publishes the actual conversion formula.

## What Forever changed for Cat
- **The old `Furor` powershifting trick is dead, but Blizzard has deliberately reintroduced the gameplay in a different form — this is confirmed by Blizzard, not a player rumour.** `Furor` was rewritten: instead of a flat 40 energy on entering Cat Form, five points let you regenerate Energy out of form *"as if they were in Cat Form"* — and Blizzard states the penalty in the same breath: *"But if they go to Bear Form, all that Energy is lost, to prevent 'bear-weaving' gameplay."* **The numbers are client text, not published:** the tooltip reads *"regain 100% of the Energy you had when you were last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy for each second you spent not in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, up to a maximum of 100"* at 5/5, and *"Leaving an empty bar and instantly re-entering supplies no flat refill."* Blizzard then adds: *"We realize this removed the 'power shifting' gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the `Shifting Power` talent in the Feral talent tree."* See `Shifting Power` above for its numbers.
- **[[Tiger's Fury]] was removed in the October 1 build.** Blizzard's class deep dive flagged it under baseline shapeshifting changes: *"Tiger's Fury: (Removed.) This has been replaced with a new Feral-specific talent called Shifting Power."* The datamined [[Tiger's Fury]] rework some guides still describe (no cost, 30-second cooldown, flat +15% physical damage) never shipped; `Shifting Power` (above) is what replaced it.
- **Bleeds carry the spec now, and [[Shred]] got stronger too.** Blizzard: Shred’s *"damage increased significantly to need less talent support to be effective"* — no figures are published either way. [[Rip]] was buffed enormously. Rank 6 deals 243/396/549/702/855 **over 12 seconds** by combo point, against Classic's 45/73/101/129/157, and it now scales with attack power.
- **[[Claw]] became a weapon-damage multiplier**: *"110% normal damage plus 27"*, where Classic gave a flat *"27 additional damage."* [[Ferocious Bite]] is unchanged from Classic, and trains at 32.
- **New talents, net of October 1:** `Primal Bite`, `Predatory Strikes`, `Natural Reaction`, `Rend and Tear`, [[Berserk]], `Shifting Power`, `Improved Shifting Power`. `King of the Jungle` was also new to Forever but **has now been removed outright** — it is gone from the tree, not merely absent from a build list. `Heart of the Wild` moved to tier 1. `Feral Instinct` was rewritten from bear threat to **+30% [[Swipe]] damage**.
- **A rename and a merge, both confirmed directly by Blizzard's class deep dive:** `Mangle` is now the `Primal Bite` talent and **is gone from the spellbook** — *"We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle."* Classic's `Primal Fury` and `Blood Frenzy` were merged into one node, and Blizzard's own text says the merged node **is `Blood Frenzy`**: *"The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent."* It gained a cat clause giving combo-point generators a chance at an extra point on a crit. **`Primal Fury` is not selectable**, so lists naming it cannot be entered as printed.
- **Periodic effects were fixed to allow critical hits one at a time, not as a blanket rule.** On the Druid side Blizzard names [[Rejuvenation]], [[Tranquility]] and [[Wild Growth]]; elsewhere Renew, Devouring Plague, Riptide, `Lacerating Strikes`, Hellfire and `Contingency Plan`. October 1 ran the other way for one case — Gnome’s `Eureka!` *"no longer benefits periodic effects at all."*
## At level 60
Unknown. The open question is whether bleed-centric damage with `Rend and Tear` beats a Rogue once [[Berserk]] and real weapons exist.
