How to read this

  • Priority is how firm the intention is. A 1st pick is what someone means to play; a 2nd or 3rd pick is a maybe. Weaker picks are shown more quietly, and they are never left out of a count.
  • “a member” is a real person who asked not to be listed by name. They are in every table and every total.
  • gap marks a profession or a role nobody has taken. Those rows stay on the page precisely because they are empty.
  • note is a button, and only rows that have one show it. Press it and the member's own words about that character open in a bubble under it; press it again, press Escape, or click anywhere else to close it. One is open at a time. Most characters do not have a note, and it says nothing about who the member is — an unnamed member's note opens like anyone else's.
  • A class colour is decoration — the class name is always written out beside it.
